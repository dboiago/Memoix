## Barley and Candied Winter Melon Drink

Author: [Anita Jacobson](https://dailycookingquest.com/about.html)

Categories: [Drink](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/drink.html) [Vegetarian](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/vegetarian.html)

Cuisines: [Chinese](https://dailycookingquest.com/cuisines/chinese.html)

Ingredients: [Vegetables](https://dailycookingquest.com/ingredients/vegetables.html)

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time: 1 hour 30 mins

Total Time: 1 hour 40 mins

Serves: 12

[Print Recipe](https://dailycookingquest.com/card/barley-and-candied-winter-melon-drink.html)

### Ingredients

-   100 gram barley (Indonesian: jali), washed and drained a couple of times
-   125 gram candied winter melon (Indonesian: baligo/beligo/kundur)
-   3 pandan leaves, washed and knotted
-   3 liter water
-   sugar (optional), to taste

### Instructions

1.  Place all ingredients in a pot and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 1 1/2 hour until barley is soft.
2.  Taste if it is sweet enough, usually it is, but you can add more sugar if you prefer a sweeter drink.
3.  Turn off heat. Can be served hot, at room temperature, or chilled.
