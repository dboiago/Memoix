**RECIPE:** _Jacob Grier, Portland, Oregon_

![](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/corpse-reviver-no-blue-jacob-briars-horizontal-crdt-emma-janzen.jpg)

Back in 2007, Jacob Briars introduced this cheeky homage to the [Corpse Reviver No. 2](https://imbibemagazine.com/corpse-reviver-2-recipe/) with [blue curaçao](https://imbibemagazine.com/elements-blue-curacao/) instead of Cointreau. “It tastes broadly and very much like a classic cocktail from the 1920s and ’30s—it just happens to be blue,” Briars says. He usually reaches for a blue bottle of Bombay Sapphire gin to seal the deal.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. gin
-   1 oz. Lillet Blanc (or Cocchi Americano)
-   1 oz. blue curaçao
-   1 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1 dash absinthe

## Preparation

Shake ingredients with ice to chill, then fine strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish.
