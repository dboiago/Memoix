Here’s the next dish in our “[Cooking with Grandma](https://thewoksoflife.com/2013/07/cooking-with-grandma/ "Cooking with Grandma")” series: Cold Tofu Salad.

In a Chinese meal, you’ll often start with cold dishes. These often consist of cold veggies, tofu, or meat dressed with light sauces and dressings. They’re especially great in the summer, when you want to eat food that’s light and cooling. Here’s one healthy, easy, fool-proof example.

## Recipe Instructions

Start by blanching your cut carrot and [long hot green pepper](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/#long-green-hot-peppers) in some simmering water for one minute.

[![cold tofu salad](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/cold-tofu-salad-1.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/cold-tofu-salad-1.jpg)

Drain the veggies in a colander and then throw them into a bowl of ice water. This will guarantee that your carrots stay bright orange and your peppers stay bright green.

[![cold tofu salad](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/cold-tofu-salad-2.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/cold-tofu-salad-2.jpg)

In a serving bowl, add the raw [tofu “noodles”](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-tofu-bean-curd/#tofu-noodles) with your veggies. No worries about eating the tofu raw, it’s totally good.

[![cold tofu salad](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/cold-tofu-salad-3.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/cold-tofu-salad-3.jpg)

Then toss in the [sesame oil](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-sauces-vinegars-oils/#sesame-oil), salt, and [white pepper](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-spices-condiments/#white-pepper). Mix it together well and garnish with toasted black or white [sesame seeds](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-spices-condiments/#sesame-seeds).

[![cold-tofu-salad](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/cold-tofu-salad-4.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/cold-tofu-salad-4.jpg)

 And that’s it! Serve it as a side dish or as a first course. It’s a great dish for summertime.

## Recipe

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 5 minutes

Total: 10 minutes

-   ▢ 1 package [tofu “noodles”](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-tofu-bean-curd/#tofu-noodles)
-   ▢ 1 carrot (sliced into thin matchsticks)
-   ▢ 1 long hot green pepper (sliced into long, thin strips)
-   ▢ 1 ½ teaspoons [sesame oil](https://thewoksoflife.com/sesame-oil/)
-   ▢ salt and white pepper
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [toasted sesame seeds](https://thewoksoflife.com/sesame-seeds/)

-   Start by blanching your cut carrot and green pepper in some simmering water for one minute. Remove the veggies from the pot and place them into a bowl of ice water to preserve color and crunch.
    
-   In a serving bowl, add the raw tofu noodles with your veggies. Toss in the sesame oil, salt, and white pepper. Mix it together well and garnish with toasted black or white sesame seeds.
