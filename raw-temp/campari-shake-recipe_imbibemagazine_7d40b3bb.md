Fresh grapefruit and warm honey brighten and sweeten this bittersweet Campari shake.

## Ingredients

Yield:4

-   1/2 cup Campari
-   1/4 cup granulated sugar
-   1/4 cup honey
-   Pinch kosher salt
-   1/2 cup grapefruit pulp
-   8 scoops vanilla ice cream

## Preparation

Blend first 4 ingredients. Add remaining ingredients and blend until just combined. Pour into glasses and garnish.
