This baked [eggs recipe](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/egg-recipes) from the [Galvin Brothers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/galvin-brothers) is a true [breakfast](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/breakfast-recipes-brunch-recipes) winner. Ramekins filled with egg, cherry tomatoes and ham make a great option for brunch and will be particularly enjoyed by younger [kids](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kids-recipes), who can help assemble the pots before they go in the oven. Served with brown bread soldiers, they are a step up from your regular boiled eggs and take less than a half-hour to make.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   4 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   8 [cherry tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-tomato-recipes)
-   1 knob of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100g of [spinach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spinach-recipes)
-   2 slices of [ham](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ham-recipes)
-   25g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   4 slices of [brown bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

-   Ramekins

1

In a bowl, lightly crush the cherry tomatoes. Place in the bottom of each ramekin

-   8 [cherry tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-tomato-recipes)

2

Place a pan over a medium heat and add a knob of butter. Add the spinach and cook for about 1 minute until wilted down. Spoon the wilted spinach into the ramekins

-   100g of [spinach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spinach-recipes)
-   1 knob of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

3

Tear the ham into strips and place on top of the spinach

-   2 slices of [ham](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ham-recipes)

4

Crack 1 egg into each of the ramekins and season with a pinch of salt and pepper. Sprinkle grated cheese over the top

-   4 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   25g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

5

Cook in an oven set to 160°C/gas mark 3 for 10-15 minutes, or until the egg whites have set

6

Serve immediately with toasted brown bread cut into soldiers

-   4 slices of [brown bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
