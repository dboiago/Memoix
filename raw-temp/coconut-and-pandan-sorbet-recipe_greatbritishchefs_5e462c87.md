[Anna Hansen](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/anna-hansen)'s coconut [sorbet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes) recipe exemplifies her diverse approach to cuisine - the fragrant, aromatic flavour of pandan essence melds beautifully with [coconut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/coconut-milk-recipes). Pandan essence can be tricky to track down but should be available in well-stocked East Asian supermarkets, or is readily available online.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   500ml of [coconut milk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes)
-   180ml of water
-   130g of caster sugar
-   65g of liquid glucose
-   2 drops of pandan essence
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juice and zest
-   [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes) to taste
-   1 pinch of [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

-   Ice cream maker

1

First, make a syrup for the sorbet. Place the water, sugar and glucose in pan and bring to the boil. Simmer for 3 minutes or so, then cool and refrigerate until needed

-   180ml of water
-   130g of caster sugar
-   65g of liquid glucose

2

Measure out 275ml of the sorbet syrup and place in a bowl with the rest of the ingredients. Whisk until thoroughly combined, then churn in an ice cream maker

-   500ml of [coconut milk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes)
-   2 drops of pandan essence
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juice and zest
-   [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes) to taste
-   1 pinch of [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Serve a scoop of the sorbet as it is, or with a sprinkling of praline on top
