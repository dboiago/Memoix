Rhum agricole stars in this alternative to the vodka-based classic.

Looking for an alternative to the vodka-based Espresso Martini, Buck & Breck brushed up a 1930s recipe using rhum agricole.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. rhum agricole
-   2/3 oz. espresso
-   1 tsp. rich simple syrup (2:1)

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled glass. Garnish.
