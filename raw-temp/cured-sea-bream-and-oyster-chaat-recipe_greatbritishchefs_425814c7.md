#### To serve

-   1/4 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1/4 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1/2 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced and zested
-   1/2 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded and finely chopped
-   1 knob of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), small, finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), finely chopped
-   1/4 [avocado](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), ripe, diced

1

Begin by making a cure. Crush the coriander seeds, peppercorns and chilli flakes in a mortar and pestle then mix with the salt and sugar. Apply the mix to the fleshy side of the fish and leave in the fridge to cure for 2 hours

-   1 pinch of [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   10g of [sea salt flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   10g of caster sugar
-   1 [sea bream fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bream-recipes), weighing approx. 140g, pin-boned

2

After this time, wash the cure off the bream and use a sharp knife to remove any silverskin on the fillet. Skin the fish, then cut it into a small dice. Reserve in the fridge or in a bowl set over a bowl of ice

3

Shuck the oysters, carefully retaining all the juices. Clean the shells and reserve for serving. Carefully cut the oysters into a similar-sized dice as the bream, then mix with the fish, pouring in the reserved juices

-   4 [oysters](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oyster-recipes)

4

To make the dressing, place the unripe mango flesh in a pan with 100ml of water. Simmer for 5-7 minutes, until it breaks down and forms a marmalade-like consistency. Add the spices, salt and sugar, then cook for a further minute. Taste to check the balance of seasonings, then chill as quickly as possible

-   30g of [green mango](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), (unripe mango), peeled and diced
-   100ml of water
-   1 pinch of [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes), toasted and ground
-   1 pinch of [black salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [red chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 pinch of caster sugar

5

Add the zest and juice of the lemon, lime and orange to the fish mixture, then stir to combine. Mix in a few tablespoons of the mango dressing, taste to check you're happy with the flavour, then mix in the ginger, chilli, mint and coriander

-   1/4 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1/4 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1/2 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced and zested
-   1/2 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded and finely chopped
-   1 knob of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), small, finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), finely chopped

6

Divide the mixture between the clean oyster shells and finish with the diced avocado

-   1/4 [avocado](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), ripe, diced
