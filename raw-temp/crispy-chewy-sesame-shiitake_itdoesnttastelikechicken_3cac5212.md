### **To Make Crispy & Chewy Sesame Shiitake:**

You are going to need dried shiitake mushrooms. Fresh mushrooms won't work the same way as they don't have the same chewy texture as the dried ones. I found a big bag of dried shiitake at my local grocery store, but if your store doesn't carry them, you can [order them online](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00PXRL39C/ref=as_li_qf_sp_asin_il_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=itdottalich-20&camp=1789&creative=9325&linkCode=as2&creativeASIN=B00PXRL39C&linkId=02b2d52103b6a8faaad22955d6dc2607). One bag lasts a long time!

Rehydrate the mushrooms by covering them in boiling water. I always put a bowl on top to help push to mushrooms down and keep them submerged. Soak them for around 40 minutes until they are nice and tender.

![Crispy & Chewy Sesame Shiitake! Slightly crispy on the outside, super chewy and juicy on the inside, coated in a sticky sweet and spicy sauce. A vegan version of sesame beef. #itdoesnttastelikechicken](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Crispy-and-Chewy-Sesame-Shiitake-mushrooms-beef-vegan-recipe-07.jpg)

Drain the mushrooms. If you wish you can save the liquid to use as a mushroom broth. Now squeeze out the excess water from the mushrooms. Don't mush them to death, but just lightly squeeze them and shake off the water so they are on the drier side. Cut them into nice thick slices, about ½ inch.

![Crispy & Chewy Sesame Shiitake! Slightly crispy on the outside, super chewy and juicy on the inside, coated in a sticky sweet and spicy sauce. A vegan version of sesame beef. #itdoesnttastelikechicken](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Crispy-and-Chewy-Sesame-Shiitake-mushrooms-beef-vegan-recipe-08.jpg)

Toss them in the cornstarch so they are evenly coated and shake off the excess.

![Crispy & Chewy Sesame Shiitake! Slightly crispy on the outside, super chewy and juicy on the inside, coated in a sticky sweet and spicy sauce. A vegan version of sesame beef. #itdoesnttastelikechicken](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Crispy-and-Chewy-Sesame-Shiitake-mushrooms-beef-vegan-recipe-09.jpg)

Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Make sure the oil is nice and hot before adding in the mushrooms so that they fry, and not just soak up the oil. Fry the mushrooms until they are lightly golden on all sides, and no white cornstarch remains.

![Crispy & Chewy Sesame Shiitake! Slightly crispy on the outside, super chewy and juicy on the inside, coated in a sticky sweet and spicy sauce. A vegan version of sesame beef. #itdoesnttastelikechicken](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Crispy-and-Chewy-Sesame-Shiitake-mushrooms-beef-vegan-recipe-06.jpg)

In the meantime make the sauce by adding all of the ingredients to a small saucepan. Cook the sauce for about 2 minutes until it begins to thicken. Add in the fried shiitakes and toss to coat. Serve immediately over rice or in any way that you desire.

##### 

Prep: 40 minutes

Cook: 10 minutes

Total: 50 minutes

Servings: 2 Servings as a main, 4 as a side

#### For the shiitake:

-   ▢ 2 cups dried shiitakes, (12-16 mushrooms)
-   ▢ Boiling water, (to soak the mushrooms)
-   ▢ ¼ cup [cornstarch](https://amzn.to/3FMwecl)
-   ▢ 2 tablespoons [light oil](https://amzn.to/466zBWe), (such as canola or peanut), plus more if needed

#### For the sesame sauce:

-   ▢ ¼ cup [soy sauce](https://amzn.to/4jkmyra)
-   ▢ 2 tablespoons [brown sugar](https://amzn.to/3SsRA64)
-   ▢ 2 tablespoons [rice vinegar](https://amzn.to/42ljTXr)
-   ▢ 2 cloves garlic, , minced
-   ▢ 1 inch piece of ginger, , minced
-   ▢ 2 teaspoons sriracha or other hot sauce, (optional)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [sesame oil](https://amzn.to/3u0O6xI)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [cornstarch](https://amzn.to/3FMwecl)

#### For garnish:

-   ▢ 2 green onion, , thinly sliced
-   ▢ 2 teaspoons [sesame seeds](https://amzn.to/3G5HgNg)

-   Cover the mushrooms in boiling water, and put something on top to keep them submerged. Soak for around 40 minutes until tender. Drain the mushrooms. If you wish you can save the liquid to use as a mushroom broth. Now squeeze out the excess water from the mushrooms. Don’t mush them to death, but just lightly squeeze them and shake off the water so they are on the drier side. Slice them into nice thick slices, about ½ inch. Toss them in the cornstarch so they are evenly coated and shake off the excess.
    
-   Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Make sure the oil is nice and hot before adding in the mushrooms so that they fry, and not just soak up the oil. Fry the mushrooms until they are lightly golden on all sides, and no white cornstarch remains.
    
-   In the meantime make the sauce by adding all of the ingredients to a small sauce pan. Cook the sauce for about 2 minutes until it begins to thicken. Add in the fried shiitakes and toss to coat. Serve immediately over rice or in anyway that you desire, and garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.
