**Cooks in:** _2h 10mins \+ 1h_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   2/3 C. of cream
-   1/2 C. of milk
-   1/4 LB. of dark chocolate
-   1/3 C. of white cane sugar
-   5 of egg yolks
-   1 vanilla bean pod
-   Salt

### Method

1

Heat the cream and the milk together. Mix the egg yolks with the seeds of half a vanilla bean pod, the sugar, and a pinch of salt; then briefly beat everything together with an electric hand mixer. Break up the chocolate into a bowl; add half of the hot milk and melt the chocolate completely.

2

Then add in the rest of the milk and mix well; finally, incorporate the egg yolks and continue to combine until you obtain a smooth, shiny mixture. On the bottom of a large baking pan with high edges, lay down 1 or 2 sheets of parchment paper so it is covered completely, then pour in about 2 inches of water and place 6 ramekins inside. Distribute the mixture evenly throughout each ramekin and bake at 300°F for 30 minutes.

3

Remove the baking pan from the oven, remove the ramekins from the water, and let them cool. Add a layer of sugar to the top of each ramekin. Use a blowtorch to caramelize the sugar or place the ramekins under the broiler until the top forms into a golden crust. Serve immediately.
