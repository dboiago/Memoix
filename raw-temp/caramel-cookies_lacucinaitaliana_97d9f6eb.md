**Cooks in:** _2h 20mins \+ 2h_

**Levels:** _Medium_

### List of Ingredients

-   4 C. of flour
-   3 1/2 STICKS of butter
-   1 C. of granulated sugar
-   1/2 C. of cane sugar
-   1/4 C. of cream
-   2 PCS. of eggs

### Method

1

Heat a copper saucepan, then add in 1 cup of granulated sugar a little at a time. In a separate saucepan, bring the cream to a boil. Remove the caramel from the heat and add in the cream, then slowly add in the room temperature butter; let this mixture cool.

2

Place the cooled mixture in a stand mixer; add in the flour, the cane sugar, 1 egg, and mix until you obtain a firm yet pliable dough. Mold the dough into cylinders 1 1/2 inches in diameter; wrap them in parchment paper and let them chill in the fridge for 1 hour.|Beat one egg. Remove the dough from the refrigerator and discard the parchment paper; brush the dough with the beaten egg, then roll the dough in cane sugar, pressing down so the granules adhere.

3

Cut the dough into slices a little less than 1/2-inch thick. Arrange the slices on a baking tray lined with parchment paper and bake at 340°F on the convection setting for about 20 minutes. Remove the cookies from the oven, let them cool, and serve. Place any leftover cookies in tin boxes and store them in a cool, dry place: they will keep for about 10 days.
