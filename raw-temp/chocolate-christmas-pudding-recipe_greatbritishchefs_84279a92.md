This chocolate [Christmas pudding](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/christmas-pudding-recipes) recipe from Paul A. Young is a great twist on the traditional festive dessert, with the inclusion of three forms of [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chocolate-recipes). Paul advises making the pudding at least 6 weeks before the big day so the heady flavours can develop and mature.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   50g of [70% dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), broken into chunks
-   50g of [white chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/white-chocolate-recipes), broken into chunks
-   25g of [cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes)
-   50g of [dried apricots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apricot-recipes)
-   100g of [dried figs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fig-recipes)
-   100g of dates
-   25ml of [brandy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brandy-recipes), plus extra for feeding the pudding
-   25ml of [port](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/port-cocktail-recipes)
-   1 tsp [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1 tsp [ground cardamom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [ground nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes)
-   75g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), soft
-   100g of light muscovado sugar
-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   200g of plain flour
-   250ml of Guinness
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), juiced and zested

-   1.5l pudding basin

2

The next day, add the sugar and butter to a large mixing bowl and cream together. Add 1 egg at a time, mixing well after each addition. Slowly stir in the flour, cocoa powder and chocolate

-   75g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), soft
-   100g of light muscovado sugar
-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   200g of plain flour
-   25g of [cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes)
-   50g of [70% dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), broken into chunks
-   50g of [white chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/white-chocolate-recipes), broken into chunks

3

Once the dry ingredients are nicely incorporated, stir in the Guinness, orange juice and zest and the soaked fruits

-   250ml of Guinness
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), juiced and zested

4

Grease the pudding basin and sprinkle with flour to coat. Pour the cake mix into the basin and lay a greased circle of parchment on top. Place a foil lid over the top of the basin, securing it tightly with a large elastic band or some string

5

Place the pudding in a steamer and set over simmering water. Cover the pan and steam for 3 hours, making sure you keep the water in the steamer topped up

6

Once the pudding is cooked, remove from the steamer and leave to cool overnight in the basin, keeping the parchment and foil lid in place

7

The following day, wrap the pudding in a clean tea-towel. Store the pudding in a cool, dark place, feeding it once a week by carefully removing the foil and parchment and drizzling the brandy over the pudding

8

Return the lid and re-wrap in the tea towel once the brandy has soaked in to the pudding

9

To serve the pudding, simply steam for 90 minutes to heat through and serve with brandy butter, cream or ice cream
