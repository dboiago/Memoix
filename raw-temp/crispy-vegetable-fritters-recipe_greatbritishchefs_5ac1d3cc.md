This crispy vegetable fritters recipe from [Colin McGurran](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/colin-mcgurran) will make a superb snack when served with a variety of dips. It is a great recipe for getting kids to enjoy their greens.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   200g of [cauliflower florets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes)
-   200g of [broccoli florets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broccoli-recipes)
-   200g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), sliced
-   500ml of sunflower oil, for frying
-   170g of plain flour
-   1 tsp [paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), sliced
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

1

Pour the oil into a pan to a depth of approximately 2.5cm. Heat the oil until it is just beginning to smoke

-   500ml of sunflower oil, for frying

3

Once the oil is hot enough, remove a couple of the florets from the bowl (it's best to fry in batches), shake off the excess flour and lower into the oil. Fry until golden, remove and place on kitchen towel to absorb the excess oil

4

Serve the fritters warm with a slice of lemon and a pinch of salt

-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), sliced
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
