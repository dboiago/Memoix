A staple of summertime sipping.

A classic Pimm’s Cup is one of the most refreshing cocktails and a staple of summertime sipping.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. Pimm's No. 1
-   ¼ oz. fresh lemon juice
-   Ginger ale

## Preparation

Pour the Pimm’s and lemon juice into an ice-filled glass, top with ginger ale, stir and garnish.
