Dominic Chapman's [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/apple-recipes) sauce recipe is a sweet, tangy delight. Serve with classic roast pork, particularly fattier cuts, where the acidity of the apples and lemon juice help to counteract all of that richness.

First published in 2019

2

Melt the butter in a pan over a low-medium heat. Add the apples, sugar and water and cook gently until soft and puréed

-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   75g of Demerara sugar
-   1 dash of water

3

Stir in the cinnamon and lemon juice, then transfer to a jar. Allow to cool a little before serving

-   2 pinches of [cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced to taste

[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/dominic-chapman)

Dominic Chapman's passion for food is in the blood: his family has owned and run the highly acclaimed Castle Hotel in Taunton for over 60 years.
