## To make cardamom caffè-latte ice cream, first drop the cardamom berries into the hot coffee to infuse for 30 minutes. Add the milk and cream and heat ...

**Cooks in:** _50mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 LB. of milk
-   6 OZ. of fresh cream
-   4 1/2 OZ. of sugar
-   3 OZ. of espresso coffee
-   1 1/2 OZ. of condensed milk
-   3/4 OZ. of honey
-   1/4 OZ. of carob flour (optional)
-   6 of cardamom berries

### Method

1

To make cardamom caffè-latte ice cream, first drop the cardamom berries into the hot coffee to infuse for 30 minutes. Add the milk and cream and heat everything up again.

2

Then add sugar, honey, condensed milk and carob flour and mix well with a whisk. Continue to heat, stirring until the cream reaches 185°F.

3

Filter and cool the mixture quickly, pouring it into a bowl with a very cold bain-marie. Blend the cream again using an immersion mixer, then pour the mixture into an ice cream maker and start the machine.
