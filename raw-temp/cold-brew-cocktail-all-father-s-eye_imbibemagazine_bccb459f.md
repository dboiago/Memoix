**RECIPE:** Joseph Glashauser for [The Last Word](https://thelastwordbar.com/), Ann Arbor, Michigan

![aquavit cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/all-fathers-eye-last-word-bar-crdt-marlee-katt.jpg)

A medley of warm spices defines this cold-brew cocktail from [The Last Word](https://thelastwordbar.com/) in Ann Arbor, Michigan. “The chocolate notes in the coffee are a natural pairing for the caraway found in aquavit, and the floral undertones of the amaro bring out the balance of aquavit’s supporting botanicals,” says the drink’s creator, Joseph Glashauser.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. aquavit (Glashauser uses Norden)
-   1 oz. Amaro Meletti
-   ¾ oz. cold-brew coffee
-   2 dashes Bittercube Cherry Bark Vanilla bitters

## Preparation

Stir all of the ingredients with ice for around 30 seconds, then strain into chilled glass and garnish.
