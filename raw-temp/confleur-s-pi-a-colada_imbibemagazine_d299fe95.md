Two types of rum make for a delicious contrast in this riff.

Experimenting with rum styles is one of the easiest ways to bring new life to the classic [Piña Colada](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe-pina-colada/), and at Confleur in Paducah, Kentucky, the staff chose a Barbados rum with hints of natural banana flavor and a spiced rum for a nice contrast of sweetness and spice.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. Bumbu Rum (Diplomatico Reserva Exclusiva or Plantation Xaymaca would also work)
-   ½ oz. spiced rum
-   ½ oz. fresh lime juice
-   ¾ oz. coconut cream
-   1½ oz. pineapple juice

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in an ice-filled cocktail shaker and shake to chill. Strain into a hurricane glass filled with pebble ice, then garnish with pineapple leaf.
