**Cooks in:** _1h 30mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _12_

### List of Ingredients

-   14 OZ. of mango
-   3/4 C. of milk
-   1/2 C. of cream
-   1/3 C. of sugar
-   1 OZ. of fresh ginger
-   4 egg yolks
-   Lime
-   2 egg whites
-   2/3 C. of powdered sugar
-   2/3 STICK of butter, melted
-   1/2 C. of flour
-   2 TBSP. of cocoa powder

### Method

1

Heat the milk; as soon as it begins to boil, add in the egg yolks beaten together with the sugar. Cook while stirring until the cream has thickened enough that it coats a spoon once lifted out of the mixture. 

2

Turn off the heat, add in the cream, and let it cool.

3

Blend 12 ounces of the mango; add the juice and zest of half a lime. Grate the ginger onto a paper towel; wring out the ginger, letting the juice drip into the blended mango. 

4

Combine the mango mixture with the cream and pour it into an ice cream maker: the ice cream will be ready in 45-60 minutes.

5

Cat’s tongue cookies: mix the egg whites with the powdered sugar, the flour, the cocoa powder, and the melted butter until you obtain a thick batter. Spoon the batter onto a baking tray lined with parchment paper and spread it out with a spatula into thin strips that measure about 8 x 2 inches. Bake the cookies at 350°F for 5-6 minutes.

6

Take the cookies out of the oven and remove them from the tray; while they are still hot and pliable, mold them around smooth cups or the center of a bundt pan. Prepare a few at a time, depending on the number of cups or pans you have available. Let them cool; they will become crispy.

7

Serve the ice cream with the cat’s tongue cookies, the remaining mango cut into cubes, and mint or lemon balm leaves to taste.
