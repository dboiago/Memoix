### Ingredients

-   1 Fuji Apple, peeled and cored (about 200 gram)
-   150 gram papaya
-   4 tablespoon strawberry yoghurt
-   2 tablespoon honey
-   200 ml water

### Instructions

1.  Put everything in a blender and process until smooth.
2.  Divide into two serving glasses and serve immediately.
