## A twist on the famous chocolate and almond cake from the island of Capri, in this recipe buttery chocolatey pie crust is filled with chocolate and almond batter, cooked and topped with caramelized pears; the definition of decadence.

**Cooks in:** _2h 30mins \+ 1h_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _12_

### List of Ingredients

-   2 CUPS of type 2 or all-purpose flour
-   1 CUP of unsalted butter
-   1 CUP of almond flour, plus extra
-   1/2 CUP of cane sugar, plus extra
-   3 1/2 OZ. of dark chocolate
-   2/3 CUP of powdered sugar
-   2 OZ. of chocolate chips
-   4 of pears
-   3 of egg yolks
-   2 of large eggs
-   rum
-   salt

### Method

1

Combine 1/2 cup plus 1 Tbsp. butter with the cane sugar, a pinch of salt and 3 egg yolks, then add the flour. This will form a soft dough. Let rest in a bowl covered with plastic wrap for 30 minutes. 

2

In the meantime, line a baking dish with removable bottom (9 1/2" in diameter by 1" tall) with parchment paper.

3

 Add 2 1/2 Tbsp. chocolate chips to the dough and roll it out. Place in the parchment-lined baking dish. Poke holes in the dough with tines of a fork.  

4

Preheat the oven to 340°F. 

5

Melt the dark chocolate in a saucepan over low heat. Beat 2 egg yolks with a pinch of salt and the powdered sugar. Add a scant 1/2 cup soft butter, the almond flour and the melted chocolate. In a separate bowl, beat the egg whites to firm peaks. Gently fold them into the chocolate mixture. Add 3 Tbsp. chocolate chips and 3 Tbsp. rum.

6

Pour the chocolate mixture into the pie shell and bake at 340°F for around 40 minutes. Remove the pie from the oven and let cool.

7

Clean and pare the pears, partially peeling them. Cut them into small chunks and caramelize them in a pan with 2 Tbsp. cane sugar. Put the pie on a serving platter. Top it with the pears and dust with almond flour.
