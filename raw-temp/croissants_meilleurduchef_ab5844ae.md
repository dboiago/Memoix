Ingredients:

250g flour T45, 5g salt, 30g castor sugar, 10g honey, 10g fresh baker's yeast, 50g unsalted butter, soft, 120g whole milk, 150g [extra dry butter](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/fr/achat/epicerie/frais/gre-beurre-special-professionnel-montaigu-2.html "Beurre de tourage - spécial feuilletage, viennoiserie - 1 plaque de 2 kg"), A few [dark chocolate baking sticks](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/en/shop/chocolate/ingredients/chocolate-baking-sticks/gre-chocolate-baking-sticks-300-cacao-barry.html "Chocolate Baking Sticks - 300 units - 1.6kg - Callebaut"), 60g egg yolks, 40g milk...

Method

Before starting this Easy Croissant and Pain au Chocolat recipe, organise all the necessary ingredients.  
**Start the day before:** Pour the flour in the stand mixer recipient. Place the salt on one side.  
Add the honey (melted in the microwave beforehand until liquid).  
Pour the castor sugar over the salt.  
Add the fresh baker's yeast on the opposite side of the sugar and salt. If you're using dehydrated yeast, reduced quantities by 2.5 times. The salt should not come into direct contact with the yeast. To make sure this doesn't happen, place the yeast at the bottom of the bowl then pour the flour on top.
