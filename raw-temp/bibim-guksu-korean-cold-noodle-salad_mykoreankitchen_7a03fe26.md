## Summer Perfect, Spicy Korean Noodle Salad: Bibim Guksu

This bibim guksu is something I am very much enjoying making and eating lately. It is very tasty and moreish. It’s loaded with lots of salads and vegetables, so you can get a good amount of crunchy texture as well.

Besides, it doesn’t require any complicated preparation or cooking, which is a definite bonus point on hot summer days.

![bibim guksu served with salad in a clear bowl](https://mykoreankitchen.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/05/Bibim-Guksu4.jpg)

## What is Bibim Guksu

Bibim guksu / bibimguksu (비빔국수) means mixed noodles; similar to [bibimbap](https://mykoreankitchen.com/bibimbap-korean-mixed-rice-with-meat-and-assorted-vegetables/) (비빔밥) being mixed rice.

Ultimately you can add whatever toppings you would like (or no toppings at all, but that would be so boring!), but as you will see below, this version of bibim guksu is made with loads of vegetables. It looks bulky when you serve, yet it’s a light and very filling meal. 

Its main taste comes from the sauce, which has a slightly spicy, sweet and tangy taste, and the nutty fragrance from the sesame oil is another pleasant experience.

Bibim guksu is a popular summer dish in Korea, because the spicy and sour taste can rejuvenate your lost appetite on drowsy, hot, and humid summer days.

## Ingredients for Bibim Guksu (Serves 2)

![bibim guksu ingredients](https://mykoreankitchen.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/05/5.-Bibim-Guksu-Ingredients.jpg)

### Noodles

-   180g/6.3 ounces dried buckwheat noodles (or [soba noodles)](http://amzn.to/2bhpLsH)

### Salad Options

-   2 lettuce leaves (40g/ 1.5 ounces), thinly sliced
-   1/4 red cabbage (140g / 5 ounces), thinly sliced
-   1/2 small cucumber (60g / 2 ounces), julienned
-   1/3 small carrot (40g / 1.4 ounces), julienned
-   (optional) 60g / 2 ounces red radish, julienned  
-   (optional) a few snow pea sprouts
-   2 Tbsp [kimchi](https://mykoreankitchen.com/kimchi-recipe/), cut into thumbnail size

### Bibim Sauce (Mix these in a bowl)

-   2 Tbsp [gochujang](http://amzn.to/2bCDmgr) (Korean chili paste)
-   2 Tbsp rice vinegar
-   1 Tbsp [soy sauce](http://amzn.to/2bgEsPd)
-   1 Tbsp honey
-   1 Tbsp brown sugar
-   1 Tbsp sesame oil
-   1 Tbsp [toasted sesame seeds](http://amzn.to/2bxtfHP)

\*1 Tbsp = 15 ml

\*\* If you want to learn more about Korean ingredients, check my [essential Korean cooking ingredients list](https://mykoreankitchen.com/essential-korean-cooking-ingredients/)!

## How to Make Bibim Guksu

Boil the noodles in rolling boiling water following the package instructions. (I boiled mine for 3 mins.) Drain the noodles and run some cold water over the noodles to cool them down. Drain.

![Boiling Soba Noodles](https://mykoreankitchen.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/05/6.-Boiling-Soba-Noodles.jpg)

Place the noodles in a large serving bowl and add the salad ingredients and sauce. Serve.

![Mixing Bibim Guksu Ingredients in a Bowl](https://mykoreankitchen.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/05/7.-How-to-Make-Bibim-Guksu.jpg)

![Korean noodle salad topped with spicy gochujang sauce](https://mykoreankitchen.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/05/3.-Bibimguksu.jpg)

To eat, mix all ingredients well with chopsticks and indulge.

![Mixing bibim guksu with chopsticks](https://mykoreankitchen.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/05/4.-Spicy-Korean-Noodle-Salad.jpg)

## Note

You can alter the toppings as you wish, like adding some boiled eggs or [radish pickles](https://mykoreankitchen.com/korean-style-pink-radish-pickles/). Canned tuna or salmon (e.g. sweet chili flavored) also works great in this noodle bowl.
