Item 1 of 1

-   4
    
-   60 minutes
    

[Pearl barley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pearl-barley-recipes) makes an excellent alternative to rice in [risottos](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/risotto-recipes), adding a nutty flavour and bite. With a fun twist of creating faces with vegetable shapes, this is [Andrew MacKenzie](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/andrew-mackenzie)'s preferred dish for encouraging his children to eat vegetables. Swap the Parmesan for a veggie alternative if desired.

First published in 2015

-   Food processor or blender

1

Soak the pearl barley in water overnight, then drain well

-   200g of [pearl barley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pearl-barley-recipes)

2

For the tomato sauce, blanch the tomatoes for a few seconds in a pan of boiling water then transfer to a bowl of iced water. Peel the skins, remove the seeds and dice the flesh

-   8 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)

3

Place a pan over a low-medium heat and add olive oil. Once the oil is hot, sweat the shallots and garlic. Add the diced tomatoes and 200ml of vegetable stock and cook for 15 minutes

-   1 tbsp of olive oil
-   3 [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), diced
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   200ml of vegetable stock

4

Transfer to a blender and blitz until smooth. Pass through a sieve and set aside

5

For the risotto, place a heavy-based pan over a medium heat and add vegetable oil. Once the oil is hot, add the diced shallots and crushed garlic and cook until transparent

-   1 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   2 [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)

6

Add the pearl barley and fry gently for 2 minutes. Then, add the tomato sauce, a little at a time, stirring constantly until all the sauce has been absorbed

7

Gradually add the remaining stock, stirring at regular intervals until the barley is tender and all the stock has been absorbed

-   600ml of vegetable stock

8

One by one, cook the baby corn, green beans, carrot and beetroot - it is important you cook this last - in lightly salted boiling water for 2 minutes each. Strain and set aside

-   4 [baby corn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes)
-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), cut into thin discs
-   8 [green beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-bean-recipes)
-   2 [beetroots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes), cooked, cut into discs
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

9

Stir in the Parmesan and divide the risotto onto 4 plates, shaping each mound into a circle

-   25g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated

10

Arrange the vegetables on top to make faces (carrots for mouth, corn for nose, beetroot for eyes and beans for eyebrows) and serve immediately
