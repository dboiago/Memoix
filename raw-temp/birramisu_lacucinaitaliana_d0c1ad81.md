## A special version of the famous tiramisu: if you have a pale ale in the house, do try birramisu.

**Cooks in:** _3h 30mins \+ 3h_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 3/4 C. of cream
-   1 1/2 C. of mascarpone cheese
-   1 1/4 C. of beer
-   3/4 C. of sugar
-   1 packet vanilla sugar
-   1 teaspoon cocoa powder
-   20 of ladyfingers

### Method

1

To make birramisu, in a stand mixer combine the cream, mascarpone, sugar, vanilla sugar and 7 tablespoons of beer until it forms a thick cream. In a container (glass or whatever you like), place a layer of cream, then a layer of ladyfingers dipped in beer, followed by another layer of cream, and so on.

2

Continuing adding layers at least 2 or 3 more times, depending on the size of the container. Keep the birramisu refrigerated for about 3 hours.

3

Before serving, dust with cocoa powder and garnish as you like.
