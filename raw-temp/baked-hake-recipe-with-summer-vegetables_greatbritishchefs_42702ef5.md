Adam Simmonds serves up a beautiful baked [hake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/hake-recipes) recipe, served with colourful seasonal vegetables and finished with a vibrant dill oil. The fish is baked on a bed of [lemongrass](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lemongrass-recipes) and shallot for an aromatic finish. Dill oil is very quick to whip up in the Vitamix but requires time to strain to give it a crystal-clear finish, so make sure you plan ahead.

First published in 2017

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Baked hake

-   4 hake fillets, each weighing approximately 160g
-   1 stick of [lemongrass](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-grass-recipes) (1), roughly chopped
-   50g of [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely sliced
-   150ml of white wine
-   30g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), diced and cold
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juice to season the sauce, zest to garnish
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### Dill oil

-   25g of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), picked
-   100ml of grapeseed oil

#### Summer vegetables

-   8 [baby carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)
-   8 [baby courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes)
-   250g of [fresh peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes), podded weight (1kg if buying peas in pods)
-   250g of [broad beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broad-bean-recipes), podded weight (1kg if buying beans in pods)
-   1 handful of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   1 handful of [chervil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chervil-recipes), chopped
-   1 handful of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

-   Vitamix Pro750 fitted with 0.9l Container
-   Muslin cloth or a coffee filter

1

Make the dill oil the day before you plan to serve the dish. Blanch the dill in salted boiling water for 5 seconds then refresh in ice water

-   25g of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), picked

2

Squeeze out any excess water from the dill and transfer to the Vitamix container with the oil. Blitz for 15–20 seconds until the dill has broken down (do not over-blend or the flavour will be compromised). Pass through a fine sieve then strain through a coffee filter or muslin cloth overnight in the fridge. The oil will keep up to 5 days, but keep in a dark place to maintain the vibrant colour

-   100ml of grapeseed oil

3

When ready to cook the final dish, use a mandoline or peeler to shave the carrots and courgettes into thin ribbons. Place in a bowl, lightly season with salt and mix gently. Set aside

-   8 [baby courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes)
-   8 [baby carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

4

Blanch the podded broad beans for 2 minutes in heavily salted boiling water, adding the podded peas for the final 30 seconds of cooking. Strain and refresh in iced water before removing the outer skins from the beans (and peas, for a very refined finish)

-   250g of [broad beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broad-bean-recipes), podded weight (1kg in pods)
-   250g of [fresh peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes), podded weight (1kg in pods)

5

Preheat the oven to 175°C/gas mark 4

6

Place the sliced shallots and lemongrass in a baking tin and place the hake fillets on top. Add the wine and bake in the oven for 5–8 minutes

-   50g of [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely sliced
-   150ml of white wine
-   1 stick of [lemongrass](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-grass-recipes) (1), roughly chopped
-   4 hake fillets, each weighing approximately 160g

7

Remove the fish from the oven and keep covered in a warm place. Pass the liquid from the tin through a sieve into a clean pan, place over a medium heat and reduce slightly. Whisk in the cold cubes of butter to emulsify, and season with salt and lemon juice to taste

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   30g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), diced and cold
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juice to season the sauce, zest to garnish

8

Drop the carrot, courgette, peas and beans into the sauce to heat through for 30 seconds, then gently stir through the chopped herbs

-   1 handful of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), chopped
-   1 handful of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   1 handful of [chervil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chervil-recipes), chopped

9

Divide the vegetables between bowls and top with the fish. Finish with drops of the dill oil, a little lemon zest and some pea shoots to finish

-   [pea shoots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-shoot-recipes)
