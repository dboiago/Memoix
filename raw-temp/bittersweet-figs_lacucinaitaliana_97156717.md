## To make bittersweet figs, first cook 8-10 figs in a pot with 4 peeled and halved shallots, 3/4 cup white wine, 3/4 cup water, 3/4 cup white wine vineg...

**Cooks in:** _20mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _5_

### List of Ingredients

-   10 of firm figs
-   4 of shallots
-   7 OZ. of white wine
-   3/4 CUP of water
-   3/4 CUP of white wine vinegar
-   1 tsp salt
-   2 tsp sugar
-   2 bay leaves
-   1 clove
-   Dried chili pepper

### Method

1

To make bittersweet figs, first cook 8-10 figs in a pot with 4 peeled and halved shallots, 3/4 cup white wine, 3/4 cup water, 3/4 cup white wine vinegar, 1 tsp salt and 2 tsp sugar, 2 bay leaves, one clove, and chili flakes to taste.

2

Cover and cook over the lowest heat setting; 1 minute after it reaches the boil, pierce the figs and cook a further 5 minutes; turn off the heat and leave to cool in the cooking liquid. Remove the figs.
