Beautifully bright and refreshing, the Americano cocktail is one of the quintessential low-alcohol cocktails. It’s said to have been named after the flurry of American expats who adopted the drink as their favorite during Prohibition.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. Campari
-   1 1/2 oz. sweet vermouth
-   Club soda

## Preparation

Pour the Campari and vermouth into an ice-filled glass. Top with soda, stir and garnish.
