These elegant ‘tacos’ from [Stu Deeley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/stu-deeley-chef "Stu Deeley") are made by baking a rich cracker over metal tubes to give them their elegant curved shape. The tubes are then filled with a rich [crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/crab-recipes "Crab") and XO mayo filling. Any small ovenproof tube-shaped mould can be used to make the crackers. This recipe makes more XO mayonnaise than you'll need, and leftovers will keep in the fridge for a few days.

First published in 2024

-   6cm pastry cutter

1

Preheat the oven to 190°C, fan speed 2

2

For the taco mix, combine everything except the egg whites and set aside to cool slightly

-   120g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), melted
-   180g of plain flour
-   32g of caster sugar
-   [zest of 1 lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)

3

Once cool, whisk in the egg whites

-   120g of [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

4

Spread the taco mix thinly into a silpat mat, and then bake for 3 minutes. Remove from the oven, but don’t turn off the oven

5

Cut out six tacos with a round cutter to 6 cm

6

Drape the taco over tubes covered with greaseproof paper, and then cook for 5 minutes or until golden brown

7

Leave on the tubes to cool. Place into a sealed container once cold

10

Fill each taco with 13g crab mix, and then garnish with violas

-   [viola flowers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/edible-flower-recipes), to garnish
