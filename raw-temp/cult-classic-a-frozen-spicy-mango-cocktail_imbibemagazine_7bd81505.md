A NOLA Jazz Fest cult classic gets a glowup.

Andrea Leibrandt, bar manager of Mister Mao in New Orleans, gives Jazz Fest cult classic, the [Mango Freeze](https://www.nolanow.com/2020/04/25/mango-freeze-jazz-fest-cult-classic/), a glowup by transforming the simple scoop of mango sorbet into a frozen spicy mango cocktail. Mango sorbet is blended with mango and yuzu liqueurs, rum, and lime juice and spiced with chamoy and tajín.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. white rum
-   1 oz. mango liqueur
-   1/2 oz. yuzu liqueur
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1 scoop mango sorbet

## Preparation

Drip the inside rim of the glass with chamoy. Add all of the ingredients to a blender, add ice as needed, and blend for 30 seconds or until smooth. Pour into the prepared glass and sprinkle with tajín.
