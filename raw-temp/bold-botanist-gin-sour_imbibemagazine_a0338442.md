An homage to one of America’s greatest national parks.

This gin sour was named after Joseph Christmas Ives, a botanist who first explored the Grand Canyon.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. gin
-   3/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   [1/2 oz. simple syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   1 egg white (pasteurized if you like)
-   6 mint leaves

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a shaker and dry shake (no ice). Add ice and shake vigorously. Strain into a chilled glass. Drop the bitters onto the surface and use a toothpick to create a star shape. Garnish with mint.
