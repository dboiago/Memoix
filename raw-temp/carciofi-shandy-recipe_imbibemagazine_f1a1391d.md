This shandy recipe was created by bartender Sofia Present when she worked at the Brooklyn location of [August Laura](https://www.augustlauranyc.com/), which has since relocated to New York’s East Village. It features a generous pour of earthy, artichoke-based Cynar. Apple cider, cinnamon and ginger beer round out the autumnal flavors, with lemon juice bringing a snap of brightness to the mix.

## Preparation

Combine the Cynar, apple cider, syrup, and lemon juice in a shaker with ice. Shake until chilled, then strain into a glass. Top with ginger beer and garnish with a few slices of apple.
