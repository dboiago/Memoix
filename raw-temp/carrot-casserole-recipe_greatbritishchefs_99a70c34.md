[Marcus Wareing](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/marcus-wareing) offers a delicious alternative to roasted or boiled [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/carrot-recipes) as a veg side dish for a roast or meaty main. This easy [oven bake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oven-bake-recipes) of grated carrots has added flavour from cheddar and coriander.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   225g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), grated
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   50ml of vegetable stock
-   1/2 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   20g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   10g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tsp olive oil
-   1 pinch of [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

1

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4

2

Place a pan over a medium heat and add the grated carrot and salt. Cook until the liquid given off from the carrot evaporates

-   225g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), grated
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Add the stock and reduce until dry. Remove the pan from the heat and set aside

-   50ml of vegetable stock

4

Place a separate pan over a low-medium heat and add oil. Once the oil is hot, sweat the onions until soft but not coloured

-   1 tsp olive oil
-   1/2 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped

5

Add the chopped onion to the carrot mixture along with the cheese, coriander and egg. Mix well to combine and season with pepper

-   20g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   10g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

6

Place the mixture in a small deep baking dish lined with parchment paper. Smooth over and place in the oven for 15 minutes

7

Remove from the oven and allow to cool for 5 minutes. Serve warm
