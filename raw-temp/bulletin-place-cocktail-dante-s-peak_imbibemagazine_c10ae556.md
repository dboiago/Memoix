**RECIPE:** _Phil Spector for [Bulletin Place](https://www.mountainskyumc.org/), Sydney_

![Bulletin Place Cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/sydney-bulletinplace-dantes-peak-crdt-alanadimou.jpg)

  
In this cocktail from Bulletin Place in Sydney, cherry liqueur and fresh citrus brighten the bold pairing of scotch whisky and Campari.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. blended scotch whisky (Spector uses Johnny Walker Black)
-   2/3 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1/2 oz. Campari
-   1/2 oz. fresh orange juice
-   2 tsp. Cherry Heering
-   [2 tsp. simple syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   Chilled soda water

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients except the soda water in a shaker and shake with a single ice cube until the cube melts. Add 1 oz. of chilled soda to the shaker and pour into an ice-filled glass, adding more soda if needed. Garnish.
