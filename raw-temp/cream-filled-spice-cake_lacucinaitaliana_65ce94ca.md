## Heat 1/2 cup of milk with the honey, without letting it come to a boil. Remove it from the heat. Toast all the spices (ground ginger, cinnamon, junipe...

**Cooks in:** _5h \+ 3h_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   1/2 LB. of raspberries
-   3/4 C. of cream
-   3/4 C. of Greek yogurt
-   2/3 C. of milk
-   1 1/4 C. of rye flour
-   2/3 C. of sugar
-   1/4 LB. of white chocolate
-   1 C. of Italian 00 flour, plus extra
-   1/3 C. of Acacia honey, plus extra
-   1/3 C. of orange marmalade
-   1 OZ. of candied ginger, cut into pieces
-   1/4 C. of cocoa powder
-   3 TSP. of baking powder
-   1 TSP. of ground ginger
-   2 STICKS of cinnamon
-   6 of juniper berries
-   4 of cloves
-   2 of cardamom pods
-   1 of star anise pod
-   1 of egg
-   Butter
-   Lemon
-   Salt
-   Szechuan peppercorns

### Method

1

Heat 1/2 cup of milk with the honey, without letting it come to a boil. Remove it from the heat. Toast all the spices (ground ginger, cinnamon, juniper, cloves, cardamom, star anise, and a bit of Szechuan peppercorns) in an ungreased pan for a few minutes, then add them to the hot milk and let them infuse for 20 minutes.

2

Mix the flours with the baking powder, the cocoa powder, the marmalade, a pinch of salt, and the strained milk. Then add in the egg and mix until you obtain a smooth, even batter. Pour the batter into a buttered and floured loaf pan (7” x 3”, 2 1/2-inch height); bake at 350°F for 40 minutes. Remove the cake from the oven, then remove it from the pan and let it cool. Line another loaf pan (8” x 3”, 3-inch height) with parchment paper.

3

Cut the spice cake into slices and use them to line the loaf pan. Break up the white chocolate and place it in a bowl. Heat the rest of the milk with a tablespoon of honey and pour it over the white chocolate; stir until everything has completely melted. Allow this mixture to cool. Whip the cream. Add the cream and yogurt to the white chocolate and gently fold so the mixture doesn't deflate. Add in the candied ginger.

4

Pour the mixture into the loaf pan, level it off, and top with a layer of spice cake. Place it in the refrigerator for 3 hours. To cut down the time, you can place it in the freezer for 1 hour. Cook the raspberries with the sugar and a splash of lemon juice for 10-12 minutes over low heat. Strain the sauce and let it cool. Remove the spice cake from the fridge; remove it from the pan and serve it in slices with the raspberry sauce. Garnish to taste with fresh raspberries.
