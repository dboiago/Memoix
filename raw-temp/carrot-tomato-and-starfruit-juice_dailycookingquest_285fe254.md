## Carrot, Tomato, and Starfruit Juice

Author: [Anita Jacobson](https://dailycookingquest.com/about.html)

Categories: [Drink](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/drink.html) [Vegetarian](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/vegetarian.html)

Cuisines: [Indonesian](https://dailycookingquest.com/cuisines/indonesian.html)

Ingredients: [Fruit](https://dailycookingquest.com/ingredients/fruit.html)

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time: 0 min

Total Time: 10 mins

Serves: 2

[Print Recipe](https://dailycookingquest.com/card/carrot-tomato-and-starfruit-juice.html)

### Ingredients

-   100 gram carrot, peeled
-   2 tomatoes (about 200 gram)
-   1 starfruit (about 150 gram), peeled
-   2 tablespoon sugar (optional)
-   200 ml cold drinking water

### Instructions

1.  Put everything in a blender and process until smooth.
2.  Strain into serving glasses and serve immediately.
