-   4
    
-   45 minutes
    

With minimal cooking indoors and a little [cooking outdoors](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-barbecue-recipes), try this marinated and charred courgette [pasta dish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quick-pasta-recipes) with a fresh and fragrant lemon and mint compound butter. Much like fettuccine alfredo that was traditionally made by emulsifying [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), pasta water and cheese together to serve, this compound butter makes an instant sauce, which is topped with sharp and crunchy fried capers.

#### COURGETTES

-   2 [large green courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes), sliced 1cm thick
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced

-   Charcoal barbecue or grill

1

For the lemon and mint butter, whisk together all the ingredients in a bowl with a pinch of salt until combined

-   250g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), softened
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested, and 1 juiced
-   1/2 bunch of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), picked and leaves finely chopped
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated

2

Place the courgettes in a bowl with the olive oil, garlic, lemon juice and a pinch of salt. Toss to combine and set aside

-   2 [large green courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes), sliced 1cm thick
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced

3

Set up a barbecue for direct grilling. Grill the courgettes until you’ve achieved bar marks all over – about 2-3 minutes each side – then remove and chop into bite-sized pieces. Set aside

4

Bring a large saucepan of salted water to the boil and cook the rigatoni until al dente. Drain and reserve a cup of pasta water

-   400g of [rigatoni pasta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pasta-recipes)

5

Meanwhile, fry the capers in the olive oil in a frying pan over a medium heat until they open slightly and become crispy. Remove with a slotted spoon onto kitchen paper to drain the excess oil

-   2 tbsp of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), drained
-   3 tbsp of olive oil

6

Toss the hot pasta together with the butter and a splash of reserved pasta water (you can add less butter if you like, but include at least 100g of it). Stir until the butter emulsifies into a sauce, and then fold through the charred courgettes

7

Divide the pasta between bowls and finish with the fried capers, Parmesan, mint leaves, a pinch of black pepper and a drizzle of olive oil

-   [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), finely grated, to serve
-   [small mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), to serve
