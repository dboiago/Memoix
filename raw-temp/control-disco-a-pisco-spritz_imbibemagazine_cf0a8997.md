At wine-centric Bar Diane in Portland, Oregon, grape-based spirits often grace the menu, making pisco a natural match for cocktails and spritzes. “The Control Disco is like an [Aperol Spritz](https://imbibemagazine.com/aperol-cocktails-2/) meets a [Pisco Sour,](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/recipe-pisco-sour/)” says Sami Gaston, owner and wine director. “It’s the kind of flavor profile that would work for brunch or a late-night dance party—bright and refreshing!”

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. pisco
-   1/2 oz. Aperol
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice
-   2-3 oz. sparkling wine

## Preparation

Add the first 3 ingredients to a glass, then fill with ice and top with sparkling wine. Garnish.
