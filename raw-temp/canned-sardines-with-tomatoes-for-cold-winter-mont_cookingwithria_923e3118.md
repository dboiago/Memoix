Using simple ingredients and a touch of parsley, this recipe is a quick, flavorful solution for cold-weather cravings. Had it been summer, I might have added fresh scallions and thyme leaves from the garden. But for this winter variation, parsley brings a fresh, vibrant note that complements the other flavors beautifully—plus that was all I had in the fridge!

## Ingredients You Will Need

  
• Canned sardines: Provides a rich, savory base for the dish Use sardines in olive oil or water, drained, for best results.  
• Extra virgin olive oil: Adds depth and helps sauté the aromatics.  
• Plum tomatoes: Fresh and juicy, they balance the saltiness of the sardines.  
• Onion: Adds sweetness and texture to the dish.  
• Garlic: Infuses the dish with its robust, aromatic flavor.  
• Parsley: A fresh and herby garnish to brighten the dish.  
• Hot pepper: Brings heat and warmth, perfect for a winter twist.

## Tips for Success

  
• Drain Thoroughly: Ensure the sardines are well-drained to prevent excess liquid in the dish.  
• Cook on High Heat: High heat helps develop the flavors and ensures the liquid evaporates quickly.  
• Adjust Spice: Use hot pepper sparingly and adjust to your preference—perfect for warming up during winter.  
• Parsley Substitutions: If parsley isn’t available, you can use cilantro or even finely chopped celery leaves for a fresh twist.

## Variations to Try

  
• Summer Garden Twist: Add fresh scallions and thyme leaves for a lighter, herbaceous flavor.  
• Curry Version: Sprinkle a pinch of curry powder into the mix for a spiced variation.  
• Vegetable Boost: Toss in diced bell peppers or shredded carrots for added texture and nutrients.

## Serving Suggestions

  
Canned Sardine with Tomatoes pairs wonderfully with [](https://cookingwithria.com/2011/11/trinidad-dhal/)[dhal](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/dhal/) and [hot steamed rice,](https://cookingwithria.com/2023/10/how-to-cook-the-perfect-basmati-rice-a-foolproof-recipe/) boiled provisions (like yams, dasheen, boiled green fig and sweet potatoes), or even [fry](https://cookingwithria.com/2011/05/trini-fried-bake/) [bake](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/bake/) and [soft](https://cookingwithria.com/2024/05/trinidad-roti/) [roti](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/roti/). Serve with a side of fried plantain or sliced avocado ([zaboca](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/zaboca-2/)) to complete the meal.

[![canned sardine with tomatoes](https://cookingwithria.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/canned-sardine-with-tomatoes-150x150.jpg)](https://cookingwithria.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/canned-sardine-with-tomatoes.jpg)

-   3 cans sardine in olive oil or water, drained
-   3 tablespoons [extra virgin olive oil](https://amzn.to/2Dn6fbv)
-   4 [plum tomatoes](https://amzn.to/38gu1DK) chopped
-   1/2 [large onion](https://amzn.to/2DcPy2g) chopped
-   4 cloves [garlic](https://amzn.to/2OiDUcB) minced
-   1/2 cup chopped parsley
-   [Hot pepper](https://amzn.to/2XGqSsl) to taste

-   Open the sardine cans and drain the excess oil or water.
    
-   Heat the olive oil in a medium, heavy-bottomed pot over high heat.
    
-   Add the onion, tomatoes, garlic, and hot pepper. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the excess liquid evaporates and the mixture begins to brown and stick to the pan.
    
-   Add the drained sardines and parsley. Stir-fry on high heat, allowing the liquid to reduce further. Continue cooking until the mixture separates from the oil and starts to stick to the base of the pot.
    
-   Remove from heat, adjust the seasoning as desired, and serve hot.
