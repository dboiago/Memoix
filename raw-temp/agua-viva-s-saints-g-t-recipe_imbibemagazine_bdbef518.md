For Chef José Andrés’ new Los Angeles rooftop restaurant, Agua Viva, cocktail director Miguel Lancha built a G&T pretty enough to rival the views of downtown. The classic drink is warmed up by a bit of ginger and softened by aloe and elderflower liqueurs.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. gin
-   [1/4 oz. ginger syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/ginger-syrup-recipe/)
-   1/2 tsp. St. Germain elderflower liqueur
-   1/2 tsp. Chareau aloe liqueur
-   4 oz. tonic water

## Preparation

Pour all of the ingredients into a glass. Fill the glass with ice, then stir to combine. Garnish.
