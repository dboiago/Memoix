## About this Brazilian Fried Rice with Ham and Egg recipe

Brazilian fried rice, or how we call it in Portuguese, _arroz maluco_, is a very popular rice dish in Brazil. Arroz maluco, which literally means crazy rice, is typically made with ham and egg, plus a few veggies and topped with [potato sticks](https://www.amazon.com/Potato-Sticks-Yoki-Batata-Palha/dp/B008S5F2X4/ref=as_li_ss_tl?crid=3AN0VOXLLKMDQ&dchild=1&keywords=batata+palha&qid=1610765145&sprefix=batata%2Caps%2C505&sr=8-3&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=f5c482890fcb7bfcfc10561ad1f87df9&language=en_US) that we call _[batata palha](https://www.amazon.com/Potato-Sticks-Yoki-Batata-Palha/dp/B008S5F2X4/ref=as_li_ss_tl?dchild=1&keywords=batata+palha+potato+sticks&qid=1610592105&sr=8-1&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=aea0d500654b4ae2dd218d599fc76a56&language=en_US)_.

The cool thing about this Brazilian fried rice with ham and egg recipe is that it’s also **super customizable**. You can **swap out and mix in other ingredients and come up with your own crazy rice recipe**! Just remember, no matter what you add in, don’t forget the [potato sticks](https://www.amazon.com/Potato-Sticks-Yoki-Batata-Palha/dp/B008S5F2X4/ref=as_li_ss_tl?crid=3AN0VOXLLKMDQ&dchild=1&keywords=batata+palha&qid=1610765145&sprefix=batata%2Caps%2C505&sr=8-3&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=f5c482890fcb7bfcfc10561ad1f87df9&language=en_US) or at least some cracked [potato chips](https://amzn.to/3HhGbNU) on top. That crunchy top is THE best part!

Aside from it being super delicious, we love this simple dish because **it’s super easy to make.** Also, it works both as a fun side dish, good enough to bring to a backyard party or as quick mid-week meal.

## How to make Brazilian Fried rice

-   ![](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/fried-rice-5-674x1024.jpg)
    
-   ![](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/fried-rice-8-702x1024.jpg)
    

**Brown the bacon** – First, you’ll want to melt the butter and brown the bacon. I like my bacon to be just brown enough without being too crispy so it doesn’t get dry or burns the butter. Then, add the onions and saute them until they’re transparent.

**Scramble the egg** – Then, you’ll want to add the egg to the pan with the ham and onion, and stir to scramble it.

**Add in the cooked rice** – Then, mix in the rice and stir to combine.

**Add in the peas** – Lastly, mix in the peas. **We want to add the frozen veggies last** in the process to avoid them turning to mush if cooked for too long. Cook just long enough to heat them up, about 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally to promote even cooking and you’re all set!

**Sprinkle the toppings** – The best part!! Top your Brazilian fried rice with the [potato sticks](https://www.amazon.com/Yoki-Potato-Sticks-Batata-Palha/dp/B078NDSG9V/ref=as_li_ss_tl?crid=1G5H7YXP2IHID&keywords=batata+palha&qid=1574285388&sprefix=batata+p,aps,206&sr=8-4&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=e1859899a7cdd2e42eea1d8d61732567&language=en_US) and sprinkle in your favorite fresh herbs.

Prep Time 10 minutes

Cook Time 10 minutes

Total Time 20 minutes

-   4 tbsp of butter
-   3 cups of ham, cubed
-   1 onion, diced
-   3 eggs
-   3 cups cooked rice
-   1 cup frozen peas no need to thaw!
-   [potato sticks](https://www.amazon.com/Yoki-Potato-Sticks-Batata-Palha/dp/B078NDSG9V/ref=as_li_ss_tl?crid=1G5H7YXP2IHID&keywords=batata+palha&qid=1574285388&sprefix=batata+p,aps,206&sr=8-4&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=e1859899a7cdd2e42eea1d8d61732567&language=en_US) to taste
-   1 tbsp fresh parseley, minced optional

-   Add the butter and the ham to a medium pan with over medium heat, and brown the ham a few minutes.
    
-   Add in the onions and saute until they are transparent.
    
-   The, add in the eggs, and stir to scramble them.
    
-   Add in the cooked rice stir, and cook until rice is warmed up – about 2-3 minutes.
