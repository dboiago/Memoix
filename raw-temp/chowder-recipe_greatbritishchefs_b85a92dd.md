Chowder is a creamy stew with a myriad of variations around the world. This elegant haddock chowder recipe from [Marcello Tully](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/marcello-tully) is devastatingly [simple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/simply-brilliant-simple-recipes) and quick to prepare and even uses 'cheat' ingredients like instant mash.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   400g of [smoked haddock](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/smoked-haddock-recipes)
-   460ml of milk
-   36g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely diced
-   330g of [leek](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes), sliced
-   130g of [potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), diced
-   2g of [nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes), ground
-   265ml of double cream
-   1 fish stock cube, dissolved in 500ml of water
-   50g of [instant mash potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)
-   25g of cornflour
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

1

Remove the skin of the fish and with tweezers, prise out the bones

-   400g of [smoked haddock](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/smoked-haddock-recipes)

2

Pour the milk into a heavy-based saucepan. Add the fish, bring to the boil and then turn down the heat and simmer gently for 3 minutes

-   460ml of milk

3

After 3 minutes, remove the fish from the milk and set aside

4

Place a pan over a low-medium heat and add oil. Once hot, add the onions, leeks and potatoes and sweat for 4-5 minutes. Then, add the milk, fish stock, and ground nutmeg and simmer for 20-25 minutes

-   36g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   330g of [leek](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes)
-   130g of [potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)
-   2g of [nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes)
-   1 fish stock cube

5

Whisk in the instant potato and ensure there are no lumps. Dilute the cornflour with water and then add to the pan, mixing well

-   50g of [instant mash potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)
-   25g of cornflour

6

Add the double cream and flake in the haddock

-   265ml of double cream

7

Before serving, season with salt and pepper - be careful not to add too much salt as the fish will add a level of salt

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
