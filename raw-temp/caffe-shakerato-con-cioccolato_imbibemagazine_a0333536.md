Iced coffee and espresso drinks make perfect summertime coolers. This one, created by Clay Gordon of [New World Chocolate Society](http://www.thechocolatelife.com/) and featured in Michael Turback’s scrumptious book, [_Mocha_](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1580088619?ie=UTF8&tag=imbibmagaz-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=390957&creativeASIN=1580088619), is a perfect example.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. espresso, room temperature
-   1 tsp. simple syrup
-   A few drops of vanilla extract
-   1 1/2 oz. chocolate syrup
-   Cracked ice

## Preparation

Fill a shaker halfway with cracked ice. Pour espresso, simple syrup and vanilla over ice. Shake vigorously for 30 seconds and strain into a chilled glass that has been lined with a spiral of chocolate syrup. Garnish. Makes one serving.
