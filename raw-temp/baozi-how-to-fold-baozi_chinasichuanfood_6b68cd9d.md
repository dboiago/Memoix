[Baozi](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/baozi/ "Baozi") is also known as steamed pork buns or steamed filled buns. Elaine is to show you how to fold baozi at home.

Guys, I have made a video showing the process. And recipe in the video can be found  [here](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/chinese-pork-bun/). Tofu version for picture tutorial can be found [here](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/vegan-baozichinese-steamed-buns/) 

Prepare the wrapper and the filling. Place the wrapper in your left hand. Place 1 ~2 tablespoon of the filling in the center of your wrapper (depending on the size of the wrapper).

[![Baozi—how to fold baozi](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/IMG_5825.jpg)](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/IMG_5825.jpg)

Fix the starting point with the thumb of right hand and begin to fold the edge counterclockwise.

[![Baozi—how to fold baozi](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/IMG_5826.jpg)](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/IMG_5826.jpg)

Move the thumb slowly in the process.

[![Baozi—how to fold baozi](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/IMG_5827.jpg)](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/IMG_5827.jpg)

Repeat the steps to seal the baozi completely.

[![Baozi—how to fold baozi](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/IMG_5828.jpg)](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/IMG_5828.jpg)

Then we have the cute baozi folded.
