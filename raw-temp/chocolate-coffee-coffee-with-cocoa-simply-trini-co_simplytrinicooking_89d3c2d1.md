## Coffee with Cocoa

Course Drink

Cuisine International

Prep Time 2 minutes

Cook Time 5 minutes

Total Time 7 minutes

Servings 2

-   1 cup water
-   1 tbsp cocoa grated
-   1 tbsp instant coffee
-   Sugar to taste

-   Boil the cocoa and water for 5 minutes.
    
-   Strain. Add the instant coffee, stir until the coffee dissolves. Add the sugar to taste. Serve hot.
