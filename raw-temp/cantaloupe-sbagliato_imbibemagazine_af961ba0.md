A summery twist on the Sbagliato.

Who says a [Sbagliato](https://imbibemagazine.com/negroni-sbagliato/) can’t be seasonal? At Cecconi’s in Miami Beach, fresh cantaloupe brings a summery flavor to the Negroni riff.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. Aperol
-   1 oz. bianco vermouth
-   1 oz. cantaloupe juice
-   Prosecco, to top

## Preparation

Combine the first 3 ingredients in a shaker with ice and shake to chill. Strain into a wine glass over fresh ice. Stir briefly to incorporate. Top with sparkling wine and garnish.
