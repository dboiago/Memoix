A beer lover’s take on the classic.

For those who love a good beer cocktail, this twist on the [Mai Tai](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/recipe-mai-tai/) is sure to satisfy.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. dark rum blend (Apothecary uses 1/2 oz. Plantation O.F.T.D. Rum and 1/2 oz. Bounty Dark Rum)
-   1/2 oz. curaçao
-   [1/2 oz. orgeat](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/homemade-orgeat-recipe/)
-   3/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   Top with cream ale

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients, except the cream ale, to a shaker, shake, and strain into an ice-filled rocks glass. Top with the cream ale and give it a light stir to combine. Garnish.
