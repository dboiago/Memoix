Scallion pancakes or Cong You Bing (葱油饼) are the quintessential Chinese snack. When we were younger, my sister and I used to come home from school and take a scallion pancake out of the freezer to relieve those after-school hunger pangs. You can find them in restaurants, the frozen foods section of the [Chinese grocery store](https://thewoksoflife.com/how-to/navigating-a-chinese-grocery-store/), or street cart vendors.

All of those places are pretty good, but why not make them at home? It’s so easy. The cong you bing dough is literally just flour and water , add scallions, salt, some elbow grease, a hot pan and Scallion pancakes are served!

There’s really no excuse, people…let’s be real here.

You can serve these Chinese pancakes with whatever dipping sauce you like, but I really like this combination of garlic, soy sauce, and a touch of vinegar.

My dad has since posted a [9 layer scallion pancake](https://thewoksoflife.com/2014/04/9-layer-scallion-pancakes/ "9-Layer Scallion Pancakes") which is similar but with a more fancy name and prep method and of course, my mom has published a traditional [Shou Zhua Bing](https://thewoksoflife.com/2015/03/shou-zhua-bing-chinese-pancakes/ "Shou Zhua Bing (Chinese Pancakes)") Chinese pancake which has a crazy-good flaky texture so be sure to check them both out but try my recipe first!

Okay. Prepare yourselves. Here we go:

## Recipe Instructions

Start by taking out a mixing bowl, and add the flour and hot water.

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-2.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-2.jpg)

Stir with a floured hand until it forms a dough.

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-3.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-3.jpg)

Now it’s time to knead! Drop your dough onto a floured surface (you can also do this right in the bowl, if you like).

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-4.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-4.jpg)

Knead the dough for five minutes until smooth.

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-5.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-5.jpg)

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-6.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-6.jpg)

Place the dough back into the bowl and cover with plastic wrap or a clean towel. Allow it to rest for 30 minutes.

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-7.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-7.jpg)

While the dough is resting, you can chop up your [scallions](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/)…

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe.jpg)

And assemble the dipping sauce. Just put all these sauce ingredients into a small bowl and stir!

-   1 tbsp [soy sauce](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-sauces-vinegars-oils/)
-   1 teaspoon [Chinese black vinegar](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-sauces-vinegars-oils/#label-black-vinegar)
-   1 tbsp water
-   Pinch of sugar
-   1 clove finely minced [garlic](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/#garlic)
-   1 tbsp chopped [scallion](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/)

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-dipping-sauce.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-dipping-sauce.jpg)

After those thirty minutes are up, cut your scallion pancake dough into three equal pieces. Take a [rolling pin](https://thewoksoflife.com/how-to/chinese-cooking-tools/#rolling-pin) and roll each piece into a seven-inch circle. Each time you roll, pick up the dough and turn it a quarter-turn to the left (Or to the right. Did I mention I’m left-handed?). This gives you a nice circle every time, and prevents the dough from sticking to the board. Brush each circle lightly with some oil.

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-8.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-8.jpg)

Sprinkle with salt and scallions. Carefully roll the dough into a cigar shape.

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-9.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-9.jpg)

Then coil that around itself into a spiral shape.

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-10.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-10.jpg)

Then roll that out into a pancake about six inches in diameter (you don’t want it to be too thin), and sprinkle with [sesame seeds](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-spices-condiments/#sesame-seeds), pressing them lightly into the dough. Repeat this process with the other two pieces of dough.

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-11.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-11.jpg)

Heat a nonstick pan or [cast iron skillet](https://thewoksoflife.com/how-to/chinese-cooking-tools/#cast-iron-cookware) with about a tablespoon of oil. Fry each cong you bing pancake, about 3-4 minutes per side, until golden brown.

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-12.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake-recipe-12.jpg)

Cut into wedges and serve with your dipping sauce!

[![Cong You Bing Scallion Pancakes with Dipping Sauce, by thewoksofife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/scallion-pancake.jpg)

## Recipe

Prep: 40 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Total: 1 hour

##### FOR THE SAUCE:

-   ▢ 1 tablespoon [soy sauce](https://thewoksoflife.com/soy-sauce/)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [Chinese black vinegar](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-sauces-vinegars-oils/#label-black-vinegar)
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon water
-   ▢ 1/8 teaspoon sugar
-   ▢ 1 clove [garlic](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/#garlic) (finely minced)
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon [scallion](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/) (chopped)

-   In a mixing bowl, add the flour and hot water and stir until it forms a dough.
    
-   On a floured surface, knead the dough for five minutes until smooth. Place back in the bowl, cover with plastic wrap or a clean towel, and allow it to rest for 30 minutes. While the dough is resting, you can slice your scallions and assemble the dipping sauce.
    
-   After those thirty minutes are up, cut your dough into three equal pieces. Take a rolling pin and roll each piece into a seven-inch circle. Each time you roll, pick up the dough and turn it a quarter-turn to the left (or the right…we’re not picky). This gives you a perfect circle every time, and prevents the dough from sticking to the board.
    
-   Brush each circle with some oil, and sprinkle with salt and scallions. Roll the dough into a cigar shape. Then coil that around itself like a snail. Then roll that out into a pancake about six inches in diameter (you don’t want it to be too thin).
    
-   Sprinkle with sesame seeds and press them lightly into the dough. Repeat this process with the other two pieces of dough.
    
-   Heat a nonstick pan or cast iron skillet with about a tablespoon of oil. Fry each pancake, about 3 minutes per side, until golden brown. Cut into wedges and serve with your dipping sauce.
