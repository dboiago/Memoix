A perfectly bittersweet spritz from Imbibe 75 Place to Watch Good Guy’s.

This spritz recipe created by Sam Ross, co-owner of [Imbibe 75 Place to Watch Good Guy’s](https://imbibemagazine.com/imbibe-75-place-to-watch-good-guys/), was inspired by a summer vacation Ross spent on Italy’s Lake Como. “That place left such an impression on me that I wanted to create a drink that could transport me back to that moment whenever I needed it,” he says. The bitter amaro and sweet passion fruit combine with sparkling wine for a refreshing, full-flavored spritz.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. Cynar
-   3/4 oz. passion fruit syrup
-   1/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   3 oz. Prosecco

## Preparation

Add the Cynar, passion fruit, and lemon to a glass. Add 1 oz Prosecco ((Ross says an exceptionally fizzy Prosecco works best), fill with ice, then top with then remaining Prosecco. Gently stir and garnish.  
**Passion Fruit Syrup**Combine equal parts passion fruit purée (Good Guys uses Boiron) and [simple syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/) in an immersion blender. Refrigerate up to 24 hours.
