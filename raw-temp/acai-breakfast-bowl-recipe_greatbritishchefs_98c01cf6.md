Kickstart your day with Marcello Tully’s hearty acai berry [breakfast](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/breakfast-recipes) bowl recipe. Acai berries have a wonderful tart flavour and are packed with antioxidants. To ensure the breakfast bowl is refreshingly chilled, Marcello freezes the acai berry purée before blending.

First published in 2016

### Imperial

#### Acai breakfast bowl

-   200g of acai berry purée, frozen
-   1 [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [orange juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), (optional, to thin the berry purée)
-   granola

-   Blender

1

Cut the banana in half lengthways and place half in a blender along with the frozen acai berry purée and honey. Blitz until smooth, adding a little orange juice to adjust the consistency as desired

-   200g of acai berry purée
-   1/2 [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [orange juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), (optional, to thin the berry purée)

2

Pour the acai mixture into a bowl and slice the remaining banana on top. Sprinkle with the granola and serve

-   1/2 [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes)
-   granola
