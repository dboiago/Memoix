Tom Aikens' [chicken salad](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-salad-recipes) recipe is in some ways traditional, in other ways contemporary. Coronation [chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-recipes) was first prepared on the eve of the Queen's Coronation in 1952, when the combination of [Indian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes) curry and chicken was seen as slightly more exotic than it is now. Tom Aikens includes a range of ingredients in his version: [apricot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/apricot-recipes) jam, sliced [almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/almond-recipes) and bay leaves all make an appearance. This coronation chicken recipe calls for a pre-cooked roast chicken.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Coronation chicken

-   600g of [roast chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-starter-recipes), shredded dark meat, sliced breast
-   25g of [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely chopped
-   10g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1/2 tbsp of curry powder
-   1/2 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   5g of [tomato paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   80ml of white wine
-   1 bay leaf
-   50ml of chicken stock
-   15g of [apricot jam](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apricot-recipes)
-   70g of [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes)
-   20ml of cream
-   20g of crème fraîche
-   10ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   5g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### Garnish

-   20g of [sliced almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes), toasted
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   4 [baby gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes), finely shredded
-   1 handful of mixed salad leaves

1

In a medium saucepan, melt the butter and sweat the shallots with the salt and pepper for 3 minutes. Add the bay leaves, curry powder and tomato paste and cook for a further 3 minutes

-   25g of [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely chopped
-   10g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   5g of [tomato paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1 bay leaf
-   1/2 tbsp of curry powder

2

Next add the wine and reduce until almost dry, followed by the apricot jam and chicken stock. Reduce over a medium heat until a thick syrup consistency is reached and set aside to cool

-   80ml of white wine
-   50ml of chicken stock
-   15g of [apricot jam](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apricot-recipes)

3

Once the mixture has cooled, whisk in all remaining ingredients, only adding the chicken once the liquid has emulsified. Leave to marinate for up to 2 hours

-   1/2 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   70g of [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes)
-   20ml of cream
-   20g of crème fraîche
-   10ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   5g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   600g of [roast chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-starter-recipes), shredded dark meat, sliced breast

4

To serve, place the shredded lettuce and mixed leaves at the bottom of each bowl. Add the coronation chicken on top and sprinkle with the almonds and spring onions to finish

-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   4 [baby gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes)
-   20g of [sliced almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   1 handful of mixed salad leaves
