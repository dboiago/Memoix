These crispy papaya [fritters](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fritter-recipes "Fritter") are a great way to showcase papaya in a savoury dish. The sweet [chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes "Chilli") sauce is a great recipe to have up your sleeve for an accompaniment to so many dishes, but when served with the fritters brings the perfect balance of sweetness and gentle heat. 

First published in 2025

### Metric

### Imperial

#### For the papaya fritters

-   100g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), sliced
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   170g of papaya, shredded
-   60g of gram flour
-   2 tbsp of cornflour
-   1/2 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 tsp [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tsp [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   10 [sprigs of fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped, plus extra for garnish
-   50ml of water
-   vegetable oil, for frying

#### For the sweet chilli sauce

-   2 tsp vegetable oil
-   10 [red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded and finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), grated
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   75ml of white wine vinegar
-   125g of caster sugar
-   1/2 tsp cornflour
-   1 tbsp of cold water

1

Add the salt to the onions, mix well and leave for 30 minutes

-   100g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), sliced
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Meanwhile, make the sweet chilli sauce. Heat the oil in a pan and then add the chillies. Cook, stirring, for 2 minutes then add the garlic and ginger and cook and stir for another 2 minutes

-   2 tsp vegetable oil
-   10 [red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded and finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), grated
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped

3

Add the white wine vinegar and the sugar, then bring to the boil

-   75ml of white wine vinegar
-   125g of caster sugar

4

Mix the cornflour with the water and then add to the pot while the mixture is boiling. Stir in and then reduce the heat to low

-   1/2 tsp cornflour
-   1 tbsp of cold water

5

Cook for 15–20 minutes or until thick

8

Gradually add the water whilst mixing to create a thick paste-like batter 

-   50ml of water

9

Set the fryer to 160°C, or heat a few inches of oil to 160°C in a heavy-bottomed, high-sided pan

-   vegetable oil, for frying

10

Fry for 5 minutes or until golden, flipping halfway through

11

Serve the freshly cooked fritters with the sweet chilli sauce. Scatter the coriander leaves over the plate
