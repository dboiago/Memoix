### Metric

### Imperial

#### Cod

-   1 [cod fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-fillet-recipes), weighing 600g, with trimmings and bones kept
-   100g of [coarse sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### Aioli

-   1 [baking potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)
-   1/2 [head of garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   350ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### Stock

-   1.5l mineral water
-   1 [fennel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes), sliced into 8 wedges with tops removed for stock
-   500g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and sliced into thick chunks, peel set aside for stock
-   250g of [green beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-bean-recipes), ends trimmed
-   1 [head of broccoli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broccoli-recipes), cut into large florets
-   2 [courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes), sliced into thick chunks
-   4 [celery sticks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), peeled and sliced into thick chunks, peel set aside for stock
-   6 [sage leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sage-recipes)
-   2 sprigs of [fresh thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 bay leaf
-   1 pinch of [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 1/2 [heads of garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 [large onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), halved
-   1 [leek](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes), finely chopped

#### To serve

-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), hard boiled

1

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4

2

For the aioli sauce, bake the potato in the oven until cooked through

-   1 [baking potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)

3

Meanwhile, start on the stock. Fill a large saucepan up with the 1.5 litres of mineral water and place the vegetable trimmings (fennel tops, carrot peel, celery peel) into the pan

-   1.5l mineral water

4

Rinse the fish trimmings and bones under cold water and add to the pan with the herbs, black peppercorns, salt, halved onion, leek and 1 1/2 of the garlic heads, sliced open. Cover the pan and simmer on a very low heat

-   6 [sage leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sage-recipes)
-   2 sprigs of [fresh thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 bay leaf
-   1 pinch of [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 1/2 [heads of garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 [large onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), halved
-   1 [leek](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes), finely chopped

5

After 20 minutes, strain the stock into a clean pan and cook each vegetable one by one in the stock until tender. Set aside the vegetables once cooked, leaving the stock to simmer

-   1 [fennel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes), sliced into 8 wedges with tops removed for stock
-   500g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and sliced into thick chunks, peel set aside for stock
-   250g of [green beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-bean-recipes), ends trimmed
-   1 [head of broccoli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broccoli-recipes), cut into large florets
-   2 [courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes), sliced into thick chunks
-   4 [celery sticks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), peeled and sliced into thick chunks, peel set aside for stock

6

To prepare the cod, slice the cod into four chunks. Sprinkle a tray with coarse salt and lay the fish on top, sprinkle with more salt to cover the fish. Place in the fridge for half an hour

-   1 [cod fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-fillet-recipes), weighing 600g, with trimmings and bones kept
-   100g of [coarse sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

7

Remove the potato from the oven and scoop out the flesh. Place the 1/2 a head of garlic in a pestle and mash with a mortar. Add the flesh of the potato, 2 tablespoons of the stock and the egg yolk. Continue to crush the mix with the mortar until smooth

-   1/2 [head of garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

8

Once smooth, start to incorporate the olive oil incrementally, like you would do for a mayonnaise. Season to taste and set aside

-   350ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

9

Rinse the salt from the cod and poach in the stock on a very gentle simmer. Add all of the vegetables into the broth to warm through after 5 minutes of cooking

10

Arrange the cod and vegetables between plates with hard boiled egg halves, drizzle with the aioli sauce and serve immediately

-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), hard boiled
