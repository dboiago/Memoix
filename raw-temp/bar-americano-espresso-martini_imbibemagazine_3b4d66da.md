Salted toffee notes create a decadent take on a modern classic.

Thanks to PX sherry and salted demerara syrup, Bar Americano’s decadent take on the Espresso Martini “has notes that resemble salted toffee … and a darker than usual roasted flavor from the Borghetti Espresso Liqueur,” says Steve Kämmerer of New York’s Bar Americano. 

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. vodka
-   1 1/2 oz. diluted cold-brew concentrate
-   1/2 oz. coffee liqueur (Bar Americano uses Borghetti Espresso)
-   1/4 oz. PX sherry
-   1/4 oz. salted demerara syrup

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a shaker with ice and shake vigorously for 10 to 15 seconds. Strain into a chilled glass. Garnish.  
**Diluted Cold-Brew Concentrate**Mix cold-brew concentrate (or equivalent) with water at a 60:40 ratio.  
**Salted Demerara Syrup**Add 2 1/2 cups of cold water, 4 cups of demerara sugar, and 2 3/4 Tbsp. of Maldon sea salt to a pot. Stir at medium/high temperature until mixed and reach 140F. Remove from the heat, let cool, store in the fridge. Will keep in a sealed container in the refrigerator for 30 days.
