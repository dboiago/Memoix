A sublimely seasonal coffee drink.

An autumnal citrus and spice syrup meets fresh apple cider in this sublimely seasonal cold-brew coffee drink from [Horizon Line](https://imbibemagazine.com/horizon-line-coffee-des-moines/) in Des Moines, Iowa.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. spiced citrus syrup
-   2½ oz. apple cider
-   8½ oz. cold-brew coffee

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients with ice and strain into a chilled glass with no ice. Topped with an orange peel.

Spiced Citrus Syrup:  
1½ cups orange juice  
2½ cups water  
2 cups brown sugar  
3 grams whole clove  
3 cinnamon sticks  
2 grams nutmeg  
5 grams cinnamon  
Half of an orange, juiced & sliced

Boil ingredients together for 10 minutes and strain. Store syrup in the refrigerator when not in use.
