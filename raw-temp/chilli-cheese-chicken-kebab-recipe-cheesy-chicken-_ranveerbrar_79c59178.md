## मुर्ग़ मलाई कबाब | Creamy Chili Cheese Kebab | मलाईदार Chicken Kabab | Chef Ranveer Brar

Chilli Cheese Chicken Kebab – juicy chicken keema, melted cheese और spicy garlic sauce से बना cheesy, restaurant-style kebab.

Prep Time: 15-20 minutes Cook Time: 20-25 minutes

Total Time 30-35 minutes

### Ingredients

### For Kebab Mixture

-   2-3 no. Green Chillies (less spicy & chopped), हरी मिर्च
-   1 large Potato (boiled & mashed), उबला हुआ आलू
-   2-3 tbsp fresh Coriander leaves (chopped), धनिया पत्ता
-   1 ½ inch Ginger (peeled & grated), अदरक
-   Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार
-   600 gms Chicken mince, चिकन का कीमा
-   1 tsp Garam masala powder, गरम मसाला पाउडर
-   2 tbsp Cheese blend, चीज़

### Other Ingredients

-   Oil for greasing, तेल
-   ¼ cup Cheese blend, चीज़

### For Chilli Cheese Kebab

-   1-2 tbsp Oil, तेल
-   Prepared Kebabs, तैयार किए हुए कबाब
-   2-3 no. Garlic cloves (chopped), लहसुन
-   2 tsp Chilli flakes, चिली फ्लेक्स
-   ½ cup Processed Cheese (grated), चीज़
-   2-3 heaped tbsp Fresh cream, ताज़ा मलाई
-   2-3 tbsp Processed Cheese (grated), चीज़
-   1 tsp Chilli flakes, चिली फ्लेक्स
-   1 no. Green Chilli (less spicy & chopped), हरी मिर्च
-   1-2 tsp fresh Coriander leaves (chopped), धनिया पत्ता

### For Garnish

-   Coriander sprig, धनिया पत्ता

### Process

### For Kebab Mixture

-   In a bowl, add green chillies, boiled potato, fresh coriander leaves, ginger, chicken mince, garam masala powder, cheese blend and give it a good mix.
-   Divide the mixture into the medium size lemon balls and stuff with the cheese blend.
-   Apply on the tray and transfer the kebabs into the tray.
-   Keep it aside for further use.

### For Chilli Cheese Kebab

-   In a pan, add oil, once it’s hot, add prepared kebabs and cook on both sides for a while or until it’s cooked.
-   Add garlic, chilli flakes, grated cheese, fresh cream and cook for 2-3 minutes.
-   Add grated cheese, chilli flakes, green chilli and give it a quick boil.
-   Finish it with fresh coriander leaves and transfer it into the serving platter or dish.
-   Garnish it with coriander sprig.
-   Serve hot with rumali roti.
