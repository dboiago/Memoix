**Recipe:** _Cory Miller, [Sweet Afton](https://www.sweetaftonbar.com/), Astoria, New York_

![Coffee Tonic](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/high-road-sweet-afton-crdt-noah-fecks.jpg)

Taking notes from the classic espresso and tonic, this fancy reinvention adds a little bump of spice from fresh ginger.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. cold-brew coffee
-   ½ oz. fresh grapefruit juice
-   ½ oz. tonic syrup (Sweet Afton uses Haber's)
-   ¼ oz. fresh ginger juice
-   Splash soda water

## Preparation

Combine the cold-brew coffee, grapefruit juice, tonic syrup and ginger juice in a shaker. Shake until well chilled. Strain into a glass over a single large ice cube. Add a splash of soda water.
