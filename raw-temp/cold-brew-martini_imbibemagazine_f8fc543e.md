“[Espresso Martinis](https://imbibemagazine.com/vodka-espresso-recipe/) have been around forever, but today we’re seeing bartenders refresh the cocktail with new and unique ingredients and pairings,” says bar director Alejandro Dieguez of the inspiration behind this Cold-Brew Martini, which is served on nitro at J. Bespoke. “The amaro lends the drink notes of vanilla and orange peel, while the honey balances out the cold brew with a touch of sweetness.”

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. vodka
-   ½ oz. Amaro Montenegro
-   ½ oz. honey
-   1 oz. cold-brew concentrate

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a shaker with ice and shake to chill. Strain into a chilled coupe and garnish.
