**RECIPE:** _David Kinsey for [Sycamore Den](https://www.sycamoreden.com/), San Diego_

![Beer Cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/prince-charles-dont-surf-crdt-lara-ferroni.jpg)

A riff on the Army & Navy cocktail, this San Diego sipper adds gin and citrus to an orgeat-rich highball topped with beer. The drink’s unexpected elements find a harmonious balance “that creates a refreshingly daytime, sun-is-out drinking experience,” says San Diego bartender David Kinsey.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. gin
-   1/2 oz. orgeat
-   1/2 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1 dash Angostura bitters
-   4 oz. blonde ale

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients except beer, and shake with ice. Strain into an ice-filled glass, top with beer and garnish.
