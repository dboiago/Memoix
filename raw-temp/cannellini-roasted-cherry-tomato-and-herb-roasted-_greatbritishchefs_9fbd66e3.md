This [vegan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes "Vegan") piadina recipe uses [Crosta & Mollica's](https://www.crostamollica.com/) Organic Wholeblend Piadina, which is topped with a creamy cannellini bean and [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes "Garlic") spread, roasted cherry tomatoes, and [olives](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/olive-recipes "Olive") that have been roasted with lemon and herbs. 

First published in 2022

Crosta & Mollica Organic Wholeblend Piadina are available from Ocado, Waitrose & Partners and Sainsbury’s.
