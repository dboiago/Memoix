This stunning braised whole [beef shin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-shin-recipes) recipe is a warming delight. This cut is normally served with very heavy, rich flavours, but this recipe sees the beef [braised](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/braising-slow-cooking-recipes) in [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/orange-recipes) and red wine and served with an orange and olive gremolata, which gives the gorgeous slow-cooked beef a wonderfully aromatic flavour. Serve with some striking [Camargue black rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/camargue-black-rice-recipe) on the side, or serve the beef as the centrepiece of a [Sunday roast](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sunday-roasts-recipes).

First published in 2017

-   Microplane

1

Preheat the oven to 160°C/gas mark 3

2

To begin, season the beef shin with salt and pepper. Heat some olive oil in a cast iron casserole dish and once hot, sear the beef until brown all over. Remove from the pan and set aside

-   2kg [beef shin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes)

4

Place the beef on top of the vegetables and cover with the wine, stock and orange juice. Bring to the boil, cover with a lid and place in the oven for 2.5 hours

-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), juiced
-   1 [bottle of red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   500ml of beef stock

5

To make the gremolata, pick the parsley leaves from stalks, finely chop and place in a mixing bowl. Using a microplane or fine grater, grate in the orange zest and garlic cloves. Finely chop the black olives, add to the bowl and mix well. Season with a little salt and pepper

-   1 handful of [black olives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/black-olive-recipes), pitted (Niçoise or Kalamata)
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1/2 bunch of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)

6

To serve, carve the shin and divide between plates. Spoon over some cooking juices and sprinkle with the gremolata to finish
