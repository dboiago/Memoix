**RECIPE:** _Taylor Hall for Tandem Bar, Newtown, Australia  
(Note: This bar has permanently closed.)_

![Dessert Drink](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/clockwork-orange-tandem-bar-vertical-crdt-stephen-woodburn.jpg)

Aquavit anchors coffee, orange, Aperol and bitters in this lovely nightcap.

When imagining a dessert cocktail that would mimic the flavors of one of his favorite sweet treats, [jaffa cake,](https://www.foodandwine.com/news/best-way-eat-jaffa-cake-according-mcvities) bartender Taylor Hall combined spice-forward aquavit with chocolate and orange bitters, Aperol and coffee.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. aquavit
-   1 oz. French press coffee (chilled)
-   1 oz. Aperol
-   [½ oz. simple syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   2 dashes chocolate bitters
-   6 dashes orange bitters

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients together with ice, then fine strain into a rocks glass over a single large ice cube. Carefully flame the orange peel over the top of the drink and garnish.
