Paul Ainsworth's chilli [chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-recipes) pasta recipe can be rustled up in no time and provides a beautiful dish perfect for a [lunch](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lunch-recipes) or midweek supper. Serve the [pasta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pasta-recipes) with some crusty garlic bread and a light summer [salad](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salad-recipes).

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   4 [free range chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes), skinned and cut into 2cm strips
-   400g of [strozzapreti pasta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pasta-recipes), or any fresh pasta of your choice
-   1 large red onion, finely sliced
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 tbsp of [garlic purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   80ml of vegetable stock
-   4 tbsp of [sweet chilli sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [red pesto](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pesto-recipes)
-   6 tbsp of crème fraîche
-   1 bunch of [basil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes), ripped
-   2 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), finely diced
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground
-   2 dashes of olive oil

1

Place a pot of salted water over a medium-high heat and bring to the boil

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Place a large pan over a medium heat and add a dash of olive oil. Add the chicken strips and fry until nicely browned on one side. Season the chicken with salt and pepper as it cooks

-   1 dash of olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   4 [free range chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes)

3

Turn over the chicken and add the onions. Fry until the onions and chicken pieces are golden brown all over

-   1 large red onion
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)

4

Add the garlic and vegetable stock to the pan, using a wooden spoon to scrape the base of the pan and incorporate the contents into the sauce. Bring up to a gentle simmer and cook until the stock reduces to the consistency of a sauce

-   1 tbsp of [garlic purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   80ml of vegetable stock

5

Now, drop the pasta into a pan of boiling salted water and cook for about 8 minutes, leaving a nice bite to the pasta

-   400g of [strozzapreti pasta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pasta-recipes)
-   1 dash of olive oil

6

Add the red pesto, sweet chilli and crème fraiche to the sauce and leave to gently simmer

-   4 tbsp of [sweet chilli sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   6 tbsp of crème fraîche
-   2 tbsp of [red pesto](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pesto-recipes)

7

Strain the pasta, add to the sauce and mix well. Season with salt and pepper to taste and add a dash of lemon juice. Divide into 4 bowls, garnish with some chopped basil and diced tomato and serve

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), season
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), season
-   1 bunch of [basil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes)
-   2 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
