![Hidden World](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/hidden-world-crdt-dylan-and-jeni-crop.jpg)

Bright and tropical.

The Hidden World employs the complementary qualities of mezcal and aquavit to ground flavors of mango, ginger, and vanilla.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. mezcal (D&C uses Del Maguey Santo Domingo Albarradas)
-   1 oz. aquavit (D&C uses Krogstad)
-   ¾ oz. fresh lime juice
-   ½ oz. vanilla syrup
-   2 tsp. mango puree
-   1 tsp. ginger syrup
-   1 dash saline solution (1:5)

## Preparation

Add all ingredients to an ice-filled shaker, then shake until combined. Strain the cocktail over one large ice cube in a double Old Fashioned glass. Garnish.
