### Cattle Drive Steaks

## Recipe Notes

-   **Yield:** Serves 4 to 6

## Ingredients

-   2 tablespoons cold brewed coffee
-   2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
-   4 T-bone or porterhouse steaks, each at least 1 inch thick
-   4 tablespoons [Project Smoke Santa Fe Rub](http://barbecuebible.com/product/steven-raichlen-project-smoke-bbq-spice-rub-seasoning/)

## Recipe Steps

**Step 1:** Combine the coffee and oil in a bowl. Brush each steak on both sides with this mixture and season generously with the rub.

**Step 2:** Set up the grill for direct grilling and brush and oil the grill grate.

**Step 3:** Grill the steaks over high heat, turning once, until nicely seared and medium-rare, about 4 minutes per side. Let rest for 3 minutes before serving.
