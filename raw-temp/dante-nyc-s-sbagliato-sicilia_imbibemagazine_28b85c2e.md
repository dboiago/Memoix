**RECIPE:** _Chris Moore for [Dante NYC](https://www.dante-nyc.com/), New York City_

![Dante NYC Sbagliato Sicilia](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Sicilian-Sbagliatocrop-crdt-Giada-Paoloni.jpg)

For a warm-weather sparkler, Dante NYC created this Sbagliato recipe featuring lemons, which Sicily is known for. Light and fragrant, the cocktail sings a citrusy character and a slightly bitter finish. Dante makes a proprietary limoncello, but any [limoncello](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/homemade-limoncello/) will work.

## Ingredients

-   3/4 oz. Luxardo Bitter Bianco
-   3/4 oz. Dolin Blanc
-   [1/2 oz. limoncello](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/homemade-limoncello/)
-   2 dashes lemon bitters
-   2 1/2 oz. prosecco
-   1/2 oz. sparkling water

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to the glass over ice. Garnish.
