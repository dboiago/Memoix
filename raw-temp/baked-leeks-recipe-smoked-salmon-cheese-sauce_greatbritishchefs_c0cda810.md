Leeks are fantastic when paired with [smoked salmon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/smoked-salmon-recipes) - their nuanced flavour providing a great contrast to the smoked fish. These cheesy leeks by [Nathan Outlaw](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/nathan-outlaw) are a great pescatarian main course, particularly for someone who loves [cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cheese-recipes). For a simple mid-week dinner or a weekend lunch, serve this cheese leek recipe with a mixed leaf salad.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   8 [leeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes), cleaned and cut into 8cm pieces
-   300g of [smoked salmon slices](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/smoked-salmon-recipes)
-   175g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), plus extra for greasing
-   50g of plain flour
-   450ml of milk
-   2 [free-range egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tsp [English mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   2 tsp [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes) to season

1

Preheat the oven to 200°C/gas mark 6

2

Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil, then add the leeks and cook for 5 minutes

-   8 [leeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes)

3

When the leeks are cooked, drain and place into a buttered ovenproof dish. Pile the salmon over the leeks

-   300g of [smoked salmon slices](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/smoked-salmon-recipes)

4

Melt the butter in a small pan and add the flour. Cook, stirring for a few minutes until lightly golden

-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   50g of plain flour

5

Remove from the heat and gradually whisk in the milk to form a smooth sauce. Return to the heat, stir and simmer for 2-3 minutes

-   450ml of milk

7

Place in the oven and cook for 10 minutes, until golden brown. Serve immediately
