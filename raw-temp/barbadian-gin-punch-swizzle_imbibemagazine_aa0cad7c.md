Malty Dutch gin meets cooling coconut water in this summer swizzle.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. genever
-   2 oz. coconut water
-   ½ oz. fresh lime juice
-   ½ oz. rich Demerara syrup (2:1)
-   2 dashes Angostura bitters  (optional)

## Preparation

In a tall glass filled with crushed ice, combine all ingredients and swizzle until the glass is frosted.
