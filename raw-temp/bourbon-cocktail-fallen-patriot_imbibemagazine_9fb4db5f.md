Keep the Bourbon Heritage Month festivities going with this lovely Lion’s Tail riff.

Loosely inspired by the classic [Lion’s Tail](https://imbibemagazine.com/lions-tail-recipe/), this bourbon cocktail from bartender Nicholas Flower strikes a beautiful balance between baking spice flavor, vanilla, and fresh citrus.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. bourbon (Flower uses Elijah Craig)
-   3/4 oz. Licor 43
-   3/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1/4 oz. allspice dram
-   10 mint leaves

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a shaker with ice and shake until chilled. Strain into a chilled coupe and garnish.
