Enjoy the delicious, comforting flavours of [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cauliflower-recipes) cheese in soup form. Gently simmered in milk for an extra creamy texture, our cauliflower soup is flavoured with [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes) and [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/parmesan-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/parmesan-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/parmesan-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/parmesan-recipes) but feel free to add your favourite mixture of cheeses. Top with luxuriously buttery Parmesan-coated croutons to add a bit of crunch. Omit the Worcestershire sauce and choose a vegetarian Parmesan substitute if you wish to make the soup [vegetarian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes).

### Imperial

-   vegetable oil, for frying
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), diced
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), sliced
-   2 [sprigs of thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), plus a few extra leaves to garnish
-   1 [small cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes)
-   500ml of milk
-   200ml of water, or vegetable stock
-   150g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   20g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated (or a vegetarian alternative)
-   1 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 dash of white wine vinegar
-   1 dash of Worcestershire sauce, (optional)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### Parmesan croutons

-   2 slices of [bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), diced
-   70g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   2 [sprigs of thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), bashed
-   10g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), finely grated (or a vegetarian alternative)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 dash of olive oil, for drizzling

-   Blender

1

Heat a drizzle of vegetable oil in a large pan and sweat the onion and garlic with a pinch of salt until soft without colour

2

Meanwhile, cut the cauliflower into small florets and finely slice the stalk. Add to the pan with the thyme and cover with the milk and water or vegetable stock. Leave to simmer for 20 minutes or until the cauliflower is very soft

3

While the soup simmers, make the croutons. Heat the butter in a frying pan until melted and starting to foam. Add the thyme, garlic and bread cubes and fry in the butter until golden and crisp. Once crisp, grate in some Parmesan and toss so the cheese clings to the croutons. Keep warm

4

Add the cheeses and mustard to the soup, then use a hand blender or transfer to a blender jug to blitz to a smooth soup. Taste and add vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper until you're happy with the flavour

5

Serve the soup in bowls and garnish with the croutons, a drizzle of olive oil, a few thyme leaves and a crack of black pepper
