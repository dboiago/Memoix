[Andy Waters](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/andy-waters)' simple chicken and vegetable bake recipe makes a perfect half term dinner. The mix of vegetables, sweet maple syrup and tender [chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-breast-recipes) will go down a treat with the little ones. Poaching chicken is a lot healthier than frying, which makes this recipe even more [child-friendly](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kids-recipes).

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   4 [chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes), cut in half
-   250ml of chicken stock
-   6 [broccoli florets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broccoli-recipes)
-   6 [cauliflower florets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes)
-   1 [sweet potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled and diced
-   1/2 [red pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), diced
-   1 tbsp of [maple syrup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/maple-syrup-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

1

Preheat the oven to 200°C/gas mark 6

2

Remove the skin from the chicken breasts and place in a roasting dish. Pour over the chicken stock and roast in the oven for 30 minutes

-   4 [chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes), cut in half
-   250ml of chicken stock

4

Remove the chicken from the oven and sprinkle over the par-cooked broccoli and cauliflower, sweet potato and red pepper

-   1 [sweet potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled and diced
-   1/2 [red pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), diced

5

Drizzle the maple syrup over the vegetables and return to the oven. Roast for 20-30 minutes until the vegetables are nice and golden. Remove from the oven and serve with seasonal greens or as preferred

-   1 tbsp of [maple syrup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/maple-syrup-recipes)
