Rudolph would surely enjoy this hearty breakfast after a long night of delivering presents! This great recipe from William Drabble is perfect for Christmas and will give everyone the energy they need. Let the [kids](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kids-recipes) choose their own favourite wintry fruit.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   200g of [rolled porridge oats](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oat-recipes)
-   400ml of milk
-   40g of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   160g of half fat crème fraîche, plus extra for garnish
-   100g of [grapes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grape-recipes)
-   1 [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes), chopped
-   1 [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), roughly chopped
-   100g of [dried cranberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)
-   4 [glacé cherries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-recipes)

1

Place the porridge oats into a bowl and mix in the milk, honey and crème fraîche. Leave in the fridge to soak overnight (if it goes too thick, add a little more milk in the morning)

-   200g of [rolled porridge oats](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oat-recipes)
-   400ml of milk
-   160g of half fat crème fraîche
-   40g of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)

2

The next day, add some cut grapes, banana, dried cranberries, apple and a small dollop of crème fraîche

-   1 [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes)
-   1 [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), roughly chopped
-   100g of [grapes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grape-recipes)
-   100g of [dried cranberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)

3

Divide the porridge into bowls and top with small dollops of crème fraîche. Place a cherry on top of each dollop to make Rudolph's nose, serve with a little extra milk if necessary

-   4 [glacé cherries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-recipes)
