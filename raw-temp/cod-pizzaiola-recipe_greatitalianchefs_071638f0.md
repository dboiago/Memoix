Angelo Sabatelli serves this [cod](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/cod-recipes) pizzaiola recipe with some gourmet twists, such as dehydrated black olive crumb and [potato](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes) cream made with a siphon. The classic flavours shine through, though, and this dish could be easily adapted to suit home kitchens by serving the potato cream un-whipped and simply dicing some black olive without dehydrating.

First published in 2016

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Cod

-   280g of [cod loin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-loin-recipes), cut into 4 portions
-   30g of [egg white](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50g of cornflour
-   peanut oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### Potato cream

-   500g of [potatoes](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled
-   120g of cream
-   6g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   50g of olive oil

-   Dehydrator
-   Blender
-   Siphon bottle or cream whipper
-   N2O cartridge 3

1

To prepare the black olives, spread out onto a dehydrator tray and dry overnight at 60°C

-   100g of [black olives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/black-olive-recipes)

2

The next morning, finely chop with a knife and store in an airtight container

3

To make the pizzaiola sauce, sweat the garlic in some olive oil then add the capers. Stir in the passata and oregano and cook for 30 minutes

-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), sliced
-   olive oil
-   1 tbsp of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes)
-   300g of [passata](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1 sprig of oregano

4

After this time, blitz in a blender until smooth then reserve until needed

5

For the potato cream, boil the potatoes in salted boiling water until soft, then blend with the cream, 100g of the cooking water, the salt and olive oil. Transfer to a siphon, charge with 3 gas canisters and keep warm

-   500g of [potatoes](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled
-   120g of cream
-   6g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   50g of olive oil

6

Preheat a deep-fryer or deep pan of peanut oil to 180°C

-   peanut oil

7

To cook the cod, roll each piece in egg white, followed by the cornflour. Deep-fry the cod pieces until cooked through and crispy then drain on kitchen paper and season with salt

-   50g of cornflour
-   280g of [cod loin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-loin-recipes), cut into 4 portions
-   30g of [egg white](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

8

To serve, roll the cod in the pizzaiola sauce until very well-coated and place in serving dishes. Squirt a shot of the potato cream over the top, sprinkle over the black olive crumb and serve
