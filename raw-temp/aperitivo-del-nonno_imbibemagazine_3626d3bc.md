A bright and bitter aperitivo that transcends the seasons.

By blending the dark bitter notes of Amaro Lucano with the bright, floral character of Aperol and sparkling rosé, Nicola Scarnera has created an aperitivo that transcends the seasons.

## Ingredients

-   1/3 oz. Amaro Lucano Anniversario
-   1 oz. Aperol
-   1/3 oz. sparkling rosé
-   2 dashes orange bitters
-   Tonic water, to top

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a glass and add ice. Stir to combine. Garnish.
