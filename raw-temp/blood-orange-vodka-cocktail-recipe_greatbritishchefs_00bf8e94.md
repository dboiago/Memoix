Created specially for [Valentine's Day](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/valentines-day-romantic-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/valentines-day-romantic-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/valentines-day-romantic-recipes), Mark Hix's blood orange[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blood-orange-recipes) [vodka cocktail](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vodka-cocktail-recipes) is the perfect drink to toast any romantic evening. The vodka base is softened with sweet peach liqueur, sour lemon and zesty [blood orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blood-orange-recipes) for an easy cocktail to share.

First published in 2016

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Place all the cocktail ingredients except the cherries in a cocktail shaker. Shake well for 30 seconds until combined and lightly frothy

-   100ml of [vodka](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vodka-cocktail-recipes), preferable Black Cow Vodka
-   30ml of blood orange syrup
-   50ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   20ml of peach liqueur
-   2 [egg whites](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

2

Divide between 2 coupes or cocktail glasses to serve, garnishing each drink with a cherry

-   [cherries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-recipes), to garnish
