While shop-bought [sauces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sauce-recipes) are often loaded with sugar, Lisa Allen's homemade brown sauce recipe proves the old adage 'fresh is best'. Don't be put off by the amount of ingredients – they simply need mixing together and simmering to achieve a beautiful sauce with perfectly balanced layers of flavour. Serve alongside sausages, eggs and bacon for a fabulous [breakfast](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/breakfast-recipes) treat.

First published in 2016

#### Brown sauce

-   500g of [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   112g of [cooking apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), peeled, cored and finely chopped
-   700ml of water
-   225g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   15g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   100ml of red wine vinegar
-   50g of [dried apricots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apricot-recipes)
-   1 bay leaf
-   1/2 [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   2 [cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)
-   1/2 [chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   2 pinches of [white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   5 [juniper berries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/juniper-berry-recipes)
-   12 [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [allspice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/allspice-recipes)
-   4 tsp Worcestershire sauce
-   4 tsp [black treacle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/treacle-recipes)
-   oil

-   Blender

1

Begin by preparing the tomatoes. Using the tip of a sharp knife, score a cross in the base of each tomato. Blanch carefully in boiling water, immediately plunging into ice water to cool (this process should loosen the skins of the tomatoes). Peel and deseed the tomatoes, then chop the flesh into chunks and set to one side

-   500g of [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)

3

Stir well to combine, then leave to reduce gently over a light simmer. Add the vinegar, continuing to reduce until the mixture thickens to form a sauce-like consistency

-   100ml of red wine vinegar

4

Fish out and discard the cloves, star anise and bay leaf, then pour the sauce into a blender and blitz until smooth, adjusting the seasoning to taste. Pass through a fine sieve into a clean jar and set aside to cool. Serve immediately once cool, or store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to a week
