### Cherry Barbecue Sauce

## Ingredients

-   1 cup cherry cola
-   1 cup ketchup
-   1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
-   1/4 cup A-1 Steak Sauce
-   1 teaspoon liquid smoke
-   1/2 teaspoon onion powder
-   1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
-   1/2 teaspoon black pepper

## Recipe Steps

**Step 1:** Combine the ingredients in a heavy saucepan and gradually bring to a boil over medium heat. Reduce the heat slightly to obtain a gentle simmer. Simmer the sauce until reduced by a quarter—6 to 8 minutes.

**Step 2:** Transfer the sauce to clean jars and let cool to room temperature, then refrigerate until serving. The sauce will keep for several months.

## Recipe Tips

How to use: Pretty much as you would any sauce, noting the strong affinity this sauce has for chicken, ribs, and pork.
