This simple recipe from [Alessandro Gavagna](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/chefs/alessandro-gavagna) makes an elegant summer dish when [courgettes](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes) are in abundance. The delicate flavour of the flowers and sweet red peppers is paired alongside tangy apple, punchy horseradish root and grated Montasio, a Venetian cheese with a wonderfully creamy flavour. Montasio is made from animal rennet, so to make this dish truly vegetarian seek out an alternative cheese with a similar mild flavour.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Gently rinse the courgette flowers then leave to drain upside down on some kitchen paper

-   36 courgette flowers

2

Melt half of the butter in a hot pan, then add the pepper strips and roast until crisp. In a separate pan, sauté the courgette flowers in the remaining butter on a high heat, until they just start to wilt. Season with salt to taste

-   1 [red pepper](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), washed and cut into thin strips
-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

4

Top with the pepper strips, then the wilted courgette flowers, and a final grating of fresh horseradish

-   1 [horseradish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/horseradish-recipes), root

[](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/chefs/alessandro-gavagna)

With a love for the region of Friuli and its ingredients, Alessandro Gavagna's unique food is a perfect marriage of Italian and Slovenian cuisine.
