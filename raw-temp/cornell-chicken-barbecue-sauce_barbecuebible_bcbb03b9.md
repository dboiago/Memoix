### Cornell Chicken Barbecue Sauce

## Recipe Notes

-   **Yield:** Makes 1 1/2 cups

## Ingredients

-   1 cup mayonnaise, preferably Hellmann’s
-   ½ cup cider vinegar
-   1 tablespoon poultry seasoning
-   1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
-   Salt to taste

## Recipe Steps

**1:** Combine the ingredients in a bowl and whisk to mix, adding salt and pepper to taste.  Cover and refrigerate for up to a week if not using immediately.
