This seasonal fizz is made with a luscious syrup infused with [apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes "Apple") and rosemary, and a measure of Discarded grape skin [vodka](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vodka-cocktail-recipes "Vodka cocktail"), which brings a unique, fruit-forward flavour to the drink. The rest of the apples from this recipe can be used to make Chantelle’s incredibly delicious [Baked Apple and Miso Shortcake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/baked-apple-miso-shortcake-recipe "Baked apple and miso shortcake").

#### Fizz

-   200ml of [vodka](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vodka-cocktail-recipes)
-   300ml of soda water
-   100ml of apple and rosemary syrup
-   ice cubes

1

For the syrup, place all ingredients into a small saucepan with 150ml water. Bring to the boil and cook for 5 minutes, then simmer rapidly for a further 5 minutes. Set aside off the heat for a further 5 minutes

-   4 [apple cores](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), and peel from 2 apples
-   50g of caster sugar
-   1 [sprig of rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes)

2

Strain the syrup through a fine sieve or muslin and chill

3

To make the fizz, add ice cubes to four wine glasses then top each with 50ml of vodka, 25ml apple and rosemary syrup and 75ml of soda
