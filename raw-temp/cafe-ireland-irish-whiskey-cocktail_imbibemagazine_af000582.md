A simple but robust whiskey sipper.

Likening this cocktail to a dry Irish whiskey Manhattan, Yanni Kehagiaras notes that the drink  
should be stirred very briefly with ice. “It’s about retaining the texture and viscosity,” says Kehagiaras, co-owner of Stoa in San Francisco, one of this year’s Imbibe 75 Places to Watch. “Just a brief chill and it’s good.”

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. Irish whiskey (Stoa uses Powers)
-   1/2 oz. dry vermouth (such as Dolin)
-   1/2 oz. coffee liqueur (Stoa uses St. George Spirits NOLA Coffee Liqueur)

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients with ice and stir very briefly, only for 5 seconds or so. Strain into the glass and serve.
