**Cooks in:** _1h 40mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   21 OZ. of organic clementines
-   2 C. of Italian 00 flour
-   1 C. of granulated sugar
-   2 STICKS of butter
-   10 TBSP. of almond flour
-   8 G of pectin
-   1 of egg
-   1 of egg yolk
-   Salt
-   Powdered sugar

### Method

1

Beat the 00-style flour with the almond flour, 1/2 cup sugar and a pinch of salt; mix in the butter until it forms a sandy mixture, then add in the egg and the yolk, mixing gently and not too long to get the shortbread; divide it into 2 balls and let them rest, covered, in the refrigerator, for 1 hour. Flour a sheet of parchment paper and, using a rolling pin, roll out one of the balls to 1 inch thick. Cut out 8 round cookies (3 inches across). Roll out the second ball thinner, about 2/3 inch thick. Cut it with a crostata-shaped pasta cutter (3 inches in diameter) to get 8 perforated disks that are 8 inches in diameter. Or you can cut them into donut shapes, also 3 inches in diameter. Set all the cookies on a baking tray lined with parchment paper and bake at 350°F for 15-17 minutes. Remove the perforated cookies and bake the others for another 2-3 minutes.

2

For the clementine compote: While the pastry is resting, wash the clementines and peel half of them. Put the clementines with the peels in a saucepan of cold water; bring to a boil and pour off the water, then repeat the operation a second time. Chop the peeled clementines into pieces and also chop the boiled ones; cook them all together with 3/4 cup sugar and the pectin for 30 minutes, stirring often; let it cool and puree it. Assemble the cookies by spreading a tablespoon of the clementine compote on each solid cookie; cover them with the perforated cookies, pressing down gently. Dust them with a little powdered sugar before serving.
