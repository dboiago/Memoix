The combination of [pumpkin](https://greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pumpkin-recipes) and sage works brilliantly in [Paul Heathcote](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/paul-heathcote)'s warming soup. Make this pumpkin soup recipe for a starter at a dinner party or a [quick lunch](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quick-lunch-recipes), paired with a slice of crusty bread. Vegetarians can replace the chicken stock in this recipe with vegetable stock.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   500g of [pumpkin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pumpkin-recipes)
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   500ml of chicken stock
-   1 tsp sugar
-   1 tbsp of cornflour
-   1 1/2 tbsp of water
-   150ml of cream
-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes) to season
-   1 dash of olive oil
-   6 [sage leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sage-recipes)

-   Liquidiser

1

Cut the pumpkin into large pieces, place in a hot pan with a little olive oil for about 3 minutes until lightly coloured

2

Add the chicken stock, onion and sugar and simmer until the pumpkin and onion are cooked for about 15 minutes

3

Add the cream and bring to the boil, mix the cornflour and water together and stir in.

4

Allow to cool a little before placing in a liquidiser, with the butter for about 1 minute.

5

Return to the pan and add the sage, bring to the boil and season with freshly milled pepper and salt as necessary and finish with a drizzle of olive oil and a few sage leaves
