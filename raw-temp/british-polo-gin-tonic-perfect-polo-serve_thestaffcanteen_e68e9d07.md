## British Polo Gin & Tonic - Perfect Polo Serve

Feeling funky? Deploy alongside premium tonic with a strawberry & basil garnish for an outrageously smooth G&T made from Great Taste award-winning British Polo Gin

## Ingredients

-   \- 1 Part Great Taste award-winning British Polo Gin
-   \- 3 Parts Premium tonic
-   \- 4 Halves of sliced strawberries
-   \- 1 head of leafy basil

## Method

Combine ingredients and serve over ice.
