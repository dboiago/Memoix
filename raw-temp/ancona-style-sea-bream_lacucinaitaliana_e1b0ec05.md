## It's not surprising that seafood plays a large part in dishes from Ancona, the main coastal city in the Marche region overlooking the Adriatic Sea. Super fresh marinated and grilled sea bream is simply delicious.

**Cooks in:** _2h 40mins \+ 2h_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   2 of sea bream
-   1 SLICE of white bread
-   8 of salt-packed anchovies
-   4 of large eggs
-   3 of shallots
-   2 SPRIG of thyme
-   1 of garlic clove
-   bay leaves
-   nutmeg
-   white wine
-   white wine vinegar
-   extra-virgin olive oil
-   salt
-   pepper

### Method

1

Clean and mince shallots. Strip leaves from thyme sprig and crumble 1 bay leaf. Mix shallots, thyme, bay leaf, 1 cup wine, 3 Tbsp. oil, a pinch of salt, a grind of pepper and a grating of nutmeg for the marinade.

2

Clean the sea bream and cover with the marinade. Let marinate for 2 hours. Remove from marinade and fill with a little of the marinade. Cook on a hot grill for 15 minutes per side. 

3

Hard boil the eggs, then separate yolks from whites.

4

Soak bread in 3 Tbsp. vinegar. Remove and press out any excess vinegar. Rinse salt from the anchovies under running water. In a blender, mix the bread, egg yolks, anchovies, peeled garlic clove and 1/2 cup oil until smooth. Serve sauce with sea bream.
