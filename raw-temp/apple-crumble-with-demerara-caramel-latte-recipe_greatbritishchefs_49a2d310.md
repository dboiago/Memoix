Is there anything more comforting than an [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes "Apple") crumble? Billington's light brown soft sugar, apple and oats is a heavenly combination, especially when finished with a generous amount of ice cream or custard. We've paired this with a Billington's demerara [caramel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caramel-recipes "Caramel") latte. The demerara gives it an extra caramelly depth that pairs beautifully with the sweet apples. We recommend using a fresh, crisp apple like Gala or Pink Lady.

First published in 2024

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Apple crumble filling

-   750g of [dessert apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), about 5-6, peeled, cored and chopped into 2 cm chunks
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1 tsp [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   100g of soft light brown sugar

#### Apple crumble topping

-   125g of plain flour
-   50g of [rolled oats](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oat-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   75g of soft light brown sugar
-   100g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), cold and cubed
-   3 tbsp of Demerara sugar

#### Demerara caramel latte (per serving)

-   2 tsp [caramel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caramel-recipes), plus extra to serve
-   1 tsp Demerara sugar
-   2 [shots of espresso](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coffee-recipes), about 60ml
-   50ml of double cream
-   175ml of whole milk, hot
-   whipped cream, optional

1

Preheat the oven to 190°C / 170°C fan

2

For the filling, mix the apples, orange zest and juice, cinnamon and sugar. Tip into a 22 cm round dish or rectangular casserole baking dish

-   750g of [dessert apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), about 5-6, peeled, cored and chopped into 2 cm chunks
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1 tsp [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   100g of soft light brown sugar

3

For the topping, mix together the flour, oats, a pinch of salt, light brown sugar and butter. Using your fingertips, rub the butter into flour until you have a bread crumb consistency. Squeeze handfuls of the crumble so it forms clusters

-   125g of plain flour
-   50g of [rolled oats](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oat-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   75g of soft light brown sugar
-   100g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), cold and cubed

4

Sprinkle the crumble on top of the apple mixture then sprinkle over the demerara sugar. Bake for 40–45 minutes until the crumble is golden and apples are cooked through. This is best served hot alongside ice cream or custard

-   3 tbsp of Demerara sugar

5

For each coffee, mix the caramel, demerara, espresso, and cream together in a mug. Top with the hot milk. Finish with whipped cream and more caramel if you like

-   2 tsp [caramel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caramel-recipes), plus extra to serve
-   1 tsp Demerara sugar
-   2 [shots of espresso](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coffee-recipes), about 60ml
-   50ml of double cream
-   175ml of whole milk, hot
-   whipped cream, optional
