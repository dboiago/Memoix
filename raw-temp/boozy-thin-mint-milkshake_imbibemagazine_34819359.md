Everyone’s favorite Girl Scout cookie gets a boozy boost.

Everyone’s favorite Girl Scout cookie gets a boozy boost in this Thin Mint Milkshake with vanilla ice cream and crème de menthe.

## Ingredients

-   1 cup vanilla ice cream
-   3-4 Tbsp. crème de menthe
-   7 Thin Mint cookies

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients and blend until smooth. Pour into a glass and garnish.
