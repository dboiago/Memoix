Toasted crumpets are topped with a light and fluffy whipped [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/compound-butter-recipes "Compound butter") flavoured with [seaweed](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/seaweed-recipes "Seaweed") and masago caviar. The seaweed adds a toasted iron-richness to the butter while the masago seasons it and adds a subtle seafood flavour.

First published in 2023

### Metric

### Imperial

-   9 crumpets, toasted

1

Soften the butter at room temperature for at least 30 minutes

-   250g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

2

Whip the butter on high speed for 5 minutes, or until well aerated and creamy in texture

3

Reduce the speed to incorporate the milk and seaweed, then turn up the speed and whip for another 5 minutes

-   50ml of milk
-   5g of [dulse seaweed flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/seaweed-recipes), toasted

-   50g of masago

5

Use to top the crumpets, adding more masago, if you like

-   9 crumpets, toasted
