[Adam Stokes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/adam-stokes)' beautifully moist and delicately spiced [carrot cake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-cake-recipes) recipe is best combined with a gorgeous scoop of [ice cream](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ice-cream-recipes) - vanilla or cinnamon.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   300g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), grated
-   250g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   250g of light brown sugar
-   5 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested and juiced
-   50g of [almond meal](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   50g of [nibbed almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   100g of [walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/walnut-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   170g of self-raising flour
-   1 tsp baking powder
-   1 tsp [cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes), grated
-   1 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

-   Cake tin

1

In a large bowl, cream the butter and light brown sugar together. Gradually add the eggs to the mix while stirring. Add the zest and juice of the orange

-   250g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   250g of light brown sugar
-   5 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes)

2

In a separate bowl, mix the almond meal, nibbed almonds, walnuts and ginger. Sift in the self raising flour, baking powder, cinnamon and grated nutmeg

-   50g of [almond meal](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   50g of [nibbed almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   100g of [walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/walnut-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   170g of self-raising flour
-   1 tsp baking powder
-   1 tsp [cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes)

3

Add the mix from step 2 into the bowl from step 1and mix to combine. Add the grated carrot and sea salt

-   300g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)
-   1 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

4

Line the cake tin or loaf tin with baking paper. Pour in the cake mix and bake for 40 minutes at 170°C/gas mark 3

5

Remove the tin from the oven and allow to cool on a wire rack. Slice the cake up and serve
