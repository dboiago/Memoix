This classic American [cheeseburger recipe](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/burger-recipes) is given a rustic twist, as [Marcus Wareing](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/marcus-wareing) cooks up his version in toasted ciabatta and stuffs it with delicious, sweet, sticky caramelised onions. This is an excellent rendition of the classic [beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beef-recipes) burger, with fresh parsley, tarragon and garlic bringing extra flavour to the beef patties. Peppery rocket, creamy hummus and Emmental finish off this rich burger.

First published in 2015

### Imperial

#### Burgers

-   500g of [beef mince](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes)
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), peeled and diced
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 [free-range egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), lightly beaten
-   50g of brown breadcrumbs
-   2 tbsp of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of [tarragon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes), chopped
-   1 tbsp of [tomato purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [tomato ketchup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   vegetable oil

#### To plate

-   4 slices of Emmental
-   2 [ciabatta loaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), halved horizontally
-   4 tbsp of [hummus](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hummus-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of extra virgin olive oil
-   4 handfuls of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)

2

For the caramelised onions, melt the butter in a medium-sized frying pan, add the sliced onions, thyme and salt, and cook for around 20-25 minutes until golden and sticky

-   25g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   3 [onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)

3

Heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil in a large frying pan and brown the burgers until cooked through, about 6-7 minutes each side. Remove them from the heat and place a slice of cheese on top of each one to melt gently

-   4 slices of Emmental
-   vegetable oil

4

Brush the inside of the ciabatta with the oil and grill or toast lightly. Spread one half with hummus, then lay the beef patties on the other

-   2 [ciabatta loaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of [hummus](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hummus-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of extra virgin olive oil

5

Add the caramelised onions and rocket and sandwich together. Cut into sections and serve

-   4 handfuls of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)
