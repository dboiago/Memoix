## Celery and Shimeji Stir Fry

Author: [Anita Jacobson](https://dailycookingquest.com/about.html)

Categories: [Side Dish](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/side-dish.html)

Cuisines: [Chinese](https://dailycookingquest.com/cuisines/chinese.html)

Ingredients: [Vegetables](https://dailycookingquest.com/ingredients/vegetables.html)

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time: 10 mins

Total Time: 20 mins

Serves: 4

[Print Recipe](https://dailycookingquest.com/card/celery-and-shimeji-stir-fry.html)

### Ingredients

-   2 tablespoon oil
-   5 cloves garlic, minced
-   2 package shimeji (I use 1 white and 1 brown shimeji), tear into pieces
-   1 head celery, cut into diagonal slices about 1/2" thick
-   4 tablespoon oyster sauce
-   4 tablespoon water
-   1 tablespoon sugar
-   1 - 2 teaspoon ground white pepper

### Instructions

1.  Heat oil in a wok/frying pan on medium high heat, sauté garlic until fragrant, about 2-3 minutes.
2.  Add shimeji, mix well, stir fry for 2 minutes, or until the mushroom starts to look wilted.
3.  Add celery, mix well, season with oyster sauce, water, sugar, and white pepper. Cover the wok/pan with a lid, let it cook for 2 minutes.
4.  Turn off heat, take the lid off, transfer to a serving plate and serve with steamed white rice.
