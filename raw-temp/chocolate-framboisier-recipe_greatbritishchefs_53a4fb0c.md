The marriage of chocolate and [raspberry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/raspberry-recipes) is a very happy one, demonstrated perfectly in this chocolate framboisier. There are lots of different interpretations of the framboisier, but in essence it is a cake with layers of sponge, cream or mousse and raspberries. This version is a chocolate framboisier with layers of chocolate sponge cake, chocolate mousse and a raspberry glaze.

Pâtisserie is something anyone can create and with its increase in popularity thanks to TV shows like [The Great British Bake Off](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/great-british-bake-off-recipes) and its spin-off show [Crème de la Crème](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/tv-series/bake-off-creme-de-la-creme-2016), lots of people are beginning to make professional-style pâtisserie at home.

This framboisier is perfect at this time of year when raspberries are in season. The sharp, sweet fruit cuts through the layers of [cake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cake-recipes) and mousse and adds a delicious refreshing hit in contrast to the chocolate. This is great to serve as part of [afternoon tea](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/afternoon-tea-recipes), or you could just trim the edges and leave it whole to serve it as a celebration cake.
