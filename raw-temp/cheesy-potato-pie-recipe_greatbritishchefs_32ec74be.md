Rosana McPhee serves up these delightful cheesy [potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/potato-recipes) ramekins, comprised of a comforting mix of potato, [cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), egg and [herbs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/fresh-herbs-recipes). Perfect for a wonderfully simple midweek meal.

First published in 2019

### Metric

### Imperial

-   4 [large mashing potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled and cut into cubes (approximately 500g)
-   175g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   100ml of cream
-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 [medium onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   100g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated
-   6 [cherry tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-tomato-recipes), halved
-   3 tbsp of [crispy shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   1 handful of oregano, finely chopped
-   1 handful of [basil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes), finely chopped

-   Ramekins 4
-   Food processor

1

To begin, cook the cubed potatoes in salted boiling water until soft

2

Drain and let them cool slightly. Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4

3

In a food processor, blend the cooked potatoes, egg, cream, grated cheddar (reserving a few spoonfuls of cheese for later) and onion until smooth. Season with salt and pepper to taste

4

Spoon half of the mixture into a buttered baking dish or individual ramekins, add a layer of grated cheese and herbs, then pour the rest of the potato mixture on top. Place the cherry tomato halves and some more grated cheese on top to finish

5

Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with the Parmesan and bake in the oven for 20–30 minutes, until golden brown

6

Once ready, take out of the oven, sprinkle with some fresh herbs and crispy shallots and serve immediately
