Orange marmalade makes this low-abv cocktail sing.

Bright and bittersweet—with a spoonful of marmalade shaken in for good measure—this low-alcohol cocktail is perfect for brunch.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. Carpano Antica sweet vermouth
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   [1/2 oz. simple syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   1 tsp. Brancamenta
-   1 barspoon orange marmalade

## Preparation

Add all the ingredients to a shaker with ice and shake well. Strain into a double rocks glass filled with crushed ice.
