Summer vacation captured in a glass.

Although Anaheim tropical bar Strong Water, which is a freeway exit away from Disneyland, features a rum-heavy menu, bartender Lynette Le-Lim wanted to create a vodka drink that tasted like biting into fresh fruit on a hot day. Her Birds of Paradise cocktail not only does that, but she says it’s “approachable to anyone who wants to enjoy escapism with a libation.” 

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. vodka
-   1 oz. fresh pineapple juice
-   1/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   [1/2 oz. rich simple syrup (2:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/rich-simple-syrup-recipe/)
-   1 fresh whole strawberry
-   3 hot house cucumber slices
-   1 dash peach bitters (Le-Lim uses Fee Brothers)

## Preparation

Muddle strawberry, cucumber, and syrup together in the shaker. Add rest of ingredients and ice to the shaker. Shake and double strain over pebble ice in a glass. Garnish.
