[Cheek](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beef-cheek-recipes) is often considered a tough cut, but in this glorious braised beef recipe by Adam Bennett the meat is cooked for a couple of hours in red wine and Madeira until tender and full of flavour. While [beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beef-recipes) and [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/red-wine-recipes) might be a classic combination, the more unusual addition of pickled [walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/walnut-recipes) and [anchovies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/anchovy-recipes) to the sauce adds a real depth of flavour, enhancing the taste without overpowering it.

#### Braised beef cheeks

-   4 [beef cheeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-cheek-recipes), trimmed
-   50ml of vegetable oil
-   100g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), chopped
-   100g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   50g of [celery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), chopped
-   1 [head of garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), cut horizontally in half
-   375ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   150ml of Madeira
-   100g of [passata](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)

-   4 sprigs of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   4 [bay leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bay-leaf-recipes)
-   15g of [dried ceps](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cep-recipes)
-   4 [juniper berries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/juniper-berry-recipes), crushed
-   4 [smoked anchovy fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/anchovy-recipes)
-   4 [pickled walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/walnut-recipes), chopped

#### For the mash

-   1kg [potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), preferably Maris Piper or Yukon Gold
-   150ml of milk, hot
-   150ml of cream, hot
-   175g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), room temperature and diced

-   Drum sieve

1

Begin by preparing the beef. A day before you intend on serving season the beef cheeks with salt and pepper, cover and refrigerate for 24 hours. This step is not essential if you are pushed for time, although it ensures an even seasoning throughout the meat

-   4 [beef cheeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-cheek-recipes), trimmed

2

Preheat the oven to 160°C/as mark 3

3

Remove the beef cheeks from the fridge. Heat the vegetable oil on high in a heavy frying pan, then add the beef cheeks to the pan to sear on all sides. Once seared transfer the cheeks to a casserole dish

-   50ml of vegetable oil

4

In the same heavy frying pan fry the garlic, onion, carrots and celery over a moderate heat until caramelised. Add both the red wine and Madeira to the pan, stir well and simmer until the liquid has reduced by half. Add the beef stock and passata and bring to the boil, skimming off any scum on the surface of the liquid

-   1 [head of garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), cut horizontally in half
-   100g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   100g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), chopped
-   50g of [celery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), chopped
-   375ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   150ml of Madeira
-   100g of [passata](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1.5l beef stock

6

Meanwhile, peel the potatoes and slice them into even pieces. Place them in a pan with enough salted water to cover and bring to the boil. Once boiling reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes, until the potatoes are soft and tender

-   1kg [potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), preferably Maris Piper or Yukon Gold

7

Take the casserole dish out of the oven and remove the beef cheeks. Place onto a tray and cover with foil, keeping warm. Strain the liquid from the casserole dish through a sieve into a clean saucepan and heat gently. Allow the liquid to reduce until it forms a sauce-like consistency, then reduce the heat and keep warm until ready to serve

8

To make the glazed carrots lay the carrots in a single layer across a wide shallow pan along with a pinch of salt. Add the butter to the pan along with enough carrot juice to cover, place a lid over the pan and bring the liquid to the boil. Cook until the carrots are tender, topping up the liquid with water if necessary, then remove the lid and allow the liquid to reduce until it forms a glaze. Squeeze over a little lemon juice to taste

-   300g of [chantenay carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), cut in half
-   75g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100g of carrot juice
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), to taste

9

Drain the potatoes and return them to the hot pan and, stirring constantly, allow the potatoes to dry fry over a low heat for a couple of minutes. Mash the potatoes, passing through a drum sieve for smoother consistency, then add in the diced butter, warm milk and cream. Season to taste

-   150ml of milk, hot
-   150ml of cream, hot
-   175g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), room temperature and diced

10

To serve spoon a generous helping of mash potato onto each plate followed by the beef cheek and carrots. Add the remaining chopped walnuts to the warm sauce, stir and spoon over the beef cheeks and around the plate. Top with the chopped parsley and serve immediately

-   2 tbsp of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
