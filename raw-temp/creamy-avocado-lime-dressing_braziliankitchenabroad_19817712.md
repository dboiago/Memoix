## How to Make Creamy Avocado Dressing

[![Collage showing how to make Avocado Lime Dressing in a food processor, before and after blending](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/lime-avocado-dressing-steps-2-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/lime-avocado-dressing-steps-2.png)

Dump the ripe avocado, garlic, olive oil, cilantro, lime juice and water into the [food processor](https://amzn.to/3pqUglP). Season with salt and pepper, too.

**_Buzz, buzz, buzz!_** Give it a taste, add more salt and pepper if desired, and this salad dressing recipe is complete.

I think this is the perfect creamy consistency. However, if you want the dressing to be more pourable, you can add a splash more of water to thin it more. If you do, you’ll probably have to adjust salt and pepper as well. You might also have to add another squeeze of the lime to up the acidity, too, so keep that in mind and taste as you go.

Store the dressing for up to a week in the refrigerator and enjoy it on everything!

This six ingredient Avocado Lime Dressing is deliciously creamy and perfect for any salad. This creamy avocado dressing recipe is great on leafy greens, all kinds of veggies, on top of bowls and more. All you need are a handful of minutes to make this delicious homemade salad dressing.

Servings 4 Servings

Calories 207 kcal

Prep Time 10 minutes

Total Time 10 minutes

-   1 Ripe Avocado
-   3 cloves of garlic
-   1/4 cup [of extra virgin olive oil](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07BCWJY7J?linkCode=ssc&tag=onamzbrazil08-20&creativeASIN=B07BCWJY7J&asc_item-id=amzn1.ideas.13SWMAD4SQ7NS&ref_=aip_sf_list_spv_ons_mixed_d_asin)
-   1/4 cup of fresh cilantro leaves
-   1/4 cup of lime juice freshly squeezed
-   1/2 cup of water
-   salt and pepper to taste

_As an Amazon Associate and member of other affiliate programs, I earn from qualifying purchases._

-   Add the ripe avocado, garlic, olive oil, cilantro, lime juice and water to a [food processor](https://amzn.to/3pqUglP). Season with salt and pepper.
    
-   Blend until creamy.
    
-   Taste, and add additional salt and pepper, if necessary. Add extra water if you want the dressing to be more pourable. (You might also have to add additional salt, pepper and lime juice if you do this.)
    
-   Serve or store.

**How to store:** Store in a mason jar or another storage container in the refrigerator for up to a week.
