Delicate white [crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/crab-recipes) meat is the perfect base for the aromatic Asian flavours of [Martin Wishart](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/martin-wishart)'s crab cakes recipe. These little cakes pack a punch with lime, ginger, garlic and spring onions all adding flavour and make a wonderful starter if served with a salad, or perhaps even a [canapé](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/canape-recipes) if served in bite-sized portions.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Crab cakes

-   450g of [white crab meat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes)
-   100g of breadcrumbs, fine
-   2 [large eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   6 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 tbsp of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 tsp [Thai fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   1 knob of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), around 2.5cm in length, peeled and finely grated
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   plain flour
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste
-   [white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground
-   4 tbsp of vegetable oil

#### To Serve

-   1 dash of olive oil
-   1 dash of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   salad leaves

1

For the crab cakes, carefully pick through the crab meat for any pieces of cartilage or shell. Combine in a bowl with the eggs, breadcrumbs, spring onions, fish and soy sauces, ginger, garlic and lime juice

-   450g of [white crab meat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes)
-   2 [large eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   100g of breadcrumbs, finely ground
-   6 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 tsp [Thai fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   1 knob of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), around 2.5cm in length, peeled and finely grated
-   1 tbsp of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)

2

Season to taste with a little salt and pepper. Divide the mix into 50g portions and shape into even-sized round cakes and lightly coat with flour

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground
-   plain flour

3

Heat the vegetable oil in a large non-stick frying pan on a medium heat. When the pan has come to temperature, carefully arrange the crab cakes in the pan, making sure they don’t touch each other

-   4 tbsp of vegetable oil

4

Allow the cakes to cook for 3-4 minutes on each side so they are hot in the centre and golden-brown on the outside. Use a palette knife to transfer the crab cakes from the pan on to kitchen paper to drain off excess oil

5

Serve the crab cakes on a warm plate with a garnish of a few salad leaves dressed with a little olive oil and a squeeze of lime juice

-   1 dash of olive oil
-   1 dash of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   salad leaves
