Black [treacle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/treacle-recipes) may sound like an unusual cure ingredient for fish, but the sweet smokiness goes perfectly, particularly when paired with aromatic [caraway](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/caraway-recipes) and fruity citrus flavours. The finishing touches of beetroot, caviar and cumin sour cream elevate this black treacle-cured [salmon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/salmon-recipes) recipe from [Mark Dodson](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/mark-dodson) to majestic heights.

First published in 2015

1

To prepare the marinade, combine the salt, sugar, pepper, orange juice, lemon juice, caraway and treacle in a large container. Remove the skin from the salmon, then thoroughly coat the flesh in the marinade and set aside in the fridge for 24 hours, turning over after 12 hours

-   200g of [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   300g of sugar
-   8g of [Mignonette pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   285ml of [orange juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes)
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juice and zest
-   1 tsp [caraway seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caraway-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [black treacle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/treacle-recipes), plus extra for garnish
-   1 side of salmon

2

To prepare the beetroot, remove the tops and tails and place into a saucepan of cold water. Bring to a gentle boil and cook until tender - this will take approximately 20-40 minutes. Strain and allow to cool slightly, then peel and dice into 1cm cubes. Set aside until required

-   2 [large beetroots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes)

3

To prepare the garnish, simply mix the sour cream with the cumin, adding a little more to taste if necessary, and refrigerate until required

-   200g of sour cream
-   1 pinch of [ground cumin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)

4

Run the salmon under cold water to wash off the marinade. Pat dry with kitchen towel then brush with a little more black treacle. Using a very sharp knife, slice thinly across the grain, starting at the top of the fillet until you have 4-5 slices per portion

5

Spoon some sour cream onto the corner of each plate and arrange slices of the salmon on top. Arrange the beetroot and caviar around each plate and finish with a sprinkling of the chives and cress

-   50g of [salmon caviar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caviar-recipes)
-   50g of [Avruga caviar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caviar-recipes)
-   1 handful of [micro cress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cress-recipes)
-   1/2 bunch of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), finely chopped
