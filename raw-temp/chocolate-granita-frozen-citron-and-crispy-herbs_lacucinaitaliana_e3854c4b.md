## Granita in its simplest form is a slightly slushy, grainy mass of water that has been sweetened, flavored, frozen, and crushed. In this recipe, mint and citron are deliciously paired with dark chocolate granita for a refreshing summer dessert.

**Cooks in:** _8h 45mins \+ 8h_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   9 OZ. of heavy cream
-   7 OZ. of brown sugar
-   6 OZ. of 65% dark chocolate, chopped
-   1 1/3 OZ. of cocoa
-   1 or 2 citron
-   1 egg white
-   20 leaves of lemon balm
-   20 LEAVES of mint
-   sugar

### Method

1

For the chocolate granita: place the chopped chocolate, cane sugar, cocoa, and 3 cups water in a pot; place over medium heat and stir with a whisk until fully melted and combined (do not bring to a boil, or the chocolate will spoil).

2

Transfer the mixture to a bowl and leave to cool at room temperature, then place in the freezer. Stir every 30-45 minutes: after chilling for 6-8 hours, a granular granita will form.

3

For the frozen citron: cut the citron peel into pieces, including the white part; dredge it in some sugar and arrange on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Place in the freezer alongside the granita.

4

For the crispy herbs: gently brush 20 mint leaves and 20 lemon balm leaves with beaten egg white. Dip in the sugar and arrange on a cooking rack. Let dry for a few hours until crisp.

5

For the whipped cream: beat the cream and 1 Tbsp. granulated sugar in a medium bowl with a handheld mixer or whisk until soft peaks just begin to form. Cover and chill until ready to serve.

6

Divide the granita between frozen glasses, top with the whipped cream and serve with the frozen pieces of citron zest and the crispy herbs. Serve.
