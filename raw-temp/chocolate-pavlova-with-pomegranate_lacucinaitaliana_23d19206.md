**Cooks in:** _4h 40mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _10_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 1/4 C. of cream
-   1 C. of fine granulated sugar
-   3/4 C. of egg whites (about 4 large)
-   1/4 LB. of dark chocolate
-   3 TBSP. of cornstarch
-   2 TBSP. of unsweetened cocoa powder
-   Apple cider vinegar
-   Pomegranate
-   Currants
-   Salt

### Method

1

Add the egg whites and a pinch of salt into a bowl, then begin to beat with an electric hand mixer. When it begins to froth, add in the granulated sugar a little at a time. Continue to beat until the sugar has completely melted in: you should not be able to see any granules. Incorporate the sifted cocoa powder while continuing to beat.|Add in the sifted cornstarch and continue to beat the egg whites until they form stiff peaks, almost like the consistency of shaving cream.|Add in a tablespoon of apple cider vinegar and beat for a few more minutes.|Preheat the oven to 300°F. With a pen, draw a circle (6 1/2-inch diameter) on a sheet of parchment paper, then turn over the sheet and lay it on a baking tray (in this way, you will see the design without the ink coming into contact with the meringue). Distribute the meringue inside the circle and, with an icing spatula, form a sort of small mound; it will expand slightly while baking. Form a reasonably sized hollow inside the meringue. Bake on the convection setting, reducing the temperature to 250°F, for 1 hour and 30 minutes. Turn off the oven and let the meringue cool inside with the door open. It is normal for it to deflate a bit while cooling off.|Heat half of the cream until it almost comes to a boil, then pour it over the chopped chocolate in a bowl. Gently stir until it has melted. Let the ganache cool.|Pour the ganache inside the hollow of the meringue and put it in the fridge for 1 hour. Remove it from the fridge and top with the rest of the cream, whipped and dolloped on in spoonfuls, and with pomegranate seeds and currants. Serve immediately.
