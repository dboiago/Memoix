Baking spices and caramel apple flavors make this a cozy matcha latte for chilly days.

Golden Hour Coffee Company in Portland, Oregon, makes the matcha latte cozy for cold-weather enjoyment by sweetening it with a syrup of caramel sauce, apple butter, and baking spices.

## Ingredients

-   8 oz. steamed milk of choice
-   2 oz. hot water (175°F / 80°C)
-   1 oz. caramel apple butter syrup
-   2 tsp. high-quality matcha powder
-   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
-   Caramel Apple Butter Syrup
-   3/4 cup granulated sugar
-   3/4 cup water
-   1/4 cup light brown sugar
-   2 Tbsp. apple butter
-   2 Tbsp. caramel sauce
-   1/2 tsp. vanilla extract
-   1/4 tsp. salt
-   1/4 tsp. cinnamon
-   1/8 tsp. butter
-   1/8 tsp. nutmeg

## Preparation

In a mug, whisk the matcha powder with the hot water until smooth. Stir in the syrup and add the steamed milk. Garnish.  
**Caramel Apple Butter Syrup**Add the butters to a saucepan over medium heat. Once melted follow with caramel sauce and vanilla extract. Stir to combine and add the rest of the ingredients. Stir until clump-free. Will keep in the refrigerator for 2 weeks.
