### Glass:

Serve in a [Highball (max 10oz/300ml)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 fl oz

[Cacao & coffee spiced rum](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/6456/black-tears-spiced-rum)

2 fl oz

[Pineapple verdita](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/1771/cocktails/verdita)

1⁄3 fl oz

[Pimento dram/Allspice liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/2913/st-elizabeth-allspice-dram)

1⁄2 fl oz

Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)

1⁄2 fl oz

Demerara/Muscovado/brown sugar syrup (2 sugar to 1 water)

## How to make:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [HIGHBALL (MAX 10OZ/300ML) GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of fresh or dehydrated pineapple spice & mint springs.
3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-strain-a-cocktail) into ice-filled glass.
