Michael Bremner serves cured mackerel with fermented gooseberries and a fresh, zingy [elderflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/elderflower-recipes) dressing for a beautifully summery dish. He uses yoghurt whey to ferment the [gooseberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gooseberry-recipes), so you will need to plan ahead for this dish. Follow Michael's [guide to making whey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/how-to-cook/how-to-make-whey) for best results.

First published in 2018

### Imperial

#### Fermented gooseberries

-   250g of [gooseberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gooseberry-recipes)
-   250ml of yoghurt whey

-   Blender
-   Fish tweezers

1

Wash the gooseberries, remove any stalks and place in a sterile sealable container. Pour in the whey, ensuring the berries are completely submerged, and seal the container. Store at room temperature for at least 7 days

-   250g of [gooseberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gooseberry-recipes)
-   250ml of yoghurt whey

2

On the day of serving, fillet the mackerel (you can ask your fishmonger to do this for you, if preferred), season with salt and sugar and set aside to cure for 1 hour

-   2 [large mackerel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mackerel-pate-recipes), as fresh as possible
-   20g of caster sugar
-   75g of [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Meanwhile, make the elderflower dressing. Blend the citrus juices with the chilli and cucumber until smooth and pass through a fine sieve

-   1/2 [mild red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded
-   6 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   6 [limes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   1/2 [cucumber](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cucumber-recipes), peeled

4

Season with elderflower cordial to taste – it’s best to use around 25ml to start, then add more if needed. Add the xanthan gum and blend again until hydrated to thicken the mixture

-   elderflower cordial, 25–50ml
-   1.5g of xanthan gum

5

Drain a handful of the fermented gooseberries from the whey and pat dry. Cut into slices and set aside

6

Peel away the fine skin of each mackerel fillet and remove any pin bones. Trim the fillets to neaten. To serve, slice the mackerel into thin slices (1cm maximum) at a 45 degree angle. Arrange on plates, dress with the elderflower dressing and garnish with fermented gooseberries and nasturtium leaves

-   [nasturtium leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/edible-flower-recipes)
