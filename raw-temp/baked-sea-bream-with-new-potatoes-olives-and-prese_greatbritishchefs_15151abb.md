-   50g of [green olives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-olive-recipes), plus 1 tablespoon of their brine
-   1 [fresh bay leaf](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bay-leaf-recipes)

-   200ml of vegetable stock
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

2

Preheat the oven to 190ºC/gas mark 6

3

Add the white wine to the pan and reduce by half. Then add in the sliced potatoes, the olives with the brine, the preserved lemons, bay leaf and vegetable stock and simmer for around 8 minutes – the potatoes should be starting to soften slightly but not cooked through

-   75ml of white wine
-   50g of [green olives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-olive-recipes), plus 1 tablespoon of their brine
-   400g of [waxy potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), in 1cm thick slices
-   1 [preserved lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), sliced into eighths
-   1 [fresh bay leaf](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bay-leaf-recipes)
-   200ml of vegetable stock

4

Transfer the potato mixture to a large oven-proof tray, and place the sea bream on top. Drizzle the bream with a little oil and season with salt then bake for 30 minutes until the potatoes are tender, and the bream is starting to crisp up
