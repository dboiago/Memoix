The vodka classic by way of Italy.

Since every cocktail at Saint Theo’s is a nod to an Italian name, ingredient, or garnish, beverage director Simon Sebbah transformed the [Moscow Mule](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/recipe-moscow-mule/) into an Italian Mule by sweetening it with [amaretto](https://imbibemagazine.com/elements-amaretto/).

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. mezcal
-   1 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1/2 oz. amaretto
-   Top with ginger beer (Saint Theo's uses Fever-Tree)

## Preparation

Add the ingredients, except the ginger beer, to a shaker with ice and shake vigorously. Pour into a highball filled with crushed ice and top with ginger beer, then garnish.
