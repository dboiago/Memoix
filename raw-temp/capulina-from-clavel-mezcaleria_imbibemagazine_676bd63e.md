Coffee, amaro and mezcal create a heavenly mix of smoke and spice in this cocktail from [Clavel Mezcaleria](https://barclavel.com/) in Baltimore.

## Ingredients

-   1 ½ oz. mezcal espadín
-   1 ½ oz. cold-brew coffee
-   1 oz. evaporated milk
-   ¾ oz. Henri-Vallet Amargo (or amaro of your choice)
-   ½ oz. agave nectar
-   5 drops Bittermens Xocolatl Mole bitters

## Preparation

Shake all the ingredients together with ice in a shaker. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Garnish.
