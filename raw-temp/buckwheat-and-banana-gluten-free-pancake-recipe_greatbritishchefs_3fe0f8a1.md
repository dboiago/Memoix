One of the things I find hardest to stick to in terms of maintaining a [healthy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/healthy-recipes) lifestyle is just getting enough sleep. So I asked my husband to give my boy breakfast, whilst I sleepily addressed the very important matter of what my body was craving.

Unlike days of care-free eating in the past, my body is now giving me warning signals (as well as my doctor) and though weight isn’t the challenge here, my family has a history of diabetes and I cannot fight the genes with sugar and win.

I had never really seriously considered [buckwheat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/buckwheat-recipes) pancakes as an alternative to the plain flour version; the healthy option isn’t usually as tasty is it? Well, let me tell you my friends, these are so fluffy and light that I had to share the recipe with you. They are moist and airy, not gritty or clumpy at all so don’t worry on that front; I didn’t add any sugar but you could if you really wanted to. The [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/banana-recipes) is chopped into small pieces rather than mashed to avoid making them heavy and the passion … it comes through. I used pure maple syrup on top, but just a few drops.
