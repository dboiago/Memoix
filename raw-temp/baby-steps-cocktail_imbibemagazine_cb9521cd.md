**RECIPE:** _Brian Means for Fifth Floor, San Francisco  
(Note: This restaurant has permanently closed.)_

![baby steps](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/recipe_c_baby_steps.jpg)

Consider this cocktail your baby step to a more bracingly bitter Negroni.

Not into the hyper-bitter kick Campari adds to the traditional Negroni? Try this recipe with Aperol, instead. There’s nothing wrong with taking baby steps.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. gin
-   1 oz. Aperol
-   1 oz. sweet vermouth

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass and stir with ice to chill. Strain into a chilled glass and garnish with a lemon peel.
