[Emily Watkins'](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/emily-watkins/) warm salad of butter beans, chorizo and sweet confit Piccolo cherry tomatoes is a real crowdpleaser and makes a great [lunch](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lunch-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lunch-recipes) or [side dish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/side-dish-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/side-dish-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/side-dish-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/side-dish-recipes). She takes the tomato-flavoured oil leftover from the confit to create a punchy [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/saffron-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/saffron-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/saffron-recipes) aioli, which is a fantastic way to reduce food waste.

First published in 2019

### Metric

### Imperial

-   130ml of olive oil, ideally cold-pressed
-   1 pinch of [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/saffron-recipes)
-   1 [bay leaf](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bay-leaf-recipes)
-   250g of [Piccolo cherry tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-tomato-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of light olive oil
-   2 [banana shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes)
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   100g of [chorizo sausage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes)
-   400g of [tinned butter beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-bean-recipes)
-   1 handful of [basil leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes)

1

Place the oil, saffron and bay in a small saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer and lower in the tomatoes. Turn the heat straight off and leave the tomatoes to cook gently in the oil for at least 20 minutes (they will stay safely in the oil for 48 hours if needed)

-   1 pinch of [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/saffron-recipes)
-   1 [bay leaf](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bay-leaf-recipes)
-   125ml of olive oil, ideally cold-pressed
-   250g of [Piccolo cherry tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-tomato-recipes)

2

Meanwhile, finely slice the shallots and garlic and sweat down in a couple of tablespoons of light cooking oil

-   2 tbsp of light olive oil
-   2 [banana shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes)
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)

3

Cut the chorizo into chunks. Add to the shallots and sauté for a couple of minutes

-   100g of [chorizo sausage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes)

4

Drain the tinned beans and add to the shallots and chorizo. Very carefully lift out the tomatoes from the oil and add them to the beans and chorizo

-   400g of [tinned butter beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-bean-recipes)

5

Remove the bay leaf from the oil, then pour the tomato-flavoured oil into a jug and leave to cool. You will need around 125ml of the oil for the aioli

6

To make the aioli, place the egg yolk in a bowl with a pinch of salt and the lemon juice. Using a hand or an electric whisk, mix in the lemon juice and then very slowly start adding the oil. Whisk the mixture constantly, allowing the oil to become emulsified

-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1/4 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

7

Stir in the garlic, then taste and adjust the seasoning if necessary

-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced

8

Spoon the beans and tomato into bowls and tear the basil over the top. Serve with the aioli on the side

-   1 handful of [basil leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes)
