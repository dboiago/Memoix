Delicious [blackberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/blackberry-recipes) are infused in [gin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/gin-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/gin-recipes) to create a spirit full of flavour in this easy recipe. Feel free to double (or triple) the recipe to ensure you have enough to see you through the colder, less fruit-filled months.

First published in 2019

-   Muslin cloth
-   Sterilised 500ml jar

1

Wash the berries and place in a sterilised jar

2

Add the sugar and gin, close the jar and give it a shake

3

Store in a cool dark place for 1 week, giving the jar a shake every other day to make sure the sugar has dissolved

4

Strain the gin through a muslin cloth and bottle. Best consumed within 1 month
