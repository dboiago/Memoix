Ayo Adeyemi serves fresh Orkney [scallops](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/scallop-recipes "Scallop") here with ayamase stew, a [West African](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/features/west-african-cooking-in-the-uk "An introduction to West African cooking") stew made from green peppers, plantain chips and shito XO sauce. This recipe makes more shito XO sauce than you need, but it's so delicious you'll use it up in no time.

First published in 2023

### Imperial

#### Shito XO

-   500ml of grapeseed oil
-   500g of [tiger prawns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/prawn-recipes), peeled, de-veined
-   1 [smoked mackerel fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/smoked-mackerel-recipes)
-   250g of red onion, finely diced
-   5g Scotch bonnet paste
-   10g of [ginger paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   15g of [garlic paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   350g of [tomato paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   45ml of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   45ml of [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)

#### Ayamase stew

-   100g of grapeseed oil
-   400g of [yellow onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   300g of [green pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), deseeded, finely sliced
-   10g of [garlic paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   5g of [fine salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   5g of caster sugar
-   5g of [scotch bonnet chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1g of [ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   100ml of chicken stock
-   250g of [baby spinach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spinach-recipes)

1

Shuck the live scallops and remove the skirt and roe attached. Put the scallop roe in ice water then pat it dry. Smoke the roe over wood chips

-   6 [XL live hand-dived scallops](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-scallop-recipes)

2

Once smoked, place the roe on an oven tray and dehydrate at 60°C for 12 hours. Once dehydrated, blend to a powder

3

To make the shito XO, heat the oil to 180°C in a wide saucepan and fry the prawns and mackerel until golden brown. Strain and reserve the oil for later 

-   500ml of grapeseed oil
-   500g of [tiger prawns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/prawn-recipes), peeled, de-veined
-   1 [smoked mackerel fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/smoked-mackerel-recipes)

4

Blend the cooked fish in a food processor for 1 minute until it forms fine crumbs

5

Use 50ml of the reserved oil to sweat the onions in saucepan until soft but not coloured. Add the Scotch bonnet, ginger and garlic pastes. Add the tomato paste and slowly cook out for 10–15 minutes until roasted and dark in colour

-   250g of red onion, finely diced
-   5g Scotch bonnet paste
-   10g of [ginger paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   15g of [garlic paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   350g of [tomato paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)

6

Add the blended fish, soy and fish sauce and mix well then remove from the heat

-   45ml of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   45ml of [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)

7

Cover with the remaining reserved oil and leave to cool down overnight in fridge

8

For the ayamase stew, sweat the onions with the oil in a wide saucepan until softened

-   100g of grapeseed oil
-   400g of [yellow onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced

10

Blend everything together on high speed until smooth

11

Blanch the spinach for 1 minute and then shock it in iced water

-   250g of [baby spinach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spinach-recipes)

12

Squeeze out excess water from the spinach and blend with the other ingredients until smooth, and then pass through a fine sieve

13

For the green plantain chips, peel the plantains and cut them into thin strips using knife or a mandolin

-   2 green plantains

14

Heat oil in a saucepan to 170°C and fry the plantain strips for 2 minutes or until golden and crispy

-   oil, for deep-frying

16

Dust over the crispy plantain chips and sprinkle with sea salt

-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

17

Heat a non-stick pan with a splash of oil, and sear the scallops on service side for 1 minute on high heat until they form a golden brown crust

-   grapeseed oil

18

Remove the pan from the heat, add some butter and lemon juice and then flip the scallop and baste on the other side

-   [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

19

Season with sea salt to finish and allow to rest before serving

-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

20

To serve, spoon some ayamase stew onto a plate. Strain the shito from the oil and place a heaped spoonful on top of the scallop. Top the shito with the green plaintain crisps. Finish with crispy plantain on top of the scallop as well
