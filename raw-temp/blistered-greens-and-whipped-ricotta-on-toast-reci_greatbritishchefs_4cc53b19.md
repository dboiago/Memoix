Blanched and blistered [greens](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetable-recipes) make for a deeply flavourful and fresh finish topping for some whipped-[ricotta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ricotta-recipes)\-topped toasts. Served with some classic Mediterranean flavour pairings of lemon, basil, pine nuts and red chilli, it’s vibrant, creamy and bright all at once. Heat a large flat griddle pan or tray with a drizzle of vegetable oil over a high heat. Once hot, turn down to medium-high and add the blanched greens and courgette ribbons in a single layer. Allow the vegetables to blacken and blister on one side without stirring – about 2-4 minutes

Turn the veg over and repeat until cooked through and nicely coloured – another 2-4 minutes

Meanwhile, toast the sourdough and spread over a generous layer of whipped ricotta. Arrange the blistered greens on top and finish with a drizzle of olive oil, the remaining lemon zest and juice, sliced basil, finely sliced red chilli, toasted pine nuts and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt
