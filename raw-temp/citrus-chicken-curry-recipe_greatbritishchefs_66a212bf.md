Item 1 of 1

-   2, or 4 with sides
    
-   50 minutes
    

This soothing, comforting [chicken curry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-curry-recipes) recipe is mildly spiced, but the real hit of flavour comes from the [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lime-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lime-recipes) and orange in the coconut milk-based sauce, of which there's plenty for mopping up with breads and rice. A wonderfully warming dish to prepare which is guaranteed to please the whole family.

First published in 2019

### Metric

### Imperial

-   600g of [chicken thigh](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), boneless and diced into 3cm cubes
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   [chopped coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), to garnish
-   [rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes), naan breads, mango chutney and lime wedges, to serve

#### Marinade

-   15g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   1/2 tsp [red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tsp [paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1 tsp [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   1 tsp [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), zested
-   120ml of [plain yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [mustard oil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes), or vegetable oil

#### Sauce

-   20ml of vegetable oil
-   1 pinch of [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground cardamom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)
-   1 bay leaf
-   1 [large onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), diced
-   1 tsp [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), grated
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed

#### To finish the sauce

-   1 [tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), chopped
-   1 pinch of [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp fenugreek leaves, dried
-   100ml of [plain yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   50ml of single cream
-   200ml of chicken stock
-   300ml of [coconut milk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes)
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), juiced

2

Preheat an oven to 200°C/gas mark 7. Transfer the chicken (along with its marinade) into an ovenproof dish and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from the oven and set aside

3

To make the sauce, add the vegetable oil to a large saucepan over a medium heat. Add the chilli, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves and bay leaf and cook for a few seconds until fragrant

-   20ml of vegetable oil
-   1 pinch of [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground cardamom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)
-   1 bay leaf

4

Add the onion, ginger and garlic and cook until softened and beginning to brown. Add the chopped tomato and remaining spices, the cook until you see the oil separating around the sides of the pan (about 10 minutes)

-   1 [large onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), diced
-   1 tsp [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), grated
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   1 [tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), chopped
-   1 pinch of [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp fenugreek leaves, dried

5

Add the yoghurt, single cream, coconut milk, orange juice and chicken stock, season then add the chicken thighs to the sauce. Cook until the sauce is thick and rich in texture (about 15 minutes)

-   100ml of [plain yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   50ml of single cream
-   200ml of chicken stock
-   300ml of [coconut milk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes)
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), juiced

6

Remove the chicken from the sauce and set aside, then transfer the sauce to a blender and blitz until smooth and creamy

7

To serve, place the chicken back into the sauce and divide between bowls. Top with a little lime juice and coriander and serve with rice, breads and chutney

-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   [chopped coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), to garnish
-   [rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes), naan breads, mango chutney and lime wedges, to serve
