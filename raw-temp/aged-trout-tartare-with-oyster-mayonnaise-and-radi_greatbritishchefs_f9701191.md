### Imperial

-   2 [green meat radishes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/radish-recipes), sliced as thinly as possible
-   100g of chardonnay vinegar
-   50g of olive oil
-   1 tsp [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   600g of [aged sea trout](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sea-trout-recipes), skinned and pin-boned (or use fresh sea trout)
-   1 [cucumber](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cucumber-recipes), cut into 0.5cm dice
-   3 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   4 [oysters](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oyster-recipes)
-   150g of rapeseed oil
-   [horseradish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/horseradish-recipes) to taste, grated
-   1 bunch of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

-   Stick blender
-   Piping bags

1

Combine the sliced radishes, olive oil, chardonnay vinegar and honey together and set aside to marinate

-   2 [green meat radishes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/radish-recipes), sliced as thinly as possible
-   50g of olive oil
-   100g of chardonnay vinegar
-   1 tsp [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)

2

Dice the sea trout into a smallish dice, and keep cool in a mixing bowl over ice. Add the cucumber, the juice of 2 lemons and some salt to season and mix well to combine

-   1 [cucumber](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cucumber-recipes), cut into 0.5cm dice
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

3

Shuck the oysters and place them and their juices into a container with the juice of the remaining lemon. Using a hand blender blitz the oysters and lemon juice to a puree

-   4 [oysters](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oyster-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced

4

Very slowly whisk in the rapeseed oil (drop by drop at first, then in a thin stream) to make a mayonnaise. Season with salt, transfer the mixture to a piping bag and refrigerate

-   150g of rapeseed oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

5

Divide the trout mixture between 6 plates, then pipe on a few dots of oyster mayonnaise. Cover with the marinated radishes and a few sprigs of dill. Grate a little horseradish over the top and serve

-   [horseradish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/horseradish-recipes) to taste, grated
-   1 bunch of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes)
