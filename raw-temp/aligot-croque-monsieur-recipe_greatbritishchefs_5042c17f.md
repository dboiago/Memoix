This is our luxurious take on the classic [French](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/french-recipes "French") sandwich, with layers of ham and aligot between slices of buttery, toasted bread. We topped it with béchamel sauce and [Gruyère](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gruyere-recipes "Gruyère"), then baked everything until golden. 

First published in 2024

### Metric

### Imperial

-   4 slices of [white bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), sliced around 1 cm thick
-   2 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   4 slices of [ham](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ham-recipes)
-   50g of [Gruyère](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gruyere-recipes), grated

#### Aligot

-   300g of [waxy potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), such as Yukon golds, peeled and diced
-   75g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), diced
-   100ml of whole milk
-   150g of [low-moisture mozzarella](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mozzarella-recipes), grated
-   80g of [Gruyère](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gruyere-recipes), grated
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

#### Béchamel

-   15g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   15g of plain flour
-   150ml of whole milk
-   1 pinch of [freshly ground nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes)

1

Begin by preparing the aligot. Place the diced potatoes into a pot of cold, salted water and bring up to a boil. Cook until the potatoes are fork tender – this should take around 15 minutes

-   300g of [waxy potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), such as Yukon golds, peeled and diced

2

Drain the potatoes and return them to the pot, then mash them until smooth 

3

Over a low heat, gradually add the butter to the potatoes whilst stirring

-   75g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), diced

4

Warm the milk in a separate pan and gradually add it into the potato mix, stirring until fully incorporated 

-   100ml of whole milk

5

Stir in the grated mozzarella and Gruyère cheese. Keep stirring until the mix is smooth, elastic and stretchy. Season to taste with salt and pepper

-   150g of [low-moisture mozzarella](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mozzarella-recipes), grated
-   80g of [Gruyère](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gruyere-recipes), grated
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

6

Next, prepare the béchamel sauce. In a small saucepan melt down the butter over a medium heat before adding in the flour and stirring to form a roux

-   15g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   15g of plain flour

7

Gradually whisk in the milk, making sure there are no lumps

-   150ml of whole milk

8

Cook the sauce over a medium heat, stirring frequently, until it thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon

10

Next, preheat the oven to 180°C fan

11

Lightly toast the bread on either side until golden, then spread a thin layer of Dijon mustard on both of the slices

-   4 slices of [white bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), sliced around 1 cm thick
-   2 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)

12

Spread a generous layer of the aligot over the mustard, then place a slice of ham over the top before covering with another slice of toasted bread

-   4 slices of [ham](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ham-recipes)

13

Spread a thin layer of béchamel over each of the sandwiches, then sprinkle Gruyère directly over the béchamel

-   50g of [Gruyère](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gruyere-recipes), grated

14

Bake each of the sandwiches on a baking tray lined with parchment paper for around 10 minutes, or until the cheese is golden brown and melted
