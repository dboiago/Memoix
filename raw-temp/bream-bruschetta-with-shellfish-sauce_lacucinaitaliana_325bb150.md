## Bruschetta topped with grilled bream makes for a hearty meal.

**Cooks in:** _50mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   18 OZ. of clams, purged
-   18 OZ. of mussels, cleaned
-   1 OZ. of bell pepper
-   2 WHOLE of bream
-   4 CHERRY of tomatoes
-   4 SLICES of rustic bread
-   1 SPRIG of thyme
-   1/2 BUNCH of basil, chopped
-   extra-virgin olive oil
-   salt

### Method

1

Wash the clams and mussels and place them in two separate pots with a little bit of oil, cover and let them open over medium heat. Shell them and filter their juice through a mesh strainer lined with cheesecloth. 

2

Cut the tomatoes into 1/4" slices, chop the bell pepper and thyme leaves. Sauté everything in a pan with 2 Tbsp. oil for 2-3 minutes over medium heat. Add the shellfish with a little of their cooking liquid. Let flavors meld for 1-2 minutes and season with chopped basil.

3

Fillet the bream and make three slices in the skin of the fillets. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium heat, grease the filets with oil and grill them flesh-side down; turn after 4 minutes and cook skin-side down for another 3 minutes, then add salt.

4

Toast the slices of bread, place the fish fillets on top with 2-3 spoonfuls of shellfish sauce and serve immediately.
