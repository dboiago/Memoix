Inspired by the Taiwanese comfort dish _lu rou fan_, this quick and tasty braised pork mince recipe makes for a perfect [midweek meal](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/midweek-meal-recipes). The pork is caramelised and then braised in soy sauce with [Chinese five-spice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chinese-five-spice-recipes). It's traditionally served on a bed of sticky rice with steamed mustard greens and an egg, but feel free to swap these out for whatever you fancy.

### Metric

### Imperial

-   500g of [pork mince](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-recipes)
-   1 [shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), minced
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 tsp [Chinese five-spice powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chinese-five-spice-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of rock sugar
-   100ml of chicken stock, hot
-   3 tbsp of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [dark soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 [potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), diced into 1cm cubes
-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), diced into 1cm cubes

#### To serve

-   4 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® eggs](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/)
-   100ml of [dark soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   50ml of mirin
-   [sticky rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes)
-   [black mustard leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes), or mustard greens

1

Begin by boiling the eggs for 6 minutes, then plunge into iced water to cool. Peel, then place in a bowl. Cover with the soy sauce and mirin and leave to cure whilst you make the braised pork

-   4 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® eggs](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/)
-   100ml of [dark soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   50ml of mirin

2

Place a high-sided frying pan over a medium–high heat and add the pork mince. Keep stirring until well-browned all over – you may have to pour out some of the fat which comes out of the meat so that the pork caramelises and doesn’t begin to stew

-   500g of [pork mince](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-recipes)

4

Place the rock sugar into a jug and add the hot stock. Stir until dissolved, then add to the pan along with the light and dark soy sauces

-   1 tbsp of rock sugar
-   100ml of chicken stock
-   3 tbsp of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [dark soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)

5

Add the cubed potato and carrot, stir to combine then turn the heat to low. Place a lid on the pan and leave to braise for 15–20 minutes or until the potato and carrot is cooked through

6

Serve with rice, wilted mustard greens and the soy-cured eggs
