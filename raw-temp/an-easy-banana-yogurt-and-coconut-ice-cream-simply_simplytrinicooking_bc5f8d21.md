### Ingredients:

-   2 peeled bananas
-   1/4 cup yogurt
-   1/4 cup coconut cream
-   1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
-   vanilla extract
-   1/2 cup of chopped soaked raisins

### Direction:

Wrap the bananas in plastic and place in the freezer. Unwrap frozen bananas and place in a food processor with the ingredients. You may now place the ingredients in an ice cream maker.

Note: Choose full bananas that have an even colour. If it has brown spots, it is getting overripe. The raisins may be soaked in water (or red wine or alcohol for a variation).

## Easy Banana Yogurt and Coconut Ice Cream

Course Dessert

Cuisine International /Fusion

Keyword banana ice cream, banana yogurt and coconut ice cream, healthy ice cream, ice cream

Prep Time 5 minutes

30 minutes

Total Time 35 minutes

Servings 2 persons

-   2 peeled bananas
-   ¼ cup yogurt (favourite)
-   ¼ cup coconut cream
-   ½ tsp ground cinnamon
-   vanilla extract
-   ½ cup chopped soaked raisins

-   Wrap the bananas in plastic and place in the freezer. Unwrap frozen bananas and place in a food processor with the ingredients.
    
-   You may now place the ingredients in an ice cream maker.
    
-   Keep chill in the refrigerator before serving.
    
-   Serve and garnish with cinnamon
    

1.  Choose full bananas that have an even colour. If it has brown spots, it is getting overripe.
2.   Raisins may be soaked in water (or even cherry wine or alcohol as a variation.)
