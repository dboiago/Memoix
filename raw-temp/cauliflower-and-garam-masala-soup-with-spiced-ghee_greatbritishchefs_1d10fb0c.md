[Cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes "Cauliflower") is perfect for soup because it blends to a beautiful silky texture. We’ve flavoured this one with [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/tags/ingredients/garam-masala "garam masala"), which adds warmth. Roasting the cauliflower before simmering it adds even more toasty depth to this soup, which is topped with a spiced ghee flavoured with curry leaves and a touch more garam masala.

First published in 2023

### Metric

### Imperial

-   1 [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes), broken into florets
-   1 tbsp of [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), diced
-   1 stick of [celery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), sliced
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   750ml of vegetable stock
-   neutral oil, for cooking
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste
-   single cream, enough to swirl in each bowl

1

Preheat the oven to 200ºC fan

2

Toss the cauliflower florets with a dash of oil, garam masala and some salt and roast for 15 minutes

-   1 [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes), broken into florets
-   1 tbsp of [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)

3

Add a dash of oil to a saucepan and cook the onions and celery, stirring regularly until softened and beginning to colour

-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), diced
-   1 stick of [celery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), sliced

4

Add the garlic and turmeric and cook for 1 minute, stirring, then add the stock and roasted cauliflower

-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   750ml of vegetable stock

5

Simmer until the cauliflower is very tender, then blend with a stick blender until very smooth

6

Season to taste with salt and pepper

8

Top each bowl of soup with a swirl of single cream and some spiced butter

-   single cream, enough to swirl in each bowl
