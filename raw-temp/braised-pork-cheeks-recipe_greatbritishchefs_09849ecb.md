This extraordinary [pork](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pork-recipes) cheeks recipe from [Graham Campbell](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/graham-campbell) features a memorable caraway jus and a sultana ragu, as well as good old fashioned mash. This recipe does require a sous vide machine though, so make sure you have one or are comfortable following the alternative method before attempting to make it. Visit our [sous vide collection](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sous-vide-recipes) for more inspiration.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Pork cheeks

-   1000g of [pork cheeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-recipes)
-   50ml of sherry vinegar
-   400ml of white wine
-   200ml of Worcestershire sauce
-   5g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   1/2 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [celery stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 bay leaf
-   10g of [caraway seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caraway-recipes)

#### Baby leeks

-   12 [baby leeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes)
-   50ml of white wine
-   100ml of white wine vinegar
-   50g of sugar

#### Mash

-   500g of [potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled
-   150g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), melted
-   15ml of milk
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

#### Sultana ragu

-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   1 knob of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   225g of sultanas
-   100ml of sherry vinegar
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   500ml of water
-   200ml of Worcestershire sauce

#### Caraway jus

-   1 [celery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), chopped
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), chopped
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   50ml of red wine vinegar
-   200ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   10g of [caraway seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caraway-recipes)
-   500ml of beef stock

-   Water bath
-   Sous vide bags

1

For the pork cheeks, sweat off the onion, celery, carrots and garlic in a large saucepan over a low-medium heat. Cook until soft but not coloured

-   1/2 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 [celery stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes)

2

Add the sherry vinegar, white wine and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a gentle boil and then simmer for 30 minutes

-   50ml of sherry vinegar
-   400ml of white wine
-   200ml of Worcestershire sauce

3

Add the bay leaf, 10g caraway seeds and a pinch of salt and simmer for a further 10 minutes

-   1 bay leaf
-   10g of [caraway seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caraway-recipes)
-   5g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

4

Pass the liquor through a fine strainer and set aside to cool

5

Trim any sinew and fat away from the pork cheeks and place into a large vac pack bag. Add the cooled liquor, seal and marinate for 24 hours

-   1000g of [pork cheeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-recipes)

6

Once marinated, seal in a vac pack and cook in a water bath at 80˚C for 6 hours

7

For the baby leeks, pour the 50ml white wine, 100ml white wine vinegar and 50g sugar in a pot, bring to the boil. Remove from the heat and allow to cool

-   50ml of white wine
-   100ml of white wine vinegar
-   50g of sugar

8

Trim the roots and dark green tips off the leeks. Bring the white wine mixture back to the boil for a second time and pour over the baby leeks, leave to cool in the liquor

-   12 [baby leeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes)

9

For the sultana ragu, place the finely diced onion, garlic and butter in a pot over a low-medium heat. Sweat until soft but not coloured

-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 knob of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

10

Add the sultanas, sherry vinegar, Worcestershire sauce and water. Bring to the boil and leave to simmer for 1/2 hour

-   225g of sultanas
-   100ml of sherry vinegar
-   500ml of water
-   200ml of Worcestershire sauce

11

For the creamy mash potato, add the potatoes to a saucepan, cover with water and add a pinch of salt. Bring to the boil, reduce to a simmer and cool for 12-16 minutes or until cooked through. Drain and allow to steam for 2 minutes

-   500g of [potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled

12

Mash the potatoes thoroughly or pass through a ricer or mouli until smooth. Fold through the butter and milk and season to taste. Add to a saucepan, cover and keep warm

-   150g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), melted
-   15ml of milk
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

13

For the caraway seed jus, add oil to a hot pan. Once the hoil is hot, add the onion, carrot, celery and garlic and leave to sweat

-   1 [celery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), chopped
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), chopped

14

Add red wine vinegar and reduce until almost dry. Repeat this process with the red wine before adding the beef stock and caraway seeds. Reduce down to 200ml and then pass through a fine strainer. Season to taste with salt and set aside

-   50ml of red wine vinegar
-   200ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   500ml of beef stock
-   10g of [caraway seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caraway-recipes)

15

Warm the pork cheeks in the reserved cooking liquor until very hot

16

Place 2 quenelles of mash onto each plate. Add a portion of the sultana ragu 3 pork cheeks - 2 on the mash and 1 on the sultana ragu. Finish with the baby leek and a drizzle of the caraway jus. Serve immediately
