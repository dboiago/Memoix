Shelly Waters, bartender of Root & Flower in Vail, Colorado, wanted to create a spirit-forward mezcal cocktail evocative of camping up in the mountains. While mezcal conjures up campfires, the pine liqueur and gin are meant to replicate the scent of evergreen trees.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. mezcal
-   1 oz. Amaro Nonino
-   1/2 oz. sweet vermouth
-   1/4 oz. gin
-   1/4 oz. Zirbenz Stone Pine Liqueur (or Braulio)
-   2 dashes smoked cinnamon bitters (or Angostura bitters)

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a mixing glass with ice and stir. Strain over a large ice cube into the glass. Garnish.
