This is a recipe for a [Japanese drink](https://www.chopstickchronicles.com/amazake/) called Amazake, which is a Japanese fermented beauty drink. It is naturally sweet, delicious, and healthy!

Plus, you can add various ingredients to it to make different flavours, like this Amazake lemonade!

For today’s recipe, I am adding fresh lemon juice to it to give it a yummy citrus flavour. It is so refreshing on a hot day but can also be served warm and drunk on a cold day!

## FAQ

Before I get to the recipe in the printable recipe card, here are some questions people often ask about how to make this drink.

### Can you use brown rice instead of white rice?

This Amazake lemonade is made from white rice but you could use brown rice instead to make it even healthier, but you will need to add more water to the recipe. This drink is a perfect healthy snack and tastes so delicious!

### Why is it called a beauty drink?

Amazake is called a beauty drink because it is so good for you that it supports your body’s beauty from the inside out.

### How long does Amazake last in the refrigerator?

Drink this up and keep making fresh batches. Amazake lemonade only lasts about a week in your refrigerator.

Amazake Lemonade is a delicious and healthy Japanese fermented beauty drink made from rice and rice mold! It’s a great refreshing snack!

Prep Time 1 minute

Cook Time 2 minutes

Total Time 3 minutes

Servings: 2

-   ▢ 400 ml Amazake
-   ▢ 2 tbs lemon juice
-   ▢ 2 tsp honey optional

-   Follow the instructions for making [**amazake**](https://www.chopstickchronicles.com/amazake/) (by clicking the link).
    
-   Pour Amazake in a cup or glass.
    
-   Add lemon juice and honey and stir.
    

For the original Amazake recipe, check [**_here_**](https://www.chopstickchronicles.com/amazake/)
