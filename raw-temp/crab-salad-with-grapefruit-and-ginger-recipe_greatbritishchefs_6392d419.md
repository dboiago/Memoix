Sweet, delicate [crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/crab-recipes) goes so well with fresh, citrusy flavours – something this delightful [salad](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salad-recipes) from Raymond Blanc showcases perfectly. Ideal for serving during spring and autumn, it's a showstopping starter which will put your culinary prowess to the test! Check out our guides for [preparing crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/how-to-cook/how-to-remove-meat-from-a-crab) and [segmenting grapefruit](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/how-to-cook/how-to-segment-grapefruit) if you need to brush up on your skills.

_'Way back in 1977 I started to play with flavours from elsewhere, and one of my first dishes was a crab and avocado salad with grapefruit and ginger. I remember it very well; the flavours were warm and spicy, while the sweetness of the crab was nicely complemented by the bitterness of the grapefruit and the spiciness of the ginger. But it was also the ugliest dish I have ever made as the salad was finished off with a ball of avocado topped with a slice of black olive – it looked like a monstrous black and green eye. Many better versions followed featuring mango and grapefruit. That is the way dishes progress, to always look at a dish with curiosity._'
