![carrot margarita](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/pedro-rabbit-vertical-corrected-crdt-arlene-ibarra.jpg)

Drink your veggies.

Riffing on the [classic Margarita](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe-margarita/), this cocktail gets a refreshingly earthy flavor thanks to the addition of fresh carrot juice.

## Preparation

Shake the first 3 ingredients together in a shaker tin with ice. Strain into a Collins glass over fresh ice. Top with carrot juice to taste. Stir to incorporate. Garnish.
