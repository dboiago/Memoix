To begin, peel and chop into chunks, the acorn squash and the beets. Other squash such as butternut would work well too. Put them into separate bowls so the beets don't bleed onto the squash. Drizzle over the olive oil, salt, and pepper, and toss well to combine. Spread the acorn squash on a baking sheet, and the beets on another, and roast away until they are browned and fork tender. About 30 minutes, stopping to flip halfway through.

![Beet, Squash & Kale Autumn Bowl! Sweet roasted beets, tender squash, and zesty kale on a bed of brown rice, all drizzled with maple mustard dressing. Vegan and gluten free Buddha bowl. #itdoesnttastelikechicken](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Autumn-Bowl-roasted-veggies-grain-vegan-recipe-01.jpg)

In the meantime, de-stem and chop the kale. Add it to a large bowl and squeeze the lemon overtop. Now get in there with your hands to really massage the kale and spread the lemon juice. This helps the kale break down and become tender. Once massaged you can let it rest for up to 2 hours. The longer it rests, the more tender it will get.

Assemble your bowls by adding some of each veggie, and the rice to a bowl. Sprinkle with sliced almonds, and then drizzle the maple mustard dressing to taste.

Servings: 4

#### For the beets:

-   ▢ 4 medium beets, , peeled and chopped (about 2 ½ Cups)
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon olive oil
-   ▢ ¼ teaspoon salt
-   ▢ ⅛ teaspoon black pepper

#### For the squash:

-   ▢ 1 acorn squash, , peeled and cubed
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon olive oil
-   ▢ ¼ teaspoon salt
-   ▢ ⅛ teaspoon black pepper

#### For the kale:

-   ▢ 1 bunch of kale, , de-stemmed and shredded
-   ▢ ½ lemon, , juiced

-   Preheat your oven to 425F (220C).
    
-   Add the beets ingredients to a bowl and toss to combine, then spread on a baking sheet. Add the squash ingredients to another bowl and toss to combine, and spread on a second baking sheet. (You want to keep the veggies separate or the beets will bleed onto the squash). Roast for 30 to 35 minutes until they are golden and fork tender, stopping halfway through to give them a stir.
    
-   For the kale, add it to a bowl with them lemon, and then use your hands to massage the kale. This helps it break down and get tender. Set it aside for up to 2 hours. The longer it sits the more tender it will get.
    
-   To assemble the bowls, add some of each veggie and some rice to a bowl. Drizzle with the maple mustard dressing to taste, and sprinkle some almond slices on top. Bon Appetegan!
