Move over Mimosa—there’s a new drink at the brunch table.

Move over mimosa, this [Breakfast Bramble](http://craftandcocktails.co/2016/07/18/breakfast-bramble-cocktail/) might just be the perfect brunch cocktail. Ashley Rose Conway, from [Craft+Cocktails](http://craftandcocktails.co/), combines two breakfast table staples—jam and [yogurt](https://imbibemagazine.com/elements-yogurt/)—to a create a cocktail that’s lightly creamy and perfectly sweet and tangy.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. gin
-   3/4 oz. crème de mûre (blackberry liqueur)
-   1 1/2 Tbsp. stone fruit jam (Conway uses apricot rosemary jam from The Jam Lab)
-   1 Tbsp. plain Greek yogurt
-   1/2 oz. fresh lemon juice

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a shaker with ice. Shake and strain into a glass with fresh crushed ice and garnish.
