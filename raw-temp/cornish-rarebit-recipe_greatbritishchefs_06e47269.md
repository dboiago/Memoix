[Nathan Outlaw](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/nathan-outlaw)'s [rarebit](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rarebit-recipes) recipe is a wonderful take on traditional cheese on toast. Use your favourite [cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cheese-recipes) to create a tangy mixture with beer and mustard.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Grate the cheddar and place in a saucepan with the beer over a medium heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the cheese melts and the mixture starts to bubble

-   120g of [mature cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes)
-   60ml of [lager beer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beer-recipes)

2

Stir in the flour, breadcrumbs, and mustard and continue cooking until the mixture comes cleanly away from the sides of the pan and forms a ball. Remove from the heat and leave to cool

-   30g of plain flour
-   30g of breadcrumbs
-   1 tbsp of [English mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)

3

Once cool, beat in the egg yolks until you have a smooth mixture, then the rarebit is ready to use. Spread across some slices of bread and pop under the grill until bubbling. Cut into soldiers and serve immediately

-   3 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 [loaf of bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
