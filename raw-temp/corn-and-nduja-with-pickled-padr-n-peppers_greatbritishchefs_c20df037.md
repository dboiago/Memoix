Dan Wilson serves up a delicious mix of charred [sweetcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/sweetcorn-recipes) and fiery ['nduja](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/nduja-recipes), served with pickled Padrón peppers and a dollop of crème fraîche for some relief from the sweet, smoky flavours on the plate. A [brunch](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brunch-recipes) dish for those who like their day to start with a bracing plate of powerful flavours.

First published in 2017

I used to make this with the brilliant Greek woman who first taught me how to cook. We used to use frozen corn though and fry it up in a dry pan. Now, with a barbecue and British corn we burn it on the fire and then strip it. It’s simple and f\*\*\*\*\*g tasty. Bootleg food at its best.

1

Begin by making the pickled padron peppers. Bring all the ingredients (except the peppers) to the boil and allow to roughly boil for 5 minutes, then reduce to a simmer for 10 minutes

-   1 pinch of [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   200ml of red wine vinegar
-   200ml of water
-   100g of brown sugar

2

Place the peppers (washed) in a sterilised jar. Tip over the pickling liquid, seal and set aside for at least 2 hours

-   200g of [Padrón peppers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes)

3

Light your barbecue and burn the corn until starting to blacken. Remove from the heat, then slice off the kernels

-   3 [sweetcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes)

4

Over a high heat cook down the 'nduja in a pan, then add the corn kernels and half the juice of a lime. Mix with a wooden spoon to incorporate the mess

-   50g of ['nduja](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sausage-recipes)
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)

5

Place a dollop of crème fraîche in the middle of each plate and cover with the corn and 'nduja. Make a little well in the centre and add an egg yolk and a generous pinch of salt

-   50g of crème fraiche
-   2 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

6

Cover in pickled peppers and serve with a lime wedge
