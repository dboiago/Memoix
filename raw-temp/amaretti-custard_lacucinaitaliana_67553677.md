## In a food processor, blend the bread and amaretti cookies into crumbs. In a bowl, beat the eggs and egg yolks with 1/2 cup of sugar, the cocoa powder,...

**Cooks in:** _1h 20mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _12_

### List of Ingredients

-   2 C. of milk
-   1 C. of sugar
-   2 OZ. of stale bread
-   1.5 OZ. of amaretti cookies
-   1/2 C. of unsweetened cocoa powder
-   2 of eggs
-   2 of egg yolks
-   Vanilla bean pod
-   1 of Lemon

### Method

1

In a food processor, blend the bread and amaretti cookies into crumbs. In a bowl, beat the eggs and egg yolks with 1/2 cup of sugar, the cocoa powder, the bread and cookie crumbs, and the seeds from half a vanilla bean pod; then add in the milk.

2

In a small saucepan, melt the rest of the sugar together with the juice of half a lemon until it begins to caramelize. Pour this caramel into 12 flan molds, then fill them with the cacao mixture; place them in a baking pan with high edges on top of a sheet of parchment paper.

3

Fill the baking pan with hot water so that the molds are immersed about 2/3 of the way; bake at 300°F for about 45 minutes. Take them out of the oven and remove them from the bain-marie; let them cool completely before removing them from the molds. Serve with dollops of whipped cream, if desired.
