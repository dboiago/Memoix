Luke Holder's barbecue sous vide [chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes) recipe is great for summer, with the thighs cooked to a perfect temperature in a water bath before finishing on the barbecue for a smoky finish and wonderful texture. You can buy boneless [thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes) but it is simple to debone them yourself.

First published in 2015

-   Barbecue
-   Vacuum bags
-   Water bath
-   Bar sealer

1

Preheat a water bath to 70°C

2

To prepare the chicken thighs, run the tip of a sharp paring knife along the thigh bone. To release the bone, carefully run the blade around and under the bone, ensuring you remove all of the cartilage

-   400g of [chicken thigh](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), bones removed

3

Place in a vacuum bag with the thyme, oil, salt and lemon zest and seal with a bar sealer. Cook for 35 minutes in the water bath

-   [lemon thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), 1 bunch
-   10ml of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 slice of [lemon zest](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

4

While the chicken is cooking, barbecue the sweetcorn on a hot, dry barbecue or under a grill until very lightly blackened. Remove from the heat and place in a bowl. Cover with cling film and leave to steam for 10-15 minutes

-   500g of [sweetcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes), on cob, husk removed

5

Using a sharp knife, remove the corn kernels from the cob (being careful not to cut too close to the cob) and set aside in a bowl. Cook the chorizo in a pan until the oils leach out, then add the garlic and corn

-   100g of [chorizo sausage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), broken or chopped in small pieces
-   5g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)

6

Add the red wine vinegar and paprika and reduce until dry. Finally, remove from the heat and stir in the coriander and mint

-   2g of [paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   10g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   5g of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes)
-   30g of red wine vinegar

7

At this point the chicken should be cooked. Take out of the water bath, cut open the bag and remove the chicken, making sure to retain the juices in the bag

8

To finish the chicken, cook on a hot barbecue or under a grill to achieve a dark golden colour on both sides, approximately 2-3 minutes. Serve on top of the chorizo and sweetcorn salsa with a drizzle of the cooking juices
