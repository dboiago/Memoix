A classic recipe for modern palates.

Scotch can sometimes prove challenging in a cocktail context, but one trick bartenders have discovered is to match scotch’s character with that of another spirit, giving it a good dancing partner in the glass. Featured in Harry McElhone’s 1920s London bar guide, Cameron’s Kick is a classic recipe that pairs whiskies from Scotland and Ireland and uses orgeat and lemon to brighten the mix.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. blended Scotch whisky
-   1 oz. Irish whiskey
-   1⁄2 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1⁄2 oz. orgeat

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients with ice to chill, strain into a chilled glass, then garnish.
