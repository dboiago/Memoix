### List of Ingredients

-   7 OZ. of milk chocolate
-   1/3 LB. of dark chocolate, plus extra
-   1/2 C. of fresh milk
-   1/3 C. of peanut butter
-   1/3 C. of condensed milk
-   1/2 C. of salted peanuts
-   2/3 STICK of butter

### Method

1

Chop the milk chocolate and place it in a bowl. Heat the condensed milk together with the fresh milk and 2 tablespoons of butter, then pour it over the chocolate while still hot. Add the peanut butter and mix until smooth.

2

Add the salted peanuts and pour the mixture in a square baking pan (6.5”, 1” deep) lined with parchment paper on the bottom and sides. Level it off with a spatula. Chop the dark chocolate and melt it in a double boiler.

3

As soon as it has melted, add the remaining butter and combine. Pour the dark chocolate into the baking pan on top of the chocolate peanut mixture, forming an 1/8-inch thick later. Refrigerate for at least 12 hours.

4

Cut the fudge into cubes with a sharp smooth knife. Garnish to taste with candied peanuts, held down with drops of melted dark chocolate.
