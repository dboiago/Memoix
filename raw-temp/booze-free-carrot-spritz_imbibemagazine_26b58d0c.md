Carrot juice folds perfectly into a medley of ginger and lemon.

For a fresh way to drink your veggies this season, try this booze-free carrot spritz. The recipe offers a delicious medley of ginger, lemon and carrot juice.

## Ingredients

-   3 oz. fresh carrot juice
-   1 oz. ginger syrup
-   ¾ oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1 drop vanilla extract
-   1½ oz. ginger beer (Mlynarczyk uses Fever-Tree)

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients except the ginger beer to a shaker and shake hard for 5 seconds with ice. Pour the ginger beer into the shaker, then strain the contents into a glass over fresh ice.
