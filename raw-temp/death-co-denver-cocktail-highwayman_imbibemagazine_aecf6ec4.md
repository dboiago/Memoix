A sultry mix of smoke, fruit, and spice.

Death & Co. beverage director Tyson Buhler combines flavors of smoke, fruit and spice in this stunning stirred cocktail.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/4 oz. bourbon
-   1/2 oz. Scotch
-   1/4 oz. Jamaican rum
-   2 tsp. coffee liqueur
-   1 tsp. passion fruit liqueur

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a mixing glass with ice and stir to chill. Strain into an Old Fashioned glass over a single ice cube. Garnish.
