## Fresh baby spinach, fennel and orange slices add extra color, crispness, and flavor to this cuttlefish salad.

**Cooks in:** _30mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 LB. of cuttlefish, cleaned
-   4 OZ. of fennel (around 1/2 of a bulb)
-   2 OZ. of baby spinach
-   2 of oranges
-   1 of apple
-   parsley
-   shallot
-   wasabi
-   dry white wine
-   extra-virgin olive oil

### Method

1

Remove the tentacles from the cuttlefish and place them in a saucepan with a sprig of parsley, 2 Tbsp. oil, and 2 ladlefuls of water; cook over minimum heat with the lid on for 10-12 minutes. Add the cuttlefish bodies and cook for another 10 minutes. Turn off the heat and let the cuttlefish rest in their water. 

2

Slice the fennel thinly and place in water. Grate the zest from the oranges, then remove the peel and white pith, and cut the flesh into slices.

3

Peel the apple and cut it into quarters. Cook in a saucepan with half a sliced shallot, a splash of wine, and a ladleful of water for 10 minutes; add wasabi to taste and three pieces of orange peel. Remove the orange peels and blend the rest to obtain a sauce.

4

Drain the cuttlefish and the fennel. Serve the cuttlefish with the orange and fennel slices, and spinach; dress with the sauce and top with grated orange zest.
