**RECIPE:** John Peterson for Little Bird, Portland, OR

![](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/American-2BTrilogy-2B_little-2Bbird_edited.jpg)

Inspired by a cocktail originally poured at NYC’s [Little Branch](https://www.facebook.com/pages/Little-Branch/41435210381), winter’s chill is no match for this whiskey-rich tipple.

1 oz. applejack  
1 oz. rye whiskey  
½ oz. maple simple syrup (see below)  
2 dashes orange bitters  
Tools: mixing glass, barspoon  
Glass: Old Fashioned  
Garnish: orange peel

Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass and stir. Pour into an Old Fashioned glass over 3 medium-sized ice cubes. Garnish.

**Maple Simple Syrup** Combine ½ cup of water and 1 cup of grade B maple syrup in a small saucepan over medium heat and stir until the syrup and water have melted together. Remove from heat and reserve until use.
