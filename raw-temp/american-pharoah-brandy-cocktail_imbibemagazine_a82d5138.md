Brandy, sherry, strawberry, and mint make for the perfect warm-weather sipper.

This brandy cocktail from acclaimed bartender Jeff Bell combines the spirit with sherry, strawberry, and mint for the perfect warm-weather sipper.

## Ingredients

-   1 3/4 oz. brandy
-   1/4 oz. Lustau East India sherry
-   1/4 oz. strawberry syrup
-   6 mint sprigs

## Preparation

Muddle the mint with the strawberry syrup in a chilled julep cup, then add the remaining ingredients and fill with crushed ice. Swizzle until the cup frosts over, then top with crushed ice. Garnish.

**Strawberry Syrup**Make a 1:1 [simple syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/) (16 oz. of granulated sugar and 16 oz. of water). Combine 16 oz. of washed and de-stemmed fresh strawberries with 16 oz. of the simple syrup in an immersion blender. Bottle and refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.
