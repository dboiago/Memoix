These cute little toasts are ideal for serving with a glass of bubbly before a meal or as drinks party nibbles. The quantities in this [crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/crab-recipes) dip are easily increased for larger numbers but go easy on the [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cayenne-pepper-recipes) – a little goes a very long way. Trendy micro pea shoots, used here as a garnish, are readily available online, or you can garnish this canapé from [Adam Gray](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/adam-gray) with small sprigs of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/thyme-recipes) instead.

First published in 2015

-   Fine sieve

1

To make the crab dip, squeeze the grapefruit juice into a saucepan and bring to the boil. Simmer gently until reduced by half

-   1 [pink grapefruit](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grapefruit-recipes)

2

Remove from the heat and pass through a fine sieve, then leave to cool to room temperature

4

Add the reduced pink grapefruit juice to taste and mix in thoroughly. Check the seasoning, cover and refrigerate

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

5

Preheat the oven 180˚C/Gas mark 4. Slice the wholemeal rolls into thin slices and arrange on a baking tray. Drizzle with the rapeseed oil and place another baking tray on top

-   100ml of rapeseed oil
-   4 [wholemeal bread rolls](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)

6

Transfer to the oven and cook for 15-18 minutes or until golden brown. Remove the sliced bread from the trays and leave to cool to room temperature

7

To serve, place the dip alongside the toasts on a board. Add a few pink grapefruit segments and garnish with the micro pea shoots

-   8 [micro pea shoots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes)
-   1 [pink grapefruit](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grapefruit-recipes), segmented, to serve
