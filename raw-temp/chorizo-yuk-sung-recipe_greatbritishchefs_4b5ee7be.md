-   Makes approx. 20 wraps

[Stuart Collins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/stuart-collins "Stuart Collins") takes inspiration from both China and [Spain](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spanish-recipes "Spanish") to create this delicious fusion snack. Yuk sung is a [Chinese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chinese-recipes "Chinese") recipe of fried mince flavoured with ginger, garlic and soy sauce and served in easy-to-eat lettuce wraps. The addition of spicy Spanish [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes "Chorizo") makes these little bites even more moreish. You could can line the lettuce leaves with some cold, cooked [noodles](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/noodle-recipes "Noodle") instead of the crispy rice if you prefer.

#### Chorizo yuk sung

-   200g of [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), picante or regular, finely diced
-   300g of [pork mince](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-recipes)
-   75g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and finely diced (brunoise)
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   20g of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely sliced into matchsticks
-   200g of [button mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes), finely sliced
-   35g of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   25g of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   10g of corn starch, mixed with 40g water
-   75g of [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced

#### To serve

-   50g of [wild rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes)
-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   2 [little gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes), separated into 20 leaves and washed
-   1/2 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely sliced

1

For the wild rice, heat up a small pan of oil to around 200ºC. Add the rice and wait until it puffs up and turns slightly golden. Use a slotted spoon to remove the rice, drain on kitchen paper and lightly season with salt

-   50g of [wild rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes)
-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Place a frying pan over a medium heat and add the diced chorizo. Cook until the fat has rendered out and the chorizo is nicely golden, then remove the chorizo from the pan leaving the bright orange oil behind (reserve the cooked chorizo)

-   200g of [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), picante or regular, finely diced

3

Add the pork mince to the pan and cook for a few minutes in the chorizo oil until almost cooked. Use a slotted spoon to remove the mince from the pan and set it aside with the chorizo. Again, try to leave as much of the chorizo oil in the pan as possible

-   300g of [pork mince](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-recipes)

4

Return the pan with the oil to the heat and add the diced carrot. Sweat for a couple of minutes, then add the garlic and ginger. Cook for another 2 minutes then add the mushrooms

-   75g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and finely diced (brunoise)
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   20g of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely sliced into matchsticks
-   200g of [button mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes), finely sliced

5

Once the mushrooms have softened, add the chorizo and pork mince back into the pan and turn up the heat. Add the light soy sauce with the honey and bring to the boil, then add the cornstarch-water mixture and cook out for a minute until the sauce has thickened slightly

-   35g of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   25g of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   10g of corn starch, mixed with 40g water

6

Remove from the heat and stir the spring onions through the mixture (reserving a small handful for garnish)

-   75g of [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced

7

Lay out the little gem leaves onto serving plates and add a spoonful of the crispy puffed rice onto each, followed by a spoonful of the mince mixture. Top with the sliced chillies, more spring onion and a final sprinkle of the puffed rice and serve immediately

-   1/2 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 handful of [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   [puffed rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes)
