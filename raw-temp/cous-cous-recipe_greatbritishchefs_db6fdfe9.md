This fresh and healthy couscous recipe goes brilliantly with any number of main course meals –marinated [lamb](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lamb-recipes), grilled [chicken breast](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-breast-recipes), falafel wraps and roasted vegetables just a few examples. Paul Heathcote's recipe for this [Middle Eastern](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/middle-eastern-recipes) favourite is quick and easy to follow, making it a great midweek dinner or [lunch](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lunch-recipes) and perfect for [picnic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/picnic-recipes) or [barbecue sides](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/barbecue-sides-recipes).

First published in 2015

### Imperial

-   125g of [couscous](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/couscous-recipes)
-   50g of [fresh peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes)
-   25g of [sweetcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes)
-   25g of [whole almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1/2 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   25g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   6 [green olives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-olive-recipes)
-   4 [fresh mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes)
-   50g of [broad beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broad-bean-recipes)
-   25g of [raisins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raisin-recipes)
-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground

1

Check the maker’s recipe for couscous and measure out the water for 125g of cous cous (recommendations vary slightly although it is normally around equal quantities).

2

Prepare the vegetables for the couscous. Start with the cooking and shelling the broad beans. Cook the sweetcorn and peas until tender

-   0g of [fresh peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes)
-   50g of [broad beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broad-bean-recipes)
-   25g of [sweetcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes)

3

Chop the onions and finely crush the garlic. Add them both to a thick-bottomed pan with the butter and gently fry them for a couple of minutes

-   1/2 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   25g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

4

Add the cooked peas, beans and corn to the onions and garlic. Chop the olives and add them to the mixture along with the raisins and the whole almonds

-   25g of [whole almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   25g of [raisins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raisin-recipes)
-   6 [green olives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-olive-recipes)

5

Add the water, bring to the boil and remove from the heat

6

Chop the mint and add along with the couscous. Give a good stir before placing a lid on top and leave for 3 minutes

-   4 [fresh mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes)
-   125g of [couscous](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/couscous-recipes)

7

The cous cous will now be cooked, season with sea salt and freshly milled pepper.

-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
