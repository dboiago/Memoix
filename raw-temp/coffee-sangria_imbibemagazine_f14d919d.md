**RECIPE:** _Alan Gomez, Commodity Coffee, Long Beach, California  
(This coffee shop has permanently closed.)_

![coffee sangria](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/commodity-coffee-sangria-horizontal.jpg)

Inspired by classic sangria, Commodity Coffee owner Alan Gomez created this alcohol-free coffee sangria by infusing orange, strawberries and grapes into a batch of cold brew. Gomez says the recipe is “very forgiving,” so feel free to adjust the amounts (or types) of fruit.

## Ingredients

-   1 lb. ground coffee
-   1 gallon water
-   1 whole orange, peeled and cut into segments
-   1 handful fresh strawberries
-   1 handful fresh red grapes
-   [simple syrup (1:1), to taste](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)

## Preparation

Start by brewing about a gallon of cold-brew coffee. Combine the coffee and water in the Toddy (or whichever brew method suits you) and let sit for 10-12 hours. Strain the coffee through the filter and decant into a jug. Set aside.

When the coffee is strained and ready, zest the orange into the batch, then add all the fruit into the finished coffee and give it a stir. Let it sit for about 4-6 hours, then strain the coffee and discard the fruit. Taste, then add simple syrup to taste.
