A citrusy riff on the Lion’s Tail.

This twist on the classic [Lion’s Tail](https://imbibemagazine.com/lions-tail-recipe/) connects the toasty flavors of allspice and whiskey with lemon juice and orange bitters.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. bourbon
-   ¾ oz. fresh lemon juice
-   ½ oz. honey syrup
-   ¼ oz. St. Elizabeth's Allspice Dram
-   2 dashes orange bitters

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients into a shaker and shake for at least 15 seconds. Strain over fresh ice in a rocks glass. Garnish.
