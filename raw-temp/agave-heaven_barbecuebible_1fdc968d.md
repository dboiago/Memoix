### [Drinks and Cocktails](https://barbecuebible.com/recipe-type/drinks-and-cocktails/)

  

This smoky twist on a margarita is the house specialty at the historic Hotel Fauchere in Milford, Pennsylvania. The smoke comes from the mescal (made with fire-roasted cactus hearts). The brand the hotel uses is Sombra. Strega is an herb-flavored liqueur from Italy; agave syrup is a sweetener made from agave cactus and widely available in supermarkets.

### Agave Heaven

## Recipe Notes

-   **Yield:** Serves 1 and can be multiplied as desired

## Ingredients

-   1 ounce mescal (preferably Sombra)
-   1 ounce white tequila (preferably Two Fingers)
-   1 ounce freshly squeezed lime juice
-   1/2 ounce (1 tablespoon) Strega
-   1/2 ounce (1 tablespoon) agave syrup
-   Celery bitters or bitters of choice

## Recipe Steps

**Step 1:** Chill a cocktail glass in the freezer or with ice.

**Step 2:** Combine the mescal, tequila, lime juice, Strega, and agave syrup in a bar shaker with 1 cup ice cubes. Shake hard and strain into a chilled cocktail glass. Add a dash or two of celery bitters and serve.

## Recipe Tips

One good brand of Celery bitters is Frye Brothers.
