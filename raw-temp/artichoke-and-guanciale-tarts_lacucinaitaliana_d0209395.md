**Cooks in:** _1h 45mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 2/3 C. of flour
-   2/3 C. of Greek yogurt
-   1 1/3 STICK of cold butter
-   1/4 LB. of parsley, chopped
-   16 of slices guanciale (or pancetta)
-   2 TBSP. of milk
-   1 1/2 TBSP. of potato starch
-   Salt
-   1/3 C. of grated Grana Padano cheese
-   5 of hardboiled egg yolks
-   4 of artichokes
-   2 of lemons
-   Dry white wine

### Method

1

Press the egg yolks through a sieve. Mix the flour with the starch, butter, Grana Padano cheese, and 1 1/4 teaspoons of salt; add the egg yolks and milk, then continue to mix until you obtain a shortbread-type crust. Cover and let it rest in the refrigerator for 1 hour.

2

Trim the artichokes and cut them into four wedges; remove the inner fuzzy choke and submerge them in water mixed with the juice of 1 lemon. Drain them and then boil them for 10 minutes in salted water with 1/2 cup of wine.

3

Drain the artichokes and dry them on a paper towel; wrap each wedge in one slice of guanciale and cook them in a pan until the guanciale becomes crispy. Season the yogurt with the parsley and a pinch of salt, mixing until it becomes creamy. 

4

Roll out 1/3 of the dough until it is about 1/8-inch thick; cut out 16 ovals with a cookie cutter (with a maximum diameter of 2.5 inches).

5

Roll out the rest of the dough until it is about 1/4-inch thick; with the same cookie cutter, cut out rings with edges just under 1/2-inch thick: adhere the rings to the oval bases, pressing down gently. 

6

Place the tarts on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake at 320°F for 15 minutes. Remove from the oven and let cool.

7

Fill the tarts with the parsley cream and top with a guanciale-wrapped artichoke wedge.
