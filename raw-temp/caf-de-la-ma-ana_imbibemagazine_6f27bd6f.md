Tequila-spiked coffee? You betcha!

Tequila-spiked coffee? You betcha! This Café de la Mañana coffee cocktail from Quentin Ertel, owner of [The Saint Social Club](http://www.thesaintsocialclub.com/) in Seattle, is perfect for livening up an ordinary cup of joe.

## Ingredients

-   1 ½ oz. reposado tequila
-   ½ oz. Kahlua coffee liqueur
-   8 oz. fresh-brewed coffee
-   Fresh agave nectar whipped cream

## Preparation

Add the tequila and Kahlua to a glass and top with fresh, hot coffee. Fold the whipped cream onto top of drink. Dust with freshly grated nutmeg.

Agave Nectar Whipped Cream: Mix 1 cup heavy cream, 2 Tbsp. agave nectar, and 1 tsp. vanilla extract together in a bowl until firm.
