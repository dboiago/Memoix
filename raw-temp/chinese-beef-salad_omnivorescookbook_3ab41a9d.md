## How to serve Chinese beef salad – three ways

The beauty of this salad is, you can serve it many different ways.

Traditionally, Chinese beef salad is served as a cold appetizer during a multi-course meal. It pairs very well with a can of cold beer and some [spicy peanuts](https://omnivorescookbook.com/szechuan-spicy-peanuts/). 

However, I’ve found that it works very well on a bed of freshly steamed rice, as a one bowl meal. In this case, I usually add a soft-boiled egg on my rice bowl, with an extra spoonful of braising liquid from the [braised beef shank](https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-braised-beef-shank/). If you’ve already made my [braised beef shank](https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-braised-beef-shank/), definitely use the broth to marinate some boiled eggs. After two days or so, the eggs will become deeply flavorful, so that you can use them as a topping for rice and noodle bowls, or as a super-umami snack.

The other way to use Chinese beef salad is to make a sandwich from it. Whether you prefer the Taiwanese [Gua Bao](https://omnivorescookbook.com/gua-bao/) or Northern Chinese [Rou Jia Mo](https://omnivorescookbook.com/recipes/authentic-pork-belly-bun), the braised beef shank can be used to replace the pork filling in both of these recipes! If you’re making Gua Bao, try to add some mayonnaise and sriracha and it will work wonders. For Rou Jia Mo, fresh [homemade flatbread](https://omnivorescookbook.com/how-to-make-flatbread/) is the gold standard. But you can totally use pita bread instead.

A super easy Chinese beef salad you can put together in 10 minutes with no cooking required! Serve it as an appetizer for a multi-course meal, top it on steamed rice for a one-bowl lunch, or make a sandwich with it using pita bread. {Gluten-Free adaptable}

To make the dish gluten-free, use tamari to replace light soy sauce and use rice vinegar instead of Chinkiang vinegar.

-   1 cup [homemade braised beef shank](https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-braised-beef-shank/) , thinly sliced (or roast beef deli meat)
-   1 stalk Chinese celery , thinly sliced (or 1/2 stalk regular celery, diced)
-   1 green onion , thinly sliced
-   2 birds eye chilis , sliced (Optional)
-   1/4 cup cilantro , chopped
-   1 tablespoon peanuts , chopped

#### Dressing

-   2 to 3 tablespoons [homemade chili oil](https://omnivorescookbook.com/how-to-make-chili-oil/)
-   2 teaspoons [light soy sauce](https://omnivorescookbook.com/light-soy-sauce)
-   1 teaspoon [Chinkiang vinegar](https://omnivorescookbook.com/rice-vinegar)
-   1 tablespoon [broth from homemade braised beef shank](https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-braised-beef-shank/) , heated up in the microwave (Optional) (\*Footnote 1)

-   Combine the dressing ingredients in a small bowl, stir, and set aside.
    
-   Add to a bowl the beef shank, celery, green onion, chilis, and half of the cilantro and peanuts. Drizzle the dressing onto it and gently toss.
    
-   Plate the salad, drizzle with some extra chili oil if desired, and garnish with the remaining cilantro and peanuts.
    

[Ingredient Substitution Guide](https://omnivorescookbook.com/ingredient-substitutions-for-chinese-cooking/)

1.  If braising stock isn’t available, add another teaspoon of light soy sauce and 1/8 teaspoon sugar.
