Peppery celeriac and sour blue [cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cheese-recipes) are a match made in heaven and combine in this delicious celeriac [soup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/seasonal-soup-recipes) recipe from [Marcello Tully](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/marcello-tully). Serve this soup as a [starter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/starter-recipes) over the festive period - it's a great way to make the most of leftover Stilton.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   800g of [celeriac](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celeriac-recipes), trimmed and cut into small chunks
-   50ml of sunflower oil
-   1000ml of water
-   300g of [blue cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blue-cheese-recipes)
-   500ml of double cream
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   20g of caster sugar
-   5g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   100ml of white wine
-   200g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced

-   Blender

1

Heat the oil in a heavy based saucepan and add the onions, allowing to sweat (but not brown) for 3-4 minutes

-   200g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   50ml of sunflower oil

2

Add the celeriac chunks and water and leave to gently simmer for 40 minutes

-   800g of [celeriac](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celeriac-recipes)
-   1000ml of water

3

After 40 minutes, or when the celeriac has softened, blitz the soup in a liquidiser or food blender until smooth, with no lumps. Season to taste with sugar and salt

-   20g of caster sugar

4

Return to a clean pan and bring to the boil. Add the blue cheese pieces, salt and the double cream. Stir gently and check seasoning

-   300g of [blue cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blue-cheese-recipes)
-   5g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   500ml of double cream

5

Add the finely grated garlic and white wine and bring back to the boil. Distribute into bowls and serve immediately

-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   100ml of white wine
