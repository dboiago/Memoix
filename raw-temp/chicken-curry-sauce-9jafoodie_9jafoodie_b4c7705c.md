## Chicken Curry Sauce

[![chicken - curry - sauce - Nigerian - 9jafoodie - gravy ](https://www.9jafoodie.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/chicken-curry-sauce-Nigerian-_-9jafoodie-600x398.jpeg "chicken - curry - sauce ")](https://www.9jafoodie.com/chicken-curry-sauce/chicken-curry-sauce-nigerian-_-9jafoodie/)

Here is a great simple that require minimal cooking effort. ditch your regular red stew for this, I promise you it’s well worth it. Serve it with some white rice and enjoy.

Ingredients:

![Chicken Curry\_Ing](https://www.9jafoodie.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/Chicken-Curry_Ing.jpg)

-   8- pieces Chicken – Leg and Thigh (Cut into chunks)
-   3 – cubes maggi
-   Salt – to taste
-   4 – Tablespoons Cooking oil
-   3 – cups cubed Sweet Potatoes or Yam
-   2 – (400ml) Cans Coconut cream (milk)
-   2-3 cups Vegetables of choice
-   1 – Tablespoon curry powder
-   1 – Teaspoon Turmeric powder
-   1 – Scotch Bonnet pepper (Atarodo) – Minced

Direction

-   Season the cut chicken with salt and 1 cube maggi – Set aside
-   Heat a large skillet over high heat. Add in the oil. Add in the seasoned chicken and sear on all sides until brown.

![Chicken Curry \_1](https://www.9jafoodie.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/Chicken-Curry-_1.jpg)

-   Add in all left over ingredients with the chicken. Stir to combine

![Chicken Curry\_4](https://www.9jafoodie.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/Chicken-Curry_4.jpg)

-   Simmer on medium heat until chicken is cooked through and yam is tender. Adjust with water for thickness

![Chicken Curry\_5](https://www.9jafoodie.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/Chicken-Curry_5.jpg)

Serve with rice
