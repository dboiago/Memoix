##### 

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 10 minutes

Total: 15 minutes

Servings: 4 (as a side)

[PRINT](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wprm_print/creamy-coconut-garlic-mushrooms) [PIN](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/create/bookmarklet/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fitdoesnttastelikechicken.com%2Fcreamy-coconut-garlic-mushrooms%2F&media=https%3A%2F%2Fitdoesnttastelikechicken.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2015%2F01%2Fcreamy-coconut-garlic-mushrooms.jpg&description=Creamy+Coconut+Garlic+Mushrooms&is_video=false) [COMMENT](#commentform)

-   ▢ 1 tablespoon [coconut oil](https://amzn.to/3Myajtn), (or other light oil)
-   ▢ 16 oz mushrooms,, sliced
-   ▢ 4 cloves garlic,, minced
-   ▢ ⅓ cup [full-fat coconut milk](https://amzn.to/3GiQyW8), (the kind in a can)
-   ▢ ½ teaspoon [dried thyme leaves](https://amzn.to/3u5mcR9)
-   ▢ ½ teaspoon [dried oregano](https://amzn.to/3MwxC6W)
-   ▢ Salt & pepper to taste
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon [nutritional yeast](https://amzn.to/42WEhi4), (optional)
-   ▢ 2 tablespoons cilantro or parsley

-   Heat the coconut oil in a pan over medium-high heat. When it's hot, add the mushrooms and garlic. Sauté the mushrooms for about 5 minutes until they soften and begin to release their juices. 
    
-   Stir in the coconut milk, thyme, oregano, salt and pepper. Reduce heat to a simmer. Continue to cook for about another 5 minutes.
    
-   Stir in nutritional yeast if using, and cilantro or parsley. If you feel it needs a bit more sauce, add a dash more coconut milk as desired.
