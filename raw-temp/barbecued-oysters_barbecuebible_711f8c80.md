### Barbecued Oysters

## Recipe Notes

-   **Yield:** Makes 24
-   **Equipment:** Best of Barbecue Shellfish Rack

## Ingredients

-   24 oysters in the shell
-   1 cup of your favorite barbecue sauce (I’m partial to [Best of Barbecue Chipotle Molasses Barbecue Sauce](http://www.smokenfire.com/proddetail.php?prod=SR8053))
-   8 ounces pepper Jack cheese, coarsely grated
-   2 tablespoons of your favorite barbecue rub (I’m partial to [Best of Barbecue All-Purpose Rub](http://barbecuebible.com/product/best-barbecue-all-purpose-barbecue-rub/))
-   A splash of your favorite hot sauce (optional)

## Recipe Steps

**Step 1:** Set up your grill for direct grilling and preheat to medium.

**Step 2:** Meanwhile, shuck the oysters using a [shucking knife](http://barbecuebible.com/product/stainless-steel-shellfish-opener-knife/), taking care not to spill the juices. Discard the top shell and arrange the oysters in the [shellfish rack](http://barbecuebible.com/product/best-barbecue-shellfish-grilling-rack/). Top each with 2 teaspoons barbecue sauce, grated cheese, a sprinkle of barbecue rub, and a splash of hot sauce (if using).

**Step 3:** Place the rack with the oysters on the grill and lower the grill lid. Grill the oysters until just cooked and the cheese and sauce are melted and bubbling, 3 to 5 minutes.
