This crisp, earthy spirit-free cocktail lives up to its name.

To accompany her winning cocktail recipe, [Sip Du Soleil](https://imbibemagazine.com/sip-du-soleil-a-gin-cocktail/), for the [Charleston Wine + Food](https://charlestonwineandfood.com/) 2020 festival, bartender Nikki Fairman created this vibrant spirit-free cocktail. Combining alcohol-free [Seedlip](https://seedlipdrinks.com/uk/), carrot juice and turmeric syrup, the Basically a Salad lives up to its name with a crisp, earthy flavor.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. Seedlip Garden 108
-   ½ oz. turmeric syrup
-   ½ oz. carrot juice
-   ½ oz. fresh orange juice
-   ½ oz. fresh lime juice
-   Soda water

## Preparation

Shake the first 4 ingredients with ice until well chilled. Strain into an Old Fashioned glass over fresh ice, then top with soda water and garnish.

Turmeric Syrup: In a medium saucepan add 2 cups water, 2 cups granulated sugar, and 2 medium pieces of fresh turmeric root, finely grated. Bring the mixture to a low simmer, then remove from heat and let steep for 10 minutes. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer, then bottle and refrigerate for use within 1 month.
