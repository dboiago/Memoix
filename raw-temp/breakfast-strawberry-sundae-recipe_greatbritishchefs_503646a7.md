This strawberry sundae recipe makes a fantastic treat, using [yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/yoghurt-recipes) and granola to create a quick and easy breakfast to get out of bed for. Feel free to experiment with other seasonal berries and fruits. If you're looking for strawberry recipe inspiration, have a look at our [collection](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/strawberry-recipes).

First published in 2015

-   4 tall glasses

1

Remove the tops from the strawberries and place in a bowl with the icing sugar

-   150g of [strawberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/strawberry-recipes)
-   20g of icing sugar

2

Use a fork to lightly crush the strawberries

3

Place some granola in the bottom of each glass, followed by some strawberry yoghurt. Top with the crushed fresh strawberries and serve

-   160g of granola
-   250g of [low fat strawberry yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
