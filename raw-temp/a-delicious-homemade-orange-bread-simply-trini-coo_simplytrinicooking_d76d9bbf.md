I like it because it uplifts the various delicacies added when you make a sandwich with it – be it [ham](https://simplytrinicooking.com/trini-christmas-ham/), cheese, sausage, black pudding, jam… pig foot souse… got ya there hahaha 🙂 . It is especially wonderful with butter while still hot. Just think about it for a while… you have a half melted, salted butter in between two “_cushiony_“layers of orange bread slices; the aroma tickles your olfactory nerves preparing you for a wonderful and satisfying “_citrusy_” experience. Your brain registers a citrus salted butter flavour…. Oh, I could taste it right now as I write.

### ORANGE BREAD

_**Ingredients**_:

5 cups all purpose flour, divided  
1 1/2 tbsp. yeast  
3 tbsp. white sugar  
1 cup orange juice (from concentrate)  
1 cup warm water  
1 egg yolk, beaten  
2 tbsp. melted butter  
1 tsp. grated orange rind  
1 tsp salt

**_Direction_**:

Blend the juice, water, sugar, egg, butter, and orange rind.

[![IMG 2454](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "A Delicious Homemade Orange Bread 4")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/IMG_2454.jpg)

Combine the 4 cups of flour and yeast. Then sift the flour at yeast twice.

[![IMG 2456](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "A Delicious Homemade Orange Bread 5")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/IMG_2456.jpg)

Add 2 cups of the flour into the liquid mixture. Beat well.

[![IMG 2460](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "A Delicious Homemade Orange Bread 6")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/IMG_2460.jpg)

Then **knead in** the next 2 cups of flour.

**Note:** As much as 1 cup of flour would have to be added to help make a smooth dough. That makes 5 cups in total 🙂

[![IMG 2463](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "A Delicious Homemade Orange Bread 7")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/IMG_2463.jpg)

Divide the dough into two and place into 2 (9″ or 23 cm) greased baking pans or dishes.

[![IMG 2469](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "A Delicious Homemade Orange Bread 8")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/IMG_2469.jpg)

## Orange Bread

Course Breads

Cuisine Fusion

Prep Time 1 hour 30 minutes

Cook Time 30 minutes

Total Time 2 hours

Servings 2 loaves

-   5 cups all purpose flour divided
-   1 1/2 tbsp. yeast
-   3 tbsp. white sugar
-   1 cup orange juice from concentrate
-   1 cup warm water
-   1 egg yolk beaten
-   2 tbsp. melted butter
-   1 tsp. grated orange rind
-   1 tsp salt

-   Blend the juice, water, sugar, egg, butter, and orange rind.
    
-   Combine the 4 cups of flour and yeast. Then sift the flour at yeast twice.
    
-   Add 2 cups of the flour into the liquid mixture and beat well. Then knead in the next 2 cups of flour. (Note: As much as 1 cup of flour would have to be added to help make a smooth dough. That makes 5 cups in total).
    
-   Divide the dough into two and place into 2 (9″ or 23 cm) greased baking pans or dishes.Allow to rest until it doubles in size (about 1 hour)
    
-   Brush with melted butter. Bake for about 40 minutes at 350 degrees F or until golden and firm. Then remove from oven and cool before serving.
