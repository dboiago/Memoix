This simple [vegetarian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes) [fennel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/fennel-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/fennel-recipes) side dish is packed with flavour thanks to fragrant [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/saffron-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/saffron-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/saffron-recipes) and fiery [harissa](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/harissa-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/harissa-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/harissa-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/harissa-recipes). With the bulb gently simmered until yielding and soft, it's the perfect small plate to serve as part of a larger [Middle Eastern](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/middle-eastern-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/middle-eastern-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/middle-eastern-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/middle-eastern-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/middle-eastern-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/middle-eastern-recipes) feast.

### Imperial

-   2 [fennel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes), halved
-   60g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   50g of caster sugar
-   4g of [table salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   370ml of water
-   1 pinch of [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/saffron-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of [natural yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of [harissa](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/harissa-recipes)
-   [fronds of fresh dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), to garnish

1

Preheat an oven to 180°C/gas mark 4. Place the butter, sugar, salt, water and saffron in a small saucepan and bring to a simmer

2

Place the fennel halves cut-side down in a baking dish big enough to hold them in a snug single layer. Pour over the simmering saffron mixture, then cover the tray in foil. Cook in the oven for 45 minutes, turning the fennel halfway through, until soft

3

Place a tablespoon of yoghurt and a tablespoon of harissa on each plate. Top with a fennel half, drizzling it with some of the cooking liquor. Garnish with dill fronds and serve
