-   22cm square baking tin

1

Heat the oil in a pan and fry the almonds until browned, then season with salt and leave to cool

-   1 tbsp of oil
-   100g of [blanched almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Heat the oven to 170°C/Gas mark 3. Cream the sugar and eggs together. Melt the butter and stir this in with the flour and cocoa

-   225g of caster sugar
-   3 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   160g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   55g of plain flour, sieved
-   55g of [cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes)

3

Melt the chocolate in a microwave or over a pan of simmering water. Add the melted chocolate and salted almonds to the cream mixture and stir in

-   200g of [dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)
-   100g of [milk chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)

4

Tip the mixture into a 22cm square baking tin tray lined with silicone paper, level the top and bake for 30 - 40 minutes. Leave to cool

5

Cut into small squares and dust with cocoa powder. Arrange on a serving plate and top with a whole almond per square and a little fleck of gold leaf

-   [cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes) for dusting
-   16 [blanched almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   4 sheets of gold leaf
