This recipe is part of a series of ideas for using this [lacto-fermented chilli sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/lacto-fermented-chilli-sauce-recipe "Lacto-fermented chilli sauce"). Charred [pineapple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pineapple-recipes "Pineapple") and [halloumi](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/halloumi-recipes "Halloumi") combine to make a sweet and salty salad that’s as cheerful in winter as it is in the warmer months. The fermented chilli sauce adds plenty of interest and a further pop of vibrant colour.

First published in 2023

1

Mix all the dressing ingredients together and add a small splash to the cabbage, massaging with your hands to soften

-   3 tbsp of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   1 tsp [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of olive oil
-   2 tbsp of fermented chilli sauce, see above
-   1/4 [red cabbage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-cabbage-recipes), thinly sliced

2

Heat a griddle pan or barbecue. Rub the pineapple and halloumi slices with a little oil and griddle/grill for 2-3 minutes, or until charred on both sides

-   1 [pineapple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pineapple-recipes), peeled, cored and rings cut into quarters
-   225g of [halloumi](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/halloumi-recipes), sliced
-   oil

3

Arrange the cabbage on a serving plate and top with the pineapple and halloumi. Add the green chilli, mint and spring onion and top with the remaining dressing. Scatter over the peanuts to serve

-   1 [green chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), thinly sliced
-   1 handful of [mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes)
-   1 [spring onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), thinly sliced
-   1 handful of [toasted peanuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-recipes)
