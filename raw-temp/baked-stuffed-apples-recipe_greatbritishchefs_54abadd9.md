This baked stuffed apple recipe make a simple but pleasurable dessert. [William Drabble](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/william-drabble) uses blackberries, raisins and ground almonds for his filling but feel free to experiment with different ingredients. This is a great way to use apples when you have had a particularly good harvest.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Core the apples completely through. Use a knife to score around the equator of the apple, piercing the skin

-   6 [apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes)

2

Preheat the oven to 150°C/gas mark 2

3

Using your hands, mix together the butter, brown sugar, raisins and ground almonds in a bowl

-   30g of [ground almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   2 tsp brown sugar
-   30g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   40g of [raisins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raisin-recipes)

4

Fill each apple to halfway with the almond mixture, then add a couple of blackberries to the centre as well

-   100g of [blackberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blackberry-recipes), washed

5

Bake the stuffed apples in the oven until soft, approximately 20-30 minutes. Serve warm with remaining blackberries

-   50g of [blackberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blackberry-recipes), washed
