Boost your [cocktail](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocktail-recipes "Cocktail") game with this [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes "Apple") and [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes "Honey") [sake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sake-recipes "Sake") sour. Shaking sake, apple juice, lemon juice, and a touch of honey syrup over ice, this cocktail is the perfect balance of sweet, tart, and citrusy notes. It's topped with a twist of [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes "Lemon") zest and homemade dried apple slices.

### Imperial

1

A day ahead of serving, prepare the dried apple slices. Preheat the oven to the lowest heat setting – around 60°C

-   2 [russet apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), finely sliced on a mandolin

2

Brush the apple slices with lemon juice. This will help stop them browning

-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced

3

Dehydrate the apple slices on a wire rack in the oven for 3 hours, or until dry but still flexible

4

Leave the apple slices overnight at room temperature to finish drying, then store in an airtight container until needed 

5

The next day, make the honey syrup by mixing together the honey and warm water

-   45ml of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   45ml of warm water

7

Add a generous handful of ice cubes to the shaker, then secure the lid and shake well for around 30 seconds to emulsify the egg whites and chill the mix 

-   1 handful of ice cubes, plus more as needed

8

Strain the cocktail into three chilled glasses filled with ice cubes, garnish each one with a lemon twist and apple slice, then serve 

-   3 [lemon zest](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), twisted
