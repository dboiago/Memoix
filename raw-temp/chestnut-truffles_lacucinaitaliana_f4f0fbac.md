### List of Ingredients

-   1 2/3 C. of boiled chestnuts
-   7 OZ. of white chocolate
-   Unsweetened cocoa powder

### Method

1

Chop the chocolate, then melt it in a double boiler. Puree the chestnuts and mix them with the melted chocolate in a bowl; cover the bowl with plastic wrap and put it in the refrigerator for about 20 minutes. Use your hands to form balls about half an ounce each: you will get about 30 small truffles.

2

Roll the truffles in unsweetened cocoa powder and serve. You can also store them in the refrigerator for a few days after having put them in small paper cups and sealing them in an airtight container.
