[Adam Gray](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/adam-gray)'s beautiful beer marinated [pork belly](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pork-belly-recipes) recipe takes a bit of forward planning, but is simple to pull off and well worth the wait for the heady flavours imparted by the Pilsner, [bay leaf](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/bay-leaf-recipes) and [peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/peppercorn-recipes). If you don't have a barbecue, a very hot griddle pan will do nicely.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### To serve

-   12 [asparagus spears](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes), English, stems peeled and sliced
-   dandelion leaves, or wild rocket
-   100ml of rapeseed oil

1

To start the dish, mix all of the marinade ingredients together. Place the pork belly in a suitably-sized deep container, fat-side down, and cover with the marinade. Leave in the fridge to marinate for 8-10 hours

-   10g of [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), cracked
-   1 [bulb of garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), roughly chopped
-   5 bay leaves
-   1 [pork belly](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-belly-recipes), 2-3kg in weight, free-range, boneless
-   1.32l [Meantime Pilsner](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beer-recipes)

2

The next day, preheat the oven to 130°C/gas mark 1

3

Remove the belly from the marinade and place in the oven for 2 1/2-3 hours until soft and tender. Once cooked, remove from the oven, allow to cool and refrigerate until firm

4

Once cold, cut into 1cm wide slices. Either grill on a hot barbecue or a hot griddle pan until lightly crispy on both sides

5

Meanwhile, blanch the asparagus until tender then drain

-   12 [asparagus spears](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes), English, stems peeled and sliced

6

Divide the asparagus onto plates along with the dandelion leaves. Add the slices of pork belly and drizzle with some rapeseed oil. Serve immediately

-   dandelion leaves, or wild rocket
-   100ml of rapeseed oil
