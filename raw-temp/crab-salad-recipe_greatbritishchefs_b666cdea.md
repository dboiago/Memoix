Delicately sweet [crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/crab-recipes) meat is well partnered by a voluptuous avocado cream and a zesty lemongrass vinaigrette in this wonderful crab salad recipe from [Andy Waters](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/andy-waters). Crab is an excellent ingredient to use in seafood starters, to see more options for appetisers featuring crab and more fruits of the sea, check out our entire collection of [seafood starter recipes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/seafood-starter-recipes).

First published in 2015

1

For the vinaigrette, finely chop the lemongrass and place into a pot with the oil. Fry until the lemongrass starts to sizzle

-   1 [lemon grass stalk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-grass-recipes), heart only
-   125ml of olive oil

2

Remove from the heat and allow to infuse for 30minutes - 1 hour before straining the oil into a bowl and discarding the lemongrass

3

For the avocado cream, blend the avocado flesh with the crème fraîche and half the lemon juice until smooth

-   2 [avocados](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), large
-   2 tbsp of crème fraîche
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced

5

Season well and pour the remaining lemon juice on top. Chill until ready to serve

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced

6

To make the salad, marinate the carrots and turnips in the lemongrass vinaigrette for 10 minutes

-   2 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), finely sliced
-   2 [turnips](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turnip-recipes), finely diced

7

Meanwhile, bring a large pan of water to the boil. Add the crabs and cook for 6 minutes

-   4 [crabs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes), live

8

Plunge into cold water for another 5 minutes before removing the flesh. Mix with crème fraîche, lemon juice, chilli and herbs. Season to taste with salt and pepper

-   3 tbsp of crème fraîche
-   2 tbsp of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [tarragon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

9

Serve onto plates with the marinated vegetables, tomato quarters and pink grapefruit segments. Pour over the avocado cream and garnish with mint leaves, chervil and watercress

-   4 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), quartered
-   2 [pink grapefruit](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grapefruit-recipes), segmented
-   1 handful of [mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes)
-   4 sprigs of [chervil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chervil-recipes)
-   1 handful of [watercress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/watercress-recipes), sprigs only
