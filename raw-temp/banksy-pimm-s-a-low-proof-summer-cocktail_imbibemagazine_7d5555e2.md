Bring the flavors of summer from garden to glass.

Take your backyard bounty from the garden to the glass with this low-proof riff on the classic [Pimm’s Cup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/recipe-classic-pimm-s-cup/).

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. Pimm’s No. 1
-   1/2 oz. gin
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   [1/2 oz. simple syrup \[1:1\]](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   1 dash Angostura bitters
-   1–1 1/2 oz. ginger beer

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients except the ginger beer in a shaker and shake with ice. Strain into an ice-filled glass, top with ginger beer, and garnish.

**Tip**Garnish with whatever fresh herbs you have on hand; cilantro and rosemary will add a savory edge, while mint, lemon balm, and chamomile will bring out the natural sweetness of Pimm’s.
