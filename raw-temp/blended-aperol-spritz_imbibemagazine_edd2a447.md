**Recipe:** _Adam Bernbach for 2 Birds 1 Stone, Washington, DC_

![](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/frozen-drinks-blended-aperol-spritz-vertical-crdt-lara-ferroni.jpg)

Fresh lime and orange juice bring a sweet, citrusy zip to this frozen spritz.

Fresh lime and orange juice bring a sweet, citrusy zip to this frozen spritz.

## Ingredients

-   3 oz. sparkling wine
-   1 oz. orange-infused Aperol
-   1 oz. simple syrup (1:1)
-   ½ oz. vodka
-   ½ oz. fresh orange juice
-   ¼ oz. fresh lime juice
-   Pinch salt

## Preparation

Pour in a blender and add 12 oz. of ice. Makes one 16 oz. serving.

Orange-infused Aperol: Add the grated zest of 4 large oranges to ½ liter of Aperol. Put in blender and process until very fine and emulsified. Let sit for 24 hours and strain.
