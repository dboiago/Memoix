These quick and easy tartlets will curb any weekend cravings for a full fry up, as they're already topped with all the essentials: [bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/bacon-recipes), [sausage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/sausage-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/sausage-recipes) and a baked quails egg. Ren's [puff pastry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/puff-pastry-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/puff-pastry-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/puff-pastry-recipes) tartlets will be sure to impress.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   1 sheet of puff pastry, ready-rolled
-   1 tbsp of flour, for dusting
-   6 tsp [tomato purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   3 [bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bacon-recipes), rashers
-   3 chipolatas
-   6 [quail eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quail-egg-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [chopped chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes)

1

Preheat the oven to 200°C/gas mark 6

2

Cook the bacon and the sausages on a baking tray for 15 minutes, or until cooked through. Set aside to cool slightly

3

Unroll the pastry onto a lightly floured board and cut into six rectangles. Using a sharp knife, score a border 1cm from the edge so that it looks like a picture frame, but being careful not to cut all the way through the pastry

4

Spread 1 tsp of tomato purée over the centre of each tartlet, leaving the border clear. Chop the cooked bacon and sausages into small pieces and scatter over the tomato base, leaving a small area in the centre for the egg

5

Transfer the pastry rectangles to a baking tray lined with baking parchment, leaving a small gap in between each tartlet

6

Bake in the oven for 5 minutes, by which time the pastry border should have puffed up around the edge. Carefully crack a quail egg into the centre of each tartlet and bake for a further 5 minutes

7

Remove from the oven and scatter each tartlet with some chopped chives before serving immediately
