Chicken paillard is a wonderful dish to try at home, and this version from [Tom Aikens](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/tom-aikens) is among the best. Pounded thin, the [chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-breast-recipes) will cook evenly and quickly, making a great dinner. Marinating the meat overnight will ensure the chicken breasts are full of flavour, which will pair well with the punchy rocket, tasty [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/tomato-recipes) and salty [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/caper-recipes).

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Chicken paillard

-   4 [chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes), butterflied
-   200g of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)
-   20 [basil leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes)
-   20 [caperberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes)
-   40g of [black olives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/black-olive-recipes), cut in half
-   50g of [Parmesan shavings](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes)
-   20ml of balsamic vinegar

#### Marinade

-   400ml of olive oil
-   2 tbsp of [rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), chopped
-   12 [basil leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes), picked and scrunched
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juice and zest only

#### Plum tomatoes

-   8 [plum tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), cut into quarters
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 tsp [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), chopped
-   150ml of olive oil
-   1 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tsp [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   8g of icing sugar

1

Mix together all of the ingredients for the marinade in a bowl. Separate out 30ml to dress the salad at the end, the rest will be used for the chicken. Set aside in the fridge until needed

-   400ml of olive oil
-   2 tbsp of [rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   12 [basil leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes)
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

2

Take the butterflied chicken breasts and wrap each one in cling film. Use a large pan to lightly bash each breast so they flatten out slightly (like you would do for an escalope but not as severe)

-   4 [chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes)

3

Cover the breasts in the marinade and leave to marinate for as long as you like, preferably overnight

4

Preheat the oven to 90°C. Place the quartered plum tomatoes on a baking tray. Mix all the other ingredients in a bowl and cover the tomatoes. Cook the plum tomatoes for 2 hours

-   8 [plum tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 tsp [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   150ml of olive oil
-   1 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tsp [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   8g of icing sugar

5

Once the tomatoes are ready, put a griddle pan over a medium heat and once scorching, drop in the marinated chicken breasts (if you're pan is not large enough to accomodate all 4 breasts, grill in batches of 2). Grill for 2-3 minutes on each side (alternatively you can cook on the barbecue which may take slightly longer)

6

Lay the breasts of chicken onto plates. Toss the rocket and basil in the marinade that was separated in step 1 and then layer on top of the chicken. Finish with the caper berries, black olives, Parmesan shavings, slow roasted tomatoes and a drizzling of balsamic round the edge

-   200g of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)
-   20 [basil leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes)
-   20 [caperberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes)
-   40g of [black olives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/black-olive-recipes)
-   50g of [Parmesan shavings](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes)
-   20ml of balsamic vinegar
