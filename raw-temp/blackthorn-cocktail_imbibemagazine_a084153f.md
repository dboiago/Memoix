One of the few classic cocktails to highlight Irish whiskey.

With roots dating back to 19th-century New Orleans, the Blackthorn cocktail is one of the few classics to highlight Irish whiskey. Here’s how they make it at [Lazy Bird](https://www.lazybirdchicago.com/) in Chicago.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. Irish whiskey
-   1/2 oz. dry vermouth
-   1/2 oz. sweet vermouth
-   2 dashes Bogart's bitters (or sub with Angostura)
-   1 dash absinthe

## Preparation

Stir all of the ingredients with ice, then strain into a glass filled with fresh ice, and garnish.
