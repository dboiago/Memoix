### Glass:

Serve in a [Collins glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

3 1⁄2 fl oz

Apple juice/apple cider (cloudy & unsweetened)

1 1⁄2 fl oz

Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)

3⁄4 fl oz

Maple syrup

## How to make:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [COLLINS GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of apple slice.
3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into ice-filled glass.

## Review:

Refreshing and balanced with just the right amount of citrus acidity.
