Blackberry vinegar, is a sweet and sour, thick-ish vinegar that works especially well with fruit, sweet root vegetables and [goat's cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/goats-cheese-recipes). I use it as I would balsamic – simply tossed into a [salad](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salad-recipes) with olive oil, salt and pepper, with perhaps a little extra blackberry vinegar drizzle at the end.

So far, my favourite use for the blackberry vinegar has been in a beetroot and orzo salad with goats cheese, spinach and pine nuts, tossed with olive oil and a good drizzle of blackberry vinegar. This same approach works equally well with any combination of grain, fruit and nuts. I especially love [strawberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/strawberry-recipes), [peaches](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/peach-recipes) or [figs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/fig-recipes) with toasted pistachios or cashews.

You could also use the blackberry vinegar to make a vinaigrette – I’ve been meaning to adapt this [raspberry vinaigrette recipe](http://allrecipes.com/recipe/raspberry-vinaigrette-dressing/) but so far haven't been able to diverge from enjoying the blackberry vinegar in its pure form.
