This vibrant blood orange sorbet recipe from [Galton Blackiston](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/galton-blackiston) will make the perfect refreshing counterpart to a citrussy dessert, such as a [blood orange mousse](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/blood-orange-recipe-mousse-sorbet), or you can enjoy a scoop on its own.

First published in 2015

-   Ice cream maker

1

Place the sugar and water in a pan and bring to the boil. Keep cooking until all of the sugar has dissolved, then leave to simmer for a further 5 minutes

-   275ml of water
-   175g of caster sugar

2

Set aside to cool, then mix in the blood orange and lemon juices. Set aside in the fridge until chilled, then churn in an ice cream maker. Freeze until ready to serve

-   6 [blood oranges](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blood-orange-recipes), juiced
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
