The spice is nice in this sultry sipper.

The smokiness of scotch meets the spice of ginger, the sweetness of passion fruit syrup and the brightness of lime juice.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. Scotch
-   1 oz. passion fruit syrup
-   ¾ oz. fresh lime juice
-   ½ oz. ginger syrup

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients into a shaker with ice, then shake vigorously. Double strain into a coupe, then garnish.

Passion fruit syrup: Combine 3 parts simple syrup (1:1) with 1 part [passion fruit purée](https://perfectpuree.com/product/passion-fruit-concentrate/). Store in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.

Ginger Syrup: Combine 1 part fresh ginger (by volume) with 1 part cold simple syrup (1:1). Blend on high for 1 minute, then strain. Store in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
