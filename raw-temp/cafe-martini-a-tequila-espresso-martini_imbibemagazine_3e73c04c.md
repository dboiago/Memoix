At Saint Theo’s in New York, beverage director Simon Sebbah elevates the Espresso Martini by trading out vodka for reposado tequila, giving it “a little extra kick and dimension.”

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. reposado tequila (Saint Theo's uses Casa Del Sol)
-   1 oz. coffee liqueur
-   1 oz. cold espresso

## Preparation

Add all the ingredients to a shaker with ice. Shake and strain into the glass. Garnish.
