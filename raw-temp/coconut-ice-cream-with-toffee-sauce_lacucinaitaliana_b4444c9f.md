**Cooks in:** _4h \+ 50mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   2 CUPS of coconut milk
-   6 OZ. of fresh cream
-   5 OZ. of sugar
-   3/4 OZ. of acacia honey
-   2 OZ. of freshly grated coconut
-   5 of egg yolks

### Method

1

To make coconut ice cream with toffee sauce, first boil the coconut milk with 4 oz cream and melt the honey. Work 4 oz sugar with the egg yolks, diluting them with the coconut milk and heating the cream until it reaches about 180°F. (Fun fact: In Italian, we call this _cottura alla rosa_, which translates to ‘rose cooking’ in English – so called because blowing on the spoon forms little “petal” shapes.) Let the cream cool, then put it in the ice-cream maker. Set for the time indicated on the machine instructions. (It should take about 1 hour.)

2

In the meantime, put a plate in the freezer to cool. Shape the ice cream – as soon as it is ready – between 2 wet spoons and form 6 large dumpling shapes. Put a wooden stick in the center of each one and transfer to the cold plate. Let them cool in the freezer for about 3 hours.

3

When you’re ready to serve the ice cream, make the toffee sauce: Pour 2 ½ oz of sugar and a tablespoon of water into a small saucepan. Turn the heat on low and let the sugar caramelize – cooking until the sauce becomes a dark amber color. Still cooking over low heat, add 3 oz of cream, stirring well. Sprinkle the ice cream with grated coconut and serve with the toffee sauce.
