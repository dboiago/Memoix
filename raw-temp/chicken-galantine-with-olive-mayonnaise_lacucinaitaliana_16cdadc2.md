## Open the chicken breast by slicing it horizontally to create a large, thin slice. Sprinkle with salt and pepper. In a food processor, mix the ricotta ...

**Cooks in:** _50mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   9 1/2 OZ. of chicken breast
-   1/2 C. of ricotta cheese
-   1 of egg
-   1 of egg yolk
-   Olive paté
-   Raisins
-   Dried apricots
-   Shelled and peeled pistachios
-   Mustard
-   White vinegar
-   Lemon
-   Rosemary
-   shallot
-   Garlic
-   Butter
-   dry white wine
-   Sunflower or canola oil
-   Extravirgin olive oil
-   Fine and coarse salt
-   Pepper

### Method

1

Open the chicken breast by slicing it horizontally to create a large, thin slice. Sprinkle with salt and pepper. In a food processor, mix the ricotta with a teaspoon of olive paté, salt, a few raisins, 1 tablespoon of pistachios and 4 chopped apricots. Spread this mixture on the chicken, roll it up and tie it up like a roast with cooking twine and wrap it in wet parchment paper and then aluminum foil, twisting the ends closed like a candy.

2

Immerse this in a saucepan full of cold water, bring to a boil, then cook for 20-30 minutes over very low heat. Drain and remove the covering. Meanwhile, beat the egg and the yolk with a teaspoon of mustard, 1 tablespoon white vinegar, the juice of 1/2 lemon and a pinch of salt. Drizzle in 1 1/2 cups sunflower seed oil, blending to form a mayonnaise.

3

In a pan, heat 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil and a small pat of butter and fry 1 shallot and 2 cloves unpeeled garlic, slightly crushed. Add a sprig of rosemary, some coarse salt and pepper and brown the chicken in this mixture for 5-6 minutes with a splash of white wine. Mix 5 tablespoons of mayonnaise with 2 tablespoons olive paté and serve this sauce with the galantine.
