[Barbecued](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/barbecue-recipes) spatchcock chicken is the ultimate summer treat. Brining the [chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-recipes) beforehand will ensure the meat is packed with flavour, while a side serving of coleslaw just adds to the fun. Buy a good quality bird for this sublime [barbecued](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/barbecue-recipes) chicken recipe from [Josh Eggleton](https://plus.google.com/114367199794581980430?rel=author). If you've never made a brine before, follow Josh's handy [video guide](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/how-to-cook/how-to-make-a-brine) for full instructions and ingredients.

First published in 2015

#### Coleslaw

-   200g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), finely sliced
-   200g of [red cabbage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-cabbage-recipes), shredded
-   1 [banana shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely sliced
-   50g of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), finely chopped
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   50ml of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground

-   Thermometer
-   Barbecue with a lid

1

Start by submerging the chicken in brine - you can make the brine by making a 95% water and 5% salt solution. Leave the chicken in brine for at least 3 hours

-   1 [spatchcock spring chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-starter-recipes)

2

Remove the chicken from the brine, season with freshly ground black pepper and rub with olive oil

-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   olive oil, to rub into the chicken

3

Place on the barbecue for 2 1/2 minutes. Turn over and colour the other side for 2 1/2 minutes

4

Move the chicken up to a higher shelf and close the lid of the barbecue. Reduce the temperature of the barbecue and cook for a further 20 minutes - this will only work if you have an electric barbecue, if not, transfer to the grill and cook on a low heat for a similar amount of time. To check that its cooked, stick a thermometer in the chicken thigh - 63-65°C will indicate that the chicken is done

6

To serve, remove the chicken from the barbecue or grill, slice and serve with the coleslaw
