A proper Yorkshire pudding is a thing of true beauty, and this recipe from Sharp’s _Just Add Beer_ cookbook ensures perfectly risen puds every time. The addition of Doom Bar in the batter adds a sweet maltiness to the final flavour. Equally good as an accompaniment to a roast, or just on their own enjoyed with a beer.

First published in 2021
