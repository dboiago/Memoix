This rich [coconut milk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes "Coconut milk") and red lentil dhal is just the thing for cold spring days. It’s topped with salty and moreish fried [artichoke](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/artichoke-recipes "Artichoke") hearts and lentils. The whole dish is finished with a perfect poached egg.

First published in 2025

1

Sauté the onion and garlic in the olive oil until soft

-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced

3

In a separate pan, sauté the leeks and artichoke hearts in olive oil until golden and crispy

-   2 [leeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes), sliced
-   200g of [artichoke hearts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/artichoke-recipes), jarred or canned, drained and halved
-   1 tbsp of olive oil

4

Bring a pan of water to a gentle simmer, add vinegar, and swirl the water. Crack in the eggs and poach for 3 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon

-   1 tbsp of white wine vinegar
-   4 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® eggs](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/)

5

Spoon the dhal into bowls, top with leeks, artichokes and a poached egg. Garnish with fresh parsley

-   [fresh parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
