## High Protein Momos | बिना मैदा के Healthy Chicken Momos | Easy Momo Recipe | Chef Ranveer Brar

Chicken Momos – soft semolina dough, juicy chicken stuffing और spicy chilli dip के साथ बनी delicious street-style momos.

Prep Time: 15-20 minutes Cook Time: 25-30 minutes

Total Time 25-35 minutes

### Ingredients

### For Semolina Dough

-   150-175 ml Warm Water, गर्म पानी
-   500 gms Semolina, सूजी
-   Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार
-   1 tsp Oil, तेल

### For Roasted Tomato Chutney

-   1 tsp Oil, तेल
-   5 large Tomatoes (cut into halves), टमाटर
-   2 tsp Oil, तेल
-   5-6 no. Garlic cloves (crushed), लहसुन
-   3-4 no. Green Chillies (slit), हरी मिर्च
-   ¼ cup Roasted Peanuts, भुनी हुई मूंगफली
-   1 tbsp Tender Coriander stems (chopped), धनिया के डंठल
-   Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार
-   Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार
-   1 tsp Sugar, चीनी

### For Chicken Stuffing

-   1 tbsp Oil, तेल
-   3-4 no. Green Chillies (chopped), हरी मिर्च
-   1 inch Ginger (peeled & finely chopped), अदरक
-   1 tsp Tender Coriander stems (finely chopped), धनिया के डंठल
-   ½ tsp White Sesame seeds (optional), सफेद तिल
-   200 gms Chicken mince, चिकन का कीमा
-   Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार

### For Chilli Dip

-   1-2 no. Green Chillies (less spicy & finely chopped), हरी मिर्च
-   1 tbsp Dark soy sauce, डार्क सोया सॉस
-   1 tbsp Vinegar, सिरका
-   Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार
-   1 tbsp Oil, तेल
-   2 tbsp Chilli Oil, मिर्च का तेल

Other Ingredients

-   2-3 tbsp Refined flour (for rolling momo sheets), मैदा
-   1-2 tsp Oil (for greasing), तेल

### For Garnish

-   Coriander sprig, धनिया पत्ता

### Process

### For Semolina Dough

-   In a bowl, add warm water, semolina and give it a good mix.
-   Add salt to taste, start continuously mixing to form a semisoft dough.
-   Add oil, knead well and keep it aside for a while.

### For Roasted Tomato Chutney

-   In a pan, add oil, once it’s hot, add tomatoes and cook until tomatoes get charred texture.
-   Transfer it into the bowl and keep it aside.
-   In the same pan, add oil, once it’s hot, add garlic, green chillies and cook for 2-3 minutes on high flames until garlic turns golden brown in colour.
-   Add roasted peanuts, tender coriander stems, salt to taste and toss it for a minute.  
    Transfer it into the same bowl and give it a good mix.
-   Transfer the mixture into a mixer grinder jar and grind it into the smooth fine paste.
-   Pour it into the bowl and add salt to the taste, sugar and give it a good mix.
-   Transfer it into the serving bowl and garnish it with coriander sprig.

### For Chicken Stuffing

-   In a pan, add green chillies, ginger, tender coriander stems, white sesame seeds and cook for 2-3 minutes.
-   Transfer it into the bowl, add chicken mince, salt to taste and give it a good mix

### For Chilli Dip

-   In a bowl, add green chillies, dark soy sauce, vinegar, salt to taste, oil, chilli oil and give it a good mix.
-   Keep it aside for further use.

### For Cooking Momo

-   Take prepared dough, divided into small lemon size balls, flatten with the rolling pin and add a spoonful of prepared stuffing.
-   Close the edges, shape them by making pleats on one side and sticking on the other side. Make it a crescent or any desired shape.
-   Place the momos into the greased steaming plate, place the plate into the steamer, cover it with the lid and let it cook for 12-15 minutes or until it’s cooked.
-   Once it’s cooked properly then transfer it into the serving platter or dish.
-   Serve hot with prepared chilli dip and roasted tomato chutney.
