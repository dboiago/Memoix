Smoky pancetta, sweet [peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pea-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pea-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pea-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pea-recipes) and earthy lettuce, all topped with creamy and salty Saint Agur blue cheese – this [springtime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spring-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spring-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spring-recipes) side dish is an ideal accompaniment to a [roast dinner](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sunday-roasts-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sunday-roasts-recipes)– in particular a nice slow-braised leg of [lamb](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lamb-recipes).

First published in 2020

This is one of our favourite things to do with baby gem lettuce! Charring and then braising brings out a whole new flavour profile from what you're used to from a lettuce; nutty, sweet and a melt-in-the-mouth texture. Salty, crispy pancetta or bacon complements it wonderfully but can of course be omitted if preferred.

We'd highly recommend serving with a slow-braised leg or shoulder of lamb, then use the resulting lamb stock to braise the baby gem in. A perfect springtime feast.

### Metric

### Imperial

-   vegetable oil, for frying
-   150g of [smoked pancetta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pancetta-recipes), diced
-   4 [baby gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes), washed and quartered
-   500ml of chicken stock, or fresh lamb stock
-   100g of frozen peas
-   1 sprig of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), leaves picked and chopped
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested
-   60g of [Saint Agur Blue Cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blue-cheese-recipes), crumbled

1

Drizzle a small amount of oil into a large nonstick frying pan and add the lardons. Cook until caramelised and starting to go crispy

2

Push the lardons to one side of the pan and add the baby gems, cut-side down. Brown on each side for a few minutes

3

Once browned, add the warm stock and peas and leave to simmer for 10 minutes over a low heat

4

After 10 minutes, stir in the fresh chopped mint and take off the heat

5

Transfer to a serving platter and crumble over the Saint Agur blue cheese to finish
