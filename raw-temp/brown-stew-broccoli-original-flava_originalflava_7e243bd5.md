**Brown stew broccoli & cauliflower**

Add some FLAVA to your vegetables! Say goodbye to your boring vegetables, this recipes will add some much need flavour and make you look forward to your 5 a day!. Great as a side dish or on its own. Stay tuned for our Vegan E-book coming out soon!

-   100g Fresh broccoli
-   100g Fresh Cauliflower
-   2 tsp sea salt
-   2 tsp black pepper
-   2 tsp Browning
-   2 tsp Pimento / All spice
-   2 tsp paprika
-   ½ Red bell pepper (Sliced)
-   ½ green bell pepper (Sliced)
-   ½ Yellow bell pepper (Sliced)
-   1 tbsp. Vegetable oil
-   ½ medium onions (diced)
-   4 garlic cloves (diced)
-   1 tsp. Thyme
-   1 scotch bonnet pepper (sliced)
-   1 cup water
-   4 sprigs fresh thyme

1.  First you’ll need to wash your fresh broccoli and cauliflower and cut into florets then add to a mixing bowl.
2.  Then season with 1tsp salt, pepper, browning, pimento, paprika, half of bell peppers, fresh thyme then mix together and marinate for a few hours.
3.  In your frying pan add vegetable oil on a high heat, then add your onions and garlic and saute for a few minutes.
4.  Add 1 tsp browning, thyme, black pepper, 1 sliced scotch bonnet and mix until you form a brown paste.
5.  Add your marinated broccoli & cauliflower and mix together.
6.  Add 1 cup of water, and steam vegetable until soft. Give it one last mix together and add the remaining bell peppers.
7.  Then serve as a side dish on eat on its own!
