[Chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chocolate-recipes) and spice can be a surprisingly good match - as anyone who has tried chilli chocolate will agree. Flavoured with [cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chocolate-recipes), cayenne and black pepper, these spiced chocolate biscuits from Vivek Singh pack a real punch.

First published in 2016

### Metric

### Imperial

-   100g of [white chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/white-chocolate-recipes)
-   85g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), softened
-   70g of soft brown sugar
-   185g of caster sugar
-   1 [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tsp vanilla essence, or extract
-   150g of plain flour
-   60g of [cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes)
-   4 pinches of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   4 pinches of [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)
-   1 tsp [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1 tsp ajwain seeds, optional
-   1/4 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp bicarbonate of soda
-   1 dash of olive oil

-   Electric mixer

1

Using an electric mixer, beat the butter until smooth. Add both sugars and continue beating on a high speed for 1 minute until fully incorporated

-   85g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), softened
-   70g of soft brown sugar
-   185g of caster sugar

2

Add the egg white and vanilla and beat until smooth, scraping down the side of the bowl as necessary

-   1 [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tsp vanilla essence

4

Beat on a low speed until a soft dough forms. If the dough is too dry, add a splash of milk to the mix

5

Divide the dough into 2 equal portions, then roll into 2.5cm thick logs. Wrap tightly in cling film and chill for at least 10 minutes

6

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4

7

Before baking, cut horizontally across the logs to make 2-3mm thick discs and arrange on an oiled baking sheet. Transfer to the oven and bake for 11 minutes

-   1 dash of olive oil

8

Remove the biscuits from the oven and allow to cool on a wire rack

9

Meanwhile, place the white chocolate in a bowl and heat on a low power in the microwave - stirring every 45 seconds until completely melted

-   100g of [white chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/white-chocolate-recipes)

10

Once the biscuits have cooled, drizzle with chocolate and place in the fridge to set. Then, place in an airtight container and store at room temperature before serving
