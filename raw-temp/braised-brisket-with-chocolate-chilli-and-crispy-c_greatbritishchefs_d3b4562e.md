-   6-8
    
-   1 hour 50 minutes plus 4 hours braising
    

Adding dark [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/savoury-chocolate-recipes "Savoury chocolate") to a slow braise like this one adds savoury richness, helps to bring out the flavours of spices like ancho and chipotle [chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes "Chilli") and balances out acidity from tomatoes. We’ve served this stew with cumin [polenta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/polenta-recipes "Polenta") enriched with Taleggio, a fresh lemon gremolata and crispy capers.

#### SPICE RUB

-   1 tbsp of [chipotle flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chipotle-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of garlic powder
-   1 tbsp of [onion powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [allspice powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/allspice-recipes)

#### BRAISED BEEF BRISKET

-   2kg [beef brisket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes), trimmed of silverskin and excess fat
-   1 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   2 red onions, roughly chopped
-   3 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and roughly chopped
-   2 [celery sticks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), roughly chopped
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 tbsp of plain flour
-   2 [ancho chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), rehydrated in boiling water and finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [chipotle paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   150ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   1 [tin of chopped tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   500ml of beef stock
-   30g of [70% dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), finely chopped
-   3 [anchovy fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/anchovy-recipes), roughly chopped (optional)
-   cornflour, to thicken if needed

#### CRISPY CAPERS

-   125g of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), drained and patted dry
-   vegetable oil, for shallow-frying

#### LEMON GREMOLATA

-   1/2 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1/2 bunch of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 [shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), roughly chopped
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested and juiced
-   150ml of olive oil

#### CUMIN POLENTA

-   1.2l chicken stock
-   300g of [polenta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/polenta-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes), lightly crushed
-   1 tsp garlic powder
-   350g of Taleggio, chopped
-   150g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated
-   50ml of whole milk
-   150g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), cubed

1

Preheat the oven to 150°C

3

Place a large ovenproof casserole dish on a medium heat with the vegetable oil. Once it reaches smoking point, sear the brisket on all sides until deeply golden, then remove and turn the heat down to low

-   1 tbsp of vegetable oil

4

Add the onions, carrots and celery to the pan and sweat until softened but without colour. Add in the garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant then stir through the flour. Cook out the flour for 1 minute or so, then stir through the ancho chillies and chipotle chilli paste

-   2 red onions, roughly chopped
-   3 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and roughly chopped
-   2 [celery sticks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), roughly chopped
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 tbsp of plain flour
-   2 [ancho chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), rehydrated in boiling water and finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [chipotle paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)

5

Deglaze with the red wine and turn up the heat slightly. Simmer to reduce the wine by half, then add in the chopped tomatoes and beef stock. Bring to a simmer, season with salt and pepper then add the beef brisket back into the dish

-   150ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   1 [tin of chopped tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   500ml of beef stock

6

Cover, transfer to the oven and cook for 4-5 hours until the meat is falling apart

7

For the crispy capers, cover the base of a wide pan with vegetable oil. Once shimmering hot, lower in all but 1 tbsp of the capers and fry for around 3 minutes until crisp. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper. Set aside

-   125g of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), drained and patted dry, 1 tablespoon reserved
-   vegetable oil, for shallow-frying

8

For the lemon gremolata, blitz all of the ingredients except the olive oil in a food processor with the reserved capers. Stir through the olive oil then season to taste with salt and pepper. Remove and set aside

-   1/2 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1/2 bunch of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 [shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), roughly chopped
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested and juiced
-   150ml of olive oil

9

An hour before you want to eat, make the cumin polenta. Bring the chicken stock to a boil on a high heat then add in the polenta, cumin and garlic powder while whisking well. Turn the heat down to low and cook for 45 minutes, stirring regularly to break up any lumps

-   300g of [polenta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/polenta-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes), lightly crushed
-   1.2l chicken stock
-   1 tsp garlic powder

10

After 45 minutes, add the Taleggio and Parmesan and whisk well to combine. Whisk through the milk and butter to loosen slightly, then taste and season with salt and pepper as needed

-   350g of Taleggio, chopped
-   150g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated
-   50ml of whole milk
-   150g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), cubed

11

Once the beef is tender and falling apart, remove it from the sauce and shred it using two forks. Set aside briefly

12

Put the sauce back on the heat, add the dark chocolate and anchovies, if using, and simmer gently to reduce and thicken it, stirring regularly and adding a slurry of cornflour and water to help thicken the stew if needed. Once thickened to your liking, season to taste with salt and pepper, add the shredded beef back in and stir to coat

-   30g of [70% dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), finely chopped
-   3 [anchovy fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/anchovy-recipes), roughly chopped (optional)
-   cornflour, to thicken if needed

13

To serve, plate up the polenta into bowls, followed by the stew. Spoon over the gremolata, crispy capers and a final twist of pepper
