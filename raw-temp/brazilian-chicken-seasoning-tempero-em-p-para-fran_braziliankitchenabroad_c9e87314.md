## Good seasoning for chicken? How about the BEST!

_Oi Gente!_

This Brazilian Chicken Seasoning, **tempero em pó para frango** as we say in Portuguese, is my go-to weapon against boring, bland chicken.

You can use this homemade spice mix on chicken thighs, tenders, drumsticks, a whole chicken and yes, even skin-on or skinless chicken breasts. In fact, I think it’s the best chicken breast seasoning out there. It brings a lot of flavor to every cut, and I even toss it on veggies when they need a little something extra.

### So, what makes this a good seasoning for chicken?

Turmeric and achiote give the chicken that gorgeous golden color. Onion and garlic powder lay down a savory base, while chives and parsley bring in a fresh, herby layer. Cayenne and paprika add a little heat to wake everything up.

But, my favorite ingredient in this spice blend are the bay leaves. They add beautiful depth of flavor, and when combined with turmeric, you get those sexy, earthy, woody notes that make this mix stand out. I just love how all these flavors come together.

The _best_ part of mixing your own chicken seasoning powder is that you’re in control. Adjust the salt. Dial up your favorite spices. Tone down anything you don’t love. Make it your own.

[![A woman seasoning two chicken breasts in a white baking dish next to a small jar of chicken seasoning](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Chicken-Seasoning-13-683x1024.jpg)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Chicken-Seasoning-13.jpg)

This spice mix works whether you’re baking, grilling, pan-frying, slow cooking, pressure cooking. It’s also a perfect dry rub before grilling, and a delicious boost to any chicken marinade… whatever your chicken mood calls for.

It’s super easy to make and stores beautifully in your spice cabinet so you’ll always have it ready when chicken’s on the menu.

## How to make Brazilian Chicken Seasoning

[![A collage showing how to make the best Chicken Seasoning powder](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/chicken-seasoning-683x1024.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/chicken-seasoning.png)

Add all the ingredients to a spice grinder and grind to a powder. It’s important to grind them well to break up the chunkier ingredients, like the bay leaves, parsley and chives.

Place your fresh chicken seasoning blend in a jar and cover with a lid. Keep it stored in a dry place for months and use whenever you’re in the mood for tasty, flavorful chicken.

## How to Season Chicken

Just season with salt, then sprinkle in the seasoning and pick your favorite cooking method.

[![A collage with images of a hand seasoning chicken breasts with salt on one side and with chicken seasoning on the other side. ](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Brazilian-Chicken-Seasoning-Steps-2-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Brazilian-Chicken-Seasoning-Steps-2.png)

**Start with salt.** For about one pound of chicken breast, you’ll need about 1-2 teaspoons of kosher salt to properly season it. We all have different tolerance to salt, so start with one teaspoon, then figure out if and how you need to adjust based on your taste.

**Sprinkle on the seasoning.** For about one pound of chicken breast, I use 2 teaspoons of this seasoning blend, but you can add as much or as little you and your family like.

**Rub it with some fat.** I always drizzle my chicken breasts with some olive oil, regardless of how I cook it.

**Check out [How to Pan Sear Chicken Breasts](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/how-to-pan-sear-chicken-breasts/)!**

[![A collage of two images showing two chicken breasts on a cast iron pan, one cooking is in progress and the other the chicken is cooked perfectly. ](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Brazilian-Chicken-Seasoning-Steps-3-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Brazilian-Chicken-Seasoning-Steps-3.png)

**Choose a cooking method and cook it.** If you choose to grill or pan fry I recommend you let the chicken sit on the counter for about 10-15 minutes before cooking. If you choose to bake it, no need to let it sit, just add a bit of butter on top of each breast, then bake it at 375F for about 30 minutes. (Cooking time will vary depending on your oven and the thickness of the chicken breasts. Keep an eye on them so they don’t overcook because dry chicken is the saddest.)
