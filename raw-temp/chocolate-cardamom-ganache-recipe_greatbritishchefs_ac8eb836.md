This is an adorably rich [petit four](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/petit-four-recipes) where the deep bitter [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chocolate-recipes) flavour is restrained by the addition of the spicy cardamom pods. Use the best chocolate you can (minimum 70% cocoa solids) in [Agnar Sverisson](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/agnar-sverrisson)'s recipe.

First published in 2015

1

Bring the cardamom, trimoline and whipping cream to the boil. Take off the heat and infuse for 15 minutes. Pass through a fine sieve

-   7 [cardamom pods](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)
-   75g of trimoline
-   360ml of whipping cream

3

Mix in the cardamom infusion. Spread out the ganache mixture on a tray and leave to set overnight

4

Shape the ganache into rough balls by hand. Once made, coat in cocoa powder and store in the fridge for 2 hours to set

-   [cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes) for dusting

5

Place the ganache balls in a small bowl and serve
