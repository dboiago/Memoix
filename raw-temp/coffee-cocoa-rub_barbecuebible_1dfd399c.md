This Coffee-Cocoa Rub Recipe is a rich and aromatic blend that’s perfect for enhancing the natural flavors of meats like lamb, pork, or beef. With a base of brown sugar complemented by the depth of ground coffee and the warmth of sweet paprika, this rub promises to add a deliciously complex crust to your grilled or roasted dishes. ### Coffee-Cocoa Rub

## Recipe Notes

-   **Yield:** Makes 1-1/2 cups

## Ingredients

-   3/4 cup brown sugar
-   6 tablespoons sweet paprika
-   6 tablespoons ground coffee
-   1/4 cup coarse salt (kosher or sea)
-   2 tablespoons freshly ground black pepper
-   1 tablespoon cocoa powder

## Recipe Steps

**Step 1:** Combine all the ingredients in a small mixing bowl and break up any lumps with your fingers or a whisk.

**Step 2:** Store in a tightly covered container away from heat or light. Store for up to 6 months. Use on lamb, pork, or beef.
