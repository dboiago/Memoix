You can’t get much easier than the classic Daiquiri recipe, and yet it remains one of the most delicious cocktails around.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. white rum
-   1 oz. fresh lime juice
-   ½ oz. simple syrup (1:1)

## Preparation

Combine ingredients in a shaker with ice, shake to chill and strain into a chilled glass.
