Salmorejo is a Spanish chilled [tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes "Tomato") and bread soup, like a very thick gazpacho. Here, [Caroline Martins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/caroline-martins "Caroline Martins") serves it with pickled coconut and vivid green [lovage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lovage-recipes "Lovage") oil, for a colourful light supper or lunch.

First published in 2024

1

Bring the apple cider vinegar, sugar, and salt to a boil. Remove from the heat and transfer to a heat proof container

-   100ml of apple cider vinegar
-   100g of caster sugar
-   10g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

4

Chill in the fridge until needed

5

Blanch the lovage in boiling water then refresh it in cold water. Squeeze out any excess water

-   200g of [lovage leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lovage-recipes)

6

Blitz the lovage with vegetable oil until the mixture is heated and acquires a bright green colour

-   100g of vegetable oil

7

Pass the oil through muslin cloth, discarding the solids, and refrigerate until ready to serve

8

Serve the salmorejo in a bowl, top with a few dots of lovage oil, and then garnish with the chopped tomatoes and radishes

-   [heirloom tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), chopped into small chunks
-   [watermelon radish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/radish-recipes), sliced into thin triangles
