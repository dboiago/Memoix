A vibrant mix.

This mix of tea-infused vodka, lemonade, and elderflower liqueur is a refreshing option for the hottest of summer days.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. Earl Grey vodka
-   2 oz. lemonade (1:1 simple syrup to fresh lemon juice)
-   1/2 oz. St. Germain elderflower liqueur

## Preparation

Combine ingredients in a shaker tin with ice and shake to chill. Strain

Earl Grey Vodka: Place 2 Earl Grey teabags in a 750-ml. bottle of vodka. Let infuse for at least 2 hours. Remove the teabags.
