## Beef Stroganoff – A Classic Dish With a Brazilian Twist

#### Oi Gente!!

**Beef Stroganoff**, or _Estrogonofe de Carne_, as we call it in Portuguese, is a very popular dish in Brazil. Despite the fact that _estrogonofe de carne_ is part of the everyday Brazilian life, this dish is actually Russian!

The biggest difference between the classic Russian and the Brazilian stroganoff is that the classic version uses mustard, vegetable or beef stock (occasionally uses tomato sauce, too) and is finished with sour cream.

The Brazilian version uses tomato sauce (or paste) mixed with heavy cream and is almost always served with white rice or [potato sticks](https://www.amazon.com/Potato-Sticks-Yoki-Batata-Palha/dp/B008S5F2X4/ref=as_li_ss_tl?crid=3AN0VOXLLKMDQ&dchild=1&keywords=batata+palha&qid=1610765145&sprefix=batata%2Caps%2C505&sr=8-3&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=f5c482890fcb7bfcfc10561ad1f87df9&language=en_US) (_[batata palha](https://www.amazon.com/Potato-Sticks-Yoki-Batata-Palha/dp/B008S5F2X4/ref=as_li_ss_tl?dchild=1&keywords=batata+palha+potato+sticks&qid=1610592105&sr=8-1&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=aea0d500654b4ae2dd218d599fc76a56&language=en_US)_).

In addition to the Brazilian version, there are so many other different **variations of beef stroganoff around the world**, and different ways to serve it. Some places serve it with crepes, pancakes, or noodles and others make the dish with different meats altogether, even sausage!

In Brazil we love stroganoff so much we have 2 more variations of Brazilian stroganoff, one with chicken and one with shrimp.

#### The Sear:

**Step 1**. Season your beef and let it sit on the counter for about 20 minutes. We’re doing this to make sure the beef isn’t straight-out-of-the-fridge cold when we add it to the pan. If the beef is cold, it reduces the heat in the pan, and it slow cooks the meat, causing it to become chewy. No thanks!! Then, place the beef in a hot oiled pan and give them a **quick sear** (30 seconds to a minute). **The quick sear is important** for the same reason as the temperature of the meat going into the pan. If you saute it for too long, it starts to get tough.

**Step 2**. Remove the beef and set it aside on a plate and then add the onions to the hot pan. Saute the onions for 2-3 minutes and then add in the garlic and saute for another minute or two.

**Step 3**. Add in the sliced mushrooms.

Step 5

#### The Sauce:

**Step 4**. Cook the mushrooms for about 5 minutes until they have wilted down.

**Step 5**. Add the beef back in, then the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, and ketchup. Saute for about 2 minutes while stirring.

**Step 6**. Add water and let the pan simmer for a few minutes. Make sure to stir and scrape any bits of food off of the sides and the bottom of the pan.

**Step 7**. Add in the heavy cream and let the beef stroganoff simmer just a few minutes longer. Give it a final stir, and that’s it.

**Step 8.** Scoop, garnish and serve!
