This gorgeous [dessert](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/italian-dessert-recipes) recipe by Andrea Sarri draws on the classic combination of [chocolate](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/chocolate-recipes) and [orange](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes). The campari gives the [sorbet](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes) a sharp kick that works beautifully alongside the dark chocolate.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Chocolate ganache

-   100g of [dark chocolate](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/chocolate-recipes), from Madagascar
-   180g of double cream

#### Sorbet

-   1.2l water
-   400g of sugar
-   280ml of [orange juice](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes)
-   30ml of Campari

#### Crumble

-   100g of flour
-   100g of sugar
-   100g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   60g of almond flour

-   6cm ring mould 4
-   Ice cream maker

1

To make the sorbet, combine the water and sugar and bring to the boil to form a syrup. Set aside to cool

-   1.2l water
-   400g of sugar

2

Add the orange juice and Campari and churn in an ice cream maker. Freeze until ready to serve

-   280ml of [orange juice](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes)
-   30ml of Campari

3

Melt the chocolate in a microwave then stir in the cream until combined. Pour the mixture into a 6cm ring mould and place in the fridge to set

-   100g of [dark chocolate](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/chocolate-recipes), from Madagascar
-   180g of double cream

4

To make the crumble, mix the flour with the sugar and butter, then knead in the almond flour. When the dough is combined and consistent, place in the freezer

-   100g of flour
-   100g of sugar
-   100g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   60g of almond flour

5

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4

6

Once the dough has frozen, cut into small chunks and cook in the oven for approximately 7 minutes, until golden

7

To serve, remove the chocolate from the rings by warming with your hands and place onto chilled plates. Top with the crumble, a quenelle of sorbet and garnish with freeze dried raspberries and a mint leaf

-   [raspberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raspberry-recipes), dehydrated for sprinkling
-   4 [mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes)
