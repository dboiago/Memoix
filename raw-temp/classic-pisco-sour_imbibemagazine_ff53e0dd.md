The classic Pisco Sour is one of the best examples of how egg whites can bolster the character of a cocktail.

## Ingredients

-   3 oz. pisco
-   [3/4 oz. simple syrup (1:1) or 2 tsp. sugar](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   1 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1 fresh egg white (pasteurized if you like)
-   2 dashes Angostura bitters

## Preparation

In a shaker, combine the pisco, simple syrup, lemon juice and egg white and shake vigorously with ice. Strain into a glass and dash with bitters.
