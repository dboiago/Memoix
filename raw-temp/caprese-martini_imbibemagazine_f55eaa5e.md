According to Jac’s on Bond head bartender Albert Nieto, this Caprese Martini, which is inspired by the Caprese Salad, took two years of trial and error to perfect. The result is a balanced, savory cocktail that fans of dirty Martinis will love. It features vodka infused with olive oil, tomatoes, basil, and a couple dashes of balsamic vinegar.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. olive oil, tomato, and basil-infused vodka
-   1 1/2 oz. blanc vermouth (Jac's on Bond uses Lustau Blanco)
-   2 dashes balsamic vinegar
-   pinch of salt
-   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
-   Olive Oil, Tomato, and Basil-Infused Vodka
-   16 oz. vodka
-   2 basil leaves
-   1 medium tomato
-   4 oz. extra-virgin olive oil

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a mixing glass with ice and stir to chill. Strain into a chilled glass. Garnish.  
**Olive Oil, Tomato, and Basil-Infused Vodka**Combine the vodka, tomato, and basil in a large jar. Freeze for 24 hours. Strain vodka through a fine-mesh strainer and return to the jar. Add the olive oil, shake well, and freeze for 12 hours. After 12 hours, the oil will freeze in one hard layer on the top, remove oil layer before using and store in fridge.

Note: The bar recommends using the infused vodka within 3 to 5 days “to get the best-tasting product.”
