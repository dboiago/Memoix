G&T pops could be the coolest contribution to your next neighborhood barbecue.

What’s better than a classic G&T? One frozen in popsicle form! Whip up a batch of these cucumber-studded Gin & Tonic popsicles for the coolest contribution to your next neighborhood BBQ.

## Ingredients

Yield:6

-   3 cups tonic water
-   1 1/2 oz. gin
-   1/4 lime, juiced
-   1/2 cup cucumber slices

## Preparation

In a pitcher, combine all ingredients, except cucumber slices, and stir. Place cucumber slices into popsicle molds, then pour in tonic mix. Freeze overnight before serving. Makes about 6 popsicles, depending on the size of your mold.
