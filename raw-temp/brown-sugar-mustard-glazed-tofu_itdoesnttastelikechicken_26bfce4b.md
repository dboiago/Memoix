## To make the Brown Sugar & Mustard Glazed Tofu:

![Cut a diamond patter and marinate the tofu.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/brown-sugar-and-mustard-glazed-tofu-ham-roast-holiday-vegan-vegetarian-01.jpg)

Prepare the tofu by draining it, and then pressing it for 30 minutes - 1 hour. I love using my [Tofuture tofu press](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B01698J0RU/ref=as_li_qf_asin_il_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=itdottalich-20&creative=9325&linkCode=as2&creativeASIN=B01698J0RU&linkId=4ff7df6a81ba367b4035022ec12f5eb4), but you can also follow [these instructions](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/ultimate-guide-pressing-tofu/) for a DIY tofu pressing method. Pressing the tofu squeezes excess water out so that the tofu can suck up more of the marinade and become more flavourful!

Once pressed, remove the tofu and pat dry. Use a knife to score a diamond pattern across the surface of the tofu cutting about ¼ inch deep. Add the soy sauce and liquid smoke to a sealable bag or container, then add the tofu. Let the tofu marinate for a minimum of 1 hour or as long as overnight in the fridge, flipping the tofu every now and then to ensure both sides get marinated.

![Insert whole cloves into the tofu.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/brown-sugar-and-mustard-glazed-tofu-ham-roast-holiday-vegan-vegetarian-02.jpg)

When ready to roast the tofu, preheat your oven to 375F (190C). Line a small baking dish with parchment paper.

Remove the tofu from the marinade and place on the prepared baking dish. Poke a whole clove into the center of each diamond. Bake for 50 - 60 minutes until the tofu is a dark golden brown.

![Whisk up the simple glaze while the tofu is baking.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/brown-sugar-and-mustard-glazed-tofu-ham-roast-holiday-vegan-vegetarian-03.jpg)

While the tofu is baking make the glaze by adding the brown sugar, vegan butter, water, mustard, garlic, and cinnamon to a small pot. Put over medium-high heat, bring to a simmer while whisking often, and cook for about two minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.

![The tofu should be dark golden brown.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/brown-sugar-and-mustard-glazed-tofu-ham-roast-holiday-vegan-vegetarian-04.jpg)

This is how the tofu should look after baking for 50 - 60 minutes.

![Pour the glaze over the tofu and bake more.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/brown-sugar-and-mustard-glazed-tofu-ham-roast-holiday-vegan-vegetarian-05.jpg)

Once the tofu has baked and is golden brown, remove from the oven, pour the prepared glaze on top, then return to the oven and bake for another 10 - 15 minutes until the glaze is bubbling.

![Brown Sugar & Mustard Glazed Tofu! Just 10 ingredients and easy to make. Inspired by glazed ham this tofu roast is the perfect centrepiece for Easter dinner or a holiday feast. #itdoesnttastelikechicken #veganrecipes #easterrecipe](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/brown-sugar-and-mustard-glazed-tofu-ham-roast-holiday-vegan-vegetarian-06.jpg)

Remove from the oven and let cool 10 minutes. Remove the cloves using a fork or [kitchen tweezers](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0799NTCM3/ref=as_li_qf_asin_il_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=itdottalich-20&creative=9325&linkCode=as2&creativeASIN=B0799NTCM3&linkId=193726d99243cd05ed486ea6fc886498) before serving. I also like to use a pastry brush to gently brush excess glaze off the top of the tofu so you can see the diamond pattern better for presentation. Enjoy hot or cold!
