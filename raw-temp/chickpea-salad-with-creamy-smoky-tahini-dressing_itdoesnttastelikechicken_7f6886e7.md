## Ingredients:

-   **Chickpeas**: The hearty, protein-rich base. Canned and rinsed for ease.
-   **Cucumber**: Adds fresh crunch and coolness.
-   **Cherry tomatoes**: Juicy and sweet with a pop of color.
-   **Red onion**: Sharp bite and vibrant contrast.
-   **Fresh dill**: Herby brightness. Try parsley or cilantro instead.
-   **Roasted salted pepitas**: Crunchy, nutty topping. Sub pistachios, sunflower seeds, or slivered almonds.
-   **Tahini dressing**: Tahini, olive oil (or water), lime juice, Dijon, maple syrup or agave, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt & pepper.

![This chickpea salad is packed with healthy ingredients!](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Chickpea-Salad-with-Creamy-Smoky-Tahini-Dressing-easy-protein-vegan-dressing-simple-best-recipe-01.jpg)

## HOW TO MAKE CHICKPEA SALAD:

1.  **Make the dressing:** In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the tahini, olive oil, lime juice, smoked paprika, Dijon, maple syrup, salt, garlic powder, and black pepper. Add water 1 tablespoon at a time, whisking until the dressing is creamy and pourable.

![Make the simple creamy tahini dressing with smoked paprika for a hint of smokiness.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Chickpea-Salad-with-Creamy-Smoky-Tahini-Dressing-easy-protein-vegan-dressing-simple-best-recipe-02.jpg)

2.  **Assemble the salad:** In a large bowl, combine the chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, and dill. Pour about half of the dressing over the salad and toss to coat. Taste and add more dressing as desired, you may not need it all. Sprinkle the pepitas on top just before serving.

![Assemble the salad. In a large bowl add the chickpeas, veggies, herbs, drizzle with dressing, and toss well.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Chickpea-Salad-with-Creamy-Smoky-Tahini-Dressing-easy-protein-vegan-dressing-simple-best-recipe-03.jpg)

3.  **Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days. If prepping ahead, keep the pepitas separate and add just before serving so they stay crunchy. The flavor gets even better as it sits!

#### For the smoky tahini dressing:

-   ▢ ¼ cup [tahini](https://amzn.to/4i5LcLb)
-   ▢ 2 tablespoons [olive oil](https://amzn.to/3FNGBNa), (sub water for oil-free)
-   ▢ 2 tablespoons [lime juice](https://amzn.to/3FLDPYz), (or lemon juice)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [smoked paprika](https://amzn.to/3SM4XgD)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [Dijon mustard](https://amzn.to/47g0yYs)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [maple syrup or agave](https://amzn.to/45WnpHC)
-   ▢ ¾ teaspoon [salt](https://amzn.to/4jq9UHf), (or to taste)
-   ▢ ½ teaspoon [garlic powder](https://amzn.to/40udWX6)
-   ▢ ¼ teaspoon [black pepper](https://amzn.to/3Mt9F04)
-   ▢ 1 -2 tablespoons water, as needed

#### For the salad:

-   ▢ 2 cans (14oz each) [chickpeas](https://amzn.to/3MsbFpd), drained and rinsed (3 cups)
-   ▢ ½ English cucumber, diced (1 cup)
-   ▢ 1 cup grape or cherry tomatoes, halved
-   ▢ ½ small red onion, finely chopped
-   ▢ ¼ cup fresh dill, finely chopped (or sub parsley or cilantro)
-   ▢ ¼ cup roasted salted pepitas, (or sub pistachios, sunflower seeds, or slivered almonds)

-   **Make the dressing:** In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the tahini, olive oil, lime juice, smoked paprika, Dijon, maple syrup, salt, garlic powder, and black pepper. Add water 1 tablespoon at a time, whisking until the dressing is creamy and pourable.
    
    ![](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/square02-Chickpea-Salad-with-Creamy-Smoky-Tahini-Dressing-easy-protein-vegan-dressing-simple-best-recipe-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Assemble the salad:** In a large mixing bowl, combine the chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, and dill. Pour about half of the dressing over the salad and toss to coat. Taste and add more dressing as desired, you may not need it all. Sprinkle the pepitas on top just before serving.
    
    ![](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/square07-Chickpea-Salad-with-Creamy-Smoky-Tahini-Dressing-easy-protein-vegan-dressing-simple-best-recipe-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Storage:** Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days. If prepping ahead, keep the pepitas separate and add just before serving so they stay crunchy. The flavor gets even better as it sits!

-   Stuff into pitas or wraps with leafy greens for a satisfying sandwich.
