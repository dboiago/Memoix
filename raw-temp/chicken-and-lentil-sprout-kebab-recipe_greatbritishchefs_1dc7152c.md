[Chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-recipes) and lentil sprouts come together to create an incredibly light and succulent [kebab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kebab-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kebab-recipes) in this beautiful recipe from Dayashankar Sharma. Served on a base of [yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/yoghurt-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/yoghurt-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/yoghurt-recipes) mixed with Kasundi – a mustard sauce popular in Bengali cuisine that can be found in specialist grocers or online – and topped with a creamy [beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beetroot-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beetroot-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beetroot-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beetroot-recipes) dip, it's a colourful dish which shows how versatile [Indian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes) kebabs can be.

-   1/2 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped, plus a few small sprigs to garnish

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

#### Beetroot dip

-   1 [beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes), cooked and finely chopped
-   100g of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   1 knob of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [green chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped

1

Heat a generous glug of oil in a pan over a medium heat and add the chicken. Stir-fry for 2 minutes, then add the ginger, green chilli, turmeric powder, chilli flakes, mango and a pinch of salt. Turn the heat down and cook for a further 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through

-   vegetable oil, for cooking
-   600g of [boneless chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), roughly chopped
-   40g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely chopped
-   20g of [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 tsp [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   2 tsp [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 [green mango](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), peeled and finely chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

2

Remove the chicken mixture from the heat and leave to cool. Meanwhile, blanch the lentil sprouts in boiling water for 30 seconds, then drain

-   300g of lentil sprouts

3

Mix together the chicken mixture and the sprouts and finely chop everything together until minced and well combined. Stir in the garam masala, coriander and breadcrumbs, then form into 8 tight, dense patties and place in the fridge to firm up for 30 minutes

-   8g of [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   1/2 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped, plus a few small sprigs to garnish
-   20g of fresh breadcrumbs

4

While the kebabs chill, make the beetroot dip by mixing all the ingredients together. Season and set aside

-   1 [beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes), cooked and finely chopped
-   100g of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   1 knob of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [green chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped

6

To finish cooking the kebabs, heat a thin layer of oil in a wide frying pan over a medium-high heat. Once hot, carefully add the patties, frying for a few minutes on each side until crisp and golden

7

To serve, spoon the mustard yoghurt onto each plate, then place a kebab on top of each. Top with a little of the beetroot dip, then garnish with a coriander sprig
