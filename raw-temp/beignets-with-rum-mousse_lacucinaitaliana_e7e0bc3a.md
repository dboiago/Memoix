## Over a double boiler, melt the chocolate, then let it cool to 83°F....

**Cooks in:** _30mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   18 small beignets
-   3/4 C. of cream
-   3 1/2 OZ. of dark chocolate
-   Rum

### Method

1

Over a double boiler, melt the chocolate, then let it cool to 83°F.

2

Then, combine the melted chocolate with the whipped cream and 1 spoonful of rum to form a soft mousse.

3

Use the cream as a filling for the beignets. Fill the beignets just before serving for a perfect result. Serve immediately.
