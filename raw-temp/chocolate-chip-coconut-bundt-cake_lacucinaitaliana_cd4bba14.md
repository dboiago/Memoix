**Cooks in:** _1h 20mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   1/2 C. of coconut milk
-   1 C. of Italian 00 flour, plus extra
-   3/4 STICK of softened butter, plus extra
-   2 OZ. of dark chocolate
-   1/4 C. of cream
-   1/3 C. of coconut flour
-   1/3 C. of powdered sugar
-   2 TBSP. of chocolate chips
-   1 TBSP. of Acacia honey
-   1/2 TSP. of baking powder
-   2 of eggs

### Method

1

Soak the coconut flour in the coconut milk for 10 minutes. Mix the baking powder with the 00 flour. Cream 4 tablespoons of butter with the powdered sugar and the honey; when they are thoroughly combined, incorporate the eggs one at a time, the coconut flour and coconut milk mixture, and the 00 flour and baking powder; finally, mix in the chocolate chips.

2

Butter and flour a bundt pan (8-inch diameter); pour in the batter and bake at 350°F for about 30 minutes. Chop the chocolate. Bring the cream to a boil, then remove it from the heat; add in the chopped chocolate and 2 tablespoons of butter, then mix well to obtain a ganache, which you will use to glaze the cake.

3

Let the ganache cool at room temperature. Remove the bundt cake from the oven, remove it from the pan, and let it cool on a grate. Pour the ganache onto the cake and top with thin slices of fresh coconut.
