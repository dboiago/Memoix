Traditionally, Ascot pie is like a long, rectangular pork [pie](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pie-recipes "Pie"), made with hot water pastry and eaten cold. Adam Smith's [Michelin star](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/michelin-star-recipes "Michelin star") version is as luxurious as a pie can possibly get, containing a mixture of lardo-wrapped chicken, [guinea fowl](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/guinea-fowl-recipes "Guinea fowl") and [foie gras](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/foie-gras "Foie gras"), with a solid core of pure truffle. Instead of a pastry lid the pie is topped with more truffle and a clarified consommé jelly. The poached rhubarb and verjus gel on the side provide sharp contrast with the incredibly indulgent filling. It requires plenty of time and lots of classical technique to make, but as it is served at room temperature you can prepare everything in advance.

This recipe will make a single large pie big enough to feed 12, so make sure you have the right pie tin – Adam uses a 24cm oval game pie tin, which are available online.
