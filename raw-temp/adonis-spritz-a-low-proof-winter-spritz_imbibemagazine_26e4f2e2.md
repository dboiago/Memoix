A cold-weather sparkler.

Spritzes seem made for summer, but this mashup with a classic sherry cocktail manages to winterize the sparkling drink. “This spritz is the marriage of two beloved classics, a stirred sherry and vermouth cocktail named Adonis, and a classic Italian spritz,” says Bethany Heinze, co-owner and beverage director at Vern’s in Charleston. “Cappelletti is wine-based and has the cherry, herbal notes of vermouth, so I built a minimalist recipe that highlights the nutty sherry and bitter aperitif. It’s simple in execution, but tastes complex.”

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 Aperitivo Cappelletti
-   1 1/2 oz. amontillado sherry (Vern's uses Valdespino)
-   5 dashes orange bitters
-   3 oz. dry sparkling wine

## Preparation

Combine the first 3 ingredients in a wine glass and add ice. Top with sparkling wine.
