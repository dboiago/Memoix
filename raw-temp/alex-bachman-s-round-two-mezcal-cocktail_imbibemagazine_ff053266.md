**RECIPE:** _Alex Bachman for Gideon Sweet, Chicago  
(Note: This bar has permanently closed.)_

![](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/gideon-sweet-round-two-vertical-crdt-emma-janzen.jpg)

A mezcal cocktail that’ll warm you to the core.

A small measure of sotol adds an extra layer of complexity to this mezcal cocktail from [Imbibe 75](https://imbibemagazine.com/issue/no-71-janfeb-2018-the-imbibe-75/) person to watch Alex Bachman. At the bar they use a housemade barley bitters, but Bachman says black walnut bitters from Fee Bros. will lend the same complexity.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. mezcal (Bachman uses Siete Misterios DobaYej)
-   1/4 oz. reposado sotol
-   1/2 oz. sweet vermouth
-   1/2 oz. Gran Classico
-   1 dash black walnut bitters

## Preparation

Stir all the ingredients together with ice in a mixing glass. Strain into a chilled glass.
