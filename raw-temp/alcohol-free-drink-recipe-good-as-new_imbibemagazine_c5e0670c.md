**RECIPE:** _Cory Miller, [Sweet Afton](https://www.sweetaftonbar.com/), Astoria, New York_

![](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/good-as-new-horizontal-crdt-noah-fecks.jpg)

Orgeat adds a layer of nutty flavor to this refreshing alcohol-free drink recipe.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. fresh grapefruit juice
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1/2 oz. orgeat
-   [1/4 oz. simple syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   Soda water

## Preparation

Combine the grapefruit juice, lemon juice, orgeat and simple syrup in shaker and shake with ice until cold. Strain into a highball glass over ice. Top with soda water. Garnish.
