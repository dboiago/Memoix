If you like a good sweet vermouth Spritz, you’ll love this iteration with orange curaçao.

Orange curaçao adds a dash of bittersweet depth to this lush Spritz.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. sweet vermouth (Monteverde uses Carpano Antica)
-   ½ oz. dry orange curaçao (Monteverde uses Pierre Ferrand)
-   1½ oz. club soda
-   2 oz. Prosecco
-   Dash Angostura bitters

## Preparation

Combine the ingredients in a wine glass, except the Prosecco. Add ice and stir to chill. Top with Prosecco. Garnish.
