For whiskey lovers, the [Boulevardier](https://imbibemagazine.com/the-boulevardier-cocktail-recipe/) offers a tempting twist on the classic [Negroni](https://imbibemagazine.com/negroni-recipe/). In this riff from the Ace Hotel in New York City, coffee adds an extra boost of flavor.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. bourbon
-   1 oz. Campari
-   ¾ oz. coffee liqueur
-   ¼ oz. Punt e Mes

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a mixing glass with ice and stir to chill. Strain into a coupe and garnish.
