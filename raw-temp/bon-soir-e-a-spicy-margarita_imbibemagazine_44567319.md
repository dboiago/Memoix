This cocktail from Hailey Knight and Casey Knight (no relation) of Charleston’s Félix Cocktails et Cuisine takes the spicy Margarita for an extra-savory spin. Saline lends a saltiness, while Lillet Blanc rounds out the edges of the fresh lime juice. Meanwhile, Knight recommends using your favorite hot sauce to spice it up. “The key is to use a vinegar-based hot sauce to complement the cocktail’s flavors rather than compete with them,” she says. “I’m partial to [Slap Ya Mama](https://store.slapyamama.com/collections/cajun-hot-sauces?srsltid=AfmBOooclXzrO_PHO0cdES51nFzVdymw0S7c-T1PTUS-UAItTKmIoAwn) hot sauce.”

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. blanco tequila
-   3/4 oz. Lillet Blanc
-   3/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1/4 oz. saline solution
-   2 dashes of hot sauce

## Preparation

Rim a chilled glass with tajin. Combine the ingredients in a shaker with ice. Shake to chill, then strain into the prepared glass.
