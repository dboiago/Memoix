Raspberry-infused agave meets some jalapeño spice in this beer-capped tequila sipper.

Raspberry-infused agave syrup meets some jalapeño spice in this beer-capped tequila cocktail from Los Angeles’ [Good Times at Davey Wayne’s](http://goodtimesatdaveywaynes.com/).

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. jalapeño-infused tequila
-   3/4 oz. raspberry agave syrup
-   1/2 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   2 oz. light beer
-   1 dash absinthe

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients, except the beer, and shake with ice. Strain into an ice-filled glass, top with beer and garnish.

For the raspberry agave syrup: Muddle 1/4 cup of fresh raspberries in 1/4 cup of agave syrup. Strain before using.

For the jalapeño-infused tequila: Infuse 1 3/4 of a sliced, fresh jalapeños in 750 ml. of blanco tequila for 2 hours, or until desired heat level is reached. Strain before using.
