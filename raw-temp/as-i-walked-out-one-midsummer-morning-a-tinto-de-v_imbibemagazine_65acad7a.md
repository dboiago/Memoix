Cold-brew coffee meets red wine, sherry, lemon and tonic.

Named after [a memoir by British poet Laurie Lee](https://www.amazon.com/Walked-Midsummer-Morning-Nonpareil-Books/dp/1567923925/?tag=imbibmagaz-20) about his travels through Spain, this variation on the Tinto de Verano brings two Spanish gems together with cold-brew, lemon and tonic for a refreshing highball that’s great year-round.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. full-bodied Spanish Garnacha (Cox uses Familia Bastiada Paraje de Titos Garnacha)
-   ½ oz. Lustau East India Solera Sherry
-   ¾ oz. cold-brew coffee
-   Scant ½ oz. rich simple syrup (2:1 brown sugar to water)
-   ¼ oz. fresh lemon juice
-   Tonic water, to top

## Preparation

Add the first 5 ingredients to a shaker and shake with ice for 5 seconds. Double strain into an ice-filled glass filled and top with tonic. Garnish.
