If your looking to shred some pounds after christmas or if you just want to try out a vegan recipe, This is a Great recipe for your lunch at work or a light snack and it only takes 20 minutes to make. Give it a try!

**Ackee and Avocado salad**

-   1 can Ackee 540g
-   1tsp sea salt
-   1tsp black pepper
-   1tsp Thyme
-   1tsp scotch bonnet pepper
-   3 cloves garlic
-   1/4 onion
-   1 spring onion
-   1 tbsp. Cocunut oil
-   1/4 Lettuce
-   1 tomato
-   1/4  red pepper
-   1/4 Green pepper
-   1/2 cucumber
-   1 avocado
-   Lemon juice

1.  Chop and Combine 1/4 Lettuce, 1 tomato, 1/4  redpepper, 1/4 Green pepper, 1/2 cucumber  
    1 avacado in a bowl and mix together. Then place In fridge.
2.  On a frying pan on high heat use 1 tbsp. Cocunut oil sortay 1/2 chopped onion, 3 cloves of garlic 1 chopped spring onion 1tsp scotch bonnet pepper for 3 mins
3.  Then drain the tinned ackee and carefully pour into frying pan and let it sit.
4.  Add 1 tsp sea salt, 1tsp black pepper, 1tsp thyme and carefully fold over the ackee and simmer for 5 mins.
5.  Combine ackee with salad squeeze lemon juice and serve.
