A sumptuous brown shrimp sauce and an earthy [broccoli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/broccoli-recipes) purée flatter the pan-fried fillets of fish in [Tom Aikens](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/tom-aikens)' innovative dish. This black bream recipe takes just over an hour to cook so it's a good recipe for dinner with friends.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Broccoli purée

-   300g of [broccoli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broccoli-recipes)
-   100ml of white chicken stock
-   30ml of double cream
-   20g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   5g of [parsley leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), lightly chopped
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground, four turns

#### Brown shrimp

-   60g of [brown shrimp](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shrimp-recipes)
-   280g of [banana shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced
-   1 bay leaf, small
-   1 [bulb of garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 pinch of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), chopped
-   70g of [golden raisins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raisin-recipes)
-   45g of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes)
-   60ml of white wine vinegar
-   30ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   130g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground
-   olive oil

-   Blender

1

For the broccoli purée, wash and chop down finely from the tips of the florets until you reach the stalks

-   300g of [broccoli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broccoli-recipes)

2

In a large, flat casserole pan, bring the chicken stock, salt, pepper, cream and butter to a simmer

-   100ml of white chicken stock
-   30ml of double cream
-   20g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground, four turns

3

Add the broccoli and cover. Cook for 5-8 minutes so that the broccoli is just soft but still a vibrant green colour. Add the parsley for 30 seconds to wilt, then remove from the heat

-   5g of [parsley leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), lightly chopped

4

Strain and reserve the stock. Transfer the broccoli to a blender and pour in half of the stock. Blitz continuously until very smooth and consistent in colour. You may need to add a little more stock to achieve the right consistency. Remove from the blender and store in the fridge immediately

5

For the brown shrimp sauce, start by making a beurre noisette. Heat the butter in a pan until it turns a nut-brown colour, then remove from the heat and set aside

-   130g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

6

Place a separate pan over a low-medium heat and add a dash of olive oil. Add the shallots and sweat until tender, then add the seasoning, the golden raisins, garlic, thyme, bay leaf, capers, vinegar and lemon juice

-   280g of [banana shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   70g of [golden raisins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raisin-recipes)
-   1 bay leaf, small
-   45g of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes)
-   30ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   60ml of white wine vinegar
-   1 [bulb of garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 pinch of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   olive oil

7

Bring up to a simmer and cook until the raisins are soft. Then, add the beurre noisette to the pan along with the shrimps. Keep on a low heat until ready to serve

8

Meanwhile, add the purple sprouting broccoli to a pan of boiling, salted water and blanch until just cooked, approximately 1 minute - take care not to overcook. Once tender, refresh in some iced water

-   400g of [purple sprouting broccoli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/purple-sprouting-broccoli-recipes)
-   10g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

9

To cook the bream, add the olive oil to a large frying pan over a medium heat. Once hot, add the butter and when it begins to foam, add the bream, skin-side down, and cook for 6-8 minutes. Then, flip the bream fillets over, season, and cook for 1 minute on the flesh side

-   4 [black bream fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bream-recipes)
-   40g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   olive oil
-   10g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

10

Before serving, reheat the broccoli purée in a pan over a low heat

11

To serve, quenelle the purée onto plates. Squeeze a little lemon juice over the fish and place on top of the purée. Spoon some of the brown shrimp sauce over and around the dish. Arrange the broccoli spears on top and scatter a few toasted almonds and parsley around the plate. Serve immediately

-   60g of [whole almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes), toasted
-   1 pinch of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
