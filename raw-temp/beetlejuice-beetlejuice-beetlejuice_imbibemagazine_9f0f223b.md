**Recipe:** _Paul Taylor for PUB Dread, Washington, D.C._

![spooky sips Beetlejuice](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/pub-dread-beetlejuice-horizontal2-crdt-farrah-skeiky.jpg)

For fans of the classic [Americano cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe-americano/), this bittersweet highball was on the menu of the 2017 [halloween-themed pop-up](https://imbibemagazine.com/derek-browns-halloween-playlist/) PUB Dread in Washington, DC. At the bar they make the drink with a special Twizzler Soda, but here’s how they suggest mixing your own version at home.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. Aperol
-   1/4 oz. Campari
-   1/2 oz. sweet vermouth
-   1 oz. strawberry syrup
-   1 barspoon Pernod absinthe
-   3 oz. sparkling water

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a highball over ice, stir, then garnish.

**Strawberry Syrup**1 cup granulated sugar  
1 cup strawberries (tops removed and cut in half)  
1 cup water

Heat all the ingredients in saucepan until the sugar is dissolved. Allow to cool, then strain into a bottle and refrigerate.
