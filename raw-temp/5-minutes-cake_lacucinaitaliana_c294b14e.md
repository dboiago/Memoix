## Pour the two flours onto your work surface. Cut the butter into small pieces. Knead the butter, sugar, egg, a pinch of salt and a shot glass of rum in...

**Cooks in:** _2h 50mins \+ 2h_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   200 G of farina 00
-   200 G of farina di mandorle
-   200 G of burro ammorbidito
-   200 G of zucchero
-   un uovo
-   rum
-   sale

### Method

1

Pour the two flours onto your work surface. Cut the butter into small pieces. Knead the butter, sugar, egg, a pinch of salt and a shot glass of rum into the flours. Knead together for 5 minutes. Line a baking pan (9 1/2 inches in diameter) with parchment paper, or butter it generously.

2

Put the mixture into the pan, pat it down and smooth the surface, then use the tines of a fork to make a checkerboard pattern on the top. Bake the cake at 350°F for about 30 minutes, then remove it from the oven and let it cool for 2 hours before serving.
