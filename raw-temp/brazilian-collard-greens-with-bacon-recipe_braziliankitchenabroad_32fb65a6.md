#### **_Dia da Consciencia Negra_** – The Brazilian Celebration

In Brazil, slavery wasn’t abolished until twenty-some years after the US, in May of 1888 – _AND_ this is not the date we celebrate our freedom, or our African roots.

**_Dia da Consciencia Negra_**, **Black Awareness Day or Black Consciousness Day**, is observed annually in Brazil, on November 20th, to celebrate the regained awareness by the Brazilian black community, and to highlight our great worth and contribution to the country.

The celebration takes place in November, particularly to honor Zumbi’s death – a Brazilian of Congo origins, who was a freedom fighter, and symbol of freedom for pioneering the movement to resist colonialism and slavery in Brazil.

And what better way to celebrate if not through food?

In the 150+ years since, Black families have commemorated freedom and liberation with food traditions that tell a story of the perseverance of our people.

## How to prepare Brazilian Collard Greens

1.  **Wash and pat dry the greens with paper towel**
    
    We don’t want the greens to be wet when they hit the pan as this would alter the end result of the dish. In this dish, the greens are gently sauteed to keep its texture somewhat firm. If there’s any water left in the leaves, the greens will steam instead and will become bitter.
    
2.  **Cut off the thicker part of the stems**
    
    ![Couve a Mineira - Brazilian collard greens with bacon](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/couve1-1.jpg)
    
3.  **Stack 2-3 leaves on top of each other, then roll them like a cigar, as tight as you can**
    
    ![Couve a Mineira - Brazilian collard greens with bacon](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/couve2-1.jpg)
    
4.  **Then, thinly slice it into a chiffonade  
    **
    
    This also step also helps prevent bitterness!  
    ![Couve a Mineira - Brazilian collard greens with bacon](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/couve3-1.jpg)
    
5.  **Dice the bacon**
    
    Or not, if you need a **vegan recipe**, just skip this step!
    
6.  **Dice the onion and mince the garlic**
    
    Or if you have that [Brazilian sofrito](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/brazilian-sofrito/) ready in the fridge, use that and save a few minutes of prep!
    

Then, on to sautéing it. Just heat up the [oil](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07BCWJY7J?linkCode=ssc&tag=onamzbrazil08-20&creativeASIN=B07BCWJY7J&asc_item-id=amzn1.ideas.13SWMAD4SQ7NS&ref_=aip_sf_list_spv_ons_mixed_d_asin), and cook the bacon until its crispy, then add the onion and the garlic until they smell oh-so-irresistibly delicious. And then… \*drum roll\* 

### The big secret for mastering this Brazilian collard greens recipe is…

**Turn off the heat** _**then**_ add the greens to the pan! Then quickly season with salt and pepper and toss until wilted a little bit. This step will make sure your greens have a nice bite to it, and aren’t bitter. 

This side dish is perfect for a quick and easy mid-week dinner. It tastes great with [rice](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/brazilian-white-rice/), [beans,](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/everyday-black-beans/) a nice piece of chicken, meat, or just eat the whole thing just by itself… straight off of the skillet, because… I’ve done it, so I won’t judge!

## Couve a Mineira | Brazilian Collard Greens with Bacon

Couve a Mineira aka Brazilian collard greens with bacon is a crisp, garlicky, side dish full of bacon bits. This easy collard greens recipe is THE perfect mid-week side dish your familia will fall in love with.

Servings 2 servings

Calories 107 kcal

Prep Time 10 minutes

Cook Time 5 minutes

Total Time 15 minutes

-   1 bunch of collard greens stems removed and thinly sliced
-   1-2 thick strips of smoked bacon diced
-   1/2 onion diced
-   2 colves of garlic minced
-   1 tbsp [of extra virgin olive oil](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07BCWJY7J?linkCode=ssc&tag=onamzbrazil08-20&creativeASIN=B07BCWJY7J&asc_item-id=amzn1.ideas.13SWMAD4SQ7NS&ref_=aip_sf_list_spv_ons_mixed_d_asin)
-   Salt and pepper to taste

_As an Amazon Associate and member of other affiliate programs, I earn from qualifying purchases._

-   Heat the olive oil in a medium skillet over medium to high heat
    
-   Add the bacon and cook until browned, about 3-4 mins
    
-   Add the onions and cook until transparent, about 2 mins
    
-   Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about a minute
    
-   Turn ff the heat and add the collard greens and toss gently until the greens wilt, about 1 minute
