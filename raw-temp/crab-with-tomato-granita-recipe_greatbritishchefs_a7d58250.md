[Graham Campbell](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/graham-campbell)'s sublime crab with tomato [granita](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/granita-recipes) recipe produces a fresh and vibrant dish, using beautiful micro herbs, [king crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collection/alaska-king-crab-recipes) and a refreshing [tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/tomato-recipes) consommé granita to evoke the flavours of [summer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/summer-recipes).

First published in 2015

-   Muslin cloth
-   Blender

2

Scrape into a bowl and add the cold water, white wine vinegar, lemon zest, lemon juice and white wine. Mix well, then leave to stand for 1 hour at room temperature

-   750ml of water, cold
-   50g of white wine
-   1/4 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced and zested
-   18g of white wine vinegar

3

Add the mixture to a muslin bag suspended over a jug and leave to hang overnight - in the morning all of the liquid should have dripped through. Taste for seasoning and place in a container in the freezer

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

4

Scrape the mixture with a fork every hour until you are left with granita-style ice crystals. Keep in the freezer until ready to serve

5

For the vinaigrette, add the white wine vinegar and Dijon mustard to a mixing bowl. Pour in the olive oil in a thin, steady stream, mixing with a whisk as you go. Once all of the olive oil is incorporated, season with salt and set aside

-   20g of white wine vinegar
-   1 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   80g of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
