**RECIPE:** _Jason Stevens for [Mattie’s](http://www.mattiesaustin.com/), Austin_

![sidecar](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/matties-austin-sidecar-riff-horizontal-crdt-nick-simonite.jpg)

Calvados amplifies the fruity side of aged brandy in this twist on the classic [Sidecar](https://imbibemagazine.com/sidecar-recipe/) cocktail.

## Ingredients

-   3/4 oz. Calvados
-   1/2 oz. Cognac
-   1 oz. Cointreau
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   Half rim of vanilla sugar

## Preparation

Prepare a coupe by rimming the edge with vanilla sugar. Combine the ingredients in a shaker with ice and shake well. Double strain into the sugar-rimmed coupe.

**Vanilla Sugar**Combine 1 cup of fine white sugar with 1/2 a scraped vanilla bean in a sealed jar. Allow to sit for several days until the sugar take on a vanilla aroma and flavor.
