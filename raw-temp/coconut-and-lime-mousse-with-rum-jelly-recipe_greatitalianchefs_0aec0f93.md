A refreshing combination of lime and coconut with punchy rum jelly adding extra kick, this creamy mousse recipe by [Giorgio and Gian Pietro](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/chefs/giorgio-gian-pietro) makes a truly stunning alcoholic [dessert](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/italian-dessert-recipes). This dish is a great option for dinner parties as it can be put together quickly at the last minute – just remember to prepare the rum jelly in advance to give it enough time to set.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Mousse

-   500g of coconut purée
-   125ml of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   175g of cream
-   50g of icing sugar

#### Jelly

-   350g of water
-   180g of brown sugar
-   290g of [dark rum](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rum-cocktail-recipes)
-   9 gelatine leaves, soaked in cold water

-   Siphon bottle or cream whipper

1

For the rum jelly, boil the water with the brown sugar. Add the rum and gelatine, then transfer to a tray to set

-   350g of water
-   180g of brown sugar
-   9 gelatine leaves, soaked in cold water
-   290g of [dark rum](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rum-cocktail-recipes)

2

For the mousse, mix the coconut and lime juice with the cream and icing sugar until thoroughly combined. Transfer the mixture to a siphon with 1 gas charge to form a mousse

-   500g of coconut purée
-   125ml of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   175g of cream
-   50g of icing sugar

3

To serve, arrange 6 pieces of popcorn on the bottom of a glass, covering with a generous helping of the mousse. Cut the rum jelly into rough chunks and sprinkle over the mousse, topping with a little more popcorn and grated lime zest

-   100g of [popcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/popcorn-recipes), salted
-   [lime zest](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
