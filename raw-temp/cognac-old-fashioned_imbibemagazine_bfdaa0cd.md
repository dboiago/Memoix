**RECIPE:** _Mike Lay for Bon Temps, Los Angeles (Note: This bar has permanently closed.)_

October 22, 2019

![Cognac Old Fashioned](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/cognac-old-fashioned-bon-temps-crdt-.jpg)

For this Old Fashioned-inspired cocktail, Bon Temps beverage director Mike Lay mixes Cognac with rich simple syrup, bitters, maraschino liqueur and absinthe.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. Cognac (Lay uses Dudognon 5-Year Selection)
-   ¼ oz. maraschino liqueur
-   [¼ oz. rich simple syrup (2:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/rich-simple-syrup-recipe/)
-   4 dashes Angostura bitters
-   4 dashes orange bitters
-   4 dashes Peychaud’s bitters
-   Absinthe

## Preparation

Prepare a rocks glass by coating with a small amount of absinthe. Combine the remaining ingredients in a mixing glass and briefly stir to chill without adding much dilution. Strain over a large ice cube in the prepped glass, then twist the citrus peels over the drink and use as a garnish.
