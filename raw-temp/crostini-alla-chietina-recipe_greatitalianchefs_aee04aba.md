Battered and fried bread slices topped with anchovy fillets and capers: does anything sound better?

_Crostini alla chietina_ hail from the town of Chieti, in central [Abruzzo](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/abruzzese-recipes). They are often served as a snack alongside an [aperitif](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/features/best-italian-cocktails-aperitivo), or as part of a spread of antipasti.

The recipe is, as many [traditional Italian recipes](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/traditional-italian-recipes), pretty straightforward, and counts on a short list of ingredients. Opt for the best anchovies you can find, here, for their quality will determine how good these will turn out.

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Heat the olive oil in a large skillet set over a medium heat

2

Whisk the eggs with the milk. Dip the bread slices in the egg mixture and then fry them in the hot oil until deeply golden on both sides (about 3 minutes per side)

3

Transfer them to a plate. Top each bread slice with an anchovy fillet and a caper. Serve right away – these are best when freshly fried
