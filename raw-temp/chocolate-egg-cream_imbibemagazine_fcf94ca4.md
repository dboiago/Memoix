The family recipe from Russ & Daughters in NYC.

Careful preparation is at the heart of a perfect chocolate egg cream at Russ & Daughters, where they’re known for this and other variations on the [classic frothy delight.](https://imbibemagazine.com/egg-cream/)

## Ingredients

-   4 Tbsp. Fox’s U-bet chocolate syrup
-   4 oz. whole milk
-   Chilled seltzer

## Preparation

Add the syrup and milk (it’s essential that the milk is very cold) to a tall glass. Add seltzer until the level is one inch below the top of the glass. Stir in a back-and-forth motion (not a circular swirl) to combine. Let the mixture settle for a moment, then top with additional seltzer, producing a thick, white head of foam. Serve with a straw.
