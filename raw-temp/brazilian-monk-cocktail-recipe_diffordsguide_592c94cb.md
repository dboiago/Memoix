### Glass:

Serve in a [Hurricane glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 fl oz

[Hazelnut liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/927/fratello-hazelnut-liqueur)

1 fl oz

[Galliano Espresso Coffee liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/4564/galliano-espresso)

1 fl oz

Dark crème de cacao liqueur

3 scoop

Vanilla ice cream

Garnish: Crumbled Cadbury's Flake bar

## How to make:

BLEND ingredients with 12oz scoop of crushed ice. Pour into glass and serve with straws.

## Allergens:

Recipe contains the following **allergens:**

-   Hazelnut liqueur - [Nuts](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/3814/cocktails/peanuts-and-tree-nuts-allergy)
-   Vanilla ice cream - [Dairy](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/4176/cocktails/milk-and-dairy-protein-allergy-and-intolerance)

## Review:

Nutty and rich dessert in a glass.
