## Chicken and Red Bean Soup

Cuisines: [Chinese](https://dailycookingquest.com/cuisines/chinese.html)

Prep Time: 30 mins

Cook Time: 2 hours

Total Time: 2 hours 30 mins

Serves: 4

[Print Recipe](https://dailycookingquest.com/card/chicken-and-red-bean-soup.html)

### Ingredients

-   1 free range chicken (Indonesian: ayam kampung), cut into 4-8 pieces
-   150 gram dried red beans, washed and drained
-   2 medium size carrots, peeled and cut into bite size pieces
-   2 medium size tomatoes, cored (cut a cone around the core and remove it)
-   2 teaspoon salt
-   2 liter water

### Instructions

1.  Place all ingredients in a soup pot and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 1 to 2 hours or until chicken is tender and fully cooked and the bean is soft to your likeness. Adjust salt as needed.
2.  Remove from heat and serve warm.
