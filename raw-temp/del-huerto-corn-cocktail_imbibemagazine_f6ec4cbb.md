Sweet corn perfectly complements the earthy notes of tequila.

One of fall’s most delicious ingredients complements the earthy notes of tequila in this fresh corn cocktail. 

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. blanco tequila
-   2 oz. grilled corn (by weight)
-   3/4 oz.  fresh lime juice
-   1/2 oz. agave syrup

## Preparation

Muddle the corn in a shaker tin. Add the remaining ingredients with ice and shake to chill. Double strain over fresh ice into a rocks glass rimmed with salt.
