A simple mix of gin, crème de cassis, lemon, and simple syrup.

Taking note from Dick Bradsell’s classic [Bramble](https://imbibemagazine.com/classic-bramble-recipe/) recipe, Fessier Stone uses crème de cassis instead of blackberry liqueur for a tart twist.

## Preparation

In a glass, combine the cassis, gin, lemon and simple syrup. Stir to combine. Top with crushed ice.
