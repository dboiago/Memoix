I75er Kuduowl’s nostalgic take on a classic.

Imbibe 75 Place to Watch Kuduowl is a destination bar outside of downtown Los Angeles, where bar owner and bartender Bad Birdy uses ingredients that people don’t often see in cocktails. Case in point: this Bubblegum Daiquiri, which she says is sure to “evoke emotion and get people smiling.”

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. bubblegum-infused rum
-   3/4 oz. Galliano
-   3/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   2 drops of 20% saline solution

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a shaker with ice. Shake to chill and strain into a chilled glass.  
**Bubblegum-Infused Rum**Add 3 1/2 oz. of Hubba Bubba Original Bubble Gum to 10 oz. of light rum in a food-safe vessel and let it infuse for 24 hours. Double strain the mixture and bottle. Does not expire.
