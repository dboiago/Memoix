These delicious bite-sized delicacies of rich cheesy [potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/potato-recipes) are aways a real crowdpleaser, and while they look impressive they are surprisingly [simple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-recipes) to make – all you need is a piping bag with a star-shaped nozzle.

First published in 2020

### Metric

### Imperial

-   800g of [Maris Piper potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled and chopped
-   2 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   60g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   80g of Comté cheese, finely grated
-   1 pinch of [nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® egg](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/), beaten with a pinch of salt for glazing

-   Piping bag with star nozzle
-   Potato ricer

1

Cook the potatoes in salted boiling water until soft, about 20 minutes. Drain and leave to dry out a little in the colander for a few minutes – be aware than the potatoes still need to be warm for the next step, however

2

Preheat the oven to 190°C/gas mark 5 and line a baking tray with greaseproof paper

3

Use a potato ricer or masher to mash the potatoes into a smooth mash then beat in 2 of the egg yolks, the butter and grated Comté. Season with grated nutmeg and salt to taste

4

Transfer the mixture to a piping bag fitted with a star nozzle. Pipe stars of the mixture onto the tray, around 3cm in diameter. You may need more than one tray, or to work in batches

5

Brush the pommes duchesse with the egg wash and bake for 20 minutes or until golden. Serve hot
