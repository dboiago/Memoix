This breakfast or brunch sandwich is made with Hovis Soft White medium sliced bread, rich [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes "Egg") mayo, chopped [watercress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/watercress-recipes "Watercress") salad, crispy smoked bacon and a roasted tomato and chipotle sauce.

First published in 2025

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Place the eggs in a saucepan and cover with cold water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 9 minutes. Drain and transfer to ice water

-   6 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® eggs](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/)

3

Next, prepare the roasted tomato and chipotle sauce. Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan)

4

Place the tomatoes on a baking tray, drizzle with olive oil, and season with salt and smoked paprika. Roast for 15 minutes until soft

-   3 [medium tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), halved
-   1 tsp olive oil
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)

5

Meanwhile, mix together all the watercress salad ingredients

-   30g of [watercress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/watercress-recipes)
-   1/2 small red onion, finely chopped
-   1 tsp olive oil
-   1 tsp [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

7

Blend the roasted tomatoes with chipotle paste to make a smooth sauce

-   1/2 tsp [chipotle paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)

8

To assemble the sandwich, spread the chipotle sauce on either side of the bread. Spread a layer of egg salad on the bottom half, then top with the watercress salad and crispy bacon. Place the top half of the bread on and serve

-   2 slices of [medium-cut bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
