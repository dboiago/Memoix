2 baking trays, 16 to 20 portions

The brownie-like batter for this cookie spreads to fill two baking trays, and once baked, you crack it into pieces like candy nut brittle. It can be enjoyed on its own like a cookie, or it can be crumbled onto ice cream or used in a confection like my Rocky Road Bars.

![](https://annaolson.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BrownieBrittle_9707-tall.jpg "BrownieBrittle_9707-tall")

**NOTES:** The brittle will keep in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.

If you’re looking for more of a confection than a cookie, sprinkle the top of the brittle batter with chocolate chips, walnut pieces or pecan pieces before baking, or drizzle the brittle with melted chocolate and sprinkle with these items after baking.

-   **¾ cup (150 g)** granulated sugar
-   **½ cup (100 g)** packed light brown sugar
-   **4 oz (120 g)** dark baking chocolate
-   **¼ cup (60 mL)** 2% milk
-   **2** large eggs, at room temperature
-   **½ cup (125 mL)** sunflower oil

-   **2 tsp** vanilla extract
-   **¾ cup (110 g)** all-purpose flour
-   **⅓ cup (40 g)** cocoa powder
-   **½ tsp** fine salt
-   **¼ tsp** baking powder

## Step by Step Instructions

##### Step 1

Preheat the oven to 325°F (160°C) and line two baking trays with parchment paper.

##### Step 2

In a medium saucepan over low heat, stir the granulated and brown sugars, chocolate and milk until the chocolate is melted—the sugar will not dissolve. Remove the pan from the heat.

##### Step 3

Whisk the eggs, oil and vanilla into the saucepan until evenly combined. Whisk in the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder and salt until smooth. Pour the batter onto the two baking trays and spread to level until the batter almost reaches the edges of the trays (it does not have to touch them)

##### Step 4

Bake the brittle for about 30 minutes, until it appears dry on the surface. Cool the brittle on the trays on racks.

##### Step 5

Break the brittle into pieces to serve and store.
