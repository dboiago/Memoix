The technique of glazing meat – in this case chicken – as it cooks on the [barbecue](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-barbecue-recipes "Easy barbecue") creates layers of flavour that is both deeply satisfying to cook and eat. This recipe has two glazes to create a sweet and tangy crust – a [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes "Banana") ketchup and a brown sugar glaze, the latter of which includes flavours of Filipino [chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/barbecued-chicken-recipes "Barbecued chicken") adobo. Marinating the chicken in an adobo marinade tenderises the chicken and adds another layer of flavour here too. Judging the temperature of the grill and moving the skewers to a cooler area if the chicken is colouring too quickly is key.

-   1 tbsp of apple cider vinegar, or Filipino cane vinegar if you can find it
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated

1

For the marinade, mix all of the ingredients together with a generous pinch of black pepper. Add the chicken, mix well and set aside in the fridge for at least 30 minutes, or ideally overnight

-   120ml of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   90ml of rice wine vinegar, or Filipino cane vinegar if you can find it
-   30g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated

-   1.5kg [boneless chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), chopped into 2.5cm pieces

2

For the banana ketchup, heat the vegetable oil in a saucepan. Once hot, soften the shallots for about 5 minutes, then add the garlic, green chilli, ginger, turmeric, allspice and smoked paprika and fry for a further minute until fragrant

-   2 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   2 [banana shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely chopped
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   1 [small green chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   30g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely grated
-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [ground allspice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/allspice-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)

3

Stir in the remaining ingredients then bring to a simmer. Turn down the heat to low and cover and cook for 15-20 minutes, until thickened. Leave to cool

-   400g of [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes), (about 4), mashed
-   100ml of mirin
-   100ml of apple cider vinegar
-   2 tbsp of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   50g of brown sugar
-   2 tbsp of [dark rum](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rum-cocktail-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [tomato purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)

4

Once cooled, taste and season as needed, then blend in a food processor until smooth, loosening with a splash of water if needed. Set aside a little in a small serving bowl for dipping, then pour the rest in a bowl to use for glazing the chicken as it cooks

5

For the brown sugar glaze, mix together all of the ingredients in a saucepan. Simmer for 5 minutes then reserve a little for brushing at the end of cooking. Pour the rest in a bowl to use for glazing the chicken as it cooks

-   100g of dark brown sugar
-   60ml of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   1 tbsp of apple cider vinegar, or Filipino cane vinegar if you can find it
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   1 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)

6

If you are using wooden skewers, soak them in water for at least 20 minutes, then drain

7

Skewer the chicken onto skewers and secure a lime half onto the end of each skewer to keep everything in place. Set aside

-   5 [limes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), 3 halved and 2 with their cheeks cut off, central part discarded

8

Light a barbecue for direct cooking on a medium-high heat

9

Brush the chicken skewers with a little oil and place them on the grill. Cook for 15-20 minutes, with the lid on and vents open, turning every 2 minutes and basting with the banana ketchup and brown sugar glaze in turn. You may need to move the chicken to a cooler area of the grill if colouring too quickly. The final internal temperature should be at least 74°C

10

Meanwhile, char the lime cheeks until nicely coloured, then remove and set aside

11

Arrange the skewers on a platter (remove the skewers if you like) and brush over the reserved glaze with a clean brush. Sprinkle with chopped coriander and serve with the charred lime cheeks and reserved banana ketchup
