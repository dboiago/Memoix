[Tony Fleming](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/tony-fleming)’s father is half-Punjabi and he grew up eating a mix of British and Asian food. In this curried [cod](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cod-recipes) recipe, he pairs the white fish - he recommends Cornish cod - with a range of subcontinental flavours for an elegant take on a [fish curry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-curry-recipes). Mango relish provides a fantastic sweetness and lovely colour, while the [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cauliflower-recipes) purée subtly spices the dish.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Cod

-   4 [cod fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-fillet-recipes), skin removed, each weighing 140g
-   1/2 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1 tbsp of mild curry powder
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   100g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   vegetable oil

#### Cauliflower purée

-   500g of [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes)
-   10g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), diced
-   10g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), diced
-   3g of [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   15g of curry powder
-   100g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   250g of milk
-   250g of double cream
-   50g of [mango chutney](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), good quality from a jar
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [juice of half a lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

#### Mango relish

-   2 [mangoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), flesh cut into 0.5cm dice - 1 stone reserved
-   100ml of white wine vinegar
-   100g of caster sugar
-   70ml of water
-   1/2 tsp [black onion seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/black-onion-seed-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [white mustard seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-seed-recipes)
-   2 [red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 red onion, finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped

#### Garnish

-   4 [purple cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes), raw
-   1 handful of [cauliflower florets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes)
-   1 handful of [coriander cress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   vegetable oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

-   Mandoline
-   Blender
-   Ovenproof frying pan
-   Fine chinois

1

For the mango relish, boil the vinegar, sugar, water, reserved mango stone and onion and mustard seeds until you achieve a syrup - the liquid should be reduced by half (approximately). Discard the mango stone

-   100ml of white wine vinegar
-   100g of caster sugar
-   70ml of water
-   1/2 tsp [black onion seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/black-onion-seed-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [white mustard seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-seed-recipes)

2

Mix the mango with the chilli and red onion. Add the mango mixture to the hot mango stone stock, cover tightly with cling film and leave for 1 hour at room temperature. Chill in the fridge (you will need to bring the relish up to room temperature and mix in the coriander before serving)

-   2 [mangoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), flesh cut into 0.5cm dice - 1 stone reserved
-   2 [red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 red onion, finely chopped

3

For the purée, melt the butter in a pan over a medium heat and cook the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin seeds for 4-5 minutes. Add the chopped cauliflower, season with salt and cook for a further 5 minutes until starting to soften

-   500g of [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes)
-   10g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), diced
-   10g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), diced
-   3g of [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   100g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   15g of curry powder

4

Add the milk and simmer until the cauliflower is cooked. Add the cream and simmer until the cream is reduced and thickened. Transfer to a blender with the mango chutney and lemon juice until smooth, then pass through a fine sieve

-   50g of [mango chutney](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), good quality from a jar
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [juice of half a lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   250g of milk
-   250g of double cream

5

Preheat the oven to 180˚C/gas mark 4

6

Season the cod fillets with salt. Place a non-stick, ovenproof pan over a medium-high heat and add a small dash of vegetable oil. Once the oil is hot, cook the cod fillets for 2 minutes, then transfer to the oven and cook for 5 minutes

-   vegetable oil
-   4 [cod fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-fillet-recipes), skin removed, each weighing 140g

7

Meanwhile, place a pan over a medium-high heat and add a dash of vegetable oil. Once the oil is hot, add the florets and cook until golden, then season to taste with salt and remove from the pan

-   vegetable oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 handful of [cauliflower florets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes)

8

Once ready, remove the cod from the oven and add curry powder and butter to the pan. Once the butter begins to foam, use a spoon to baste the fish for 2 minutes, then finish with lemon juice, coriander and a pinch of rock salt. Leave in a warm place to rest for 1 minute

-   1/2 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1 tbsp of mild curry powder
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   100g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

9

To finish, place the fillets of cod onto plates and dot the purée around the fish. Arrange the pan-fried cauliflower florets onto the fish and spoon on some mango relish (with the tablespoon of chopped coriander mixed in). Use a mandoline to shave some of the purple cauliflower and arrange the shavings onto each plate. Finish with some coriander cress and serve immediately

-   1 handful of [coriander cress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   4 [purple cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes), raw
-   1 tbsp of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
