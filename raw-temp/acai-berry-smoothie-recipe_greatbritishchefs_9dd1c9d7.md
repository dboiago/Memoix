Marcello Tully shares his easy acai berry smoothie recipe. Acai berries are rich in antioxidants and have a wonderful tart flavour. The inclusion of apple and [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/carrot-recipes) help take the acidic edge off the berries and further increase the health benefits of this [vegan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes) smoothie.

#### Acai smoothie

-   1 [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), peeled and cored
-   100g of acai berry purée
-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled
-   200ml of almond milk

-   Blender
-   Straws 2

1

Place all of the ingredients in a blender and blitz until smooth. Pour into 2 glasses, pop in the straws and serve immediately
