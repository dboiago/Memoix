### List of Ingredients

-   250 G of ricotta
-   125 G of farina 00
-   125 G of lamponi
-   45 G of cioccolato fondente
-   35 G of zucchero semolato
-   25 G of olio di semi di arachide
-   4 G of lievito in polvere per dolci
-   1 of uovo
-   zucchero a velo
-   sale

### Method

1

In a large bowl, mix the flour with the sugar, egg, baking powder, oil and a pinch of salt, using a spoon first and then with your hands. Melt the chocolate in a double boiler and add it to the dough, kneading it until it is smooth. Let it rest covered in the refrigerator for 30 minutes.

2

Roll it out to 3-4 mm thick with a rolling pin; cut it into strips 1 1/4 inches wide, then into diamonds, obtaining about 25 cookies.

3

Place them on a baking tray lined with parchment paper and bake at 340°F for 12-15 minutes. Dab the ricotta to remove any excess liquid. Mix it with 1 tablespoon of powdered sugar. Serve the cookies with the ricotta and raspberries cut in half: you can make small balls using two teaspoons, or distribute it with the help of a pastry bag.
