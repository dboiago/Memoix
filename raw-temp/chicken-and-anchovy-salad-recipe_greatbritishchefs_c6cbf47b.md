[Pete Biggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/pete-biggs), head chef of [Outlaw's at The Capital](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/restaurants/outlaws-at-the-capital), serves up a particularly gourmet take on [chicken salad](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-salad-recipes), making treacle bread croutons from scratch to compliment the punchy flavours of [anchovy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/anchovy-recipes) and Parmesan. If you are short on time, feel free to make slightly more low-key croutons, as the rest of the salad is relatively straightforward and makes a flavour-packed lunch recipe.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Treacle bread

-   100g of [black treacle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/treacle-recipes)
-   250g of white bread flour
-   250g of wholemeal flour
-   30g of fresh yeast
-   30g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   220ml of warm water
-   15g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### Marinade

-   1 bunch of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 bunch of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), finely chopped
-   20g of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), chopped
-   40g of [anchovies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/anchovy-recipes), blitzed to a paste
-   400ml of rapeseed oil

-   Food mixer
-   Blender
-   Loaf tin
-   Temperature probe

1

To start the dish, make the treacle bread. Add all of the ingredients (except the salt) to the bowl of an upright food mixer and mix for 6 minutes. Add the salt and mix for a further 2 minutes

-   250g of white bread flour
-   250g of wholemeal flour
-   30g of fresh yeast
-   30g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100g of [black treacle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/treacle-recipes)
-   220ml of warm water
-   15g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Transfer the dough to a bowl, cover with cling film and set aside to prove for 1 hour

3

After 1 hour (the mix should have doubled in size) roll out the dough and place into a loaf tin. Set aside for a second prove for a further 30 minutes

4

Preheat the oven to 220°C/gas mark 8

5

Add a bowl of water to the oven to produce steam and cook the loaf for 15 minutes. After this time, spray with water and cook for a further 3 minutes

6

Leave the bread to cool completely before placing in the freezer. Once semi-frozen, slice the bread thinly on a meat slicer or with a sharp serrated knife. Once the bread is sliced, dehydrate in a warm, dry place for 2 hours or until it is crispy

7

Add the chicken breasts to a pan and pour in enough milk to cover. Poach the breasts over a low heat until the internal temperature of the meat reaches 60°C

-   2 [chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes), each weighing 170g
-   milk

8

Mix all of the marinade ingredients together in a large bowl. Slice the chicken breasts into thick strips and add to the bowl with the marinade, mixing to coat. Set aside to marinate for 30 minutes

-   1 bunch of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 bunch of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), finely chopped
-   20g of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), chopped
-   40g of [anchovies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/anchovy-recipes), blitzed to a paste
-   400ml of rapeseed oil

9

To make the herb mayonnaise, add all of the ingredients to a blender, blitz to a paste then gradually add the oil in a thin, slow stream until emulsified

-   25g of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
-   25g of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)
-   15g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes)
-   15ml of white wine vinegar
-   5g of [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   5g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   2 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   250g of vegetable oil

10

To serve, add the marinated chicken to a bowl with the remaining salad ingredients, stirring to incorporate all of the ingredients evenly. Divide the salad between four serving bowls and top with 3 pieces of treacle bread per portion. Drizzle over the herb mayonnaise and serve

-   100g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated
-   16 [anchovy fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/anchovy-recipes), chopped
-   100g of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)
-   100g of [watercress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/watercress-recipes)
-   2 [baby gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes)
