## Quick, savory, and delicious, coppa and robiola canapés are a guaranteed crowd pleaser.

### List of Ingredients

-   3 slices white bread
-   2 slices coppa
-   1 OZ. of robiola cheese
-   2 pink peppercorns, crushed
-   unsalted butter

### Method

1

Using different sized cookie cutters (ours are flower-shaped, measuring 3”, 2 1/2”, and 1”), cut out each piece of bread. Toast the bread in a pan with the butter for 30 seconds. 

2

Place a slice of coppa, the robiola, and some crumbled pink peppercorn on the large and medium slices. Stack the slices and top with the smallest slice.
