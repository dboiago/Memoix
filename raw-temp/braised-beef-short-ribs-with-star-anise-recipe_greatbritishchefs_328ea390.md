These slow-braised beef short ribs are cooked with [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes "Star anise") and soy, to create a rich, spiced sauce. The [prunes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/prune-recipes "Prune") break down in the sauce and add sweetness, which complements the beef perfectly, although you could also use honey or sugar. Serve this with plenty of white [rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes "Rice") and some greens such as choi sum or [pak choi](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pak-choi-recipes "Pak choi").

### Imperial

#### Beef short ribs

-   1 dash of neutral oil
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste
-   6 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), grated to a paste
-   1 knob of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), grated to a paste
-   1 tsp [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 tsp [five-spice powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chinese-five-spice-recipes)
-   800ml of beef stock, plus an extra 250ml for topping up if needed
-   4 [beef short ribs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes), about 1kg
-   2 [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [dark soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of rice wine vinegar
-   1/2 tsp [ground white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   100g of [prunes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/prune-recipes), or use 2 tbsps honey

#### Garnish

-   1 handful of [fresh mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), chopped
-   1 handful of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1 [mild red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 [spring onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced

1

Preheat the oven to 170°C/gas mark 3

2

Place a lidded casserole dish large enough to hold the ribs over a medium heat, and add a dash of oil. Once the oil is hot, add the onion and a pinch of salt. Cook the onion over a medium heat until soft, 10-15 minutes

-   1 dash of neutral oil
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

3

Add the garlic and ginger and cook, stirring, until fragrant and soft. This should be about a minute or so

-   6 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), grated to a paste
-   1 knob of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), grated to a paste

4

Add the coriander seeds, cumin seeds and five spice then cook for another minute. Take care not to burn the spices and add another dash of oil if necessary

-   1 tsp [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 tsp [five-spice powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chinese-five-spice-recipes)

5

Add the stock, followed by the short ribs, star anise, soy sauce, vinegar and some salt and the white pepper. Put the lid on and transfer to the oven for 3 hours, 30 minutes, or until the ribs are tender (the exact cooking time will vary depending on size and shape of the ribs)

-   800ml of beef stock
-   4 [beef short ribs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes), about 1kg
-   2 [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [dark soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of rice wine vinegar
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste
-   1/2 tsp [ground white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

6

After the ribs have been cooking for 2 hours 30 minutes, add the prunes and top up with more stock if needed

-   100g of [prunes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/prune-recipes)
-   250ml of beef stock
