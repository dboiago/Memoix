[Nathan Outlaw](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/nathan-outlaw)'s simple [cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cheese-recipes) scones are perfect for those seeking a savoury option for afternoon tea, and an excellent treat to add to a [picnic basket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/picnic-recipes) - especially when combined with grilled [figs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/fig-recipes) and pickled celery.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Crackler cheddar scones

-   225g of self-raising flour, plus more for dusting
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   55g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), cold and cut into small pieces, plus more for greasing
-   25g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   150ml of milk, plus more to brush on scones

#### Pickled celery

-   1 [head of celery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), washed and sliced thinly
-   100ml of white wine
-   100ml of white wine vinegar
-   100ml of water
-   100g of sugar
-   30g of [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes), toasted
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### To plate

-   200g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), sliced
-   4 [figs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fig-recipes)
-   salad leaves, to plate
-   olive oil, to drizzle

-   5cm pastry cutter

1

To make the cheese scones, heat the oven to 220°C/Gas mark 7. Lightly grease a baking sheet or cover the baking sheet with parchment paper

2

Mix together the flour and salt and rub in the butter. Stir in the cheddar cheese and then the milk to make a soft dough

-   225g of self-raising flour
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   55g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   150ml of milk
-   25g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated

3

Turn on to a floured work surface and knead very lightly. Pat out to a round 2cm thick. Use a 5cm cutter to stamp out rounds and place on the baking sheet. Lightly knead together the rest of the dough and stamp out more scones to use it up

4

Brush the tops of the scones with a little milk. Bake for 12-15 minutes until well risen and golden. Cool cheddar scones on a wire rack 

5

For the pickled celery, place celery, white wine, vinegar, water, sugar, fennel seeds and salt into a sauce pan and bring to a simmer, uncovered, for about 2 minutes

-   1 [head of celery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes)
-   100ml of white wine
-   100ml of white wine vinegar
-   100ml of water
-   100g of sugar
-   30g of [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

6

Allow to cool and store the pickled celery in the fridge in an airtight container until ready to use

7

To plate, start by grilling the figs. Quarter the figs, then grill for 2-3 minutes

-   4 [figs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fig-recipes)

8

Cut the scones in half. On the cut side, place a spoon of celery followed by some cheese. Place the figs on top of the cheese and then add a little more cheese

-   200g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), sliced

9

Grill the scones until the cheese starts to melt. Serve with a few salad leaves, a little olive oil and more pickled celery

-   olive oil
-   salad leaves
