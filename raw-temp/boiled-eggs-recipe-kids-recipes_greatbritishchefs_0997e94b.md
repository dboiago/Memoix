Though this dish is undeniably a [kids recipe](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kids-recipes) – and a joyful one at that – the crispy [asparagus](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/asparagus-recipes) soldiers are a real gourmet touch and will taste sublime once dunked into the [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/egg-recipes).

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   6 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   16 [asparagus spears](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes), trimmed and washed
-   100g of plain flour
-   100g of breadcrumbs
-   500ml of vegetable oil
-   2 pinches of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   iced water

-   Deep-fryer
-   4 egg cups

1

Bring a pot of salted water to the boil. Blanch the asparagus for 1 minute and then plunge into iced water for 1 minute. Drain on some kitchen paper

-   iced water
-   16 [asparagus spears](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes), trimmed and washed
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Prepare 3 bowls for crumbing. Add flour to the first, 2 beaten eggs to the second and breadcrumbs to the third

-   100g of plain flour
-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), whisked
-   100g of breadcrumbs

3

Coat each asparagus spear in the flour, then the egg and finally the breadcrumbs until evenly coated

4

Heat a deep-fat fryer to 180°C. While it heats, draw faces on the eggs in non-toxic pen or pencil

-   500ml of vegetable oil
-   6 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

5

Bring a pan of water to the boil. Lower the eggs into the boiling water and cook for around 3 1/2 minutes for soft boiled, 6-7 minutes for hard boiled

6

Meanwhile, fry the asparagus (do this in 2 batches of 8) for 2-3 minutes, until golden brown and crispy. Drain well on kitchen paper and season with salt

-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

7

Place each egg in an egg cup, remove the tops and serve with the crispy asparagus soldiers
