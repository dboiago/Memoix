This fun take on an ice cream float combines [peanut butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-butter-recipes "Peanut butter") with sweet banana and a creamy Tassimo Costa Latte to make a drink that works as well as a dessert as it does a midmorning treat. Decorating the glass with melted chocolate and hazelnuts looks impressive but it’s actually incredibly simple!

First published in 2023

### Metric

### Imperial

-   2 [caffè lattes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coffee-recipes), 325ml each
-   150g of [dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), for melting, plus extra for shaving over the top
-   50g of [hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), toasted and chopped
-   2 [bananas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes), peeled, sliced and then frozen
-   2 tbsp of [peanut butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-butter-recipes), smooth
-   2 handfuls of ice cubes
-   [vanilla ice cream](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ice-cream-recipes), 6-8 scoops

1

Place two glasses or serving bowls into the freezer

2

Prepare two lattes and allow to cool

-   2 [caffè lattes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coffee-recipes), 325ml each

3

Gently melt the chocolate for the glasses in a bain marie, and scatter the hazelnuts across a plate in an even layer

-   150g of [dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), for melting, plus extra for shaving over the top
-   50g of [hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), toasted and chopped

4

Remove a glass from the freezer and use a spoon to swirl the chocolate around the inside of the glass to make a pattern. Dip the rim into the melted chocolate then into the nuts. Transfer to the freezer and repeat with the second glass

5

Add the frozen bananas to the blender with the ice cubes, peanut butter and coffee and blend until smooth

-   2 [bananas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes), peeled, sliced and then frozen
-   2 tbsp of [peanut butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-butter-recipes), smooth
-   2 handfuls of ice cubes

6

Scoop the ice cream into glasses, then pour the coffee mixture over the top. Garnish with grated chocolate to serve

-   [vanilla ice cream](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ice-cream-recipes), 6-8 scoops
