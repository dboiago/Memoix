### Caper Dill Sauce

## Recipe Notes

-   **Yield:** Makes about 1-1/4 cup

## Ingredients

-   1/2 cup sour cream
-   1/2 cup mayonnaise
-   2 teaspoons lemon juice
-   1 teaspoon lemon zest
-   1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill
-   1 tablespoon brined capers, drained and roughly chopped, or more to taste
-   Coarse salt and freshly ground pepper to taste

## Recipe Steps

**Step 1:** Whisk the sour cream, mayonnaise, lemon juice, and lemon zest in a mixing bowl. Stir in the dill and capers.

**Step 2:** Add salt and pepper to taste. (Remember, the capers are salty.)
