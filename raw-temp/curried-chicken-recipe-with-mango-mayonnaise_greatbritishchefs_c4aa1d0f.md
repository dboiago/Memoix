Here, [Galton Blackiston](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/galton-blackiston) fuses well-loved flavours in a simple and elegant way: this [chicken recipe](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-recipes) is perfect for making at the start of the week, the leftovers making a tasty [picnic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/picnic-recipes) snack or lunchbox treat.

#### Curried chicken

-   6 [chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes), skin on
-   1 [large shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [inch piece ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), chopped
-   2 [lemongrass stalks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-grass-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   200ml of [coconut milk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes)
-   200ml of [natural yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   2 [red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), seeds removed and finely chopped
-   1 handful of [fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### Mayonnaise

-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tsp white wine vinegar
-   1/2 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   275ml of sunflower oil
-   1/2 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), finely grated zest and juice only
-   1 [mango](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), chopped

-   Food processor or blender

2

Cut each chicken breast lengthways into 3, place in the bowl and mix carefully. Make sure the chicken is well coated. Cover with cling film and chill in the fridge to marinate for as long as possible, preferably overnight

-   6 [chicken breasts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes)

3

Pre-heat the oven to 190°C/Gas mark 5

4

Place the chicken strips in a roasting tin and bake for about 15 minutes, turning them fairly frequently to ensure they are cooked right through

5

While the chicken is roasting, put the egg, vinegar and Dijon mustard in a food processor, season with salt and pepper and combine well. With the machine still running, slowly pour in the sunflower oil in a steady stream until the mixture has emulsified and thickened

-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tsp white wine vinegar
-   1/2 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   275ml of sunflower oil

6

Once you have added all the oil, mix in the lime zest and juice. Scrape the mayonnaise out of the food processor into a bowl. Fold the chopped mango flesh into the mayonnaise, taste and re-season if necessary

-   1/2 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   1 [mango](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes)

7

Serve the curried chicken hot or cold, with the mango mayonnaise and garnish with some baby watercress

-   1 bunch of [baby watercress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/watercress-recipes)
