Opposites attract in this modern twist on the classic.

The idea for the Golden Ghost came from the classic [Bijou cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/bijou-cocktail-recipe/), says Brian Evans of Sunday in Brooklyn. “With this drink, I wanted to pair flavors of bitterness, chocolate and smokiness. I’ve found that what might seem almost like opposites, all pleasantly attract.”

## Ingredients

-   ¾ oz. mezcal espadín
-   ¾ oz. blanco tequila
-   ¾ oz. white crème de cacao
-   ¾ oz. génépy
-   1 dash orange bitters

## Preparation

Stir all the ingredients together with ice in mixing glass. Strain into a chilled glass, then garnish.

_Note: At the bar, Evans soaks 1 star anise in overproof vodka, then lights it aflame over the drink before garnishing._
