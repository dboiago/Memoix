At Kettner Exchange in San Diego, this Old Fashioned riff combines bourbon with maraschino liqueur, Cynar and bitters.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. bourbon
-   ¼ oz. Cynar
-   ¼ oz. maraschino liqueur
-   2-3 dashes orange bitters

## Preparation

Stir all the ingredients together with ice in a mixing glass. Strain into an Old Fashioned glass over fresh ice. Garnish.
