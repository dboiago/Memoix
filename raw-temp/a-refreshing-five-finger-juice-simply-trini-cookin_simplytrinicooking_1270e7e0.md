### FIVE FINGER JUICE

**_Ingredients_**:

4 ripe five finger (star fruit)  
sugar to taste  
1 dash of bitters  
3 cups water (divided)

**_Direction_**:

Wash and cut up the five finger.

Blend with 1 cup of water and strain. Add two more cups of water.

**Note:** Squeeze the pulp to extract more juice.

Add bitters and sugar to taste and stir. Mix well. Adjust sugar if necessary. Serve chilled or with ice.

## Star Fruit or Five Finger Juice FAQs:

-   I would like to say that you should not drink too much of this juice.  It has been known to cause nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, and backache, especially if consumed on an empty stomach ([Source](http://www.zhion.com/Food/Star_Fruit.html)).

-   **How do I know the five finger is right to make juice?** It would look yellow and feel firm to the touch. The more yellow it is, it is sweeter.
