Budgie Montoya's crispy [chicken wings](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-wing-recipes "Chicken wing") are coated in a sweet and tart spiced banana [ketchup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ketchup-recipes "Ketchup") and garnished with [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes "Coriander") and [chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes "Chilli"). Don't forget the napkins! 

First published in 2022

#### Chicken wings

-   12 [chicken wings](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-wing-recipes)
-   300ml of cane vinegar
-   300ml of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   30g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   30g of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   600g of plain flour
-   400g of potato starch
-   vegetable oil, for deep frying
-   10g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   5g of [ground white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### Banana Ketchup

-   1 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   2 [banana shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced
-   3 very ripe bananas , mashed
-   60ml of cane vinegar
-   60ml of water
-   20ml of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   20g of palm sugar
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   20g of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tsp [turmeric powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [allspice powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/allspice-recipes)
-   10g of [tomato paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1 [bird's eye chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped

1

Make the banana ketchup by heating the oil in a medium non-stick pan on medium heat. Add the shallots and cook until translucent

-   1 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   2 [banana shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced

3

Add the tomato paste and cook out for a minute, stirring well. Add the vinegar, soy sauce, sugar, chilli and banana. Simmer on low heat for 10-15 minutes, stirring often until it starts to thicken

-   10g of [tomato paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   60ml of cane vinegar
-   20ml of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   20g of palm sugar
-   1 [bird's eye chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   3 very ripe bananas , mashed

4

Add the water, taste and add seasoning to your liking. Take off the heat. Place mixture into blender and blitz till you get a smooth texture. Set aside 

-   60ml of water
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season
-   [ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes) to season

5

Joint the chicken wings (discard the wing tips or use for stock)

-   12 [chicken wings](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-wing-recipes)

6

Combine the vinegar, soy sauce, garlic and ginger in a bowl. Place the jointed wings in the bowl and marinate for at least 4 hrs or overnight

-   300ml of cane vinegar
-   300ml of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   30g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   30g of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)

7

Pour the vegetable oil into a deep fat fryer or high sided saucepan (make sure it is no more than half full) and heat to a temperature of 160C. In a bowl, combine the flour, potato starch and some salt and pepper and whisk until well combined

-   vegetable oil, for deep frying
-   600g of plain flour
-   400g of potato starch
-   10g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   5g of [ground white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

8

Strain the wings, removing all the excess marinade. Add the wings to the flour mix and coat them well, ensuring you press the flour into the chicken. Fry the wings a few at a time for 6 minutes, then remove and place on a tray with a rack

9

Increase the oil temperature to 180c and fry the wings for a second time for 4 minutes, then place on a rack to drain excess oil

10

Place the wings in a large mixing bowl, add the banana ketchup and toss until all the wings are well coated, then sprinkle chopped coriander and sliced red chilli to garnish 

-   1 handful of [fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), leaves only
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), sliced
