One of the world’s most refreshing cocktails.

The classic Margarita recipe—a simple mix of tequila, lime and Cointreau, is one of the world’s most refreshing cocktails.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. silver tequila (or reposado if you like)
-   1 oz. fresh lime juice
-   ¾ oz. Cointreau

## Preparation

Combine ingredients with ice in a shaker and shake vigorously. Strain into an ice-filled glass and garnish.
