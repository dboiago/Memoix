### Imperial

#### Potato cakes

-   240g of [Maris Piper potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)
-   240g of [quinoa](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quinoa-recipes)
-   10g of [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   25g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1/2 tbsp of [ground cumin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1/4 tbsp of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of cornflour, heaped
-   80g of [smoked cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying

#### Yoghurt

-   100g of [yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes), extra-thick or hung
-   1/2 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   1/4 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

#### Salad

-   200g of [cherry tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-tomato-recipes), quartered
-   1 handful of baby salad leaves
-   1 [cooked beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetable-recipes), diced
-   2 [radishes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/radish-recipes), finely sliced
-   50ml of olive oil
-   20ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   20ml of [runny honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   20g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), chopped
-   1 tsp [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

#### Lentils

-   100g of [split yellow moong lentils](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lentil-recipes), soaked for 2 hours in cold water then drained
-   vegetable oil, for frying
-   1/2 tsp [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [amchur powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), (also known as dried mango powder)
-   1/2 tsp chaat masala
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

1

Bring 2 pans of salted water to the boil. Meanwhile, rinse the quinoa under cold water and peel the potatoes, cutting them into even sizes if needed. Cook the potatoes in 1 pan and the quinoa in the other for around 15 minutes, until the potatoes are just cooked through and the quinoa is tender. Drain both and leave to cool

-   240g of [quinoa](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quinoa-recipes)
-   240g of [Maris Piper potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)

2

Coarsely grate the potato and place in a large mixing bowl. Add the quinoa, chilli, ginger, ground coriander and cumin, salt, coriander leaves and cornflour, then mix well until combined

-   10g of [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   25g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1/2 tbsp of [ground cumin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1/4 tbsp of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of cornflour, heaped

3

Divide the mixture into 8 equal portions, then flatten each portion into a disc. Add around 10g of the smoked cheese into the centre of each disc, then use your hands to bring the mixture up and around the cheese and roll it into a smooth ball. Place on a plate or tray and into the fridge to chill for at least 20 minutes

-   80g of [smoked cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated

4

While the potato cakes chill, prepare the other elements. Combine all the ingredients for the yoghurt and season to taste with salt. Transfer to a piping bag if you have one, then reserve in the fridge

-   100g of [yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes), extra-thick or hung
-   1/2 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   1/4 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

5

For the lentils, add a generous glug of oil to a frying pan over a medium heat and add the soaked lentils. Fry for 5-10 minutes until golden and crisp, then drain on kitchen paper. Whilst still hot, toss the fried lentils with the pepper, amchur, chaat masala and a pinch of salt and set aside

-   vegetable oil, for frying
-   100g of [split yellow moong lentils](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lentil-recipes), soaked for 2 hours in cold water then drained
-   1/2 tsp [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [amchur powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), (also known as dried mango powder)
-   1/2 tsp chaat masala
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

6

Make the dressing for the salad by placing the olive oil, lemon juice, honey, ginger, pepper and a pinch of salt in a food processor or blender. Blitz until completely smooth, then set aside

-   50ml of olive oil
-   20ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   20ml of [runny honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   20g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), chopped
-   1 tsp [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

7

When ready to cook the potato cakes, bring a deep-fat fryer or deep pan of oil to 180°C

-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying

8

Deep-fry the potato cakes for 3-5 minutes, until dark brown all over

9

Meanwhile, toss the tomatoes and baby salad leaves in a generous amount of the dressing

-   200g of [cherry tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-tomato-recipes), quartered
-   1 handful of baby salad leaves

10

To serve, pipe or spoon 2 mounds of yoghurt onto each plate, then top each mound with a fried potato cake. Spoon some of the dressed salad alongside, garnishing with diced beetroot, sliced radish and a scattering of the fried lentils. Finish with a drizzle of mint and tamarind chutney on top of the cakes

-   [tamarind chutney](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chutney-recipes)
-   1 [cooked beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetable-recipes), diced
-   2 [radishes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/radish-recipes), finely sliced
