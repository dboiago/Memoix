This [chocolate mousse](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-mousse-recipes "Chocolate mousse") from [Peter Sanchez-Iglesias](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/peter-sanchez-iglesias "Peter Sanchez-Iglesias") is made light and airy from whipped [egg whites](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-white-recipes "Egg white"), but has a touch of extra richness and pepperiness from arbequina olive oil. It’s a simple dessert, and definitely worth investing in some good [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-recipes "Chocolate") for. At the restaurant it’s garnished with slivers of toasted sourdough.

First published in 2023

### Metric

### Imperial

-   200g of [70% dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), Peter used Amedei Toscano Black 70% chocolate
-   80g of milk
-   100g of [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   35g of caster sugar
-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   10ml of arbequina olive oil

-   5cm metal pastry rings 8
-   Piping bags

1

Line 8 mousse moulds (2 inches wide and 2 inches deep) with cling film

2

Melt chocolate over a gentle bain-marie. Once melted remove from the heat. Warm the milk in a separate pan to no more than 60°C

-   200g of [70% dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), Peter used Amedei Toscano Black 70% chocolate
-   80g of milk

3

Whip the egg whites, gradually adding the sugar as you go, until they form soft peaks

-   100g of [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   35g of caster sugar

4

Whisk the egg yolk into the warm, melted chocolate. Next, slowly add the warmed milk to the egg yolk mixture and whisk gently. Finally, add in the olive oil

-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   10ml of arbequina olive oil

5

Add the whipped egg white, starting with a small amount to stabilise the mixture, and then gently folding in the remainder with a rubber spatula

6

Decant the mousse into piping bags, and distribute evenly between the lined moulds. Drizzle with more olive oil to serve
