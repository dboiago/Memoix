Creamy Red Pepper Cashew Dip. A healthy, vegan, dairy free, whole, oil free, gluten free, raw AND takes only 10 minutes to make! Darn tasty, creamy, almost cheesy, nacho-like, yum.

##### 

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 10 minutes

Total: 20 minutes

Servings: 6

[PRINT](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wprm_print/creamy-red-pepper-cashew-dip) [PIN](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/create/bookmarklet/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fitdoesnttastelikechicken.com%2Fcreamy-red-pepper-cashew-dip%2F&media=https%3A%2F%2Fitdoesnttastelikechicken.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2014%2F12%2Fcreamy-red-pepper-cashew-dip.jpg&description=Creamy+Red+Pepper+Cashew+Dip&is_video=false) [COMMENT](#commentform)

-   ▢ 1 ½ cups [raw cashews,](https://amzn.to/42zDSmJ), soaked or boiled (see nots)
-   ▢ ½ - 1 cup [plant-based milk](https://amzn.to/4jwulCt), such as soy or almond
-   ▢ ½ of a red pepper
-   ▢ 2 tablespoons [nutritional yeast](https://amzn.to/42WEhi4)
-   ▢ 1 clove garlic,, minced
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon [lemon juice](https://amzn.to/3Sr4L7x)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [chili powder](https://amzn.to/4jbosuS)
-   ▢ ½ teaspoon [salt](https://amzn.to/4jq9UHf)

-   Add the raw cashews, ½ cup non-dairy milk, the red pepper, nutritional yeast, garlic, lemon juice, chili powder, and salt to a blender. Combine until smooth, stopping to scrape the sides as needed, and adding up to ½ cup more non-dairy milk as needed to reach desired consistency.  
    

To boil the cashews, add the cashews to a pot and cover with water, bring to a boil and cook for about 10 minutes until cashews are tender. Drain and rinse.

To soak the cashews, put the cashews in a bowl and cover with cold water, soak for 4 hours or overnight until tender. Drain.
