Lyre’s American Malt is a premium non-alcoholic whiskey-style beverage, crafted with caramel, vanilla and cedary oak. Smooth, mellow and gently spiced, delivering a long, warming finish.

Flavour Profile

Mellow caramel and vanilla layered with toasted nuts, sweet spice and charred cedary oak, finishing smooth and gently spiced.

###### HOW TO ENJOY

Best enjoyed mixed - serve over ice with cola for a smooth, classic pour  
Shake into a non-alcoholic Whiskey Sour with citrus and sugar  
Stir into a Boulevardier with Lyre’s Apéritif Rosso and Italian Orange  
Mix a Manhattan-style serve with Apéritif Rosso, bitters and orange peel

**Bottle size:** 23.7 Fl Oz / 700mL  
**Closure:** Screw cap  
**Storage:** Store in cool, dry conditions. Once opened, refrigerate.  
**Serving note:** Designed to be mixed. Not intended to be consumed neat.

###### TASTE PROFILE

**Bouquet:** Honey and straw hues with aromas of vanilla, rye spice, caramel and charred cedar oak.  
**Taste:** Generous vanilla and toasted nut flavours with baked fruit and sweet spice, delivering a mellow palate and long, lingering finish.

##### What’s the best way to drink it?
