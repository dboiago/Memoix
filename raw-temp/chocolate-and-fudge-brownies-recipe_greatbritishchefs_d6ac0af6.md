These dark [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chocolate-recipes) and [fudge](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fudge-recipes) brownies from [Nathan Outlaw](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/nathan-outlaw) are a grown-up treat. Melting, rather than beating, the chocolate, butter and sugar together in this recipe gives them a thick, fudge-like texture, making these [rich chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocoholic-chocolate-recipes) desserts even more decadent.

First published in 2015

-   Electric hand whisk
-   Greaseproof paper
-   20cm x 30cm baking tray

1

Begin the brownies by preheating the oven to 150°C/gas mark 2

3

Meanwhile, in a separate bowl, whisk the eggs until light and foamy

-   5 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

4

When the chocolate mixture is melted, carefully fold it into the eggs

5

Sieve the flour and fold it into the chocolate mix. Add in the chopped fudge and the sea salt

-   200g of plain flour
-   10g of [Cornish sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   100g of [fudge](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fudge-recipes)

6

Pour the batter into a greaseproof paper-lined baking tray and bake for 20 minutes, or until the brownies are cooked so that they are set but still slightly soft in the middle

7

Remove from the oven and cool. Once cool, cut into desired shapes and serve
