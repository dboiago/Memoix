This glorious crayfish cocktail recipe form Louise Robinson uses plump, fresh crayfish instead of the usual [prawns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/prawn-recipes) for a twist on this classic [seafood starter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/seafood-starter-recipes), served with a pretty salad of red chicory, fennel and [avocado](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/avocado-recipes).

First published in 2017

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Crayfish Cocktail

-   2kg large live crayfish, (or 200g of cooked fresh crayfish tails)

#### Dressing

-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   200ml of olive oil
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   60g of [tomato ketchup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1 dash of Tabasco
-   1 dash of Worcestershire sauce
-   1 dash of [brandy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brandy-recipes)
-   1/2 bunch of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), finely chopped

#### Salad

-   1 [fennel bulb](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes), finely sliced, reserving any fronds
-   2 [baby gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes), each cut into 6 wedges
-   2 [red chicory](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicory-recipes), trimmed and separated into leaves
-   1 tbsp of extra virgin olive oil
-   1 [avocado](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), peeled, stoned, diced and drizzled with a little lemon juice
-   [chive flowers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), or chives, to garnish
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), cut into wedges, to serve
-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

1

Put a large pan of water over a medium heat, add the salt and bring to the boil. Cook the crayfish in batches for 4 minutes per batch. Set aside to drain in a colander until cool enough to peel

2

Prepare the mayonnaise by putting the egg yolk and mustard in a bowl or food processor. Add a drop of oil and whisk to combine completely. Gradually add the rest of the oil a few drops at a time, whisking continuously and then in a slow trickle until the mayonnaise thickens. Whisk in the juice of 1 lemon and season to taste

-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   200ml of olive oil
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced

3

Place 140ml of the mayonnaise in a bowl (the rest will keep, covered in the fridge for up to a week). Add the tomato ketchup and a few drops of Tabasco, Worcestershire sauce and brandy to taste. Add the chopped dill, stir to combine and check the seasoning

-   60g of [tomato ketchup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1 dash of Tabasco
-   1 dash of Worcestershire sauce
-   1 dash of [brandy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brandy-recipes)
-   1/2 bunch of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), finely chopped

4

When ready to serve, place the sliced fennel, chicory leaves and baby gem quarters into a bowl, drizzle with a little extra virgin olive oil and a little of the remaining lemon juice

-   1 [fennel bulb](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes), finely sliced, reserving any fronds
-   2 [baby gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes), each cut into 6 wedges
-   2 [red chicory](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicory-recipes), trimmed and separated into leaves
-   1 tbsp of extra virgin olive oil

5

Arrange 3 leaves of chicory on each plate along with 3 wedges of lettuce. Drizzle over some cocktail sauce and add some of the sliced fennel and diced avocado. Divide the crayfish between the four plates, drizzle over a little more of the sauce and garnish with chives or chive flowers
