Named after the [Raymond Chandler novel](https://www.amazon.com/Long-Goodbye-Raymond-Chandler/dp/0394757688/?tag=imbibmagaz-20) of the same name, the Long Goodbye gives Cynar a starring role, backed up beautifully by mezcal, citrus, simple syrup and soda water.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. Cynar
-   ½ oz. mezcal
-   1 oz. fresh orange juice
-   ¼ oz. fresh lime juice
-   [¼ oz. rich simple syrup (2:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/rich-simple-syrup-recipe)
-   1 oz. soda water

## Preparation

Add the soda water to a Collins glass. Shake the remaining ingredients with ice. Double-strain into a Collins glass. Top with ice, then garnish.
