This super speedy [paneer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paneer-recipes "Paneer") cheese recipe from Hoppers' Karan Gokani is full of [chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes "Chilli") heat - perfect served as a main with some fried [rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes "Rice") or [noodles](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/noodle-recipes "Noodle"), or as a side with other dishes. 

First published in 2022

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Paneer

-   400g of [paneer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paneer-recipes), cut into 2.5cm cubes or rectangles
-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   3 tbsp of rapeseed oil
-   1 [banana pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes) , deseeded and cut into 2.5cm cubes
-   1 small red onion, cut into 2.5cm cubes
-   2 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded and sliced
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped

#### Sauce

-   2 tbsp of rapeseed oil
-   1 tbsp of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), minced
-   1 tbsp of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   12 [curry leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/curry-leaf-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 [cinnamon stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes), (around 5cm in length)
-   1 tbsp of [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   2 tsp [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [double concentrated tomato paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   100g of [tomato ketchup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of white vinegar
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

1

Mix the paneer, ground turmeric and salt in a bowl and leave to marinate for 15 minutes

-   400g of [paneer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paneer-recipes), cut into 2.5cm cubes or rectangles
-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Make the devilled sauce. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat and fry the ginger and garlic for about two minutes until fragrant and golden

-   2 tbsp of rapeseed oil
-   1 tbsp of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), minced
-   1 tbsp of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced

4

Heat the rapeseed oil for the paneer in a clean deep non-stick pan or wok and fry the paneer on all sides until golden

-   3 tbsp of rapeseed oil
-   400g of [paneer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paneer-recipes), cut into 2.5cm cubes or rectangles

5

Add the banana pepper, red onion and green chillies. Stir fry for 30 seconds, then add the devilled sauce and continue to stir fry for a further 2-3 minutes until the sauce is coating the paneer and vegetables and piping hot. Garnish with the spring onions and serve straight away

-   1 [banana pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes) , deseeded and cut into 2.5cm cubes
-   1 small red onion, cut into 2.5cm cubes
-   2 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded and sliced
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
