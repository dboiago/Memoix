## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. bottled-in-bond bourbon (Old Granddad recommended)
-   3/4 oz. Becherovka
-   3/4 oz. dry Riesling (Trimbach recommended)
-   1/2 oz. banana liqueur (such as Giffard)
-   12 dashes chocolate bitters

## Preparation

Stir all the ingredients with ice to chill, and strain into a glass holding a single large ice cube. Garnish.
