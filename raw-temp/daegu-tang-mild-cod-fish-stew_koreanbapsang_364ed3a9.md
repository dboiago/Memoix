_Daegu tang (or daegu jiri) is a mild Korean fish stew made with cod fish and vegetables. Refreshingly delicious!_

![DSC6086 - Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/DSC6086.jpg "Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)")[](https://pinterest.com/pin/create/bookmarklet/?media=https://www.koreanbapsang.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/DSC6086.jpg&url=https://www.koreanbapsang.com/daegu-tangjiri-mild-cod-fish-stew/&description=Daegu%20Tang%20\(Mild%20Cod%20Fish%20Stew\))

If you think all Korean stews are spicy and pungent, you’re in for a pleasant surprise. Called daegutang (대구탕), this delicate stew made with cod fish, daegu (대구), is highly popular in Korea. This stew is very refreshing and satisfying! I love its subtle yet deep flavor! It’s a healthy, warming stew.

Cod fish is mild-flavored and has white flaky flesh. There are two distinct types of stew made with cod fish – spicy (daegu [maeuntang](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/domi-maeuntang-spicy-fish-stew-with-red-snapper/)) and mild (daegu tang or jiri). You can probably find both dishes on the menu at most Korean restaurants.

Although it will no longer be called daegutang, you can also make a similar stew with any mild-flavored white fish, such as sea bass, tilapia, halibut, grouper, etc. 

## **The broth**

The broth should be light to highlight the natural taste of the fresh fish. Of course, the fish must be very fresh! [Dasima (kombu) broth](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/vegetable-broth-for-korean-cooking/) or [Anchovy broth](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/how-to-make-anchovy-broth-for-korean/) can be used for this dish to add depth to the stew. In this recipe, I simply boiled a piece of dasima with Korean radish (mu) to make a stew base. I also like to throw in some clams or shrimp to add another layer of flavor to the broth.

## **Vegetable options**

The main vegetables used in this dish is Korean mu (white radish) and scallions. The radish is often used in a Korean soup or stew broth because it adds refreshing flavor. You can also use napa cabbage, zucchini, and/or kongnamul (soybean sprouts). It’s also typical to add a fragrant herb type vegetable for this. Ssukgat (crown daisy) and minari are most common types. You can also use watercress, if the others are not available. The chili pepper adds a nicely clean spicy kick, but it can be omitted.

You only need a little bit of each one you decide to use. All these vegetables can be used for other dishes such as [baechu guk](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/baechu-doenjang-guk-korean-soy-bean/) and [mu guk](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/korean-radish-soup-mu-gukmoo-guk/) or [namul](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/mu-namul-korean-radish-namul/).

![DSC6086 300x300 - Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/DSC6086-300x300.jpg "Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)")

-   ▢ 1 pound cod fish (daegu, 대구) preferably steak cuts, but fillets are okay too
-   ▢ 3-4 little neck clams or shrimp
-   ▢ 3 ounces Korean radish
-   ▢ 1 piece dasima (dried kelp, kombu), about 3-inch square
-   ▢ 6 ounces tofu
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon minced garlic
-   ▢ 1 or 2 scallions
-   ▢ 1 red or green chili pepper
-   ▢ 1 ounce enoki mushrooms optional
-   ▢ a handful of watercress, crown daisy leaves (ssukgat) or minari.
-   ▢ salt and pepper (a little bit of saeujeot (salted shrimp or fish sauce is great as an additional seasoning.)

-   Clean the fish and clams (or shrimp).
    
    ![Screen Shot 2022 03 25 at 5.26.28 PM 5 3 e1649480886533 - Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/Screen-Shot-2022-03-25-at-5.26.28-PM-5-3-e1649480886533.jpg "Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)")
    
-   Clean the vegetables. Cut the radish into thin bite sizes. Roughly slice the scallions and chili pepper. Cut the tofu into 1/2-inch thick bite size pieces.
    
    ![DSC6016 4 768x513 - Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/DSC6016-4-768x513.jpg "Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)")
    
-   Add 4 cups of water (or anchovy broth) to a medium size pot along with the radish and dasima (dried kelp). Bring it to a boil over high heat. Cook until the radish turns soft, about 5 minutes. Remove the dried kelp. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
    
    ![Screen Shot 2022 03 25 at 5.26.28 PM 5 4 e1649481698288 - Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/Screen-Shot-2022-03-25-at-5.26.28-PM-5-4-e1649481698288.jpg "Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)")
    
-   Drop in the fish, clams and tofu. Add the garlic. When it comes to a boil again, reduce the heat to medium high. Cook until the clams are open and the fish is almost cooked through, 4 to 5 minutes. (Do not overcook. The fish will continue to cook in the boiling hot broth while being served and eaten.) Adjust the seasoning if needed.
    
    ![DSC6036 e1649481762226 768x513 - Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/DSC6036-e1649481762226-768x513.jpg "Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)")
    
-   Add the enoki mushrooms, if using, scallions, and chili pepper. Continue to boil for a minute, and add the watercress or crown daisy leaves. Turn the heat off. Serve piping hot.
    
    ![DSC6039 2 e1649481843421 768x513 - Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)](https://www.koreanbapsang.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/DSC6039-2-e1649481843421-768x513.jpg "Daegu Tang (Mild Cod Fish Stew)")
