This time of the year [apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/apple-recipes) form a large part of our diet as we have an apple tree brimming with ripe fruit in the garden. If we didn’t have enough apples already we also relieved my brother in law of some jumbo apples from his trees, which were promptly peeled, cored and sliced and loaded into the freezer. If you find yourselves with lots of apples that need peeling I would definitely recommend investing in an inexpensive time saving apple peeling machine, which has been a lifesaver to me.

Choux buns, [eclairs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/eclair-recipes) and [profiteroles](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/profiteroles-recipes) are easier than you may imagine to make, and can be filled with cream, custard and/or fruit. These choux buns are filled with cinnamon apple and caramel cream, and topped with more [caramel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/caramel-recipes).
