### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [COUPE GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of orange zest twist and [Luxardo Maraschino Cherry](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/2048/luxardo-maraschino-cherries).

### How to make:

3.  [STIR](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-stir-a-cocktail) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into chilled glass.

### Garnish:

5.  [EXPRESS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1269/cocktail-garnishes/citrus-zest-twists) orange zest twist over cocktail and discard.
6.  Garnish with skewered maraschino cherry.

### Variations/similar cocktails:

There are numerous blackthorn recipes, but they fall into two camps: one based on Irish whiskey with vermouth and absinthe and the other with gin/sloe gin and vermouth. The numbering of the various Blackthorn variations starts in the 1930 _Swallows_ edition of Boothby's _World's Drinks And How To Mix Them_ and continues in his posthumously published 1934 edition, where he adds the Blackthorn No. 5.  
[**Blackthorn No. 1**](https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/22468/blackthorn-no-1) - sloe gin, rosso vermouth, dry vermouth, aromatic bitters, orange bitters.  
[**Blackthorn No. 2**](https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/10063/blackthorn-no-2) - sloe gin, rosso vermouth, sugar syrup, lemon juice, aromatic bitters, orange bitters.  
[**Blackthorn No. 3 (AKA Blackthorn Irish)**](https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/240/blackthorn-no-3) - Irish whiskey, dry vermouth, absinthe, Bokers bitters.  
[**Blackthorn No. 4**](https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/554/blackthorn-no-4) - gin, Dubonnet, kirshwasser, orange bitters.  
[**Blackthorn No. 5**](https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/22472/blackthorn-no-5) - gin, sloe gin, dry vermouth, lemon juice, sugar syrup, aromatic bitters.

## History:

The Blackthorn dates back to the 1890s and the Blackthorn No. 1 appears in [William "Cocktail Bill" Boothby](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/2963/people/william-t-boothby) features a version of this cocktail in his 1908 _World's Drinks And How To Mix Them_. This version is adapted from one of five different Blackthorn recipes in Stanley M. Jones' 1977 _Jones' Complete Barguide_.

> BLACKTHORN  
> Cocktail Glass Stir  
> Variation  
> 1 oz gin  
> 1 oz sloe gin  
> 1/4 oz dry vermouth  
> 1/4 oz sweet vermouth  
> 1 dash orange bitters  
> 1 dash Angostura bitters  
> Lemon twist, cherry
> 
> Stanley M. Jones, 1977
