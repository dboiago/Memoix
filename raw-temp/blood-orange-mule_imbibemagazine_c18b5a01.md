A crushable take on the classic highball.

Fresh blood orange juice adds the right amount of sweetness to this take on the classic Moscow Mule.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. vodka
-   1 oz. blood orange juice
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice
-   [1/2 oz. simple syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   ginger beer

## Preparation

Pour all of the ingredients in the glass, add fresh ice, stir, and top with ginger beer. Garnish.
