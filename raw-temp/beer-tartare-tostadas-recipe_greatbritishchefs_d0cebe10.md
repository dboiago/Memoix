This spectacular beef [tartare](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tartare-recipes) tostada recipe is spiked with habanero chilli and served with a cured egg yolk for plenty of visual appeal – the perfect starter for a [Mexico](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mexican-recipes)\-inspired [dinner party](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dinner-party-recipes). Make sure you get the egg yolks in the cure the morning before serving for the optimum result.

First published in 2017

1

To begin, cure the egg yolks. Whisk together the soy sauce, sake and sugar and add to a bowl. Separate the yolks from the whites and add to the marinade, ensuring they are completely covered

-   50g of [sake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sake-recipes)
-   200g of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   50g of sugar
-   4 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

2

Place in the fridge for 8 hours before serving to cure (the level of curing will depend on how long you leave the yolks – a longer cure will result in more solid yolks)

4

To serve, spoon the tartare onto tostadas, leaving a little well in the centre for the egg yolks. Carefully spoon the yolks into the centre of each tostada and serve

-   4 corn tortillas, deep-fried until crisp
