Enjoy Piña Colada vibes without any of the booze by shaking up this alcohol-free pineapple coconut drink by Enrique Rodriguez of So & So’s Neighborhood Piano Bar in New York City.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. coconut milk
-   2 oz. pineapple juice
-   1 1/2 oz. vanilla cinnamon syrup
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice

## Preparation

Add all the ingredients to a shaker with ice. Shake to chill and strain into the cup. Top with coconut foam.  
**Vanilla Cinnamon Syrup**Combine 1 1/2 cups of white granulated sugar, 1 cup of hot/nearly boiling water, 2 cinnamon sticks and 1 1/2 oz. bourbon vanilla extract into a pot and stir until sugar is dissolved. Cool syrup in the refrigerator for 2 hours, then strain into a bottle for use. Keeps refrigerated for up to 1 month.  
**Coconut Foam**Mix 5 oz. of demerara sugar, 6 oz. of coconut milk, 1/3 tsp. of salt, and 3 oz. of water together and pour into a whipped cream dispenser. Add 2 oz of egg white into the dispenser, shake, charge with ISI 3 times and you are ready to foam. An alternative to the ISI is to use a handheld mixer to whip the same ingredients and measurements. Keeps refrigerated for up to 5 days.
