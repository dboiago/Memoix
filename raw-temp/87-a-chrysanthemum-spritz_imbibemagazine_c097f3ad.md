A spritz-like spin on an obscure classic.

Crisp, refreshing, and unfussy, the spritz represents summer in a glass. It can also provide a solid foundation for bringing classic drinks into modern times. At Bellwether Bar in Portland, Oregon, the #87 cocktail integrates sparkling wine into the framework of the [Chrysanthemum](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/chrysanthemum-cocktail/), a semi-obscure classic combining dry vermouth, Bénédictine, and absinthe. The inclusion of bubbles adds the effervescent pop of a spritz, while the wine’s acidity also dials back the original drink’s sweetness.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. dry vermouth
-   3/4 oz. Bénédictine
-   1 tsp. absinthe
-   4 dashes grapefruit bitters
-   chilled dry sparkling wine, to top

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients, except the sparkling wine, to an ice-filled glass and stir to combine. Top with chilled sparkling wine and garnish.
