**Cooks in:** _5h_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _10_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 2/3 C. of granulated sugar
-   1 1/4 C. of grapefruit juice
-   1/2 C. of egg whites
-   2/3 C. of powdered sugar
-   1/2 C. of unsweetened cocoa powder

### Method

1

Sift the powdered sugar together with the cocoa powder. Beat the egg whites to stiff peaks with 1/3 cup of granulated sugar. Combine the beaten egg whites with the powdered sugar and cocoa powder, folding from the bottom upwards so the mixture doesn't deflate. Transfer it into a pastry bag with a 12 mm open star tip and form 20 meringues on a baking tray lined with parchment paper. Bake at 100°F for 4 hours.

2

Bring the rest of the granulated sugar to a boil with 1/3 cup of water. Remove the pan from the heat, add in the grapefruit juice, stir and let cool. Pour the mixture into an ice cream maker and start the machine. Make meringue sandwiches by filling two with the grapefruit sorbet and serve immediately.
