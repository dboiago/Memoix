-   60 minutes
    

When paired together, [cocoa](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/savoury-chocolate-recipes "Savoury chocolate") and [coffee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coffee-recipes "Coffee") provide a rich, roasted flavour in spice rubs, which works brilliantly with [lamb](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lamb-recipes "Lamb"). We’ve served these cutlets with soft flatbreads, mint yoghurt and pickled shallot rings to cut through the richness.

#### FLATBREADS

-   250g of plain flour
-   1 tsp baking powder
-   1/2 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   150g of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of water, or a little more as needed, to bring the dough together
-   olive oil, for brushing

#### MINT YOGHURT

-   300g of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   1 [small garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   1/2 [bunch of mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), leaves picked and finely chopped
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested
-   1 tbsp of olive oil

#### LAMB CUTLETS

-   1 tbsp of [unsweetened cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [coffee beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coffee-recipes), or ground coffee
-   1 tbsp of [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes), plus extra to serve
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 tsp [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of olive oil
-   12 [lamb cutlets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/roast-lamb-recipes), French-trimmed, at room temperature
-   olive oil, to serve
-   [mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), to serve
-   sumac, to serve

-   Spice grinder
-   Cast iron griddle pan

1

For the pickled shallots, place the shallot rings in a heatproof bowl. Combine the vinegar, water, sugar, salt, peppercorns and coriander seeds in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer then remove from the heat and pour over the sliced shallots. Set aside for 30 minutes at room temperature then chill in the fridge until needed

-   6 [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), sliced into rings
-   150ml of white wine vinegar
-   150ml of water
-   1 tbsp of caster sugar
-   1 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tsp [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 tsp [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)

2

For the flatbreads, combine the flour, baking powder and salt in a large mixing bowl. Add the yoghurt and mix to form a soft dough, adding the water slowly until you have a soft dough. Lightly knead for 1-2 minutes until smooth then cover and rest

-   250g of plain flour
-   1 tsp baking powder
-   1/2 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   150g of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   water, 4-5 tbsp, to bring the dough together

3

For the mint yoghurt, combine all the ingredients. Season to taste with salt then set aside in the fridge to let the flavours infuse

-   300g of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   1 [small garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   1/2 [bunch of mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), leaves picked and finely chopped
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested
-   1 tbsp of olive oil

5

Pat dry the lamb cutlets, then rub with the olive oil and coat all over with the rub. Set aside

-   12 [lamb cutlets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/roast-lamb-recipes), French-trimmed, at room temperature
-   1 tbsp of olive oil

6

To cook the flatbreads, divide the dough into 4 even-sized pieces and roll into rounds 0.5cm thick. Heat up a cast-iron pan over a medium-high heat then cook the flatbreads one at a time for 1-2 minutes on each side until puffed up and golden. Brush with olive oil, slice into pieces and keep warm, wrapped in a clean cloth

-   olive oil, for brushing

7

Meanwhile, to grill the lamb, heat up a griddle or frying pan until very hot

8

Grill the cutlets for 2-3 minutes on each side, so they form a dark aromatic crust – you may need to work in batches. Remove and leave to rest for 5 minutes

9

While the lamb rests, remove some shallot rings from their pickling liquid, to garnish, saving the rest in the fridge for other dishes

10

To serve, spread the mint yoghurt across plates with the back of a spoon. Divide up the flatbreads, arrange 3 lamb cutlets onto each place to give some height and scatter over the pickled shallot rings. Drizzle over a little olive oil and serve with the mint leaves and a dusting of sumac, smoked paprika, salt and pepper

-   olive oil, to serve
-   sumac, to serve
-   [mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), to serve
-   [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes), to serve
