**Cooks in:** _1h \+ 50mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   2 LB. of swordfish, sliced
-   2 CUPS of tomato purée
-   5 OZ. of spicy provolone
-   3/4 OZ. of pistachios, shelled and coarsely chopped
-   3/4 OZ. of pine nuts
-   1 of egg
-   Breadcrumbs

-   1 bunch of chopped parsley
-   Onion
-   Garlic
-   Dry oregano
-   Desalinated capers
-   Extra-virgin olive oil

### Method

1

Dice half an onion and mince a clove of garlic. Brown the onion and garlic in a saucepan with a tablespoon of olive oil, then add the tomato purée, salt and pepper. Cook for about 25 minutes. Complete the sauce with dried oregano and some capers to taste.

2

Beat 8 slices of swordfish with a wet meat tenderizer (so it won't stick to the fish) to thin the meat slices out. Blend the remaining fish together with the provolone, egg, 30 g breadcrumbs, a teaspoon of parsley, pistachios and pine nuts to make the swordfish ‘chop’ filling.

3

Arrange the filling over the swordfish slices and close them in a roll. Dip each roll into breadcrumbs and brown in a pan with a drizzle of olive oi, cooking on both sides for a total of about 5-6 minutes. Salt only if needed (the provolone is already quite salty) and serve with tomato sauce.
