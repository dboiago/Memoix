A rich and indulgent twist on the classic grilled cheese. Earthy [artichokes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/artichoke-recipes "Artichoke") are roasted in butter until tender and golden, then layered between slices of sourdough with fontina cheese and griddled until crisp and gooey. Served with a fresh radish and fennel salad, smoky garlic dip, and a generous helping of crispy [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes "Caper"), this is decadent, savoury, and deeply comforting.

First published in 2025

### Metric

### Imperial

#### For the crispy capers

-   3 tbsp of neutral oil, like vegetable or groundnut oil
-   1 handful of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), rinsed and patted dry

#### For the sandwich

-   4 slices of sourdough
-   300g of fontina cheese, grated
-   [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes), as needed
-   [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), as needed

1

Preheat the oven to 180°C

2

Start by preparing the artichokes. Trim away the tough outer leaves and stalks, then use a spoon to scoop out the fuzzy chokes from the hearts. Immediately place in a bowl of water with the lemon juice to prevent browning

-   4 [globe artichokes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/artichoke-recipes), outer leaves trimmed
-   [juice of 1 lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

4

Arrange the artichoke hearts in a baking dish, then pour over the garlic butter

5

Cover with foil and roast for 25-30 minutes, basting once or twice, until tender. Allow to cool slightly, then slice thinly

6

Meanwhile, mix together all the ingredients for the lemon and parsley butter. Season lightly with salt. Set aside

-   250g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), softened
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1/2 bunch of [flat leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fresh-herbs-recipes), finely chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

7

Now, make the salad. In a large bowl, toss together the shaved fennel and radishes

-   1 [bulb of fennel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes), trimmed and finely shaved on a mandolin
-   2 [radishes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/radish-recipes), finely sliced

8

Whisk together the olive oil, vinegar and mustard. Dress the vegetables with the dressing, season and set aside

-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   2 tsp white wine vinegar
-   1 tsp [wholegrain mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

9

For the smoky garlic dip, stir together all the ingredients with a good pinch of salt and pepper. Chill until needed

-   1 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   200g of crème fraîche
-   1 tsp [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), finely chopped
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

10

To crisp the capers, heat the neutral oil in a small frying pan until it shimmers. Fry the capers until puffed and golden (1–2 minutes), then transfer to kitchen paper to drain

-   3 tbsp of neutral oil, like vegetable or groundnut oil
-   1 handful of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), rinsed and patted dry

11

Spread one side of each slice of bread with the lemon parsley butter. Layer two slices of roasted artichoke and a generous handful of Fontina. Spread a tablespoon or so of mayonnaise on the outer side of the toastie

-   4 slices of sourdough
-   300g of fontina cheese, grated
-   [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes), as needed

12

Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium-low heat. Add some butter and, once melted, cook the sandwiches for 3-4 minutes on each side, pressing down gently with a spatula, until golden and crisp and the cheese is melted

-   [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), as needed

13

Halve the sandwiches and plate with the salad, a dollop of smoky garlic dip, and a scattering of crispy capers over everything for salty crunch
