“The inspiration behind this cocktail began with my love for the [Margarita](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/recipe-margarita/),” says Omar Bautista, beverage manager of Boro Brine in New York City. Using the tequila classic as a template, he layered the bold flavors of passion fruit and Aperol with the sweet vegetal notes of ají peppers for a refreshing and aromatic cocktail. Bautista says that any tropical pepper will work, too, or swap in red or yellow bell peppers for a more vegetal profile.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. blanco tequila
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1/2 oz. Aperol
-   1/2 oz. passion fruit liqueur
-   4 muddled ají dulce peppers
-   1/4 oz. cane syrup (2:1)
-   2 dashes orange bitters (optional)

## Preparation

Place 4 ají dulce peppers in a shaker and muddle. Add the rest of the ingredients along with ice. Shake hard for about 5 seconds, or until the outside of the shaker is frosty. Strain into the glass over ice.
