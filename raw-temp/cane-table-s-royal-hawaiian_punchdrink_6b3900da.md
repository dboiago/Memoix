After serving [Royal Hawaiians](https://punchdrink.com/articles/royal-hawaiian-tropical-gin-cocktail/) at Cane & Table for over a decade, Matt Young is very particular about every aspect of the drink, from the overproof gin to the freshly juiced pineapple and exacting orgeat. Young breaks with the original spec and makes the drink more New Orleans–friendly by serving it over pebble ice.

##### Ingredients

Serving: 1

-   1 1/2 ounces London Dry gin, such as Bombay Dry
-   1 ounce fresh pineapple juice
-   1/2 ounce lemon juice
-   1/2 ounce orgeat (see Editor’s Note)

Garnish: mint bouquet  

##### Directions

1.  Combine all ingredients in a shaker.
2.  Add a handful of pebble ice and whip-shake.
3.  Pour into a chilled double rocks glass, and top with more pebble ice.
4.  Garnish with a mint bouquet and serve with a straw.

##### Editor's Note

**Orgeat**  
2 cups white cane sugar  
1 cup unsweetened almond milk  
1/4 cup whole raw almonds  
1/4 teaspoon orange blossom water

Combine the cane sugar and almond milk in a saucepan and heat until the sugar is dissolved.  
Toast the almonds in a dry pan until fragrant, then add to syrup. Allow the mixture to cool.  
Add the orange blossom water to aromatize.  
After 24 hours, strain out the almond solids and bottle.
