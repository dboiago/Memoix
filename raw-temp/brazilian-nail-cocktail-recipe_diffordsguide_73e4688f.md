### Glass:

Serve in an [Old-fashioned glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 1⁄2 fl oz

[Cachaça (unaged)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/149/bws/cachaca)

1⁄2 fl oz

Blended Scotch whisky

3⁄4 fl oz

[Honey herbal liqueur (e.g. Drambuie)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/109/drambuie-liqueur)

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill an [OLD-FASHIONED GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of lime zest twist.

### How to make:

3.  [STIR](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-stir-a-cocktail) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-strain-a-cocktail) into ice-filled glass.

### Garnish:

5.  Express lime zest twist over the cocktail and use as garnish.

## Review:

A rift on the classic Rusty Nail using cachaça as the main base spirit with a hint of Scotch maintaining its lineage to the Drambuie Nail family.
