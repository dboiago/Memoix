Island vibes abound in this classic highball.

The classic Dark and Stormy is a summertime staple that matches the molasses-rich flavors of dark rum with the spice of ginger beer. Lime juice can be adjusted to taste, but at [The Stables Bar](http://thestablesbar.com.au/) in Australia, they squeeze about half a lime for best results.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. dark Bermuda rum (Gosling's)
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice
-   Ginger beer

## Preparation

Mix the ingredients together in the glass. Add ice and stir until chilled. Garnish.
