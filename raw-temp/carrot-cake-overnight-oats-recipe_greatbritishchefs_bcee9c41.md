These carrot cake overnight oats have all the classic flavours of [carrot cake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-cake-recipes "Carrot cake") but in a more breakfast-friendly form. Between the crunchy nutmeg crumb, silky yoghurt frosting and scattering of sweet banana you'll never dare call [oats](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oat-recipes "Oat") boring again. Any leftover nutmeg crumb can be stored in an airtight jar at room temperature.

First published in 2026

-   Piping bag with star nozzle

2

The next day, to make the nutmeg crumb, preheat the oven to 170°C

3

Spread the nuts and oats on a lined baking tray. Dot around the butter and sprinkle over the nutmeg and a pinch of salt before using your hands to toss. Roast in the oven for around 15 minutes or until golden

-   100g of pecan nuts, roughly chopped
-   100g of [hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), roughly chopped
-   100g of [almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes), roughly chopped
-   100g of [rolled oats](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/oat-recipes)
-   100g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), diced
-   2 tbsp of [ground nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes), plus extra for garnish

4

For the yoghurt frosting, first mix the yoghurt with an electric whisk, then whisk in the vanilla bean paste. Add the icing sugar bit by bit, then transfer to a piping bag fitted with a star nozzle

-   500g of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   1 tsp [vanilla bean paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vanilla-recipes)
-   150g of icing sugar

5

Take the oats out of the fridge, pipe over the whipped yoghurt frosting, and sprinkle over the nutmeg crumb, some chia seeds, and a pinch of nutmeg. Finish with a drizzle of honey and some sliced banana
