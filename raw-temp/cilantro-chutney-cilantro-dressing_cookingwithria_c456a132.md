This is a versatile cilantro chutney that can be used as a salad dressing or as a chutney on rice(see my cilantro rice), grilled or baked meat or seafood dishes! You can also substitute with [culantro](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/culantro/) also known as [bandhania](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/bandhania/) (shado beni) in the Caribbean. Hope you enjoy!

![](https://cookingwithria.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/cilantro-chutney-2-1024x694.jpg)

[![](https://cookingwithria.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/cilantro-chutney-4-150x150.jpg)](https://cookingwithria.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/cilantro-chutney-4.jpg)

-   1 cup [extra virgin olive oil](https://amzn.to/2Dn6fbv)
-   1 small bunch cilantro or culantro (bandhania)
-   1 [jalapeno](https://www.target.com/p/jalapenos-10oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-82675341?aflt=plt) seeded
-   1 pimento or hot pepper optional
-   1 tsp [cumin](https://amzn.to/2L56yvR)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://amzn.to/37sGeoL) or to taste
-   2 tablespoons [vinegar](https://www.target.com/p/white-distilled-vinegar-128oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-54633873?aflt=plt) optional

-   Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
    
-   Use as a dressing for grilled, baked or bbq’d meats and or seafood.
