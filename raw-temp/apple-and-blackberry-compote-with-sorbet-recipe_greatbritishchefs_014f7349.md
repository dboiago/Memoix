Anna Hansen re-imagines an apple and blackberry [crumble](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/crumble-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/crumble-recipes) recipe in this stunning [dessert](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dessert-recipes). She serves a delicious nut crumb atop a [blackberry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/blackberry-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/blackberry-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/blackberry-recipes) and apple compote, and finished the dish with a blackberry and liquorice [sorbet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes) for a real feast. If preferred, you could skip the sorbet and serve the dish with cream of crème fraîche.

First published in 2016

### Metric

### Imperial

-   Ice cream maker
-   Food processor
-   Mandoline

1

To begin, make the sorbet. Add the sugar, glucose and water to a saucepan and heat gently until the sugar has dissolved

-   150g of caster sugar
-   38g of glucose
-   300g of water

2

Whisk in the blackberry purée, liquorice paste and lemon juice until combined. Allow to cool completely before churning in an ice cream maker

-   500g of [liquorice paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/liquorice-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juice only
-   500g of [blackberry purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blackberry-recipes)

3

Preheat the oven to 170°C/gas mark 3

4

For the chestnut and hazelnut crumble, cream the butter and sugars together in a mixing bowl. Fold in the hazelnuts, oats, coconut and chestnuts

-   100g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   175g of light brown sugar
-   60g of caster sugar
-   85g of [ground hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes)
-   150g of oats, gluten-free, a mixture of jumbo and rolles
-   38g of [desiccated coconut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-recipes)
-   200g of [chestnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chestnut-recipes), cooked

5

Bake the crumble until golden, allow to cool and then pulse in a food processor until slightly broken down

6

To make the compote, peel, quarter and chop the apples, removing the cores. Add to a pan with the vanilla pod, lemon juice and sugar and simmer gently until just starting to break down

-   125g of caster sugar
-   4 [Bramley apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes)
-   1 vanilla pod, split
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juice only

7

Add the blackberries and simmer for a further 5 minutes

-   500g of [blackberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blackberry-recipes)

8

To prepare the apple garnish, cut the core of the apple out using a corer and slice into thin slices horizontally using a mandoline. If not using immediately, store in water with a squeeze of lemon juice to prevent browning

-   1 [Granny Smith apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes)

9

To serve, divide the warm compote between plates and top with the crumble. Garnish with whole blackberries, a piece of fresh apple and a scoop of the sorbet. Serve immediately

-   [fresh blackberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blackberry-recipes)
