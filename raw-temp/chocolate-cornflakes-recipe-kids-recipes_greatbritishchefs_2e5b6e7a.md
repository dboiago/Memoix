A childhood favourite these cornflake cakes are super simple, but always sure to bring a smile. [Dominic Chapman](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/dominic-chapman)'s recipe is perfect for a [kids](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kids-recipes) party and you could be as creative as you like with edible sprinkles and decorations.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100g of [milk chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), broken up
-   3 tbsp of golden syrup
-   100g of cornflakes
-   6 tsp hundreds and thousands, for sprinkling

-   12 cupcake cases

1

Place the butter, chocolate and golden syrup in a saucepan over a low heat. Cook until the chocolate melts

-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100g of [milk chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), broken up
-   3 tbsp of golden syrup

2

Allow to cool a little, then pour over the cornflakes

-   100g of cornflakes

3

Use a wooden spoon to gently stir all the ingredients together

4

Spoon the mixture into a tin lined with cupcake cases and sprinkle with hundreds and thousands. Place in the fridge to set, then serve

-   6 tsp hundreds and thousands, for sprinkling
