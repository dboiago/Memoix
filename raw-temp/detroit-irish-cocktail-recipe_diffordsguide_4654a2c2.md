### Glass:

Serve in a [Martini glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 1⁄2 fl oz

[Bourbon whiskey](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/69/makers-mark-bourbon)

1 fl oz

[Tuaca liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/174/tuaca-liqueur)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Grand Marnier or other cognac orange liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/114/grand-marnier-cordon-rouge)

1⁄4 fl oz

Vanilla sugar syrup

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [MARTINI GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of mint leaf (or shamrock).

### How to make:

3.  [STIR](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-stir-a-cocktail) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into chilled glass.

### Garnish:

5.  Garnish with mint leaf (or shamrock).

## Review:

There's nothing Irish about this drink, but it's good all the same.
