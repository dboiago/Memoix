### Imperial

-   20ml of [vodka](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vodka-cocktail-recipes), preferably Ketel One
-   20ml of [vodka](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vodka-cocktail-recipes), citroen
-   20ml of Grand Marnier, or Mandarin Napoléon
-   30ml of [cranberry juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)
-   15ml of [bergamot juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bergamot-orange-recipes)

1

Mix all the ingredients in a cocktail shaker with plenty of ice

2

Shake well and strain in a glass, garnish with bergamot peel
