The complexity of a classic Italian Vermouth di Torino forms the base for this cocktail, accented by the flavors of coffee, cardamom, and a French herbal liqueur.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. sweet vermouth (preferably an Italian Vermouth di Torino)
-   1/2 oz. cold-brew coffee
-   1/4 oz. Bénédictine
-   2 dashes cardamom bitters

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a glass and fill with ice, then stir briefly to combine. (Alternately, gently heat the ingredients in a small pan and serve warm in a coffee mug.) While using a lit match or lighter, squeeze a lemon twist so the oils spray into the flame and across the drink’s surface, then use the twist as garnish.
