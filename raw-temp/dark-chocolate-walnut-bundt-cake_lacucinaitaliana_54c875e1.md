**Cooks in:** _2h 10mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   1/3 LB. of walnuts, pecans, Brazil nuts
-   1 C. of flour, plus extra
-   1/2 C. of sugar
-   1 STICK of butter, plus extra
-   1/2 C. of milk
-   2 OZ. of dark chocolate
-   1/3 C. of cocoa powder
-   1 TSP. of baking powder
-   3 of eggs
-   Salt

### Method

1

Melt the chocolate in the warm milk, then add in the sugar; let it cool and incorporate the eggs, one at a time. In a bowl, mix the butter until it is softened; then add in the liquid mixture a little at a time, stirring with a whisk.

2

Add a pinch of salt; sift the baking powder, cocoa powder, and flour together, then add them to the mixture. Finally, incorporate all of the coarsely chopped nuts. Pour the mixture into a buttered and floured bundt pan (8-inch outer diameter, 3-inch height, 2 1/2-inch inner diameter). Bake at 350°F; after 40 minutes, lower the temperature to 330°F and continue baking for another 20 minutes.

3

Use a toothpick to ensure it is completely baked, then remove from the oven. Remove the cake from the pan and let it cool on a grate. Serve in slices for breakfast, accompanied with a glass of chocolate milk.
