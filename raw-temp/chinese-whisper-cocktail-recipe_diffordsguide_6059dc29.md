### Glass:

Serve in a [Martini glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

2 slice

Fresh ginger root (thumbnail-sized slice)

2 fl oz

[Ketel One Citroen Vodka](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/1181/ketel-one-citroen-vodka)

1 fl oz

Lychee liqueur

1⁄2 fl oz

Lime juice (freshly squeezed)

1⁄4 fl oz

Ginger syrup

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [MARTINI GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of lemon zest twist.

### How to make:

3.  [MUDDLE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-muddle) ginger in base of shaker.
4.  Add other ingredients and [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) with ice.
5.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into chilled glass.

### Garnish:

6.  [EXPRESS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1269/cocktail-garnishes/citrus-zest-twists) lemon zest twist over the cocktail and use as garnish.

## Review:

There's more than a whisper of ginger in this spicy cocktail.
