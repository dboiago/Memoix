Apple brandy lends an extra fruity layer to this variation. 

This [Brown Derby](https://imbibemagazine.com/brown-derby-cocktail/) riff replaces bourbon with apple brandy, dials back the grapefruit juice and adds a touch of salt for a cocktail that tastes like a salted caramel apple.

## Preparation

Shake the first 3 ingredients with ice to chill. Prepare a chilled glass by rinsing it with the salt solution, then discarding the excess. Strain the cocktail into the glass. Garnish.

**Sea-salt solution**Combine 1 Tbsp. of sea salt with 4 Tbsp. of water, then stir until dissolved.
