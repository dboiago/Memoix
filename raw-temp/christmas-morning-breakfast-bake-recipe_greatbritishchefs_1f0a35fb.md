Karen Burns-Booth serves up a hearty start to [Christmas Day](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/christmas-recipes). Packed with bacon, [sausages](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/sausage-recipes), tomatoes and spinach – and bound together with paprika-spiced eggs and milk – this dish is sure to please all the family.

First published in 2015

A wonderful [breakfast](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/breakfast-recipes) bake that is the perfect way to start the day on Christmas morning – crisp bacon, herby sausages, [sun-dried tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sun-dried-tomato-recipes) and baby spinach give this breakfast bake a festive look and taste which is all baked in a lightly spiced egg and milk mixture. This can be prepared the night before ready for popping into the oven on [Christmas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/christmas-recipes) morning, thus giving you an easy, tasty and hassle-free celebration breakfast, or brunch, depending on what time you get up!

### Metric

### Imperial

-   200g of [smoked streaky bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bacon-recipes), diced, or lardons
-   350g of [pork and herb sausages](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sausage-recipes), skinned and cut into small pieces
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), peeled and diced
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), peeled and finely diced
-   450g of [baby spinach leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spinach-recipes)
-   4 [sun-dried tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sun-dried-tomato-recipes), drained, finely chopped
-   1 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   600ml of whole milk
-   9 [free-range eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), lightly beaten
-   1 [baguette](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), large, cut into cubes
-   2 tbsp of rapeseed oil
-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

1

Add the rapeseed oil to a large frying pan and gently sauté the bacon, sausage, onion andgarlic for about 5–8 minutes until the bacon is crisp and the sausages are cooked. Add the spinach and cook until it is wilted, for about 2 minutes

2

Add the chopped sun-dried tomatoes to the mixture and set aside

3

Mix the milk with the eggs and season to taste with salt, pepper and the smoked paprika

4

Scatter the baguette cubes over the bottom of a large baking dish, and top with half of the spinach mixture

5

Top with the remaining baguette cubes and spinach mixture and then pour the egg and milk mixture over the top

6

Push the bread down into the egg mixture, cover and allow to sit for at least 2–3 hours (or overnight)

7

Preheat the oven to 200°C/gas mark 6

8

Bake uncovered for 45–50 minutes, until the bake is puffed up and golden brown. Allow to cool for 5 minutes before serving
