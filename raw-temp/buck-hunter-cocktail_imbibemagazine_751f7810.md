This recipe from our 2015 Bartender of the Year, Derek, Brown, might just be the perfect pitcher cocktail.

## Ingredients

-   6 oz. bourbon
-   2 1/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   2 1/4 oz. ginger syrup
-   1 1/2 oz. water
-   4 oz. sparkling water
-   3 dashes Angostura bitters

## Preparation

Add ingredients to a pitcher. Add ice, stir to chill, pour into glasses and garnish.
