This is developed from local street food, street-style potatoes. But it is different because we add Bok Choy as the second ingredient. Adding some ubiquitous dark green vegetables could elevate it to a different level.

![bok choy and crispy potato | chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/potato-and-bok-choy-stir-fry-20-scaled.webp)

I found this firstly in a restaurant near my office. They are selling potatoes with a magic ingredient- fresh fennel. There are many variations of crispy potatoes, in addition to the popular [salt-and\-pepper variety](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/sauteed-small-potatoes-with-salt-and-pepper/).

If you are a Bok Choy lover, this is your way of using Bok Choy not as a main ingredient but as a magic add-on.

The taste of bok choy in this dish is unique: not crunchy like fresh stir-fry, but with a texture similar to pickled vegetables. At my first test, I used only one bok choy with three medium-sized potatoes. But I regretted it after the first bite. The bok choy’s texture was so unique that I decided to add more bok choy to the dish.

**This is the basic vegan version** that’s already amazing on its own – but here’s a little secret: if you have pork belly on hand, you can skip the cooking oil and render the pork belly fat instead. It adds incredible depth of flavor, making everything taste even more fragrant and irresistible. Totally optional, but if you’re a meat lover, you’re going to want to try it that way!

## Step by Step

Start with a pot of water, bring it to a boil, then blanch the bok choy for 20 seconds. Transfer them out and chill in cold water. Cut the bok choy into small pieces and then squeeze out the water.

![bok choy and crispy potatoes| chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/bok-choy-and-crispy-potatoes-step1.webp)

Peel and cut the potatoes into 3cm cubes. Steam them for 10 minutes in the same pot of water, or boil them for 7 miuntes. I use the same water for blanching bok choy. Transfer out and drain thoroughly.

![bok choy and crispy potatoes| chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/potato-and-bok-choy-stir-fry-3.webp)

![bok choy and crispy potatoes| chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/potato-and-bok-choy-stir-fry-8.webp)

Get a pan or a wok, add some oil, and lay the boiled potatoes one by one until they have a crispy shell. This may take some time.

Move the potatoes to the edges of the wok. Fry garlic and scallions until aromatic.

Add the Bok Choy in. Season it with salt, pepper, and light soy sauce. If you want a spicy flavor, add some pepper flakes.

![bok choy and crispy potatoes| chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/bok-choy-and-crispy-potatoes-step2.webp)

![bok choy and crispy potato | chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/potato-and-bok-choy-stir-fry-18-scaled.webp)

![bok choy and crispy potatoes| chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/potato-and-bok-choy-stir-fry-28-500x500.webp)

-   2–3 heads bok choy
-   2 medium potatoes
-   3-4 tbsp. cooking oil 3 for pan| 4 for wok
-   2 scallions , minced
-   4 cloves garlic , minced
-   ½ tsp. salt or to taste
-   ¼ tsp. black pepper or to taste
-   1 tbsp. [light soy sauce](https://amzn.to/3ZdN4Md)
-   ½ tsp. [Pepper flakes (adjust for spice)](https://amzn.to/3EdDQqF) ,optional

-   Start with a pot of water, bring it to a boil, then blanch the bok choy for 20 seconds. Transfer them out, cool down. Cut into small pieces and then sqeeze the water out.
    
-   Peel and cut the potatoes into 3cm cubes and then cook them in boiling water for 7 minutes until soft.
    
-   Add a little bit more oil and then place the potato cubes in. Fry until all of the potato cubes are crispy and golden brown.
    
-   Add garlic and scallion. Fry until aromatic.
    
-   Add the Bok Choy in. Season it with salt, pepper and light soy sauce. Mix well. Optionally, you can sprinkle some chili flakes for a spicy and hot version.
