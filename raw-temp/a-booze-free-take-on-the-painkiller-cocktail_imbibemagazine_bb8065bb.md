A zero-proof take on a beloved cocktail. 

As the booze-free craze rages on, more and more bartenders are putting zero-proof spins on classic cocktails, and the results are often delicious. Take this N/A Painkiller from [Wright & Company](http://wrightdetroit.com/) in Detroit_._ It skips the rum and adds raspberry and lime to a mix of pineapple juice, orange juice and coconut cream, all shaken and served over crushed ice for an ultra-refreshing taste of summer.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. pineapple juice
-   3/4 oz. orange juice
-   3/4 oz. coconut cream
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1/4 oz. raspberry syrup
-   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
-   Raspberry Syrup
-   1 cup raspberry juice
-   3/4 cup white sugar

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a shaker with pebble or crushed ice, shake to chill, then pour the contents into a glass. Garnish.

**Raspberry Syrup**Combine raspberry juice with white sugar and shake to dissolve.
