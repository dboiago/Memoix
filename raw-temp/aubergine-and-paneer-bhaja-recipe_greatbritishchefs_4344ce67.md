This rich, colourful [vegetarian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes) starter has its origins in Bengal, where [aubergine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/aubergine-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/aubergine-recipes) fritters are known as _bhaja_. Topped with spiced grated [paneer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/paneer-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/paneer-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/paneer-recipes) and a dollop of fresh mint chutney, it's a wonderful way to start an [Indian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes) meal.

First published in 2020

-   2 [aubergines](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/aubergine-recipes)
-   vegetable oil, for frying
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 [green pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [red pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 red onion, finely chopped
-   1 [tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 tsp [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   3 tsp [red chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   2 tsp [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   2 tsp [ground cumin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   200g of [paneer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paneer-recipes), coarsely grated
-   30g of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

#### Mint chutney

-   40g of [fresh mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes)
-   60g of [fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   10g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

2

Pour a thin layer of vegetable oil into a wide frying pan and place over a medium heat. Thickly slice the aubergines, then sprinkle with salt. Fry the aubergines on both sides for around 20 minutes, turning occasionally, until crisp and golden

-   vegetable oil, for frying
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste
-   2 [aubergines](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/aubergine-recipes)

3

While the aubergines cook, pour a glug of vegetable oil into a separate non-stick pan and place over a medium heat. Add the cumin seeds, then once they start to crackle add the green pepper, red pepper, red onion and tomato. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft, then add the turmeric, red chilli powder, ground coriander, ground cumin and a pinch of salt. Cook out for 2 minutes, then add the grated paneer and cook for a further 2 minutes. Stir in the coriander leaves, then set aside and keep warm

-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 [green pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [red pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 red onion, finely chopped
-   1 [tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 tsp [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   3 tsp [red chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   2 tsp [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   2 tsp [ground cumin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   200g of [paneer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paneer-recipes), coarsely grated
-   30g of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped

4

To serve, place slices of the cooked aubergine on each plate, then top each with a spoonful of the paneer mixture. Top with a smaller spoonful of mint chutney then serve with a side salad (if desired)
