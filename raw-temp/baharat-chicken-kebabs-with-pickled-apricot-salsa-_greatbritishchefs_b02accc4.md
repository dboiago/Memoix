These grilled chicken [kebabs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kebab-recipes "Kebab") are flavoured with baharat, a spice blend used widely in Eastern Mediterranean cooking. It generally consists of black pepper, cardamom, cloves, cumin, nutmeg, coriander and paprika. It adds a beautiful warmth to these kebabs, which are served with a pickled [apricot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apricot-recipes "Apricot") salsa and harissa-spiked yoghurt.
