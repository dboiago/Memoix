A match made in bitters heaven.

When the alpine goodness of Braulio meets the smoky depth of Sfumato, this Amaro Daiquiri becomes a match made in bitters heaven.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. Braulio amaro
-   1 oz. Sfumato Rabarbaro
-   ½ oz. gomme syrup (or ¾ oz. of 1:1 simple syrup)
-   ¾ oz. fresh lime juice

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a shaker with ice, then shake to chill. Strain into a coupe and garnish.
