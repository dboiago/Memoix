There's nothing quite like a pile of warm [scones](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/scone-recipes "Scone") straight from the oven. This savoury scone recipe by Liberty Mendez is beautifully simple, combining nutty Doves Farm organic spelt flours with punchy [mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes "Mustard") and mature cheddar. These are so moreish you'll be sure to keep coming back to this recipe!

First published in 2025

### Metric

### Imperial

-   200g of white spelt flour
-   200g of [wholemeal spelt flour](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spelt-recipes)
-   1 1/2 tbsp of [mustard powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   4 tsp baking powder
-   1 tbsp of caster sugar
-   25g of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   100g of [salted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), cubed and cold, plus extra for serving
-   150g of [mature cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), half coarsely grated and half crumbled into small 1 cm chunks, plus extra finely grated for the top
-   180ml of whole milk
-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), beaten

1

Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan) and line a flat baking tray with baking paper

2

Whisk together the white and wholemeal spelt flour, mustard powder, baking powder, sugar, chives and a big pinch of salt in a large mixing bowl until combined

-   200g of white spelt flour
-   200g of [wholemeal spelt flour](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spelt-recipes)
-   1 1/2 tbsp of [mustard powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   4 tsp baking powder
-   1 tbsp of caster sugar
-   25g of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Add in the butter and rub into the flour between your fingertips until you have a breadcrumb-like/sandy consistency. Stir through the cheddar

-   100g of [salted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), cubed and cold, plus extra for serving
-   150g of [mature cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), half coarsely grated and half crumbled into small 1 cm chunks, plus extra finely grated for the top

4

Pour in the milk and mix until you have a shaggy dough. Knead slightly for 2 minutes until you have a smooth mix

-   180ml of whole milk

5

Gently roll out the dough to a 2 cm-thick circle (ours was 22 cm). Cut into 8 triangles, like a pizza, spreading them all out so they’re about 5 cm apart but still in a circle. Brush the top of the triangles with the beaten egg and top with a small grating of cheddar

-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), beaten

6

Bake for 17–20 mins until golden brown. Leave to cool before tearing open and serving with salted butter
