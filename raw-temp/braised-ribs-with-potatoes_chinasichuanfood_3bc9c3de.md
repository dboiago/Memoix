Sichuan style braised ribs with potatoes.

Though noodles are staple food in northern China, Sichuan style noodles and Chongqing noodles enjoy a national popularity too. Different from the northern provinces, people pay more attention to make chewy handmade noodles. Then the noodles can be served with several stir-fries or salad. In Sichuan cuisine, we make lots of yummy noodles bowls with lot of seasonings and stews.  And here we are introducing one type of ribs noodles popular in Sichuan. You can use potatoes, taro and other vegetables as side dish.

![braised rib and potato|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/braised-rib-and-potato_-11.jpg)

It requires very basic and common ingredients for Sichuan people. The most season is [doubanjiang](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/doubanjiang/), douchi and several spices. I use fresh small potato in this dish and they turn out so good. So if you get fresh peppers around, try with this at least once.

This dish is quite great with both noodles and steamed rice. When serving with noodles, keep around 1/3 of the juicy after thickening the sauce. If you want to serve this with steamed rice, you can thicken the sauce for better flavored and stronger ribs. Besides, potatoes can be substituted with other seasonal vegetables for example white radish in winter, carrot and bamboo.

To bring this dish to the next level, you can serve it with [homemade noodles](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/homemade-noodles/) or [easy hand pulled noodles](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/hand-pulled-noodles-easier-version/).

### Ingredients

-   800g ribs
-   2 slices of ginger
-   4  scallions, divided
-   2 tbsp. cooking oil
-   2 tbsp. [doubanjiang](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/doubanjiang/)
-   1 tbsp. [dou-chi](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/dou-chi-fermented-black-beans/)
-   10 slices of ginger
-   1 tbsp. minced garlic
-   1 tbsp. light soy sauce
-   1 tbsp. Chinese cooking wine (Shaoxing wine)
-   1 tsp. rock sugar
-   2 star anises

### Instructions

Place the ribs in a deep pot with enough water to cover. Add scallion in. Bring to a boiling and then cook for 1-2 minutes. Transfer out and clean with warm water. Drain and set aside.

![braised rib and potato|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/braised-rib-and-potato_.jpg)

Prepare a clay pot or stew pot, add around 2 tablespoons of oil and fry doubanjiang over slow fire until the oil turns slightly red. This is a very important step to remove the raw taste from doubanjiang.

![braised rib and potato|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/braised-rib-and-potato_-2.jpg)

Add ginger, garlic and fermented black beans.

![braised rib and potato|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/braised-rib-and-potato_-3.jpg)

Add the ribs in and let them stay for a while until slightly browned. Add light soy sauce, cooking wine, sugar, star anise and scallion and fry for another 1-2 minutes.

![braised rib and potato|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/braised-rib-and-potato_-5.jpg)

Pour enough warm water and add small potatoes in. Cover the lid and simmer for 20 minutes until the potatoes are well softened. Uncover and continue cooking for 10 minutes until the sauce is reduced to 1/3 again.

![braised rib and potato|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/braised-rib-and-potato_-7.jpg)

Cook noodles and serve with the braised ribs.

-   Cook noodles according to the instructions. Add around 1/2 cup of water for cooking the noodles and a small pinch of salt and pepper in each serving bowl. Transfer the noodles in.
    
-   Scoop braised ribs along with the juice.
