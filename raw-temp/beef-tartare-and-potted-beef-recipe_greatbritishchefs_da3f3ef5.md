-   medium
    
-   4
    
-   4 hours 30 minutes
    

This stunning [beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beef-recipes) rump [starter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/starter-recipes) uses this beautiful cut for two different preparations – a classic [beef tartare](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tartare-recipes) and delicious potted beef recipe. Paul serves the dish with his spin on gentleman's relish – to save time you can use a good-quality brown sauce instead if preferred. The relish makes more than is needed for this recipe, but it can be stored in [sterilised jars](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/how-to-cook/how-to-sterilise-jars) and kept for a host of other dishes.

#### Potted beef

-   500g of [beef rump](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes)
-   750ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   3 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and cut into 4 pieces
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   2 [onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), peeled and halved
-   1 sprig of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   1l beef stock
-   75g of [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced
-   25ml of sherry vinegar
-   sunflower oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### Gentleman's relish

-   4kg [plums](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/plum-recipes)
-   500ml of white wine vinegar
-   500g of [pitted prunes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/prune-recipes)
-   350g of soft brown sugar
-   100ml of Worcestershire sauce
-   5 [onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   10 [Granny Smith apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), cored and chopped (no need to peel)
-   10 [red plums](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/plum-recipes), stoned and chopped
-   15 [anchovy fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/anchovy-recipes)
-   5 tbsp of [English mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   3 tbsp of ground mixed spice
-   250ml of [tamarind paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tamarind-recipes)
-   250ml of water
-   200ml of [fresh orange juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes)
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 tsp [ground white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

-   Apple wood chips
-   Water bath
-   Liquidiser
-   Piping bags 2
-   5cm round mould

1

To begin, prepare the beef rump for the potted beef. Add the rump to a large container and add the wine, vegetables (not including the diced shallots) and thyme to cover. Leave to marinate for 24 hours

-   500g of [beef rump](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes)
-   1 sprig of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   750ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   3 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and cut into 4 pieces
-   2 [onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), peeled and halved
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)

2

The night before serving, cut the plum tomatoes for the gentleman's relish in half. Spread applewood chips in a deep flameproof baking tray and place over a high flame, allowing the chips to smoke and catch fire. As soon as the chips catch, smother with a lid to extinguish and top with a wire rack that fits inside the tray

-   4kg [plums](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/plum-recipes)

3

Place the tomato halves on the rack and seal the entire tray with tin foil to create a smoke box. At this point, remove from the heat and leave the tomatoes covered for a few hours to infuse. Transfer to a container with a sealable lid and place in the fridge overnight

5

Drain the beef rump from the marinade and reserve the wine and vegetables separately

6

Place a large pan over a medium-high heat with a dash of oil and add the beef. Colour all over until nicely caramelised then remove from the pan and add the vegetables. Brown the vegetables nicely then transfer to a large saucepan, along with the beef

-   sunflower oil

7

Add the wine to the pan and reduce by half. Transfer to the saucepan with the beef and vegetables, add the stock and top the pan with a lid. Cook over a low heat for 3–4 hours until the beef is tender and breaking apart

-   1l beef stock

8

Meanwhile, place the eggs for the confit egg yolks in a waterbath preheated to 62°C and cook for 2 hours

-   6 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

9

Add the finely diced shallots for the beef to a small pan and submerge in oil. Confit very slowly over a low heat until the shallots are soft and caramelised. Drain and set aside

-   75g of [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced
-   sunflower oil

10

When the beef is ready, remove the meat from the pan and flake into a mixing bowl. Pass the cooking liquid through a sieve into a clean pan and reduce the liquid until thick and rich. Add to the meat, season with salt and pepper and stir through. Season with sherry vinegar and the confit shallots

-   25ml of sherry vinegar
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

11

Once all mixed and tasting good, transfer to small pots and even out the top. Place in the fridge to set before capping with the beef dripping

12

Transfer the gentleman's relish to a liquidiser, blend until smooth then pass through a fine sieve. Transfer to sterilised jars and keep in the fridge

13

To finish the confit egg yolk, peel each egg and blend the yolks in a liquidiser until smooth. Pass through a fine sieve and transfer to piping bags or squeezy bottles

14

To prepare the dripping to cap the potted beef, add the fat, water and thyme to a pan and place over a low heat. Allow the fat to slowly render

-   10g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   500g of [beef fat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes), minced
-   100ml of water
-   [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)

15

Once all of the fat has been released, pass through a fine sieve and season with salt. Pour over the set rump pots (it’s crucial that the beef is cold and set before topping with the fat). Chill in the fridge to set, then remove prior to serving to allow the fat to soften slightly

16

To make the gribiche for the tartare, simply stir together all of the ingredients

-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), hard-boiled for 10 minutes then grated
-   37g of cornichons, finely chopped
-   30g of [lilliput capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes)
-   10g of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   5g of [tarragon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes), chopped
-   10g of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

17

Finely slice then dice the beef rump for the tartare and add to a bowl. Add the shallots and gribiche, then a few drops of Tabasco to taste for a kick of heat

-   Tabasco, 2–3 drops
-   200g of [beef rump](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes), trimmed of fat and sinew
-   20g of [banana shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced

18

To serve, place the ring mould on a plate and add a portion of beef tartare. Remove the ring and pipe a round of egg yolk on top before repeating with the other plates

19

Add a dish of the potted beef to the plate and pipe on some gentleman's relish. Finish with slices of sourdough toast and wood sorrel leaves

-   [wood sorrel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorrel-recipes)
-   [sourdough bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), toasted
