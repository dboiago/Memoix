In this recipe from from Hoppers' Karan Gokani, a whole [sea bream](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sea-bream-recipes "Sea bream") is smothered in an intense spice paste and wrapped in a banana leaf to cook; this allows all the flavours to cook inside the parcel with the fish, while the banana leaf adds a unique, grassy flavour. This is such a fun way to cook [fish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-recipes "Fish") - make sure to open the parcel at the table!  

First published in 2022
