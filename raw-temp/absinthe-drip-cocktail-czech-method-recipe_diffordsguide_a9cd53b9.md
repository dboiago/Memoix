### Glass:

Serve in an [Absinthe glass or old-fashioned glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 1⁄2 fl oz

[La Fée Parisienne absinthe](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/293/la-fee-parisienne-absinthe)

2 fl oz

[Chilled water/mineral water](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/3711/cocktails/some-cocktails-benefit-from-added-water)

1 cube

Superfine/Caster/Baker's special sugar (white)

## How to make:

BEWARE: please take care. Flames and alcohol are a dangerous combination!.  
Place a sugar cube on a slotted absinthe spoon resting across the top of the glass. POUR the absinthe over the sugar cube into the glass. Light the absinthe-soaked cube and leave it to burn and caramelise. Using a bottle of chilled mineral water with a small hole in the cap, DRIP water over what's left of the sugar so it dissolves and drips into the glass. Add ice, stir, and serve.

## Review:

More about the theatrics involved in its making than the taste of the finished drink.

## History:

This supposedly bohemian method of serving absinthe became popular back in 1998 with the [UK launch of Hill's Absinthe](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1102/absinthe/absinthe-renaissance). Accordingly, if you are going to make this drink, then it is best to use a Czech-style absinthe such as [La Fée Bohemian](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/spirits/absinthe-pastis-and-aniseed/BWS001557/la-fee-bohemian-70).
