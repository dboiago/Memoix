## What is Arroz con Pollo?

Arroz con Pollo is a traditional Latin American dish that combines chicken, rice, and vegetables, all cooked together until the flavors meld into one comforting one-pot meal. It has all the flavors of [Mexican Rice (Arroz Rojo)](https://muybuenoblog.com/mexican-rice-arroz-mexicano-video/) with juicy chicken simmered right in, making it a complete meal in one pan.

![Yvette Marquez muy bueno Mexican food blogger in an orange dress holding a molcajete.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/yvette-1x1-1.jpg)

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

-   **One-pan meal** – everything simmers together in one skillet, which means less cleanup and more flavor.
-   **Juicy, flavorful chicken** – bone-in thighs and drumsticks keep the meat tender and rich.
-   **Quick seasoning** – no overnight marinade required, just season the chicken while prepping.
-   **Customizable** – swap in chicken breasts for a lighter dish, or stir in peas during the resting time for extra color and nutrition.
-   **Cozy comfort food** – perfect for fall nights, family dinners, or anytime you want a hearty homecooked meal.

## Ingredients & Substitutions

-   **Fire-roasted tomatoes** – smoky depth, though regular diced tomatoes also work.
-   **Jalapeño** (optional) – for extra heat.

## How to Make Arroz con Pollo

**Step 1: Season the chicken.** Pat chicken dry and season with salt, pepper, oregano, onion powder, garlic powder, and chili powder.

![A clay dish filled with spices for Arroz con Pollo, including salt, black pepper, chili powder, onion powder, garlic powder, and dried oregano.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/taco-seasoning-spices.jpeg)

![Raw chicken pieces seasoned with spices before cooking Arroz con Pollo.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/spice-season-arroz-con-pollo.jpeg)

**Step 2: Brown the chicken.** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken until golden, about 5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.

![Bone-in chicken thighs browning in a skillet until golden.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/brown-pollo-chicken.jpeg)

**Step 3: Cook the veggies.** In the same skillet, sauté onion, bell pepper, and garlic until softened, about 3 minutes.

![Chopped onions and bell peppers sautéing in a skillet for Arroz con Pollo.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/saute-onion-peppers-arroz-con-pollo.jpeg)

**Step 4: Toast the rice.** Stir in the rice and cook until lightly toasted, about 3 minutes.

**Step 5: Add liquids and chicken.** Stir in tomato sauce, broth, and fire-roasted tomatoes, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Bring to a boil, then nestle chicken back into the pan with jalapeño if using.

![One-pan Arroz con Pollo cooking on the stove with chicken, rice, and a jalapeño on top.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/one-pan-dinner-arroz-con-pollo.jpeg)

**Step 6: Simmer.** Cover, reduce heat, and cook until the chicken is cooked through and rice is tender, 25 to 30 minutes.

**Step 7: Rest.** Remove from heat, cover, and let rest for 10 minutes. Stir in peas if using.

**Step 8: Garnish and serve.** Taste, adjust seasoning with salt if needed, and top with cilantro. Serve warm.

![Bowl of Arroz con Pollo topped with cilantro and avocado slices on a lace tablecloth.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/muy-bueno-arroz-con-pollo.jpeg)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What kind of rice is best for Arroz con Pollo?**

Long-grain rice works best for this arroz con pollo recipe because it cooks up fluffy and doesn’t clump together.

**Can I make Arroz con Pollo with chicken breasts?**

Yes, you can substitute chicken breasts, but watch the cook time to prevent them from drying out.

**Do I need to marinate the chicken for Mexican chicken and rice?**

No long marinating is required — just season while prepping your other ingredients.

**Can I add vegetables to Arroz con Pollo?**

Absolutely! Peas, carrots, or corn are common. Peas are especially nice stirred in during the resting period.

**Can I use taco seasoning in Arroz con Pollo?**

Yes! My [Homemade Taco Seasoning](https://muybuenoblog.com/taco-seasoning/) works beautifully in this recipe. It’s made with the same spices—garlic, onion, oregano, and pure ground chile—so it’s a quick shortcut without sacrificing flavor.
