### Glass:

Serve in a [Coupe glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 1⁄2 fl oz

[Bourbon whiskey](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/69/makers-mark-bourbon)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Apple Schnapps liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/531/berentzen-apple-schnapps)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Giffard Crème de Myrtille liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/1688/giffard-creme-de-myrtille)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Cranberry juice (sweetened)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/3375/bws/cranberry-juice)

1⁄2 fl oz

Apple juice/apple cider (cloudy & unsweetened)

1⁄4 fl oz

Lime juice (freshly squeezed)

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [COUPE GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of dehydrated apple slice.

### How to make:

3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into chilled glass.

### Garnish:

5.  Garnish with dehydrated apple slice floated on cocktail.
