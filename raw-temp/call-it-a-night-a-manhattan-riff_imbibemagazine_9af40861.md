Averna, PX sherry and a whisper of absinthe bring new flavor to the original.

The Manhattan travels to Milwaukee in this rich and fragrant cocktail from Goodkind.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. bourbon (Goodkind uses Woodford Reserve)
-   ¾ oz. Carpano Antica Formula vermouth
-   ½ oz. Pedro Ximenez sherry
-   1 tsp. Averna
-   1 tsp. absinthe

## Preparation

Stir the first 4 ingredients with ice until chilled. Rinse a chilled coupe with absinthe, discarding the excess, then strain the cocktail into the prepared glass and garnish.
