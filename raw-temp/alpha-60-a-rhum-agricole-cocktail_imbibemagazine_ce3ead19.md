A delicately savory drink.

In this cocktail from Máté Hartai of Houston’s Refuge, sugar snap peas complement the grassy funkiness of rhum agricole. At Refuge, Hartai uses 1 oz. of Rhum JM and 1/2 oz. of Neisson Blanc, “combining both Rhum JM, a very robust and characteristic example of the category with Niesson, a funkier and more complex cousin.” Alternatively, you can use one bottle of rhum agricole for the full amount.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. rhum agricole
-   3/4 oz. sugar snap pea and white pepper syrup
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
-   Sugar Snap Pea and White Pepper Syrup
-   300g sugar snap peas
-   10g white pepper
-   600g water
-   25g parsley
-   650g granulated sugar

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a shaker with ice. Shake to chill and strain into a coupe.

**Sugar Snap Pea and White Pepper Syrup**Blanche the snap peas and parsley for 30 seconds and chill them in an ice bath (best done separately). Add all of the ingredients, except the sugar, to a blender in proportionate amounts. Run on low for 20 seconds, then switch to high for 20 seconds. Fine strain twice through a large fine-mesh strainer, leaving the solids from the first filtering in to catch smaller particles of pepper. Add the sugar, then agitate until dissolved. Will keep in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days. Store in freezer for extended periods.
