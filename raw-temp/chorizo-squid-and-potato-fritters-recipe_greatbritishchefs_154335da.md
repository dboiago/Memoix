[Fritters](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fritter-recipes) are invariably delicious, but these incredible little bites from Kuba Winkowski are a cut above. The combination of flavourful [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chorizo-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chorizo-recipes) and tender strips of [squid](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/squid-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/squid-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/squid-recipes), bound together with [potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/potato-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/potato-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/potato-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/potato-recipes), egg and flour results in an incredibly flavourful fritter that goes perfectly with the golden saffron aioli dip.

First published in 2020

### Imperial

#### Fritters

-   200g of [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), finely chopped
-   80g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   240g of [squid](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/squid-recipes), cleaned and cut into thin strips
-   450g of [Maris Piper potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)
-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), beaten
-   100g of plain flour
-   1 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying
-   1 tbsp of [chopped parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)

#### Aioli

-   2 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   1 tsp [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   3g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/saffron-recipes)
-   150g of rapeseed oil

1

For the aioli, place the saffron in a small bowl and pour over 20ml of boiling water. Allow to infuse and cool for 5 minutes

-   1 pinch of [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/saffron-recipes)

2

Place the egg yolks, garlic and cooled saffron water in a small blender (or use a hand blender) and blitz until smooth. Blend in the lemon juice and salt, then, with the blender still running, slowly drizzle in the oil until a fully emulsified aioli forms. Cover and set aside until ready to serve

-   2 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated
-   1 tsp [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   3g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   150g of rapeseed oil

3

For the fritters, peel and coarsely grate the potatoes, then squeeze the mixture in a tea towel to remove as much water as possible. Combine with the rest of the ingredients (apart from the vegetable oil and parsley) to create a smooth, dropping consistency which isn’t too loose and liquid

-   450g of [Maris Piper potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)
-   200g of [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), finely chopped
-   80g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   240g of [squid](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/squid-recipes), cleaned and cut into thin strips
-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), beaten
-   100g of plain flour
-   1 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

4

Bring a deep saucepan of oil or a deep-fat fryer to 170°C, then deep fry the fritters in batches until golden and crisp (around 3 minutes). Drain on a tea towel, very lightly season (as the chorizo is already quite salty) and sprinkle with the chopped parsley. Serve alongside the saffron aioli for dipping

-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying
-   1 tbsp of [chopped parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
