This cherry chocolate cocktail recipe makes a fabulous [Valentine's Day](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/valentines-day-romantic-recipes) [cocktail](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocktail-recipes). [Paul A. Young](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/paul-a-young) uses Kirsch in this recipe to complement the [cherries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cherry-recipes), but you can personalise the drink by using your loved one's favourite tipple.
