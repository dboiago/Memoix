**Cooks in:** _1h_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 LB. of ricotta cheese
-   1 CUP of milk
-   9 OZ. of cream
-   1/2 CUP of flour
-   4 OZ. of raw spinach, cleaned and stemmed
-   2 OZ. of dried cherry tomatoes
-   2 TBSP. of grated Grana Padano (or Parmigiano) cheese
-   2 of eggs
-   Butter
-   Salt

### Method

1

For the crespelle pancakes with ricotta and dried tomatoes recipe, prepare a batter: mix the milk and flour with a whisk, then mix in the eggs and a pinch of salt. Boil the spinach in salted water, squeeze it well and blend it with the batter. Melt a knob of butter in a pan; pour the melted butter into the batter and stir. 

2

Prepare the pancakes, pouring 1 small ladle of mixture into the greased pan; cook for about 1 minute until the edges begin to curl; then turn the pancake and continue cooking for another 30-40 seconds. Repeat the operation until the mixture is finished: you will get about 12 pancakes. 

3

Mix the ricotta with the grated cheese and 30 chopped tomatoes, obtaining the filling for the pancakes. Spread 1 tablespoon of filling in the center of each pancake; close it, first folding the edges inward, then rolling it up to form a cannelloni. Thicken the cream by boiling it in a pan for 6-7 minutes.

4

Arrange the pancakes on the plates, dress in the cream and finish with the remaining dried tomatoes.
