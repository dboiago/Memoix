In China, soup is something that is always on the table, particularly at dinner. According to Chinese tradition, your dinner should always include at least three dishes and one soup (三菜一汤, san cai yi tang). 

This bok choy soup is super easy. If you have always wanted to do something different with bok choy rather than just put it in [stir-fry](https://omnivorescookbook.com/baby-bok-choy-stir-fry/), you need to try this bok choy soup.

Chinese soups are usually made with simple ingredients and are very easy to make. My bok choy soup is no exception to this rule. Along with fresh bok choy, it uses ginger and zha cai (榨菜), a Chinese pickled mustard plant stem, for big flavor.

## What is Zha Cai

[Zha cai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zha_cai) is a type of pickled Chinese mustard stem. It has a wonderful combination of spicy and sour and salty all together. It kind of reminds me of sauerkraut with a hint of hot chili pepper and sweetness. The texture is interesting, too. It’s crunchy but also tender. The only thing I can really liken it to is a pickle. 

![Zha Cai, Chinese pickled mustard stem](https://omnivorescookbook.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/200618_Chinese-Bok-Choy-Soup_7.jpg)

I recommend finding zha cai at your Chinese grocery store or [on Amazon](https://amzn.to/38MvhzG). It is what gives this bok choy soup such a spectacular flavor and aroma. Believe it or not, this soup takes just 20 minutes to make, from start to finish. With just six ingredients, it will make the perfect complement to your Asian-inspired dinner. Plus, it’s vegan and gluten-free so it’s a soup anyone can enjoy.

Most people only know [wonton soup](https://omnivorescookbook.com/recipes/wonton-soup), [egg drop soup](https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-egg-drop-soup/), and [hot and sour soup](https://omnivorescookbook.com/recipes/authentic-hot-and-sour-soup) from their neighborhood Chinese restaurant. While there’s no denying those soups are delicious, try my bok choy soup. It’s a simple recipe that will add more color and nutrition to your meal.

Try this truly Chinese-style bok choy soup for a uniquely savory and flavorful way to enjoy more vegetables with your dinner. {Gluten-Free, Vegan}

-   8 heads baby bok choy ,  (300 g)
-   1 tablespoon [sesame oil](https://omnivorescookbook.com/sesame-oil)
-   1/4 cup [zha cai](https://amzn.to/38MvhzG) (Chinese pickled mustard plant stem) (\*Footnote 1)
-   2 ” (5 cm) ginger ,  julienned 
-   1 green onion , sliced
-   4 cups chicken soup  (or vegetable broth)
-   1/2 teaspoon chicken bouillon (or mushroom powder), or to taste

-   Trim off the tough ends of the baby bok choy. Separate the larger outer leaves, then halve the cores lengthwise. Soak the bok choy in a large bowl of cold water for 5 minutes. Gently rub the stems to remove any dirt trapped between the leaves. Rinse well and drain.
    
-   Heat the sesame oil in a medium pot over medium-low heat. Add the zha cai, ginger, and the white parts of the green onion. Cook, stirring occasionally, until fragrant and the ginger just begins to brown, 3 to 5 minutes.
    
-   Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat to a simmer and cook for 3 minutes to infuse the broth.
    
-   Add the bok choy and return the soup to a gentle simmer. Cook until the bok choy is tender but still crisp, 2 to 3 minutes.
    
-   Add the green parts of the green onion. Taste the soup and adjust the seasoning with more chicken bouillon or mushroom powder if needed. If you'd like a brighter, tangier flavor, stir in a splash of the zha cai pickling liquid. Drizzle with a little more sesame oil, if desired. Serve hot.
    

[Ingredient Substitution Guide](https://omnivorescookbook.com/ingredient-substitutions-for-chinese-cooking/)

1.  Depending on the brand of zha cai you buy, it comes in different sizes. If you bought the whole stems, cutting them into thin slices is the best way to release the flavor. If you bought the cut type, then you can add them directly into the soup.
2.  The flavor of the soup is largely determined by the type of vegetable stock and the brand of zha cai you buy. Adjust the taste at the end according to your taste.
