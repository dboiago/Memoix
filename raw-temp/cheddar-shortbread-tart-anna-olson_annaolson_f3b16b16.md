#### Makes:

1x 9-inch (23 cm) fluted tart

Introduction

### About this Recipe

[print this recipe](#print-button-row)

This savoury cheesecake makes for a great and creative appetizer. Serve it on its own or with a side salad as a meal itself.

### Cheddar Shortbread Crust:

-   **1 ¾ cups (260 g)** all-purpose flour
-   **1 Tbsp** granulated sugar
-   **¼ tsp** ground black pepper
-   **½ cup (115 g)** cold unsalted butter, cut into pieces
-   **8 oz (240 g)** grated old or extra old Cheddar cheese

**NOTES**  
The cheese tart can be enjoyed warm or at room temperature, and is lovely served with a side salad.

### Cheese Filling:

-   **1 Tbsp (15 g)** butter
-   **1** medium onion, sliced
-   **2 tsp (10 mL)** chopped fresh thyme
-   **1 Tbsp (15 mL)** balsamic vinegar
-   Salt and pepper
-   **1 (250 g) pkg** cream cheese, softened
-   **1** large egg yolk
-   **1** large egg
-   **6 oz (180 g)** grated old or extra old Cheddar cheese
-   **2 Tbsp (30 mL)** chopped Italian parsley

## Step by Step Instructions

##### Step 1

For the crust, use a food processor to pulse the flour, sugar and pepper to combine, then add the butter and pulse in until no large pieces of butter are visible. Add the grated Cheddar and blend until the dough comes together in a ball.

##### Step 2

Immediately turn the dough out onto a lightly floured work surface and roll out into a circle just under ½ inch (13 mm) thick. Line a lightly floured 9-inch (23 cm) removable-bottom fluted tart pan and press in, trimming the edges. Chill this until firm, about 2 hours.

##### Step 3

Preheat the oven to 350 ºF (180 ºC). Dock the shortbread with a fork and bake until golden brown, about 30 minutes. Cool the shell before filling. Leave the oven at 350 ºF (180 ºC).

##### Step 4

For the filling, melt the butter in a sauté pan over medium heat and add the onions and thyme. Sauté the onions until they are caramelized, about 20 minutes. Add the balsamic vinegar and stir until the liquid has evaporated. Season lightly and remove the pan from the heat.

##### Step 5

In a mixing bowl, beat the cream cheese by hand to soften. Add the egg yolk and beat in and then beat in the whole egg. Stir in the Cheddar cheese and parsley and then stir in the caramelized onions. Scrape this into the cooled crust and bake for 35-40 minutes until the outside edge of the cheesecake just begins to soufflé a touch. Allow the cheesecake to sit for 10 minutes before slicing.
