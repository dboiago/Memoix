### Glass:

Serve in a [Coupe glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

6 fresh

Mint leaves

2 fl oz

[Cacao & coffee spiced rum](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/6456/black-tears-spiced-rum)

2⁄3 fl oz

[Lime cordial (sweetened lime juice)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/7618/bickfords-lime-juice-cordial)

2 dash

[Orange Bitters by Angostura](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/3505/angostura-orange-bitters)

1 grind

Black pepper

1 2⁄3 fl oz

[Brut champagne/sparkling wine](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1123/champagne/about-champagne)

Garnish: Mint leaf

## How to make:

MUDDLE mint in base of glass. Add next 4 ingredients, STIR with ice and fine strain into chilled glass. TOP with champagne.

## Allergens:

Recipe contains the following **allergens:**

-   Brut champagne/sparkling wine - [Sulphur Dioxide/Sulphites](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/4177/cocktails/allergy-awareness-and-the-14-major-allergens)

## History:

Recipe created in 2019 by Helder Fortes Silva, Rotterdam, Netherlands. Helder's original recipe uses homemade mint-lime cordial and black pepper tincture.
