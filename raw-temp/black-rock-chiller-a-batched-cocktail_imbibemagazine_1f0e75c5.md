A bittersweet delight perfect for sharing.

In this simple batched cocktail, Branca Menta’s minty cooling power matches crisp tequila and earthy gentian liqueur. As an equal-parts drink, this recipe can be easily scaled up or down; this version makes roughly 8 servings.

## Ingredients

Yield:8 servings

-   8 oz. reposado tequila
-   8 oz. Suze
-   8 oz. Branca Menta

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a bottle or flask and shake to combine. Serve neat.
