### Glass:

Serve in a [Highball (max 10oz/300ml)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 1⁄3 fl oz

[Cacao & coffee spiced rum](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/6456/black-tears-spiced-rum)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Strucchi Red Bitter (Campari-style liqueur)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/8203/strucchi-bitter)

1⁄2 fl oz

Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)

8 drop

Chocolate bitters

2 1⁄2 fl oz

Root beer

Garnish: Long lemon zest twist

## How to make:

SHAKE first 4 ingredients with ice and strain into ice-filled glass. TOP with rootbeer.

## Allergens:

Recipe contains the following **allergens:**

-   Root beer - [Cereals containing gluten](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/4177/cocktails/allergy-awareness-and-the-14-major-allergens)

## Review:

Root beer's vanilla, anise or liquorice notes combine with coffee and cacao rum with underlying bitter and citrus.
