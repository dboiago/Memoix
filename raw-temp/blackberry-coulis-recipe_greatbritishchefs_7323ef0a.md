Try out our [quick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quick-recipes) and easy [blackberry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/blackberry-recipes) coulis recipe to use up this years glut. This versatile sweet sauce can be drizzled over fruit or [ice cream](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ice-cream-recipes), or used to decorated cakes.

First published in 2019

-   Blender

1

Place the ingredients in a small pan and crush the berries a little

2

Simmer on a low heat for 5-10 minutes or until the berries are completely broken down

3

Taste, if the berries are particularly tart, you may want to add a little more icing sugar

4

Transfer to a blender and blitz to a smooth puree

5

Pass through a sieve for an extra smooth finish

6

The coulis can be reheated or served cold, it also freezes well
