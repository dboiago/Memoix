[Peter Gordon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/peter-gordon) serves this baobab smoothie recipe at [Providores](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/restaurants/the-providores-tapa-room), the ideal pick-me-up to go with one of his delicious fusion creations. Baobab powder is being hailed as a mighty superfood, and is well-loved by health food enthusiasts. More importantly, its sweetly sour, citrussy flavour is quite tantalising, especially when blitzed with blueberry purée and maple syrup in this [dairy-free recipe](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dairy-free-recipes).

First published in 2015

-   Blender

1

Place all of the ingredients into a blender and blitz until smooth. To serve, pour into a tall glass with a straw

-   1 tsp baobab powder
-   75ml of [blueberry purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blueberry-recipes)
-   150ml of soya milk, rice milk or almond milk
-   1 tbsp of [maple syrup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/maple-syrup-recipes)
-   2 tsp [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   8 ice cubes

Often described as 'the godfather of fusion cooking', New Zealander Peter Gordon has been championing international flavours in the UK since 1995, with food that holds flavour and texture, rather than tradition and provenance, as its central tenets.
