## What is a Carnitas Burrito?

A **Carnitas Burrito** is a rolled flour tortilla stuffed with Mexican pulled pork (carnitas) and a variety of flavorful fillings like refried beans, salsa, guacamole, and cheese. Unlike tacos, burritos are completely enclosed, making them perfect for handheld meals or on-the-go lunches.

## How to Make a Carnitas Burrito

**Step 1: Warm the tortilla** on a hot griddle or comal until soft and pliable (about 30 seconds).  
**Step 2: Spread refried beans** across the center, just below the middle of the tortilla.  
**Step 3: Add hot carnitas**, followed by cheese, if using.  
**Step 4: Top with salsa and guacamole** to your liking.  
**Step 5: Fold in the sides**, then roll the tortilla tightly into a burrito.  
**Step 6 (Optional): Seal the burrito** by placing seam-side down on a hot griddle until golden, about 2 minutes.  
**Step 7: Wrap to go** by rolling in parchment and foil for easy transport.

## Carnitas Burrito Expert Tips & Tricks

-   **Warm your tortilla.** It’s the key to preventing cracks and tears.
-   **Don’t overfill.** If you’re generous with the fillings, get the _largest_ tortillas you can find!
-   **Layer smartly.** Refried beans help hold everything together. If using whole beans, melt the cheese on the tortilla first.
-   **Line it up below center.** This makes it easier to roll tightly.

## Storage & Reheating

-   **Room Temperature:** Wrap in foil and parchment for up to 1 hour if eating soon.
-   **Refrigerated:** Store leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The tortilla may soften over time.
-   **Frozen:** Assemble and freeze burritos (without fresh toppings) for up to 2 months.
-   **Reheat:**
    -   _Oven:_ Bake at 375ºF (wrapped) for 15 minutes.
    -   _Microwave:_ Wrap in a damp paper towel and heat at 50% power for 2–3 minutes. Let rest before eating!

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do you make a carnitas burrito like a restaurant?**

To make a restaurant-style carnitas burrito, start with a large flour tortilla and warm it until soft and pliable. Layer the ingredients—refried beans, juicy carnitas, cheese, salsa, and guacamole—just below the center, then roll it tightly. For that authentic touch, griddle the burrito seam-side down until golden and crispy.

**What toppings go on a carnitas burrito?**

Traditional toppings include refried beans, cheese, salsa, and guacamole. You can also add chopped onions and cilantro, crema, shredded lettuce, or fajita-style veggies. For extra flair, try smothering your burrito in green chile sauce or serving it with escabeche on the side.

**Can you freeze carnitas burritos?**

Yes! Carnitas burritos are freezer-friendly. Assemble and wrap them in parchment and foil, then place in a freezer-safe bag. For best results, skip fresh ingredients like lettuce or pico de gallo and add those after reheating. Frozen burritos will keep well for up to 2 months.

**Are carnitas burritos spicy?**

Carnitas themselves aren’t spicy—they’re savory and rich from slow-cooked pork and seasonings. The heat usually comes from whatever salsa or toppings you add, so you can make your burrito as mild or spicy as you like.

**What cut of pork is best for carnitas burritos?**

Pork shoulder (also known as pork butt) is ideal for making carnitas. It’s well-marbled, tender when braised or slow-cooked, and shreds beautifully—perfect for packing into burritos.
