**Recipe:** _Fede Cuco, [Verne Cocktail Club](http://www.vernecocktailclub.com/), Buenos Aires_

![rum cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/buenos-aires-verne-countess-of-the-caribbean-gallery-crdt-eugenio-mazzinghi.jpg)

A rich mix of aged rum, sherry and two Italian liqueurs.

Cynar and Aperol blend with aged rum and sherry in this sultry cocktail from Verne in Buenos Aires.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. aged Cuban-style rum
-   ½ oz. Aperol
-   ½ oz. Cynar
-   ½ oz. fino sherry
-   1 dash fresh lemon juice

## Preparation

Stir all of the ingredients with ice to chill, then strain into a glass over a large ice cube.
