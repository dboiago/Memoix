While, yes, [dhal](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lentil-recipes "Lentil") would generally be served with roti or rice, it works really well with slices of crisp Hovis Seeded [Sourdough](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bread-recipes "Bread"), perfect for soaking up the thick sauce. Rich with coconut milk and lightly spiced, we’re sure you’ll be wiping the plate to get every last drop.

#### Dhal

-   1 tbsp of vegetable oil, or ghee
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 tbsp of [finely grated ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   1 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tsp [ground cumin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 tsp [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   200g of [red lentils](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lentil-recipes), rinsed
-   400ml of vegetable stock
-   200ml of [coconut milk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes)

1

Heat the oil or ghee in a saucepan. Add the onion and cook until soft and golden, about 10 minutes

-   1 tbsp of vegetable oil, or ghee
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped

3

Add the lentils, stock and coconut milk. Simmer for 20-25 minutes until the lentils are soft and creamy. Season to taste

-   200g of [red lentils](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lentil-recipes), rinsed
-   400ml of vegetable stock
-   200ml of [coconut milk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes)

4

Meanwhile, make the toppings. Finely chop the coriander stems and mix with lime juice, vinegar and a pinch of salt. Leave to pickle while the dhal simmers

-   1 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), leaves picked and stems reserved
-   1 tbsp of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), plus extra wedges and a little more juice to finish
-   1 tbsp of rice vinegar

5

Heat up a few centimetres of oil in a small saucepan, and fry the sliced onion until deep golden brown and crisp. Drain on paper towels

-   1 [small onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), thinly sliced
-   vegetable oil, for frying

6

Toast the seeded sourdough slices until crisp

-   4 slices of seeded sourdough

7

Spoon the warm dhal over each slice. Top with crispy onions, pickled coriander stems and fresh coriander leaves. Add a final squeeze of lime and serve immediately
