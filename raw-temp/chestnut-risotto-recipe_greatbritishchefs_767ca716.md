Commonly associated with the festive season, chestnuts are actually widely available from September onwards and this sumptuous [risotto](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/risotto-recipes) recipe by [Theo Randall](https://content.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/theo-randall) is a fantastic way to make the most of these distinctive little nuts. Remember, the trick to a good risotto is lovingly adding the stock, one ladle at a time – this recipe, with a dash of brandy lending a rich, heartiness to an Italian classic, is well worth a little patience.

#### Chestnut risotto

-   1/2 red onion, chopped
-   1/2 [celery stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), chopped
-   75g of [pancetta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pancetta-recipes), cubed
-   1 sprig of [fresh rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of olive oil
-   200g of [Arborio risotto rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes)
-   800ml of chicken stock
-   100g of [chestnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chestnut-recipes), cooked, peeled and finely chopped
-   50ml of [brandy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brandy-recipes)
-   75g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground

2

Bring the stock to a simmer in a separate pan. Add to the rice pan, ladle by ladle waiting for each to be absorberd before adding the next. Stir continuously for 15 minutes or until the rice is al dente and most of the liquid has been absorbed

-   800ml of chicken stock

3

When all the stock has been added, add the chestnuts with the brandy and stir through

-   100g of [chestnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chestnut-recipes)
-   50ml of [brandy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brandy-recipes)
