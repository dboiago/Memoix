A simply brilliant crispy chicken [canapé](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/canape-recipes) recipe from [Marcus Wareing](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/marcus-wareing) combines buttermilk-soaked, crispy-fried pieces of [chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-recipes) with a delectable mustard mayonnaise. The coating mix makes a fairly large batch, so simply buy a few more [chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes) if you want to scale up the recipe for larger events.

First published in 2015

-   Deep-fryer

1

To make the coating for the chicken, toast the onion powder, garlic powder, cayenne pepper, celery seeds and pepper in a dry pan and freshly grind together

-   1 tsp [onion powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of garlic powder
-   1 tsp [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)
-   1 tsp [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   2 tsp celery seeds

2

Add the salt and flour to the mix and stir thoroughly. You will have more than you need for 4 people, but this mix will keep well in an airtight container ready for next time

-   400g of plain flour
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Cut the chicken into 4 equal pieces, removing any large pieces of sinew or fat

-   1 [chicken thigh](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), de-boned

4

Toss the chicken in the seasoned flour, then carefully coat in the buttermilk

-   100ml of buttermilk

5

Place the chicken back into the flour mixture for a second coating. The chicken must be fully coated, but it should be uneven as this will produce a crispier end result

7

In a deep-fat fryer, cook the chicken at 170°C until golden brown and cooked through. Serve with the mayonnaise
