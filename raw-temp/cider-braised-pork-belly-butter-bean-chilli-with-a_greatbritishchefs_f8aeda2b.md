In this recipe, [pork belly](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-belly-recipes "Pork belly") is gently braised in cider until tender. Butter beans soak up the sauce, and a dash of chilli brings a gentle heat. A spiced [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes "Apple") relish finishes the dish, and makes for a fun take on a classic chilli.

First published in 2025

1

Soak the beans in plenty of salted water overnight, or for at least 8 hours

-   250g of [dried butter beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-bean-recipes)

2

Drain and rinse the beans. Place in a pot, cover with fresh salted water, and bring to a boil. Simmer for 1 hour 15 minutes or until tender, then drain

3

Next, prep the pork. Preheat the oven to 160°C. Pat the pork belly dry and score the skin. Slice into 2 inch chunks

-   500g of [pork belly](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-belly-recipes)

4

Heat the oil in a pan and sear pork belly, skin-side down, until crisp. Remove and set aside

-   1 tbsp of olive oil

6

Pour in the cider, stock and honey, then return the pork belly and beans to the pot. Cover and cook in the oven for 2 hours 30 minutes, then season to taste

-   500ml of [dry cider](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cider-recipes)
-   500ml of chicken stock
-   1 tbsp of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

7

Meanwhile, make the relish. Mix the apple, vinegar, sugar, cinnamon and cloves. Let sit for 20 minutes

-   1 [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), finely diced
-   1 tbsp of cider vinegar
-   1 tsp brown sugar
-   1/2 tsp [cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [ground cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)

8

Spoon into bowls, and serve topped with the spiced apple relish and chopped parsley 

-   1 handful of [fresh parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
