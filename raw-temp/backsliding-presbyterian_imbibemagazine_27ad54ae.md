A modern whiskey highball with Campari, ginger syrup and lime.

Tall and bursting with bittersweet orange and ginger flavors, this whiskey highball proves that aged spirits aren’t just for winter sipping.

## Preparation

Shake all ingredients (except club soda) with ice and strain into an ice-filled glass. Top with club soda and garnish.
