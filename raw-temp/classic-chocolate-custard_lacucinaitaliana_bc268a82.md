### List of Ingredients

-   1 QT. of milk
-   7 OZ. of 6570% dark chocolate
-   3/4 C. of sugar
-   1 STICK of butter
-   2/3 C. of flour
-   1/4 C. of unsweetened cocoa powder
-   Rum

### Method

1

Heat the milk, bringing it almost to a boil. In another saucepan, melt the butter with the sugar, then incorporate the flour and the cocoa powder sifted together. Gradually pour the milk in and combine; this will prevent lumps from forming. Bring back over the heat and cook for 4-5 minutes after it comes to a boil.

2

Turn off the heat, add the finely chopped chocolate, and stir until it has melted. Pour the mixture into a mold brushed with rum. Tap it on a work surface to prevent air bubbles from forming; cover with aluminum foil and let cool, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours. Briefly immerse the mold in hot water, then turn the custard over onto a serving dish.
