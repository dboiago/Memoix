## Why I love this easy pumpkin soup recipe

Growing up, when the weather cooled down, my mom would make a delicious **pumpkin soup with cinnamon**, cloves and [coconut milk](https://amzn.to/3dhDG0M) that I absolutely loved. We would always eat it with loads of olive oil drizzled on top, and scoop it all up with buttered pieces of crusty bread. Sooo good times infinity!

When I moved to the US, I became completely obsessed with all the other **fall spices** that take over the season. I upgraded my mom’s soup with a few more **spices**, added **ginger**, **sweet potatoes** and of course, some **cayenne** pepper for a spicy kick. For garnishes I added 2 other brand new faves of mine, **[dried cranberries](https://amzn.to/3of0fIu)**, and **pumpkin seeds.**

This **spiced pumpkin soup** **with ginger and sweet potatoes** is my version of my mom’s soup, and it has been on my Fall menu for the for the many years I’ve lived in California. It’s a total hit!

## How to make Pumpkin Soup

You can make this soup on **stove top or in an [instant pot](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00FLYWNYQ/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=B00FLYWNYQ&linkCode=as2&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=549aacbc3c270f3864ed3b30c081d662)** pretty much the same way, the main difference is that the soup will be done way faster when cooked in the [instant pot](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00FLYWNYQ/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=B00FLYWNYQ&linkCode=as2&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=549aacbc3c270f3864ed3b30c081d662). Here are the easy steps:

**Saute the aromatics** – saute the onions, then the garlic, then add in the ginger.

**Add the ground spices** – after you saute the aromatics, add in the ground spices, then add in the sweet potatoes.

-   ![](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/pumpkin-soup-7-668x1024.jpg)
    
-   ![](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/pumpkin-soup-8-684x1024.jpg)
    

**Add the liquid** – add the pumpkin, stir to combine and add the cooking liquid of your choice.

### Now the big secret!

**The BIG SECRET** **of how to make creamy pumpkin soup** is not in adding cream to it, it’s **the veggies to liquid ratio**. The veggies to liquid ratio is key, so that when you blend, you get a perfectly creamy and silky soup. Adding cream or [coconut milk](https://amzn.to/3dhDG0M) to it, is just cherry on top!

**Top with the whole spices** – after you add the liquid add the whole spices to the liquid, keeping them at the very top, so they’re easy to remove before we blend. Alternatively, you can just make a little pouch with a cheese cloth to make it easier to scoop out.

-   ![](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/pumpkin-soup-9.jpg)
    
-   ![](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/pumpkin-soup-10-675x1024.jpg)
    

**Cook the soup** – after the spices are all in, either close the [instant pot](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00FLYWNYQ/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=B00FLYWNYQ&linkCode=as2&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=549aacbc3c270f3864ed3b30c081d662) and set it to 25 mins on manual, or bring it to a boil in a regular [stock pot](https://www.amazon.com/Cuisinart-766-24-Classic-8-Quart-Stockpot/dp/B00008CM6J/ref=as_li_ss_tl?keywords=8+qt+stock+pot&qid=1571206911&sr=8-1&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=64a345e0faca667cff78c9ead41342e1&language=en_US) on stove top, cover and let it simmer covered until the pumpkins and sweet potatoes are soft.

**Blend until smooth** – When the potatoes are soft, remove the whole spices and discard, then blend everything with an [immersion blender](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B005GFXK1K/ref=as_li_ss_tl?aaxitk=Not0K5KRqHqJhbMCRRbSvg&pd_rd_i=B005GFXK1K&pf_rd_p=44fc3e0f-4b9e-4ed8-b33b-363a7257163d&hsa_cr_id=9870149430701&sb-ci-n=asinImage&sb-ci-v=https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/513sOnSfRvL.jpg&sb-ci-a=B005GFXK1K&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=08cda2b6ff4e991de9f15cac71307438&language=en_US), until it’s smooth.

![](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/pumpkin-soup-11.jpg)

**Chef alert!** The worst burn scars I have collected on my arms come from making blended soup, like this one. Yikes! Be careful, _meus amigos!_

This **easy and creamy pumpkin soup recipe** is full of delicious **flavors from seasonal spices**, with **a hot kick of cayenne** pepper. All those **bold flavors** come together beautifully, and are softened up by **a splash of [coconut milk](https://amzn.to/3dhDG0M)** and olive oil at the end. Another thing to love about this **silky soup** is the **extra layer of textures and flavors** provided by the garnishes. Seriously, do not skip them! The best part? This classic soup recipe can be made in the [instant pot](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00FLYWNYQ/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=B00FLYWNYQ&linkCode=as2&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=549aacbc3c270f3864ed3b30c081d662) in a little over **30 minutes**, or on stove top, pretty much just as easily.

Prep Time 20 minutes

Cook Time 25 minutes

Total Time 45 minutes

-   8 cups of pumpkin peeled and cut into cubes
-   1 medium sweet potato, peeled and cut into cubes about 3 cups
-   2 Tbsp of olive oil plus more for drizzling and garnish
-   1 whole onion diced
-   3 cloves of garlic, or a tbsp of [Brazilian Sofrito](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/brazilian-sofrito/)
-   1 inch piece of fresh ginger peeled and smashed
-   2 whole star anise
-   2 [cinnamon sticks](https://amzn.to/3of6goP) or about a tsp ground
-   3 whole cloves or about 1/4 tsp ground
-   a generous pinch of nutmeg about 1/8 of a tsp
-   1/4 tsp cayenne pepper or more if you like it hot
-   5 cups of chicken stock, water, or vegetable stock
-   Salt and pepper to taste

-   With your [instant pot](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00FLYWNYQ/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=B00FLYWNYQ&linkCode=as2&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=549aacbc3c270f3864ed3b30c081d662) on sauté function, heat 2 tbsp of olive oil.
    
-   Add the onion and cook it until the onion is transparent, approx 3 mins.
    
-   Add the garlic and saute until golden, about 2 minutes.
    
-   Add the ginger, the cayenne, and the nutmeg and stir.
    
-   Turn off the [instant pot](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00FLYWNYQ/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=B00FLYWNYQ&linkCode=as2&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=549aacbc3c270f3864ed3b30c081d662), and add the sweet potatoes and stir.
    
-   Then, add the pumpkins, and stir.
    
-   Add the liquid and then the cinnamon, the cloves and the star anis (try to keep the whole spices on top so they are easier to scoop out before blending – alternatively, you can just make a little pouch with a cheese cloth with the spices inside, then just remove the little pouch all at once).
    
-   Close the [instant pot](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00FLYWNYQ/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=B00FLYWNYQ&linkCode=as2&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=549aacbc3c270f3864ed3b30c081d662), and set to 25 minutes on manual.
    
-   After 25 minutes, release the pressure manually, and carefully open the pot when you are able.
    
-   Remove the spices and discard them.
    
-   Using an [immersion blender](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B005GFXK1K/ref=as_li_ss_tl?aaxitk=Not0K5KRqHqJhbMCRRbSvg&pd_rd_i=B005GFXK1K&pf_rd_p=44fc3e0f-4b9e-4ed8-b33b-363a7257163d&hsa_cr_id=9870149430701&sb-ci-n=asinImage&sb-ci-v=https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/513sOnSfRvL.jpg&sb-ci-a=B005GFXK1K&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=08cda2b6ff4e991de9f15cac71307438&language=en_US), blend the soup until smooth.
    
-   Season with salt and pepper.
    
-   Serve and garnish with [coconut milk](https://amzn.to/3dhDG0M), olive oil, pumpkin seeds and [dried cranberries](https://amzn.to/3of0fIu) of top.
