This [vegetarian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes) butternut squash [curry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/curry-recipes) recipe is effortlessly simple to make but boasts all the flavour of something that's been slowly cooked for hours. The earthy [mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/mushroom-recipes) and fresh spinach play perfect supporting acts, and the subtly spiced tomato sauce is a guaranteed crowdpleaser.

#### Butternut squash curry

-   1kg [butternut squash](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butternut-squash-recipes), peeled, deseeded and cut into large chunks
-   4 [green cardamom pods](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes), (or 1⁄8 tsp ground cardamom or ground cinnamon)
-   2 [red peppers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), quartered and deseeded
-   3 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), quartered
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), more or less to taste
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), peeled
-   1 knob of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), thumb-sized, peeled and sliced
-   3 tbsp of [tomato paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of water
-   1 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   6 [portobello mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes), quartered
-   400g of [spinach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spinach-recipes), washed and stalks removed
-   250ml of [plain yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)

-   Blender

1

Boil or steam the butternut squash until it’s only half cooked – it should have some resistance when pushing a knife through to test it

-   1kg [butternut squash](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butternut-squash-recipes), peeled, deseeded and cut into large chunks

2

Bash the cardamom pods and pull the seeds out from the green husks. It might seem fiddly but the flavour is worth it

-   4 [green cardamom pods](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes), (or 1⁄8 tsp ground cardamom or ground cinnamon)

3

Purée the peppers, tomatoes, chilli, garlic, ginger and tomato paste in a blender with the salt and water

-   2 [red peppers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), quartered and deseeded
-   3 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), quartered
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), more or less to taste
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), peeled
-   1 knob of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), thumb-sized, peeled and sliced
-   3 tbsp of [tomato paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of water

4

Pour the purée into a wide pan with the cardamom seeds (or ground cardamom or cinnamon) and oil and bring to the boil, stirring frequently. It will ‘plop’ a bit so keep stirring it slowly. Cook for 1 minute

-   1 tbsp of vegetable oil

5

Once it’s warmed up, stir in the butternut. Put a lid on the pan and simmer until the butternut is just about cooked

7

Lastly, stir in the spinach until it wilts, then mix in the yoghurt and take off the heat

-   400g of [spinach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spinach-recipes), washed and stalks removed
-   250ml of [plain yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
