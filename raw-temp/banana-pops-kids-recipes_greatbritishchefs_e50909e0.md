A playful recipe that [kids](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kids-recipes) can enjoy making at home, [Lisa Allen](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/lisa-allen) has created a simple and fun dessert that allows for plenty of creative decoration. You could try with different flavoured yoghurts, using milk or white [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chocolate-recipes), and whatever edible decorations you choose.

First published in 2015

-   Skewers

1

Peel the bananas and cut each one in half to get 4 chunks. Pierce a skewer into the bottom of each piece

-   2 [bananas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes), ripe

2

Add the chocolate to a glass bowl. Heat in the microwave until melted, approximately 1-2 minutes, stirring after a minute to make sure it all melts

-   50g of [dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)

3

Place the vanilla yoghurt into a bowl, then dip each banana into the yoghurt to cover one side

-   100g of [low fat vanilla yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)

4

Using a spoon, drizzle the melted chocolate over the other halves. Sprinkle hundreds and thousands over the top and serve

-   20g of hundreds and thousands
