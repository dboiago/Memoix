**RECIPE:** _Mattias Hagglund for [Heritage](http://www.heritagerva.com/), Richmond, Virginia_

August 13, 2013

![bitter about my hot friend recipe](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/recipe_c_Bitter-about-my-hot-friend.jpg)

Campari, tequila and lemon juice collide in summery harmony in this cocktail from Virginia.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. reposado tequila
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   3/4 oz. agave
-   3/4 oz. Campari
-   habanero tincture to taste

## Preparation

Add all ingredients to a mixing tin with ice. Shake and double strain over fresh ice into a chilled  glass. Garnish.

**Habanero Tincture**With latex gloves on, de-stem and chop 4 habaneros. Add the chopped chiles to 1 bottle of vodka and allow to macerate for 2 weeks. Strain in a bottle.
