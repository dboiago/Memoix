Try Simon Hulstone’s light and elegant starter that showcases contrasting textures of [Jerusalem artichoke](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/jerusalem-artichoke "Jerusalem artichoke") and [crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/crab-recipes "Crab"). Earthy artichokes are crushed, puréed and deep-fried; brown crab meat is blended into a smoky [pâté](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pate-rillette-recipes "Pâté and rillette") and sweet white crab meat is simply dressed in a hazelnut mayonnaise. Brown butter brings richness and ponzu, chives and lemon sorrel provide bright notes.

#### CRAB TUILES

-   50g of [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50g of [brown butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   50g of plain flour
-   200g of crab or shellfish stock, reduced to 50g and the consistency of yeast extract
-   100g of [maple syrup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/maple-syrup-recipes)
-   25g of [hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), finely chopped

-   Non-stick baking mat

1

To make the Jerusalem artichoke crush, place the artichokes in a pan of water, bring to the boil then cook until soft

-   8 [Jerusalem artichokes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/jerusalem-artichoke)

2

Once cool enough to handle, peel off and discard the skins, then roughly crush the flesh. Lightly combine with the butter and ponzu then set aside to cool completely. Add the chives and season to taste. Keep in the fridge until needed

-   25g of [brown butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   15ml of ponzu
-   10g of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), finely chopped

3

For the crab tuile, preheat the oven to 150°C. Combine all of the ingredients except the hazelnuts in a blender and blend until smooth

-   50g of [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50g of [brown butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   50g of plain flour
-   200g of crab or shellfish stock, reduced to 50g and the consistency of yeast extract
-   100g of [maple syrup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/maple-syrup-recipes)

4

Spread very thinly over a nonstick baking mat or baking sheet lined with baking paper then evenly sprinkle over the hazelnuts. Bake in the oven for 3 minutes until golden brown, then leave to cool. Break into shards and set aside

-   25g of [hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), finely chopped

5

Check the white crab meat for any shell then put in a bowl. Combine the mayonnaise and hazelnut oil to create a hazelnut mayo (you want a subtle hazelnut flavour), then stir this through the crab. Season to taste and set aside in the fridge until needed

-   150g of [white crab meat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes), picked
-   15g of [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes)
-   1 dash of hazelnut oil

6

For the brown crab pâté, check the brown crab meat for any shell then place in a blender and blend with the panko and paprika until smooth. Transfer to a piping bag and keep in the fridge until needed

-   100g of [brown crab meat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes)
-   20g of panko breadcrumbs
-   1 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)

7

For the Jerusalem artichoke purée, preheat an oven to 180°C. Place the whole artichokes on a baking sheet and bake until soft. Once cool enough to handle, halve the artichokes and scrape out the flesh, trying to keep the skin intact and removing as much flesh as possible. Set aside the skins

-   10 [Jerusalem artichokes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/jerusalem-artichoke)

8

Blend the artichoke flesh, butter and cream to a smooth purée then season to taste. Transfer to a piping bag

-   100g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100g of double cream

9

To serve, bring the crab out of the fridge to come up to room temperature. Heat a pan a third full of vegetable oil or a deep-fat fryer to 190°C. Deep-fry the skins until crispy, then season

-   vegetable oil, for frying

10

To plate, quenelle two mounds of the white crab per portion alongside a quenelle of the artichoke crush. Pipe the artichoke purée and crab pâté around in dots. Place around the hazelnuts and lemon sorrel

-   25g of [roasted hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), halved
-   [lemon sorrel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorrel-recipes)

11

Just before serving, place the crab tuiles and fried artichoke skins onto the plate and serve
