A trifecta of Southern flavors–bourbon, peach and mint.

## Ingredients

-   2 1/2 oz. bourbon
-   1 oz. peach syrup (such as Torani or Monin)
-   12-15 fresh mint leaves
-   Crushed ice

## Preparation

Place the mint and 1/4 ounce of the peach syrup in a julep cup or Old Fashioned glass and gently muddle, working the leaves up the side of the glass. Loosely pack the glass with crushed ice, then add bourbon and drizzle remaining peach syrup on top. Garnish.
