## Cantaloupe Juice

Author: [Anita Jacobson](https://dailycookingquest.com/about.html)

Categories: [Drink](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/drink.html) [Vegetarian](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/vegetarian.html)

Cuisines: [American](https://dailycookingquest.com/cuisines/american.html)

Ingredients: [Fruit](https://dailycookingquest.com/ingredients/fruit.html)

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time: 0 min

Total Time: 10 mins

Serves: 2

[Print Recipe](https://dailycookingquest.com/card/cantaloupe-juice.html)

### Ingredients

-   half a cantaloupe (about 400 gram)
-   100 ml cold drinking water
-   3 tablespoon honey

### Instructions

1.  Put everything in a blender and process until smooth.
2.  Transfer to pretty serving glasses (or jars) and serve immediately
