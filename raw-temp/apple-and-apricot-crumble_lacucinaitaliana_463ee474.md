## Fruit crumble is a quick and easy dessert for any time of the year. In this foolproof recipe, sweet crumble tops bubbly apples and apricots. Serve warm with your favorite gelato.

**Cooks in:** _25mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   7 CUPS of rennet apples
-   2/3 CUP of cane sugar
-   9 TBSP. of unsalted butter, softened
-   1 CUP of all-purpose flour
-   2 OZ. of dried, pitted apricots
-   ground cinnamon

### Method

1

Preheat the oven to 410°F. 

2

Peel and core the apples and cut them into cubes. Arrange in a single layer in a round baking dish suitable for serving. Chop the apricots into small pieces and distribute over the apples. Sprinkle the fruit with 2 Tbsp. sugar.

3

Mix the softened butter with the remaining sugar (1/2 cup), the flour and a dash of cinnamon to form a crumbly dough. Cover the apples and apricots with the crumble. Bake in the preheated oven for around 20 minutes. Serve warm.
