**Cooks in:** _1h 15mins \+ 45mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _10_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 C. of sugar
-   1/2 C. of milk
-   1/2 C. of cream
-   1/4 LB. of 50% dark chocolate
-   1 STICK of butter
-   1 C. of bread crumbs
-   6 PCS. of egg yolks
-   2 PCS. of egg whites
-   Vanilla bean pod
-   Powdered sugar

### Method

1

For the creme anglaise, heat the cream and milk together with half a vanilla bean pod opened lengthwise; beat 4 egg yolks with 1/3 cup of sugar; strain the milk directly into the egg yolks and bring everything back over the heat until it reaches 185°F; turn off the heat and let it cool.

2

Beat 2 egg yolks together with 1/3 cup of sugar, then add in the bread crumbs; melt the chocolate and the butter together over a bain-marie, then add it into the bread crumb mixture and combine. Beat the egg whites to stiff peaks with 1/3 cup of sugar. Gently fold the chocolate mixture into the egg whites.|Pour this mixture into a shallow baking pan (9-inch diameter) lined with parchment paper and bake at 350°F for 30-35 minutes. Serve the cake dusted with powdered sugar and topped with the creme anglaise.
