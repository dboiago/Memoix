During a visit to Panama in 2017, Bratislav Glisic, head bartender and co-founder of Dear Strangers in New York, had his first taste of maracujá, aka passion fruit. Enamored by its appearance and flavor, he decided to create a tropical cocktail to showcase the fruit. Using the classic [Pisco Sour](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/recipe-pisco-sour/) as a template, Glisic balances the sweet tartness of passion fruit with gin, elderflower, and lemon. The earthiness from a sprinkle of matcha powder rounds out the flavors.

## Ingredients

-   1 3/4 oz. gin
-   3/4 oz. passion fruit syrup
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1/2 oz. elderflower liqueur

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a shaker with ice. Shake to chill, then strain into the glass. Garnish.  
**Passion Fruit Syrup**Combine 1 cup of passion fruit purée (such as [Perfect Puree](https://perfectpuree.com/product/passion-fruit-concentrate/)) and 1 cup of superfine sugar in a pot and boil. Allow to cool, then strain. Will keep in the refrigerator for 2 weeks.
