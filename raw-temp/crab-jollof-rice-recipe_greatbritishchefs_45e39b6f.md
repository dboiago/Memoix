Jollof [rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/rice-recipes) is a staple of West African cuisine, but Jeremy Chan adds his signature twist to the recipe by turning this spicy rice dish into a fiery umami bomb. He cooks the rice in a complex, carefully balanced mixture of vegetables, spices and seasonings, before finishing the dish with a luxurious [crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/crab-recipes) custard, a fresh crab salad and a _wok hei_ paste of garlic, ginger and chilli, borrowed from his Cantonese heritage. The final dish hits every corner of your palate – an incredible display of precision cooking.

First published in 2019

-   33g of [scotch bonnet chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded

#### Crab salad

-   113g of [white crab meat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes), picked and chilled in the fridge
-   1/4 bunch of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   13g of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes)
-   1/4 [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes)
-   2g of [paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   2g of [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   2g of [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste
-   [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), to taste
-   8g of olive oil

#### Crab custard

-   225g of [brown crab meat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes)
-   50g of [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   15g of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   10g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1/2 [scotch bonnet chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded
-   75g of whipping cream, you might need a dash more
-   75g of milk
-   [smoked salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

-   High-power blender
-   Muslin cloth
-   Cooking thermometer
-   Steamer
-   Heavy-duty gloves

1

To begin, make the jollof sauce – this will later be used to cook the rice. Add a generous amount of grapeseed oil to a very hot pan and, when smoking, add the tomatoes. Leave the tomatoes to sizzle in the oil for several minutes, allowing them to slightly burn and smoke on the bottom. Turn over after 5 minutes and continue to cook on a high heat until reduced and broken down

-   grapeseed oil
-   975g of [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), chopped

2

Toss the roughly chopped peppers, onions, garlic and ginger in some oil and grill until very charred. Be sure to char the garlic and ginger for less time as they can become bitter. Place all the charred vegetables into the reduced tomatoes, along with the spices and other condiments (save the dashi for later). Cover with a lid and cook on a low heat until soft and reduced

-   225g of red onion, cut into large chunks
-   375g of [red pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), cut into large chunks
-   38g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), cut into large chunks
-   38g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), cut into large chunks
-   15g of [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   15g of [black garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   23g of Madras curry powder
-   22g of [hot paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   23g of crayfish powder
-   33g of [scotch bonnet chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded
-   13g of smoke powder, Jeremy uses Sosa smoke powder
-   8g of [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)
-   11g of [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   8g of [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes), toasted then ground
-   8g of Tabasco
-   15g of [tamari](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   13g of [dried chipotle chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   49g of sugar
-   15g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   8g of Worcestershire sauce

3

Blitz very well, loosening with the dashi to adjust the consistency to that of a smooth sauce, and pass through a sieve. Transfer the pulp remaining in the sieve to a bag made from double layers of muslin and use this to sqeeze out the last drops of sauce

-   750g of [dashi](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dashi-recipes), to loosen the mix (you might not need all of it)

4

Now make the wok hei paste. Blitz the garlic, ginger and spring onion to a smooth paste. Place in a deep pot

-   2/3 bunch of [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   330g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   165g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)

5

Heat the oil to over 280°C. Wearing heavy-duty gloves and staying well away from any open flames, immediately and carefully pour the oil over the paste and stir quickly. Set aside

-   165g of vegetable oil

8

For the crab custard, blitz all the ingredients in a high-power blender for 5 minutes, then pass through a fine sieve into a pan. Heat the mixture gently on the hob, until thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon

-   225g of [brown crab meat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes)
-   75g of whipping cream, you might need a dash more
-   15g of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   50g of [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   10g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   [smoked salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   75g of milk
-   1/2 [scotch bonnet chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded

9

Wash the rice well, then drain. Place in a large pan with the jollof sauce and simmer until cooked through

-   500g of [jasmine rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes)

10

To serve, roast the rice in a hot pan with a spoonful of the wok hei paste. Divide between bowls, drizzle with the crab custard and top with the crab salad. Serve immediately garnished with fresh sorrel

-   [sorrel leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorrel-recipes)
