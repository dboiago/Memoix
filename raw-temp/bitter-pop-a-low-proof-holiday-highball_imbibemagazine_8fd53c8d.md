A rich amaro meets vanilla and ginger ale in this simple holiday highball.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. Amaro Sfumato Rabarbaro
-   3/4 oz. vanilla syrup
-   4 oz. ginger ale (such as Boylan)

## Preparation

Add the first 2 ingredients to a glass and top with ginger ale (to create an aromatic foam, pour the ginger ale from a few inches above the glass). Add ice, and garnish. (Optional: Using a kitchen torch, carefully sear the orange slice before using as garnish.)
