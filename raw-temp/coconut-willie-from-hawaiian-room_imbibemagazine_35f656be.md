_Adapted from “Rum Is Romantic,” by James A. Beard,_ House & Garden_, July 1956 p. 4_

![Coconut Willie](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/coconut-willie-crdt-eugene-lee.jpg)

Before the [Piña Colada](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/recipe-pina-colada/) populated beachside resorts and tropical bars everywhere, there was the Coconut Willie. In his [Mixopedia piece](https://imbibemagazine.com/mixopedia-before-pina-colada-there-was-coconut-willie/) for the [May/June 2022 issue](https://imbibemagazine.com/past-issues/no-97-may-june-2022/), writer Wayne Curtis describes this popular coconut drink as “a Piña Colada with benefits, with lemon added for acidity and orange curaçao for complexity.” It gained notoriety in the 1940s from [Hotel Lexington](https://www.lexingtonhotelnyc.com/rooms/specialty-suites/the-hawaiian-room)‘s Hawaiian Room in New York City. Rum lover James Beard was able to snag the recipe and publish it on _House & Garden_ in 1956.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. coconut milk
-   1 oz. pineapple juice
-   3/4 oz. Jamaican rum
-   1/2 oz. light rum
-   1/2 oz. lemon juice
-   1/2 oz. orange curaçao
-   1/2 tsp fine sugar

## Preparation

Cut large coconut in half and use as the container for the drink. Add ingredients to shaker with crushed ice and shake. Pour unstrained contents into the coconut.
