This chermoula recipe shows just how easy it is to prepare one of north Africa's most popular condiments. Used as a [dip](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dip-recipes) or marinade for meats, fish and vegetables, it has a fresh grassy flavour with a zingy, salty finish thanks to the addition of preserved [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lemon-recipes).

First published in 2018

### Metric

### Imperial

-   50g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), roughly chopped
-   20g of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), leaves picked and roughly chopped
-   10g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), (about 2 medium cloves)
-   35g of [preserved lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), halved and pips removed (about 1 medium lemon)
-   80ml of olive oil
-   1/2 tsp [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [cumin powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/allspice-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)
-   1 [lemon wedge](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced, to taste

-   Food processor or blender

1

Place all the ingredients apart from the lemon wedge into a blender and blitz to a coarse paste

2

Taste and season with fresh lemon juice if needed
