With its black ink, cuttlefish adds drama and intrigue to any dish. [Cristina Bowerman](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/chefs/cristina-bowerman)’s simple recipe for [spaghetti](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/spaghetti-recipes) balances the sweetness of the cuttlefish with the tang of citrus and creates an elegant [pasta](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/pasta-recipes) dish that captures the clean and fresh flavours of Italian cuisine.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

1

In a large saucepan of boiling water, cook the spaghetti for 4 minutes

-   320g of [spaghetti](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/spaghetti-recipes)

2

Drizzle the trombolotto oil in a medium saucepan, add the fish stock and bring to the boil. Add the pasta and cook for another 3 minutes

-   4 tbsp of trombolotto oil
-   300g of fish stock

3

Briefly sauté the cuttlefish in a frying pan, then add it to the spaghetti. Stir the ingredients well, then add some orange zest

-   200g of [cuttlefish](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/cuttlefish-recipes), cleaned and sliced
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zest only

4

To plate, use the cuttlefish ink to paint the plate, then place some spaghetti in the middle. Serve hot
