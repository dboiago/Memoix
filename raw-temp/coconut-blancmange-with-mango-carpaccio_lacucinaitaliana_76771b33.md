**Cooks in:** _2h 50mins \+ 2h_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 of mango
-   2/3 C. of milk
-   1/2 C. of sugar
-   1/3 C. of cream, whipped
-   1/4 C. of coconut flour
-   1 1/2 SHEETS of gelatin
-   2 of egg whites

### Method

1

Soak the gelatin in cold water. Bring the milk to a boil with the coconut flour, then turn off the heat and let it sit for ten minutes. In another saucepan over very low heat, melt half the sugar with a tablespoon of water to obtain a syrup: bring it to a boil then turn off the heat.

2

Slightly beat the egg whites with an electric hand mixer, then drizzle in the boiling syrup and continue beating until you achieve a meringue consistency. Blend the milk and coconut flour to obtain a cream, pass it through a fine sieve, and place it back over the heat and melt in the drained gelatin. Turn off the heat after 1 minute and let it cool.

3

Transfer the beaten egg whites into a bowl and gently incorporate the coconut cream, the fresh whipped cream, and the rest of the sugar until you obtain a light and airy mousse, otherwise known as a blancmange. Let it set in the refrigerator for a few hours at least. Trim and peel the mango, slice it finely, and serve it with the blancmange.
