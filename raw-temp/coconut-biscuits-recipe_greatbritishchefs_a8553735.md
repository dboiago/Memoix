This coconut biscuit recipe from [Andy Waters](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/andy-waters) is a fun and simple [biscuit](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/biscuit-recipes) to prepare from pantry essentials you may already have in your cupboard. Children can help by rolling and flattening the dough on the baking tray. Make these cookies as a sweet snack or a lunchbox treat for [kids](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kids-recipes) to enjoy.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   150g of jumbo rolled oats
-   100g of plain flour
-   100g of light brown sugar
-   100g of [desiccated coconut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-recipes)
-   100g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of golden syrup
-   1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
-   2 tbsp of water, boiling

1

Preheat the oven to 175°C/gas mark 4

2

Line a baking tray with baking parchment

3

In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour, sugar, coconut and oats, blending well

-   100g of plain flour
-   100g of light brown sugar
-   100g of [desiccated coconut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-recipes)
-   150g of jumbo rolled oats

4

In a small pan, melt the butter and golden syrup over a low heat

-   100g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of golden syrup

5

In a small bowl, dissolve the bicarbonate soda in the 2 tbsp of boiling water. Combine with the golden syrup and butter

-   1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
-   2 tbsp of water, boiling

6

Make a well in the centre of the dry ingredients, pour in the warm golden syrup mixture, mixing well to form a dough

7

Separate the mixture into balls. Place the balls onto a baking tray and press down slightly to flatten into cookies

8

Place into the preheated oven for 15-20 minutes until the biscuits are golden

9

Once baked, remove and place straight onto a wire rack to cool. Serve
