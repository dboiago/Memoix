Batata vada ([potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes "Potato") fritters) are a popular street food from Maharashtra, a region in western [India](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-vegetarian-recipes "Indian vegetarian"). Mashed potato is mixed with spices, coated in a chickpea flour batter and deep-fried until golden and crispy. Batata vada are traditionally served very hot, straight from the fryer, with green chillies and green [chutney](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chutney-recipes "Chutney"). Nanna Tate potatoes work a treat here, making for a light and fluffy filling.

#### FILLING

-   800g of [Nanna Tate potatoes](https://nannatate.com/), peeled and cut into chunks
-   2 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   1 tsp [mustard seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-seed-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   10 [curry leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/curry-leaf-recipes)
-   2 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   5cm of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely grated
-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tsp [fine sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   1/2 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped

#### BATTER

-   200g of gram flour
-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp baking powder
-   1 tsp [fine sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   230ml of water
-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying

#### CORIANDER CHUTNEY

-   2 bunches of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), a few leaves reserved for garnish
-   1/4 bunch of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), leaves picked
-   1 [green chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   1 tbsp of [roasted peanuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of water

-   Thermometer

1

Place the potatoes in a pan of cold salted water and bring to the boil. Simmer for 15-18 minutes until tender, then drain well and allow to steam dry

-   800g of [Nanna Tate potatoes](https://nannatate.com/), peeled and cut into chunks

2

Meanwhile for the coriander chutney, blitz all of the ingredients in a food processor or blender, adding water as needed to achieve a spoonable consistency. Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt and black pepper. Set aside

-   2 bunches of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), a few leaves reserved for garnish
-   1/4 bunch of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), leaves picked
-   1 [green chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   1 tbsp of [roasted peanuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of water

3

For the batter, whisk together the gram flour, turmeric, chilli powder, baking powder and salt in a bowl. Gradually whisk in enough water to form a thick, smooth batter – it should coat the back of a spoon (you may need slightly more or less water). Set aside to rest

-   200g of gram flour
-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp baking powder
-   1 tsp [fine sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   230ml of water

4

Once the potatoes are tender, mash them until smooth but still with a little texture – a slightly rougher texture gives a better bite after frying

5

Heat the vegetable oil in a pan over a medium heat. Add the mustard seeds and allow them to pop, then add the cumin seeds and curry leaves. Add the chopped green chillies and ginger, cooking for 1 minute more until fragrant

-   2 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   1 tsp [mustard seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-seed-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   10 [curry leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/curry-leaf-recipes)
-   2 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   5cm of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely grated

6

Stir in the turmeric, add the mashed potatoes and salt and mix thoroughly. Finally stir in lemon juice and coriander

-   1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tsp [fine sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   1/2 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped

7

Allow the mixture to cool slightly, then shape into golf ball-sized portions approximately 40-45g each

8

Place the oil in a deep saucepan no more than two thirds full and heat to 170-175°C

-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying

9

Dip each potato ball into the batter to fully coat, then carefully lower into the oil. Fry for 4-5 minutes until golden, crisp and the batter is cooked through, turning occasionally – you will need to fry in batches to maintain the oil temperature at around 170°C. Once done, remove with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper

10

Arrange the vada in a serving dish. Serve with the coriander chutney, reserved coriander leaves, some lemon zest and a pinch of cayenne pepper

-   [lemon zest](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)
