-   4
    
-   40 minutes plus 20 minutes brining time
    

Celebrate the winning combination of pan-fried [salmon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salmon-recipes), simple sautéed seasonal greens (like [asparagus](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes), [courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes) and [peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes), and buttered [new potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/new-potato-recipes). The dish is complemented by a yoghurt and [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes) sauce for a fresh, bright and tangy flavour profile that balances out the richness of the butter and oily salmon. [Brining](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brine-recipes) the salmon with aromats like lemon and black peppercorn imparts a fresh and layered seasoning and allows the salmon to retain its moisture during cooking.
