The Bourbon Renewal is one of the most popular cocktails on the menu of [Clyde Common](http://www.clydecommon.com/) in Portland, Oregon. It combines bourbon, cassis, bitters, lemon, and gomme syrup for some extra body.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. bourbon
-   ¾ oz. fresh lemon juice
-   ½ oz. creme de cassis
-   [2 tsp. gomme syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/gomme-syrup-recipe/)
-   1 dash Angostura bitters

-   Tools:shaker, strainer
-   Glass:Old Fashioned

## Preparation

Combine ingredients with ice in a shaker and shake to chill. Strain into and ice-filled glass.
