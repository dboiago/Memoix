Babka is a beloved Jewish enriched [bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bread-recipes "Bread"), often made with chocolate and traditionally made with lots of eggs. Here, however, we’ve made a version that is both savoury and vegan. Although it has a touch of sweetness from the caramelised onions, this is a decidedly savoury affair. Fresh, it’s perfect dunked in our [Roasted Butternut Squash Soup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/roasted-butternut-squash-soup-recipe "Roasted butternut squash soup"). Any leftovers are delicious lightly toasted and smothered with vegan cream cheese and everything but the bagel seasoning.

-   500g of plain flour
-   1 tbsp of cane sugar
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   7g of quick yeast
-   250ml of warm water
-   4 tbsp of olive oil
-   2 [large onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), thinly sliced
-   2 tbsp of balsamic vinegar
-   2 tbsp of brown sugar
-   3 [sprigs of thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), tough stems discarded and chopped

-   Loaf tin

1

Start by preparing the dough. Mix the flour, sugar, salt and yeast in a bowl. Add in the warm water and olive oil, then knead until smooth. Cover and let rise for 1 hour

-   500g of plain flour
-   1 tbsp of cane sugar
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   7g of quick yeast
-   250ml of warm water
-   4 tbsp of olive oil

2

Sauté the onions in the olive oil with the balsamic vinegar and brown sugar until deeply caramelised, about 30–40 minutes. Set aside

-   4 tbsp of olive oil
-   2 [large onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), thinly sliced
-   2 tbsp of balsamic vinegar
-   2 tbsp of brown sugar

3

Roll the dough into a 30 x 30 cm square, spread the caramelised onions across the dough, then sprinkle with the thyme leaves

-   3 [sprigs of thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), tough stems discarded and chopped

4

Roll the dough up into a long, skinny log, slice down the middle into two skinner logs, then twist the two lengths together. Tuck the ends underneath

5

Place the loaf in a greased and lined loaf tin, then cover and let rise for another 30 minutes

6

Preheat the oven to 180°C

7

Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden. Let cool before slicing
