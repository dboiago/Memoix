A Margarita with a bittersweet edge.

Looking for a Margarita with a bitter edge? Shake up this amaro Margarita from Chris Elford, co-founder of Seattle’s Navy Strength. Inspired by his days at New York bitters bar [Amor Y Amargo](https://imbibemagazine.com/inside-look-amor-y-amargo-brooklyn/), Elford set out to create a Margarita “through the lens of the [Boukman Daiquiri](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/boukman-daiquiri-recipe/) and the also classic Donga Punch.” He balances the baking spices of Averna with a rich cinnamon syrup and the amaro’s bitter notes with an oleo saccharum.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/4 oz. blanco tequila
-   1 oz. fresh lime juice
-   3/4 oz. Averna
-   1/2 oz. rich cinnamon syrup
-   [1/2 oz. citrus oleo saccharum](https://imbibemagazine.com/how-to-make-oleo-saccharum/)

## Preparation

Rim the edge of a glass with salt, fill with ice, and set aside. Add all of the ingredients to a shaker with ice and shake vigorously. Strain into the prepared glass, then add the garnish.  
**Rich Cinnamon Syrup**Slice 6 cinnamon sticks thinly lengthwise to expose more surface area for more flavor. Add 9 oz. of demerara sugar to 6 oz. of boiling water, stir, and allow the sugar to dissolve completely. Add the cinnamon and allow to steep overnight. Strain out the cinnamon sticks. Will keep for about a month in the refrigerator.
