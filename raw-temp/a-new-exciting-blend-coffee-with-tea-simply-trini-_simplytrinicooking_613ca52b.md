## Coffee with Tea Anyone?

Maybe Jonathan Swift is correct when he said, “Coffee makes us severe, and grave, and philosophical“. So today I thought I’d show you what I have been doing with coffee. My father-in-law is a coffee addict and he suggested I try coffee with tea. Being a newcomer to coffee, I agree it was a good introduction to coffee.

If you are a season coffee drinker, like my father-in-law and a newbie like myself, I know you will like this. The red tea gives the coffee a subtle tea-flavor that may have even the most seasoned coffee drinker wanting more. It may even have you questioning what exotic blend of coffee is this.

In continuing with coffee week, we have for you today coffee with tea. If you want you could try other blends of tea, such as mint or chamomile. Here is coffee with tea.

1 tsp. instant coffee  
1/3 cup water

Course Drink

Cuisine Fusion

Prep Time 5 minutes

Cook Time 2 minutes

Total Time 7 minutes

Calories 3.57kcal

-   1 tsp. instant coffee
-   1/3 cup water
-   1 tea bag original recipe used Red Rose tea bag
-   Sugar to taste

-   Set the water in a kettle to boil. Pour the hot water into a teacup. Dip the tea bag into the water (about 8 times).
    
-   Then add 1 teaspoon of instant coffee. Stir until the coffee dissolves. Add sugar for taste. Serve hot.
