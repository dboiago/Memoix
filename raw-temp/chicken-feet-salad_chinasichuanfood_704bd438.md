## Why we love chicken feet

Chinese people not only love chicken feet for their taste but also for their nutritional value – making them one of the most popular Chinese dishes. In China, chicken feet are considered a delicacy. They are served at restaurants and eaten as snacks, with some people even making chicken feet a regular part of their diet. It is reported that chicken feet have many health benefits, such as providing essential vitamins, minerals, and nutrients to the body. Additionally, chicken feet provide high amounts of collagen which is believed to be able to reduce wrinkles and improve skin elasticity.

## How long to cook the chicken feet

You can adjust the cooking time of chicken feet. If you want a chewier texture, cook the for 8 minutes. If you want a softer version, cook for 12 to 15 minutes. However don’t overcook them, otherwise, the skin and shape will be destroyed.

If you prefer a super chew version, 5 minutes are enough.

## Soak the cooked chicken feet in cold water

Soak the chicken feet in iced water can help to tighten the skins and thus brings a better elastic texture.

## 🔪Instructions

Cut the claws and then cut each chicken foot into 2 pieces.  Smaller pieces mean better flavors in a shorter time.

![chicken feet salad|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/chicken-feet-salad-8.webp)

In a deep pot with enough cold water, add chicken feet, cooking wine, and rice vinegar. Bring the content to a boil and then cook for 8 minutes (depending on how soft you want the chicken feet to be).

![chicken feet salad|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/chicken-feet-salad-10.webp)

Once cooked, transfer out and clean with warm water (removing extra colloid) . Then pour in cold water to soak the chicken feet for 20 minutes. Soaking with cold water makes the chicken feet’ skin more elastic.

![chicken feet salad|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/chicken-feet-salad-9.webp)

Prepare all the seasonings: add lemon pieces, garlic, ginger, chili peppers, coriander steam, and coriander.

![chicken feet salad|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/chicken-feet-salad-11.webp)

Mix in light soy sauce, black vinegar, and sugar. Make a message with the chicken feet for 1 or 2 minutes. Let the sauce combine completely.

![chicken feet salad|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/chicken-feet-salad-12.webp)

Cover with plastic wrapper and set aside in the fridge overnight.

## How long can this salad last

You can store this in the fridge for 3 days. But in most cases, you will finish them up on the second day. This can be a great dish for friend parties.

![chicken feet salad|chinasichuanfood.com](https://www.chinasichuanfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/chicken-feet-salad-2-360x360.webp)

-   20 chicken feet
-   1 tbsp. Chinese cooking wine
-   1/2 tbsp. white rice vinegar

#### Sauce

-   3 tbsp. light soy sauce
-   1/3 cup [rice vinegar](http://amzn.to/2fVPcnc) , or black vinegar
-   2 lemon , smaller ones (around 200g)
-   1/4 cup chopped coriander
-   1 tbsp. chopped ginger
-   2 garlic cloves ,smashed
-   1 tbsp. sugar
-   3 fresh Thai chili pepper , one red and one green| cut into small circles. This can be adjusted by persoanl perference
-   1 tsp. salt

-   Cut the claws and then cut each chicken foot into 2 pieces.  Smaller pieces mean better flavors in a shorter time.
    
-   In a deep pot with enough cold water, add chicken feet, cooking wine, and rice vinegar. Bring the content to a boil and then cook for 8 minutes (depending on how soft you want the chicken feet to be).
    
-   Once cooked, transfer out and clean with warm water (removing extra colloid) . Then pour in cold water to soak the chicken feet for 20 minutes. Soaking with cold water makes the chicken feet' skin more elastic.
    
-   Prepare all the seasonings and herbs: add lemon pieces, garlic, ginger, chili peppers, coriander steam, and coriander.
    
-   Mix in light soy sauce, vinegar, and sugar. Make a message with the chicken feet for 1 or 2 minutes. Let the sauce combine completely.
    
-   Cover with plastic wrapper and set aside in the fridge overnight.
