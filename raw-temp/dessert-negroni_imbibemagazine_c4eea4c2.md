Recipe: Francesco LaFranconi , Las Vegas

June 02, 2017

![classic negroni](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/dessert-negroni.png)

The Negroni’s favorite ambassador, Francesco LaFranconi, created this sweet ode to the classic Negroni.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. Cinzano 1757 Rosso vermouth
-   ¾ oz. Campari
-   ¾ oz. gin
-   1 oz. Chinotto soda
-   1 scoop of blood orange sorbet

-   Tools:mixing glass, barspoon, strainer
-   Glass:Old Fashioned
-   Garnish:cherry, orange wheel and bay leaf

## Preparation

Fill a mixing glass with ice. Add the vermouth, gin and Campari, stir to chill, and then strain over ice into an Old Fashioned glass. Top with the soda, stir and add a scoop of blood orange sorbet. Garnish.
