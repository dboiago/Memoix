## For a unique, garden-fresh appetizer!

**Cooks in:** _2h_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 LB. of eggplant (two)
-   3 C. of flour
-   32 of cherry tomatoes
-   1 TBSP. of brewer’s yeast
-   1 egg yolk
-   Milk
-   Butter
-   Sugar
-   Basil
-   Extravirgin olive oil
-   Salt

### Method

1

Blend 1/3 cup of milk with 3 basil leaves. Mix the flour with the crumbled yeast, 1 teaspoon of salt, and 1 teaspoon of sugar; combine it with the milk mixture, 2/3 cup of water, 2 1/2 tablespoons of melted butter, and the egg yolk. Place the dough into a bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let it rise for 2 hours; then transfer it into a buttered loaf pan (12 x 5 x 3 inches), brush it with milk, and let it rise again under a damp cloth for another hour. Then bake at 375°F for 40-45 minutes.

2

Remove the bread from the oven and let it cool on its side; cut 8 slices, bake again at 350°F for 5-6 minutes, then cut the slices in half. Grease the eggplant and bake at 350°F for 25 minutes; peel the eggplant, cut the flesh into strips, top with chopped basil and oil, and let it rest for 30 minutes. Place the tomatoes onto a baking tray; top with oil, salt, and a sprinkle of sugar. Bake at 320°F for 30 minutes. Serve on the crostini with the eggplant.
