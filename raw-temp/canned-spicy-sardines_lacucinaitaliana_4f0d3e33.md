## Mince about 1/4 of a Cruschi bell pepper, 1 chili pepper, 2 mint leaves and a little fennel; mix with 1/2 cup of extra-virgin olive oil. In a jam jar,...

**Cooks in:** _45mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

### List of Ingredients

-   30 of sardine fillets
-   1/4 PC. of Cruschi (crunchy) pepper
-   1 chili pepper
-   2 of mint leaves
-   Fennel fronds
-   1/2 OZ. of Extravirgin olive oil

### Method

1

Mince about 1/4 of a Cruschi bell pepper, 1 chili pepper, 2 mint leaves and a little fennel; mix with 1/2 cup of extra-virgin olive oil. In a jam jar, set 30 sardine fillets and cover them with the oil, leaving a little space at the top. Vacuum seal the jar and boil it submerged in water for 30 minutes.
