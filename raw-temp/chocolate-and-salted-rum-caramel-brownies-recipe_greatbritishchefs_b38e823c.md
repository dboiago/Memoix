These boozy salted [rum](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rum-cocktail-recipes "Rum cocktail") [caramel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caramel-recipes "Caramel") brownies come together dangerously quickly, and are the perfect step up from classic [chocolate brownies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brownie-recipes "Brownie"). They use shop-bought salted caramel and a mixture of milk and dark chocolate to make a brownie that's just that touch more special.

First published in 2023

### Metric

### Imperial

-   23cm square baking tin

1

Preheat the oven to 160ºC fan/gas mark 4

2

Grease and line a 23cm square baking tin with baking paper

3

Melt the butter in a saucepan then add the chocolate. Remove from heat and allow it to melt for a couple of minutes before stirring until smooth

-   100g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100g of [milk chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), chopped
-   100g of [dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), chopped

5

Combine the the flour, cocoa powder, the baking powder and a pinch of salt in another bowl then sift onto the chocolate mixture and beat until just combined

-   100g of plain flour
-   50g of [cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes)
-   1 tsp baking powder
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

6

Combine the caramel with the rum and mix well

-   260g of salted caramel
-   2 tbsp of [rum](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rum-cocktail-recipes)

7

Spoon half the brownie mix into the tin

8

Add half the caramel in 3 evenly spaced stripes

9

Add the second half of the brownie mix

10

Add the remaining caramel in 3 stripes on top of the brownies then use a toothpick or chopstick to swirl it in

11

Bake for 25 minutes - the centre should still have a wobble
