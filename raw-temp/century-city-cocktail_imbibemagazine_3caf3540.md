A California-centric take on the Vieux Carré.

In step with the tradition of naming cocktails after neighborhoods (think Brooklyn, [Bronx](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe-bronx-cocktail/), [Manhattan](https://imbibemagazine.com/manhattan/)), the Century City cocktail is an homage to Los Angeles but takes its structure from the classic [Vieux Carré](https://imbibemagazine.com/vieux-carre-recipe/). In the drink, mezcal replaces whiskey, and Bénédictine steps aside for Ancho Reyes, a spicy chili liqueur.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. mezcal
-   1 oz. Cognac
-   3/4 oz. sweet vermouth
-   1/4 oz. Ancho Reyes
-   2 dashes orange bitters

## Preparation

Add the ingredients to mixing glass with ice. Stir the cocktail until chilled (about 10 seconds), then strain into a chilled glass.
