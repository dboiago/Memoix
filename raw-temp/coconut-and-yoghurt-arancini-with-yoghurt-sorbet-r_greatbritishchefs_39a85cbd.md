Item 1 of 1

-   Makes 25 balls
    
-   60 minutes
    

These little deep-fried rice balls are flavoured with [coconut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/coconut-recipes), sesame and yoghurt, turning the classic idea of Sicilian arancini on its head. Served with tropical diced [mango](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/mango-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/mango-recipes) and a creamy yoghurt [sorbet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes), this is a stunning dessert that's sure to impress.

First published in 2019

-   Ice cream maker

1

Begin by making the yoghurt sorbet. Ensure the bowl of your ice cream machine is fully frozen

2

Place the double cream, sugar, honey and glucose in a pan and gently heat, stirring to dissolve the sugar and glucose. Remove from the heat and cool

-   250g of double cream
-   50g of sugar
-   100g of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   50g of glucose

3

Once cooled, stir in the yoghurt until equally combined. Churn in the ice cream machine and then freeze until ready to serve

-   500g of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)

4

Lightly fry the rice in the tablespoon of oil until toasted and starting to turn light golden

-   1 tbsp of neutral oil
-   125g of [pudding rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes), Michael also suggests risotto rice for a different texture

5

Add half the water and stir until thick, then add the rest of the water. Stir every now and then to avoid sticking to the pan

-   250ml of water

6

After about 10–15 minutes, when the rice is almost cooked, add the desiccated coconut, creamed coconut, black sesame seeds and sugar then remove from the heat and allow to cool

-   50g of [desiccated coconut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-recipes)
-   40g of [creamed coconut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes)
-   5g of [black sesame seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sesame-seeds-recipes)
-   60g of sugar

7

Once the rice is at room temperature add the yoghurt and mix well to combine. Place the mixture in the fridge until chilled

-   60g of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)

8

Roll the rice mixture out into 20g balls

9

Set up three shallow bowls; one with the flour, the next with the beaten egg and the final with the Panko breadcrumbs

-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   100g of flour
-   150g of panko breadcrumbs

10

Roll the balls in the flour, then egg, then coat in breadcrumbs. Store in the fridge until needed

11

To serve, heat a pan of oil until it reaches 170°C. Deep-fry the balls for around 4–6 minutes, or until the balls are nice and golden and hot in the centre

-   oil, for deep-frying

12

Serve with the diced mango and the yoghurt sorbet on the side

-   [mango](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes), diced
