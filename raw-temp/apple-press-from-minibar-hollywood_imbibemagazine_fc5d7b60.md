A cocktail bursting with fall flavor.

Apple is the star ingredient in this autumnal cocktail from MiniBar Hollywood.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. apple brandy
-   1 oz. bourbon
-   1 oz. fresh apple juice
-   ½ oz. apple cider vinegar
-   2 dashes walnut bitters (optional)

## Preparation

Shake all the ingredients with ice and strain into a chilled glass. Garnish with a floating apple slice then dust with cinnamon and star anise.
