This super-smooth blended avocado daiquiri is a fantastic way to put your fruits to use. Look for this and other recipes in Lucy Brennan’s book, [Hip Sips](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0811849589?ie=UTF8&tag=imbibmagaz-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=390957&creativeASIN=0811849589).

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. silver rum
-   2 oz. gold rum
-   [2 oz. simple syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   1/2 oz. half and half
-   1/4 medium ripe avocado, peeled and pitted
-   1/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1 1/2 cups ice cubes

## Preparation

Blend ingredients well. Pour into a glass. For garnish, spoon the pomegranate concentrate (the purée, not the juice; it’s available online and at some gourmet markets or health food stores) into a plastic squeeze bottle or a pastry tube and draw a zigzag pattern across the top. Drag two straws through the middle to make a series of pomegranate hearts. Serve with a straw.
