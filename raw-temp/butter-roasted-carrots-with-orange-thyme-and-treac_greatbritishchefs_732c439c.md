 Sweet heritage [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes "Carrot") roasted are in butter until caramelised, tossed in a glossy [treacle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/treacle-recipes "Treacle") glaze, and finished with a fresh carrot top gremolata and fresh orange slices. These carrots are the perfect side to serve with a pie, such as Calum Franklin's Festive Cracker, created in partnership with Dickinson & Morris.

First published in 2025

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Roasted carrots

-   800g of [heritage carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), trimmed and washed with tops reserved
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   75g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), juice and zested
-   2 tbsp of [black treacle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/treacle-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [fresh thyme leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of light brown sugar
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### Carrot top gremolata

-   1 handful of [carrot tops](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), washed and finely chopped
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1 [bunch of parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 tbsp of [roasted hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), finely chopped
-   100ml of olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

#### Garnish

-   2 [oranges](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested and sliced into wedges
-   1 handful of [fresh parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [pink peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), crushed
-   [thyme sprigs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)

1

Preheat the oven to 200°C fan

2

Place the carrots on a roasting tray, drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and toss to coat. Dot the butter evenly over the tray

-   800g of [heritage carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), trimmed and washed with tops reserved
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   75g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

3

Roast for 25–30 minutes, turning once, until the carrots are tender and beginning to caramelise at the edges

4

While the carrots roast, combine the orange juice and zest, treacle, thyme leaves and brown sugar in a small saucepan. Simmer gently over medium heat for 5–6 minutes until glossy and slightly reduced

-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), juice and zested
-   2 tbsp of [black treacle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/treacle-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [fresh thyme leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of light brown sugar

5

Mix together all of the gremolata ingredients and season with salt to taste, then add to a food processor then blitz until mostly smooth

-   1 handful of [carrot tops](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), washed and finely chopped
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1 [bunch of parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 tbsp of [roasted hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), finely chopped
-   100ml of olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

6

Pour the glaze over the roasted carrots and toss well to coat. Return to the oven for 5 minutes until sticky and shiny

7

Arrange the glazed carrots on a serving platter. Spoon over a little of the carrot-top gremolata, leaving some to serve alongside. Garnish with extra thyme sprigs, a few parsley leaves, orange segments and a final dusting of orange zest for brightness

-   2 [oranges](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested and sliced into wedges
-   1 handful of [fresh parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [pink peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), crushed
-   [thyme sprigs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
