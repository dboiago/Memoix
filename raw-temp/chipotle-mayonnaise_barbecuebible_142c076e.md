### Chipotle Mayonnaise

## Recipe Notes

-   **Yield:** Makes about 1/2 cup

## Ingredients

-   ½ cup mayonnaise, preferably Hellmann’s
-   1 to 2 chipotle chiles in adobo, minced, plus 2 teaspoons can juices
-   1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
-   ½ teaspoon ground cumin

## Recipe Steps

**1:** Place the mayonnaise, chipotle(s), and paprika in a small nonreactive bowl and whisk to mix.

**2:** If not serving at once, cover and refrigerate. The mayonnaise will keep for several days, covered, in the refrigerator.
