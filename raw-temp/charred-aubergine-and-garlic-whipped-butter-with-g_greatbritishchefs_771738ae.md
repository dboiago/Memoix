[Compound butters](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/compound-butter-recipes "Compound butter") are a wonderful way of incorporating more flavour into your dishes. This charred [aubergine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/aubergine-recipes "Aubergine"), garlic and marjoram whipped compound butter is served with [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes "Caper") and a touch of lemon zest for that perfect balance of acidity. We serve ours simply with toasted sourdough, but this butter can be melted down and used to cook any ingredients from fish to vegetables for an added burst of flavour. 

First published in 2024

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Begin by preparing the grilled aubergine. Drizzle the aubergine with olive oil and season with salt

-   2 [aubergines](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/aubergine-recipes), whole
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to season

2

Prepare the grill for direct cooking. When ready, place the aubergine on the grill 

3

Cook for 5–7 minutes before turning over and grilling the other side. Once the skin has slightly charred and the aubergine flesh has softened, remove from the grill and leave for a couple of minutes to cool slightly 

4

Peel any overly charred skin off of the aubergines, then finely chop the flesh 

5

Coat the cherry tomatoes in olive oil then season well with flaky sea salt 

6

Toss the cherry tomatoes in olive oil and season with salt, then grill for 5 minutes, or until they are tender 

-   1 handful of [cherry tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-tomato-recipes), on the vine
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to season

8

Blend until smooth, then spoon onto a serving plate 

9

Serve along with the sourdough toast, and garnish with a handful of caper berries, grilled cherry tomatoes, lemon zest, salt and pepper

-   1 handful of [caperberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to season
-   1 pinch of [ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), to season
-   2 slices of [sourdough bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), toasted
