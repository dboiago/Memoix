Prime Granny Smith [apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/apple-recipes) are frozen, blitzed and churned in this gorgeous apple [sorbet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sorbet-recipes) recipe. To serve with the garnishes pictured, check out [Simon Haigh](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/simon-haigh)'s original recipe – he serves this sorbet with a heavenly [gingerbread soufflé](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/gingerbread-souffle-recipe-apple-sorbet).

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   Ice cream maker
-   Blender

1

Peel and core the apples, then chop the flesh. Place in a suitable container and freeze until the apples are hard and frozen

-   5 [Granny Smith apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes)

2

Place the chopped frozen apples in a blender with the sugar syrup and lemon juice. Blitz until smooth, then pass through a fine sieve

-   570ml of sugar syrup
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced

3

Churn the mix in an ice cream maker, then keep frozen until ready to serve

[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/simon-haigh)

Simon Haigh cut his teeth at the famed Le Manoir aux Quat’Saisons, working with Raymond Blanc, whom he cites as a key inspiration to this day.
