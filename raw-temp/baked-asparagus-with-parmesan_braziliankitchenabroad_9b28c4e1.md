-   **Olive oil**
-   **Breadcrumbs**—if you’re using [homemade bread crumbs](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/homemade-bread-crumbs/), use the bigger, crunchier bits, too, as they add even more texture. If you don’t have that, go for Panko, or you can use broken up, unflavored croutons, or pieces of old bread.
-   **Salt and pepper**—use a light hand with the salt. This dish can get too salty too fast because of the parmesan.

## How to Roast Asparagus at 425F

-   [![A woman cuts a bunch of asparagus on a cutting board](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Asparagus-3-683x1024.jpg)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Asparagus-3.jpg)
    
-   [![A parmesan topping sprinkles onto a sheet pan of asparagus](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Asparagus-6-683x1024.jpg)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Asparagus-6.jpg)
    

**Preheat the oven to 425F.** Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment or foil for easier clean up, and set aside.

**Cut off the woody ends** of the asparagus. We do this because parts of the stems can become too tough to chew otherwise. Take a single spear of asparagus and gently bend it in your hand near the end. It will snap where it needs to be cut, so use this as a guide for the rest of your asparagus.

Once cut, place the asparagus onto the prepared baking sheet in a single layer, and **drizzle with olive oil.** You don’t need a lot! Then, massage the oil into the asparagus, so it’s evenly coating all of them.

**Mix the topping.** In a bowl, mix the [Brazilian sofrito](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/brazilian-sofrito/) with the freshly grated parmesan, breadcrumbs and a little extra olive oil. Stir to combine.

**Sprinkle the topping onto the asparagus.** Try to do this in an even layer, either with your hands or with a spoon. Sprinkle on additional parmesan, then a little salt and pepper. _Use a light hand with the salt because the parmesan is already adding saltiness to our roasted asparagus recipe._

**Bake.**

## How Long to Roast Asparagus at 425F

-   [![Parmesan is sprinkled on top of roasted asparagus](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Asparagus-9-683x1024.jpg)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Asparagus-9.jpg)
    
-   [![A woman holds a sheet pan of Baked Asparagus with Parmesan](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Asparagus-11-683x1024.jpg)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Asparagus-11.jpg)
    

The roasting time will be dependent on the thickness of your asparagus.

**If the asparagus is thick**, roast for 15 minutes. Rotate the pan halfway through the roast, and check on them then.

**If the asparagus is pencil thin**, roast for 7 or so minutes. Like the thicker ones, you’ll want to rotate the pan halfway through, too. Keep an eye on these because they can burn.

Either way, keep an eye on the asparagus as it roasts.

When the Baked Asparagus with Parmesan comes out of the oven, sprinkle on more freshly grated parmesan cheese while still piping hot. Serve immediately with a squeeze of lemon juice, if you want.

Servings 4 people

Calories 88 kcal

Prep Time 5 minutes

Cook Time 15 minutes

-   1 lb trimmed asparagus
-   1 tablespoon [of Brazilian sofrito](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/brazilian-sofrito/) or minced garlic
-   3 tablespoons of freshly grated parmesan cheese
-   1 tablespoon of olive oil
-   1/4 cup [homemade breadcrumbs](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/homemade-bread-crumbs/) or panko
-   salt and pepper to taste

-   Preheat the oven to 425F.
    
-   Arrange the asparagus in a baking sheet and drizzle with just a little olive oil.
    
-   In a bowl, mix the [Brazilian sofrito](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/brazilian-sofrito/), the parmesan, the breadcrumbs and the tablespoon of olive oil.
