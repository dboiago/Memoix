This coffee ice cream recipe from [Matthew Tomkinson](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/matthew-tomkinson) is perfect for those who appreciate desserts with a bit of prep. Coffee [ice cream](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ice-cream-recipes) makes a decadent accompaniment to a [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocoholic-chocolate-recipes) dessert, or can be served in a glass with a shot of espresso.

First published in 2015

-   Ice cream maker

1

Add the egg yolks and sugar to a bowl and whisk until pale and smooth. Meanwhile, bring the cream, milk and coffee to the boil then remove from the heat

-   18 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   340g of sugar
-   8 [shots of ground coffee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coffee-recipes)
-   850ml of milk
-   850ml of cream

2

Gradually whisk the hot milk and cream mix into the eggs and sugar until well combined, then tip into a pan and heat to 80°C or until the mixture coats the back of a spoon

3

Pass the mixture through a fine sieve into a bowl and set aside to chill. Once cold, churn in an ice cream maker and store in the freezer until ready to serve
