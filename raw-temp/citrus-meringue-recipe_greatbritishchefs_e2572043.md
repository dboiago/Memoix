Ollie Moore's [meringue](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/meringue-recipes) recipe shows how easy it can be to add flavour to a simple base mixture. [Lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lemon-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lemon-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lemon-recipes) and lime zest add tartness to this sweet meringue, although other citrus fruits, such as orange or grapefruit, could also be used. Snapped into shards this makes an elegant decoration for cakes and desserts, or alternatively could be piped into individual meringue nests or one large [pavlova](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pavlova-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pavlova-recipes), just adjust the cooking time accordingly.

#### Citrus meringue

-   4 [egg whites](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   150g of caster sugar
-   150g of icing sugar
-   1 tsp cream of tartar
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zest only
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), zest only

1

Place the egg whites in a large, spotlessly clean bowl – any fat in the bowl will stop the whites from expanding to their full volume

-   4 [egg whites](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

2

Add the caster sugar then sieve in the icing sugar and cream of tartar. Grate in the citrus zests

-   150g of caster sugar
-   150g of icing sugar
-   1 tsp cream of tartar
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zest only
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), zest only

3

Using a handheld electric whisk, or the whisk attachment on a stand mixer, whisk everything together for 5 minutes, or until the meringue holds in stiff peaks when the whisk is lifted out of the mixture

4

Preheat the oven to 90°C/gas mark 1/4

5

Spread the meringue out in a thin layer on a silicone baking mat or a baking tray lined with baking paper. Alternatively, transfer to a piping bag and pipe shapes as desired

6

Bake in the oven for 1 hour and 15 minutes or until the meringue is crisp on top but not colouring (thicker shapes may take slightly longer)

7

Allow to cool completely on the tray then peel off the shapes or snap the sheet into shards to serve
