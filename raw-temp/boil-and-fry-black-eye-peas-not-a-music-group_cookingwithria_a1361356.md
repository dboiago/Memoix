Nowadays, with all the pressure of staying healthy and exercising, I am desiring real food for breakfast–something substantial. I wanted a meal that was vegetarian with lots of nutrition, fiber and protein to get me through my mornings. This dish has not disappointed.

**This boil and fry black-eyed peas dish, referred to as “Googanee” by my Mom, is made traditionally with only onion, garlic, culantro (shado beni) and pepper, but I added veggies for additional flavor and nutrients. I usually grate a carrot into it also. Googanee or boil and fry black-eyed peas is eaten in Trinidad with [roti](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/roti/) for breakfast. I eat it alone or if I’m very hungry or stressed  I eat it with a scrambled egg or two.**

[![boil and fry black-eyed peas ](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-1wltHHWbEjg/VNAsHOio71I/AAAAAAAAEBw/p_HGv6HNDTg/s640/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-1wltHHWbEjg/VNAsHOio71I/AAAAAAAAEBw/p_HGv6HNDTg/s1600/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas.jpg)

If you’re seeking a healthy alternative for breakfast a few days a week(or a wonderful side dish), then this is your answer. Did I mention that it’s very affordable?!! A pound of beans is enough for 8-10 servings, that’s about 15 cents per meal. Warning—If you are preparing this recipe only for your self, make only one cup at a time, or else you will get all “blackeyed out” after the fourth day!

**Ingredients required for [Trinidadian](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/trinidadian/) Boil and Fry BlackEyed Peas \[Googanee\]**

Makes 4-6 servings

**To boil:**

1 cup dried black-eyed peas

6 cups water

1 teaspoon salt

**To Cook:**

3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil

1 medium onion, chopped ( I only use sweet onion)

½ large red sweet pepper, optional

½ large orange or yellow sweet pepper, optional

1 tablespoon minced or chopped garlic (if you love garlic like me, use two tablespoons)

¼ cup chopped fresh herbs (scallion, cilantro or culantro/shado beni)

1 teaspoon thyme leaves

½ teaspoon ground roasted cumin([geera](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/geera/)), optional

Hot pepper, (scotch Bonnet, habanero), to taste, optional

2 pimento peppers, chopped

Salt and freshly ground pepper, to taste

Pick over beans. Soak beans overnight or a few hours.

[![boil and fry black-eyed peas ](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-uSemHflpqDY/VM-IK1QfClI/AAAAAAAAD_s/ZWJFUfqd_j4/s640/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-2.jpg)](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-uSemHflpqDY/VM-IK1QfClI/AAAAAAAAD_s/ZWJFUfqd_j4/s1600/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-2.jpg)

Place drained black eye peas,  6 cups of clean water (meaning not the water you soaked it in) and salt in a pot over high heat. Bring to a boil, lower heat and cook until tender but still whole. Drain. While beans are boiling, chop onion, mince or grate garlic, chop peppers and herbs.

[![](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-vo3NwIrMNHQ/VM-Ii4kj7NI/AAAAAAAAD_0/Tq1Sxbhvabg/s640/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-3.jpg)](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-vo3NwIrMNHQ/VM-Ii4kj7NI/AAAAAAAAD_0/Tq1Sxbhvabg/s1600/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-3.jpg)

Heat oil in a dutch oven or heavy bottomed pot, over medium heat. Add onion, Cook 1 minute or until translucent. Add garlic and cook another minute.

[![](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-R8ey5oxn3PA/VM-I0ImJ3oI/AAAAAAAAEAM/nZ-fuUnTe_c/s640/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-8.jpg)](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-R8ey5oxn3PA/VM-I0ImJ3oI/AAAAAAAAEAM/nZ-fuUnTe_c/s1600/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-8.jpg)

Add peppers and herbs including thyme leaves and cook for 3-5 minutes until tender.

[![boil and fry black-eyed peas ](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-HSzqiXe9hIA/VM-I7i840KI/AAAAAAAAEAU/q4ZMkeSA5Js/s640/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-9.jpg)](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-HSzqiXe9hIA/VM-I7i840KI/AAAAAAAAEAU/q4ZMkeSA5Js/s1600/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-9.jpg)

[![boil and fry black-eyed peas ](https://4.bp.blogspot.com/-aJn6c_m1BG0/VM-JJNSbDsI/AAAAAAAAEAc/Tm161dxca3A/s640/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-10.jpg)](https://4.bp.blogspot.com/-aJn6c_m1BG0/VM-JJNSbDsI/AAAAAAAAEAc/Tm161dxca3A/s1600/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-10.jpg)

Add black eye peas, salt, freshly ground black pepper and cook about 5 minutes, stirring constantly, to prevent sticking. Add cumin, stir and remove from the heat. Test for salt and add more if desired.

[![boil and fry black-eyed peas ](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-jqF_nz96Bhk/VM-JUrxjU6I/AAAAAAAAEAk/Ru3Mia5E8t0/s640/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-11.jpg)](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-jqF_nz96Bhk/VM-JUrxjU6I/AAAAAAAAEAk/Ru3Mia5E8t0/s1600/Boil%2Band%2BFry%2BBlack%2BEye%2BPeas-11.jpg)

For a complete vegetarian meal, eat alone, with roti or serve in a “[hops](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/hops/)” bread(rolls), or with freshly steamed jasmine or basmati rice. For a down home comfort meal, eat with [cornbread](https://cookingwithria.com/2013/10/grandmothers-buttermilk-cornbread.html)…bbq chicken or steamed lobster….or with grilled salmon and sautéed baby spinach if your curves are important to you.
