This flatbread dough is a bit of a hybrid, developed over years of making naan, pita and pizza doughs. I like that it needs less than an hour of rising before I can use it, and in addition to its uses in this book, you can bake it, pan-cook it or grill it plain to serve alongside a curry, soup or stew, or you can top it with ingredients like I do for my [Charcuterie Flatbread with Prosciutto, Peaches & Pistachios](https://annaolson.ca/project/charcuterie-flatbread-with-prosciutto-peaches-pistachios/).

![](https://annaolson.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/FlatBreadDough_9560.jpeg "FlatBreadDough_9560")

-   **2 cups (300 g)** all-purpose flour
-   **2 tsp** fine salt
-   **1½ tsp** granulated sugar or honey
-   **1¼ tsp** instant dry yeast
-   **⅔ cup (160 mL)** lukewarm water
-   **6 Tbsp (90 mL)** plain Greek yogurt
-   1 **Tbsp (15 mL)** extra-virgin olive oil, plus extra for brushing

**NOTES**

Baked flatbreads will keep well-wrapped on the counter for up to 1 day or can be frozen for up to 3 months.

 Unsure if to bake, pan-fry or grill your flatbread dough? Let your main dish be your guide.  If topping your flatbread with ingredients to serve as a starter, pre-bake your flatbread in the oven and then reheat after adding your toppings. If the grill is already set on high heat to cook a steak or chops, then grilling your flatbread is easiest, and if you’re serving a curry, then pan-frying to get those naan-like bubble blisters is the way to go.

## Step by Step Instructions

##### Step 1

Into a large mixing bowl, measure the flour, salt, sugar (if using) and yeast. Add the water, honey (if using in place of sugar), yogurt and 1 Tbsp (15 mL) olive oil and stir with a wooden spoon until too difficult to do so.

##### Step 2

Turn the dough out onto a worktable and knead for about 5 minutes, until elastic. Return to the bowl, cover and let rise on the counter for 40 minutes before shaping and baking.

##### Step 3

Here are easy ways to shape and cook your flatbread, depending on how you wish to serve it:

##### To bake the flatbread:

Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Divide the dough into two pieces and shape each into a ball. On a lightly floured work surface and using a rolling pin, roll each piece of dough into a flat circle or oblong shape just under ½ inch (1.2 cm) thick. Place these onto a parchment-lined baking tray and dock with a fork.

Bake for 12 to 14 minutes, until golden brown. If they happen to puff up like pita bread, pierce each with a skewer to allow it to deflate. Brush the flatbreads with olive oil after they out of the oven.

##### To pan-fry the flatbread:

Preheat a heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat (do not grease the pan). (You can On a lightly floured work surface and using a rolling pin, roll the dough into a flat circle or oblong shape just under ½ inch (1.2 cm) thick (you can divide the dough first into three or four pieces to better fit into your skillet, if needed.).

Drop in a flatbread into the skillet and cook for about 3 minutes on each side, until the bread blisters and the blisters brown. Repeat with the remaining flatbread and then brush each with olive oil before serving.

##### To grill the flatbread:

Preheat your grill on high heat. Divide the dough into two pieces and shape each into a ball. On a lightly floured work surface and using a rolling pin, roll each piece of dough into a flat circle or oblong shape just under ½ inch (1.2 cm) thick. Place these on a tray to bring to the grill.

Grilling one at a time, set a flatbread on the grill carefully, cover the grill and cook for 2 to 3 minutes on each side (use tongs to flip the bread over), until blistered and grill marks appear. Remove and brush each with olive oil before serving.
