A piquant, pepper-spiked cocktail that’s more like a pinch than a sting.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. reposado tequila
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   [3/4 oz. honey syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/honey-syrup-recipe/)
-   1 fresh jalapeño wheel (seeds optional)

## Preparation

Gently muddle the fresh jalapeño wheel, add remaining ingredients and shake with ice. Strain into a chilled glass and garnish.
