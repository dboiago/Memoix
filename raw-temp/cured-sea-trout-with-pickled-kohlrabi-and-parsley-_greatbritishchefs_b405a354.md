[Scott Smith](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/scott-smith/)'s elegant [sea trout](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/sea-trout-recipes) dish is one of those wonderful recipes that can be made almost entirely in advance. There are a few things here that require time – pickling the [kohlrabi](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kohlrabi-recipes), straining the [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/parsley-recipes) oil and curing the sea trout – but get them done in advance and all you'll have to do on the day is plate up! If you can't source any sea trout, good quality [salmon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/salmon-recipes) fillet would also work nicely.

First published in 2019

### Metric

### Imperial

-   Mandoline
-   Blender

1

Make a pickle liquor for the kohlrabi by bringing the sugar, vinegar and water to the boil. Remove from the heat and allow to cool

-   100ml of water
-   100g of caster sugar
-   200ml of cider vinegar

2

Thinly slice the kohlrabi to about 1mm thickness using a mandoline, then pour over the pickle liquor, cover and leave in the fridge for 3 days

-   1 [kohlrabi](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kohlrabi-recipes)

3

For the parsley emulsion, start by making the parsley oil. Heat the vegetable oil to around 75ºC, then pour into a blender along with the parsley. Blend for about 8 minutes until the oil is as smooth as possible, then strain through a double layer of muslin cloth overnight in the fridge

-   300ml of vegetable oil
-   1/2 bunch of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)

4

Blend together the egg yolk, mustard and cider vinegar as you would to make a mayonnaise, then gradually add 250ml of the cold parsley oil whilst blending to make the parsley emulsion. Season and store in a squeezy bottle ready for plating

-   1/2 tbsp of [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of cider vinegar

5

For the trout, clean and trim the fish and coat in the sugar and sea salt. Place in the fridge to cure for 4 hours, then rinse off the salt and dice the trout into small cubes

-   100g of caster sugar
-   300g of [sea trout](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/trout-recipes)
-   100g of [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

6

To plate, dress the trout in a little parsley emulsion, sliced chives, lime juice and zest, and fill the kohlrabi with the mixture to make little parcels. Place five parcels on a plate along with five dots of the parsley emulsion. Dress the plate with some of the leftover pickle juice, a little bit of sea salt, extra parsley oil and a few sprigs of dill

-   [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 bunch of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   [fronds of fresh dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes)
