This [cod fillet recipe](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cod-recipes) by [Vineet Bhatia](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/vineet-bhatia) of Rasoi is a fun [Indian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes)\-style alternative to traditional seaside [battered fish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/collections/battered-fish-recipes) and mushy [peas](https://greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pea-recipes). You can make your own chaat masala, a complex blend of spices, but it is also available to buy online, along with gram (chickpea) flour and carom (ajwain) seeds.

First published in 2015

### Imperial

#### Cod

-   1/2 tsp [ginger paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [garlic paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   1/4 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   120g of [cod fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-fillet-recipes), cut into 4 pieces
-   1000ml of vegetable oil
-   1/2 tsp chaat masala

#### Batter

-   2 tbsp of gram flour
-   1 tbsp of cornflour
-   1/2 tsp carom seeds
-   1/4 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely chopped
-   1/4 tsp [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tsp [fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1/2 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), lightly beaten
-   1/4 tsp deep orange food colouring
-   1 tbsp of water
-   1/4 tsp [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

-   Deep fat fryer

1

To make the garlic and green pea chutney, heat the oil in a deep pan and add the cumin seeds. As they begin to crackle, add the chopped garlic and cook until it turns golden brown

-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1/2 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)

2

Add the finely chopped ginger and green chilli and sauté for a minute, then stir in the peas. Cook for 2 minutes. Add the coconut milk and a pinch of salt, and cook until the peas are tender

-   1 tsp [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   1 tsp [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   120g of [peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes)
-   100ml of [coconut milk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-milk-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Crush the peas lightly with the back of a spoon and leave to cool

4

For the cod, mix the ginger paste, garlic paste, turmeric, one teaspoon of the lemon juice, chilli powder and a pinch of salt together. Gently rub it over the cod. Set the cod aside for 20 minutes. Reserve the rest of the lemon juice for the batter

-   1/2 tsp [ginger paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [garlic paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

5

To make the batter, mix the food colouring with 1 tablespoon of water, and measure out one teaspoon of the lemon juice

-   1/4 tsp deep orange food colouring
-   1 tbsp of water
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

6

Mix all the ingredients for the batter together, adding just enough water to form a thick, smooth batter

-   2 tbsp of gram flour
-   1 tbsp of cornflour
-   1/2 tsp carom seeds
-   1 pinch of [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1g of [cod fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-fillet-recipes)

7

Add the marinated cod to the batter mixture and place in the fridge for 30 minutes

8

To cook the cod, heat the vegetable oil to 180°C in a deep-fat fryer or a deep pan. Add the cod and fry until crisp

-   1000ml of vegetable oil

9

Drain the cod on kitchen paper, sprinkle with the chaat masala and serve with the garlic and green pea chutney

-   1/2 tsp chaat masala
