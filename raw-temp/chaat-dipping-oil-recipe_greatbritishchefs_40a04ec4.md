This tangy chaat-inspired dipping oil gets a boost from [tamarind](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tamarind-recipes "Tamarind"), toasted whole spices and crunchy [peanuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-recipes "Peanut"). It’s ideal with crispy papads or naan.

### Imperial

1

Warm the mustard oil in a small pan over medium heat. Add the cumin, coriander and fennel seeds, and toast until fragrant, about 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat and let cool slightly

-   150ml of [mustard oil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 tsp [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes)

2

In a bowl, combine the red onion, green chilli, peanuts, tamarind paste, coriander, chaat masala and lime juice

-   1 small red onion, finely chopped
-   1 [small green chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   50g of [roasted peanuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-recipes), coarsely ground
-   1 tbsp of [tamarind paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tamarind-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1/2 tsp chaat masala
-   1/2 [unwaxed lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced

3

Once the oil is cooler, stir it into the peanut mixture. Season with salt and adjust the tanginess with more lime or tamarind, if needed 

-   1 pinch of [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
