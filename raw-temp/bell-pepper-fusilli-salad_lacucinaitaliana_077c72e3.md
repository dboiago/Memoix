## Toss yellow bell pepper and tuna with fusilli; drizzle with a mustard-Worcestershire sauce-lemon dressing and garnish with hard boiled eggs for this quick and hearty pasta salad.

**Cooks in:** _40mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   9 OZ. of fusilli pasta
-   7 OZ. of oil-packed tuna, drained
-   1 yellow bell pepper
-   2 hardboiled eggs
-   1/3 CUP of extra-virgin olive oil
-   mustard
-   lemon
-   Worcestershire sauce
-   paprika
-   salt

### Method

1

Cut a section of the yellow bell pepper. Clean and remove the seeds and inner membranes, and dice into cubes. Place the diced pepper in a mixing bowl large enough to hold the pasta. Crumble in the tuna using a fork, add a pinch of salt, and set aside.

2

Cook the fusilli in boiling salted water and drain once al dente. 

3

Peel the eggs and cut them carefully into wedges.

4

For the dressing: In a small bowl, mix 2 Tbsp. mustard with the strained juice of half a lemon, the olive oil, a few splashes of Worcestershire sauce, and a pinch of salt and paprika.

5

Pour the fusilli into the large mixing bowl, adding a drizzle of olive oil so the pasta does not stick, and stir. Once cold, top the pasta with the dressing, garnish with the eggs, and serve.
