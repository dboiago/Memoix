An old tom cocktail worth revisiting.

Bartenders at Boston’s Backbar stick close to the original Casino recipe, as published in 1930 in [_The Savoy Cocktail Book_](http://www.amazon.com/Savoy-Cocktail-Book-Harry-Craddock/dp/1614274304%20/?tag=imbibmagaz-20). It’s a classic Old Tom gin cocktail that still stands out in a modern drinkscape.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. Old Tom gin
-   ¼ oz. maraschino liqueur
-   ¼ oz. fresh lemon juice
-   2 dashes Regan's orange bitters

## Preparation

Combine ingredients in an ice-filled rocks glass, stir to combine and garnish.
