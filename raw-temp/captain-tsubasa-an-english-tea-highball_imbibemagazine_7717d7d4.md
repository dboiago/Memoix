The Japanese highball gets a spot of English tea.

Bartenders at Toffee Club—an English-style pub in Portland, Oregon—add a unique twist to the classic Japanese highball by incorporating an English tea syrup and a dash of bitters, naming the drink after a dynamic Japanese manga series that helped popularize association football.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. Japanese whisky
-   1/4 oz. English Breakfast tea syrup (Toffee Club uses Yorkshire)
-   1 dash Angostura bitters
-   chilled soda water

## Preparation

Fill a glass with ice cubes and add a splash of chilled soda. Add the tea syrup and whisky, then fill with more soda. Dash the bitters on top of the drink, then garnish.

**English Tea Syrup**Combine equal parts strong-steeped English Breakfast tea and granulated sugar, and stir until the sugar dissolves. Keeps refrigerated for up to 1 month.
