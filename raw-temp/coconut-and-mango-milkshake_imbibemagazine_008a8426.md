A quick and delicious riff on a traditional Indian dessert.

This coconut and mango milkshake from Chitra Agrawal’s new book, _[Vibrant India](https://www.amazon.com/Vibrant-India-Vegetarian-Bangalore-Brooklyn/dp/1607747340/?tag=imbibmagaz-20)_, is an easy interpretation of the traditional pudding-like dessert _mavina hannu seekarane_.

## Ingredients

-   2 mangoes, peeled and cubed
-   1 can (13.5 oz.) unsweetened coconut milk
-   1/2 cup plain yogurt
-   1/2 tsp. cardamom powder
-   3 to 4 Tbsp. sugar
-   2 ice cubes

## Preparation

Place all ingredients in a blender and purée until smooth. Serve immediately.
