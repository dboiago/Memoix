A simple low-proof highball with layers of flavors.

This simple aperitivo cocktail offers layers of flavor from Melbourne’s Pendant Public Bar.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. chilled tonic water
-   1 oz. Cocchi Americano
-   1 oz. fino sherry
-   1 oz. Campari
-   4 dashes absinthe

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a glass with a large ice cube and briefly stir to combine, and garnish.
