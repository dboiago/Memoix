This twist on the classic [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/egg-recipes) Benedict recipe includes both white and brown [crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/crab-recipes) meat and is a favourite at [Marcus Wareing](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/marcus-wareing)'s [Gilbert Scott](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/restaurants/thegilbertscott) restaurant in St Pancras. Marcus suggests using Dorset crab for this [brunch](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/search?search=brunch) dish, a variety known for its sweet and delicate meat.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Crab mix

-   25g of [brown crab meat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes)
-   100g of [white crab meat](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes)
-   1 tsp [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly ground

#### Hollandaise

-   3 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), free-range
-   1 [small shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), peeled and sliced
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), peeled and sliced
-   1 bay leaf
-   3 [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)
-   2 [white peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 sprig of [tarragon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes)
-   100ml of white wine vinegar
-   125g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), melted

1

Begin the dish by making a reduction for the hollandaise. Place all of the hollandaise ingredients, except the egg yolks and butter, in a small pan and bring to the boil

-   1 [small shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), peeled and sliced
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), peeled and sliced
-   1 bay leaf
-   3 [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)
-   2 [white peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 sprig of [tarragon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes)
-   100ml of white wine vinegar

2

Leave to simmer until the liquid has reduced by just over half. Strain through a fine sieve and set aside - discard the other ingredients

4

Place the egg yolks for the hollandaise in a heatproof bowl and set over a pan of gently simmering water. Whisk the yolks until soft and fluffy and, while still whisking, slowly pour the melted butter into the yolks

-   3 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), free-range
-   125g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), melted

5

When the mix has thickened, add a little of the reserved reduction to taste and check the seasoning. Remove the bowl from the pan of water, cover with cling film and set aside in a warm place

6

To poach the eggs, bring a medium saucepan of water to the boil with a pinch of salt and a dash of white wine vinegar. Crack each egg into a teacup and swirl the water round in the pan using a whisk

-   1 dash of white wine vinegar
-   2 [free-range eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

7

Gently pour in the eggs, poaching until they are cooked to your taste. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper

8

Meanwhile, toast the English muffins until golden. Place the muffins onto plates and divide the crab mix between them. Top with the poached eggs, then spoon the hollandaise. Serve immediately

-   2 [English muffins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
