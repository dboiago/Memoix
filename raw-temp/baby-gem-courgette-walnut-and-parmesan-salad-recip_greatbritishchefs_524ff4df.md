Bryn Williams' healthy take on a Caesar salad swaps mayonnaise for [walnut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/walnut-recipes) oil, and adds walnuts and [courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/courgette-recipes) into the bargain. Toasting the walnuts is key to get that earthy, nutty flavour running through the dish, as well as giving the [salad](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salad-recipes) a satisfying crunch. Serve as a light [summer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/summer-recipes) main, or alongside a larger meal.

First published in 2018

1

Preheat the oven to 160°C/gas mark 3

2

To begin, place the walnuts on a baking tray and bake for 6 minutes, until nicely toasted. Place the walnuts in a bowl, season with salt and pepper, and add the lemon juice and the walnut oil to create a dressing. Set aside

-   100g of [walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/walnut-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   100ml of walnut oil

3

Cut the onion in quarters, then cut into fine slices. Slice the courgettes into ribbons using a mandoline or vegetable peeler and place on a tray. Season with some of the walnut dressing

-   1 red onion
-   2 [courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes)
-   2 [yellow courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes)

4

Wash the baby gems, then pull the leaves away from the cores. Place them in large bowl and season with some more of the dressing

-   4 [baby gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes)

5

To assemble the salad, place three of the largest lettuce leaves in the center of each plate in a triangular shape. Scatter one third of the sliced courgettes, onions and walnuts on the lettuce, then grate over a little fresh Parmesan

-   100g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes)

6

Repeat this process twice more to create three layers in total. Finish with the rest of the dressing and a little more grated Parmesan
