**RECIPE:** _Jackson Cannon for [Greydon House](https://greydonhouse.com/), Nantucket_

![Broad Street Irish Coffee riff](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/broad-street-coffee-cocktial-horizontal-crdt-tim-williams.jpg)

This icy update of the classic Irish Coffee achieves new levels of deliciousness with the addition of cold-brew coffee, bitters, and boozy whipped cream.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. single malt Irish whisky
-   2 oz. cold-brew coffee
-   [3/4 oz. demerara syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/demerara-syrup-or-turbinado-syrup-recipe/)
-   1 dash Bittermen’s mole bitters (chocolate bitters can also be used)
-   Bénédictine whipped cream

## Preparation

Shake the whisky, coffee, syrup and bitters together in a shaker with 1 cube of ice. Strain into a Collins glass over fresh ice. Top with the whipped cream.

**Bénédictine Whipped Cream**Place 4 oz. of heavy cream, 1 oz. of Bénédictine, and 1/2 oz. of simple syrup in a mixing tin and whip by hand until light and fluffy. Alternatively, place all 3 ingredients in a blender and lightly pulse until the cream becomes fluffy.
