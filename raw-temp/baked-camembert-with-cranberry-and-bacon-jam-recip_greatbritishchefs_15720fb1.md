A sweet and savoury [cranberry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes "Cranberry") and [bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bacon-recipes "Bacon") jam is served with an oozing baked camembert spiked with sage leaves. [Apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes "Apple") wedges make excellent dipping tools, we recommend a sharp Granny Smith in contrast to the creamy melted [cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheese-recipes "Cheese"). The recipe makes more jam than needed, but it can be stored in the fridge for around a week and makes an excellent addition to a [roast dinner.](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sunday-roasts-recipes "Sunday roast")

First published in 2021

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Begin by making the cranberry and bacon jam. Finely dice the bacon, then place a pan over a high heat with a smash dash of oil and add the bacon. Cook until any water evaporates off and the bacon becomes dark and crispy

-   8 [rashers of smoked streaky bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bacon-recipes)
-   1 dash of olive oil

2

Add the port and scrape with a wooden spoon to remove all the flavour from the bottom of the pan. Tip in the cranberries, sugar, orange zest and juice and simmer for 10 minutes. Once the cranberries have broken down to create a sauce, have a taste and add more sugar if it's too tart. Remove from the heat and leave to cool

-   50ml of [port](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/port-cocktail-recipes)
-   250g of [cranberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)
-   3 tbsp of brown sugar
-   1/4 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested and juiced

3

Preheat an oven to 180ºC/gas mark 4. Remove the camembert from its case and discard the paper wrapping. Place it back in its container and onto an ovenproof tray

-   1 Camembert

4

Use a small knife to slash a diagonal cross-hatch pattern in the top of the cheese. Poke the sage leaves into the slits and drizzle with a little olive oil. Bake in the oven for 10–15 minutes

-   4 [sage leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sage-recipes)
-   1 dash of olive oil

5

Serve up hot with a big spoonful of the jam and a bowl of apple wedges for dipping

-   2 [Granny Smith apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), sliced into wedges, core removed
