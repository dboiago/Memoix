Chile liqueur fires up this Mexican-style hot chocolate.

Smoky ancho chile liqueur lends some heat and spice to this tequila-fueled hot chocolate.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. reposado tequila
-   1 oz. Ancho Reyes Chile Liqueur
-   5 oz. hot chocolate
-   Pinch of cinnamon

## Preparation

Combine ingredients in the mug, stir to combine, then garnish.
