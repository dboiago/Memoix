This festive, vegan version of Eastern European cabbage rolls is filled with earthy [chestnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chestnut-recipes "Chestnut") and chewy wild rice. They are perfect for Christmas Day as a [vegan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes "Vegan") side, but are also a nourishing way to use up any leftover chestnuts you have lying around if you want a lighter supper.

First published in 2024

### Metric

### Imperial

-   12 [large Savoy cabbage leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/savoy-cabbage-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   200g of [wild rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes), cooked
-   150g of [cooked chestnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chestnut-recipes), chopped
-   1 tbsp of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [fresh parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped, plus extra for garnish
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

1

Preheat the oven to 180°C

2

Start by blanching the cabbage leaves. Bring a large pot of salted water to a pot and blanch cabbage leaves for 2–3 minutes until soft. Drain and set aside

-   12 [large Savoy cabbage leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/savoy-cabbage-recipes)

3

Next, make the filling. Sauté the onion and garlic in the olive oil. Add the wild rice, chestnuts, soy sauce and parsley. Season to taste with salt and pepper

-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   200g of [wild rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes), cooked
-   150g of [cooked chestnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chestnut-recipes), chopped
-   1 tbsp of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [fresh parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped, plus extra for garnish

4

Place the filling in the centre of each cabbage leaf, roll tightly and place seam-side down in a baking dish

5

Cover with foil and bake for 20 minutes until heated through. To cook, serve topped with fresh parsley leaves, salt and pepper
