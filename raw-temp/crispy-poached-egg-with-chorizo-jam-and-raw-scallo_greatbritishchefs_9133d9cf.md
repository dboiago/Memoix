This intriguing recipe from [Robert Thompson](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/robert-thompson-recipes) combines salty and sweet raw [scallops](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/scallop-starter-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/scallop-starter-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/scallop-starter-recipes) with an oozy, crispy egg and a hit of tangy [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chorizo-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chorizo-recipes) jam. The key is getting that egg right – crisp and golden on the outside and perfectly gooey in the centre.

First published in 2019

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Chorizo oil

-   250g of [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), diced
-   350g of pomace oil (1), or another neutral oil

#### Chorizo jam (you will make more than needed, leftovers will keep for a week in the fridge)

-   43g of [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50g of glucose syrup
-   4g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   250g of [chorizo oil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes)

#### Crispy eggs

-   5 [large eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50ml of white wine vinegar
-   100g of plain flour
-   200g of panko breadcrumbs
-   vegetable oil, for deep frying

-   Blender
-   Thermometer

1

Begin by making the chorizo oil, it needs to infuse for 24 hours so bear this in mind when prepping for the dish. Sauté the chorizo until well coloured and then cover with the oil. Take off the heat and once cooled, refrigerate. Leave to infuse for 24 hours then strain

-   250g of [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), diced
-   350g of pomace oil (1), or another neutral oil

2

To make the chorizo jam, blend the yolk in a food processor on a low speed. Heat the glucose and salt to 86°C and slowly add to the yolks. Increase speed to medium and blend for two minutes

-   43g of [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50g of glucose syrup
-   4g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   250g of [chorizo oil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes)

3

Slowly add the chorizo oil and blend until mixture has thickened. Cool over ice and reserve

4

Next poach the eggs. Bring a large deep pan of unsalted water to boil, add the vinegar, turn down to a simmer and then stir to create a gentle whirlpool. Gently crack four of the eggs one by one as close to the surface as possible. Poach until the eggs are firm enough to lift out into iced water (but still with runny yolks)

-   4 [large eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50ml of white wine vinegar

5

Gently heat a pan of vegetable oil to 180°C ready to cook the eggs

-   vegetable oil, for deep frying

6

Carefully drain the eggs and pat dry before dusting in seasoned flour, shake off any excess and then roll in the remaining egg which has been lightly beaten. Coat in the breadcrumbs

-   1 [large egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   100g of plain flour
-   200g of panko breadcrumbs

8

Deep fry the eggs until crisp and golden, be careful you don't overcook them as you still want a runny centre

9

Spoon the dressed scallops into 4 bowls and top with the diced chorizo and chorizo jam. Carefully slice a v-shaped section out of the eggs and place in the centre of the bowls. Garnish with sprigs of dill

-   150g of [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), diced to 5mm
-   [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes)
