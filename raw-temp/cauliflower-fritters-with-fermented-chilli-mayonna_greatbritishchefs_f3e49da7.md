This recipe is part of a series of ideas for using this l[acto-fermented chilli sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/lacto-fermented-chilli-sauce-recipe "Lacto-fermented chilli sauce"). [Cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes "Cauliflower") florets make lovely, light fritters that are perfect for swooping through a wobbly bowl of [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes "Mayonnaise") spiked with homemade fermented hot sauce.

First published in 2023

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Mayonnaise

-   2 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® egg yolks](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/)
-   1 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   120ml of vegetable oil
-   120ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   fermented chilli sauce to taste, see above

#### Fritters

-   1/2 [large cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes), small leaves and florets separated
-   70g of plain flour
-   1 tsp baking powder
-   1 1/2 tsp curry powder
-   2 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® eggs](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/)
-   oil, for deep-frying

2

Slowly drizzle in the vegetable oil a drop at a time, whisking all the time, until a thick, pale yellow emulsion forms. Once the oil and egg yolks begin to emulsify, you can add the oil slightly faster

-   120ml of vegetable oil

3

Once all the vegetable oil has been incorporated, add the olive oil in a steady stream, whisking constantly. Taste, and adjust seasoning as needed, then stir in fermented hot sauce to taste and set aside

-   120ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   fermented chilli sauce to taste, see above

4

Cook the cauliflower florets and leaves for 3 minutes in boiling salted water, then drain and rinse under cold water

-   1/2 [large cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes), small leaves and florets separated

5

Sift the flour and baking powder into a bowl and mix in the curry powder. Make a well in the centre and add the eggs, then mix well. Add the florets and leaves and mix to coat

-   70g of plain flour
-   1 tsp baking powder
-   1 1/2 tsp curry powder
-   2 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® eggs](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/)

6

Heat oil for deep-frying to a temperature of 180°C. Deep fry the leaves and florets for a couple of minutes, or until golden brown. Drain on kitchen paper

-   oil, for deep-frying

7

Serve the fritters with the fermented chilli mayonnaise
