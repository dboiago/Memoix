Bright, bold, and aromatic.

Honey and allspice lend aromatics and depth to this rye whiskey cocktail created by Nobian Henan for Julep Cocktail Club in Kansas City, Missouri. Inspired by the [Brown Derby](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/brown-derby-cocktail/), the cocktail uses tropical elements to complement the rye. “Rye is my go-to spirit when developing tropical cocktails because it holds up beautifully next to citrus juices and spices,” Henan explains.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. rye whiskey
-   1 oz. fresh grapefruit juice
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   [3/4 oz. honey syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/honey-syrup-recipe/)
-   1/4 oz. allspice liqueur
-   2 dashes orange bitters

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients with ice, then strain into a glass filled with pebble-size crushed ice, and garnish.
