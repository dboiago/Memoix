Hopped grapefruit bitters add a new dimension to this Old Fashioned recipe from Bar Mash.

The Bar Mash Old Fashioned riff features a staff-selected Four Roses single-barrel bourbon, making for extra-special sipping.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. bourbon
-   [¼ oz. rich simple syrup (2:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/rich-simple-syrup-recipe/)
-   4 dashes Angostura bitters
-   2 dashes Bittermens hopped grapefruit bitters
-   1 strip orange peel
-   1 strip lemon peel

## Preparation

Muddle the orange peel with the simple syrup in an Old Fashioned glass. Add the bourbon and bitters and stir, then add a large chunk of ice and stir to chill. Twist a lemon peel over the drink to express the citrus oils and discard.
