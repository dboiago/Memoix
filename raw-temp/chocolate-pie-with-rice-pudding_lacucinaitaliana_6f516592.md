**Cooks in:** _3h 30mins \+ 2h_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   7/8 C. of all purpose flour
-   1/3 C. of butter
-   1/4 C. of sugar
-   2 TBSP. of almond flour
-   1 TBSP. of cocoa powder
-   1/2 egg
-   Salt
-   1 2/3 C. of whole milk
-   1/2 C. of Greek yogurt
-   1/2 C. of Originario rice (round rice)
-   3 TBSP. of sugar
-   1 1/2 TBSP. of Acacia honey
-   1 vanilla pod
-   Salt

### Method

1

Mix the flours, sugar and cocoa powder with a pinch of salt. Add the cold butter cut into small pieces and mix the ingredients until you obtain a coarse mixture. Quickly mix it with half an egg. Shape it into a ball and let it rest in the fridge for 1 hour in a bowl covered with plastic wrap.

2

 Roll out the pastry to 3 mm. thick and use it to coat a mold (ø 7 in.) with straight edges (h 3/4 in.). Place a circle of parchment paper above it, fill it with dried beans and bake at 350 °F for 20 minutes. Remove from the oven, remove the paper and beans and let cool.

3

Add the rice to lightly salted boiling water and cook it for 5 minutes from when it returns to a boil, letting it lose a bit of its starch. Drain and pour it into a saucepan with the cold milk, sugar and and a vanilla pod sliced in half lengthwise. As soon as it comes to a boil, turn the heat to low and cook for 45-60 minutes, stirring often so it does not stick to the pan. Cook until all the milk has absorbed and the rice has a creamy texture without being too dry. Then transfer it to a bowl and let it fully cool to room temperature.

4

Then mix it with the honey and yogurt and add aroma, if you like, with a teaspoon of rose water or orange blossom water. Fill the pie with the rice pudding and put it in the fridge for 1 hour. Decorate as desired just before serving with sliced pan-toasted almonds, shortbread crumbs, cocoa beans (chopped, peeled and pan-toasted) and rose petals.
