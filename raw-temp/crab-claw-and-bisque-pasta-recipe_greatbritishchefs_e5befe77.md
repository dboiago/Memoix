This super-quick crab [pasta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pasta-recipes) recipe combines top-quality ingredients such as lobster bisque and [crab](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/crab-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/crab-recipes) claws to create a stunning dish that takes just minutes to prepare. Flambéing the claws in pastis lends a wonderful aniseed flavour.

First published in 2019

### Metric

### Imperial

-   400g of [conchiglie pasta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pasta-recipes)
-   20g of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   20 [crab claws](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes), cooked and peeled
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   50ml of pastis
-   200g of [lobster bisque](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lobster-recipes)
-   10g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   sea aster, or tarragon, to garnish

1

Bring a large pan of water to the boil and season generously with salt. Drop the pasta into the water and cook for 8 minutes

-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   400g of [conchiglie pasta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pasta-recipes)

2

Meanwhile, place the olive oil in a pan over a medium heat and add the chilli, garlic and crab claws. Season with salt and cook for a few minutes, then flambé with the pastis

-   20g of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   20 [crab claws](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-crab-recipes), cooked and peeled
-   50ml of pastis

3

Add the lobster bisque and bring to a gentle simmer. When the pasta is cooked, drain and add to the pan with the crab

-   200g of [lobster bisque](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lobster-recipes)

4

Stir in the butter and sea aster (or tarragon), give everything a good stir and serve

-   10g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   sea aster, or tarragon, to garnish
