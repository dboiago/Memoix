Francesco Mazzei[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/francesco-mazzei) serves a vibrant [blood orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blood-orange-recipes) salad recipe with [burrata](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/burrata-recipes), a soft, rich Italian cheese made from [mozzarella](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/mozzarella-recipes) and cream, in this delicious antipasti recipe. This recipe is from Francesco Mazzei's book, [Recipes from Southern Italy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/features/mezzogiorno-francesco-mazzei-recipes-from-southern-italy-book-review) (Penguin Random House, November 2015).

First published in 2015

1

Leave the burrata at room temperature for 1 hour before starting to prepare the dish

-   1 [burrata](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/burrata-recipes)

2

Cut the blood oranges into thin slices crossways then squeeze the juice of the remaining half into a bowl and set aside

-   2 1/2 [blood oranges](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blood-orange-recipes), peeled, white pith removed, plus the juice of the extra 1/2

3

Place the radicchio, watercress and rocket leaves in a bowl of iced water and set aside. Drain and dry when ready to serve

-   30g of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)
-   30g of [radicchio](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicory-recipes)
-   30g of [watercress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/watercress-recipes)

4

Add the lemon juice and zest to the bowl of reserved orange juice, together with the olive oil, honey and some salt and pepper and whisk energetically until nice and creamy

-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zest only
-   2 tsp [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   3 tsp [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of extra virgin olive oil
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

5

To serve, open the burrata on to one side of a serving plate, arrange the drained leaves on the other side, then top the leaves with the orange slices and capers. Drizzle with the dressing and finish by scattering the chopped hazelnuts on top

-   30g of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes)
-   10 [hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), toasted and chopped
