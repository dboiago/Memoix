The classic BLT sandwich is given an extra flavour punch thanks to the cheddar and garlic mayonnaise in this recipe from [Nathan Outlaw](https://greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/nathan-outlaw). Serve this [bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/bacon-recipes) sandwich with a side of chips or a salad for a great lunchtime meal.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Sandwich

-   16 slices of [smoked streaky bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bacon-recipes)
-   12 slices of [bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
-   80g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), shredded or sliced
-   4 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), hard boiled and thinly sliced
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season
-   3 tbsp of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), chopped
-   4 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), thinly sliced
-   mixed herb leaves, such as flat-leaf parsley, basil and coriander

-   Food processor or blender

1

To begin making the sandwich, prepare the cheddar and garlic mayonnaise by placing the egg yolks into a food processor with the cheese, mustard, vinegar and garlic. Blend for 1 minute and then slowly pour in the oil whilst the processor is running

-   3 [free-range egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   2 tsp [English mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of white wine vinegar
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   500ml of sunflower oil
-   50g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated

2

When all the oil is incorporated stop the blender and add a little salt to taste. Set aside

-   [Cornish sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

3

For the sandwich, cook the bacon slices in a frying pan until crisp. Toast the slices of bread, with 4 slices toasted with shredded or sliced cheddar on top. Season the hard boiled eggs with salt to taste. Stir the chives into the cheddar and garlic mayonnaise

-   12 slices of [bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
-   16 slices of [smoked streaky bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bacon-recipes)
-   4 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season
-   3 tbsp of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes)
-   80g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), shredded or sliced

4

To assemble the sandwich, spread 4 slices of toast with a layer of mayonnaise. Top with half the egg, tomato, bacon and herbs. Spread both sides of 4 more slices of toast with mayonnaise and place on top. Cover with remaining egg, tomato, bacon, herbs, and finish with the slices of cheese on toast

-   4 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   mixed herb leaves

5

Secure sandwiches with picks and cut into triangles to serve
