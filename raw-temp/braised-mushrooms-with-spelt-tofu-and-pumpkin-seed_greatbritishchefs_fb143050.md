These braised [mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/mushroom-recipes) are paired with nutty spelt, a smooth [tofu](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tofu-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tofu-recipes) dressing, grilled baby gem [lettuce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lettuce-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lettuce-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lettuce-recipes) and a simple pumpkin seed [pesto](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pesto-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pesto-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pesto-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pesto-recipes) for a truly showstopping naturally [vegan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes) main. The combination of textures and earthy, savoury flavours come together to prove that vegan cooking has become a thrilling cuisine in its own right.

#### Spelt

-   80g of [spelt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spelt-recipes), use whole spelt if possible but pearled is fine

-   Food processor or blender
-   Hand blender

1

To begin, cook the spelt according to the packet instructions. Set aside and keep warm or gently reheat just before serving

-   80g of [spelt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spelt-recipes)

2

To make the tofu dressing, place all the ingredients into a small blender and blitz until smooth. Season to taste and set aside

-   1 knob of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), 8cm in length, peeled and grated
-   150g of [firm tofu](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tofu-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [tamari](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes), or soy sauce
-   1 tbsp of [tahini](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tahini-recipes)

3

To cook the mushrooms, heat the olive oil in a large frying pan. When hot, add the mushrooms, skin-side down, seasoning well with salt and pepper. Brown well then turn over. In a separate bowl, whisk together the miso paste and tamari with 4 tablespoons of water until smooth. Add this to the pan and gently simmer the mushrooms until they are cooked through (around 5–8 minutes)

-   1 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 tsp [white miso](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/miso-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [tamari](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes), or soy sauce
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   4 [large flat mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes)

4

While the mushrooms are cooking, make the pesto. Place all ingredients into a food processor with 2 tablespoons of water and pulse until combined but still a little chunky. Season to taste and set aside

-   50g of [pumpkin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pumpkin-recipes)
-   50g of extra virgin olive oil
-   20g of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), plus extra for garnish
-   10g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)

5

To cook the baby gem, heat a small frying pan over a high heat. Add the olive oil and, when hot, place the gem halves in the pan, cut-side down. Leave to cook for a few minutes until nice and golden brown on the flat side

-   1 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 [baby gem lettuce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes), halved

6

To serve, place a large spoonful of the tofu dressing in the bottom of each bowl. Divide the spelt between each bowl then add the mushrooms, along with any remaining pan juices. Finish with the pesto and baby gem halves. Garnish with parsley and parsley cress, if desired

-   parsley cress, (optional)
