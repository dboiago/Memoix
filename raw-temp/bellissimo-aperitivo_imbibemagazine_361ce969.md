A balance of sweet vermouth with dry fino sherry and Fernet-Branca.

Created for the Expo in Milan—a food and beverage–oriented world’s fair—this aperitivo cocktail balances the rich notes of sweet vermouth with dry fino sherry and the ornate bitterness of Fernet-Branca.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. Carpano Antica vermouth
-   1 ½ oz. fino sherry
-   2 tsp. Fernet-Branca
-   1 dash maraschino liqueur
-   1 dash Angostura bitters

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a mixing glass and fill with ice. Stir to chill, strain into a chilled glass, twist a lemon peel over the drink and use as garnish.
