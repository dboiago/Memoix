Scotch Lodge highlights the versatility of their namesake spirit.

With a 1,000-plus bottle selection and an approachable, whisky-centric cocktail menu, [Scotch Lodge](https://scotchlodge.com/) in Portland, Oregon, was one of our 2020 Imbibe 75 Places to Watch. Their cocktail menu includes originals and twists on classics, such as this smoky and spiced Brooklyn cocktail riff. 

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. mezcal (Scotch Lodge uses Los Vecinos)
-   1/2 oz. Scotch (Scotch Lodge uses Glenmorangie 10 year)
-   1/2 oz. Amer liqueur
-   1/4 oz. Bénédictine
-   1/4 oz. coriander-infused dry vermouth

## Preparation

Stir all of the ingredients with ice to chill, then strain into a glass.

**Coriander-Infused Dry Vermouth**Add 15 grams of crushed coriander seed to one 750 ml. bottle of dry vermouth into a sealed freezer bag and submerge in a very hot water bath for 15 minutes (or sous vide at 145 degrees F). Strain and rebottle for use.
