If you’ve ever dined at an Asian-fusion restaurant, chances are you’ve seen garlic noodles on the menu. It’s a seemingly simple dish of yellow noodles but it’s packed full of garlicky, buttery and umami flavors.

These are my kids’ favorite, and mine too. It’s the perfect side dish to the Viet-fusion seafood boil that is served at these restaurants.

If you want to make it at home, keep on reading.

![Asian Butter Garlic Noodles (Mi Xao Toi Bo)](https://i0.wp.com/vickypham.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/a8b9b-2024_03_23eosm508382.jpg)

## What You Need

To make these buttery garlic noodles, gather the following ingredients:

-   **Spaghetti or Egg Noodles:** I’m using [dried spaghetti](https://amzn.to/3TyVN77) in this recipe but you can also use thick egg noodles, the kind you commonly use for chow or lo mein. You can usually find these thick egg noodles in the refrigerated aisle of Asian supermarkets.
-   **Unsalted Butter:** Adds the bulk of the richness to the noodles. We aren’t skimping either. We are using a whole stick!
-   **Garlic:** The star of the show. We are using both fresh garlic and garlic powder. If you can get your hands on [granulated garlic,](https://amzn.to/3xb5ktK) which is a little coarser than powder, it’s even better.
-   **Sugar:** Balances out the savory flavors and enhances the overall taste. I’m using [granulated cane sugar.](https://amzn.to/4aapaE0)

![Spaghetti, sugar, soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, butter, garlic, parmesan cheese, garlic powder, salt (if needed) - Ingredients for Asian Garlic Noodles.](https://i0.wp.com/vickypham.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/dbb32-asiangarlicnoodles.jpg)

-   **Soy sauce:** You can use either [light soy sauce,](https://amzn.to/3VwRA6y) [liquid seasoning,](https://amzn.to/3VDwfZv) or [Maggi sauce.](https://amzn.to/3vpII8k) These add the salty umami flavors to the dish. For this recipe, I’m using Maggi sauce.
-   **Oyster Sauce:** Oyster sauce adds another level of Asian savory goodness. I religiously use [Lee Kum Kee Premium](https://amzn.to/4cyssCO) [oyster sauce](https://amzn.to/3TzsYHF) in my kitchen. If you’re vegetarian, you can substitute with this [vegetarian alternative.](https://amzn.to/3vrp5wu)
-   **Fish Sauce:** Adds another layer of salty Asian umami flavor. If you’re looking for a vegetarian alternative, you can simply omit and replace with salt, if needed. My choice of fish sauce is [Three Crabs brand from Viet Huong.](https://amzn.to/4cppBfj)
-   **Parmesan Cheese:** Last but not least, shredded parmesan is added at the end for that layer of cheesy flavor and creamy texture. Don’t add too much. Just enough to give it oomph and a light creamy coating on the noodles. But of course, grate more on top if you’re a parmesan enthusiast. I’m using a [zester/grater](https://amzn.to/49c7Gpm) to finely shred the cheese.

## How to Make It

**Step 1: Cook the Noodles**

Cook the egg noodles or spaghetti according to the package instructions. Drain, rinse lightly, and set aside.

![Cooking Spaghetti for Asian Garlic Noodles](https://i0.wp.com/vickypham.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/6af98-2024_03_23eosm508341.jpg)

**Step 2: Prepare the Sauce**

In a pan over medium heat, melt the butter. Once melted, add the minced garlic. Sauté the garlic until softened and fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in sugar, soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, and garlic powder. Mix well to combine. Give it a taste and adjust with salt, if needed.

![Melting butter for Asian garlic noodles](https://i0.wp.com/vickypham.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/83a8b-meltingbutterasiangarlicnoodles.jpg)

![Making sauce for Asian garlic noodles](https://i0.wp.com/vickypham.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/30cd8-2024_03_23eosm508323.jpg)

**Step 3: Combine Everything Together**

Add the cooked noodles to the pan, followed by the parmesan cheese. Toss everything together until cheese is melted (about 2 minutes). Turn off heat and serve.

### Description

Inspired by the classic Asian-fusion restaurant’s garlic noodles, this dish is packed with garlicky goodness, a touch of sweetness, and umami richness. It’s perfect as a quick meal and a favorite of picky eaters, big and small.

* * *

-   16 oz [dried spaghetti](https://amzn.to/3TyVN77)
-   1 stick unsalted butter (8 tablespoons)
-   3 tablespoons fresh garlic (minced)
-   1/2 tablespoon [sugar](https://amzn.to/4aapaE0)
-   1 tablespoon [Maggi sauce](https://amzn.to/3vpII8k) (see notes)
-   1 tablespoon [oyster sauce](https://amzn.to/3TzsYHF) (see notes)
-   1 tablespoon [fish sauce](https://amzn.to/4cppBfj) (see notes)
-   1 teaspoon [garlic powder](https://amzn.to/3xb5ktK)
-   3 tablespoons grated parmesan cheese

* * *

1.  **Cook the egg noodles or spaghetti** according to the package instructions. Drain, rinse lightly, and set aside.
2.  **Melt butter** in a medium pan**.**
3.  **Add garlic** and sauté until softened and fragrant, about 30 seconds.
4.  **Add seasonings:** Stir in sugar, soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, and garlic powder. Mix well to combine. Adjust with salt, if needed.
5.  **Add the cooked noodles to the pan, followed by the parmesan cheese.** Toss everything together until cheese is melted (about 2 minutes). Turn off heat and serve.

-   Prep Time: 5 minutes
-   Cook Time: 15 minutes
