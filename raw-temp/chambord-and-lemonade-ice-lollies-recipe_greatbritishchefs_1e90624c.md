The perfect [summer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/summer-recipes) treat, Sally's Chambord and lemonade ice lollies make deliciously easy frozen [cocktails](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocktail-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocktail-recipes). A boozy twist on a [peach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/peach-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/peach-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/peach-recipes) Melba, fresh raspberries and peaches are frozen within a raspberry liqueur and lemonade ice lolly.

### Imperial

#### Chambord and lemonade ice lollies

-   1 [peach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peach-recipes), halved and stone removed
-   12 [raspberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raspberry-recipes), washed
-   80ml of Chambord, or other raspberry liqueur
-   300ml of lemonade

1

Cut the peach into thin slices and halve the raspberries. Divide between 6 ice lolly moulds

2

Mix the Chambord with the lemonade in a jug and pour into the moulds before adding the lolly sticks

3

Place in the freezer for at least 6 hours, or preferably overnight, to allow the lollies to freeze solid

4

When completely frozen, gently warm the moulds to loosen and release the lollies (either rub gently between your hands or run under warm water). Enjoy!
