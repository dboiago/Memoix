The classic Margarita gets a savory twist with the addition of fresh-pressed celery juice and a pinch of salt.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. blanco tequila
-   3/4 oz. fresh celery juice
-   1/2 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1/2 oz. honey
-   2 drops salt water (1:1)

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients and shake. Strain into an ice-filled glass and garnish.
