[Butternut squash](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butternut-squash-recipes "Butternut squash") is the star of the show in this colourful caramelised butternut squash tostada from Unilever chef Dana Cohen. It has a crispy kale, sage and pumpkin seed topping and is served with a mayonnaise and feta sauce. This recipe was created as part of Unilever’s 2024 Future Menus trend report (which can be downloaded [here](https://www.unileverfoodsolutions.co.uk/chef-inspiration/future-menus-2024-report.html?utm_source=GBC&utm_medium=Digital_Banners&utm_campaign=FutureMenus_2024)), to showcase the Irresistible Vegetables trend. This trend is all about putting vegetables front and centre on the plate to add colour and vibrancy to a dish, just as the butternut squash does in this recipe.

First published in 2024

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Pitas

-   10 ancient grain pittas, toasted until crisp
-   175g of [green apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), brunoise

1

Preheat the oven to 230°C

2

Toss the butternut squash with hot honey, then add the oil, thyme, bouillon, garlic, cinnamon, and smoked paprika

-   1.3kg [butternut squash](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butternut-squash-recipes), cut into 1 in cubes
-   15ml of [hot honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   48g of extra virgin olive oil
-   2g of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), chopped
-   10g of vegetable bouillon powder
-   8g of garlic powder
-   1g of [cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1g of [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)

3

Spread on a parchment-lined sheet pan and roast until golden brown and caramelised – about 25 mins – tossing occasionally

4

Deep-fry the sage and kale leaves. Remove from oil and season with salt

-   20 [sage leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sage-recipes)
-   100g of [kale](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kale-recipes), roughly chopped
-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying, as needed

5

In a medium bowl, toss together the sage, kale and pepitas then break up to a crumble texture. Add enough salsa macha to coat

-   120g of [pepitas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pumpkin-recipes), roasted and chopped
-   [salsa macha](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-oil-recipes), as needed

7

To assemble the tostadas, spread the whipped feta on the toasted pita. Top each with butternut squash

-   10 ancient grain pittas, toasted until crisp

8

Sprinkle the sage crumble on top. Garnish with brunoised apples

-   175g of [green apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), brunoise
