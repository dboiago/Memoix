This brown [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes "Butter") sea bass dish is a brilliant way to make the most of tangerines’ floral fragrance. It comes together very quickly, and is perfect for a light supper. The baked [sea bass](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sea-bass-recipes "Sea bass") can be eaten with baby [lettuce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes "Lettuce") and [herbs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fresh-herbs-recipes "Fresh herb") or soft corn tortillas. The spicy tangerine dipping sauce can be made up to 2 days ahead.

_Extracted from Mezcla by Ixta Belfrage (Ebury Press, £26) All photography by Yuki Sugiura._ 

First published in 2022

Ixta says: _'Don’t be put off by the longish ingredients list and method. Read through it once and you’ll realise this recipe is actually very straightforward and requires very little hands-on cooking. This is a great interactive sharing dish; stuff pieces of fish into the lettuce leaves (fresh corn tortillas would be great, too), top with the herbs and dipping sauce and let it trickle down your wrists as you eat with your hands. You’ll need four tangerines and two limes in total for this recipe.'_

## Ingredients

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Sea bass

-   1 [sea bass](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-sea-bass-recipes), (around 800g, and at room temperature)
-   2 sprigs of [rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes)
-   40g of [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), (about 4 thin ones)
-   1/4 [fine salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### Citrus butter

-   40g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   30g of olive oil
-   1 1/2 tbsp of tangerine juice
-   2 tsp [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), skin left on and crushed with the side of a knife
-   1 tsp [fine salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes) to taste, (about 20 twists of the mill)

#### Tangerine dipping sauce

-   1 1/2 tsp [pul biber chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), (use red bell pepper flakes if you don’t like heat)
-   1 [small garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   1/4 tsp [finely grated ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   3/4 [tomato purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [fine salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 1/2 tbsp of mild olive oil
-   40g of [datterini tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), or other very ripe cherry tomatoes, very finely chopped
-   4 tbsp of tangerine juice
-   2 tsp [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes) to season, or light soy sauce

#### To serve

-   1 [baby gem lettuce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes), leaves separated
-   5g of [fresh mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes)
-   5g of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   5g of [basil leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes)
-   4 [lime wedges](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), to serve

## Method

1

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 6

2

Pat the sea bass with kitchen towel to make sure it’s as dry as possible

-   1 [sea bass](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-sea-bass-recipes), (around 800g, and at room temperature)

3

Transfer the sea bass to a 33 x 26cm non-stick high-sided baking tray, arranging it diagonally, so it fits. The tray shouldn’t be much bigger than the fish, otherwise the citrus butter will burn. Season the cavity with ¼ teaspoon of fine salt, then stuff with the rosemary and spring onions

-   1/4 [fine salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   2 sprigs of [rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes)
-   40g of [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), (about 4 thin ones)

4

Put all the ingredients for the citrus butter into a small saucepan and place on a medium heat until the butter has melted. Pour over and around the fish

-   40g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   30g of olive oil
-   1 1/2 tbsp of tangerine juice
-   2 tsp [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), skin left on and crushed with the side of a knife
-   1 tsp [fine salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes) to taste, (about 20 twists of the mill)

5

Bake for 14 minutes (or longer if your fish is larger than 800g)

6

Baste the fish well and turn the oven to the highest grill setting. Grill on the top shelf of the oven for 4–6 minutes, or until the skin begins to bubble and crisp. Keep a close eye, as some grills are much hotter than others

8

Heat the oil in a small saucepan on a medium heat for 1 minute, then pour the hot oil into the mortar

-   1 1/2 tbsp of mild olive oil

9

Stir together, then add the chopped tomatoes, tangerine juice, lime juice and fish sauce

-   40g of [datterini tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), or other very ripe cherry tomatoes, very finely chopped
-   4 tbsp of tangerine juice
-   2 tsp [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes) to season, or light soy sauce

10

Once cooked, transfer the sea bass to a large platter with the browned citrus butter. Discard the garlic skins

11

Serve with the lettuce and soft herbs to make parcels with, and the dipping sauce and lime wedges alongside
