It is always handy to have a great [chocolate brownie](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-brownie-recipes) recipe up your sleeve. In [Frances Atkins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/frances-atkins)' version, the soft, chewy brownies are speckled with pieces of [white chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/white-chocolate-recipes). Cut into small cubes and serve with tea or coffee for a fabulous afternoon treat.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   160g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   200g of [plain chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)
-   225g of caster sugar
-   3 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   55g of plain flour
-   55g of [cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes)
-   100g of [white chocolate buttons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/white-chocolate-recipes)
-   100g of [hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes), chopped
-   icing sugar for dusting

-   Swiss roll tin

1

Preheat the oven to 170ºC/Gas mark 3

3

Whisk together the sugar and eggs. Add the melted butter and plain chocolate and stir well to combine

-   225g of caster sugar
-   3 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

4

Add the sieved flour and cocoa, white chocolate buttons and chopped nuts and pour on to a greased and lined Swiss roll tin. Bake for 20–30 minutes

-   55g of plain flour
-   55g of [cocoa powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocoa-recipes)
-   100g of [white chocolate buttons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/white-chocolate-recipes)
-   100g of [hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes)

5

Once cooked, allow to cool slightly, then cut into small squares and dust with icing sugar

-   icing sugar for dusting
