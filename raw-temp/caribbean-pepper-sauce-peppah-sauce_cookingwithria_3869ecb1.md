Hot sauce, known as pepper sauce in the Caribbean is a must have in our home and most homes. Even better is home made pepper sauce; we either have our own home made pepper sauce or pepper sauce from another family member or friend because no two hot sauce is alike. At least I have never tasted two pepper sauce that tastes identical. That is the beauty of pepper sauce, the variations are endless, exciting, interesting and always satisfying.

This is my Mum’s –Indra- secret pepper sauce recipe that we have devoured over the years.  I recall pouring it on microwaved, sliced hot dogs (the entire package) and enjoying with my sister KK as an after school snack, while sitting on the stairs of our home. A memory that still lives on happily in my mind.

Mummy’s pepper sauce goes on everything and anything. It’s feel good, burn your mouth, make you teary eyed good.

I am sure you hot sauce and pepper sauce lovers will enjoy it. Do give it a try and let me know how it turns out.

Great with [BBQ Chicken](https://cookingwithria.com/2019/01/oven-bbq-chicken-with-rias-homemade-bbq-sauce-recipes.html), [fried](https://cookingwithria.com/2020/01/trinidad-fried-aloo.html) [aloo](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/aloo/), [pelau](https://cookingwithria.com/2011/07/trinidad-pelau.html), [holiday ham](https://cookingwithria.com/2014/12/rias-simple-trini-christmas-is-de-best-holiday-ham.html) and [home made bread](https://cookingwithria.com/2011/09/rias-homemade-bread.html) too!

[![](https://cookingwithria.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Pepper-Sauce-Hot-sauce-copy-150x150.png)](https://cookingwithria.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Pepper-Sauce-Hot-sauce-copy.png)

-   ½ lb red hot peppers habanero, scotch bonnet, cherry pepper, wiri wiri pepper
-   ½ [carrot](https://www.target.com/p/organic-carrots-1lb-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-85760297?aflt=plt#lnk=sametab) small
-   6 cloves [garlic](https://amzn.to/2OiDUcB)
-   2 tbs [yellow mustard](https://amzn.to/34jrnef)
-   4 leaves [culantro](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/culantro/) [bandhania](https://cookingwithria.com/glossary/bandhania/)
-   1 ½ cups [vinegar](https://www.target.com/p/white-distilled-vinegar-128oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-54633873?aflt=plt) or enough to cover
-   1 tsp [salt](https://amzn.to/37sGeoL) or to taste

-   Chop pepper and carrot into chunks large enough for the blender.
    
-   Add all the ingredients to the blender and pulse until smooth. Add more salt if required.
