This [Chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chocolate-recipes) Rice Krispie cakes recipe harks back to a childhood favourite, but [Josh Eggleton](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/josh-eggleton) takes them to another level with the addition of orange for a little zesty flavour, and popping candy for an unexpected surprise. No baking required, these can be stirred together in moments for a quick snack or [children's party](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/childrens-party-recipes) treat.

First published in 2016

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Line a baking tray (32 x 23 x 5cm) with baking paper

2

Place the chocolate in a bowl and heat on a low power in the microwave - stirring every 45 seconds until completely melted

-   250g of [dark cooking chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)

3

Stir in the crème fraîche, zest of 2 oranges and the Rice Krispies. Allow to cool slightly

-   100g of crème fraîche
-   2 [oranges](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zest only
-   200g of [Rice Krispies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes)

4

Gently stir in the popping candy. Pour onto the lined tray

-   15g of popping candy

5

Use the back of a large spoon to spread evenly onto the tray and while still soft sprinkle with the zest of the 3rd orange and the grated white chocolate

-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zest only
-   20g of [white chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/white-chocolate-recipes), grated

6

Place in the fridge to set for a minimum of 2 hours before cutting into bite-size squares and serving
