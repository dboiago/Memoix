Beer lends extra depth to a classic cocktail.

In the Americano Shandy recipe, a hoppy lager lends extra depth to the [classic combo](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe-americano/) of bitter Campari and sweet vermouth.

## Ingredients

Yield:2

-   1 oz. Campari
-   1 oz. sweet vermouth
-   12 oz. lager

## Preparation

Divide the Campari and vermouth between 2 glasses. Add ice and top up each glass with the beer, stirring gently to mix.
