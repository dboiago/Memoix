Matty Eggleston, beverage director at Monterey’s Stokes Adobe (one of our [favorite places to drink](https://imbibemagazine.com/where-to-drink-monterey-california/) in the Central Coast California town), recommends batching this cocktail to enjoy as a [freezer martini](https://imbibemagazine.com/drink-of-the-week-freezer-martinis/) (see below). With a base of tequila, a couple of dashes of mezcal, two types of vermouth, and a bit of amaro, Eggleston says “all the flavors and botanicals sort of roll over the palate in waves.”

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. blanco tequila
-   1/2 oz. extra dry vermouth
-   1/2 oz. blanc vermouth
-   2 dashes mezcal (see Note)
-   [2 dashes Amaro Brucato Chaparral](https://imbibemagazine.com/drink-of-the-week-brucato-chaparral-amaro/)
-   lime peel

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a mixing glass filled with lots of ice. Strain into a frozen glass. Garnish with a floating nasturtium leaf. Squeeze a lime peel (peel side out) over the drink, dripping some juice both on the leaf and in the drink. Discard the lime peel.

**Note**For the mezcal, Eggleston says any single-varietal that isn’t espadin will work.

**Freezer Martini**Combine 375ml dry vermouth, 375ml blanco vermouth, 64 oz. tequila, and 18 oz. filtered water in a bottle and store in the freezer. Add dashes of amaro and mezcal to order.
