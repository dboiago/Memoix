An ultra-refreshing combo of cucumber, mint, lime and your choice of gin or vodka.

In this cocktail from Dilworth Tasting Room, cucumber, lime and mint make for an ultra-refreshing trifecta of flavors.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. vodka or gin
-   ¾ oz. fresh cucumber juice
-   ¾ oz. fresh lime juice
-   [¾ oz. mint syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/mint-syrup-recipe/)

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a shaker with ice and shake to chill. Strain into a chilled glass and garnish.
