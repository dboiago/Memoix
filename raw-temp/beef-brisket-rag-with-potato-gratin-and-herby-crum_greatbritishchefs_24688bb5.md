In this recipe, [brisket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-brisket-recipes "Beef brisket") is slow cooked in a rich red wine sauce with a touch of Sarson's Malt Vinegar for acidity and balance. Once tender it is served with a creamy potato [gratin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gratin-recipes "Gratin") and sprinkled with a toasted seed and herby crumb. The perfect comfort food that works beautifully all year round.

First published in 2025

### Imperial

#### For the brisket ragù

-   1kg [beef brisket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely diced
-   2 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and diced
-   2 [celery sticks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), diced
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   200ml of malt vinegar
-   200ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   400g of [tin of chopped tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [tomato purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   500ml of beef stock
-   2 [bay leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bay-leaf-recipes)
-   1 sprig of [rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes)
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

#### For the gratin

-   1kg [russet potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled
-   300ml of whole milk
-   300ml of cream
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   150g of [Gruyère](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gruyere-recipes)
-   150g of [brie](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheese-recipes), thinly sliced
-   2 tbsp of [salted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

#### For the herby crumble

-   1 slice of [sourdough bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), thick, torn into small pieces
-   2 tbsp of mixed seeds, such as pumpkin and sunflower
-   2 tbsp of [walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/walnut-recipes), roughly chopped
-   1 [small bunch of parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of oregano leaves
-   [zest of 1 lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

2

Preheat the oven to 140°C

3

In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat the olive oil and sear the brisket on all sides until golden. Remove and set aside

-   2 tbsp of olive oil

4

In the same pan, sauté the onion, carrot and celery over medium heat for 10 minutes until softened and beginning to brown. Add garlic and cook for 2 minutes more

-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely diced
-   2 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and diced
-   2 [celery sticks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), diced
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed

5

Deglaze the pan with the vinegar, scraping up any sticky bits from the base

-   200ml of malt vinegar

6

Pour in the red wine and reduce by half

-   200ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)

8

Add herbs, return the brisket to the pan, cover, and cook in the oven for 5–6 hours, or until the brisket is tender. Once cooked, shred with forks into the sauce

-   2 [bay leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bay-leaf-recipes)
-   1 sprig of [rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes)

9

For the gratin, preheat the oven to 175°C fan

10

Thinly slice the potatoes using a mandolin

-   1kg [russet potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled

11

Add the potatoes, milk, cream, garlic, salt and pepper to a saucepan. Bring to a simmer, then cook over a low heat for around 10 minutes

-   300ml of whole milk
-   300ml of cream
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

12

Remove from the heat then add the grated Gruyère and stir until fully melted. Pour the potato mix into a greased baking dish then arrange the brie and butter over the top

-   150g of [Gruyère](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gruyere-recipes)
-   150g of [brie](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheese-recipes), thinly sliced
-   2 tbsp of [salted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

13

Bake for 40 minutes until browned and bubbling 

14

For the crumble, toast the bread, seeds and walnuts in a dry pan until golden

-   1 slice of [sourdough bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), thick, torn into small pieces
-   2 tbsp of mixed seeds, such as pumpkin and sunflower
-   2 tbsp of [walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/walnut-recipes), roughly chopped

16

To serve, spoon the gratin into a serving bowl then top with a generous spoon of the ragù, herby crumble and black pepper

-   [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes) to taste
