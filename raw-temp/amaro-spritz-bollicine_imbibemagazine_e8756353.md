If you like your spritzes especially dry and bitter, try this recipe combining two types of Italian amari with sparkling wine and bitters.

## Ingredients

-   ¾ oz. Amaro Montenegro
-   ¾ oz. Cynar
-   3-4 dashes grapefruit bitters
-   3 oz prosecco, or other sparkling wine

## Preparation

Combine both kinds of amari and bitters in a mixing glass with ice. Stir to chill, then strain into a wine glass over fresh ice. Top with sparkling wine
