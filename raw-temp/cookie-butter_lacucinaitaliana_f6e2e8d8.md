## Great spread on cookies or as an ice cream topping.

### List of Ingredients

-   1/3 LB. of speculoos cookies
-   1/3 C. of milk
-   3 OZ. of white chocolate
-   2 1/2 TBSP. of honey
-   3 1/2 TBSP. of sunflower seed oil
-   1 1/2 TBSP. of raw cane sugar

### Method

1

Blend the cookies until they become a fine powder. 

2

Combine with the chopped white chocolate. Heat the milk with the honey and the cane sugar. 

3

Once it begins to boil, pour it over the chocolate and cookies and combine well, then add in the oil.

4

Let the cream set before distributing it into jars.
