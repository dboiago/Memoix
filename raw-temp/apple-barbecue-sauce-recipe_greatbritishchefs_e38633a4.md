[Adam Gray](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/adam-gray) serves this tangy apple barbecue sauce alongside [pork belly](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pork-belly-recipes) at Skylon, but the sauce also works well with chicken and seafood. Start work on this barbecue sauce recipe a few days before a weekend [barbecue](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/barbecue-recipes) to get ahead.

#### BBQ sauce

-   2 [Braeburn apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), peeled and 2cm diced
-   2 [large onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely diced
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   1/2 tsp [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   100g of brown sugar, dark soft
-   4 tbsp of cider vinegar
-   100ml of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   300ml of [tomato ketchup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   150ml of chicken stock
-   50ml of extra virgin rapeseed oil

-   Blender
-   Fine chinoise

1

Heat the rapeseed oil in a thick bottomed saucepan and fry the onion and garlic until just softened with no colour

-   50ml of extra virgin rapeseed oil
-   2 [large onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)

2

Add the apple and chilli and fry for a further minute. Stir in the sugar, soy sauce, chicken stock, vinegar and tomato ketchup

-   2 [Braeburn apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   100g of brown sugar
-   100ml of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of cider vinegar
-   300ml of [tomato ketchup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   150ml of chicken stock

3

Simmer gently until the apples are softened. Transfer to a blender and blitz until smooth. Pass through a fine strainer. The sauce can be served warm or at room temperature and can be stored in the fridge for up to 4 days
