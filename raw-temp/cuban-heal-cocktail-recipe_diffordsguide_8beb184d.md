### Glass:

Serve in a [Coupe glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

2 1⁄2 fl oz

[Light white rum (charcoal-filtered 1-4 years old)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/2224/don-q-cristal-rum)

1⁄4 fl oz

[Cynar or other carciofo amaro](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/859/cynar)

1⁄4 fl oz

[Pallini Limoncello liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/354/pallini-limoncello)

1⁄4 fl oz

Honey sugar syrup

2 dash

Peychaud's or other Creole-style bitters

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [COUPE GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of lemon zest twist.

### How to make:

3.  [STIR](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-stir-a-cocktail) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into chilled glass.

### Garnish:

5.  [EXPRESS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1269/cocktail-garnishes/citrus-zest-twists) lemon zest twist over the cocktail and use as garnish.

## Review:

Honey-ed and initially sweet with herbal spirity rum notes adding depth.

[View readers' comments](#comments)

## History:

Created in 2008 by Matthew Keegan at Blanch House, Brighton, England. The ingredients were chosen due to their historically being believed to be remedies.
