Jun Tanaka serves [cavolo nero](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cavolo-nero-recipes) with a punchy [hazelnut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/hazelnut-recipes) pesto in this stunning side dish, a great way to elevate this dark leafy veg to new heights. The [pesto](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pesto-recipes) would work equally well with other iron-rich leafy veg such as savoy cabbage or kale, if preferred.

First published in 2018

-   Food processor or blender

1

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4

2

Begin by toasting the hazelnuts. Place the nuts on a baking tray in an even single layer and toast in the oven for 5 minutes. Check, stir and toast for a further 3 minutes until golden brown

-   100g of [blanched hazelnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/hazelnut-recipes)

3

Allow the nuts to cool then transfer to a blender with the garlic, Parmesan and hazelnut oil. Blitz to a coarse pesto, taste, and season with salt and pepper

-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1/2 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   100ml of hazelnut oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   20g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated (use a similar hard cheese made with vegetarian rennet, if preferred)

4

Blanch the cavolo nero in salted boiling water for 3 minutes

-   400g of [cavolo nero](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cavolo-nero-recipes), trimmed, thick stalks cut out

5

Drain and toss with the pesto and a squeeze of lemon. Season to taste

-   [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
