Inspired by the [Zombie cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe-zombie/), this petite version is “slightly less boozy than the original, but will still drop an elephant at 50 paces,” says The Penrose bartender Luis Serrano. Applejack throws a welcome curveball into the mix.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. applejack
-   1 oz. passion fruit purée
-   3/4 oz. Plantation Stiggins' Fancy pineapple rum
-   3/4 oz. Jamaican rum
-   1/2 oz. orange liqueur
-   1/2 oz. falernum
-   1/2 oz. grenadine
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice
-   2 dashes Angostura bitters

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a shaker filled with ice. Shake to chill and strain into the mug. Fill with crushed ice. Garnish.
