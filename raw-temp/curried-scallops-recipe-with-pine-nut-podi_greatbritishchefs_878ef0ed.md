This stunning curried [scallops](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/scallop-recipes) recipe from Atul Kochhar is served with a caramelised ginger and [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/apple-recipes) purée, pickled celeriac and [pine nut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pine-nut-recipes) podi for a medley of sweet, sour and spicy flavour.

First published in 2017

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Curried scallops

-   12 [hand-dived scallops](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-scallop-recipes), corals removed, patted dry
-   1 tsp [sesame seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sesame-seeds-recipes), black and white, toasted
-   70g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [micro cress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cress-recipes), to garnish

#### Caramelised apple and ginger purée

-   10 [Cox's apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), peeled and cored
-   200g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), chopped
-   150ml of cider vinegar
-   caster sugar, to taste

#### Benares curry powder

-   1 [cinnamon stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes), 5cm in length
-   10 [green cardamom pods](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)
-   10 [cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)
-   4 [dried chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   2 tsp [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes)
-   1 tsp [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 1/2 [Kashmiri chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 1/2 [dried mango powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mango-recipes)
-   1 1/2 tsp [turmeric powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)

#### Pickled celariac

-   3 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   3 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   5 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), split lengthways
-   600g of [celeriac](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celeriac-recipes), peeled and cut julienne
-   50g of caster sugar
-   4 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### Pine nut podi

-   100g of [pine nuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pine-nut-recipes)
-   5 whole dried kashmiri chillies
-   15 [curry leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/curry-leaf-recipes)
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 tsp [turmeric powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

-   Food processor or blender
-   Squeezy bottle
-   Spice grinder

1

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4

2

Start by making the apple purée. Chop the apples into 8 pieces and roast for approximately 1 1/2 hours, stirring every 20 minutes until very dark brown (almost black)

-   10 [Cox's apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), peeled and cored

3

Allow to cool slightly, then blend in a powerful blender with the ginger and vinegar until very smooth. Add warm water to achieve the desired consistency and add sugar to taste. Pass through a sieve if needed, then transfer to a squeezy bottle

-   200g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), chopped
-   150ml of cider vinegar
-   caster sugar, to taste

6

Next, pickle the celeriac. Heat the vegetable oil in a pan add the cumin seeds. Cook until they crackle then add the green chillies and cook for 1 minute

-   3 tbsp of vegetable oil
-   3 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   5 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), split lengthways

7

Add the remaining ingredients and cook over a high heat for 5 minutes until the celeriac has softened but still has a little crunch. Remove from the pan and allow to cool

-   600g of [celeriac](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celeriac-recipes), peeled and cut julienne
-   50g of caster sugar
-   4 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

8

Preheat the oven to 140°C/gas mark 1

9

To make the pine nut podi, mix all the ingredients together and toast for 10 minutes until the pine nuts are golden brown. Allow to cool then pulse in a food processor until lightly broken up

-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   100g of [pine nuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pine-nut-recipes)
-   5 whole dried kashmiri chillies
-   15 [curry leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/curry-leaf-recipes)
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 tsp [turmeric powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)

10

Once all your components are ready, place a non stick pan on a high heat until smoking hot. Add the vegetable oil and cook the scallops for 2–3 minutes until a nice brown colour

11

Turn the scallops over, add 2 tbsp of tthe curry powder and finish with a knob of butter and a squeeze of lemon juice. Place 3 on a plate or slate and add a mound of the pickled celeriac next to it. Add a squeeze of the apple purée, a spoonful of podi and finish with the cress. Serve immediately

Combining the flavours of north, east and south India with the finest British produce, Atul Kochhar was the first ever Indian chef to receive a Michelin star in 2001.
