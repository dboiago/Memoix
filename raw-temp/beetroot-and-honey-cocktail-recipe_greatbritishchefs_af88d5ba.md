### Imperial

#### Beetroot rum (makes 1 bottle)

-   750ml of [rum](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rum-cocktail-recipes), Adam uses Havana Club 3 Year Old
-   1 [beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes), thinly sliced

#### Honey syrup (makes 180ml)

-   120ml of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   60ml of water

-   Ice
-   Boston glass and shaker

1

Begin by infusing the rum. Place the rum and beetroot into a container and leave, covered, at room temperature for 6 hours to infuse. Strain and reserve in the bottle

-   1 [beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes), thinly sliced
-   750ml of [rum](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rum-cocktail-recipes), Adam uses Havana Club 3 Year Old

2

To make the honey syrup simply combine the honey and water, mixing well. Reserve in a bottle

-   60ml of water
-   120ml of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)

3

Fill a cocktail glass with ice to chill

4

To make the cocktail, carefully measure all the ingredients into the tin of a Boston shaker. Add a large scoop of ice

-   1 [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   5ml of cassis
-   10ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   30ml of [rum](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rum-cocktail-recipes), infused with beetroot (see above)
-   20ml of [rum](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rum-cocktail-recipes), Adam uses Havana 7 Year Old
-   30ml of [honey syrup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes), (see above)

5

Shake hard for 15 seconds, then strain into the empty half of the shaker. Throw away the ice and shake again without ice for 15-20 seconds. Throw the ice out of the glass and strain the cocktail though a tea strainer into the glass

6

To garnish, dust the top of the cocktail with beetroot powder, then place the red amaranth in the centre of the drink

-   [beetroot powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes)
-   red amaranth
