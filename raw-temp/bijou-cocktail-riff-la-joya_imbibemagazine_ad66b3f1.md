Mezcal brings new depth to the classic.

For this [Bijou cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/bijou-cocktail-recipe/) riff, [The Standby](https://www.standbydetroit.com/)‘s Joe Robinson reached for mezcal and PX sherry to take the classic in an extra-rich direction. “The deep fruit of the PX sherry stands up to the intensity of the mezcal, resulting in a smoky, medicinal, and hard-hitting cocktail,” Robinson says of the drink.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. mezcal
-   3/4 oz. green Chartreuse
-   3/4 oz. PX sherry
-   6 dashes Bittermens Hopped Grapefruit Bitters

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a mixing glass, add ice, and stir. Strain into a chilled glass.
