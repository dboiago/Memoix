A resurrected treasure from The Savoy Cocktail Book.

Originally featuring Kina Lillet, the Campden classic appears in _[The Savoy Cocktail Book](https://www.amazon.com/Savoy-Cocktail-Book-Harry-Craddock/dp/1614278377/?tag=imbibmagaz-20)_ by Harry Craddock. To modernize the drink, Lobster Bar in Hong Kong employs Lillet Blanc for its similarities to the original ingredient.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. dry gin
-   ⅓ oz. Cointreau
-   ⅓ oz. Lillet Blanc

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a mixing glass with ice, then stir to chill. Strain into a chilled glass and garnish.
