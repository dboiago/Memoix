**Cooks in:** _1h 30mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _10_

### List of Ingredients

-   2/3 LB. of eggs
-   1/2 LB. of 50% dark chocolate
-   1/2 LB. of almond paste
-   3/4 STICK of butter, plus extra
-   1/4 C. of flour, plus extra
-   1 1/2 TSP. of baking powder
-   Powdered sugar

### Method

1

Chop of the almond paste as finely as possible; using a whisk, dilute it by adding in a few eggs at a time. Once everything has been thoroughly combined, continue to whisk briskly until the eggs are fluffy.

2

Melt the chocolate together with the butter over a bain-marie; let it cool until it reaches 105°F, then incorporate it into the egg mixture; finally, incorporate the flour and the baking powder. Butter and flour a springform pan (9 1/2-inch diameter); pour in the batter and bake at 350°F for about 20 minutes.

3

Ensure that it has baked all the way through by inserting a toothpick: it should come out dry. Remove the torte from the oven and let it cool; remove the springform pan and serve dusted with plenty of powdered sugar.
