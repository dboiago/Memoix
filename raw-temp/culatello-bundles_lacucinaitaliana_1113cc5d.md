## Made in Lombardy and Emilia Romagna, culatello is one of Italy's finest cured meats. Filled with an olive-caper-mayonnaise mixture, culatello bundles are a perfect bite size appetizer.

**Cooks in:** _45mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   7 OZ. of carrots
-   12 slices culatello or other cured ham
-   4 1/2 OZ. of mayonnaise
-   4 OZ. of green olives, pitted
-   2 OZ. of celery
-   1 TBSP. of salt packed capers, rinsed
-   1 large egg
-   salt

### Method

1

Peel, dice and boil the carrots. Drain when cooked, cool and transfer to a bowl. 

2

Boil the egg for exactly 10 minutes from when the water comes to a boil. Run under water to cool and peel. Finely dice the celery. Coarsely chop the olives with 1 Tbsp. capers.

3

Add the olives, capers, diced celery and mayonnaise to the carrots and mix. Press the hard-boiled egg through a sieve and add this to the mixture; mix everything together and season with a pinch of salt.

4

Line the inside of a small ladle with a slice of _culatello_ or other fine cured ham; fill with a spoonful of the mixture and fold the ham over to close. Flip the onto a plate, so the seam of the bundle is on the bottom. Repeat with the remaining 11 slices of culatello, using up all the filling. Garnish the bundles and serve.
