## Ingredients

-   3 cups rolled oats
-   1/3 cup wheat germ
-   ½ cup silvered almonds
-   1 pinch ground nutmeg
-   1 ½ teaspoons ground cinnamon
-   ½ cup apple juice, non-sweetened
-   ½ cup [molasses](https://amzn.to/3YtAwQw "molasses")
-   1 teaspoon vanilla extract
-   1 cup dried mixed fruit
-   1 cup quartered dried apricots
-   Cooking spray

## Directions

-   Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Prepare two cookie sheets with cooking spray.
-   In a large bowl, combine oats, wheat germ, almonds, cinnamon and nutmeg. In a separate bowl, mix apple juice, molasses and extract. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients, stirring to coat. Spread mixture onto baking sheets. Bake for 30 minutes in preheated oven, stirring mixture every  10 to 15 minutes.  Or until granola has a golden brown colour. Let cool. Stir in dried fruit. Store in an airtight container.

## Why Homemade Granola Mix is the Best Choice

Homemade granola mix stands out as the best choice for several compelling reasons. First and foremost, it offers complete control over the ingredients, allowing individuals to tailor the mix to their personal tastes and dietary needs. By selecting high-quality oats, nuts, seeds, and sweeteners, one can create a nutritious blend that aligns perfectly with their health goals.

Additionally, homemade granola is free from artificial preservatives and unhealthy additives commonly found in store-bought options, making it a cleaner and more wholesome choice. The process of making granola at home also provides an opportunity for creativity, as various flavors and textures can be experimented with.

Finally, homemade granola can be more cost-effective compared to pre-packaged varieties, enabling one to enjoy a delicious and healthy snack without breaking the bank. In essence, a homemade granola mix combines health, flavor, and economy, making it the ultimate choice for anyone seeking a nutritious and satisfying snack.

Enjoy your homemade granola mix.
