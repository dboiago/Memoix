[Marcus Wareing](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/marcus-wareing)'s bruschetta recipe is delightfully straightforward - the key is to source the best possible [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/tomato-recipes) and basil to make the flavour as dynamic as possible. Topping with a soft poached [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/egg-recipes) adds a gourmet finish to this popular starter dish.

First published in 2015

1

Place the tomatoes, garlic, thyme, sugar and a pinch of sea salt into a small saucepan over a moderate heat and then leave to gently simmer

-   8 [plum tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), large, diced
-   1/4 bunch of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), leaves picked
-   1 tsp sugar
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   4 slices of [sourdough bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of olive oil

2

Meanwhile, heat a large griddle pan until hot. Oil each side of the slices of bread and sprinkle with sea salt. Grill each slice until well coloured, then set aside

-   4 slices of [sourdough bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of olive oil

3

Add vinegar and salt to a pot of water and bring to a gentle simmer. Break the eggs into cups or ramekins, whisk the water then gently pour the eggs in, ensuring the water is still swirling

-   4 [large eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tsp vinegar

4

Once the whites are set, remove with a slotted spoon and place the poached eggs onto paper towels

5

Sprinkle the tomato mix with sea salt and pepper. Mix the chopped basil into the mix and then spoon onto the 4 toasts. Top each with a poached egg and serve immediately

-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1/2 bunch of [basil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes), chopped
-   1 pinch of [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
