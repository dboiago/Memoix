### Glass:

Serve in a [Coupe glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

2 fl oz

[Cognac (brandy)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/9460/louis-royer-vsop-cognac)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Strucchi Dry Vermouth](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/8201/strucchi-vermouth-dry)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Giffard Menthe Pastille white crème de menthe](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/855/giffard-menthe-pastille)

1⁄2 fl oz

Orange juice (freshly squeezed)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Grenadine/pomegranate syrup](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/29/cocktails/pomegranate-grenadine-syrup)

1⁄6 fl oz

Claret wine (Bordeaux red)

### Prepare:

1.  Select a [COUPE GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of mint leaf or red rose petal

### How to make:

3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) first 5 ingredients with CRUSHED ICE.
4.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into chilled glass.
5.  FLOAT red wine.

### Garnish:

6.  Garnish with mint leaf or rose petal floated on surface.

Both fresh and refreshing. A subtle hint of peppermint gives zing to this cognac-based cocktail.

### Variations/similar cocktails:

When served in a tall glass with crushed ice this is called an American Beauty Punch.

## History:

Adapted from a recipe in [David A. Embury](https://www.diffordsguide.com/on-this-day/november/03)'s 1948 [_The Fine Art of Mixing Drinks_](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1239/vintage-cocktail-books/1935-to-1949).

> AMERICAN BEAUTY  
> 1 part French Vermouth  
> 1 part Grenadine  
> 1 part White Creme de Menthe  
> 1 part Orange Juice  
> 4 parts Cognac  
> Shake well with crushed ice. Float 1 teaspoonful claret on top of each drink.  
> This drink is sometimes served in a tall glass filled with crushed ice and then called American Beauty Punch.
> 
> David A. Embury, 1948
