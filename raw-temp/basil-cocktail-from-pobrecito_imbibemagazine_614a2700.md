**RECIPE:** _Jackson Donahue, Phoenix_

![basil cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/basil-cocktail-pobrecito-phoenix-crdt-emma-janzen.jpg)

  
In this cocktail from Phoenix, fresh bell pepper and citrus create a bright baseline for vodka and Chartreuse.

## Ingredients

-   1 3/4 oz. vodka
-   1/4 oz. yellow Chartreuse
-   1/2 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1/2 oz. fresh orange juice
-   1/2 oz. yellow bell pepper syrup
-   3 basil leaves
-   1 dash orange bitters
-   [1 dash Hellfire Habanero Shrub](http://bittermens.com/products/hellfire-habanero-shrub/)

## Preparation

Muddle 2 basil leaves in a shaker tin. Add the remaining ingredients and ice. Shake until chilled. Fine strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Garnish.  
**Yellow Bell Pepper Syrup**Juice 1 yellow bell pepper and combine the juice in a saucepan with equal parts of white sugar. Stir over medium heat until the sugar dissolves. Remove from the heat, cool, strain into a bottle and store in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
