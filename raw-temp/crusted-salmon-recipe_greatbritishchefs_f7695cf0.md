Texture is a key component of technically ambitious food, and this crusted [salmon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/salmon-recipes) recipe from [Anna Hansen](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/anna-hansen) offers evidence of this. The [salmon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/salmon-recipes) is topped with a textural mix of black garlic, macadamia and [liquorice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/liquorice-recipes), with tomatillo salsa offering a refreshing counterpoint.

First published in 2015

#### Tomatillo salsa

-   500g of tomatillos, washed and finely diced
-   90g of [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), flesh only, diced
-   75g of [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced
-   150ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   50ml of moscatel vinegar
-   20g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped
-   12g of [chervil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chervil-recipes), finely chopped
-   12g of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), finely chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

-   Food processor or blender

1

To begin, grind the liquorice and salt together until a fine powder forms. Toss this together with the macadamias and water and lightly toast in a pan over a medium heat. Cool the mix, coarsely grind in a food processor, then add the black garlic and mix thoroughly

-   [liquorice juice sticks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/liquorice-recipes), around 1/6 of 1 stick
-   170g of macadamia nuts
-   1/2 tsp [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tsp water
-   25g of [black garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped

2

Preheat the oven to 200˚C/gas mark 6

3

For the tomatillo salsa, mix all of the ingredients together, season and set aside until ready to serve

-   500g of tomatillos, washed and finely diced
-   90g of [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), flesh only, diced
-   75g of [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely diced
-   150ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   50ml of moscatel vinegar
-   20g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), finely chopped
-   12g of [chervil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chervil-recipes), finely chopped
-   12g of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), finely chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

4

Place a large pan over medium heat. Rub the salmon skin in oil and place into the hot pan, skin-side down. Cook for 3 minutes, or until the salmon fillets are cooked to halfway through

-   4 [salmon fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salmon-fillet-recipes), each weighing 160g

5

Remove the salmon from the pan and place onto a baking tray lined with baking paper, skin-side up. Firmly press the macadamia crust onto the skin and place on the bottom shelf of the oven to cook for 5-6 minutes

6

Remove the salmon from the oven and place onto plates. Decorate the plates with the tomatillo salsa and watercress. Serve immediately

-   1 handful of [baby watercress](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/watercress-recipes), picked and washed
