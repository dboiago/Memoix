-   4

Try out this [Scottish-](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/scottish-recipes "Scottish")inspired [swede](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/swede-recipes "Swede") soup garnished with a crispy haggis topping – perfect for [Burns Night](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/burns-night-scottish-recipes "Burns Night") or even if you've a little leftover haggis after the celebrations. The [soup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soup-recipes "Soup") is flavoured with a little curry powder for extra warmth, and do feel free to use vegetarian haggis to make this a [meat-free](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-starter-recipes "Vegetarian starter") dish.

First published in 2022

-   Hand blender

1

Place a large pan over a medium heat with a dash of oil (or a knob of butter). Once hot, add the onion and garlic and cook for 5 minutes until soft and translucent

-   1 dash of vegetable oil, or 1 knob of butter
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), diced
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), sliced

2

Tip in the swede, potato and curry powder and cook for a further minute, stirring to coat everything in the curry powder

-   300g of [swede](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/swede-recipes), peeled and diced into 1cm cubes
-   1 [medium potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), peeled and diced into 1cm cubes
-   1 tsp curry powder

3

Add the stock, bring the boil then turn down to a gentle simmer for 30 minutes. Ensure you keep the heat low as you don't want to reduce the liquid too much

-   700g of stock, vegetable or chicken

4

As the soup simmers away, make the crispy haggis. Place a small pan over a medium heat with the butter. Once melted, add the haggis and break it up a bit with the back of a spoon. Cover the pan with a lid as the haggis will pop and spit. Cook for a few minutes until the popping stops and you're left with little pieces of crispy haggis. Strain from the butter and leave to one side

-   20g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of haggis

5

After half an hour, the swede should be soft. Use a hand blender to blitz into a smooth soup. Add a touch more stock or water if it looks too thick

7

Divide the soup between bowls and garnish with a drizzle of double cream, the crispy haggis, chopped chives and a final twist of black pepper

-   1 dash of double cream
-   2 tbsp of [chopped chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes)
-   [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
