This veggie version of the classic katsu curry uses [butternut squash](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butternut-squash-recipes "Butternut squash") to replace chicken. Fried until crisp and golden, this is served with sticky rice and a homemade [curry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/curry-recipes "Curry") sauce, along with fresh and tangy pickled radishes.

First published in 2025

#### For the quick pickled radish

-   150g of [radishes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/radish-recipes), thinly sliced
-   75ml of rice vinegar
-   1 tsp sugar
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### For the curry sauce

-   2 tbsp of neutral oil
-   2 [onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 knob of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), chopped
-   2 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), diced
-   1 tbsp of plain flour
-   1 tbsp of [tomato purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   750ml of vegetable stock
-   1 tbsp of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of mirin
-   1 tsp [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)

#### For the rice

-   300g of [sushi rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes), rinsed and then soaked for 20 minutes
-   400ml of water

-   Mandoline
-   Spice grinder

2

Mix together the radish slices with vinegar, sugar and salt then set aside

-   150g of [radishes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/radish-recipes), thinly sliced
-   75ml of rice vinegar
-   1 tsp sugar
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

For the curry sauce, heat the oil in a saucepan, then add the onion, garlic and ginger and sweat down over a medium-low heat for about 15 minutes

-   2 tbsp of neutral oil
-   2 [onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 knob of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), chopped

4

Add in the carrots, flour, tomato purée and cook out for another 2 minutes

-   2 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), diced
-   1 tbsp of plain flour
-   1 tbsp of [tomato purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)

5

Add in the curry powder, stock, soy sauce, mirin and honey, then leave to simmer for 20 minutes before blending until smooth and glossy

-   750ml of vegetable stock
-   1 tbsp of [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of mirin
-   1 tsp [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)

6

Meanwhile, make the butternut squash katsu. Dip each of the squash slices into flour, egg then panko, and shallow fry until golden on both sides and cooked through. Drain on kitchen paper and season with flaky salt

-   1 [butternut squash](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butternut-squash-recipes), peeled and sliced into 2 cm thick steaks
-   100g of plain flour
-   2 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® eggs](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/), beaten
-   150g of panko breadcrumbs
-   neutral oil, for shallow frying
-   [flaky salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

7

First drain the soaked rice well. Add the rice to a pot with the water. Bring to a boil then turn the heat down to medium-low, cover with a tea towel and lid, and cook for around 13 minutes

-   300g of [sushi rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rice-recipes), rinsed and then soaked for 20 minutes
-   400ml of water

8

Remove the rice from the heat and leave to steam for another 10 minutes, then fluff up with a fork or rice paddle

9

To serve, spoon the rice into bowls, then top with the katsu squash steaks and ladle over the curry sauce. Garnish with the pickled radish, spring onion and fresh coriander

-   2 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 handful of [fresh coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
