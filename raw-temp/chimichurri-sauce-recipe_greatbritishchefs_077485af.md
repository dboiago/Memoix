Rosana's chimichurri recipe is a true classic – a legendary Argentinian [sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sauce-recipes) made of fresh parsley, traditionally served up at [barbecues](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/barbecue-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/barbecue-recipes) with grilled meats (it makes a fantastic [steak sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/features/five-best-steak-sauces) too). An incredibly versatile sauce, it's great to have on hand to spruce up a [midweek meal](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/midweek-meal-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/midweek-meal-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/midweek-meal-recipes).

#### Chimichurri sauce

-   50g of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), fresh, finely chopped
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of oregano, fresh, finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [sweet smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of hot pepper sauce, optional
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   250ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   100ml of sherry vinegar, or red wine vinegar
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

1

To make the chimmichurri sauce, combine all of the ingredients (apart from the olive oil) in a bowl and mix thoroughly to combine

2

Vigorously stir in the olive oil to emulsify, then transfer to a jar or bowl to serve
