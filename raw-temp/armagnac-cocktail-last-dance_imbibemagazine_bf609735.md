An equal-parts cocktail perfect for winter sipping.

Brandy is the house specialty at the lobby bar of the Kimpton Marlowe in Boston, so it’s natural that Armagnac stars in this equal-parts cocktail.

## Ingredients

-   ¾ oz. Armagnac
-   ¾ oz. yellow Chartreuse
-   ¾ oz. maraschino liqueur
-   ¾ oz. fresh lemon juice

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a shaker and shake with ice until chilled. Strain into a glass, then garnish.
