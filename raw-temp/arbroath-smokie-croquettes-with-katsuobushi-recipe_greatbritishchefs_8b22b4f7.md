These Arbroath smokie croquettes from [Stuart Ralston](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/stuart-ralston "Stuart Ralston") are from his restaurant Noto. They are béchamel [croquettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/croquette-recipes "Croquette") made with a generous amount of smoked haddock and butter, and make a delicious and decadent [side dish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/side-dish-recipes "Side dish").

First published in 2023

### Metric

### Imperial

-   250g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   300g of plain flour, plus extra for the pané
-   1.5l fish stock, warmed in a pan
-   300g of [Arbroath smokies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/smoked-haddock-recipes), poached and flesh picked
-   50g of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), plus extra for the garnish
-   6g of dashi powder
-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50ml of milk
-   5g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   150g of panko breadcrumbs
-   25g of [black sesame seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sesame-seeds-recipes)
-   5g of [furikake seasoning](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/seaweed-recipes)
-   20g of tonkatsu sauce, to garnish
-   20g of [Kewpie mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes), to garnish
-   20g of pickled daikon, to garnish
-   katsuobushi, to garnish
-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying

1

Melt the butter in a pan then add the flour and cook gently until golden, about 10 minutes

-   250g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   300g of plain flour, plus extra for the pané

2

Add the warm fish stock slowly whilst whisking constantly until it turns into a glue-like texture. Cook the mixture low and slow for about 30 mins

-   1.5l fish stock, warmed in a pan

3

Add the chives and Arbroath smokies and season with salt and dashi powder. Let the mixture cool

-   50g of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), plus extra for the garnish
-   300g of [Arbroath smokies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/smoked-haddock-recipes), poached and flesh picked
-   6g of dashi powder

4

Roll the croquette mixture into 15g balls and then freeze until solid

5

Whisk together the eggs, milk and salt for the egg wash

-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50ml of milk
-   5g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

6

Toss each croquette first in flour, then egg wash, then panko and finally the sesame seeds

-   150g of panko breadcrumbs
-   25g of [black sesame seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sesame-seeds-recipes)

7

Heat a few centimetres of oil in a heavy, high-sided frying pan to 180°C for deep-frying

-   vegetable oil, for deep-frying

8

Deep-fry the croquettes until golden, about 3–4 mins per croquette

9

Garnish with furikake, tonkatsu sauce, Kewpie mayo, chives, pickled daikon and katsuobushi

-   5g of [furikake seasoning](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/seaweed-recipes)
-   20g of tonkatsu sauce, to garnish
-   20g of [Kewpie mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes), to garnish
-   20g of pickled daikon, to garnish
-   katsuobushi, to garnish
