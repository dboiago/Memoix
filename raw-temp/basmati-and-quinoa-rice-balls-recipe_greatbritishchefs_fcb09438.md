-   Makes 10
    
-   60 minutes
    

These ingenious [basmati rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basmati-rice-recipes) and [quinoa](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/quinoa-recipes) balls are a little like Italian arancini, but make the most of aromatic basmati, Middle Eastern spices and molten [mozzarella](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/mozzarella-recipes) cheese for a fascinating twist. The apple ketchup on the side is spiked with ajwain seeds, which have a flavour similar to thyme and star anise.

First published in 2019

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Apple and ajwain ketchup

-   350g of [Granny Smith apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), peeled, cored and finely sliced
-   150g of caster sugar
-   150ml of cider vinegar
-   1 tsp ajwain seeds
-   10ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

#### Rice balls

-   3 tbsp of [coconut oil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-recipes)
-   1 [white onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), medium, finely diced
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   40g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), minced
-   2 tsp baharat powder
-   4 tsp ground fenugreek
-   4 tsp [sweet smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   250g of [basmati rice and smoked quinoa mix](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basmati-rice-recipes)
-   600ml of vegetable stock
-   150g of [mozzarella cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mozzarella-recipes), firm, diced into 1cm cubes
-   100g of [polenta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/polenta-recipes)
-   oil, for deep-frying

-   Blender

1

To make the ketchup, place the apples, sugar, ajwain seeds and vinegar in a saucepan and set over a medium heat. Bring to the boil and cook the apples until they are very soft

-   150ml of cider vinegar
-   350g of [Granny Smith apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), peeled and cored weight, finely sliced
-   150g of caster sugar
-   1 tsp ajwain seeds

2

Drain the apples, reserving the liquid in the pan. Place the apple mixture into a blender and blitz until smooth. Add the lemon juice and enough of the reserved cooking liquid to reach a thick ketchup-like consistency. Store any extra ketchup in an airtight container in the fridge for a week or so

-   10ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

3

To make the rice balls, melt the coconut oil in a saucepan. Add the onion, garlic and ginger and cook for 4 minutes, then add the spices

-   3 tbsp of [coconut oil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-recipes)
-   4 tsp [sweet smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1 [white onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), medium, finely diced
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   40g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), minced
-   2 tsp baharat powder
-   4 tsp ground fenugreek

4

Continue to cook until the onion softens completely. Add the rice and quinoa, mixing well, then add the stock. Bring to the boil, stir, and reduce the heat. Cover with a lid and cook gently until the rice is tender and the liquid has evaporated. Remove from the heat, spread out on a tray and place in the fridge to cool for at least 1 hour

-   600ml of vegetable stock
-   250g of [basmati rice and smoked quinoa mix](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basmati-rice-recipes)

5

Preheat a deep-fryer or deep pan full of oil to 180°C

-   oil, for deep-frying

6

Shape the rice mixture into 30g balls and push a piece of mozzarella into the middle of each one, making sure it is completely covered in rice. Dust the balls in polenta and deep-fry until golden and crisp

-   150g of [mozzarella cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mozzarella-recipes), firm, diced into 1 cm cubes
-   100g of [polenta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/polenta-recipes)

7

Serve immediately with the apple ketchup
