Kids and adults alike will love this homemade cereal bar recipe from [Adam Gray](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/adam-gray), as they're bursting with oats, [apricots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/apricot-recipes), nuts and seeds for a [breakfast](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/breakfast-recipes-brunch-recipes) boost or afternoon snack pick-me-up. Try baking a batch of these bars on a Sunday evening to keep you well-stocked for the week ahead.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   200g of porridge oats
-   120ml of rapeseed oil
-   7 tbsp of [clear runny honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   110g of [dried apricots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apricot-recipes), roughly chopped
-   25g of [pumpkin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pumpkin-recipes)
-   110g of pecan nuts, roughly chopped
-   50g of [flaked almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)

1

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4. Line a 24cm square baking tin with greaseproof paper and set aside

2

Place a pan over a medium heat. Add the rapeseed oil and the honey, stir until the honey is hot

-   120ml of rapeseed oil
-   7 tbsp of [clear runny honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)

3

Remove from the heat and add the oats, dried fruit, nuts and seeds, stirring well to ensure the ingredients are mixed

-   200g of porridge oats
-   25g of [pumpkin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pumpkin-recipes)
-   110g of pecan nuts, roughly chopped
-   50g of [flaked almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   110g of [dried apricots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apricot-recipes), roughly chopped

4

Once mixed, make sure it's sticky enough to hold together by pressing in your hands, if not add a little more honey. Scoop the mixture into the lined baking tin, pressing down firmly

5

Bake in the oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the edges turn a golden brown colour

6

While still warm, score into 12 individual bars. Leave the cereal bars in the tin to cool completely, then serve or store in an airtight container for up to 3 days
