A brooding counterpart to the classic Manhattan.

Created by bartender Todd Smith when he worked at San Francisco’s [Bourbon & Branch](http://www.bourbonandbranch.com/), the Black Manhattan recipe uses Averna in place of sweet vermouth—a groundbreaking move when it was created in 2005.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. rye whiskey
-   1 oz. Averna
-   1 dash Angostura bitters
-   1 dash Regans’ Orange Bitters No. 6

## Preparation

Stir well with ice, strain into a chilled cocktail glass, and garnish with a cocktail cherry.
