**Cooks in:** _15mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   10 of apricots
-   10 TBSP. of sour cream
-   2 TBSP. of honey
-   1/4 CUP of pistachios
-   Lemon verbena or lemongrass

### Method

1

To make our recipe for apricots with honey, sour cream, and pistachios, start by washing the apricots. Divide them in half and remove the pit.   

2

Sprinkle a pan with honey over high heat. Once the honey starts to simmer (after a few seconds), transfer the apricots to the pan, pit side facing down. Sauté for a couple of minutes until lightly caramelized.

3

In the meantime, chop the pistachios and mince the verbena.

4

Serve the apricots with the sour cream, pistachios and verbena.

5

Extra Tip: You can replace the sour cream with mozzarella or yogurt ice cream. For a richer flavor, use infused honey.
