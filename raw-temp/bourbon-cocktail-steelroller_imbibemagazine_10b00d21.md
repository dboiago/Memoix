This bourbon cocktail is sublimely smooth and warming.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. Buffalo Trace bourbon
-   ½ oz. Aperol
-   ¼ oz. Amaro Montenegro
-   2 dashes Regan's Orange Bitters
-   2 dashes Angostura Bitters

## Preparation

Stir all of the ingredients together in a mixing glass with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over a single large ice cube. Garnish.
