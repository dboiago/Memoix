## How do you make banana bread with chocolate chips?

[![Collage showing mashed bananas and adding eggs to creamed butter and sugar](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Chocolate-Chip-Banana-Bread-with-Walnuts-Step-2-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Chocolate-Chip-Banana-Bread-with-Walnuts-Step-2.png)

**Preheat oven to 350.** Line a loaf pan with parchment paper or spritz with nonstick [baking spray](https://www.amazon.com/Baking-No-stick-Cooking-Spray-3pcs/dp/B00B20N5UE/ref=as_li_ss_tl?keywords=pam&qid=1582654634&sr=8-15&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=af417950e4493d59264320734395cddb&language=en_US), and set aside.

**Mash the bananas** with a fork. Set aside.

**Mix the wet ingredients.** Cream the butter with the sugar using a [hand mixer](https://www.amazon.com/KitchenAid-KHM7210OB-7-Speed-Digital-Accessories/dp/B00GXYTW4C/ref=as_li_ss_tl?keywords=hand+mixer&qid=1571698308&sr=8-1-spons&spLa=ZW5jcnlwdGVkUXVhbGlmaWVyPUFZS0xaM0FTRDhXTE8mZW5jcnlwdGVkSWQ9QTA4MTM1MzIzRDA5M0dURE0zR0RYJmVuY3J5cHRlZEFkSWQ9QTA5NzIwMTU1SzFBWVQ0RElTOUEmd2lkZ2V0TmFtZT1zcF9hdGYmYWN0aW9uPWNsaWNrUmVkaXJlY3QmZG9Ob3RMb2dDbGljaz10cnVl&th=1&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=e0600da21ba172e8afc8936cc0dadaf9&language=en_US). Once light and fluffy, add the eggs and vanilla extract.

[![Banana bread batter before and after adding mashed bananas](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Chocolate-Chip-Banana-Bread-with-Walnuts-Step-3-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Chocolate-Chip-Banana-Bread-with-Walnuts-Step-3.png)

With the [hand mixer](https://www.amazon.com/KitchenAid-KHM7210OB-7-Speed-Digital-Accessories/dp/B00GXYTW4C/ref=as_li_ss_tl?keywords=hand+mixer&qid=1571698308&sr=8-1-spons&spLa=ZW5jcnlwdGVkUXVhbGlmaWVyPUFZS0xaM0FTRDhXTE8mZW5jcnlwdGVkSWQ9QTA4MTM1MzIzRDA5M0dURE0zR0RYJmVuY3J5cHRlZEFkSWQ9QTA5NzIwMTU1SzFBWVQ0RElTOUEmd2lkZ2V0TmFtZT1zcF9hdGYmYWN0aW9uPWNsaWNrUmVkaXJlY3QmZG9Ob3RMb2dDbGljaz10cnVl&th=1&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=e0600da21ba172e8afc8936cc0dadaf9&language=en_US), **beat until light and fluffy**.

**Add the mashed bananas** and beat to combine.

[![Collage showing how to add in flour and milk to the banana bread batter](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Chocolate-Chip-Banana-Bread-with-Walnuts-step-6-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Chocolate-Chip-Banana-Bread-with-Walnuts-step-6.png)

**Add the dry ingredients** (flour, baking soda and salt) to the batter.

**Pour in the milk**, too. Use a spatula and stir gently to combine.

[![Collage showing how to fold nuts and chocolate chips into banana bread batter](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Chocolate-Chip-Banana-Bread-with-Walnuts-Step-4-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Chocolate-Chip-Banana-Bread-with-Walnuts-Step-4.png)

**Add in the walnuts and chocolate chips**, and stir to combine.

**Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan**. Sprinkle with the remaining walnuts and chocolate chips on top of the batter. You can also sprinkle on some coarse salt for pop of minerality and saltiness.

**Bake** until golden brown and cooked through! Test the doneness with a butter knife or skewer inserted into the center of the bread. If it comes out clean, you’re good to go.

Let the bread cool slightly before slicing.

Enjoy with coffee, as an afternoon snack, breakfast or anyway you’d like!

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you add chocolate chips to banana bread?**

Yes! Chocolate chips added to banana bread are DELICIOUS, and I highly recommend it.

**How do you make banana bread with chocolate chips moist?**

We’ve got a few ingredients in this bread keeping it moist, including the bananas themselves, as well as the unsalted butter and the milk.

**Can you substitute chocolate chips for nuts in banana bread?**

If you want to leave out the nuts in this banana bread and add extra chocolate chips to it, SURE!

**How do you keep chocolate chips from sinking in banana bread?**

The banana bread batter of this recipe should be thick enough to prevent this from happening. You do not need to toss them in flour to prevent them from sinking.

Calories 490 kcal

Cook Time 55 minutes

Total Time 1 hour

-   2/3 cup sugar
-   2 large eggs
-   1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
-   3-4 very ripe bananas, mashed

-   1 teaspoon of baking soda
-   1/2 teaspoon of kosher salt
-   1/4 cup of whole milk or yogurt
-   1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
-   1/2 cup of toasted walnut
