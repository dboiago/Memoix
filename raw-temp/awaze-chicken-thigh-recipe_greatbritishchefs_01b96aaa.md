A deeply delicious awaze spice paste is used to coat [chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes) in this recipe, a delightfully simple dinner for spice-lovers. Awaze is fiery paste made from [berbere](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/berbere-recipe), garlic and tej, a type of Ethiopian mead, but dry sherry makes a great alternative if you can't get your hands on any.

First published in 2018

### Imperial

#### Awaze paste

-   40g of berbere
-   1 tsp dried garlic granules
-   1/2 tsp [finely grated ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   80ml of tej, (Ethiopian honey wine) or a dry sherry
-   [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), to taste
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

1

Begin by making the awaze paste. Mix together all the ingredients in a bowl, taste, and adjust seasoning with salt and lime juice

2

Pour the awaze paste over the chicken thighs and leave to marinate for at least 1 hour

3

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4

4

Cook in the oven for 20 minutes, or until the juices run clear

5

Serve with the roasting juices drizzled over the top and lime wedges on the side
