This [asparagus](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/asparagus-recipes) soup recipe from [Daniel Clifford](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/daniel-clifford) makes a lovely starter or a light lunch. The soup is made with asparagus, spinach and garlic for a delicious springtime taste that can be made [vegetarian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes) by using vegetable stock instead of chicken stock. The crispy deep-fried hen's egg is a brilliant addition, along with the truffle, asparagus spears and purée. This is an excellent way to celebrate the British asparagus season.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   Food processor or blender
-   Deep fat fryer
-   Japanese noodle mandolin slicer

1

To make the asparagus purée, blanch the trimmed asparagus in boiling salted water and drain

-   500g of [asparagus](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes), trimmed

2

Heat the butter in a pan to make a beurre noisette. Boil the double cream and set aside. Blend the butter and cream in a food processor with the asparagus and spinach, then pass through a sieve and chill immediately

-   100g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   50ml of double cream
-   100g of [baby spinach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spinach-recipes)

3

Now make the soup. Sweat the onion and garlic, then add the stock and bring to the boil. Add the asparagus and cook for a further 4 minutes. In a separate pan, make a beurre noisette from the butter and add to the pan, along with the cream. Cook for a further 2 minutes

-   500ml of chicken stock
-   200g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100ml of double cream
-   375g of [asparagus](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes), trimmed and diced
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely chopped

4

Add the baby spinach, season with salt and lemon juice and blend in a food processor. Pass through a sieve and chill immediately

-   60g of [baby spinach](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spinach-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

5

Poach the eggs in simmering water for 2 minutes until still soft, then refresh in cold water

-   4 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

6

Using a Japanese noodle mandolin slicer, create long ribbons of potato and pat dry using absorbent kitchen towel. Carefully wrap them around the egg, covering each one completely

-   4 [large potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)

7

Deep-fry the eggs at 180°C until they're golden brown

-   vegetable oil

8

Blanch the trimmed asparagus spears in boiling salted water and drain. Trim the base of half of the spears, as pictured, and roll the others in the grated truffle

-   8 [asparagus spears](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes)
-   1 [black truffle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/truffle-recipes)

9

To plate, place an egg on each plate alongside a sweep of the purée. Spoon a further droplet of the purée next to the sweep, and lay the asparagus spears on top. Serve the soup in a small bowl or cup and place on the plate. Lastly, drizzle some truffle oil over the dish and serve

-   1 dash of truffle oil
