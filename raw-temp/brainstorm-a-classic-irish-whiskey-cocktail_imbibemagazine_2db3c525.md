This classic 1917 cocktail has been updated to suit contemporary tastes.

This classic Irish whiskey cocktail from Hugo Ensslin’s _1917 Recipes for Mixed Drinks_ has been adapted for contemporary use, with the French vermouth and honeyed herbal liqueur Bénédictine given greater billing in the glass.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. Irish whiskey
-   1/2 oz. dry vermouth
-   1/2 oz. Bénédictine
-   1 dash orange bitters

## Preparation

Stir all of the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled glass. Twist an orange peel over the cocktail and use as garnish.
