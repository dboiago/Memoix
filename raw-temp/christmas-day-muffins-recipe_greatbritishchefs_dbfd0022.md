These easy [muffins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/muffin-recipes) have all the classic tastes of [Christmas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/christmas-recipes). With cinnamon, nutmeg, clementine zest and dried cranberries, they'd make a perfect festive start to the day. The recipe itself comes from [Adam Byatt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/adam-byatt)'s wife and is a favourite family recipe in the Byatt household.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   250g of plain flour
-   2 1/2 tsp baking powder
-   1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
-   100g of caster sugar
-   1 tsp [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [ground nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes)
-   2 clementines
-   125ml of milk
-   75ml of vegetable oil
-   1 [large egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   130g of [dried cranberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)
-   3 tsp Demerara sugar

-   Muffin tin
-   Muffin papers

1

Preheat the oven to 200°C/gas mark 6

2

Line a 12-bun muffin tin with muffin papers - you could also use silicone inserts

3

Mix the flour, baking powder, bicarb, caster sugar and spices in a bowl

-   250g of plain flour
-   2 1/2 tsp baking powder
-   1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
-   100g of caster sugar
-   1 tsp [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [ground nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes)

4

Grate the zest of the clementine into the bowl and mix to combine. If you are doing this in advance, leave the zesting until the day of baking

-   2 clementines

5

Squeeze the juice of the clementines into a measuring jug, pour in the milk until it comes up to the 200ml mark

-   125ml of milk

6

Lightly beat in the oil and egg until just combined

-   75ml of vegetable oil
-   1 [large egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

7

Pour this liquid mixture into the bowl of dried ingredients and stir until everything is combined

8

Fold in the cranberries then spoon the batter into the muffin cases and sprinkle the Demerara sugar on top

-   3 tsp Demerara sugar
-   130g of [dried cranberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)

9

Bake in the oven for 15-20 minutes until golden brown. Remove and serve warm, or allow to cool and store in an airtight container for up to 3 days
