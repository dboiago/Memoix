Preserve the flavour of summery [blueberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/blueberry-recipes) with this simple infused [gin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/gin-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/gin-recipes) recipe. Simply mix it with tonic over ice or get more creative by adding it into a cocktail.

First published in 2019

This gorgeous violet-coloured gin is delicious simply with tonic or as part of a cocktail. Get experimental with additional flavours; a cinnamon stick or liquorice tea bag work nicely with the flavour of the blueberries.

-   Muslin cloth
-   Sterilised 500ml jar
-   Sterilised bottle

1

Wash the berries and place in a sterilsed jar

2

Use a masher or the end of a rolling pin to lightly crush the blueberries – just enough to split the skins a little

3

Add the sugar and gin, close the jar and give it a good shake

4

Leave to steep for 2–3 days, giving the jar a shake each day

5

Strain the berries through a muslin cloth and bottle the gin. Best enjoyed within 1 month
