“I love enjoying apples when they’re at their best,” says chef Marcus Samuelsson of this Apple Martini. “And the spices remind me of apple pie.”

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. vodka
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1/2 oz. apple-spiced simple syrup
-   2 dashes Angostura bitters

## Preparation

Rim a glass with the sugar and set aside. Combine all of the ingredients in a shaker and shake with ice to chill. Strain into the prepared glass and garnish with the apple ribbon.

**Apple-Spiced Simple Syrup**Combine 1 tsp. of ground cinnamon, 1 tsp. of ground ginger, 1 tsp. of ground allspice, 1 tsp. of ground nutmeg, 1 cup of granulated sugar, 1 slice of a red apple, 1/4-inch thick, and 1/2 cup water in a small saucepan, bring to a boil and stir to combine. Remove from heat and let cool to room temperature. Double strain into an airtight container and keep refrigerated for up to two weeks.
