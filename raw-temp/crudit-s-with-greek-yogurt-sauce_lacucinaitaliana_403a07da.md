**Cooks in:** _20mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   3/4 C. of Greek yogurt
-   1/4 C. of extravirgin olive oil
-   1 SLICE of sandwich bread, crust removed
-   1 TBSP. of white wine vinegar
-   1 TBSP. of parsley, chopped
-   3 PCS. of gherkins
-   1 TSP. of capers, rinsed
-   2 of oilpacked anchovy fillets
-   4 of celery stalks
-   2 of carrots
-   2 of fennel bulbs
-   1 of hardboiled egg yolk
-   1 of small head radicchio
-   1/2 clove garlic
-   1/2 red bell pepper
-   1/2 yellow bell pepper
-   Salt

### Method

1

Prepare the salsa verde as illustrated in the basic recipe, then mix it with the yogurt.

2

Trim the fennel, carrots, celery, radicchio, and peppers, then cut them into thick sticks about 4 inches in length.

3

Serve with the yogurt sauce.
