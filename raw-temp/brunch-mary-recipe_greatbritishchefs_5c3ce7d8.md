This Brunch Mary recipe by Tony Conigliaro appears simple, but it contains a few molecular mixology tricks. Żubrówka vodka is infused with fresh [horseradish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/horseradish-recipes) and then re-distilled in a rotary evaporator to achieve a beautiful clarity. Try infusing your own spirits at home - the flavour should deliver in spades. Serve this cocktail with [Bruno Loubet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/bruno-loubet)'s [oysters with beef and horseradish canapé](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/oyster-horseradish-canape-recipe) – the undertones of horseradish perfectly complement the oyster while the tomato is an ideal foil to the beef consommé jelly.
