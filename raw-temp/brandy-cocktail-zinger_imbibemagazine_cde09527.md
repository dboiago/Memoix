**RECIPE:** _Anthony Merlino for [Bergamo’s](https://www.bergamos.co/), New York City_ 

![Brandy sour](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/zinger-cocktail-bergamos-crdt-rachel-vanni.jpg)

Brandy, ginger, and lemon is all you need for this simple play on the [Penicillin cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/penicillin-cocktail/).

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. brandy
-   1 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1 oz. ginger syrup

## Preparation

Combine the brandy, lemon juice, and ginger syrup in a shaker, then strain into an Old Fashioned glass over a large ice cube.

**Ginger Syrup**Blend 1 oz. of fresh ginger juice with 1/2 oz. of white cane sugar in a blender until sugar is dissolved. Once dissolved, add a squeeze of fresh honey and blend again.
