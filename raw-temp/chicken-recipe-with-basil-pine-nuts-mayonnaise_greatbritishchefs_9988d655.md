This delicious chicken [canapé](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/canape-recipes) from [Vineet Bhatia](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/vineet-bhatia) is simple to prepare and combines some wonderful flavours including [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/ginger-recipes), [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/saffron-recipes), [cardamom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cardamom-recipes) and [basil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/basil-recipes). Make the dip earlier in the day and keep it in the fridge. The [Indian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes) spice mix, chaat masala, is easy to buy online or from Indian specialist shops.

First published in 2015

-   Fine sieve

3

To make the dip, very briefly dry roast the saffron in a small frying pan over a very low heat, then soak it in 2 tablespoons of hot water for 30 minutes

-   1/2 tsp [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/saffron-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of water

4

Mix together the Greek yogurt and mayonnaise, then stir in the saffron water. Add the lemon juice, fennel seeds, cardamom, sugar and salt, mix well and pass through a very fine sieve. Store in the fridge

-   6 tbsp of [Greek yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes)
-   1 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes)
-   0.25g of caster sugar
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [cardamom seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)

5

Sear the chicken pieces on a chargrill pan until they are almost cooked. Arrange on a baking tray, sprinkle the cheese mix on top and flash under a hot grill for 1 minute, or place in an oven preheated to 190°C/Gas Mark 5 for 3 minutes, until browned and bubbling

-   1 [boneless and skinless chicken breast](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-breast-recipes)

6

To serve, pile the chicken on to plates and serve with the dip on the side
