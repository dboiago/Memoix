A simple but delicious combo of rum, lime, mint and simple syrup.

Nothing says summer like a classic Mojito recipe, a simple but delicious combo of rum, lime, mint and simple syrup.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. white rum
-   1 lime cut in half
-   ½ oz. simple syrup (1:1)
-   4 mint leaves
-   Soda water

## Preparation

Muddle the mint and simple syrup in a mixing glass. Squeeze in juice from both lime halves, add the rum and ice and stir to chill. Pour everything into a glass, top with soda water and garnish.
