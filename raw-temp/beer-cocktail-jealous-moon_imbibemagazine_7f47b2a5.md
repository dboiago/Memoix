**Recipe:** Morgan Weber, [Coltivare](https://www.agricolehospitality.com/coltivare), Houston

![beer cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/houston-coltivare-jealous-moon-gallery2-crdt-jody-horton.jpg)

Pale ale pairs swimmingly with two types of amaro.

Italian liqueurs take center stage in this beer cocktail, which is topped with a splash of pale ale. Coltivare uses Deschutes Mirror Pond Pale Ale.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. Amaro Cia Ciaro
-   ½ oz. Fernet-Branca
-   1 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   [½ oz. rich simple syrup (2:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/rich-simple-syrup-recipe/)
-   2 oz. chilled pale ale
-   2 dashes Angostura bitters

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients except the beer in a shaker and shake with ice to chill. Strain into a snifter filled with large ice cubes. Top with beer; garnish.
