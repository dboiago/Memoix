[Sameer Taneja's](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/sameer-taneja/) aloo podi recipe is a spiced potato [side dish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/side-dish-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/side-dish-recipes). He toasts and grinds spices to create a fragrant spice blend, giving the new potatoes a deep and vibrant flavour. The dish is also completely [vegan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes).

First published in 2021

-   Spice grinder

1

Boil the new potatoes in a pan of salted boiling water for about 15-20 minutes or until perfectly cooked

-   500g of [new potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/new-potato-recipes), washed

2

As the potatoes are cooking, place a small pan over a medium heat and once hot, add the ingredients for the spice blend and dry roast for a few seconds until fragrant. Take of the heat and once cooled, blend in a spice grinder to a powder

-   2 tbsp of chana dal
-   1 1/2 tbsp of [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)
-   4 [dried red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [black peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

3

Once cooked, drain the potatoes and when cool enough to handle, rubs the skins off with your hands or a paring knife (this is easiest to do when the potatoes are still warm)

4

Place a kadai or wok over a medium heat, add the oil and once heated add the mustard seeds. When they start to pop add the urad dal and fry until they turn golden

-   1/2 tsp [mustard seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-seed-recipes)
-   1 tsp [urad dal](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lentil-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of vegetable oil

5

Add the curry leaves and asafoetida, followed by the cooked potatoes, turmeric, salt and pepper. Cook whilst continuously stirring until you have a nice golden colour on the potatoes

-   1 pinch of asafoetida
-   1/4 tsp [turmeric powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   4 [curry leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/curry-leaf-recipes), fresh

6

Add the spice blend and toss to combine then serve up hot with chopped coriander

-   1 handful of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
