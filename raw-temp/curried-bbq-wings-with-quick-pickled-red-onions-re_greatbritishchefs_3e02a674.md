-   2-4

These wings are marinated in a paste of [yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes "Yoghurt") and [curry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/curry-recipes "Curry") powder then grilled until charred in places and topped with tangy quick pickled onions and [coriander.](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes "Coriander") Any leftover pickled [onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes "Onion") will keep, refrigerated, for a week or so. 

First published in 2022

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Wings

-   6 [chicken wings](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-wing-recipes), separated into drums and flats
-   200g of [natural yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of curry powder
-   1 tsp [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), grated to a paste
-   1/4 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season

#### Quick pickled red onions

-   70ml of rice vinegar
-   170ml of hot water, (from the tap is fine)
-   25g of caster sugar
-   2 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 red onion, very finely sliced

1

Combine the yoghurt, turmeric, curry powder, garlic and lime juice with some salt and mix to combine. Add the wings, mix well, then refrigerate overnight, if possible

-   200g of [natural yoghurt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yoghurt-recipes)
-   1 tsp [turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of curry powder
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), grated to a paste
-   1/4 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to season
-   6 [chicken wings](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-wing-recipes), separated into drums and flats

2

Make the quick pickled onions by combining the vinegar, hot water, caster sugar and salt in a bowl and mixing until the sugar and salt are dissolved. Add the onions and set aside. The pickle can be made the day before and refrigerated until needed

-   70ml of rice vinegar
-   170ml of hot water, (from the tap is fine)
-   25g of caster sugar
-   2 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 red onion, very finely sliced

3

To cook the wings, light a BBQ for indirect cooking, with the coals arranged either in the centre or off to one side

4

Cook the wings over direct heat but close to the coals for around 30 minutes, or until cooked through and charred in places (move them closer to the coals as they start to burn down)

5

Transfer the wings to a serving plate and garnish with the quick pickled onions and coriander

-   1 handful of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
