## Quick and easy, pecorino cheese and black rice flour wafers are a crisp addition to any party spread.

**Cooks in:** _10mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   4 OZ. of Pecorino cheese, grated
-   1/4 CUP of black rice flour

### Method

1

Mix the Pecorino cheese with the black rice flour; place a spoonful of this mixture in a nonstick pan to form a disc.

2

Cook over low heat for one minute, turn it with a spatula and finish cooking until you obtain a crispy wafer. Repeat to make around a dozen wafers.
