Noor Murad and Yotam Ottolenghi share their recipe for smoky, blackened [chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-recipes "Chicken") and a delicious [caramel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caramel-recipes "Caramel") and [clementine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes "Orange") dressing. The sweet and sour dressing pairs well with roast chicken, pan-seared salmon, tofu or halloumi as well as blackened chicken and keeps in a sealed container in the fridge for 3 days.

_Extracted from Ottolenghi Test Kitchen: Extra Good Things by Noor Murad and Yotam Ottolenghi (Ebury Press, £25) All photography by Elena Heatherwick_

First published in 2022

Noor and Yotam say: _“Don’t be afraid to really char your chicken thighs here (making sure to ventilate your kitchen well!) – this’ll add an intense flavour alongside the vibrant dressing and make for a very punchy eating experience. That said, you can also grill your chicken thighs in the oven, or swap them out for chicken breasts, if you prefer. Use a pair of kitchen scissors to help you easily remove the bones from the thighs, or ask your butcher to do this for you, making sure to keep the skins intact. Feel free to swap out the clementines for orange segments in the dressing; it’ll work just as well.”_

## Ingredients

### Metric

### Imperial

-   6 [boneless chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), skin on, roughly 850g
-   1 1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tsp [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   12 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), trimmed, washed well and dried (about 180g)

#### CARAMEL AND CLEMENTINE DRESSING

-   85g of caster sugar
-   30g of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), peeled and julienned
-   2 [red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded, halved widthways and julienned (about 20g)
-   3 tbsp of rice wine vinegar
-   4 [limes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), 1 halved, to serve, and the rest juiced to get 3 tbsp
-   2 tbsp of [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)
-   3 clementines, peeled, about 140g after peeling, 200g before peeling
-   5g of [fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), roughly chopped, to serve

## Method

1

Put the chicken thighs, turmeric, chilli flakes, fish sauce, oil and 1 teaspoon of salt into a large bowl and mix well to combine. Leave to marinate for an hour, or cover and refrigerate for longer (up to overnight). If the latter, remove the chicken thighs from the fridge about 45 minutes before cooking

-   6 [boneless chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), skin on, roughly 850g
-   1 1/2 tsp [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 tsp [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Place a large cast-iron sauté pan on a medium-high heat and ventilate your kitchen well. Once hot, add half the chicken thighs, skin side down. Weigh down the thighs with a large saucepan around the same size, so they are well pressed into the pan. Cook for 6–8 minutes, or until the skin is charred and blackened

3

Remove the top pan and set the chicken thighs aside, then repeat with the remaining thighs. The second batch might blacken faster, so check them at the 5-minute mark. Remove all the chicken, setting it aside, and let the pan cool for 5–10 minutes

4

If needed, drain off all but 1½ tablespoons of the fat collected in the pan and return to a medium-high heat

5

Add the spring onions, using a pair of tongs to coat them in the fat, then put back the chicken thighs, skin side up, and any of their juices, nestling them into the spring onions, along with 90ml of water. Cook for 8 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and cooked through. Transfer the chicken to a cutting board and leave to rest for 5 minutes before slicing each thigh into 2cm-wide strips

-   12 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), trimmed, washed well and dried (about 180g)

6

While the chicken is cooking, make the caramel and clementine dressing. Heat a medium saucepan on a medium-high heat. Once hot, turn the heat down to medium and sprinkle in the sugar to coat the base – it should immediately start to melt and colour around the sides. Swirl the pan, without stirring, so that all the sugar melts and turns deeply golden, about 90 seconds to 2 minutes

-   85g of caster sugar

7

Add the ginger and chillies, and stir continuously for 45 seconds, just to take off the spicy edge. Quickly add the vinegar, lime juice and fish sauce, stirring to combine. Cook for 30 seconds, then remove from the heat. Leave to cool for 10 minutes

-   30g of [fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), peeled and julienned
-   2 [red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded, halved widthways and julienned (about 20g)
-   3 tbsp of rice wine vinegar
-   3 tbsp of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)

8

Use a small serrated knife to slice the clementines into 1cm thick rounds, then pull at them gently to halve them (don’t worry if they break apart further). Stir these into the cooled sauce

-   3 clementines, peeled, about 140g after peeling, 200g before peeling

9

To serve, transfer the spring onions to a large platter and top with the chicken and any pan juices, using a spatula to keep each thigh together. Pour the dressing all over, then sprinkle with the coriander. Lastly, squeeze over the lime halves

-   5g of [fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), roughly chopped, to serve
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), halved
