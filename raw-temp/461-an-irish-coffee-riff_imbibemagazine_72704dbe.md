An Irish coffee twist with banana and beer.

In this [Irish coffee](https://imbibemagazine.com/6-to-try-irish-coffee-riffs/) recipe by Sláinte Irish Pub in Oakland, Irish whiskey shows off its natural affinity for the flavors of coffee, banana, and stout.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. Irish whiskey
-   1 oz. cold-brew coffee concentrate
-   1/2 oz. banana liqueur
-   1/2 oz. Guinness syrup
-   2 dashes chocolate bitters
-   3 dashes of saline solution (or 1 small pinch of sea salt)

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a chilled glass, and garnish.  
**Guinness Syrup**Combine 8 oz. of Guinness stout with 8 oz. of demerara sugar in a saucepan. Heat over medium, stirring, until sugar has dissolved, then cool and bottle for use within 1 week.  
**Coffee-Cocoa-Dusted Cherry**Use a clean cloth or paper towel to dry a maraschino cherry, then roll it in a dish of fine-ground coffee and cocoa powder. Place on a skewer and rest on the edge of the glass as garnish.
