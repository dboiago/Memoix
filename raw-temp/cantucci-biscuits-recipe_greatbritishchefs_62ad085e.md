## Cardamom, orange, cranberry and pistachio cantucci

1.  ![Cardamom, orange, cranberry and pistachio cantucci](https://media-cdn2.greatbritishchefs.com/media/tmwk35go/img33075.whqc_768x512q90.jpg)
    

Item 1 of 1

-   makes 60
    
-   1 hour 10 minutes
    

Rachel shares a delicious recipe for [Italian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/italian-recipes) cantucci biscuits flavoured with orange zest, [cardamom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cardamom-recipes), pistachio and [cranberry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cranberry-recipes).

Not feeling this? See related recipes.

These cantucci [biscuits](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/biscuit-recipes) will keep for about a month if they don’t disappear before then, and contain some of my favourite flavours. If you’d prefer different nuts, spices, dried fruits, chocolate pieces or a little vanilla or almond essence, you can be creative and play around. Just keep the biscuit recipe as it is, and substitute the [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/orange-recipes) zest, cardamom, [pistachio](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pistachio-recipes) and [cranberry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cranberry-recipes) for flavours of your choice.

(Adapted from Maria Elia, Full Of Flavour)

## Method

1

Preheat the oven to 150˚C/gas mark 2 and line 2 baking trays with baking paper

2

Using an electric whisk or standing mixer, cream the butter and sugar together until pale and fluffy

3

Gradually add the eggs into the mixture, combining well each time. Scrape the mixture down with a spatula

4

Beat in the remaining ingredients until the mixture comes together

5

On the baking trays form the dough into 3 or 4 logs, approximately 5cm wide, and pat into an even shape using wet hands. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until risen and lightly golden

6

Transfer the logs onto a wire rack to cool slightly for 5-10 minutes. When still warm, cut the cantucci diagonally into 1cm thick pieces. Lay on the baking sheets, turn the oven down to 120˚C and bake for 30-40 minutes, turning halfway through

7

Cool on a wire rack before serving. These cantucci will keep in an airtight container for up to a month
