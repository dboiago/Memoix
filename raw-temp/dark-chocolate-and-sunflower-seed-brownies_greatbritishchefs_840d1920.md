These brownies are given a crunch with the addition of roasted pumpkin seeds. [Adam Stokes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/adam-stokes) recommends serving them warm and partnering with cream or [ice cream](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ice-cream-recipes) to provide a luxurious [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocoholic-chocolate-recipes) lovers dessert.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   140g of [70% dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes), chopped into small pieces
-   115g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   100g of flour
-   170g of [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1/4 vanilla pod, seeds only
-   255g of granulated sugar
-   30g of [pumpkin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pumpkin-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

1

Toast the pumpkin seeds on a baking tray for 5 minutes at 200˚C/gas mark 6, set aside to cool

-   30g of [pumpkin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pumpkin-recipes)

2

Place 70g of the dark chocolate and the butter and melt together using a bain marie

-   70g of [70% dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)
-   115g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

3

Mix the egg and vanilla seeds together then add the granulated sugar. Add this mix to the melted butter and chocolate

-   170g of [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1/4 vanilla pod
-   255g of granulated sugar

4

Sift in the flour and salt and fold through gently until just combined. Add the remaining 70g of chocolate pieces and mix well. Pour into a 20cm square baking tray and top with the toasted pumpkin seeds

-   99g of flour
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

5

Reduce the oven down to 175˚C/gas mark 3 and bake the brownies for 25 minutes. Gently shake the tray and if the brownie still wobbles slightly in the middle, place back in the oven until just set

6

Remove from the oven and allow to cool completely before turning out and cutting into squares
