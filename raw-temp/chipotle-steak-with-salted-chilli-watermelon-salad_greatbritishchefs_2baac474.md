This summery [steak](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/steak-recipes "Steak") recipe is served with a refreshing watermelon, charred sugar snap pea, [feta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/feta-cheese-recipes "Feta cheese") and mint [salad,](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salad-recipes "Salad") which is sprinkled with a fiery Barbacoa sea salt from [Bart](https://bart.co.uk/). The cost-effective steak cut [bavette](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bavette-flank-steak-recipes "Bavette") is cooked rare with sweet and smoky [Bart](https://bart.co.uk/) chipotle chilli flakes giving it an extra depth of flavour. 

First published in 2022

### Metric

### Imperial

2

Prepare the watermelon by slicing off the skin and cutting into small wedges. Place in a salad bowl and sprinkle with the Barbacoa sea salt flakes and toss to combine

-   600g of [watermelon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/watermelon-recipes)
-   1 tsp [Bart Barbacoa sea salt flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Place two large frying pans over a high heat. In one, add the sugar snap peas with a very small dash of oil and char in the pan until starting to blacken. Toss the pan every now and then to char on both sides. Once nicely charred, tip out onto a plate to cool 

-   1 dash of vegetable oil
-   150g of [sugar snap peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes)

4

In the second pan, add a dash of oil, then once smoking hot, add the bavette steak. Cook for 3 minutes on each side, then remove from the pan and leave to rest for 5 minutes before carving

-   400g of bavette steak

5

To assemble the salad, add the charred sugar snaps to the salted watermelon along with the feta, almonds and mint and toss to combine. Slice up the steak once rested and serve with the salad
