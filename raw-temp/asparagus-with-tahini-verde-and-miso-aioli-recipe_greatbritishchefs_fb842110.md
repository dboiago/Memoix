This minimalist [vegan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes) [asparagus](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/asparagus-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/asparagus-recipes) recipe lets the beautiful green spears shine, with a pair of delicious sauces adding extra flavour. [Tahini](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tahini-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tahini-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tahini-recipes) is blitzed with herbs for freshness, while an aioli made from aquafaba in place of egg and spiked with [miso](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/miso-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/miso-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/miso-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/miso-recipes) provides plenty of depth. A wonderfully simple starter.

First published in 2020

-   Blender
-   Hand blender

1

For the tahini verde, place all of the ingredients into a small food processor or a beaker for a stick blender, add 1 tablespoon of cold water and blend together until combined but slightly coarse. Add a little more water if needed

-   2 tbsp of [tarragon leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of [basil leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes), chopped
-   1/2 tsp [table salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   100g of [tahini](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tahini-recipes)

2

To make the miso aioli, use a hand blender to blitz the aquafaba and garlic together for 5 seconds. Slowly drizzle in the oil, a little at a time, mixing continuously until emulsified. Mix the miso paste with 1 tablespoon of water and the vinegar until smooth, then add to the aioli and blend again to combine

-   30g of [miso paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/miso-recipes)
-   200ml of olive oil
-   2 tbsp of rice wine vinegar
-   50g of aquafaba, (the liquid from a tin of chickpeas)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely grated

3

Bring a pan of salted water to the boil. Remove the woody ends from the asparagus and blanch 3 minutes, then drain

-   2 bunches of [asparagus](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

4

Serve the asparagus drizzled with the miso aioli and the tahini verde on the side. Finish with a scattering of sesame seeds

-   1 tbsp of [black sesame seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sesame-seeds-recipes)
