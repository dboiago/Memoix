An easy-drinking Boulevardier variation.

Cucumber rounds out the bitter notes of Campari in this refreshing Boulevardier-inspired cocktail from Azo Drinks in Belém, Brazil.

## Ingredients

-   1/2 oz. bourbon
-   1/2 oz. Campari
-   1/2 oz. sweet vermouth
-   1/3 oz. cucumber syrup
-   1/3 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1 tsp. triple sec
-   tonic water
-   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
-   2/3 cup macerated Japanese cucumber
-   2/3 cup water
-   2/3 cup sugar

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients, except the tonic water, to a glass with ice. Top with tonic water. Garnish.

**Cucumber Syrup**Add the water and cucumber to a saucepan and let it boil for 3 to 5 minutes over medium heat. Add the sugar and stir until it dissolves. Strain and allow to cool. Will keep in the refrigerator for 1 week.
