A refreshing aperitif from Sydney.

This spritz recipe is tailor-made for Sydney’s long, warm days.

## Ingredients

-   1/2 oz. London dry gin
-   1/2 oz. simple syrup (1:1)
-   2/3 oz. fino sherry
-   2/3 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   2 1/2 oz. chilled prosecco

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in an ice-filled wine glass and garnish.
