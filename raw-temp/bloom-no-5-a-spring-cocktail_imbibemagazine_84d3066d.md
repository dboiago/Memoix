Springtime through a Margarita lens.

“The goal behind the Bloom No. 5 was to weave floral, springtime flavors into a classic Margarita template,” says Shelby Abell, bartender at Nashville’s Martha My Dear. To do this she combines Suze and elderflower liqueur, which balance one another and create a complex, bittersweet profile. Meanwhile, ginger and lemon brighten up the drink “to nail the ‘springtime in a glass’ vibe,” Abell adds.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. blanco tequila
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   [1/2 oz. ginger syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/ginger-syrup-recipe/)
-   1/4 oz. Suze
-   1/4 oz. elderflower liqueur

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in a shaker with ice and shake vigorously. Strain into a chilled coupe. Express a lemon peel and use as a garnish.
