## Garnished with homemade mayonnaise, capers and horseradish, simple carpaccio is beautiful as an appetizer or as an entree.

**Cooks in:** _30mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 3/4 LB. of beef sliced very thinly
-   2 CUPS of corn oil
-   3 pickles
-   1 spring onion
-   1 large egg
-   1 egg yolk
-   1 small cucumber
-   1 fresh chili pepper
-   mustard
-   lemon
-   white wine vinegar
-   Greek yogurt
-   chives
-   2 TBSP. of fresh horseradish, grated
-   dehydrated capers
-   pepper
-   Salt

### Method

1

Grate the zest from one lemon and juice it. With an immersion blender, blend the egg with the egg yolk, 1 Tbsp. mustard, 1 Tbsp. lemon juice, 1 Tbsp. vinegar, and salt and pepper while slowly pouring in the oil. Continue blending to obtain a mayonnaise.

2

Chop the pickles and chives very finely and add to the mayonnaise along with 1 Tbsp. Greek yogurt.

3

Clean the spring onion and chili pepper and cut both into very thin slices. Wash the cucumber. With a mandolin, cut into paper thin slices. Place the beef slices on plates and garnish with mayonnaise, a few slices of spring onion and chili pepper, cucumber slices, 1-2 Tbsp. dehydrated capers, lemon zest and horseradish. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve as desired with crackers.
