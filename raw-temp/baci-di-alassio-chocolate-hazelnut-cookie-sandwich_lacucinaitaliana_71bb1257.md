### List of Ingredients

-   2 1/2 C. of hazelnuts, peeled
-   1 1/2 C. of sugar
-   3/4 C. of heavy cream
-   7 OZ. of dark chocolate
-   3 of egg whites
-   1 1/2 TBSP. of honey
-   1/2 C. of unsweetened cocoa powder

### Method

1

Bring the cream to a boil and pour it over the chopped chocolate; stir until it melts. Let cool. Toast the hazelnuts in a 350°F oven for about 10 minutes. Remove from the oven and let cool.

2

Blend them in a food processor with the sugar, obtaining a powder; combine this mixture with the egg whites, the honey, and the cocoa powder until you obtain a thick batter. Place the batter in a pastry bag with an open star tip; pipe small dollops on a baking tray lined with parchment paper. Bake at 400°F for 10-12 minutes; remove from the oven and let cool, then spread half with a bit of the melted chocolate and top with another cookie to form little sandwiches.
