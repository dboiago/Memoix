### Glass:

Serve in an [Old-fashioned glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

3⁄4 fl oz

[Gentian liqueur (e.g. Suze, Salers etc)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/4215/suze-gentian-liqueur)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Amaro (e.g. Montenegro)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/396/bws/amaro)

1 1⁄2 fl oz

Canadian rye whisky

1⁄2 fl oz

Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill an [OLD-FASHIONED GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of a fresh strawberry.

### How to make:

3.  [STIR](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-stir-a-cocktail) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-strain-a-cocktail) into ice-filled glass (preferably over a large cube or chunk of block ice).

### Garnish:

5.  Garnish with strawberry.

## Allergens:

Recipe contains the following **allergens:**

-   Amaro (e.g. Montenegro) - [Sulphur Dioxide/Sulphites](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/4177/cocktails/allergy-awareness-and-the-14-major-allergens)

## Review:

On the sour/dry side, this aperitif-style cocktail combines bittersweet amaro and gentian with Canadian rye whisky and citrus sourness.

[View readers' comments](#comments)

## History:

Adapted from a recipe created in 2017 by Caledonia Wright at Veneto Lounge, Victoria, Canada.

> ¾ oz Suze  
> ½ oz Amaro Abano  
> 1½ oz Lot 40 Rye Whisky  
> ½ oz fresh lemon juice
> 
> Original recipe, Caledonia Wright, 2017
