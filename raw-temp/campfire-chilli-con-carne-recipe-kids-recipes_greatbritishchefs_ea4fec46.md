[Matthew Tomkinson](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/matthew-tomkinson) adds a wonderful depth of flavour to his smoky chilli con carne recipe by using [coffee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/coffee) as the cooking liquid instead of the more traditional stock. Ramp up the heat with more or less [chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chilli-recipes) as you like, and serve with cool sour cream, fluffy rice and zingy lime.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Campfire chilli

-   800g of [diced beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   3 [large onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 tsp muscovado sugar
-   4 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), mild
-   2 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   2 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes), toasted
-   1 tsp dried oregano
-   960ml of [coffee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coffee-recipes), can use decaffeinated
-   395g of red kidney beans in chilli sauce
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### To serve

-   150ml of half-fat sour cream
-   1 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   2 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), optional
-   2 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), diced
-   2 [limes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)

1

Soak the dried chilli flakes in a little hot water for 2 minutes, then drain

-   1/2 tsp [chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)

2

Place a pan over a high heat and add olive oil. Once hot, add the diced beef and sear until nicely coloured. Set aside

-   800g of [diced beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes), cut into small cubes
-   1 tbsp of olive oil

3

Clean the pan, place over a medium heat and add a tablespoon of oil. Once hot, add the onions and garlic and cook until just beginning to brown

-   1 tbsp of olive oil
-   3 [large onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced

4

Add the meat back to the pan along with the sugar, chilli powder, paprika, cumin and oregano. Stir to combine

-   1 tsp muscovado sugar
-   4 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), mild
-   2 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes), toasted
-   1 tsp dried oregano
-   2 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)

5

Add the coffee and the soaked chilli flakes to the pan, making sure the meat is covered

-   960ml of [coffee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coffee-recipes), can be decaf

6

Cover and cook on the lowest heat possible. Simmer until the meat starts to fall apart, approximately 60-90 minutes

7

Meanwhile, add the rice to a small pot, cover in cold water. Rinse by moving the rice around with your hands. Pour off the white milky water and repeat twice more

-   300g of [basmati rice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basmati-rice-recipes)

8

Drain the excess water, leave to stand for 20 minutes. Add the 450ml of water and place the pot over a high heat. Bring to the boil, cover with a lid and turn down to the lowest heat possible

-   450ml of water

9

Cook for 12 minutes, or until the rice is just cooked. Remove from the heat and allow to stand, covered, for 2 minutes before serving

10

Remove the lid and add the beans, allow to cook until thickened and rich in flavour. Add a pinch of salt and pepper

-   395g of red kidney beans in chilli sauce
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

11

Divide the chilli across 4 warmed bowls along with the rice. Garnish with the sour cream, chopped coriander, fresh chilli and diced tomato. Serve immediately with wedges of lime

-   150ml of half-fat sour cream
-   1 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   2 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), optional
-   2 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), diced
-   2 [limes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
