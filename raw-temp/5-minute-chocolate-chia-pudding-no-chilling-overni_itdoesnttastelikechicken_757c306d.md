## Make It Your Way:

This chocolate chia seed pudding recipe is super flexible, so you can make it work for whatever you're craving:

-   **Sweeter:** Add extra maple syrup or agave, or stir in a chopped Medjool date. If you prefer a sugar-free version, use your favorite calorie-free sweetener added to taste.
-   **Richer:** I usually use soy milk or oat milk for the creaminess, but any plant-based milk will work great. You can even use canned coconut milk or vegan yogurt for a super creamy, dessert-like version.
-   **Mocha-style:** Try adding ¼ teaspoon of espresso powder along with the cocoa for a mocha vibe.
-   **Protein boost:** Swap the cocoa powder for a scoop of your favorite [vegan chocolate protein powder](https://amzn.to/4mFMMqC).
-   **Topping love:** Try [coconut whipped cream](https://amzn.to/43rTRne), berries, banana slices, coconut flakes, a peanut butter drizzle, chopped chocolate or chocolate chips, cacao nibs, a pinch of sea salt, or even a spoonful of [vegan nutella](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/3-ingredient-vegan-nutella/). 😍

## HOW TO MAKE CHOCOLATE CHIA PUDDING:

1.  **Mix the chia and water:** In a small bowl, combine the chia seeds and hot water. Stir to mix, then let sit for 2 minutes. The mixture will start watery but will quickly thicken as the chia seeds absorb the liquid.

![Chia seeds being stirred with hot water in a bowl to start the pudding base.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/5-Minute-Chocolate-Chia-Pudding-No-Chilling-Overnight-creamy-recipe-breakfast-for-one-ratio-02.jpg)

2.  **Add the milk and flavorings:** Stir in the plant-based milk, cocoa powder, maple syrup or agave, vanilla, and salt (if using). Whisk well to break up any clumps. (It's okay if the cocoa doesn't fully dissolve yet). Let it rest for another 2 - 3 minutes.

![Plant-based milk, cocoa powder, maple syrup, and vanilla being added to the thickened chia mixture.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/5-Minute-Chocolate-Chia-Pudding-No-Chilling-Overnight-creamy-recipe-breakfast-for-one-ratio-03.jpg)

3.  **Final stir:** After resting, stir again to fully incorporate the cocoa powder and break up any remaining clumps. The pudding should now be delicious, thick, and chocolatey.

![Thick and creamy chocolate chia pudding being stirred until smooth and fully mixed.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/5-Minute-Chocolate-Chia-Pudding-No-Chilling-Overnight-creamy-recipe-breakfast-for-one-ratio-04.jpg)

4.  **Serve:** Divide between two bowls, jars, or glasses. Add your favorite toppings and enjoy!

**Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days, or freeze in a sealed container for up to 1 month. Thaw in the fridge overnight and stir well before eating. Add toppings just before serving to keep them fresh.
