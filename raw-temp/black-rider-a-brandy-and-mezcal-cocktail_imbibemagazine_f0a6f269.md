![black rider brandy mezcal cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/Black-Rider_CRDT_christopher-flett.jpg)

A full-bodied brandy and a strong, smoky mezcal really make this drink sing.

Named after a Tom Waits album, a full-bodied brandy and a strong, smoky mezcal make this drink sing.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. brandy (Williams uses Torres 5-Year Solera)
-   1 oz. mezcal
-   3/4 oz. Cynar

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients, stir with ice, and strain into a chilled glass.
