### Cinnamon Grilled Pineapple

## Ingredients

-   1 ripe pineapple
-   1/2 cup melted unsalted butter
-   3/4 cup granulated sugar
-   1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

## Recipe Steps

**Step 1:** Preheat the grill to high. Brush and oil the grill grate.

**Step 2:** Cut the stem and rind off the pineapple and slice into 1/2 inch rounds. Core off each pineapple slice with a small donut hole-size cookie cutter or a small knife. Place the melted butter in one shallow bowl; mix the sugar and cinnamon in another bowl.

**Step 3:** Dip each slice of pineapple first in melted butter, then in the cinnamon-sugar mixture, shaking off the excess. Grill the pineapple slices until browned and sizzling, 4 to 5 minutes per side, rotating 90 degrees for crosshatched grill marks. Transfer the pineapple to plates or a platter.
