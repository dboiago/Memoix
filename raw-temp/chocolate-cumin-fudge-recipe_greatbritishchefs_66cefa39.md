Item 1 of 1

-   Thermometer

1

Combine the double cream, caster sugar and glucose in a pan. Place over a low-medium heat and allow to slowly come to the boil, whisking continuously

-   550g of double cream
-   500g of caster sugar
-   75g of liquid glucose

2

Bring to 118°C and remove from the heat, add the chocolate and cumin and whisk well by hand for 2 minutes

-   150g of [55% dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)

3

Cling film the bottom and sides of a 1inch deep tray. Pour in the fudge mixture and leave to set for at least 1 hour

4

Once set, slice into small cubes and serve
