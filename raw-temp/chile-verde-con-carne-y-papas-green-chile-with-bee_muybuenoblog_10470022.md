**Chile Verde con Carne y Papas** is a comforting beef stew made with tender chunks of beef, potatoes, and flavorful roasted long green chiles and tomatoes.

This recipe was originally published in our [_Muy Bueno_ cookbook](https://muybuenoblog.com/book/muy-bueno/). It’s especially perfect during the winter months or when [Hatch green chile](https://muybuenoblog.com/hatch-green-chile-recipes/) season rolls around in late summer.

![A serving of Chile Verde con Carne y Papas on a plate with warm corn tortillas, showcasing tender beef, potatoes, and green chile stew.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/authentic-Chile-Verde-con-Carne-y-Papas.jpeg)

When I first moved to Colorado, I thought “green chile” meant this exact recipe, but locals were actually referring to [Colorado style pork green chile](https://muybuenoblog.com/pork-green-chile-multicooker-giveaway/). Unlike those regional versions, this dish is inspired by the flavors of northern Mexico—where beef is more common and green chiles are often blended with tomato to create rich, rustic stews. This version is made with beef and is every bit as crave-worthy, especially served with warm [homemade corn tortillas](https://muybuenoblog.com/homemade-corn-tortillas/).

Every summer I freeze a couple bushels of roasted green chiles, which means I can make this all year long. It fills the house with the most comforting aroma and brings all the cozy, nostalgic vibes of old-world Mexico to the table.

This is also a perfect example of how a [homemade green chile salsa](https://muybuenoblog.com/salsa-casera-de-jesusita-jesusitas-house-salsa/) can transform into a satisfying and budget-friendly main dish with just a few more ingredients.

## Ingredients & Substitutions

This beef and potato stew uses simple pantry staples and flame-roasted green chiles. You’ll need:

-   **Jalapeño:** Optional for more heat; leave it out for a milder version.
-   **Canned tomatoes and tomato sauce:** My grandma used to can her own tomatoes, which is a great option if you have them. Fresh tomatoes may not give quite the same depth in this recipe, so I recommend sticking with canned for best results.

## How to Make Chile Verde con Carne y Papas

**Step 1: Blend the salsa base.** Combine roasted chiles, tomatoes, garlic, and seasonings until chunky.

![A food processor full of freshly pulsed green chile, tomato, and garlic salsa used in the chile verde recipe.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Chile-Verde-con-Carne-y-Papas-salsa.jpeg)

**Step 2: Brown the meat and potatoes.** Sauté with salt until tender.

**Step 3: Add garlic and flour.** Stir well to coat and cook off the raw flour flavor.

![Beef and potatoes simmering in a skillet with chopped green chiles and tomatoes on top.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Chile-Verde-con-Carne-y-Papas-recipe.jpeg)

**Step 4: Simmer with salsa and water.** Let it thicken and bubble until the potatoes are soft and the flavors meld.

![Close-up of tender beef and potatoes in chile verde sauce with a rolled corn tortilla and fork beside the plate.](https://muybuenoblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Carne-con-Chile-Verde-Recipe.jpeg)

## Chile Verde con Carne y Papas Expert Tips & Tricks

-   **Double the Homemade Green Chile Salsa:** If you’re already making a batch of [Homemade Green Chile Salsa](https://muybuenoblog.com/salsa-casera-de-jesusita-jesusitas-house-salsa/), consider doubling it. -   **Use poblanos for a milder version:** For a gentle heat and earthy flavor, you can substitute long green chiles with roasted poblanos.
-   **Roasted chiles:** If using frozen roasted green chiles, thaw and drain any excess liquid before blending.
-   **Customize the heat:** Adjust the amount of jalapeño to your taste, or leave it out for a milder dish.
-   **Make ahead:** The dish tastes even better the next day as the flavors deepen. It also freezes well.

## Frequently Asked Questions

******What is Chile Verde con Carne y Papas? (Green Chile Beef and Potatoes)******

It’s a Mexican-style beef stew made with tender beef, potatoes, and a chunky green chile and tomato sauce. It’s rich, comforting, and full of flavor.

******Can I make Chile Verde con Carne y Papas with pork instead of beef?******

Yes, but traditionally this version is made with beef. If you want pork, try this [Colorado-style pork green chile recipe](https://muybuenoblog.com/pork-green-chile-multicooker-giveaway/) instead.

******Can I use canned green chiles in Chile Verde con Carne y Papas?******

If you don’t have fresh or frozen roasted chiles, canned will work in a pinch, but the flavor won’t be quite as smoky or robust.

****Is Chile Verde con Carne y Papas the same as chile con carne?****

Not exactly. The term _chile con carne_ can mean different things depending on the region. In the U.S., it often refers to a Tex-Mex chili made with ground beef and beans. In northern Mexico, it might describe something like [Chile Colorado](https://muybuenoblog.com/asado-de-chile-colorado/), a red chile beef stew. In some households—including mine—_chile con carne_ could even mean a dish like [Frijoles Enchilados](https://muybuenoblog.com/frijoles-enchilados/), which is essentially a chili made with beans and ground beef. Chile Verde con Carne y Papas is its own unique recipe made with beef, long green chiles, and potatoes.

-   ▢ 1 tablespoon canola oil or shortening
-   ▢ 2 1/4 pounds stew beef, diced
-   ▢ 3 medium russet potatoes, diced
-   ▢ 1 1/4 teaspoons [salt](https://amzn.to/3vTupZx), divided, plus more to taste
-   ▢ 5 cloves garlic, divided (3 minced, 2 chopped or use a pinch of garlic powder)
-   ▢ 1 1/2 tablespoons [all-purpose flour](https://amzn.to/45btgZH)
-   ▢ 4 roasted green chile peppers, (Anaheim or Hatch), peeled and stems removed
-   ▢ 1 roasted jalapeño pepper, (optional)
-   ▢ 3/4 cup canned whole tomatoes
-   ▢ 2 tablespoons [tomato sauce](https://amzn.to/47v7dOc), (optional)
-   ▢ 2 1/2 cups water

-   In a blender, combine green chiles, jalapeño, 1/4 teaspoon salt, 2 chopped garlic cloves (or a pinch of garlic powder), tomatoes, and tomato sauce. Pulse until chunky. Set aside.
    
-   In a large skillet, heat oil. Add beef, potatoes, and 1 teaspoon salt. Cover and cook for 10 minutes.
    
-   Add 3 minced garlic cloves and cook 2 more minutes. Stir in flour and cook 2 minutes more.
    
-   Pour in the chile-tomato blend and water. Bring to a boil. Simmer uncovered for 5–10 minutes until potatoes are tender and sauce has thickened.
