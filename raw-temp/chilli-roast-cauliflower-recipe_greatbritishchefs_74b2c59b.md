Selin Kiazim's [chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chilli-recipes) roast [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cauliflower-recipes) has been on the menu since she opened Oklava, and has become one of London's most iconic [vegetarian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes) and [vegan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes) dishes. The quality of your chilli paste will have a profound effect on the final dish – you can find really good stuff online or in [Turkish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turkish-recipes) supermarkets, but most normal supermarkets also stock jars of Turkish chilli paste these days. Smear the paste all over your cauliflower, rustle up a [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/parsley-recipes) salad and you have a delicious meal in just half an hour.

First published in 2019

1

Preheat the oven to 200ºC fan or 220ºC conventional

2

Cut the cauliflower into 6 equal wedges – make sure you keep a little of the stalk on each piece to keep the wedges together. Rinse under cold water and pat dry

-   1 [large cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes), leave some of the green leaves on if they're nice

3

Mix together the açi and tatli biber chilli pastes with olive oil and a little salt. Rub the mix all over the cauliflower wedges (you might want to wear a pair of gloves whilst you do this), then place onto a baking tray lined with parchment paper. Roast the cauliflower wedges for 10-13 minutes – the cauliflower should be cooked but still have a good bite to it

-   2 tbsp of [Açi biber salçası/Turkish hot pepper paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   4 tbsp of [Tatli biber chilli paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   50ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   1 pinch of [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

4

To make the sumac dressing, combine all the ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth. Set aside for later

-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   300ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   1 tbsp of sumac
-   [fine salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

5

Take the cauliflower wedges out of the oven and place them into a hot, dry non-stick frying pan. Dry fry each side of the cauliflower wedges for a minute or so to get a slight charring on the surface

6

Place a cauliflower wedge onto a large plate and top with parsley, red onion and pistachios. Drizzle with plenty of the sumac dressing and an extra sprinkling of sumac

-   1 handful of [flat-leaf parsley leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
-   1/2 medium red onion, sliced as finely as possible
-   1 tsp sumac
-   5 tbsp of [pistachio nut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pistachio-recipes)
-   [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste
