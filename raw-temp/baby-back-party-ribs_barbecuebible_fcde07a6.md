### Baby Back Party Ribs

## Recipe Notes

-   **Active Prep:** 10 minutes
-   **Grill Time:** 1 hour
-   **Yield:** 6 people

## Ingredients

-   2 racks baby back ribs
-   2 teaspoons each: Kosher salt, coarse ground black pepper, and granulated garlic
-   1 cup of your favorite BBQ sauce

## Recipe Steps

**1:** **Preheat grill.** Set your grill up for two-zone grilling, with an overall temperature of 350 degrees F.

**2:** **Trim and slice ribs.** Remove the white membrane from the bone side of your ribs using a paper towel. Using a sharp knife, slice your ribs between the bones for individual grilling, then arrange them for seasoning.

**3:** **Season Ribs.** Season your ribs on all sides with the salt, pepper, and garlic powder.

**4:** **Grill ribs.** Place your ribs on the indirect heat side of your grill. Allow them to cook for 45 minutes to an hour, or until they reach an internal temperature of 195-200 degrees F. Rotate your ribs 3-4 times to give all sides even heat.

**5:** **Glaze and finish.** Glaze your ribs with your favorite BBQ sauce. The benefit of party ribs is you can use multiple sauces for different flavor options. Give your ribs around 5 minutes to grill until they reach an internal temperature of 205 degrees F. If you want more char, finish on the direct heat side of your grill. If you like a softer, smokier rib, then finish over indirect heat.

**6:** **Rest and serve.** Remove your ribs to a cutting board, then allow them to cool for 5 minutes before serving. Your ribs are already sliced, so they’re ready to enjoy once rested
