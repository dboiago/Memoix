## What is Brazilian Lemonade?

Oi Gente!

Brazilian lemonade, as you probably already noticed from the green photos, is actually **Brazilian** **limeade**, made with fresh limes. We call it lemonade because the word for lime in Portuguese is _limão_, so in Portuguese we call it _limonada_, which translates to lemonade.

There are a few different versions of this Brazilian lime drink, all of which feature fresh limes, but involve a different way of juicing and sweetening them. You can make _limonada_ with freshly squeezed lime juice, or you can use a whole lime, skin and everything and blend it all together to juice it. Then, you can sweeten it with sugar, [condensed milk](https://www.amazon.com/Lechera-Sweetened-Condensed-Milk-Ounce/dp/B010R3O4QA/ref=as_li_ss_tl?crid=W9LLWVTQW2E6&dchild=1&keywords=lechera+condensed+milk&qid=1601494496&sprefix=lecher,aps,201&sr=8-2-spons&psc=1&spLa=ZW5jcnlwdGVkUXVhbGlmaWVyPUEzVlhBRUQ5QVNLMVVaJmVuY3J5cHRlZElkPUEwNTYwODg4MlNMWDhFVTlSNDRWRiZlbmNyeXB0ZWRBZElkPUEwMDY5OTE2MlZTUDMwREpEVUUyNiZ3aWRnZXROYW1lPXNwX2F0ZiZhY3Rpb249Y2xpY2tSZWRpcmVjdCZkb05vdExvZ0NsaWNrPXRydWU=&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=849caf967eaf5ade9e06734ef1eb8eec&language=en_US) or sometimes both! Such a simple drink, so many fun and tasty variations.

## How to make Brazilian Lemonade

Any Brazilian lemonade recipe is super easy to make. I like to make mine _limonada Suiça_ style. Limonada suiça, aka swiss lemonade, is **limeade that is prepared using the whole limes, skin and everything.** Traditionally, _limonada Suiça_ is sweetened with sugar, but when I was growing up, my mom would make us lemonade with whole limes, water and [condensed milk](https://www.amazon.com/Lechera-Sweetened-Condensed-Milk-Ounce/dp/B010R3O4QA/ref=as_li_ss_tl?crid=W9LLWVTQW2E6&dchild=1&keywords=lechera+condensed+milk&qid=1601494496&sprefix=lecher,aps,201&sr=8-2-spons&psc=1&spLa=ZW5jcnlwdGVkUXVhbGlmaWVyPUEzVlhBRUQ5QVNLMVVaJmVuY3J5cHRlZElkPUEwNTYwODg4MlNMWDhFVTlSNDRWRiZlbmNyeXB0ZWRBZElkPUEwMDY5OTE2MlZTUDMwREpEVUUyNiZ3aWRnZXROYW1lPXNwX2F0ZiZhY3Rpb249Y2xpY2tSZWRpcmVjdCZkb05vdExvZ0NsaWNrPXRydWU=&linkCode=ll1&tag=braziliankitc-20&linkId=849caf967eaf5ade9e06734ef1eb8eec&language=en_US). ## The big secret in this Brazilian Lemonade recipe

Some people truly hate the idea of blending a whole lime with the skin on, because of how bitter they become. **The secret here is how long to blend the limes!** The longer you blend, the bitterer they’ll become shortly after they’re blended. There’s no need to blend the limes any longer than 10 or 15 seconds. Better yet, just pulse a few times, and boom – you’ll have a strong lime drink, that doesn’t turn unbearably bitter!

![](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/brazilian-lemonade-2.jpg)

Why the name swiss lemonade? [No one actually knows](https://g1.globo.com/df/distrito-federal/noticia/limonada-suica-no-dia-do-jogo-contra-o-brasil-especialista-fala-sobre-origem-do-drink.ghtml). There appears to be three different versions of why the name, none of which has been confirmed to be real or official. According to a Brazilian gastronomy instructor in an interview with G1, Brazilian news site, _“Some say a Swiss restaurant owner invented the drink. Some say the recipe was invented in a Swiss hospitality school. And lastly, there are sources that claim that the English actually came up with this recipe that blends the lime skins”_. Who cares, right? As long as it’s delicious, we can call it anything! haha
