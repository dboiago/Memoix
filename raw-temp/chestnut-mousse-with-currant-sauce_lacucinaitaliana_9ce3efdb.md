## Cook the currants with the sugar over low heat for 5 minutes after they come to a boil. Optionally, you can filter the puree through a sieve. Let cool...

### List of Ingredients

-   1 1/2 C. of chestnut puree
-   1 1/4 C. of heavy cream
-   1/4 LB. of currants
-   2 1/2 TBSP. of sugar

### Method

1

Cook the currants with the sugar over low heat for 5 minutes after they come to a boil. Optionally, you can filter the puree through a sieve. Let cool. Place the chestnut puree in a bowl and mix to soften. Whip the cream, stopping before it thickens too much (otherwise it will separate). Combine the chestnut puree with 1/4 cup of the whipped cream and distribute half into 4 glasses. Using a spatula, gently fold the remaining cream into the remaining chestnut puree; transfer the mousse into a piping bag with an open star tip and pipe into the glasses. Top with the currant sauce.
