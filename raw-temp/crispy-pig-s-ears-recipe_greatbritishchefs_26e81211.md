[Simon Hulstone](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/simon-hulstone)'s crispy [pig](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pork-recipes)'s ears recipe offers a frugal twist on pork scratchings, complete with a [floral nasturtium](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/edible-flower-recipes) tartare sauce. Feel free to double or even triple this recipe and keep the crispy pig's ears in an airtight container for 2-3 days. Hulstone uses broken up pork scratchings as part of the crumbing mixture for added crunch.

First published in 2015

-   Deep fat fryer

1

Wash the ears and place into a pan of cold water. Bring to the boil and then remove from the heat after 3 minutes

-   2 [fresh pigs' ears](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pork-recipes)

2

In a separate pan, add the shallots, carrot, leek, bay leaf, thyme and peppercorns and then add the ears. Cover with water, place on a low-medium heat and simmer for 2-3 hours

-   1 [shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes)
-   6 [peppercorns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)
-   1/2 [leek](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes)
-   1 bay leaf
-   1 sprig of [fresh thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)

3

You will know the ears are cooked when a knife passes through them easily. Remove the ears from the pan. Place between 2 sheets of greaseproof paper and press down with weights - blocks of butter will work well

4

Once cool, slice into 5cm long by 1/2cm wide strips. Prepare 3 bowls and dust each strip of ear in flour, egg and finally breadcrumbs. Set aside until needed

-   100g of plain flour
-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50ml of whole milk
-   Panko breadcrumbs (1)

6

Heat the vegetable oil to 180˚C and deep fry the ears until golden brown. Drain, season and serve with the tartare sauce
