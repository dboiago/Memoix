## Zucchini keeps this not too sweet cake moist while chocolate and pistachio nuts add body to this dessert that everyone loves.

**Cooks in:** _1h 30mins \+ 1h 55mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   1/2 LB. of zucchini, trimmed
-   1 1/4 CUPS of all-purpose flour
-   3/4 CUP of sugar
-   1/3 CUP of almond milk
-   4 TBSP. of chocolate chips
-   1/2 CUP of shelled pistachios
-   1/3 CUP of wheat starch
-   2/3 CUP of unsweetened cocoa powder
-   1/4 CUP of peanut oil
-   1 TBSP. of Acacia honey
-   1 PACKET of vanilla baking powder (Vanillina)
-   unsalted butter
-   salt

### Method

1

Preheat the oven to 350°F. 

2

Grate the zucchini into thin strips. In a food processor, blend the pistachios with the oil and the almond milk. Mix in the honey, sugar, and a pinch of salt; add in the zucchini and pulse.

3

Sift the flour, wheat starch, vanilla baking powder and cocoa powder together and add to the zucchini mixture. Blend to combine.

4

Butter and flour a bundt pan (7 1/2" diameter) and pour in the batter; top with the chocolate chips and bake for 50-55 minutes. Serve the cake cold.
