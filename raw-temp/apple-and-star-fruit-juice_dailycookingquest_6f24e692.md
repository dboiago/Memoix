> Star fruit has very high water content, so it's perfect to turn them into juice. This version with apple has just the right balance of sweetness and tart.

A star fruit looks very much like a star when you cut them (as shown in the photo), and that is most likely where the western name comes from. In Indonesia, we call this fruit _belimbing_, which has nothing to do with stars at all. They are great when juiced, often time just by itself, which is the most common form encountered in restaurants. I tend to combine multiple fruits when making a juice, so I am showing you a version of star fruit juice paired with apples and oranges.♥

### Ingredients

-   1 Fuji apple, peeled and cored
-   2 star fruits, peeled and seeded
-   3 Pontianak/Medan oranges, juiced (about 200 ml)
-   2 tablespoon honey

### Instructions

1.  Put all ingredients in a blender and process until smooth.
2.  Strain into serving glasses to get a clear juice and serve immediately.
