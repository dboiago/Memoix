### Glass:

Serve in a [Collins glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 fl oz

[Hayman's London Dry Gin](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/1473/haymans-london-dry-gin)

1 fl oz

[Lustau Amontillado Los Arcos Sherry](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/8422/lustau-amontillado-los-arcos-sherry)

1⁄2 fl oz

Lime juice (freshly squeezed)

1⁄6 fl oz

Demerara/Muscovado/brown sugar syrup (2 sugar to 1 water)

2 fl oz

[Thomas Henry Ginger Beer](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/367/thomas-henry-ginger-beer)

Garnish: Skewered lime slice wheel

## How to make:

SHAKE first 4 ingredients with ice. Open shaker and slowly pour ginger beer over other ingredients in shaker, then strain into ice-filled glass.

## Allergens:

Recipe contains the following **allergens:**

-   Amontillado sherry - [Sulphur Dioxide/Sulphites](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/4177/cocktails/allergy-awareness-and-the-14-major-allergens)

## Review:

A '[Buck](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/1989/cocktails/cocktail-categories/families)' is a tall spirit-based cocktail with ginger ale or ginger beer and lime juice. This particular [Gin Buck](https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/330/the-buck-gin-buck) is distinguished by also being based on sherry, which comes from Jerez in Andalucia, Spain – hence the name.
