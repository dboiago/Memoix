In the Old Fashioned from Chicago’s Cherry Circle Room, sherry and maple add a warming update to the classic.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. bourbon
-   1/4 oz. amontillado sherry
-   1 barspoon maple syrup
-   2 dashes Angostura bitters

## Preparation

Combine ingredients in a mixing glass with ice and sir to chill. Strain into an Old Fashioned glass over a large chunk of ice. Express the oils of an orange coin over the top of the drink, and discard.
