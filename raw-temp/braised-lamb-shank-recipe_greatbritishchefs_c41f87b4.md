This simple, one pot dish from [Shaun Hill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/shaun-hill) incorporates tangy [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chorizo-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chorizo-recipes) with soft butter beans and a rich red wine sauce. The dish is simple to make but does require the patience or foresight to begin cooking a couple of hours before you want to eat. Ask your butcher for shoulder shanks. If you can't get hold of them, you could use a whole shoulder instead.

First published in 2015

#### Lamb shanks

-   4 [lamb shanks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/roast-lamb-recipes), from the shoulder
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), chopped
-   1 stick of [celery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), chopped
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), chopped
-   400g of [tinned chopped tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   400g of [tinned butter beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-bean-recipes), drained
-   50g of [cooking chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), chopped
-   250ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

1

Warm the oil in a heavy pan or casserole dish then add the shanks and brown on all sides. Season with salt and pepper

-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   4 [lamb shanks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/roast-lamb-recipes), from the shoulder

3

Simmer for 5 minutes then add the wine and the same volume of water. Place a lid on the pot then turn down the heat to a slow simmer – or transfer to an oven at 150°C/gas mark 2 for 2 hours. Check every 30 minutes and add a little more liquid if needed

-   250ml of [red wine](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/red-wine-recipes)

4

Serve with mashed or new potatoes and plenty of chopped parsley
