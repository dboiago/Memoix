[Quail](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/quail-recipes) do pack a lot of punch for their tiny size but do ask your butcher for jumbo quail to ensure enough meat for each guest. Serve this [game](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/game-recipes) recipe from [Bruno Loubet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/bruno-loubet), instead of turkey, lamb or chicken if you're wanting to break Christmas convention.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Quails

-   4 [jumbo quails](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quail-recipes)
-   8 [pancetta slices](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pancetta-recipes)
-   300ml of chicken stock
-   6 tbsp of olive oil
-   200ml of sweet white wine
-   120g of [dried cranberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)
-   40 [red grapes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grape-recipes)

#### Stuffing

-   6 [pancetta slices](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pancetta-recipes)
-   3 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   150g of [mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes), sliced
-   1 [lemon zest](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), grated
-   125g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   8 sprigs of [fresh thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   100g of long grain rice
-   200ml of chicken stock
-   100g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   250g of [chicken livers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/liver-recipes), cleaned

-   8 cocktail sticks

1

To make the stuffing, cut 6 slices of pancetta into small pieces. In a pan, heat 3 tablespoons of oil. Add the pancetta and fry to a light colour. Add the garlic, mushrooms, onions, lemon zest and thyme

-   6 [pancetta slices](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pancetta-recipes)
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   150g of [mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes), sliced
-   125g of [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   1 [lemon zest](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), grated
-   8 sprigs of [fresh thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   3 tbsp of olive oil

2

Cook well, stirring occasionally, then add the rice. Mix well and add the 200ml of chicken stock and 50g of butter. Cover with a lid or a foil. Lower the heat and gently simmer for 15 minutes

-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   200ml of chicken stock
-   100g of long grain rice

3

Take off the heat and leave to rest for 2-3 minutes. Pour the rice onto a tray and cool down quickly

4

Clean the pan then heat the remaining butter. When it foams, cook the cleaned livers for 2 minutes only, then tip onto a chopping board and chop quite finely. Add to the cooling rice

-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   250g of [chicken livers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/liver-recipes)

5

Heat the oven to 200°C/Gas mark 6

6

When the rice is at room temperature, fill the quails with the stuffing and close each opening with 2 cocktail sticks. Wrap 2 slices of pancetta around each bird

-   4 [jumbo quails](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quail-recipes)
-   8 [pancetta slices](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pancetta-recipes)

7

Place the birds on a roasting tin and pour 6 tablespoons of olive oil over the top. Cook for 10 minutes then add the white wine to the tin and leave to cook for another 15 minutes

-   6 tbsp of olive oil
-   200ml of sweet white wine

8

Remove from the heat and place on a plate. Cover with foil and keep warm

9

Add the 300ml of stock to the roasting tray with the cranberries and the grapes. Simmer gently until the jus is a light consistency

-   120g of [dried cranberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)
-   40 [red grapes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grape-recipes)
-   300ml of chicken stock

10

To plate, place the quails on a large serving plate. Spoon the juices and fruit over and serve immediately
