The perfect marriage of whiskey and hops.

What happens when you mix the elements of a summery julep with a hoppy IPA? This Day at the Races Beer Julep!

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. rye whiskey
-   1/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1/2 oz. cane sugar syrup (1:1)
-   6-7 mint leaves
-   2 oz. IPA (Giger uses Bear Republic Racer 5)
-   Angostura bitters

## Preparation

Add the lime juice, cane syrup, whiskey, and mint leaves to a shaker. Lightly muddle the mint. Add one ice cube and shake. Add the IPA, give it a quick swirl to chill and pour the contents into a rocks glass. Top with crushed ice. Add a few drops of Angostura bitters on top of the ice, then garnish.
