A cross between two prizefighters: a Negroni and a Carajillo.

Red Fox in Boston’s North End combines two beloved classics—the [Negroni](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/negroni-recipe/) and the [Carajillo](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/carajillo/)—to create the Camparajillo. Still-strength blanco tequila and mezcal give the coffee cocktail a boozy agave backbone.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. Campari
-   1/2 oz. coffee liqueur
-   1/2 oz. Licor 43
-   1/2 oz. sweet vermouth
-   1/4 oz. still-strength blanco tequila
-   1/4 oz. mezcal

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a mixing glass with ice, then stir until chilled, about 20 seconds. Pour into a glass over a large ice cube. Express then orange twist over the cocktail and discard. Garnish.
