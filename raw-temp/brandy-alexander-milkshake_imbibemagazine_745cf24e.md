A classic dessert cocktail’s milkshake aspirations realized.

In his book _Milkshake Monday_, author and TikTok sensation Larry Canam wrote how he was inspired by John Lennon to turn the [classic dessert cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/brandy-alexander/) into a milkshake. The story goes that when Lennon first tasted a Brandy Alexander in March 1974 he “later said the drink ‘tasted like milkshakes.’” And considering the original is a simple mix of brandy, creme de cacao, and heavy cream, its transition to milkshake is seamless.

## Ingredients

-   3 scoops vanilla ice cream
-   1 1/2 oz. brandy
-   2 Tbsp. chocolate syrup

## Preparation

Chill a glass in the freezer for 5 minutes. Add all of the ingredients to a blender, then blend on high until smooth. Carefully remove the chilled glass from the freezer. Pour the blended milkshake into the chilled glass. Add the toppings to the milkshake. Begin with a generous amount of whipped cream, and grate some fresh nutmeg onto the whipped cream.
