[Xavier Boyer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/xavier-boyer) slowly confits milk-fed [lamb shoulder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lamb-shoulder-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/lamb-shoulder-recipes) resulting in succulent tender meat that falls from the bone. This is perfectly offset by bright spring vegetables and sweet [cumin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cumin-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cumin-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cumin-recipes).

First published in 2015

1

Preheat the oven to 120℃/gas mark 1/2

2

Heat a large frying pan over a medium heat, season the lamb shoulders with salt and pepper then colour each one in the pan until golden brown

-   2 [milk-fed lamb shoulders](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lamb-shoulder-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

4

In the same pan, colour the carrot and onion until caramelised, then add to the tray with the shoulder

-   1 [carrot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), chopped
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped

5

Wash the potatoes and add to the baking tray. Cover the ingredients with the olive oil and cook in the oven for 4 hours

-   10 [new potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/new-potato-recipes)
-   1l olive oil

6

While the lamb is cooking, prepare the vegetables. Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil and peel the asparagus and carrots

-   8 [asparagus spears](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes)
-   8 [baby carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)

7

Blanch the asparagus for 3 minutes, then refresh immediately in iced water. Repeat this process with the peas for 1 minute, the spring onions for 2 minutes, the carrots for 3 minutes and finally the broad beans for 2 minutes. It is important to blanch the broad beans last as they will discolour the water and affect the flavour of the other vegetables

-   100g of [peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes), podded
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   100g of [broad beans](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/broad-bean-recipes), shelled

8

Drain the vegetables, dry on kitchen paper and reserve

9

When the lamb is cooked, carefully remove from the tray and drain in a colander with the potatoes and garlic. Reserve the oil, as it can be reused

10

Place the roasting tray over a medium heat and deglaze with the chicken stock, reducing until a sauce consistency is achieved

-   250ml of chicken stock

11

Foam the butter for the vegetables in a frying pan with some salt and pepper, add the blanched vegetables and cook until nicely coloured. Finish with the cumin

-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes), crushed

12

To plate, arrange the vegetables, potatoes and garlic on two large serving dishes and place each lamb shoulder on top. Drizzle with the sauce and serve
