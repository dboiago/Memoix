This [macaroni cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/macaroni-cheese-recipes "Macaroni cheese") is one for those who enjoy the bold, sweet and spicy flavours of Mediterranean cooking. Nuggets of oily, paprika-flavoured [chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes "Chorizo") are combined with roasted red peppers and fragrant basil, all bound with a creamy three-cheese sauce.

First published in 2023

### Metric

### Imperial

-   350g of [macaroni pasta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/macaroni-recipes), or other small pasta shapes
-   2 [red peppers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes)
-   250g of [cooking chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), picante
-   1l milk
-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   50g of plain flour
-   100g of [red Leicester cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheese-recipes)
-   100g of [manchego cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheese-recipes)
-   50g of [mozzarella cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mozzarella-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [ground white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   100ml of cream
-   1 tbsp of [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 [bunch of basil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes), leaves shredded
-   olive oil, for cooking
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

1

Preheat the oven to 180ºC and place the peppers on a roasting tray

-   2 [red peppers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes)

2

Cook for 35 minutes, or until very soft and charred. Set aside to cool, then deseed, peel and rinse off the seeds. Slice into strips

3

Crumble the chorizo into a frying pan and cook until cooked through and crispy in places – around 10 minutes

-   250g of [cooking chorizo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chorizo-recipes), picante

4

Put a pan of salted water on to boil as you make the sauce

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), to taste

5

Heat the milk in a separate saucepan, but don’t let it boil

-   1l milk

6

Melt the butter in a separate saucepan and, when foaming, add the flour, whisking. Cook this mixture out for a couple of minutes, whisking constantly

-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   50g of plain flour

7

Add the milk a couple of ladlefuls at a time, whisking each addition gently into the sauce before adding the next

8

Once all the milk has been added, allow the sauce to cook for a couple of minutes, stirring regularly. This is important in order to cook out any remaining flour. Taste the sauce to ensure no floury taste remains; if it does, cook, stirring, for a couple of minutes longer

10

Stir to melt the cheeses, then add the white pepper and stir in the cream and mustard. Taste and add salt if necessary

-   1/2 tsp [ground white pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   100ml of cream
-   1 tbsp of [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)

11

Preheat a grill to medium

12

Add the pasta to the water and cook for 6 minutes, then use a slotted spoon to transfer it to the sauce, mixing well. Stir through the chorizo, peppers and basil and check the seasoning once more 

-   350g of [macaroni pasta](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/macaroni-recipes), or other small pasta shapes
-   1 [bunch of basil](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes), leaves shredded

13

Transfer the mac n cheese to an oven dish and sprinkle with the breadcrumbs, then the cheeses. They won’t cover the dish completely, but will melt together as they cook

-   50g of panko breadcrumbs
-   50g of [mozzarella cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mozzarella-recipes)
-   50g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes)

14

Place under the grill until browned and bubbling – around 10 minutes
