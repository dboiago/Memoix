Garlic roasted [chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes "Chicken thigh") with peas, [leeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes "Leek"), sweetcorn and mushrooms is mixed with a delicious creamy bechamel sauce and baked in flaky puff pastry to make the ultimate [chicken pie](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-pie-recipes "Chicken pie").

This recipe is taken from _Essential_ by Ollie Dabbous (Bloomsbury Publishing, £30). Photography by Joakim Blockstrom.

First published in 2021

Ollie says: _'This recipe is a meal in itself, but can obviously be served alongside some mashed potato and gravy, if you like. The decoration on top is optional, but it is far easier than you think. Just scatter it on and you can’t go wrong.'_

#### Pie filling

-   8 [chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), corn-fed
-   4 tbsp of garlic oil
-   2 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and quartered, then sliced across into 1cm pieces
-   25g of [salted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 [leek](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes), quartered, then sliced across into 1cm pieces
-   1 [celery stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), peeled, halved, then sliced across into 1cm pieces
-   100g of [shiitake mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes), halved
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed
-   200g of [sweetcorn kernels](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes), drained weight from a tin
-   100g of frozen peas, defrosted
-   2 tbsp of [thyme leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of [tarragon leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes), chopped
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested

#### Bechamel

-   500g of whole milk
-   1/2 [white onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), peeled and sliced
-   2 [cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [ground mace](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mace-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)
-   1 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 tsp [fine sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   50g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   25g of plain flour

#### To assemble

-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of milk, or cream
-   2 sheets of puff pastry, defrosted if frozen

1

To make the bechamel, bring the milk to the boil in a saucepan then add the onion, spices, mustard and salt. Cover and leave to infuse for 20 minutes, then pass through a sieve

-   500g of whole milk
-   1/2 [white onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), peeled and sliced
-   2 [cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [ground mace](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mace-recipes)
-   1 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 tsp [fine sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Heat the butter in a large saucepan, stir in the flour and mix until smooth

-   50g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   25g of plain flour

3

Add the hot infused milk a little at a time and whisk to combine until smooth. Once all the milk has been added, bring to the boil, whisking continuously, then remove from the heat

4

Preheat an oven to 180ºC/gas mark 4

5

Season the chicken with salt and roll it in the garlic oil, then place on a roasting tray and cook for 40 minutes, skin-side up, until the skin is crispy and the meat is tender

-   8 [chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), (corn-fed)
-   4 tbsp of garlic oil
-   [fine sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

6

Leave the chicken to rest for 20 minutes. Discard the bone and sinew and flake the meat, reserving any juices. You don’t need the skin here, but you can use it for an extra decoration of chicken crackling, if you like. Or just eat it!

7

Sweat the carrots in the butter in a medium pan for 5 minutes, lid on, then add the leek and celery, season lightly with salt, cover and cook for another 5 minutes. Add the mushrooms and garlic, then cover and cook for a final 5 minutes

-   2 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and quartered, then sliced across into 1cm pieces
-   25g of [salted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 [leek](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes), quartered, then sliced across into 1cm pieces
-   1 [celery stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), peeled, halved, then sliced across into 1cm pieces
-   100g of [shiitake mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes), halved
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), crushed

8

Add the sweetcorn, peas, thyme and tarragon, then remove from the heat and mix in the chicken and bechamel with the lemon zest. Check the seasoning and leave to cool

-   200g of [sweetcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes), (tinned, drained weight)
-   100g of frozen peas, defrosted
-   2 tbsp of [thyme leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of [tarragon leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes), chopped
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested

9

Once everything has cooled and you're ready to assemble. Preheat the oven to 190ºC/gas mark 5

10

Mix the egg yolk and milk or cream in a small bowl to make an egg wash

-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of milk, or cream

11

From the first sheet of pastry, cut out a circle using the top of an ovenproof frying pan as a guide. This is the lid

-   1 sheet of puff pastry

12

Cut a circle of greaseproof paper large enough to cover the base of the same ovenproof frying pan and come all the way up the sides. Use this as a guide to cut out a circle of pastry of the same size. This is the base. Place the circle of pastry in the pan, pushing it flat against the sides

-   1 sheet of puff pastry

13

Fill with the cooled chicken pie mix, making sure it doesn’t cover the top of the pastry rim. Top with the pastry lid, pinching the edges of both pastry circles together to crimp and join. (With some of the pastry trim, you may cut out some leaf shapes or make a simple lattice to garnish the pie)

14

Brush with egg wash and leave for 10 minutes, then brush again with egg wash and place in the oven

15

Cook for 20 minutes, then reduce the oven temperature to 170ºC and cook for another 20 minutes

16

To decorate, scatter over the vegetables, herbs and flowers, if using, and return the pie to the oven for a final 5 minutes for the decorations to crisp up, then serve

-   [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), shredded
-   red onion, cut into slim petals
-   [fennel fronds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes)
-   [fresh tarragon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes)
-   pansies
