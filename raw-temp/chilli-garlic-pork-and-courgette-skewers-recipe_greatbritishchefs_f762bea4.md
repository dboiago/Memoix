Shu Han Lee's delightful chilli [pork](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pork-recipes) skewers recipe is just the thing for [summer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/summer-recipes), with a punchy marinade of chilli garlic sauce and Chinese five-spice adding plenty of flavour to the tender pork before grilling.

First published in 2018

This marinade brings together all my favourite flavours – chilli, garlic, soy, sesame and five spice. I use it on all kinds of meat, or even more robust ‘meaty’ vegetables such as aubergine. In this instance, I’ve gone for grilled pork tenderloin, skewered along with slices of courgette for the perfect summer barbecue treat.

-   Wooden skewers 12
-   Barbecue

1

Cut the pork loin into even bite-sized cubes, about 2cm. Stir together all the ingredients for the marinade. Add the pork and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight

2

Meanwhile, place 12 wooden skewers in a large roasting tray of cold water to soak – this will stop them burning

3

30 minutes before you’re ready to cook, take the pork out of the fridge to allow it come to room temperature. Meanwhile, slice the courgettes into 1cm rounds. Toss with the groundnut oil and season with sea salt

4

Drain the skewers and alternately thread the pork and courgette onto them

5

Fire up your barbecue. Grill for 6–8 minutes until cooked through, turning often to get the pork and courgette golden and lightly charred on all sides

6

Finish with a sprinkle of sesame seeds and spring onions. Serve straight away

Shu Han Lee grew up in Singapore and moved to London in 2009. Author of the acclaimed Chicken and Rice, Shu is a food developer whose spice company, Rempapa, is stocked throughout the UK, including in Selfridges and Whole Foods. Shu is an authoritative voice in Singaporean cookery and her recipes come from a place of family, heritage and home.
