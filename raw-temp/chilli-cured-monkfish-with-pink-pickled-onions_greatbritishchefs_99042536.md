[Curing](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cured-salmon-recipes "Cured salmon") fish – once you have good quality fish! – is extremely easy. All you need to do is mix everything together in a bowl and wait. However, since it's such a simple fish we'd recommend using the best fish, citrus juice and olive oil that you can. We've used GAEA's Fresh extra virgin olive oil, which is made the first hand-picked olives of the season. This dish requires very fresh [monkfish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/monkfish-recipes "Monkfish") which is safe to eat raw.

First published in 2025

1

Mix all the cure ingredients together apart from the fish. Rub the mixture evenly over the monkfish fillet. Wrap in cling film and refrigerate for 4-6 hours to cure

-   800g of [monkfish tail](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/monkfish-recipes)
-   50g of white wine
-   50g of [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   40g of caster sugar
-   1 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested
-   1/2 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested

2

Meanwhile, make the pickled onions. Mix the vinegar, water, sugar and salt in a bowl until dissolved. Add the sliced onions, stir, and set aside for at least 30 minutes

-   100ml of rice vinegar
-   200ml of warm water
-   3 tbsp of caster sugar
-   1 1/2 tbsp of [flaky sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 red onion, thinly sliced

3

Once cured, rinse the monkfish under cold water and pat dry. Slice thinly

4

Arrange the radish slices on serving plates. Lay thin slices of monkfish over the radish
