A French twist on a classic.

At the French-inflected [Bar Chinois](https://barchinoisdc.com/), the Daiquiri gets a Gallic spin by way of apple brandy from Normandy.

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients with ice, strain into an ice-filled glass, then garnish.
