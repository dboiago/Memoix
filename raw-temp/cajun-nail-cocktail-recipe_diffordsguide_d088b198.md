### Glass:

Serve in an [Old-fashioned glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1⁄3 fl oz

[La Fée Parisienne absinthe](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/293/la-fee-parisienne-absinthe)

1 fl oz

Tennessee whiskey

1 fl oz

[Honey herbal liqueur (e.g. Drambuie)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/109/drambuie-liqueur)

2 dash

Peychaud's or other Creole-style bitters

2 dash

[Angostura Aromatic Bitters](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/145/angostura-aromatic-bitters)

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill an [OLD-FASHIONED GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of lemon zest twist.

### How to make:

3.  POUR absinthe into ice-filled glass, TOP with water and set aside.
4.  Separately, [STIR](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-stir-a-cocktail) whiskey, liqueur and bitters with ice.
5.  DISCARD contents of glass (absinthe, water and ice).
6.  [STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-strain-a-cocktail) stirred cocktail into absinthe-coated glass (no ice in glass).

### Garnish:

7.  [EXPRESS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1269/cocktail-garnishes/citrus-zest-twists) lemon zest twist over the cocktail and discard.
