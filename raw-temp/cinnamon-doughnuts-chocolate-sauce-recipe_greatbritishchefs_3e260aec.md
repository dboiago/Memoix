[Kevin Mangeolles](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/kevin-mangeolles) recipe for cinnamon [doughnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/community/recipes-national-doughnut-week-2012) with a hot [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocoholic-chocolate-recipes) sauce, makes for a fun [dessert](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetest-dessert-recipes) to get the table talking and put a smile on everyone’s faces. Not doughnuts in the strictest sense of the word but no one is going to argue.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Doughnuts

-   125g of plain flour
-   20g of sugar
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested
-   20g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   20g of yeast
-   1 tbsp of milk
-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

-   Deep fat fryer

1

To make the doughnuts, mix the flour, butter, sugar and zest together

-   125g of plain flour
-   20g of sugar
-   20g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes)

2

Mix the yeast and milk together and add to the flour along with the egg and mix

-   20g of yeast
-   1 tbsp of milk
-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

3

Place the mixture in the fridge for 2 hours. When ready, remove from the fridge, roll out to ½ cm thick and cut into thin strips

4

Heat a deep fat fryer and deep fry the doughnut strips in the hot oil. When golden brown, remove from the oil and roll in the sugar and cinnamon mix

-   sugar
-   1 pinch of [ground cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)

5

Place the cubes of chocolate and butter in a large bowl. Boil the milk and cream in a saucepan and then pour over the chocolate and butter, stirring until all of the chocolate has melted

-   5 tbsp of milk
-   3 tbsp of cream
-   75g of [dark chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)
-   10g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

6

Pour into a serving cup with the cinnamon doughnut strips on the side and serve
