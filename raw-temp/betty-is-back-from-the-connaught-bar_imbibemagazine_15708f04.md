A famed London bar creates a celebratory cocktail.

Named for the [Connaught Bar](https://www.the-connaught.co.uk/restaurants-bars/connaught-bar/)‘s head bartender during the 1940s, this sparkler was the first cocktail on the famed bar’s menu when it reopened in 2008. “Betty is Back is a dry, bubbly sip with delicate floral and citrus notes from a complex blend of Aperol … and elderflower liqueurs and fresh lemon juice,” writes Ago Perrone in his book, _The Connaught Bar: Recipes and Iconic Creations_, “while the Champagne raises a toast to this memorable wartime bartender and her customers, who kept returning to this iconic spot for more.”

## Ingredients

-   1/2 oz. Aperol
-   1 barspoon elderflower liqueur
-   1 barspoon fresh lemon juice
-   3 1/2 oz. Champagne

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients, except the Champagne, in a mixing glass filled with ice and stir until well chilled. Strain into a flute and gently pour in the Champagne. Garnish.
