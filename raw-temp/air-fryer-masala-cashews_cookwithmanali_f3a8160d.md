## Ingredients

**Cashews**: since this is the recipe for masala cashews, the cashews are obviously the main ingredient here. If you are not a fan, you can replace cashews with almonds.

**Besan (gram flour)**: for taste and texture, I have used besan here. It’s a gluten-free flour commonly used in Indian cooking.

**Rice flour**:  a little bit of rice flour makes these cashews extra-crispy. You can use cornstarch here if you don’t have rice flour.

**Ginger-garlic paste**: you can use either store bought or homemade ginger-garlic paste here. A teaspoon of this paste adds to the flavor.

**Spices**: the recipe uses three spices- chaat masala, Kashmiri red chili powder and turmeric. The Kashmiri red chili powder used here is not hot, it’s mild and mainly used for color.

If you prefer the cashews to be spicy, you can replace 1/2 teaspoon of the Kashmiri red chili powder here with regular chili powder.

Other than these, there is little oil, water and salt and pepper. 

_**Making these in the oven**_: if you want to make these in the oven, try 350 F degrees for 12 to 15 minutes. The recipe will remain same.

_**Storage**_: store these in an air tight container and they should be good for couple of weeks. 

## Method 

1- Take cashews in a large bowl and add 1 teaspoon oil and toss the cashews until they are all coated with the oil. Set aside.

2- To another bowl add- _besan_, rice flour, ginger-garlic paste, Kashmiri red chili powder, turmeric, chaat masala, black pepper and salt.

3- Using a wire whisk, mix everything together until well combined.

4- Now transfer this mixture to the bowl with cashews and toss it all together.

![step by step collage of making masala cashews in air fryer](https://www.cookwithmanali.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Air-Fryer-Masala-Cashews-Recipe-Step-1.jpg)

5- Start adding water, little by little now and keep mixing with your hands. Do not add more water than needed, you only need enough water for the spices to coat the cashews. Start with 1 tablespoon and then add more as needed.

6- I added around 2 tablespoons water here and all cashews got coated with the spices and besan mixture.

7- Pre-heat air fryer at 325 F degrees for 2 minutes. Then place the cashews in a single layer into the air fryer. If there’s any cashew which isn’t coated with the masala, you can just press some on top of that.

8- Air fry at 325 F degrees for 10 minutes, remember to give it a good shake at the 5 minutes mark. 

![step by step collage of making masala cashews in air fryer](https://www.cookwithmanali.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Air-Fryer-Masala-Cashews-Recipe-Step-2.jpg)

Remove the masala cashews from the air fryer and let them cool completely. They will crisp up as they cool. Store in an airtight container and enjoy with chai!
