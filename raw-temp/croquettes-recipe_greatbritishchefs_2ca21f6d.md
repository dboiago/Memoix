This recipe from [Marcello Tully](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/marcello-tully) enables your guests to have a choice of croquette: a meat option, containing [ham](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/ham-recipes) and pea, and a veggie option, with cheddar and sweetcorn. Serve these snacks as a [canapé](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/canape-recipes) or with a salad to make a more substantial dish.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Croquette base

-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   45g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   45g of flour
-   250ml of milk
-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), beaten
-   1 tbsp of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   80g of redcurrant jelly

#### For the coating

-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), beaten
-   2 tbsp of plain flour
-   95g of breadcrumbs
-   500ml of vegetable oil, for frying

-   Deep-fryer

1

In a heavy-based pan, fry the onion in the butter for 3-4 minutes without colouring. Add the flour and mix well

-   45g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 [onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   45g of flour

2

Add the milk and salt and continue to mix. Bring to the boil and heat until the sauce thickens. Remove from the heat, allow to cool slightly then slowly whisk in the beaten egg until incorporated

-   250ml of milk
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), beaten

3

Divide the sauce between 2 large bowls. Add the ham, peas and mint into one, and the cheddar, sweetcorn and parsley into the other

-   100g of [peas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-recipes)
-   1 tsp [fresh mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), chopped
-   100g of [sweetcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   120g of [ham](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ham-recipes)
-   50g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated

4

Cover each bowl and store in the fridge until cold and set

5

Using floured hands, divide the mix and shape into barrel-shaped croquettes (2 inches long). Keep on a floured plate, ensuring the 2 types of croquette are kept separate if catering for vegetarians

6

Gently roll the barrels in the flour. Then dip into the beaten egg mix, followed by the breadcrumbs, until the croquettes are well coated

-   2 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), beaten
-   2 tbsp of plain flour
-   95g of breadcrumbs

7

Set a deep fat fryer to 180°C (alternatively, fill a pan up to halfway with oil and heat until a sugar thermometer reads 180°C)

-   500ml of vegetable oil, for frying

8

Fry the vegetarian croquettes in the oil for 2-3 minutes until golden brown and crispy, set aside. Fry the ham, pea and mint croquettes using the same method

9

Drain the croquettes on kitchen towel to absorb any excess oil. Serve with some redcurrant jelly

-   80g of redcurrant jelly
