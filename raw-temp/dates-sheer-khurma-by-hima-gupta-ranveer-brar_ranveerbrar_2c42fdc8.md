-   Preparation Time: 11-15 minutes
-   Cooking Time: 41-50 minutes
-   Serving Size: 5

### Ingredients:

-   7 cups of milk
-   100gm roasted vermicelli
-   Few strands saffron
-   2 tbsps Ghee
-   ½ tsp green cardamom powder
-   1 cup ripe mango pulp
-   ½ cup sugar
-   1 tbsp blanched mix nuts, finely chopped
-   5-6 pcs of boiled dates
-   A pinch of nutmeg powder

### Method:

-   1\. Boil milk until it reduces to three-fourth of its volume.
-   2\. Heat some ghee in a pan, add vermicelli and stir gently.
-   3\. Add reduced milk, mango pulp, dates and stir it well.
-   4\. Finally, mix in all the remaining ingredients and stir well.
-   5\. Garnish the dish with caramelized golden sugar. Serve chilled.
