## Mix the 00 flour an the buckwheat flour; beat the egg and incorporate the flours, gradually adding in the milk. Mix until you obtain a liquid batter, ...

**Cooks in:** _1h_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 LB. of Taleggio cheese
-   14 OZ. of mixed mushrooms
-   3/4 LB. of savoy cabbage
-   1/2 C. of milk
-   1/4 C. of buckwheat flour
-   1/4 C. of Italian 00 flour
-   1 1/2 TBSP. of butter, plus extra
-   2 of cloves garlic
-   1 of egg
-   Extravirgin olive oil
-   Salt

### Method

1

Mix the 00 flour an the buckwheat flour; beat the egg and incorporate the flours, gradually adding in the milk. Mix until you obtain a liquid batter, then add a pinch of salt and continue to mix. Melt the butter in a small pot and add it to the batter.

2

Grease a pan (5 1/2-inch diameter) with a bit of butter and prepare one crepe at a time: add in a ladleful of the batter and cook for 1 minute per side; you should obtain 10 crepes. Trim the savoy cabbage and cut it into strips; clean the mushrooms and cut them into large chunks; cook the savoy cabbage and mushrooms separately with the cloves of garlic and a drizzle of oil for 10 minutes; just before they are finished cooking, remove the garlic cloves.

3

Remove the rind from the Taleggio cheese, then place the cheese in a pastry bag. Form two cakes, using 5 crepes for each: pipe a ring of Taleggio cheese along the circumference of one crepe, then add a smaller ring in the center; fill the spaces between the cheese, one with the savoy cabbage and the other with the mushrooms; cover with another crepe and repeat the process 4 times, finishing off the cake with a crepe on top. Do the same for the second cake, then bake at 350°F for 5 minutes.
