A simple twist on Irish Coffee.

This twist on classic Irish coffee from Rosewood Mayakoba’s [Zapote Bar](https://www.rosewoodhotels.com/en/mayakoba-riviera-maya/dining/zapote-bar), one of the 50 Best Bars in North America, is just the thing for a chilly day. Apapacho, which translates to “a warm hug,” combines hot coffee with bourbon and vermouth rosso and is topped with freshly whipped cream.

## Ingredients

-   3/4 oz. bourbon
-   1/4 oz. vermouth rosso
-   [1 oz. simple syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   1 1/2 oz. fresh-brewed coffee
-   1 1/2 oz. whipping cream

## Preparation

Pour the first 4 ingredients into the glass and gently stir. Add the whipping cream to a shaker with an ice cube and shake very hard. Strain over the cocktail, then grate the nutmeg over the top.

**Note:** The bar mixes a bit of [pinole](https://www.mypanier.com/products/rancho-gordo-pinole-azul-heirloom-blue-corn?adtype=pla&campaignid=18222648130&device=c&gclid=Cj0KCQiAqsitBhDlARIsAGMR1RheXpw_31VzyipmQZBBzS5qR1JEcpTS9kTAHac7kocx3TrPOJYaqV4aAi3EEALw_wcB&loc_interest_ms=&loc_physical_ms=9028784&product_channel=online&product_id=rgo054&product_language=en&product_partition_id=) with nutmeg (4:1) for the garnish, but nutmeg alone works fine if you don’t have pinole.
