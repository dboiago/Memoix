A berry-sweet taste of summer.

Florida bartenders need to know a thing or two about cooling drinks in summertime, and this bright mix of vodka, falernum, lime, and the season’s best blackberries is utterly crushable.

## Ingredients

-   1 3/4 oz. vodka
-   3/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1/2 oz. falernum
-   1/2 oz. blackberry syrup
-   2 dashes orange bitters

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients with ice, then strain into a glass filled with crushed ice, and garnish.

**Blackberry Syrup**In a saucepan over medium heat, combine 2 cups of sugar with 1 cup of water and stir until sugar has dissolved. Add 1 cup of fresh blackberries and reduce heat to a simmer; cook for about 15-20 minutes. Let the mixture cool, then strain out the solids and bottle the syrup for use. Keeps refrigerated for up to 2 weeks.
