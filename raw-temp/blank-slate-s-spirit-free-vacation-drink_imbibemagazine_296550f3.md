Obsessed with passion fruit, Blank Slate bartender Marcus Damitio wanted to use the fruit to create the ultimate tropical drink but without alcohol. In his spirit-free Vacation drink, he smoothes out the fruit’s tartness with orgeat and coconut milk and brightens everything with soda water, citrus, and mint.

## Ingredients

-   3/4 oz. coconut milk
-   3/4 oz. passion fruit syrup
-   3/4 oz. orgeat
-   3/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1 1/2 oz. soda water

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients except the soda water to a shaker with ice. Shake and strain into an ice-filled glass. Top with soda water. Garnish.
