A vibrant cocktail that cuts through the heaviness of winter.

This crisp, bright pomegranate cocktail is a refreshing counterpoint to the often rich, decadent drinks of wintertime.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. vodka
-   1 oz. pomegranate liqueur
-   3/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1/2 oz. Cointreau
-   1/2 oz. cranberry juice
-   [1/2 oz. simple syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)

## Preparation

Insert a cucumber ribbon in the glass followed by crushed ice. Add 1/2 a cucumber (cut up in 6 small pieces) and 6 mint sprigs to the shaker and lightly muddle. Add all of the ingredients to the shaker with ice. Shake and double strain over the crushed ice in the glass. Garnish.
