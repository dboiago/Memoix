Two lesser-known Italian amari meld with Campari, bitters and London dry gin in this assertively bittersweet Blessed Thistle Negroni.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. London dry gin
-   1 oz. Campari
-   ¼ oz. CioCiaro amaro
-   ¼ oz. Cardamaro
-   Dash aromatic bitters

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice and stir to chill. Strain into a rocks glass over a large cube. Garnish.
