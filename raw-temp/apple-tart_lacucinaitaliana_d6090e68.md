## The apple tart is the simplest to make and a dessert that everyone likes! Here’s the classic recipe that will win you over!

**Cooks in:** _1h_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _6_

### List of Ingredients

-   2/3 cup flour, plus extra for the pan
-   1/2 CUP of sugar
-   1/2 cup butter, plus extra for the pan
-   3 pink apples, like the Pink Apple of the Sibillini mountains
-   2 eggs
-   Lemon

### Method

1

To make a soft apple tart, use an electric mixer to whip the butter and the sugar together. 

2

Continue to mix, adding the eggs one at a time and the grated zest of 1 lemon, then the flour.

3

Peel, core, and slice the apples.

4

Transfer the dough to a buttered and floured tart pan, top with the apples.

5

Bake at 350°F for about 30 minutes.
