Light, refreshing, and spirit-free.

Quench that summertime thirst with this spirit-free grapefruit and cucumber tonic from Bar Marilou in New Orleans. Of course, there’s always the option to turn it into a cocktail, too.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. grapefruit cordial
-   1 1/2 oz. tonic water
-   3/4 oz. fresh lime juice
-   1/2 oz. cucumber juice
-   2-3 large basil leaves
-   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
-   Grapefruit Cordial
-   125 grams sugar
-   100 grams grapefruit zest (3 grapefruits)
-   12 green cardamom pods
-   250 ml grapefruit juice
-   1 1/2 grams salt

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients except the tonic to an ice-filled shaker. Shake and double strain into a Collins glass filled with crushed ice. Garnish.

**Grapefruit Cordial**Add the grapefruit zest, sugar, and green cardamom pods to a Ziploc bag and press out the air. Refrigerate overnight. Add the juice and salt to the bag and agitate it until the sugar is completely dissolved before straining. The cordial will last for 10 days in the refrigerator.

**For a Cocktail**Decrease the lime juice to 1/2 oz., the grapefruit cordial to 1 oz., and the tonic to 1 oz. Add 1 1/2 oz. of gin. Build and garnish as above.
