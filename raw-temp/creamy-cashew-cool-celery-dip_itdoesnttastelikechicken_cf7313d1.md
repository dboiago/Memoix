One of the treats I was actually pretty sad about giving up when going vegan was a classic Caesar cocktail. Now if you aren't from Canada you might not be familiar with this beverage. It's basically a Bloody Mary, but with Clamato juice. If you haven't guessed it already Clamato juice is a mixture of tomato and clam juice. I get it, not the most delicious sounding cocktail, but it actually is pretty great.

I made a vegan Caesar Cocktail in [this very old recipe](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/2013/09/30/vegan-caesar/) (terrible photography alert!) but, if you want all that flavour, and don't want to muck around with seaweed, you can just buy chips! There's a new chip flavour in town for the summer and I bet you guess what it is: **Kettle Brand** **Classic Caesar**. Boom!

My favourite part, it takes just five minutes to make!

This dip takes just 5 minutes to make and is insanely creamy without any dairy! Like cool ranch but with the added zing of celery salt. Vegan, oil free, gluten free, quick and easy recipe. Adapted from my [Creamy Cashew Caesar Dressing](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/2014/05/28/creamy-cashew-caesar-salad-dressing/)

##### 

Prep: 5 minutes

Total: 5 minutes

Servings: 1 sup

[PRINT](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wprm_print/creamy-cashew-cool-celery-dip) [PIN](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/create/bookmarklet/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fitdoesnttastelikechicken.com%2Fcreamy-cashew-cool-celery-dip%2F&media=https%3A%2F%2Fitdoesnttastelikechicken.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2016%2F06%2FCreamy-Cashew-Cool-Celery-Dip-Kettle-Caesar-it-doesnt-taste-like-chicken-sam-turnbull.jpg&description=Creamy+Cashew+Cool+Celery+Dip&is_video=false) [COMMENT](#commentform)

-   ▢ 1 cup [raw cashews,](https://amzn.to/42zDSmJ), (soaked or boiled if not using a high-powered blender, see notes)
-   ▢ ¾ cup water
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon [apple cider vinegar](https://amzn.to/4jtoG01)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon celery salt
-   ▢ ½ teaspoon [dried chives](https://amzn.to/3Mwai9i)
-   ▢ ½ teaspoon dill
-   ▢ ¼ teaspoon [garlic powder](https://amzn.to/40udWX6)

-   Add everything to a blender, and blend until completely smooth and creamy, stopping to scrape down the sides as needed. This dip will get even thicker when stored in the fridge.
    

If you do not have a high-powered blender such as a [Blendtec](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B000GIGZXM/ref=as_li_qf_sp_asin_il_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=itdottalich-20&camp=1789&creative=9325&linkCode=as2&creativeASIN=B000GIGZXM&linkId=285a9feb3befa9cef853503e84b26a6d) or [Vitamix](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00LBFUKIA/ref=as_li_qf_sp_asin_il_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=itdottalich-20&camp=1789&creative=9325&linkCode=as2&creativeASIN=B00LBFUKIA&linkId=31217a8855e0a642fd1f596edcd50a58), you will want to soak or boil the cashews first so they will be soft enough for your blender to process.  
**To boil the cashews:** add the cashews to a small pot, cover with water and boil for 10 to 15 minutes until cashews are very tender. Drain before using.  
OR  
**To soak the cashews:** cover the cashews with water and let soak for 6 hours or overnight. Drain before using.
