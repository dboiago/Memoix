December is looming fast, and the beginning of December marks the opening of advent calendars for many, whether it's traditional advent calendars, [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chocolate-recipes) calendars or something a little different, like reading a different book each day, doing a different family activity, or creating a new craft.

In the past I’ve made advent stockings out of baby socks and filled them with chocolates and treats, but mostly we have a shop bought chocolate calendar (and doors only get opened following good behaviour!). These biscuits are a simple cooking activity that can be done [with the children](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/features/children-christmas-cooking), and the only difficult part is making sure that you only eat one a day, as they are very moreish. I like to add ginger, however they can be left plain or, by substituting 25g flour for 25g cocoa powder, can be made chocolate flavoured.

Whilst keeping the [biscuits](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/biscuit-recipes) in an airtight container is best, they could also be threaded onto some string or ribbon and hung above the fireplace or on the [Christmas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/christmas-recipes) tree (just remember to poke a hole in the biscuits with a straw before baking).
