A heavenly combo of fruity, floral flavors.

Bartender Dan Sabo strikes the perfect balance of fruit and floral flavors in his blackberry-lavender soda recipe.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. blackberry purée
-   1 oz. lavender syrup
-   4-6 oz. chilled club soda
-   ½ oz. half-and-half

## Preparation

In a shaker, combine the blackberry purée and lavender syrup; add a few ice cubes and shake briefly. Add the club soda and strain into a chilled 12-ounce Collins glass filled with ice. Slowly add the half-and-half so that it creates an ombré effect. Garnish.

_Note: You can use a 10-ounce glass if you want less soda water. Adding too much water will mute the fresh berry flavors, so add a little bit at a time to suit your preference._

Lavender Syrup: In a medium bowl, combine 24 to 30 fresh lavender leaves with 2 cups of hot water; let steep for about 15 minutes. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into an airtight container; add 2 cups of superfine sugar and stir until it dissolves. Let cool completely, then cover and refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.
