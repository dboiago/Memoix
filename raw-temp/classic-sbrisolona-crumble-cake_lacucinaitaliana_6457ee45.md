## Learn how to make sbrisolona, a classic dessert of Mantua that has been prepared by local farmers as far back as the sixteenth century.

**Cooks in:** _1h 30mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _2_

### List of Ingredients

-   1/2 C. of butter, room temperature
-   1/2 C. of sugar, plus extra
-   2/3 C. of cornmeal
-   2/3 C. of flour, plus extra
-   2/3 C. of almonds, blanched
-   1/3 C. of hazelnuts, blanched
-   2 1/2 TBSP. of white wine
-   Salt
