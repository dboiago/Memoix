This floral, fruit-forward cocktail is a breeze to mix and low in alcohol—perfect for making at home on a week night or before dinner on date night.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/4 oz. Lillet Blanc
-   3/4 oz. vodka
-   1/4 oz. apricot liqueur

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice. Stir until well chilled. Strain into a coupe. Garnish.
