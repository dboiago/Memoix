Riccardo Camanini serves up his famous cacio e pepe 'en vessie' recipe, which entails cooking this classic [pasta](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/pasta-recipes) dish in a pig's bladder for a touch of theatre. The dish is a favourite at his restaurant on Lake Garda, [Lido 84](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/restaurants/lido-84).

First published in 2017

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Cacio e pepe pasta en vessie

-   1 [dehydrated pig's bladder](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/offal-recipes)
-   300g of [rigatoni pasta](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-pasta-recipes), Riccardo recommends using Felicetti as it is strong and wont disintegrate during the 30 minutes of cooking
-   135g of pecorino, aged black rind variety, grated
-   90g of extra virgin olive oil
-   3g of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly cracked
-   12g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), Riccardo uses Guérande

-   Twine

2

When the bladder is adequately hydrated, insert the other ingredients into it using a funnel, then firmly secure the bladder opening with kitchen twine

-   12g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), Riccardo uses Guérande
-   300g of [rigatoni pasta](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-pasta-recipes)
-   135g of pecorino, aged black rind variety, grated
-   90g of extra virgin olive oil
-   3g of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), freshly cracked

3

Cook the stuffed bladder in a large pan of boiling water, being sure to keep it submerged in the water and shaking it occasionally to mix the pasta inside. Cook for 30 minutes

4

Serve in front of guests by cutting open the bladder with a sharp knife and spooning the rigatoni onto plates
