This salad is full of sweet summery flavours and contrasting textures thanks to the silky [confit tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/confit-tomato-recipes "Confit tomato"), charred peaches and crunchy sourdough croutons. We’ve added a sweet-sharp tarragon vinaigrette, which compliments the fruits and creamy stracciatella cheese.
