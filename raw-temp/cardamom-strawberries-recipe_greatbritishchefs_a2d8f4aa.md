[Shaun Hill's](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/shaun-hill) cardamom strawberries recipe represents a magnificent way to spice up this [summer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/british-summer-recipes) ingredient. Serve alongside [ice cream](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ice-cream-recipes), a [summer dessert](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/summer-dessert-recipes), or simply on their own with a dollop of cream.

2

Add the orange juice, cardamom seeds, sugar and honey to a pan, bring to the boil then pour the mixture over the strawberries. Set aside for 5 minutes to cool and infuse

-   1 [orange](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), juiced
-   1 tsp [cardamom seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of caster sugar
-   1 tbsp of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)

3

Once infused, remove from the pan and serve
