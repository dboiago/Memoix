### POTATO SALAD

**_Ingredients_**:

1 pound boiled potato, cubed  
1 boiled carrot, cubed  
1 medium sweet pepper, diced  
1 pimento pepper, diced  
1 hard boil egg, diced  
1 teaspoon sweet relish (or try olives instead)  
1 celery stalk, diced  
1 teaspoon brown sugar  
2 tablespoons mayonnaise  
1 teaspoon mustard pepper to taste

**_Direction_**:

Some of the ingredients for the potato salad. All are diced. Pour all the ingredients into a deep dish.

Mix the ingredients with the mustard and mayonnaise until the mixture is moist. Above, the completed Potato salad.

Course Vegetarian

Cuisine International

Prep Time 20 minutes

Cook Time 25 minutes

Total Time 45 minutes

Servings 4

-   1 lb. boiled potato cubed
-   1 boiled carrot cubed
-   1 medium sweet pepper diced
-   1 pimento pepper diced
-   1 hard boil egg diced
-   1 tsp. sweet relish or try olives instead
-   1 celery stalk diced
-   1 tsp. brown sugar
-   2 tbsp. mayonnaise low calorie
-   1 tsp. mustard
-   Pepper to taste white or black pepper, or pepper sauce

-   Pour all the ingredients into a deep dish. Mix the ingredients with the mustard and mayonnaise until the mixture is moist.
