The Porn Star Martini meets the Mezcal Margarita.

Rye Patton, bar manager of Tango Room in Dallas, reimagines the Porn Star Martini as a mezcal margarita with some heat. “Tropical fruit and habanero peppers are a match made in heaven due to the spicy pepper’s fruity and floral notes,” he says, adding that “mezcal was the logical piece to bring this simple but delicious puzzle of a cocktail together.”

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. mezcal
-   3/4 oz. passion fruit liqueur
-   1 oz. fresh lime juice
-   [1/2 oz. vanilla syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/vanilla-syrup-recipe/)
-   3 dashes habanero tincture
-   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
-   Habanero Tincture
-   5 oz. everclear
-   5 oz. 100 proof vodka
-   1 habanero pepper (quartered and partially deveined, remove all seeds and about half of the veins)
-   Peel from half of green apple
-   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
-   Smoked Lemon and Orange Peel Salt
-   10 oz. kosher salt
-   5 oz. Maldon smoked salt
-   2 oz. dehydrated lemon peel
-   2 oz. dehydrated orange peel

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a shaker with ice. Shake and strain into a rimmed glass. Garnish.

**Habanero Tincture**Combine all of the ingredients in a bottle and let sit for 24 hours. If more time is needed, let the mixture sit until the desired spice level is achieved. Will keep indefinitely.

**Smoked Lemon and Orange Peel Salt**Add the diced orange peel and lemon peel into a spice grinder and grind until pieces are sub 1 millimeter, preferably smaller but not powdered. Add salt to the grinder and pulse once.
