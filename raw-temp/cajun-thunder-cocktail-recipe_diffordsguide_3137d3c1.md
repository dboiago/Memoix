### Glass:

Serve in a [Shot glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 fl oz

Southern/Louisiana-style whisky liqueur (e.g. Southern Comfort)

4 drop

Hot pepper sauce (red, vinegar-based e.g. Tabasco)

## How to make:

POUR ingredients into glass and serve.

## Review:

Sweet southern comfort followed by hot chilli pepper finish.
