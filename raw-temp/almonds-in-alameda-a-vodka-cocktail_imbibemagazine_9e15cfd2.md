A fruity and nutty vodka sipper.

The richness of apricot and orgeat combine forces in this vodka cocktail hailing from Oakland, California. The drink’s name is a nod to neighboring Alameda, home to distilleries including [Hangar 1](https://hangarone.com/), whose vodka is used in the cocktail.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. vodka
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   [1/2 oz. orgeat](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/homemade-orgeat-recipe/)
-   1/2 oz. apricot liqueur

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to an ice-filled cocktail shaker, then shake and double strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Garnish with a dehydrated lemon wheel and Peychaud’s bitters.
