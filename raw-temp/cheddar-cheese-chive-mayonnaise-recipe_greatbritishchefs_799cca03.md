[Nathan Outlaw](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/nathan-outlaw)'s recipe uses mature cheddar in this fantastic [cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheese-recipes) mayonnaise, which, in addition to providing an excellent dip can be used in sandwiches or on salads.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   3 [free-range egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   50g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   2 tsp [English mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of cider vinegar
-   50g of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), chopped
-   500ml of sunflower oil
-   [Cornish sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

-   Food processor or blender

1

For the cheese and chive mayonnaise, place the egg yolks into a food processor with the cheese, mustard, vinegar and chives. Blend for 1 minute, then slowly pour in the oil as the processor runs

-   3 [free-range egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   2 tsp [English mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of cider vinegar
-   50g of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes)
-   500ml of sunflower oil
-   50g of [cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated

2

Once all the oil is blended, stop the processor and add a little salt to taste. Remove the mayonnaise from the processor and transfer to a sealable container. Refrigerate until ready to use

-   [Cornish sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste
