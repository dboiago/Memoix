Inspired by his mother’s garden in Porto, Portugal, Chef Nuno Sousa brings the garden freshness of cucumbers and basil to this twist on the Margarita.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. blanco tequila
-   1 1/2 oz. fresh cucumber juice
-   3/4 oz. elderflower liqueur
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice
-   2 basil leaves

## Preparation

Add a basil leaf to a shaker and gently muddle it. Add the rest of the ingredients to the shaker with ice. Shake vigorously and strain into the glass. Garnish.
