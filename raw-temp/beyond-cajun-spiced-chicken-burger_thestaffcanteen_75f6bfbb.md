## 

The Slaw

To make the slaw, mix together the plant-based mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, sugar, apple cider vinegar, onion granules and celery salt. Put the cabbage and carrot in a larger bowl and mix with the mayonnaise.  
Season with freshly ground pepper, to taste. Set aside.

## 

Cajun Mayo

To make the Cajun mayo, mix the Cajun seasoning into the mayonnaise. Add a squeeze of lime juice. Season with sea salt and freshly ground pepper. Set aside.

## 

Burger

Cook the Beyond Burger® Chicken-Style according to the packet instructions

## 

Serving

When ready to serve, spread some of the mayo onto the bottom of the bun. Add a lettuce leaf. Top with the burger, a slice of smoky vegan cheese and a spoonful of the slaw. Serve with extra slaw on the side and grilled corn on the cob.
