Gajar ka halwa or carrot halwa is an [Indian dessert](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-dessert-recipes "Indian dessert") made simply from [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes "Carrot"), sugar and milk. Avinash recommends red carrots if you can find them, for a better colour and natural sweetness.  

First published in 2021

Avi says: _'Carrot halwa is a dish made all over India but with different variations. It can be rich, sweet and milky, but my version is almost like a carrot cake, with a few sultanas, pistachio and cardamom – not too sweet yet unctuous and moreish.'_

### Imperial

#### Halwa

-   500g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and grated
-   500ml of whole milk
-   100g of caster sugar
-   6 [cardamom pods](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes), husks removed and seeds crushed
-   1 handful of [raisins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raisin-recipes), or sultanas
-   25g of [ghee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

1

Put the grated carrot and milk in a heavy-bottomed pan, place over a high heat and bring to the boil

-   500g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and grated
-   500ml of whole milk

2

Once boiling, turn the heat down to medium and simmer, stirring regularly, until most of the liquid has evaporated. This will take about 30 minutes

3

Add the sugar, cardamom seeds and raisins (or sultanas) and continue cooking, stirring so the mixture doesn’t catch. Finally add the ghee and cook until the colour is deep orange and unctuous

-   100g of caster sugar
-   6 [cardamom pods](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes), husks removed and seeds crushed
-   1 handful of [raisins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raisin-recipes), or sultanas
-   25g of [ghee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

4

Leave the halwa to cool slightly before serving with a scoop of vanilla ice cream and chopped pistachio nuts

-   [vanilla ice cream](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ice-cream-recipes)
-   1 handful of [pistachio nut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pistachio-recipes)s, roughly chopped
