Carrot juice subtly sweetens a mix of turmeric, cinnamon and ginger.

![carrot cocktail](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/minnow-we-own-that-door-vertical-crdt-kelsey-marden-330x495.jpg)Inspired by the vibrant color and flavor of fresh carrots, this carrot cocktail adds three other earthy ingredients to the mix—turmeric, cinnamon and ginger—to complement the veggie’s inherent sweetness.

## Ingredients

-   1¾ oz. turmeric-cinnamon vodka
-   1 oz. carrot juice
-   ¾ oz. ginger liqueur
-   1 splash fresh lime juice
-   1 oz. absinthe

## Preparation

Combine the vodka, carrot juice, lime juice and ginger liqueur in a mixing glass with ice. Stir until chilled. Rinse a rocks glass with absinthe, then discard remaining spirit. Place one large ice cube in the glass, then strain contents of mixing glass into the glass. Garnish.

**Turmeric-Cinnamon Vodka**Combine 10 oz. of vodka with 1 cinnamon stick and 1 Tbsp. of ground turmeric in a jar or bottle. Let sit for 3 days in a cool environment. Strain the ingredients out of the jar before using.
