This Bitter Mai Tai calls Campari to the forefront.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. Campari
-   3/4 oz. Jamaican rum
-   1/2 oz. orange Curaçao
-   1 oz. fresh lime juice
-   3/4 oz. orgeat

## Preparation

Combine ingredients with ice in a shaker and shake to chill. Strain into a glass filled with crushed ice. Garnish.
