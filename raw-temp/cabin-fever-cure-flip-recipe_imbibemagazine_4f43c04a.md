![flip](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/cabin-fever-cure-ponyboy-okc-crdt-.jpg)

Two types of rum bolster this creamy flip.

Two types of rum meet the sweet spice of Benedictine in this creamy flip recipe.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. rum (Ponyboy uses Bacardi 8 Year)
-   1/2 oz. Plantation O.F.T.D. Rum
-   1/2 oz. Bénédictine
-   [1/2 oz. Demerara syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/demerara-syrup-or-turbinado-syrup-recipe/)
-   1 oz. heavy cream
-   1 whole egg

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients in a shaker and shake, without ice, until the cream and egg are emulsified. Add ice and shake again to chill. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
