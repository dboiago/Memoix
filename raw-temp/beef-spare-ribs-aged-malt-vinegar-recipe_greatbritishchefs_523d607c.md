[Pip Lacey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/pip-lacey "Pip Lacey") here presents a very simple but unusual way to cook brined [beef short ribs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-short-rib-recipes "Beef short rib"): deep-fry them. The brined [beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-recipes "Beef") is floured before it’s added to the oil so that it has a slightly crunchy exterior, and then served with a drizzle of aged malt vinegar.

First published in 2023

#### For frying

Once brined, cut the rack of ribs into single ribs and pat dry with paper towels
