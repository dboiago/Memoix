Inspired by [Matthew Tomkinson](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/matthew-tomkinson)'s time at Ockenden Manor, this [salmon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/salmon-recipes) with [cabbage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cabbage-recipes) recipe has since been adapted but it remains a winner - a fragrant, virtuous option for a midweek supper.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Sweet and sour cabbage

-   1/4 [white cabbage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cabbage-recipes), small, finely shredded
-   50g of icing sugar
-   100ml of white wine vinegar
-   50ml of sweet sherry
-   20g of [root ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), grated
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), sliced
-   100g of golden sultanas
-   50ml of sesame oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

#### Spicy sauce

-   450ml of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   400ml of white wine vinegar
-   1 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1 tsp [English mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   150ml of sesame oil
-   1 pinch of [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground cumin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [five-spice powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chinese-five-spice-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)
-   1 pinch of curry powder
-   1 pinch of [caraway seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caraway-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [ground cardamom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   1000ml of chicken stock

-   Skewers

1

Begin by preparing the sweet and sour cabbage. Sprinkle the cabbage with a generous pinch of salt and leave to stand in a colander for 1 hour. Rinse well under cold water and drain

-   1/4 [white cabbage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cabbage-recipes), small, finely shredded
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Heat the sesame oil in a pan and stir-fry the cabbage for 30 seconds. Add the sugar and leave to caramelise, then add the vinegar, sherry, ginger, chilli and sultanas and cook over a high heat for a few minutes until all of the liquid has evaporated. Season and leave to cool

-   50ml of sesame oil
-   50g of icing sugar
-   50ml of sweet sherry
-   100ml of white wine vinegar
-   20g of [root ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), grated
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), sliced
-   100g of golden sultanas
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

To prepare the sauce, simmer the honey and vinegar together in a pan until the mixture thickens and darkens in colour slightly. Add the coriander, mustard and oil and stir through

-   450ml of [honey](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   400ml of white wine vinegar
-   1 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), chopped
-   1 tsp [English mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   150ml of sesame oil

5

Cut each salmon portion lengthways into 4 pieces and place some of the pickled ginger between each slice. Skewer the pieces back together

-   [pickled ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), 1 small packet, sliced
-   4 [salmon fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salmon-fillet-recipes)

6

Place a large, non-stick frying pan over a high heat and add a dash of rapeseed oil. Once hot, season the salmon well and sear skin-side down until crispy. Turn over, remove from the heat and allow to rest - the residual heat should cook the fish to rare. If you prefer your salmon cooked through, keep the stove on and fry the other side of each fillet

-   rapeseed oil

7

Trim the roots from the pak choi, break down into individual leaves and wash thoroughly. Heat a dash of rapeseed oil in a hot wok or pan and stir-fry the pak choi leaves for 1-2 minutes until tender

-   4 [pak choi](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pak-choi-recipes), broken into individual stems

8

Reheat the cabbage and divide evenly onto warm plates. Place the pak choi leaves next to the cabbage and arrange the salmon on top. Pour the spicy sauce over each plate and serve immediately
