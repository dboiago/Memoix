2

Toast the buns in a pan until golden brown and crisp

-   4 [burger buns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), halved

3

Mix together the melted butter and dill, then brush on the toasted buns, reserving some for drizzling

-   50g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), melted
-   1 handful of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), finely chopped

4

Rub the mushrooms with some oil and then fry in the pan until tender and browned

-   4 [portobello mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes)
-   oil, for cooking

6

Spread the mayonnaise on the buns, then top with the mushroom, black pudding and fried eggs. Serve immediately
