This aromatic braised [beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beef-recipes) recipe is given a new lease of life thanks to the addition of [five-spice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chinese-five-spice-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chinese-five-spice-recipes), star anise, chilli and [soy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/soy-sauce-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/soy-sauce-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/soy-sauce-recipes), which bolster the rich sauce that coats the chuck steak. A perfect midweek dinner full or warming, fragrant flavours.

First published in 2019

### Metric

### Imperial

-   2 tbsp of rapeseed oil
-   1 small red onion, finely sliced
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), quartered
-   1 [inch piece ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   1 tsp [five-spice powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chinese-five-spice-recipes)
-   1kg chuck steak, diced
-   2 tbsp of [spelt flour](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spelt-recipes)
-   2 tsp brown sugar
-   125ml of sherry, or dry white wine
-   1 beef stock cube, dissolved into 500ml boiling water
-   6 tsp [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes), (reduced salt)
-   100g of [Chinese cabbage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cabbage-recipes)
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 handful of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), leaves picked

1

Preheat the oven to 150°C/gas mark 2

2

Place a large ovenproof casserole-style pot over a medium heat and add half the rapeseed oil along with the onion, ginger, garlic, chilli, star anise and the five-spice. Sweat without colour for approximately 3 minutes (or until just cooked). Remove from the pan and put to one side

-   2 tbsp of rapeseed oil
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), quartered
-   1 small red onion, finely sliced
-   1 [inch piece ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   1 tsp [five-spice powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chinese-five-spice-recipes)

3

Place the diced beef in a large bowl and toss with the spelt flour to coat evenly

-   1kg chuck steak, diced
-   2 tbsp of [spelt flour](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spelt-recipes)

4

Add the remaining oil to the pot and fry off the beef until nice and evenly coloured – you may need to work in batches

5

Return the onion and chilli mixture to the casserole with the beef. Add the brown sugar and sherry (or white wine) then bring to the boil

-   2 tsp brown sugar
-   125ml of sherry, or dry white wine

6

Once boiling, pour in the beef stock and soy sauce. Bring back to the boil

-   6 tsp [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes), (reduced salt)
-   1 beef stock cube, dissolved into 500ml boiling water

7

Place the lid on top of the pot or cover with greaseproof paper and cook in the oven for 60–90 minutes until the meat is soft and tender

8

Serve with boiled basmati rice and sprinkle the cabbage, spring onions and coriander over the top to finish

-   100g of [Chinese cabbage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cabbage-recipes)
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 handful of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes), leaves picked
