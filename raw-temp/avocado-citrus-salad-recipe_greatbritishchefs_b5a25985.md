This avocado salad is a deliciously refreshing twist on [guacamole](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/guacamole-recipes), and makes a perfect accompaniment to fish or even a Summer [barbecue](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/barbecue-recipes). [Bruno Loubet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/bruno-loubet) pairs crushed avocado with orange segments to create a perfect balance between creamy richness and zingy citrus.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   3 [avocados](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), halved and de-stoned
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   2 [oranges](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), cut into segments
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   20ml of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

1

Scoop the flesh of the avocados into a bowl and crush with a fork, making sure you keep hold of the skins

-   3 [avocados](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), halved and de-stoned

2

Season with the lemon juice and salt and pepper, then stir in the spring onions, olive oil and orange segments

-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   4 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely chopped
-   20ml of olive oil
-   2 [oranges](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), cut into segments

3

Scoop the mixture back into the skins and serve immediately
