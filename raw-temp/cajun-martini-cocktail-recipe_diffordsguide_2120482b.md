### Glass:

Serve in a [Martini glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

2 1⁄2 fl oz

Chili pepper flavoured vodka

1⁄2 fl oz

[Strucchi Dry Vermouth](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/8201/strucchi-vermouth-dry)

Garnish: Chilli pepper slice

## How to make:

STIR vermouth with ice. Strain, discarding vermouth to leave only a coating on the ice. Pour pepper vodka into mixing glass, stir with coated ice and strain into chilled glass.

## Allergens:

Recipe contains the following **allergens:**

-   Dry vermouth - [Sulphur Dioxide/Sulphites](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/4177/cocktails/allergy-awareness-and-the-14-major-allergens)
