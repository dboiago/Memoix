**Cooks in:** _1h_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   2 1/2 C. of cream
-   1 1/4 C. of milk
-   5 OZ. of dark chocolate
-   1/3 C. of sugar
-   1/4 OZ. of isinglass (or gelatin leaves)
-   3 egg yolks
-   8 ladyfingers
-   1 orange
-   Amaretto cookies
-   Grand Marnier

### Method

1

Soak the isinglass in cold water. Heat the milk without allowing it to boil. In a bowl, mix the egg yolks with the sugar and 8 crumbled amaretto cookies. Drizzle the warm milk over this, then heat the mixture over the lowest possible heat, heating it to 175°F (it must not boil), then add the softened isinglass and let it cool until it begins to thicken, then fold in 1 3/4 cups whipped cream. 

2

Meanwhile, chop the chocolate and place it in a small saucepan with 2/3 cup cold water, 4 tablespoons Grand Marnier and the grated rind of 1 orange and simmer this mixture until creamy.

3

Place a ladyfinger in the bottom of each cup, then spread the chocolate mixture over them and let them cool to room temperature. Pour the Bavarian cream over each one.

4

Put the trifles in the refrigerator and, at serving time, garnish them with dollops of the remaining whipped cream and some amaretto cookies.
