This simple [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes "Cauliflower") couscous recipe makes a wonderful and [healthy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/healthy-recipes "Healthy ") salad or side dish and happens to be both [gluten-free](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/gluten-free-recipes "Gluten-free") and [vegan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegan-recipes "Vegan"). Raw cauliflower is blended up to resemble couscous and mixed with plenty of fresh herbs, [pomegranate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pomegranate-recipes "Pomegranate") seeds and grapes for a delicious salad full of flavour and texture.

This recipe is taken from _Essential_ by Ollie Dabbous (Bloomsbury Publishing, £30). Photography by Joakim Blockstrom

First published in 2021

Ollie says: _'A great vegetarian dish that is moreish, full of nutrients and can be prepared in minutes. Feel free to add different dried fruits, spices, herbs or nuts: whatever you have in your cupboards will be just fine… Figs, basil, apricots or coriander would all be most welcome here. This is great with barbecued meat and fish, especially if it has Middle Eastern flavours.'_

-   Blender

1

Make a cup of tea with the jasmine teabag and add the sultanas. Leave to soak for a couple of hours ideally, or at least while you prepare the other ingredients

-   1 [jasmine teabag](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tea-recipes)
-   30g of sultanas

2

Blend the cauliflower in a food processor until the texture resembles couscous. Transfer to a mixing bowl and season lightly with salt

-   250g of [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes), chopped
-   [fine sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Toast the nuts and seeds in a hot dry pan – or in an oven preheated to 180ºC – until golden, season lightly with salt, then add to the cauliflower along with the herbs, olive oil, lemon zest and juice, pomegranate, grapes, garlic and drained sultanas

-   5 tbsp of mixed nuts and seeds, such as pine nuts, pistachios and poppy seeds
-   1 tbsp of [chopped dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [chopped parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), or chopped lovage
-   1 tbsp of [fresh mint leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), chopped
-   30g of olive oil
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), zested and juiced
-   1 [pomegranate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pomegranate-recipes), deseeded
-   40g of [red grapes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grape-recipes), halved
-   1/2 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced

4

Keep at room temperature until serving, as it's far more enjoyable to eat than when it’s straight out of the refrigerator. Serve the couscous in a bowl with labneh on the side, topped with a generous scattering of dukkah

-   Sesame labneh
-   dukkah
