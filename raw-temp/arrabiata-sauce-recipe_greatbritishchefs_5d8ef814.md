Arrabiata means 'angry', so it is no surprise that this traditional Italian [tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/tomato-recipes) sauce packs a bit of a punch. [Paul Ainsworth's](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/paul-ainsworth) arrabiata sauce recipe is taken from his [arancini](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/arancini-recipe-arrabiata-sauce) dish where it is served alongside the Italian snack – but the sauce can also accompany meat or pasta.

First published in 2015

-   1/2 tsp Tabasco
-   1/2 tbsp of [garlic purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)

1

Place a large pan over a medium heat and add olive oil. Once hot, sweat the onions and chilli until soft

-   1 tsp olive oil
-   1 [large white onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), diced
-   1 1/2 [red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely diced

2

Stir in the cayenne pepper, smoked paprika and seasoning, then add the balsamic vinegar and sugar. Mix well and leave to reduce until the vinegar thickens

-   1 1/2 tsp [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)
-   1 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   300ml of balsamic vinegar
-   40g of caster sugar

3

Add the tinned tomatoes, thyme, Tabasco, garlic and bay leaf, then stir well. Allow the sauce to simmer for at least 2 hours over a low heat until nice and thick. Check the seasoning throughout the cooking process

-   3kg [tinned chopped tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp Tabasco
-   1/2 tbsp of [garlic purée](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1 bay leaf

4

Store any leftover sauce in the fridge for several days after cooking, or store in sealable jars - if the jars are sterilised and sealed properly, the sauce will last for several months stored in a cool, dark place
