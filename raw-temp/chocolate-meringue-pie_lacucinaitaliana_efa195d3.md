**Cooks in:** _2h 50mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _10_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 2/3 C. of flour, plus extra
-   1 1/2 STICK of butter, plus extra
-   1/3 C. of granulated sugar
-   1/3 C. of chocolate chips
-   2 1/2 TBSP. of Powdered sugar
-   3 of egg yolks
-   Salt
-   1/2 C. of almond flour
-   1 STICK of butter
-   1/4 LB. of dark chocolate
-   2/3 C. of powdered sugar
-   1/4 C. of granulated sugar
-   2 OZ. of pine nuts
-   1 of egg white
-   2 of eggs
-   Salt

### Method

1

Place the chocolate chips in the freezer. Cream the butter with 3 egg yolks, 1/3 cup of granulated sugar, 2 1/2 tablespoons of powdered sugar, and a pinch of salt until fluffy.|Use a spoon to mix in the flour, then knead with your hands on a floured work surface. Finally, mix in the chocolate chips as soon as you remove them from the freezer. Let the dough chill in the refrigerator for 1 hour.|Beat the egg whites with a pinch of salt and 2 1/2 tablespoons of granulated sugar. Then gently incorporate the pine nuts.|Melt the chocolate. In a bowl, beat a stick of butter with the remaining granulated sugar and a pinch of salt for about 15 minutes, then add in 2 egg yolks and continue to beat for a little while longer; finally, add in the melted chocolate and the almond flour.|Continue mixing with a spoon until you obtain an even mixture. Beat 2 egg whites with 2/3 cup of powdered sugar and pinch of salt; then fold them gently into the chocolate mixture.|Butter and flour a round baking pan (10.5-inch diameter, 1.5-inch height). Roll out the dough until it is about 1/8-inch thick; use the rolling pin to help you line the baking pan with the dough. Trim the edges.|Pour the filling into the crust. Top with the meringue and bake at 350°F for 35-40 minutes.
