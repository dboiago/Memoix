Cognac and rye share the spotlight.

For his take he [classic Sazerac](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/sazerac-cocktail-recipe/), Fandi Mata Beverage Director Milos Zica combines a split base of Cognac and rye, then plays up the drink’s spice with cinnamon syrup.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. cognac
-   1 oz. rye 
-   [1/2 oz. cinnamon syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/cinnamon-syrup-recipe/) 
-   5 dashes Peychaud's bitters
-   1 dash Angostura bitters
-   absinthe
-   lemon peel

## Preparation

Add first five ingredients to a mixing glass with ice and stir. Spray absinthe on the glass or do an absinthe rinse. Strain the stirred mixture into the glass, express a lemon peel over the rim, and discard the peel.
