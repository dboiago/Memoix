**Recipe:** _Matty Clark for [Dutch Kills](https://www.dutchkillsbar.com/), NYC_

![](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/holiday-whiskey-base-camp-crdt-john-valls-scaled.jpg)

A whisk(e)y cocktail for the holidays.

Layered with hints of chocolate, orange, and spice, this cocktail from Dutch Kills head bartender Matty Clark is holiday spirit in a glass. “The scotch leaves a nice fireplace note on the finish,” says Clark. “It checks all those boxes for the Old Fashioned–style cocktail you want to drink on a snowy night.”

## Ingredients

-   1 1⁄2 oz. bourbon
-   3⁄8 oz. smoky single malt scotch (Clark uses Laphroaig 10-year-old)
-   1⁄4 oz. crème de cacao
-   1⁄4 oz. allspice liqueur
-   2 dashes orange bitters

## Preparation

Combine all of the ingredients in an Old Fashioned glass, then add ice and stir to chill. Garnish.
