[Alfred Prasad](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/alfred-prasad) exploits the meaty and robust quality of [aubergines](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/aubergine-recipes) in this striking [vegetarian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes) recipe, using the vegetable to wrap up a gently spiced and aromatic [quinoa](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/quinoa-recipes), paneer and crunchy vegetable filling. A perfect mix of textures and flavours, this recipe could easily be multiplied to feed a crowd.

First published in 2015

1

Wash and cook the quinoa as per packet instructions, using water or stock. Drain and set aside

-   100g of [quinoa](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quinoa-recipes)

2

Mix 3 1/2 tablespoons of olive oil, toasted cumin and salt in a bowl

-   3 1/2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes), toasted
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Trim the aubergines and slice lengthways into 4mm thick slices. Brush the slices generously with the oil marinade

-   3 [medium aubergines](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/aubergine-recipes)

4

Place a griddle pan or large frying pan over a medium heat and add 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Once hot, fry the aubergine slices for approximately 2-3 minutes on each side until soft and coloured

-   1 tbsp of olive oil

5

Once all the aubergine slices are cooked, set aside on kitchen towel to drain

6

For the paneer and quinoa filling, place a non-stick pan over a medium heat and add vegetable oil. Once hot, add the onion and sauté until a light golden brown colour

-   1 [small onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), chopped
-   1 dash of vegetable oil

8

Add the cooked quinoa and cook for a further 5-7 minutes, then add the grated paneer and remove from the heat. Finish with garam masala powder and chopped coriander

-   100g of [paneer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paneer-recipes), grated
-   1 tsp [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   1/4 bunch of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)

9

Lay out the aubergine slices on a chopping board. Spread a generous layer of the filling on top of each and sprinkle over the vegetables. Roll up each slice to enclose the filling

-   1 [red beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes), coarsely grated
-   2 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), coarsely grated
-   1 [red pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pepper-recipes), cut small

10

Stack the aubergine rolls onto plates and serve immediately
