### Glass:

Serve in a [Copper mug or Collins glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 fresh

Strawberries (hulled, small & ripe)

4 fresh

Basil leaves

2 fl oz

[Pallini Limoncello liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/354/pallini-limoncello)

1⁄2 fl oz

Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)

3 fl oz

[Thomas Henry Ginger Beer](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/367/thomas-henry-ginger-beer) chilled

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [COPPER MUG OR COLLINS GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of basil leaf.

### How to make:

3.  [MUDDLE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-muddle) strawberry and basil in base of shaker.
4.  ADD limoncello and lemon juice.
5.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) with ice.
6.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into ice-filled glass/mug.
7.  TOP with ginger beer.

### Garnish:

8.  Garnish with basil sprig bouquet.
