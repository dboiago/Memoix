## Mix the flours with the sugar...

### List of Ingredients

-   2 C. of flour
-   1 STICK of salted butter, softened
-   1/4 C. of almond flour
-   1 1/2 TBSP. of sugar
-   2 TBSP. of unsweetened cocoa powder
-   1 of egg
-   1 1/4 C. of heavy cream
-   1 C. of sugar
-   2 TBSP. of butter
-   Salted popcorn

### Method

1

Mix the flours with the sugar

2

and the cocoa powder, then combine with the softened butter until a crumbly mixture forms; then incorporate the egg

3

and form the dough into a ball; wrap in parchment paper and chill in the fridge for 1 hour. Roll out the dough until it is 1/8-inch thick and use it to line a pan with a removable bottom (7 to 8 inches in diameter). Line the bottom of the crust with parchment paper, cover it with dried beans, and bake at 350°F for 20 minutes. Remove from the oven, discard the parchment paper and beans, and bake for another 5 minutes. Remove the crust from the oven and let cool.

4

FOR THE CARAMEL Heat 2/3 cup of cream. In a saucepan with high edges, melt 1 cup of sugar with 3 1/2 tablespoons of water; cook until a golden caramel forms, then add in the heated cream, being careful as it may boil up vigorously; mix well until smooth and even. Remove from the heat, let cool, then add the butter, continuing to mix. Fill the crust with half of the caramel and let chill.

5

Partially whip the rest of the cream and add it to the rest of the caramel; use this to fill the rest of the crust and chill in the fridge for 2 hours. Right before serving, top with the popcorn and drizzle with caramel.
