A tequila-based spin on the classic.

In this [Bijou](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/bijou-cocktail-recipe/) riff created by Sother Teague and served at Etérea in New York City, tequila establishes an earthy base note.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/4 oz. reposado tequila
-   1 1/4 oz. sweet vermouth
-   1/2 oz. green Chartreuse
-   2 dashes orange bitters

## Preparation

Combine ingredients in a mixing glass with ice and stir to chill. Strain into a Nick & Nora glass and garnish.
