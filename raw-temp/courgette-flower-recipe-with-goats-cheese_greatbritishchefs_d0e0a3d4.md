A dreamy combination of [goats cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/goats-cheese-recipes) and violet [jelly](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/jelly-recipes), which can be bought from speciality ingredients stores, gives [Pascal Aussignac](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/pascal-aussignac)'s courgette flowers recipe a distinctive edge. This is a fabulous dish for summer when [courgette](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/courgette-recipes) flowers are in season.

### Metric

### Imperial

-   4 courgette flowers
-   150g of [goat's cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/goats-cheese-recipes), mature
-   50g of double cream
-   3 sprigs of [fresh rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes), finely chopped
-   3 [courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes), small
-   1 [Espelette pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   20ml of Orgeat syrup
-   10g of violet jelly
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   20ml of olive oil

-   Piping bag 1-2cm nozzle
-   Steamer
-   Blender

1

For the courgette flower filling filling, mix the goat's cheese, double cream and rosemary, season with salt and pepper. Place into a piping bag with a small nozzle. Set aside while you prepare the flowers

-   150g of [goat's cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/goats-cheese-recipes)
-   50g of double cream
-   1 sprig of [fresh rosemary](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/rosemary-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

2

Remove the stamen/pistils from the flowers. Pipe the goat's cheese mix into the flowers. Set aside in the fridge

-   4 courgette flowers

3

Cut the courgette into quarters and remove the white flesh in the centre. Blanch the green of the courgette in salted boiling water for 3 minutes or until tender

-   3 [courgettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/courgette-recipes)

4

Strain and place directly into ice water. Pour off the ice water and mix the green courgette skin with the olive oil, orgeat syrup, a pinch of salt and a pinch of Espelette pepper and blend until smooth

-   20ml of olive oil
-   1 [Espelette pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   20ml of Orgeat syrup
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

5

Season the violet jelly with salt and Espelette pepper and add enough water to have a runny consistency. Keep warm

-   10g of violet jelly

6

Season the flowers with olive oil and salt. Steam for 4 minutes, remove from the steamer and glaze the flowers in the jelly

7

To serve, spoon the courgette and orgeat purée onto plates and place the courgette flowers on top
