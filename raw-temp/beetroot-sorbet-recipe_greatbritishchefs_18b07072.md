With its earthy sweetness, beetroot is an incredibly versatile ingredient that can be used in starters, mains, and even in a dessert, as this recipe shows. Beetroot is twisted into a cooling sorbet in this delightful [beetroot recipe](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beetroot-recipes) from [Christoffer Hruskova](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/christoffer-hruskova). This is a perfect [summer recipe](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/british-summer-recipes) that provides a wonderful palate cleanser between courses or a light, interesting [dessert](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dessert-recipes).

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   Ice cream maker

1

Place the sugar and water into a medium sized pan to dissolve into a sugar syrup. Remove from the heat

-   150g of sugar
-   150ml of water

2

Add the sugar syrup to the beetroot juice, lemon juice and liquid glucose

-   440ml of [beetroot juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes)
-   20ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   60ml of liquid glucose

3

In a small cup, soften the gelatine leaf by soaking in cold water, squeeze any excess water off and add to the beetroot mix

-   2 gelatine leaves

4

Churn the mixture in an ice cream maker until it reaches a sorbet consistency and then freeze. Serve into bowls
