This carrot [fudge](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fudge-recipes) recipe by [Vineet Bhatia](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/vineet-bhatia) is a delightful after-dinner sweet with the spicy notes of [cardamom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/cardamom-recipes). Use young carrots as they are sweeter. Khoya is often used in [Indian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/indian-recipes) or Pakistani cuisine and is made by either drying or reducing milk. You can buy khoya and ghee from good Indian grocery stores or online.

#### Carrot fudge recipe

-   50g of [ghee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   40g of cashew nuts
-   80g of [raisins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raisin-recipes), coarsely chopped
-   250g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes), peeled and grated
-   250ml of full-fat milk
-   50g of khoya
-   50g of granulated sugar
-   1/2 tsp [ground cardamom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)
-   25 mini pastry cases

1

Heat the ghee over a medium heat. Add the cashew nuts and raisins and cook until golden. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper

-   50g of [ghee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   40g of cashew nuts
-   80g of [raisins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/raisin-recipes)

2

Grate the carrots and add them to the pan, and cook for a couple of minutes. Make sure they are well coated with the ghee. Add the milk, reduce the heat and simmer until the milk has evaporated

-   250g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)
-   250ml of full-fat milk

3

Add the khoya and cook, stirring constantly, for up to 30 minutes until the consistency is soft and fudge-like

-   50g of khoya

4

Add the sugar and return the cashew nuts and raisins to the pan. Cook, stirring, until the sugar has melted. Remove from the heat and sprinkle in the ground cardamom

-   50g of granulated sugar
-   1/2 tsp [ground cardamom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)

5

Leave to cool, then fill the pastry cases with the mixture to serve

-   25 mini pastry cases
