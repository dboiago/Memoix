Bourbon and fresh strawberries are a match made in cocktail heaven in this summery cocktail.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. bourbon
-   2 fresh strawberries, hulled and quartered
-   3/4 oz. ginger syrup
-   [1/2 oz. honey syrup (1:1)](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/honey-syrup-recipe/)
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   2 dashes rhubarb bitters
-   Club soda

## Preparation

In a shaker, muddle the strawberries with the honey syrup. Add the remaining ingredients, except the club soda, and shake with ice. Strain into an ice-filled highball glass, top with soda and garnish.
