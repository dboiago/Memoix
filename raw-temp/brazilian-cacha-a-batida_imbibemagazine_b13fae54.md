As decadent as it is delicious, we love the tropical escape of this Cachaça Batida combining the spirit with coconut and sweetened condensed milk.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. cachaça
-   2 oz. cream of coconut
-   2 oz. sweetened condensed milk
-   ½ oz. simple syrup (1:1)
-   5 oz. ice

## Preparation

Combine all the ingredients and blend until smooth. Pour into a chilled glass and garnish.
