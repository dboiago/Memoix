This [black pudding](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/black-pudding-recipes) recipe from [Paul Heathcote](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/paul-heathcote) is a modern take on a traditional Lancashire dish. Black Pudding is wrapped in a cheesy [potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/potato-recipes) mix to make delicious hash browns. These are offset perfectly with the sweetness of caramelised Conference pears.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   4 [Maris Piper potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)
-   1 [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   4 slices of [black pudding](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/black-pudding-recipes)
-   100g of Lancashire cheese
-   2 tsp [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), chopped
-   2 tsp [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   1 [conference pear](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pear-recipes)
-   2 tsp icing sugar
-   1 pinch of [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of oil
-   25g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   4 sprigs of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)

1

Leave the potatoes in their skins and cook in boiling water for 10 minutes so that they are still firm. Drain and peel. Grate the potatoes into a bowl and mix with half the herbs

-   4 [Maris Piper potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes)
-   1 tsp [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes)
-   1 tsp [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)

2

Add a pinch of sea salt to the egg white, beat with a whisk and mix with the grated potato and herb mixture; season with sea salt and freshly milled pepper

-   1 [egg white](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

3

Pat out a quarter of the mix in a circle larger than the slice of black pudding. Place black pudding and 25g of cheese in the middle, fold over the potatoes and pat into a circle. Repeat 4 times. Deep fry at 160C degrees until golden brown

-   4 slices of [black pudding](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/black-pudding-recipes)
-   100g of Lancashire cheese

4

Scoop the Conference pear into balls. Put one dessertspoon of vegetable oil into a hot frying pan with one teaspoon of sieved icing sugar. When the sugar turns golden brown, add the Conference pear balls and toss quickly

-   1 [conference pear](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pear-recipes)
-   1 tsp icing sugar, sieved
-   1 tbsp of oil

5

Melt 25g of butter in a pan and add the remaining herbs to make a buttery sauce.

-   25g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 tsp [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes)
-   1 tsp [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)

6

Serve the hash browns with a few caramelized balls of pear, the herby butter sauce. Serve with large sprigs of flat leaf parsley

-   4 sprigs of [flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
