### Glass:

Serve in an [Old-fashioned glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 1⁄2 fl oz

[Light white rum (charcoal-filtered 1-4 years old)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/2224/don-q-cristal-rum)

3⁄4 fl oz

[Triple sec liqueur (40% alc./vol.)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/2887/combier-triple-sec)

2 fl oz

Pineapple juice

1⁄4 fl oz

Lime juice (freshly squeezed)

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill an [OLD-FASHIONED GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of orange zest twist.

### How to make:

3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-strain-a-cocktail) into ice-filled glass.

### Garnish:

5.  [EXPRESS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1269/cocktail-garnishes/citrus-zest-twists) orange zest twist over the cocktail and use as garnish.

## Review:

Not that special, but certainly OK.
