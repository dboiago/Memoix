This easy roast [chicken thigh](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes "Chicken thigh") dish is all cooked on a single oven tray, and combines sweet [pineapple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pineapple-recipes "Pineapple") with spicy Calabrian [‘nduja](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nduja-recipes "'Nduja") and [Mexican](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mexican-recipes "Mexican") chipotle. 

_Extracted from Mezcla by Ixta Belfrage (Ebury Press, £26). All photography by Yuki Sugiura._

First published in 2022

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Chicken

-   4 [bone-in chicken thighs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-thigh-recipes), (skin on)
-   4 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), peeled and crushed with the side of a knife
-   1 [medium onion](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), halved and very thinly sliced on a mandoline
-   1 [pineapple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pineapple-recipes), large, and extra-ripe (peeled)
-   100g of tangerine juice, (from about 4 tangerines)
-   100g of chicken bone broth, (or stock or water)
-   2 tbsp of double cream
-   5g of [fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), cut into wedges

-   Iron skillet
-   Blowtorch

1

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 6

3

Tip the onions and garlic into a 28cm ovenproof cast-iron skillet or similar-sized baking dish and spread out. Place the chicken thighs on top, skin side up and spaced apart

4

Cut the pineapple into 4 rounds, then cut each round into quarters, removing the hard core (you should have about 300g)

-   1 [pineapple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pineapple-recipes), large, and extra-ripe (peeled)

5

Add the pineapple to the bowl with the remnants of the paste, mix to coat with whatever's left there, then arrange the pineapple around the chicken

6

Pour the tangerine juice around the chicken (don’t get the skin wet), then bake for 20 minutes

-   100g of tangerine juice, (from about 4 tangerines)

7

Remove from the oven and pour the stock or water into the pan around the chicken (again, don’t get the skin wet)

-   100g of chicken bone broth, (or stock or water)

8

Return to the oven for another 20–25 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the skin is browned and crispy

9

If you have a blowtorch, use it to char the pineapple a little

10

Drizzle the cream into the sauce

-   2 tbsp of double cream

11

Toss the coriander and the remaining sliced onions together with a tiny bit of oil and salt and arrange on top

-   5g of [fresh coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)

12

Serve from the pan, with the lime wedges alongside

-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), cut into wedges
