These filipino-inspired béchamel [croquettes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/croquette-recipes "Croquette") from [Florence Mae Maglanoc](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/florence-mae-magnaloc-chef "Florence Mae Magnaloc") are filled with [wild mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/wild-mushroom-recipes "Wild mushroom") cooked in a tangy adobo sauce. They are a great vegetarian appetiser, and work well with any mushrooms that you have.

First published in 2024

### Metric

### Imperial

1

Bring the sugar, vinegar and water for the pickled mushrooms to a boil, stirring to dissolve the sugar, then remove from the heat and let cool

-   175ml of cane sugar
-   175ml of white wine vinegar
-   350ml of water

2

Once cool, combine 175 ml pickling liquid with the sherry vinegar, confit garlic and shimeji. Seal in a sterilised container and let pickle at room temperature for 2 hours

-   4 tbsp of sherry vinegar
-   2 [confit garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   150g of [shimeji mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes), stems removed

4

Sauté the mushrooms for the filling in a pan over medium heat until they're browned and have released their moisture. 

-   200g of [mixed wild mushrooms](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mushroom-recipes)

5

Add 120 ml adobo sauce to the mushrooms, mix well, and cook until the mixture thickens. Set aside to cool

6

For the béchamel, in a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute, until fragrant but not browned

-   2 tbsp of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   2 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced

7

Stir in the ﬂour to create a roux. Cook for a couple of minutes, stirring constantly, to remove the raw ﬂour taste

-   2 tbsp of plain flour

8

Gradually whisk in the milk, ensuring there are no lumps. Continue to cook and stir until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon. Season with salt and pepper to taste

-   240ml of milk
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

9

Combine the béchamel sauce with the cooled mushroom mixture. If the mixture is too soft to shape, let it cool in the refrigerator until it ﬁrms up

10

Once the mushroom and béchamel mixture is cool and manageable, shape into small balls or croquettes

11

Dip each croquette ﬁrst into the beaten egg, then roll in bread crumbs to coat evenly

-   1 [egg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), beaten
-   breadcrumbs

12

Heat oil in a deep-fryer or a deep pan to 175°C. Fry the croquettes in batches until they are golden brown and crispy, about 4-5 minutes

-   oil, for deep-frying

13

Drain on paper towels to remove excess oil before serving topped with a pickled shimeji mushroom
