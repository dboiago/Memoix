**RECIPE:** _Horchata (permanently closed), New York_

![cucumber cocktail recipes](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/cucumber-jalapeno-margarita-vertical-crdt-noah-fecks.jpg)

Fresh jalapeño amps up the spice, while cucumber sneaks in to help cool things down.

Peppers up the spice while cucumber cools things down in this Cucumber Jalapeño Margarita.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. blanco tequila
-   1/2 oz. triple sec
-   1/2 oz. fresh lime juice
-   3/4 oz. agave syrup (1:1)
-   3 fresh jalapeño wheels
-   3 fresh cucumber wheels

## Preparation

In a shaker, muddle the jalapeño and cucumber. Add the other ingredients and shake with ice. Strain into an ice-filled glass; garnish.
