## Mix the grated Parmigiano Reggiano DOP (or parmesan if you can’t find any of the real stuff) with the crumbled bread....

**Cooks in:** _15mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _10 Pieces_

### List of Ingredients

-   4 OZ. of grated Parmigiano cheese
-   2 OZ. of sliced bread, crumbled into pieces
-   poppy seeds

### Method

1

Mix the grated Parmigiano Reggiano DOP (or parmesan if you can’t find any of the real stuff) with the crumbled bread.

2

Place 1 tablespoon of the mixture in a waffle maker, adding some poppy seeds on top. Cook for 2 minutes. Then, before the wafers cool, place each one onto a curved surface to bend them and give them a little bit of shape.

3

Alternatively, spread 2 or 3 tablespoons of the mixture at a time on a microwave-safe plate lined with baking parchment and cook at full power for 1 minute and 15 seconds.
