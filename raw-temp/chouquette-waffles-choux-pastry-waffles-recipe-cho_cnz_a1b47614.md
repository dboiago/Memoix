Picture in your mind **a love story** between a [Belgian waffle](https://cnz.to/recipes/bread-brioche/belgian-waffles-liege-style-recipe/), square and sturdy with deep grooves, and a [chouquette](https://cnz.to/recipes/cookies-small-cakes/chouquettes-sugar-puffs-recipe/), puffy and dainty with a soft heart.

Immediately they would see that they have **pearl sugar** in common. The waffle would make the chouquette feel safe, and cared for; the chouquette would give the waffle a sense that life is full of whimsy.

Now, what would happen if they had **a child** together?

This is what would happen: a **chouquette waffle**, crisp around the edges, tender inside, easy to love, with sugar crystals.

![Chouquette Waffles](https://cnz.to/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/chouquette_waffles_closeup_vo.jpg)

In my kitchen, its destiny was sealed on a Saturday afternoon\* when I had **choux pastry leftover** from an éclair test the day before, but not quite the energy to pipe it into [chouquettes](https://cnz.to/recipes/cookies-small-cakes/chouquettes-sugar-puffs-recipe/) for my children.

The waffle iron\*\* winked at me from the lower shelf of my kitchen cabinet. “I’m your guy!” it whispered. “I heat up fast, I clean up easy, and you know we have [all kinds of fun](http://www.amazon.com/dp/0761176462?tag=chocolzucchi-20) when we play together.”

And indeed, we did.

![Chouquette Waffles](https://cnz.to/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/chouquette_waffles_birdseye_vo.jpg)

I folded [pearl sugar](http://www.amazon.com/dp/B00XO3SBNI?tag=chocolzucchi-20) into my leftover choux pastry, scooped it into the iron, and within minutes we had a batch of the **most surprising, most delicious waffles** you could imagine, crunchy with the sugar but not too sweet, warm and comforting, the perfect afternoon snack.

So if you’ve always been intimidated by **choux pastry** because you always worry your puffs won’t puff or worse, will deflate, this is a low-risk recipe to dip your toes in, since the recipe’s success doesn’t rely on the rise and fall of your _pâte à choux_. And if you’re an experienced choux pastry maker looking for new ways to **work its magic**, this one’s for you too!

\* As you can tell from the photos, this was a couple of months ago, when fresh, local raspberries felt like our birthright. Skip the berries if they’re out of season.

Chouquette Waffles Recipe

![Chouquette Waffles Recipe](https://cnz.to/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/chouquette_waffles-760x507.jpg "Chouquette Waffles Recipe")

Ingredients

-   75 g (6 tablespoons) unsalted **butter**, diced
-   1/4 teaspoon fine sea **salt**
-   240 ml (1 cup) fresh **water**
-   140 g (1 cup) all-purpose **flour**
-   4 large **eggs**, at room temperature
-   140 grams **pearl sugar** ([order online](http://www.amazon.com/dp/B00XO3SBNI?tag=chocolzucchi-20))

Instructions

1.  Have all the ingredients measured out before you start.
2.  Combine the butter, salt, and water in a medium saucepan (not non-stick), and bring to a simmer over medium-low heat.
3.  Remove from the heat, add the flour all at once, and stir quickly with a wooden spoon until well blended and smooth. This is your panade.
4.  Return the pan over medium-low heat, and keep stirring until the dough is satiny and leaves a slight film at the bottom of the pan, about 5 minutes; this means that all the excess water has evaporated.
5.  Cool for 3 minutes. Add the eggs, one by one, stirring well between each addition; I like to use a [dough whisk](https://cnz.to/product/danish-dough-whisk/) for this, but a wooden spatula works too. At each extra egg it will seem like the dough won’t absorb it, but it will.
6.  Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, and up to 1 day.
7.  Just before cooking the waffles, fold in the pearl sugar.
8.  Preheat your waffle iron (I use this [griddle and waffle iron](https://cnz.to/product/cuisinart-griddler-and-waffle-iron/)) to medium-high.
9.  Scoop 1/4 cup (75 grams) of the choux pastry in each of the waffle prints, close the waffle iron, and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, until puffy and golden-brown.
10.  Cool for 5 to 10 minutes on a rack and serve, slightly warm.

Notes

These taste best fresh out of the waffle iron, but leftover waffles can be reheated in the toaster.
