**RECIPE:** _Bryn Lumsden for [Damn the Weather](http://damntheweather.com/), Seattle_

![calvados & tonic](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/calvadostonic-vertical-crdt-lara-ferroni.jpg)

A G&T riff with a French twist.

Already popular in France, this take on the G&T—which swaps gin for French apple brandy—is picking up fans on this side of the Atlantic too. “Calvados can get pegged as a winter spirit or an after-dinner drink,” says Damn the Weather owner Bryn Lumsden. “Stretching it out with tonic allows it to become a daytime drink, as well as a summer drink.”

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. Calvados
-   3/4 oz. tonic syrup (such as Bradley’s Kina Tonic)
-   3 oz. chilled soda water

## Preparation

Combine the ingredients in an ice-filled glass and stir to combine. Garnish.

Note: A quality tonic water may be substituted for the tonic syrup and soda.
