Beat the heat with this super-refreshing Americano riff from Houndstooth Coffee.

## Ingredients

-   3 oz. cold-brew coffee concentrate
-   2 oz. Cappelletti
-   2 oz. club soda

## Preparation

Combine the cold-brew concentrate and Cappelletti in a glass. Fill halfway with crushed ice and stir to chill. Add club soda and top with more crushed ice. Garnish.
