[Leeks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/leek-recipes "Leek") haven't quite had the trendy makeover that [cauliflower](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cauliflower-recipes "Cauliflower") and [kale](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kale-recipes "Kale") have had. But, they deserve better - leeks have a sweetness and fragrance that is more subtle than that of other alliums, but which really sings when paired with cream and earthy foods like lentils. This recipe pairs well with a Sunday roast, and will keep in the fridge for up to 2 days. To reheat, gently warm it in a pan with a splash of water.
