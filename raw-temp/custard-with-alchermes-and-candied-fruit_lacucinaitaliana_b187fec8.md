## This custard owes its rose color to a Alchermes; the classic crimson Italian elixir was a favorite liqueur of the Medici family that adds color and spicy citrusy notes to desserts.

**Cooks in:** _2h 40mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 CUP of milk
-   1/3 LB. of mixed candied fruit
-   1/2 CUP of cream
-   1/3 CUP of sugar
-   1/4 CUP of Alchermes liqueur
-   1/3 CUP of all-purpose flour
-   5 SHEETS of gelatin
-   3 of egg yolks
-   1 vanilla bean pod
-   salt

### Method

1

Chop the candied fruit coarsely. Soak the gelatin sheets in cold water.

2

In a small pot, bring the milk to a boil together with the vanilla bean pod opened lengthwise. Turn off the heat, let cool and remove the vanilla bean pod.

3

In a bowl, beat the egg yolks together with the sugar and a pinch of salt; then stir in the flour, and the Alchermes. Continue to mix until the ingredients are thoroughly combined (around 1 minute).

4

Pour the egg yolk mixture into the pot with the milk and place it back over minimum heat. Whisk until you obtain a pink custard. Remove from the heat and stir in the drained gelatin sheets until they completely dissolve. Stir in the chopped candied fruit. Distribute the mixture into 4 custard molds (square, 2.5” x 2.5”) or into 4 disposable aluminum molds, filling them 3/4 of the way.

5

Place in the refrigerator to set for around 2 hours. Whip the cream without adding sugar. Remove the set custards from the molds. Serve with dollops of whipped cream and extra mixed candied fruit to taste.
