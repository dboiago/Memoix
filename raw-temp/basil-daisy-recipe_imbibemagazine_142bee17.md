**RECIPE:** _Boris Van Dyck for [The Darling Oyster Bar](http://thedarling.com/), Charleston_

![](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Bail-Daisy.jpg)

This vodka cocktail from Charleston’s The Darling Oyster Bar is bright and herbaceous. Click [here](https://imbibemagazine.com/inside-look-the-darling-charleston/) for an inside look at The Darling’s beautiful interior.

## Preparation

Add all of the ingredients to a shaker with ice and hard shake until chilled. Double strain over crushed ice in a highball glass, then garnish.
