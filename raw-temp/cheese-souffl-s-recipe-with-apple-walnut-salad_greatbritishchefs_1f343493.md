[Shaun Rankin](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/shaun-rankin)'s individual cheese [soufflés](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/souffle-recipes) are twice cooked, allowing you to do the main part of the preparation well ahead of time. The soufflés are firstly steamed – you can then refrigerate them and bake them in the oven just before sitting down with guests. The [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/apple-recipes), walnut and pomegranate salad is a wonderful side in this cheese soufflé recipe, but would be beautiful on its own too. If you are making these cheese soufflés for vegetarians, substitute the [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/parmesan-recipes) for a cheese with vegetarian rennet, such as Old Winchester.

#### Soufflés

-   5 tbsp of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), or vegetarian hard cheese, grated
-   240ml of milk
-   3 tbsp of plain flour
-   60g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), plus extra for greasing
-   6 [eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes), separated
-   175g of [mature cheddar](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cheddar-recipes), grated
-   1 tbsp of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), chopped
-   [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [cracked black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### To plate

-   2 [red apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), cored and cut into eight
-   6 tbsp of [whole walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/walnut-recipes)
-   150g of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)
-   6 tbsp of [pomegranate seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pomegranate-recipes)
-   2 tsp walnut oil

-   4 x 10cm diameter ovenproof moulds/ramekins
-   Steamer

1

Pour the milk into a saucepan and warm gently over a low heat to take the chill off it. Sift the flour into the milk and mix well

-   240ml of milk
-   3 tbsp of plain flour

2

Cook on a moderate heat until you can no longer taste the flour in the mixture and there are no lumps. Remove from the heat and beat in the butter

-   60g of [unsalted butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

4

Grease the 4 moulds/ramekins with softened butter and coat the insides with the grated Parmesan

-   5 tbsp of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), or vegetarian hard cheese, grated

5

Whisk the egg whites in a bowl until you have firm peaks. Carefully fold the egg whites into the cheese sauce, taking care not to deflate them by overworking or being too heavy handed

6

Spoon the mixture into the moulds and cook in a steamer for 12 minutes. When cooked, remove from the steamer and leave to stand for 10 minutes or until they are cool enough to handle

7

Remove the soufflés from the moulds. At this stage, you can either put them in the fridge until needed or cook straight away.

8

To cook, preheat the oven to 200˚C/Gas mark 6. Put the soufflés on a baking tray lined with greaseproof paper and bake for 6–8 minutes

9

To serve, arrange the apple on 4 small plates with the walnuts, rocket and pomegranate seeds scattered over. Remove the soufflés from the oven and place straight onto the salad. Drizzle with walnut oil and serve straight away

-   2 [red apples](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), cored and cut into eight
-   6 tbsp of [whole walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/walnut-recipes)
-   150g of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)
-   6 tbsp of [pomegranate seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pomegranate-recipes)
-   2 tsp walnut oil
