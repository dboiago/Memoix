The striking colours and presentation of [Josh Eggleton](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/josh-eggleton)'s [vegetarian](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/vegetarian-recipes) dish are sure to make it a new go-to favourite. With layers of carrot and beetroot barley [risottos](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/risotto-recipes), tender roasted butternut squash cubes and crunchy almonds, these flavours are sure to match style with flavour. Swap the Parmesan for a vegetable rennet-based hard cheese if cooking for vegetarians.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   150g of [pearl barley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pearl-barley-recipes)
-   4 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)
-   4 [red beetroots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes), peeled
-   200ml of milk
-   4 knobs of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1/4 [butternut squash](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butternut-squash-recipes), peeled
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 [celery stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), diced
-   2 [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), diced
-   100ml of white wine, or non-alcoholic white wine
-   50g of [flaked almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes), toasted
-   170g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated (or use a vegetarian alternative)
-   2 pinches of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   2 pinches of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of olive oil

-   Juicer
-   Blender

1

In a juicer, juice 2 of the carrots and set aside the juice. Repeat with 2 of the beetroots, keeping the 2 juices separate

-   2 [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)
-   2 [red beetroots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes)

3

Using 2 pans, add the carrots and 100ml of milk and a knob of butter to a pan, and the beetroot with the remaining milk and butter to the other. Gently simmer both until the vegetables are cooked

-   2 knobs of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   200ml of milk

4

Add the cooked carrots to the blender with the carrot juice and blitz to form a purée, set aside. Repeat with the beetroot and its juice

5

Preheat the oven to 180°C/gas mark 4. Toss the diced butternut squash in olive oil, salt and pepper and then roast until tender, approximately 20-25 minutes. Set aside

-   1/4 [butternut squash](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butternut-squash-recipes), peeled
-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

6

Now start the risottos. Place 2 pans over a medium-high heat. Add a knob of butter to each, then add the garlic, celery and shallots and sweat until soft

-   2 knobs of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   2 [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), diced
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), minced
-   1 [celery stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes), diced

7

Wash the barley for a few seconds under running cold water. Drain and divide evenly between the 2 pans. Add the white wine and a splash of water to both pans. Cook until absorbed

-   150g of [pearl barley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pearl-barley-recipes)
-   100ml of white wine

8

Add 100g of the carrot purée to one pan and 100g of beetroot purée to the other, stirring constantly until the grains are coated

9

Once all of the purée has been absorbed, divide the chopped almonds and Parmesan (reserving some of each for garnish) between the pans and stir until melted and combined

-   40g of [flaked almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes), toasted
-   150g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated

10

As the risottos cook, keep adding more of the purée - as you would do traditionally with a stock - to ensure they don't dry out

11

Add a pinch of salt and pepper to season each pan

-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

12

Divide the carrot risotto into bowls, top with the beetroot risotto and then the reserved almonds, butternut squash and Parmesan. Serve immediately

-   10g of [flaked almonds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes), toasted
-   20g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), grated
