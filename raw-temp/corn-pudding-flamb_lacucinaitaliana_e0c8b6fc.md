## Rinse the corn and drain it. Heat the corn in a saucepan with the milk; bring to a boil and cook for 7-8 minutes....

**Cooks in:** _30mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 C. of milk
-   9 OZ. of canned corn
-   1 of egg
-   Sugar
-   Flour

### Method

1

Rinse the corn and drain it. Heat the corn in a saucepan with the milk; bring to a boil and cook for 7-8 minutes.

2

Puree it in a blender as finely as possible. For a smoother, silkier pudding, run it through a sieve.

3

In a separate bowl, break the egg and mix it with 1/4 cup sugar and 2 tablespoons flour.

4

Pour the puree over the beaten egg and heat it in a pan. Cook, stirring, for 2-3 minutes and remove from heat.

5

Pour the pudding into a cold bowl to stop the cooking, then transfer it into 4 small cups and let it cool completely.

6

Cover the surface with 1 teaspoon sugar and ignite it, or run it briefly under the broiler on maximum temperature.
