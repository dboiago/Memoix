And perhaps, someday, I’ll have the chance to taste a one – or two – by his pool. Or perhaps three.

At [Lucques](http://www.lucques.com/), Barman Christiaan Rollich, who came up with the drink, uses Dolin dry French vermouth. You could swap out another brand of vermouth. [Prosecco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prosecco) is a lively, sparkling wine from Italy. It’s a less-expensive alternative to Champagne and is an excellent mixer for cocktails, such as this one.

-   1 1/2 ounces dry vermouth, preferably Dolin
-   1 ounce Lillet blanc, (white)
-   1 ounce orange liqueur, such as Grand Marnier or Cointreau
-   1 ounce Prosecco
-   lemon wheel and orange wheel

-   Fill a small pitcher halfway with ice. Add vermouth, Lillet, and orange liqueur, and stir briskly until well-chilled. Strain into a stemmed wine glass.
    
-   Add a generous handful of fresh ice and top with a pour of Prosecco. Drop in a lemon and orange wheel, and serve.
