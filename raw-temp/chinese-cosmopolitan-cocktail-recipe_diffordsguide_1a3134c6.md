### Glass:

Serve in a [Martini glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

2 fl oz

[Krupnik spiced honey liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/4217/davna-hani)

3⁄4 fl oz

Lychee liqueur

1⁄2 fl oz

Lime juice (freshly squeezed)

1 fl oz

[Cranberry juice (sweetened)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/3375/bws/cranberry-juice)

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [MARTINI GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of orange zest twist.

### How to make:

3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into chilled glass.

### Garnish:

5.  [EXPRESS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1269/cocktail-garnishes/citrus-zest-twists) flamed orange zest twist over cocktail, then use as garnish.

## Review:

Oriental in name and style - perhaps a tad sweeter than your standard Cosmo.
