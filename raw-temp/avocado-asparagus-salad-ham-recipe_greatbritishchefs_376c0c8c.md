The cured [ham](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pork-recipes) used in [Alan Murchison](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/alan-murchison)'s salad dish is a little bit special – it features the top grade of ham produced in Spain: jamón Ibérico de Bellota. This is not to be confused with the mass-produced Serrano hams. Make the [avocado](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/avocado-recipes) purée 2 to 3 hours before you want to use it to allow the flavours to infuse, and keep it in the refrigerator.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Salad

-   8 [long croutons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/crouton-recipes), cut from a baguette
-   olive oil
-   8 [asparagus spears](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes), blanched
-   12 [red chicory leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicory-recipes)
-   4 pissant leaves
-   100g of [jamón Ibérico de Bellota](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ham-recipes), cut into batons
-   50g of [Parmesan shavings](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes)
-   1 handful of [pea shoots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-shoot-recipes)

#### Avocado purée

-   2 [avocados](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), peeled and stoned
-   2 [limes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   60ml of water
-   85ml of olive oil
-   25g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 [green chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), deseeded
-   2 [large spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/spring-onion-recipes), cut in 2 cm lengths
-   1 [large shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), peeled and quartered
-   1/2 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### Guacamole

-   2 [avocados](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), peeled and stoned
-   5g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 [chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   70g of red onion
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   1 [plum tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), peeled and deseeded
-   40ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

#### Lemon oil dressing

-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of sugar
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), skin thinly peeled
-   200ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), fresh
-   220ml of olive oil
-   220ml of groundnut oil
-   50ml of white wine vinegar
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

-   Food processor or blender

1

To make the avocado purée, blend the flesh of one avocado with the lime juice, water and olive oil until you achieve a smooth purée

-   1 [avocado](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), peeled and stoned
-   2 [limes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   60ml of water
-   85ml of olive oil

3

Scrape down the sides and finely pulse the remaining avocado flesh. Season to taste and set aside. Clean the blender ready for the guacamole

-   1 [avocado](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), peeled and stoned
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

4

To make the guacamole, very finely chop the chilli and coriander

-   5g of [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 [chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)

5

Finely chop the red onion. Add the onion, chilli, coriander and remaining ingredients to the blender until the tomato is chopped. Season to taste. Set aside and clean the blender ready for the lemon dressing

-   70g of red onion
-   2 [avocados](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/avocado-recipes), peeled and stoned
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   1 [plum tomato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes), peeled and deseeded
-   40ml of extra virgin olive oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

6

To make the lemon oil dressing, grind the salt, sugar and lemon peelings in the blender

-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of sugar
-   2 [lemons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), thinly peeled

7

Add the rest of the ingredients and blend for 1 minute. Add more sugar if necessary and season to taste. The dressing will keep in a sterilised bottle in the refrigerator for up to three weeks

-   200ml of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), fresh
-   220ml of olive oil
-   220ml of groundnut oil
-   50ml of white wine vinegar
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

8

To make the salad, brush the croutons with olive oil and bake until golden. Blanch the asparagus spears

-   8 [long croutons](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/crouton-recipes), cut from a baguette
-   olive oil, for brushing
-   8 [asparagus spears](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/asparagus-recipes), blanched

9

To plate, place a quarter of the guacamole on the top and the centre of a large square white plate

10

Place three chicory leaves into the guacamole so they are standing vertically and are evenly spaced

-   12 [red chicory leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicory-recipes)

11

Place a crouton horizontally between the first and second, and second and third chicory leaf

12

Place a single pissant leaf in front of the central chicory leaf so that it stands vertically and leaning slightly forward

-   4 pissant leaves

13

Spoon on two stripes of avocado purée from the centre of the mound of guacamole to the bottom two corners of the plate and place an asparagus spear next to each

14

Scatter over a quarter of the ham batons, Parmesan shavings and pea shoots and finish the plate with drops of the lemon dressing. Repeat the entire process on the remaining three plates

-   50g of [Parmesan shavings](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes)
-   0 handful of [pea shoots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/pea-shoot-recipes)
-   100g of bellota ham
