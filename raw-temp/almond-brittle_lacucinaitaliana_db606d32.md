### List of Ingredients

-   9 OZ. of almonds, unblanched
-   sugar

### Method

1

Chop the almonds by cutting them into three pieces with a knife.|In a pot, dissolve the sugar in 1 tablespoon of water, on medium heat, stirring.|Cook for about 4-5 minutes and, when the sugar has dissolved and begins to brown, add the almond pieces.|Stir them into the caramel and cook on low for 1-2 minutes.|Transfer to a non-stick mold, such as a star-shaped mold measuring 12 inches across.|Use a meat tenderizer to flatten the almonds into an even layer.|Trim the points of the star with a spoon to create a perfect star shape.|Let the brittle cool for 15-20 minutes, then unmold.
