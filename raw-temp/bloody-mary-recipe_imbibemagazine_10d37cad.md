Unbeatable for brunch.

The classic Bloody Mary recipe, in all its simplicity, is unbeatable for brunch.

## Ingredients

-   3 oz. vodka
-   5 oz. tomato juice
-   1/2 oz. fresh lemon Juice
-   1/2 oz. Worcestershire
-   Pinch pepper
-   Pinch celery salt
-   Dash hot sauce

## Preparation

Shake ingredients with ice, strain into ice-filled glass and garnish.
