These brownies are absolutely delicious and Caribbean, made with definite chocolate plain dark chocolate bars bringing out the unique NATURAL FLAVA, and chocolatey fudge texture!. This is the perfect recipe for everyday baking with the family!…locally sourced from Dominican ingredients in order to bring you a special Caribbean experience. Let your palate travel through the different regions of the Dominican Republic.  

-   300g Definite Cocoa bar 
-   34g cocoa powder 
-   1 tbsp. Expresso powder 
-   2 1/2 stick unsalted butter
-   1 tbsp. Granulated sugar 
-   134g Brown sugar 
-   1 tsp. Vanilla extract 
-   2 tsp. Sea salt 
-   6 eggs whisked 
-   150g all purpose flour
-   100g cocoa powder

Firstly chop your chocolate bar into small pieces then place into a mixing bowl, then add cocoa and expresso powder then set aside.

In a medium sauce pan add unsalted butter and cook until a smooth liquid  for 5 minutes. Then immediately pour onto the chocolate mixture then which until smooth.   

n a separate mixing bowl add sugar, vanilla extract, salt, then in a separate bowl crack 6 eggs and whisk into the sugar mixture individually. Continue to whisk then add the cocoa mixture while still whisking.

Now sieve in 1 cup of plain flour and cocoa powder then mix and fold. 

Pour mixture into a grease proof baking tray. Then place in a pre-heated oven for 20 minutes on 180 degrees. Take out of the oven then give it a shake. Then place back in oven for 25 more minutes. 

Cool down for 10 minutes
