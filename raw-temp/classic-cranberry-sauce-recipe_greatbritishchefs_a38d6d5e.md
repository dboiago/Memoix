Cranberry sauce is always a delicious treat at [Christmas](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/features/christmas) or with any roast dinner. This cranberry sauce recipe from [Dominic Chapman](https://plus.google.com/102125176999786310330) is generous, but can easily be finished off the following day with cold cuts of [pork](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/pork-recipes) or [chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chicken-recipes), in sandwiches or alongside cheese.

First published in 2015

1

Start the cranberry sauce by combining the orange juice, zest and spices in a heavy based saucepan. Bring to a simmer and reduce by half. Add the cranberries and cook on a low heat to slowly stew the fruit without burning

-   2 [oranges](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes), zested
-   1 pinch of [ground ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes)
-   1 pinch of mixed spice
-   1 pinch of [cinnamon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1000g of [cranberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)
-   400ml of [orange juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes)

2

As soon as the cranberry mixture resembles a loose jam like consistency, add the sugar and bring back to the boil. Reduce the heat to a simmer and cook for a further 10 minutes, then remove from the heat

-   400g of brown sugar

3

When warm, taste for sweetness, adding sugar to taste. Remember that you want to achieve a balance between sweet and sour. Freeze if storing for more than 3 days or refrigerate before serving

4

Serve either with Christmas dinner or the following day with cold cuts of meat
