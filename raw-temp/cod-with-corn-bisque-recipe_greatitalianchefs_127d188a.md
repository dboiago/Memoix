Combining the dense flesh of [cod](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/cod-recipes) with sweetcorn bisque, the [Cerea Brothers](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/chefs/cerea-brothers) create a dish that balances sweetness with flavours of the sea. Garnishes of popcorn and brioche crisps add a pleasing texture to the finished dish. Use vegetarian gelatine to make this dish pescatarian.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Cod

-   4 [cod fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-fillet-recipes), each weighing 150g

#### Corn bavarois

-   4 fresh corn on the cob
-   1 [shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), chopped
-   20g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   16g of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   18g of gelatine, softened in iced water
-   340g of cream, semi-whipped
-   5g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [Espelette pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)

#### Cold corn bisque

-   1/2 [fennel bulb](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes)
-   1/2 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   40g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   80ml of dry vermouth
-   20ml of cognac
-   [cherry tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cherry-tomato-recipes), 1 can
-   [saffron](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/saffron-recipes) to taste
-   30g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), minced
-   1 [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   3 [cardamom pods](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes)
-   [paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes) to taste
-   500ml of shellfish stock, preferably lobster
-   4 sprigs of [tarragon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes)
-   4 sprigs of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   80g of crème fraîche
-   20g of [lemon juice](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

#### To plate

-   1 [brioche loaf](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brioche-recipes)
-   4 [baby corn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes)
-   4 [quail eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quail-egg-recipes), poached
-   8 sprigs of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes)
-   1 handful of [popcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/popcorn-recipes)
-   [black salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

-   4cm round cutter
-   2cm round cutter
-   Hand blender

1

To make the bavarois, remove the sweetcorn kernels from the husk and reserve for later. Chop up the cores, cover with water and bring to the boil. Simmer for 1 hour

-   4 fresh corn on the cob

2

Strain and measure out 250ml of the stock. Sauté the shallot in butter, then add the corn stock, lime juice, salt, and pepper. Simmer for a few minutes

-   1 [shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), chopped
-   20g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   16g of [lime juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
-   5g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [Espelette pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)

3

Finely blend the mixture with a hand blender, pass through a sieve and add the gelatine, stirring until dissolved. Allow to cool, add the semi-whipped cream and allow to rest in the fridge for 2–3 hours before serving

-   18g of gelatine, softened in iced water
-   340g of cream, semi-whipped

5

Add the shellfish stock and cook for another 10 minutes, adding a bouquet of tarragon and thyme to infuse as it simmers. Mix well, remove the star anise and cardamon pods then stir in the crème fraîche and lemon juice

-   500ml of shellfish stock, preferably lobster
-   4 sprigs of [thyme](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   80g of crème fraîche
-   20g of [lemon juice](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   4 sprigs of [tarragon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tarragon-recipes)

6

Preheat the oven to 170°C/gas mark 3.5

7

Cut 4 slices of brioche as thinly as possible and cut out with a 4cm cutter Stamp out the centres with 2cm cutters and discard, leaving you with small brioche rings. Bake in the oven between two flat trays for 6–8 minutes until golden brown. Leave the oven on for the cod

-   1 [brioche loaf](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brioche-recipes)

8

Blanch the baby corn in salted boiling water then cut in half and pan-fry until golden brown

-   4 [baby corn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sweetcorn-recipes)

9

To cook the cod, heat a non-stick frying pan over high heat and add the cod skin-side down. Cook for 2–3 minutes until golden brown, then transfer to the oven for a further 2 minutes or until the fish is cooked through

-   4 [cod fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cod-fillet-recipes), each weighing 150g

10

To serve, place a poached quail egg on each bowl, season with black salt and place a circle of brioche on top

-   4 [quail eggs](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/quail-egg-recipes), poached
-   [black salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

11

Season 20g of corn kernels and the baby corn and arrange them in the bowls with some popcorn. Garnish with dill sprigs and quenelle of bavarois. Add the cod and serve the cold bisque separately in a teapot

-   20g of corn kernels
-   1 handful of [popcorn](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/popcorn-recipes)
-   8 sprigs of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes)
