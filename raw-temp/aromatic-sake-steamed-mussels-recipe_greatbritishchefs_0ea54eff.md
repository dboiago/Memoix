Incredibly fresh and fragrant, there's nothing quite like a big bowl of steaming [mussels](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/mussels-recipes). Victoria's recipe is quick to cook and has clean and crisp [Japanese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/japanese-recipes) flavours from Mizuno Gotoshi Sake, [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/ginger-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/ginger-recipes), [chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chilli-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chilli-recipes)[](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chilli-recipes) and [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/spring-onion-recipes).

First published in 2015

Deliciously fragrant and comforting, my aromatic [sake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/features/ingredient-focus-sake)\-steamed mussels take no time to cook and also have the benefit of being cheap and sustainable. Mizuno Gotoshi Sake brings a clean, crisp note to this soothing broth, which makes an elegant starter or light main. Don’t forget to get enough crusty [bread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bread-recipes) in to mop up every last drop of juice.

### Metric

### Imperial

-   1kg [mussels](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mussels-recipes)
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 [piece of fresh ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), peeled and sliced finely
-   250ml of [sake](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sake-recipes), Shirataki Jozen Mizuno Gotoshi Pure Rice Wine (Ginjo)
-   1 tbsp of [tamari](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   3 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely sliced (or more if liked)
-   1 handful of [chopped flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
-   oil, for frying

1

Clean and debeard the mussels. Tap any open ones and discard any whose shells do not close. Transfer the mussels to a bowl of salted iced water. A great tip for ensuring a grit-free dish is to scatter a handful of oats over the top of the water and set aside for 45 minutes – the mussels feed off the oats and, in feeding, expel any sand trapped inside their shells

2

In a large pan, soften the garlic in a little oil and stir through the ginger. Pour in the sake and simmer for 5 minutes before adding the tamari

3

Toss in the spring onions and chillies, followed by the mussels. Place a lid on the pan and steam for 3–5 minutes, or until the mussels have opened

4

Take the pan off the heat and scatter over the chopped parsley. Serve immediately in warmed bowls
