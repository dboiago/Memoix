An herbaceous whiskey sour that serves up perfect refreshment for warm days.

For this whiskey sour riff, bartender Lani Suyama of Redlight Kitchen & Bar in Bellingham, Washington, was inspired by the flavors of lemon meringue and the aroma of desert sage. Combining a sage-infused bourbon with tangy lemon curd and herbaceous Bénédictine makes for easy-drinking refreshment that’s perfect for warm days.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. sage-infused bourbon
-   1 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1 oz. fresh egg white (pasteurized if you like)
-   1/2 oz. Bénédictine
-   [1/2 oz. demerara syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/demerara-syrup-or-turbinado-syrup-recipe/)
-   1 barspoon lemon curd

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a rocks glass over a large cube. Garnish.  
**Sage-Infused Bourbon**Add 1 1/2 tsp. chopped fresh sage to 750ml bourbon and allow to infuse for 2 days, then strain and bottle.
