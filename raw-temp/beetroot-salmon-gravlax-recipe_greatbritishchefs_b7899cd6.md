[Anna Hansen](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/anna-hansen)'s visually striking beetroot salmon gravlax recipe is laced with diverse flavours, as is customary with [The Modern Pantry](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/restaurants/the-modern-pantry) chef. The salmon is cured with a mix of [liquorice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/liquorice-recipes), beetroot juice and soy, while the winter slaw is a crunchy blend of kohlrabi, beetroot and fennel, dressed with a tangy yuzu dressing. Stack the dish elements attractively onto crispbreads to serve.

### Imperial

#### Cured salmon

-   1 whole side of salmon, skin on
-   800ml of [beetroot juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes)
-   2 1/2 tbsp of [liquorice paste](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/liquorice-recipes)
-   600ml of [light soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   6 [green cardamom pods](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cardamom-recipes), ground

#### Coleslaw

-   1 [kohlrabi](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kohlrabi-recipes), medium in size
-   1 [golden beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes), medium in size
-   1 [fennel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes), medium in size
-   1 jicama, medium in size
-   1 [Granny Smith apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), peeled
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   1/2 bunch of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), roughly chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

1

To prepare the salmon, check the fish to ensure there are no pin bones or stray scales. Place skin-side down into a narrow dish or, alternatively, a large re-sealable bag

-   1 whole side of salmon, skin on

3

After curing, remove the salmon from the liquid, lightly rinse under cold water for 3-4 minutes and pat dry. At this stage, the fish should be firmer in texture and stained red from the beetroot and soy. Leave uncovered and return to the fridge to dry cure for a further 8 hours

4

To make the coleslaw, finely slice the kohlrabi, beetroot, fennel, jicama and apple into strips using a mandoline or Julienne peeler and combine in a bowl. Add the lemon juice, chopped dill and a pinch of salt. Toss to coat and set aside until required

-   1 [kohlrabi](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/kohlrabi-recipes), medium in size
-   1 [golden beetroot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beetroot-starter-recipes), medium in size
-   1 [fennel](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-recipes), medium in size
-   1 jicama, medium in size
-   1 [Granny Smith apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), peeled
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   1/2 bunch of [dill](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/dill-recipes), roughly chopped
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

5

For the dressing, add the crème fraîche, lemon juice, water and yuzu to a bowl and mix well until combined. Season to taste

-   150g of crème fraîche
-   50ml of water
-   3 tbsp of [yuzu juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/yuzu-recipes)
-   1/2 [lemon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes), juiced
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

6

Using a sharp fish knife, cut the cured salmon into thin slices using a long, smooth slicing action in the direction away from the tail

7

Break the crispbreads into smaller, portion-sized pieces and top with some coleslaw. Drape the salmon slices over the top, drizzle over some dressing and serve immediately

-   2 [large crispbread](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes), preferably Peter’s Yard 242mm
