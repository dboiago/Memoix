Using [beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beef-recipes) brisket in this noodle soup recipe means you will have beautifully tender meat to carry the myriad of spices, with the fish sauce resolutely bringing up the rear. [Geoffrey Smeddle](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/geoffrey-smeddle)'s soup is inspired by Vietnamese _pho_ and is not only a fantastic [winter warmer](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/winter-comforts) – it's an easy recipe for any time of the year, created originally for the Sunday Herald.

First published in 2015

#### Ingredients

-   750g of [beef brisket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes)
-   500g of [chicken bones](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-starter-recipes)
-   2l water
-   2 red onions, peeled and sliced
-   5 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes)
-   3 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), roughly sliced
-   2 [cinnamon sticks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely sliced
-   5 [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   3 tsp [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)
-   1/2 tbsp of sugar
-   300g of [rice noodles](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/noodle-recipes)
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), juiced
-   250ml of vegetable oil
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

1

Rinse the brisket and chicken bones in cold water, then put them in a large pot and cover with two litres of cold water

-   750g of [beef brisket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes)
-   500g of [chicken bones](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-starter-recipes)
-   2l water

2

Bring to the boil, skim the scum that rises to the surface and reduce the heat to a steady simmer

3

Place the oil and red onions in a wok and cook over a medium heat, stirring often, for 10 minutes until golden brown

-   250ml of vegetable oil
-   2 red onions

4

Drain the onions, discarding the oil, and spread them out on kitchen paper to absorb any excess oil

6

Simmer for around two hours or until the brisket is very tender, at which point transfer the brisket to a bowl and allow to cool slightly

7

Strain the soup into a clean saucepan and discard the remaining ingredients

8

Flake the brisket back into the pot of broth, discarding any fatty pieces, then season the pho soup to your liking with the fish sauce and sugar

-   1 tbsp of [fish sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fish-sauce-recipes)
-   1/2 tbsp of sugar

9

Put your rice noodles in a bowl of salted boiling water. Cover tight and leave for a few minutes until soft, being sure not to overcook them

-   300g of [rice noodles](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/noodle-recipes)
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

10

Divide the noodles into individual bowls

11

Pour over the soup, slice the remaining white parts of the spring onions and sprinkle them over the soup. Finish with a squeeze of lime or lemon juice

-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
