This [beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/beef-recipes) steak recipe from [Kevin Mangeolles](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/kevin-mangeolles) delivers an elegant main course, as the beef is paired with [mushroom](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/mushroom-recipes) purée and [potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/potato-recipes) cooked with [bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/bacon-recipes). The inventive beef tea brings further beefy richness to this dish, flavoured with dried ceps and thyme. Creating this dish requires a good amount of planning and preparation, so it's a good idea to prepare some of the elements, such as the potatoes and the beef tea, ahead of time. The finished meal will be incredible and worth the effort.

First published in 2015

-   Fine sieve
-   Liquidiser
-   Vacuum bag and machine
-   5 x 2cm rings

1

Trim all the fat from the rib eye (there is a layer of meat round the centre of the rib eye. Remove this and use this piece for the braised beef)

-   2kg rib-eye steak

2

Roll the centre meat in cling film until tight, then cut into steaks

4

Seal and cook in a water bath at 83°C for 4 hours

5

When ready, take the meat out of the bag, scrape off the garlic and thyme, then cut the beef in half lengthways. Roll in cling film and put in the fridge

6

Season the steaks in a hot pan with a little oil and seal on both sides

-   oil
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

7

Cut the braised beef into 3cm pieces and add to the steaks in the pan along with the butter. When the steaks are cooked to your liking, take out of the pan and rest in a warm place

-   25g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

8

For the mushroom purée, preheat the oven to 150°C/Gas mark 2

10

Liquidise and pass through a fine sieve, then reheat with a little milk

-   1 dash of milk

11

For the potatoes, preheat the oven to 200°C/Gas mark 6

13

Grill the bacon, and cut up into small pieces

-   100g of [grilled bacon](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/bacon-recipes)

14

Scoop the flesh from the potatoes and mix with the bacon and chopped spring onion. Then put into 5cm diameter by 2cm thick rings and cool

-   2 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced

15

When ready to serve, pan fry the potato rings in a little butter and oil until golden on both sides

-   oil
-   1 knob of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)

16

To make the beef tea, mix all the ingredients together in a pan, bring to the boil then turn the gas down to a simmer. Cook for 1 ½ hours

-   1000ml of beef stock
-   10g of [thyme leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/thyme-recipes)
-   75g of [dried ceps](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cep-recipes)
-   3 [tomatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   10g of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   3 [egg whites](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)

17

Pass through a muslin cloth and discard the remnants

18

Blanch some baby carrots, then peel the skins off and add 100g butter to 100ml of water and warm. Cut some little gem lettuce

-   1 [little gem lettuce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes)
-   100g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes)
-   1 handful of [baby carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)

19

To serve, place some of the mushroom purée on the plate

20

Then put the braised beef on the purée. Slice the steak and put on the Macaire potatoes. Place the carrots and little gem on the plate and serve
