One of the best ways to dress up a glass of Champagne.

With its French origins, the Kir Royale is one of the simplest examples of a sparkling cocktail, and one of the best ways to dress up a glass of Champagne.

## Ingredients

-   ½ oz. crème de cassis
-   Chilled Champagne

## Preparation

Pour chilled champagne into a flute, then slowly add the crème de cassis.
