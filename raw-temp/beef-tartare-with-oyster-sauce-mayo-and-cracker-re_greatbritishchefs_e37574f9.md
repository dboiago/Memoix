A classic [beef](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/steak-recipes) tartare gets taken to new heights with an ingenious [Lee Kum Kee](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/campaigns/lee-kum-kee) oyster sauce [mayo](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes), which binds the ingredients together with a real depth of umami sweetness. On the side, the simple cracker is again imbued with the sauce – proving it can be used for so much more than stir-fries.

First published in 2026

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Tartare

-   400g of [beef rump](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes), trimmed of any fat and chopped
-   2 [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely chopped
-   10 cornichons, chopped
-   2 tbsp of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of [chopped flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)
-   [Aleppo chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), to garnish

#### Mayo

-   2 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of white wine vinegar
-   1 tbsp of [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   150ml of vegetable oil
-   100ml of olive oil
-   2 tbsp of oyster sauce

#### Cracker

-   150g of plain flour, (or gluten-free flour if needed)
-   5g of [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes)
-   3g of [caraway seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caraway-recipes)
-   20ml of water
-   60g of oyster sauce
-   2 tbsp of olive oil

-   Baking tray

1

To make the mayo, put the egg yolks, vinegar and mustard in a bowl and whisk to a paste. Whilst constantly whisking, gradually stream in both the oils until emulsified (you can also do this in a food processor if you like). Stir in the oyster sauce, then season to taste with salt and pepper

-   2 [egg yolks](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   2 tbsp of white wine vinegar
-   1 tbsp of [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   150ml of vegetable oil
-   100mm of olive oil
-   2 tbsp of oyster sauce
-   [salt and pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes) to taste

2

To make the cracker, preheat the oven to 180°C. Put the flour, fennel and caraway seeds in a bowl. Add the water, oyster sauce and olive oil and mix thoroughly

-   150g of plain flour
-   5g of [fennel seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/fennel-seed-recipes)
-   3g of [caraway seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caraway-recipes)
-   20ml of water
-   60g of oyster sauce
-   2 tbsp of olive oil

3

Place the dough between 2 sheets of baking paper and roll out to the thickness of a pound coin. Transfer to a baking tray and remove the top piece of baking paper

4

Bake the cracker for 20 minutes, then allow to cool

5

For the tartare, mix together all the ingredients (except the Aleppo chilli) with 4 tbsp of the oyster sauce mayo. Season to taste with salt and pepper

-   400g of [beef rump](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-burger-recipes), trimmed of any fat and chopped
-   2 [shallots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely chopped
-   10 cornichons, chopped
-   2 tbsp of [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), chopped
-   2 tbsp of [chopped flat-leaf parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes)

6

Divide the beef tartare between 4 serving plates, then break the cracker into 4 pieces and sit them alongside. Finish the tartare with more blobs of mayo, then sprinkle over the Aleppo chilli

-   [Aleppo chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), to garnish
