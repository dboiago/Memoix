**RECIPE:** _Christian Siglin for [Fernside](https://www.fernsidebar.com/), San Diego_

![Kalimotxo](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/amaro-kalimocho-fernside-san-diego-horizontal-crdt-emma-janzen.jpg)

For his take on the Kalimotxo (aka [Calimocho)](https://imbibemagazine.com/calimocho-cocktail/), Christian Siglin bumps up the bitter complexities of cola with Peychaud’s and Italian amaro Ramazzotti.

## Ingredients

-   3 oz. red wine
-   1/2 oz. Ramazzotti amaro
-   3-4 dashes Peychaud's bitters
-   4-6 oz. Mexican Coca Cola, to top

## Preparation

Add the wine, Ramazotti and bitters to a Collins glass and stir to combine. Fill with ice and top with cola. Garnish.
