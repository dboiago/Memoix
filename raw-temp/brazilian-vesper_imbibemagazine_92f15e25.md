![](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/el-che-brazilian-vesper-vertical-crdt-emma-janzen-330x495.jpg)In this South American spin on the traditional [Vesper Martini](https://imbibemagazine.com/vesper-martini-recipe/), Jacyara de Oliveira uses cachaça instead of gin for a slightly earthy flavor.

1 oz. cachaça  
1 oz. vodka  
1 oz. Lillet Blanc  
1 barspoon passionfruit syrup  
Tools: mixing glass, barspoon  
Glass: cocktail  
Garnish: lemon twist

Add all the ingredients to a mixing glass with ice and stir until well chilled and diluted. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass and garnish.

_Jacyara de Oliveira, [El Che](https://www.elchebarchicago.com/), Chicago_
