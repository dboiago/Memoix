## Cut the bread into small pieces and transfer to a bowl with salt and pepper. Dice the cheese into small cubes. (We recommend edamer, tilsit, gorgonzol...

**Cooks in:** _40mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   9 OZ. of stale bread
-   11 OZ. of cheese
-   4 of eggs
-   onion
-   milk
-   butter
-   extra-virgin olive oil
-   parsley
-   flour
-   grated Parmigiano cheese

### Method

1

Cut the bread into small pieces and transfer to a bowl with salt and pepper. Dice the cheese into small cubes. (We recommend edamer, tilsit, gorgonzola or grey cheese.)

2

Finely chop ½ onion and sauté in a pan with 1 teaspoon butter and 1 teaspoon oil. Mix ¼ liter milk with 4 eggs, the sautéed onion, bread, cheese and chopped parsley. Then mix in 3 tablespoons flour for consistency.

3

Shape the dumplings into small meatball shapes. Cook in boiling salted water for 10 minutes. Drain and dress with browned butter and grated parmesan cheese. _Buon appetito!_
