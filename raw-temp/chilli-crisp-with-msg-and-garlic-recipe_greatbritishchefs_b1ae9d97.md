Chilli crisp is a regional Chinese chilli oil from Guizhou, known for its unbelievably moreish chunks of savoury onion and [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes "Garlic") mixed in with the chillies. Mixed with a touch of vinegar, it’s delicious with [cucumbers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cucumber-recipes "Cucumber") or dumplings, or simply drizzle it over rice or chicken.

First published in 2024

### Imperial

-   400ml of vegetable oil
-   7 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), thinly sliced
-   1 [shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), sliced into half moons
-   2 [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   1 [cinnamon stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1 bay leaf
-   3 tbsp of [Sichuan chilli flakes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)
-   1 tsp [smoked paprika](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/paprika-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of [sesame seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sesame-seeds-recipes)
-   1 tsp MSG (monosodium glutamate) powder
-   1/2 tsp cane sugar
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

1

Begin by heating the vegetable oil to around 160°C in a large, heavy-bottomed pot

-   400ml of vegetable oil

2

Add the garlic to the oil and fry until it just begins to turn lightly golden, then remove from the oil, and transfer to some paper towel to drain

-   7 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), thinly sliced

3

Repeat this process with the shallots

-   1 [shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), sliced into half moons

4

Place the oil over a low heat and add in the star anise, cinnamon stick and bay leaf.  Infuse the aromatics for around 10 minutes, or until they become fragrant and toasted

-   2 [star anise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/star-anise-recipes)
-   1 [cinnamon stick](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)
-   1 bay leaf

6

Remove the aromatics from the oil and discard them

7

Heat up the oil to 170°C, then pour it over the chilli flake mix. Be careful as the oil is very hot, and will bubble up as it hits the spices

8

Leave the chilli to sizzle for a minute or so, then carefully stir everything until combined. Leave to cool

9

Taste the cooled chilli oil and season with a touch of extra MSG or salt as desired  
Transfer to a sterilised jar and store in the fridge. Spoon over anything you like
