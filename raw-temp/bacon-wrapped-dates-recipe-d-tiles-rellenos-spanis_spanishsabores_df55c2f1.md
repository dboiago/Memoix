## Introduction

**My go-to appetizer for any gathering** is these bacon wrapped dates! It's a dish that _never fails to impress,_ and is incredibly easy to make.

I first fell in love with this sweet and salty _pintxo_ while visiting the Basque Country in northern Spain, where every tapas bar features their own selection of _pintxos_. These skewered morsels are **a simple delight that you won't want to miss!**

## Ingredients

![bowls of ingredients for bacon wrapped dates.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Bacon-Wrapped-Dates-Ingredients-Labeled.jpg)

Making these Basque-inspired bacon wrapped dates means **you only need three ingredients:** dates, bacon, and cheese. I got creative by adding an almond, too. Let's talk about these ingredients!

-   **Dates:** Start with whole dates. Use ones that are already pitted to make the prep even faster!
-   **Cheese**: The cheese you choose is up to you, but I almost always go for goat cheese, as it turns wonderfully gooey and its acidity cuts the fattiness of the bacon.
-   **Bacon**: And when it comes to the bacon, the thinner the better. That will help you get the crispiness you want when they come out of the oven.
-   **Almond**: You can also optionally add a Spanish Marcona almond inside (it adds a delicious crunch!).

_See recipe card for full information on ingredients and quantities._

## Variations

-   **Cheese Swaps:** Try Manchego or blue cheese for a snack with a kick.
-   **Bacon Swaps:** Substitute with _jamón serrano_ or proscuitto.
-   **Different Nuts:** Try hazelnuts, pecans, or macadamia nuts instead of the almonds.
-   **Vegetarian Version:** Simply omit the bacon and enjoy!

## How to Make Bacon Wrapped Dates

If you’d like to see the full ingredients and instructions, _scroll to the bottom of the post_ for the printable recipe card.

![pitting the dates and stuffing them with goat cheese and almonds.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Bacon-Wrapped-Dates-Process-Collage-1.jpg)

1.  **Cut each date lengthwise,** removing the pit if necessary. (image 1)
2.  **Stuff** each date with _goat cheese and the almond,_ if using. (image 2)

![wrapping the stuffed dates with bacon and piercing with a toothpick.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Bacon-Wrapped-Dates-Process-Collage-2.jpg)

3.  **Press the date closed** with your fingers. (image 3)
4.  Cut each piece of bacon in half if necessary and **tightly roll each date in bacon.** Close with a toothpick and lay on a baking tray. Leave enough space between dates so each will get nice and crispy in the oven. (image 4)

![baked bacon wrapped dates on a tray and on a plate.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Bacon-Wrapped-Dates-Process-Collage-3.jpg)

5.  **Bake at 350°F** (175°C) for about 15-20 minutes. _Turn halfway through_ to make sure both sides get nice and crispy. (image 5)
6.  Let them cool for a couple of minutes, then **serve**! Warn guests they can be very hot inside. (image 6)

## Recipe FAQs

**What is bacon wrapped dates made of?**

These bacon wrapped dates are made from pitted dates stuffed with creamy goat cheese and a Spanish Marcona almond. They're wrapped in bacon and baked until crispy.

**How long do you cook bacon dates for?**

Bake bacon wrapped dates for 15 minutes at 350°F (175°C), turning them halfway through to ensure they brown evenly.

**How do you wrap dates?**

Wrap the bacon tightly around the date, then secure it with a toothpick. Soak the toothpicks in water before using to keep the wood from charring as they cook.

**What cheese pairs with dates?**

These Basque-inspired bacon wrapped dates taste best with goat cheese, Manchego, or blue cheese, but you can get creative and try other cheeses like cream cheese or ricotta as well.

![white plate of bacon wrapped dates with toothpicks.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Bacon-Wrapped-Dates-Featured-300x300.jpg)

-   ▢ 6 strips thinly sliced bacon or serrano ham or prosciutto
-   ▢ 12 large dates
-   ▢ 3 ounces goat cheese
-   ▢ 12 toothpicks soaked in water to minimize charring
-   ▢ 12 Marcona almonds optional

-   Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
    
-   Slice the dates lengthwise and carefully remove the pit. Replace it with a small amount of goat cheese, and the almond if using. Press the edges of the dates together to seal.
    
-   Cut the strips of bacon in half, and wrap each date in one piece of bacon. Poke a toothpick through the date to secure the bacon.
    
-   Place on a rimmed baking dish, leaving space between each date. Bake for 15-20 minutes, turning halfway through, until the bacon is brown and crispy.
    
-   Leave them to cool for a few minutes, then serve and enjoy!
    

-   Feel free to **experiment with different cheeses or nuts** inside for fun and tasty variations!
-   **Use an easy-to-clean baking dish** with a raised rim to catch the bacon grease. Alternatively, use a sheet pan lined with foil to make cleanup easy.
-   This recipe makes 12 pintxos, but **you can easily change the quantities** to make as many or as few as you need for your guests or just for you.
