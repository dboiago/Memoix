This seasonal tonic recipe updates the celery soda with vodka and cucumber.

Celery soda made our 2014 [Imbibe 75](https://imbibemagazine.com/In-this-Issue-Jan/Feb-2014) list of Flavors to Try, and this Cel-Ray Spring Tonic recipe updates the deli classic with celery leaves and cucumber.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. vodka
-   1 large sprig of celery leaves
-   3 fresh cucumber wheels
-   1 fresh lime wedge
-   ⅓ oz. fresh lime juice
-   Ginger ale

## Preparation

Combine celery leaves, cucumber, and lime wedge and muddle. Add remaining ingredients, except ginger ale, and shake with ice. Strain into an ice-filled Collins glass, top with ginger ale and garnish.
