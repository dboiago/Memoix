**RECIPE:** _John Lermayer for [Sweet Liberty](http://mysweetliberty.com/), Miami_

Photo: Seth Browarnik/WorldRedEye.com

![](https://imbibemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Sweet-Liberty-Baby-Bison.jpg)

A seasonal gem from Miami, featuring bison grass vodka and apple juice.

“It’s always a crowd-pleaser,” said John Lermayer of this easy-drinking vodka cocktail. “A session cocktail if there ever was one.”

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. vodka (Sweet Liberty uses Zu bison grass vodka)
-   1 1/2 oz. apple juice
-   1/2 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1/2 oz. Aperol

## Preparation

Shake ingredients together with ice. Strain into a chilled glass, then garnish.
