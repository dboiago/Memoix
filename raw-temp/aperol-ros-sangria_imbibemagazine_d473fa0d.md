An easy sangria recipe for those last-minute summer get-togethers.

Nothing says summertime like Aperol, rosé wine, fresh berries, and a bit of bubbles. This sangria recipe from _Sips of the Season_ author [Sarah Gualtieri](https://www.instagram.com/the.boozy.ginger/) features all of that and is easy enough to throw together for last-minute get-togethers.

## Ingredients

Yield:6

-   750 ml/3 cups rosé wine
-   1/2 cup Aperol
-   1/2 cup raspberries
-   1/2 cup strawberries, hulled and chopped
-   [1/4 cup simple syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/simple-syrup/)
-   1 orange, thinly sliced
-   1 lemon, thinly sliced
-   club soda, to top

## Preparation

Pour the rosé, Aperol, and syrup into the large pitcher. Add the berries and citrus slices and stir to combine. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours. Pour into wine glasses filled with ice. Top each glass with a splash of club soda. Garnish with extra fruit.
