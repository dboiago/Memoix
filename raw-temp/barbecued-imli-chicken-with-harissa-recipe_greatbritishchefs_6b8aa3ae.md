Rohit Ghai’s imli ([tamarind](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tamarind-recipes)) [chicken](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chicken-recipes) recipe celebrates the natural pairing of sweetness, smokiness and tang. The tamarind and jaggery in the glaze provide plenty of sweet zing, which caramelises wonderfully over the coals as it cooks. Served with a punchy [harissa](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/harissa-recipes), crunchy papaya salad and a scattering of peanut thecha, it’s a wonderful dish that looks impressive but is easily made in advance.

First published in 2026

Note: You can easily swap out the poussin for regular chicken breasts if you like – just make sure you alter the cooking time accordingly. The accompaniments are also entirely optional; the star here is the chicken, which will still be wonderful even if just served with basmati rice.

#### HARISSA

-   200g of [dried red chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), soaked in cold water overnight
-   10 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   30ml of malt vinegar
-   2g of [cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)
-   20g of [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes), toasted and ground
-   10g of [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   40g of soya yoghurt

#### GLAZE

-   100g of [tamarind pulp](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tamarind-recipes)
-   60g of jaggery
-   20g of balsamic glaze
-   10g of brown sugar
-   10g of [garam masala](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garam-masala-recipes)
-   5g of [Kashmiri chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   5g of [ginger](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/ginger-recipes), finely grated
-   2 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   5g of [mint](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mint-recipes), finely chopped

#### PAPAYA SALAD

-   vegetable oil, for cooking
-   10g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 pinch of [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   100g of green papaya, (unripe), julienned
-   5ml of white vinegar
-   10g of caster sugar

#### PEANUT THECHA

-   1 dash of vegetable oil
-   3 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   5 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)
-   100g of [blanched peanuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-recipes), toasted
-   1 tsp [Kashmiri chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)

-   Squeezy bottle
-   Spice grinder
-   Charcoal barbecue or grill

2

For the harissa, drain the chillies and add to a blender with the garlic, vinegar, cloves, cumin and coriander. Blitz to a paste, season with salt, then transfer to a small saucepan. Cook gently for 15-20 minutes, stirring regularly, then leave to cool before stirring in the soya yoghurt. Transfer to a squeezy bottle

-   10 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)

-   10g of [ground coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   40g of soya yoghurt

4

For the papaya salad, heat a dash of oil in a small saucepan. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, then stir in the turmeric and cook for 1 more minute. Add the papaya, vinegar, sugar and a pinch of salt and cook for around 5 minutes, until the papaya has softened but retains its crunch. Season with black pepper and set aside to cool

-   vegetable oil, for cooking
-   10g of [garlic](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), finely sliced
-   1 pinch of [ground turmeric](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/turmeric-recipes)
-   100g of green papaya, (unripe), julienned
-   5ml of white vinegar
-   10g of caster sugar

5

For the peanut thecha, heat a dash of oil to a saucepan over a medium heat. Add the whole chillies and peeled garlic cloves and sauté for 3-4 minutes, until the chillies are blistered and the garlic is slightly softened. Add the cumin, stir for 30 seconds, then leave the mixture to cool

-   1 dash of vegetable oil
-   3 [green chillies](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   5 [garlic cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes)
-   1/2 tsp [cumin seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cumin-recipes)

6

Once cool, place in a spice grinder or food processor with the peanuts, kashmiri chilli powder and a pinch of salt. Blitz to a semi-coarse texture, then gently heat the mixture in a dry pan to dry it out slightly. Set aside

-   100g of [blanched peanuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peanut-recipes), toasted
-   1 tsp [Kashmiri chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)

7

Set up a barbecue for direct grilling. Once the coals are glowing white, grill the marinated poussin breasts for 1 minute on each side. Start liberally brushing the glaze over the poussin and cook for another 2-3 minutes (the cooking time may differ if you’re using larger cuts of chicken)

8

Serve the chicken with dots of the harissa and the papaya salad alongside. Garnish with a sprinkling of peanut thecha and the pea shoots, pickled chillies and edible flowers (if using)
