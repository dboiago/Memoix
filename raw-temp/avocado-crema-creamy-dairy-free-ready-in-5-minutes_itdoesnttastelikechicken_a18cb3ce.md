## **Ingredients for Avocado Crem**a

-   **Ripe avocado:** Choose one that gives slightly when gently pressed.
-   **Vegan yogurt:** Soy, almond, or coconut yogurt all work well, as long as it's plain.
-   **Lime juice:** Use fresh juice, not bottled, for the best flavor.
-   **Garlic:** Use a small clove; the lime juice will mellow its sharpness, so you're left with a savory garlic flavor.
-   **Salt:** Add as much as you need.
-   **Water:** Used to thin the crema to your preferred consistency.

## Tips & Variations

-   **Make a bigger batch:** This recipe is easy to double or triple if you're serving a crowd or meal prepping.
-   **Avocado ripeness:** If your avocado is even slightly underripe, the crema can taste a bit bitter. Use a ripe one that gives slightly when pressed.
-   **Yogurt tip:** Use plain, unsweetened yogurt (vanilla yogurt will taste weird here).
-   **Cilantro lime version:** Blend in fresh cilantro for a bright, herby twist.
-   **Spicy crema:** Add chipotle in adobo for smoky heat.
-   **Extra garlic flavor:** Include another small clove if you love a stronger garlic flavor.

## Storage

Store avocado crema in an airtight container in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days. To help prevent browning, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface before covering. Stir before serving.

Prep: 5 minutes

Total: 5 minutes

Servings: 4 (makes about 1 cup)

-   ▢ 1 large ripe avocado
-   ▢ ¼ cup vegan yogurt, (such as soy, almond, or coconut)
-   ▢ 1 lime, juiced (about 2 tablespoons)
-   ▢ 1 small clove garlic
-   ▢ ¼ teaspoon [salt](https://amzn.to/4jq9UHf), or to taste
-   ▢ 2 - 4 tablespoons water, to thin as needed

-   **Blend:** Add the avocado, yogurt, lime juice, garlic, and salt to a high-speed blender or small food processor and blend until completely smooth.
    
    ![Blender jar with ingredients for avocado crema.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/square-Avocado-Crema-02-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Thin:** Add water 1 tablespoon at a time until you reach your desired drizzleable consistency.
    
    ![Avocado crema in jar of blender.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/square-Avocado-Crema-03-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Taste and finish:** Taste and adjust salt and lime as needed.
    

**Storage:**Store avocado crema in an airtight container in the fridge for 3-4 days. To prevent browning, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface before sealing the container. Stir before serving.

**Double it:** This recipe makes a small batch (about 1 cup), but you can easily double or triple it if you're feeding a crowd or prepping multiple meals.

**Flavor Variations:**

-   **Cilantro Lime Crema:** Add ¼ cup fresh cilantro
-   **Spicy Crema:** Add ½-1 teaspoon chipotle in adobo
-   **Garlic Lovers:** Add 1 extra small clove garlic

**Use avocado crema on:**

-   Tacos
-   Burrito bowls
-   Sofritas bowls
-   Nachos
-   Roasted sweet potatoes
-   Quesadillas
-   Grain bowls
-   Salads
