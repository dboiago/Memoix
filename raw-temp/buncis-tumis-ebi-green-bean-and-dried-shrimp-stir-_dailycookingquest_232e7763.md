One of the simplest Indonesian way to prepare greens, especially green beans and snake beans, is definitely stir frying with dried shrimps and galangal slices. If you cannot find dried shrimps, Japanese _katsuo bushi_ (dried bonito shavings) can be used instead, but sprinkles at the very end of the cooking.

## Buncis Tumis Ebi - Green Bean and Dried Shrimp Stir Fry

Author: [Anita Jacobson](https://dailycookingquest.com/about.html)

Categories: [Side Dish](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/side-dish.html)

Cuisines: [Indonesian](https://dailycookingquest.com/cuisines/indonesian.html)

Ingredients: [Seafood](https://dailycookingquest.com/ingredients/seafood.html) [Vegetables](https://dailycookingquest.com/ingredients/vegetables.html)

Prep Time: 15 mins

Cook Time: 5 mins

Total Time: 20 mins

Serves: 4

[Print Recipe](https://dailycookingquest.com/card/buncis-tumis-ebi-green-bean-and-dried-shrimp-stir-fry.html)

### Ingredients

-   2 tablespoon oil
-   1 inch galangal (Indonesian: lengkuas), peeled and cut into thin slices
-   3 tablespoon dried shrimps (Indonesian: ebi), washed under running water and drained
-   300 gram green bean (Indonesian: buncis), remove ends and cut into 2 inch lengths
-   1 teaspoon salt
-   1/4 cup water

### Instructions

1.  Heat oil in a frying pan on high and sauté galangal until fragrant, about 1 minute.
2.  Add dried shrimps and stir for 30 seconds.
3.  Add green bean, season with salt, and mix well. Pour water and cook for 3 minutes, or until the green bean turns a darker shade of green, tender, but still crunchy. Remove from heat and serve immediately.
