### List of Ingredients

-   8 of slices lardo
-   4 of jumbo shrimp
-   1 of long slice sandwich bread, crust removed

### Method

1

Peel and devein the shrimp. Cut the bread slice into 4 rectangular pieces (4” x 1 1/2”). Place one shrimp on each piece of bread and roll up everything in 2 slices of lardo. Repeat with the remaining shrimp to make 4 “tostadas.” Bake for 4-5 minutes at 425°F: the lardo will dissolve and be absorbed into the bread. Remove from the oven and serve immediately.
