This warm whisky [cocktail](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cocktail-recipes) from Peg + Patriot bartender Matt Whiley is sure to hit the spot on a chilly evening, its spiced, buttery finish offering a real treat for the palate. You will need some micro scales to get this buttered whisky cocktail recipe just right. Read our interview to [learn more about Matt's cocktails](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/community/matt-whiley-interview).

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### To finish

-   50ml of [whisky](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/whisky-cocktail-recipes)
-   hot water

-   Micro scales
-   Food mixer with paddle attachment

1

To prepare the butter, add all of the ingredients to a food mixer with a paddle attachment and mix well for 1-2 minutes until all of the ingredients are incorporated. Alternatively, use a mixing bowl and wooden spatula

-   60ml of [whisky](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/whisky-cocktail-recipes)
-   125g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), at room temperature
-   90g of brown sugar
-   30g of [agave syrup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/honey-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1g of [nutmeg](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/nutmeg-recipes)
-   1g of [allspice powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/allspice-recipes)
-   1g of [ground cloves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/clove-recipes)
-   2.5g of [cinnamon powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cinnamon-recipes)

2

Turn out onto a sheet of cling film and spread out to make a 1/2cm thick layer wrap tightly into a block of cling film. Alternatively roll tightly in cling film to form a cylindrical shape and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or until firm

3

To assemble the cocktail, cut a 3cm square or small cylinder of the whisky butter and add to a mug. Pour in 50ml of whisky, then top up with hot water and stir to incorporate

-   50ml of [whisky](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/whisky-cocktail-recipes)
-   hot water
