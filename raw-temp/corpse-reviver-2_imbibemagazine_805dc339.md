A classic cocktail with a name that could wake the dead.

The Corpse Reviver #2 is rightfully considered by many to be the best-tasting of the Corpse Reviver cocktails. It’s one of the staples at Chicago’s gin-focused bar [Scofflaw](https://www.scofflawchicago.com/), where bar manager Luke DeYoung calls it “perfectly balanced, refreshing yet still strong. It’s an iconic cocktail that won’t go out of fashion any time soon.”

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. gin
-   1 oz. Cointreau
-   1 oz. Lillet Blanc
-   1 oz. fresh lemon juice
-   1 dash absinthe

## Preparation

Shake all ingredients with ice, strain into a chilled glass, and garnish.
