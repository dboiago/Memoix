A _balado_ dish always makes me salivate even when I have no appetite. I know it’s weird, but a plate of _balado_ is probably going to cure me from any sickness faster than a bowl of chicken soup! You can practically turn anything into a _balado_ dish with this sauce. Some of the more popular choices include [eggplants](https://dailycookingquest.com/terong-balado-eggplants-with-chili-sauce.html), [hard boiled eggs](https://dailycookingquest.com/telur-balado-eggs-with-chili-sauce.html), [tempe](https://dailycookingquest.com/balado-tempeh-dan-udang.html), and _dendeng_ (deep fried thinly sliced beef). This shrimp/potato/stink bean combo is also a popular choice for this dish. If you love spicy food, substitute cayenne chilies with bird eye chilies, which I think is what most restaurants use since their _balado_ dishes are always super hot.

## Balado Udang - Shrimps in Spicy Chili Sauce

Categories: [Main Dish](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/main-dish.html)

Cuisines: [Indonesian](https://dailycookingquest.com/cuisines/indonesian.html)

Ingredients: [Seafood](https://dailycookingquest.com/ingredients/seafood.html)

Prep Time: 30 mins

Cook Time: 15 mins

Total Time: 45 mins

Serves: 4

[Print Recipe](https://dailycookingquest.com/card/balado-udang-shrimps-in-spicy-chili-sauce.html)

### Ingredients

-   500 gram shrimps, shelled and deveined
-   juice of 1 lime
-   3 tablespoon oil
-   1 potato, peeled and cut into 1 inch cubes
-   100 gram stink beans (Indonesian: pete), halve each
-   2 tablespoon sugar, or to taste
-   2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
-   2 tomatoes
-   5-10 red cayenne chilies (Indonesian: cabe merah keriting)
-   6 asian shallots (Indonesian: bawang merah), about 75 gram
-   6 cloves garlic
-   1 scallion, thinly sliced

### Instructions

1.  In a mixing bowl, marinate shrimps and lime together for 15 minutes. Wash in cold water, drain, and set aside.
2.  Heat oil in a wok, fry potatoes and stink beans until the potatoes are half way cooked, about 3 minutes.
3.  Add spice paste into the wok, and bring to a boil.
4.  Season with salt and sugar, adjust the amount to suit your taste. Since the acidity and sweetness of tomatoes may varies, the amount of salt and sugar you need may be slightly different.
5.  Once the sauce starts to thicken and the potatoes are fully cooked, add shrimps into the wok. Stir and cook until the shrimps turn pink, about 30 seconds to 1 minute.
6.  Turn off heat, transfer to a serving plate and garnish with scallion. Serve immediately with steamed white rice.
