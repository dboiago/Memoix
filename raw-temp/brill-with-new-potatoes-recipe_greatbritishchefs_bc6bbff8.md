Brill is a flat-fish with similar qualities to [turbot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/turbot-recipes). [Frances Atkins](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/frances-atkins) makes use of the fish by preparing a simple but superb cream cheese and wasabi mix to smother over the top. Drizzle over extra virgin olive oil or a herb-infused olive oil before serving this quick and satisfying [fish dish](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/seafood-recipes).

First published in 2015

-   Blender

1

Place the potatoes in cold, salted water and bring to a gentle boil. Cook until tender, approximately 10 minutes

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   250g of [new potatoes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/new-potato-recipes)

2

In a food processor, pulse together the fresh lovage and sourdough breadcrumbs to form the crust. Set aside until required

-   90g of sourdough breadcrumbs
-   30g of [fresh lovage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lovage-recipes)

3

In a bowl, squeeze the wasabi into the soft cheese to taste

-   1 tube of wasabi
-   250g of soft cream cheese

4

Place the brill under a hot grill for 3 minutes on each side. Remove and then spread the cream cheese/wasabi mixture over the top of the fish - use the back of a spoon to do so and be gentle or the fish will start to break up. Sprinkle over the flavoured breadcrumbs and place under the grill again until the crust browns

-   4 [brill fillets](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brill-recipes)

5

To plate, arrange the potatoes onto plates and lay the brill fillets on top. Serve immediately
