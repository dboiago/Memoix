## Garnished with Greek yogurt and Jerusalem artichokes, simple beef carpaccio is a quick and tasty appetizer.

**Cooks in:** _10mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   3 OZ. of Jerusalem artichokes
-   5 OZ. of beef carpaccio
-   4 Brussels sprouts
-   1/3 CUP of Greek yogurt

### Method

1

Peel the Jerusalem artichokes and cut them into very thin slices. 

2

Divide the beef carpaccio between 4 plates. Season with a pinch of salt. Garnish with the leaves of the 4 Brussels sprouts, the sliced Jerusalem artichokes and large dollops of Greek yogurt.
