### Cider Squirt

## Recipe Notes

-   **Yield:** 4 ½ cups

## Ingredients

-   2 cups apple cider or juice
-   ¾ cup cider vinegar
-   ½ cup bourbon, or more apple cider
-   ½ cup water
-   ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce
-   ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
-   1 teaspoon salt

## Recipe Steps

**1:** Combine all the ingredients in a heavy saucepan and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer the sauce for 5 minutes. Let cool to room temperature. Transfer the sauce to a food-safe spray bottle and use right away or refrigerate. Use within 1 to 2 days of making.
