-   4
    
-   30 minutes
    

This simple cured trout recipe from Daniel Galmiche is packed with vibrant flavours of lime and [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/coriander-recipes) to complement the delicate flesh of the [trout](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/trout-recipes).

First published in 2016

1

Lay the trout, skin-side down, on a large piece of cling film. Sprinkle over the salt and sugar, followed by the coriander seeds, lime zest and coriander leaves

-   1 handful of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 [side of trout](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/trout-recipes), skin-on, pinboned
-   1 handful of [fine sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 handful of caster sugar
-   1 tbsp of [coriander seeds](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-seed-recipes), crushed
-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes), zested

2

Wrap in cling film and place flesh-side down on a baking tray. Top with another tray, then place light weights on top to gently press the trout

3

Refrigerate for 8 hours depending on the thickness of the fish, turning often

4

Once cured, remove from the fridge and carefully rinse under cold water to remove the excess cure. Pat dry

5

Slice thinly and arrange on serving plates. Brush with olive oil and garnish with celery and coriander leaves, batons of apple, salt and pepper

-   2 tbsp of olive oil
-   1 handful of [celery leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celery-recipes)
-   1 bunch of [coriander leaves](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   1 [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), green
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

6

Grate over a little more lime zest to serve

-   1 [lime](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lime-recipes)
