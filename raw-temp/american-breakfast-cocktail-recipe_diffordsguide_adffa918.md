### Glass:

Serve in an [Old-fashioned glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

2 fl oz

[Bourbon whiskey](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/69/makers-mark-bourbon)

1⁄2 fl oz

Pink grapefruit juice (freshly squeezed)

1⁄2 fl oz

Maple syrup

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill an [OLD-FASHIONED GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of grapefruit zest twist.

### How to make:

3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-strain-a-cocktail) into ice-filled glass.

### Garnish:

5.  [EXPRESS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1269/cocktail-garnishes/citrus-zest-twists) grapefruit zest twist over cocktail and use as garnish.
