## American Style Pancakes with Maple Syrup Butter and Bacon

From our breakfast menu at Urban Pantry - American Style Pancakes with Maple Syrup Butter and Bacon (not pictured).  
Makes 8-10 portions

## Ingredients

-   For the American Style Pancakes:
-   3tsp Baking Powder
-   pinch of salt
-   260g Self raising flour
-   3tbsp caster sugar
-   2 eggs
-   30 melted butter
-   280ml milk
-   For the Maple Syrup Butter:
-   400g maple syrup
-   250g butter, cut into small cubes.
-   Optional:
-   Bacon
-   Strawberries or Blueberries

## Method

For the American Style Pancakes:  
Mix all the dry ingredients ie baking powder, salt, sugar and flour together.  
Gradually add melted butter, eggs and milk.  
Beat until it’s a lovely thick batter.  
Can refrigerate for up 3 days.  
For the Maple Syrup Butter:  
Bring syrup to the boil. Cook for 5 minutes and then add the cubed butter gradually.  
Transfer mixture to a bowl and mix until it cools down.  
Can keep for up to a month, if you haven’t drank it all before.  
To Serve:  
Spoon in the batter into a medium hot frying pan. Fry on both sides until golden and delicious.  
Lay 3 pancakes on top of each other, followed by some sliced strawberries or blueberries, crispy streaky bacon and top with the maple syrup butter.
