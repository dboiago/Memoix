Since I posted the pelau recipe and said that it goes good with **cole slaw**, I decided to post it today. A simple cole slaw not only makes the plate looks attractive, it also adds some extra raw vegetables to the meal.

Cole slaw is crunchy and goes perfect with [pelau](https://simplytrinicooking.com/pelau-ii/), a rice dish. However, you can pair it with other types of rice dishes, even plain rice that goes with a fish, meat, chicken or even a pork.

![cole slaw](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSI2OTE1IiBoZWlnaHQ9IjQwNDgiIHZpZXdCb3g9IjAgMCA2OTE1IDQwNDgiPjxyZWN0IHdpZHRoPSIxMDAlIiBoZWlnaHQ9IjEwMCUiIGZpbGw9IiNjZmQ0ZGIiLz48L3N2Zz4= "Cole Slaw: A Great Way to Get Your Raw Vegetables 1")

I prefer the cabbage and carrots sliced thinly, but not minced. You can slice the cabbage and carrots it with a sharp knife. Do that carefully if you have never chopped carrots or cabbage before. These two ingredients are quite firm.

## Cole Slaw Recipe

_Cole slaw together with pelau and a slice of zaboca (avocado)._

### Cole Slaw

_**Ingredients:**_

1 cup cabbage, finely shredded  
1 small carrots, finely shredded  
2 teaspoons mayonnaise (optional)  
½ teaspoon mustard sauce  
1 teaspon sugar  
black pepper to taste

**_Direction:_**

![IM000519](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzMjAiIGhlaWdodD0iMjQwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzIwIDI0MCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "Cole Slaw: A Great Way to Get Your Raw Vegetables 2")

Wash the cabbage and peel the carrots. Finely shred the cabbage and carrots. You can do this in a food processor or by hand. The cabbage and carrots will be finer and the mixture less crunchy than if you had chopped the ingredients by hand.

![IM000520 1](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzMjAiIGhlaWdodD0iMjQwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzIwIDI0MCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "Cole Slaw: A Great Way to Get Your Raw Vegetables 3")

Mix the cabbage with the rest of the ingredients.

![IM000521](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzMjAiIGhlaWdodD0iMjQwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzIwIDI0MCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "Cole Slaw: A Great Way to Get Your Raw Vegetables 4")

Serve with your favourite rice dish.

![cole slaw](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzMjAiIGhlaWdodD0iMjQwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzIwIDI0MCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "Cole Slaw: A Great Way to Get Your Raw Vegetables 5")
