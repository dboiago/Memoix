## Mascarpone, coffee and coconut: easy, magic and delicious.

**Cooks in:** _15mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   1 prepackaged tiramisù kit (sponge cake, instant coffee, instant custard powder, and cocoa powder)
-   1 C. of mascarpone cheese
-   2 of eggs
-   4 of tbsp. shredded coconut
-   Powdered sugar

### Method

1

Prepare the instant coffee as indicated on the package. Use half of this coffee to soak one rectangle of sponge cake. In a bowl, beat 2 whole eggs with 1/3 cup of water. Add in the custard powder and beat everything for about 2 minutes with an electric hand mixer.

2

Add the mascarpone and continue to mix until everything is thoroughly combined. Spread half of the cream onto the layer of coffee-soaked sponge cake and sprinkle the coconut on top. Cover with the second piece of sponge cake, pour the rest of the coffee on top, and cover with the remaining cream. Using a sifter, dust the tiramisù with the cocoa powder.

3

For the powdered sugar decoration (see photo), cut out four equal strips of cardboard and place them on top of the tiramisù, then use a sifter to sprinkle on the powdered sugar.
