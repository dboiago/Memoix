The classic combo of iced tea and lemonade gets a twist. 

Playing with the framework of the classic [Arnold Palmer](https://imbibemagazine.com/arnold-palmer-riffs/) (a simple mix of iced tea and lemonade), Lee Zaremba introduces vodka and a refreshing hibiscus syrup to the mix in this update.

## Ingredients

-   1 1/2 oz. vodka
-   2 1/2 oz. chilled black tea
-   3/4 oz. hibiscus syrup
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients together in a shaker with ice, then strain over crushed ice. Garnish.  
**Hibiscus Syrup:** Add 12½ grams of dried hibiscus leaves to 2 cups of 1:1 [simple syrup](https://imbibemagazine.com/simple-syrup/). Heat on medium-high for 20 minutes. Remove from heat and cool in ice bath. Strain, and store in fridge.
