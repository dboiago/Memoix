A fun pudding or [party](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/childrens-party-recipes) treat for kids, this easy fondue recipe from [Marcello Tully](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/marcello-tully) is incredibly quick and versatile. Mix up the fruits and sweet treats for dunking, strawberries and pineapples work particularly well, or even chunks of chocolate brownie for a double [chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/chocolate-recipes) extravaganza.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   200g of [milk chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)
-   1 [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes), chopped
-   1 handful of [grapes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grape-recipes)
-   50g of marshmallow
-   5g of popping candy, optional
-   1 tsp edible glitter
-   1 tsp [desiccated coconut](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coconut-recipes), optional

-   Skewers

1

First, melt the chocolate. Add the chocolate to a bowl and place over a pan of hot water - don’t let the base of the bowl come into contact with the water. Gently stir until all of the chocolate melts

-   200g of [milk chocolate](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chocolate-dessert-recipes)

2

Add the chopped banana to a bowl along with the grapes. Empty the marshmallows into a separate bowl

-   1 [banana](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/banana-recipes), chopped
-   1 handful of [grapes](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/grape-recipes)
-   50g of marshmallow

3

Alternately thread the fruit and marshmallows onto cocktail sticks or skewers. Dip into the chocolate to coat and place onto a plate

4

Lastly, sprinkle over the popping candy and edible glitter, or, if you prefer, desiccated coconut

-   5g of popping candy
-   1 tsp edible glitter
