This brilliant hot dog recipe from Michelin-starred chef [James Knappett](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/james-knappett-chef "James Knappett") brings together [coleslaw](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coleslaw-recipes "Coleslaw"), bacon and barbecue sauce, to create a truly irresistible hot dog.

First published in 2024

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Barbecue sauce

-   3 tbsp of [French's classic mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of caster sugar
-   1 tbsp of brown sugar
-   1 tsp apple cider vinegar
-   1 tbsp of water
-   1 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), cold

#### Coleslaw

-   500g of [white cabbage](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cabbage-recipes)
-   50g of [carrots](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/carrot-recipes)
-   3/4 cup of [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes)
-   1/2 cup of cane sugar
-   1/3 cup of buttermilk
-   1 tbsp of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   3 tbsp of white wine vinegar
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

1

Mix all the ingredients for the barbecue sauce in a pan, except the butter and soy sauce (these two will be added close to end of the cooking time)  

-   3 tbsp of [French's classic mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   1 tbsp of caster sugar
-   1 tbsp of brown sugar
-   1 tsp apple cider vinegar
-   1 tbsp of water
-   1 tsp [chilli powder](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes)
-   1 tsp [cayenne pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cayenne-pepper-recipes)

2

Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer and cook for 1 hour until the sauce is thick and has a dark golden colour. Make sure to whisk constantly whilst simmering to make sure it doesn’t stick to the bottom of the pan

3

When thick and golden, add the soy sauce and the butter and simmer again for 5 minutes

-   1/4 tsp [soy sauce](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/soy-sauce-recipes)
-   1g of [butter](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/butter-recipes), cold

4

Then, let the sauce cool down (keep refrigerated for up to 5 days)

6

Make the coleslaw dressing by mixing all the rest of the ingredients listed. Mix well with a whisk until you get a smooth sauce (not grainy, if it does look grainy keep on mixing)

-   3/4 cup of [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes)
-   1/2 cup of cane sugar
-   1/3 cup of buttermilk
-   1 tbsp of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)
-   3 tbsp of white wine vinegar
-   1 tsp [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1/4 tsp [freshly ground black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

7

Just before serving, dress the vegetables with the sauce

8

Cook your hot dogs in a sauté pan with a bit of vegetable oil until heated through

-   4 [hot dog sausages](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/sausage-recipes)

10

Put some barbecue sauce in a roll, then the hotdog, then more barbecue sauce. Add your coleslaw and sprinkle the crispy bacon

-   4 [hot dog buns](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/easy-bread-recipes)
