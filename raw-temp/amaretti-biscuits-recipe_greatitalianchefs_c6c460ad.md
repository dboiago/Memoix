This amaretti biscuit recipe means you can create these beloved little treats at home. A simple combination of egg whites, ground [almonds](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes) and sugar is flavoured with vanilla and Amaretto, resulting in a [gluten-free](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/gluten-free-recipes)[](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/gluten-free-recipes) biscuit with a delightfully chewy texture.

First published in 2018

### Metric

### Imperial

-   200g of [ground almonds](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/almond-recipes)
-   200g of sugar
-   1/8 tsp bicarbonate of soda
-   1 tbsp of Amaretto
-   1/4 tsp vanilla essence
-   3 [medium egg whites](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-white-recipes)
-   icing sugar, to dust

1

Preheat an oven to 150°C/gas mark 2

2

Place the ground almonds, sugar and bicarbonate of soda in a bowl and mix together

3

Stir in the egg whites, vanilla essence and Amaretto, mixing until you have a thick paste

4

Transfer the mixture to a piping bag, then snip a 1.5cm hole at the end

5

Pipe even blobs, around 2.5cm in diameter, onto a baking tray lined with greaseproof paper

6

Using a damp pastry brush, dab each blob to get a smooth top, then dust with icing sugar

7

Bake for 20–25 minutes, or until the biscuits are cracked and pale gold

8

Leave to cool a little then dust with more icing sugar
