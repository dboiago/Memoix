One of the most popular classic cocktails of the past century.

With its simple yet infinitely balanced blend of ingredients, the classic Sidecar recipe has become one of the most popular classic cocktails of the past century.

## Ingredients

-   2 oz. Cognac
-   1 oz. Cointreau
-   3/4 oz. fresh lemon juice

## Preparation

Shake ingredients with ice and strain into a chilled glass.
