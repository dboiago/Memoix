A simple refresher.

With three simple ingredients, the Bee’s Knees cocktail shows why honey can be such a perfect sweetening agent for cocktails.

## Ingredients

-   1½ oz. gin
-   1 tsp. honey
-   1 tsp. fresh lemon juice

## Preparation

Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice. Shake, strain into a chilled glass and garnish.
