A complex but delicate take on the Espresso Martini.

This boldly flavored cocktail is not the [Espresso Martini](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/vodka-espresso-recipe/) recipe to which you may be accustomed. At Cane & Table in New Orleans, bartender Jordan Bearss approaches the drink from a culinary mindset, using coffee to emphasize the cocoa undertones of fernet, while the spiced rum and Grand Marnier balance the bitterness, and orgeat adds a nutty sweetness. “If there’s one thing I’ve learned from Cajun cooking, it’s that a whole mess of strong flavors can come together to create something that is both complex and delicate,” says Bearss.

## Ingredients

-   1 oz. spiced rum
-   1 oz. cold-brew concentrate
-   1/2 oz. Fernet-Branca
-   1/2 oz. Grand Marnier
-   [1/4 oz. orgeat](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/homemade-orgeat-recipe/)

## Preparation

Shake all of the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe.
