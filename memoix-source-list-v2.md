# Memoix Corpus Source List, v2 (working list)

Keep-only. Everything here is approved for the extraction pipeline. Cut sources are
in the appendix at the bottom for reference only, not for use.

Working principles:
- Authenticity from a genuine cultural or professional source outweighs popularity.
  A widely-read site by and for its own audience (RecipeTin Japan, La Cucina Italiana,
  Directo al Paladar) is not the same as a Western outlet adapting someone else's cuisine.
- Scoped exceptions exist for otherwise-banned outlets where a specific named
  author/section does real technical work outside the outlet's general content,
  limited to general technique or the outlet's own tradition, never another cuisine.
- Coverage gaps are acceptable, weak sources are not. An empty category is a known
  gap to fill later, not a reason to lower the bar.

This is a seed list, not a checklist. The pipeline scrapes and ingests at the site
or category level, not recipe by recipe. Notes below mark anything that needs
human judgment before automated extraction touches it (paywalls, liveness,
scrapability, scoping).

Reasons fall into a few buckets: explicit hard-ban match (BBC Good Food),

and pizza entries), generic amateur/hobby blogs without a real authenticity
or professional claim, forum format (not single-page scrapable), paywalled,
or not enough usable content on the domain.
