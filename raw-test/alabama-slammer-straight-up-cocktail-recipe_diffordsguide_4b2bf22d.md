### Glass:

Serve in a [Martini glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 1⁄2 fl oz

[Ketel One Vodka](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/278/ketel-one-vodka)

3⁄4 fl oz

Southern/Louisiana-style whisky liqueur (e.g. Southern Comfort)

1 fl oz

Orange juice (freshly squeezed)

1⁄4 fl oz

[Grenadine/pomegranate syrup](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/29/cocktails/pomegranate-grenadine-syrup)

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [MARTINI GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of orange zest twist.

### How to make:

3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into chilled glass.

### Garnish:

5.  [EXPRESS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1269/cocktail-garnishes/citrus-zest-twists) orange zest twist over the cocktail and use as garnish.

## Review:

None of the ingredients come from Alabama and the drink is served too long to slam. However, it's a good, rhythmic, rhyming name, if a little naff these days.
