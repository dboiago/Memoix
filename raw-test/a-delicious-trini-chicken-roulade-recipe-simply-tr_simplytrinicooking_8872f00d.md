### TRINI CHICKEN ROULADE

**_Ingredients:_**

4 boneless chicken breasts, seasoned  
3 oz parmesan cheese, grated  
1 tbsp. low fat milk  
1 small egg  
1 1/4 cup panko bread crumbs  
1 tsp. each paprika, parsley, thyme, oregano  
2 tsp. grated garlic  
20-25 spinach leaves (amaranth)  
1/2 cup flour  
Toothpicks

_**Direction**_:

Clean the chicken.

[![IMG 0818](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0818")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0818.jpg)

Place the chicken breast on a cutting board.

[![IMG 0820](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0820")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0820.jpg)

Cover with plastic wrap.

[![IMG 0822](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0822")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0822.jpg)

[![IMG 0823](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0823")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0823.jpg)

Pound the chicken breast flat…

[![IMG 0826](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0826")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0826.jpg)

…Then season the chicken.  (Note: the chicken is seasoned with the Trini green seasoning).

[![IMG 0829](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0829")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0829.jpg)

Layer each chicken breast with a 1/4 cup of cheese…

[![IMG 0832](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0832")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0832.jpg)

… and the spinach leaves (about 6 or 7 leaves each).

[![IMG 0833](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0833")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0833.jpg)

Fold and tightly roll.  Secure the ends with toothpicks.

Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for about 1 hour.

[![IMG 0824](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0824")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0824.jpg)

Blend together the paprika, parsley, garlic, thyme, and oregano with the bread crumbs.

[![IMG 0828](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0828")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0828.jpg)

Combine the milk and the egg.

[![IMG 0836](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0836")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0836.jpg)

[![IMG 0837](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0837")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0837.jpg)

Dust the the chicken with flour.

[![IMG 0838](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0838")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0838.jpg)

Dip into the milk and…

[![IMG 0839](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0839")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0839.jpg)

[![IMG 0840](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0840")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0840.jpg)

… dredge in the bread crumbs.

Preheat the oven for 10 minutes at about 180 degrees Celsius ( 356 degrees F ).

[![IMG 0849](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyB4bWxucz0iaHR0cDovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHdpZHRoPSIzOTkiIGhlaWdodD0iMzAwIiB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzk5IDMwMCI+PHJlY3Qgd2lkdGg9IjEwMCUiIGhlaWdodD0iMTAwJSIgZmlsbD0iI2NmZDRkYiIvPjwvc3ZnPg== "IMG_0849")](https://simplytrinicooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/IMG_0849.jpg)

Place the chicken roulades on a greased baking tray and bake for about 20 minutes.

Allow to cool.  Remove the toothpicks and cut into 1″ thick rolls.

### Serving Chicken Roulade

Place cutlets on a serving plate and serve.

**Note:** The cutlets may be served with any of your favorite sauce to add extra flavour.
