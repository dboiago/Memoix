## Trim the cauliflower, split it into florets, then slice them. Trim and slice the celery and celeriac as well. Crush the bread sticks and mix them with...

### List of Ingredients

-   14 OZ. of perch fillets, already cleaned
-   6 OZ. of white cauliflower
-   celeriac
-   3 OZ. of celery
-   parsley
-   dried elderflowers
-   6 of salted bread sticks
-   4 of eggs
-   2 of anchovies in oil
-   Italian 00 flour
-   Rice flour
-   Peanut oil
-   Extravirgin olive oil
-   Salt

### Method

1

Trim the cauliflower, split it into florets, then slice them. Trim and slice the celery and celeriac as well. Crush the bread sticks and mix them with the elderflowers.

2

Flour the perch fillets in the Italian 00 flour, dip them in 2 beaten eggs, then pass them in the crushed bread sticks. Beat 2 eggs, pass the vegetables in them, then in the rice flour, then fry them in peanut oil heated to 325°F for 4-5 minutes, browning them on both sides. Drain them on paper towels.

3

After having removed the vegetables, cook the perch fillets in the same oil: fry them for 2 minutes on each side and gradually drain them on paper towels. Add salt and serve the fish and vegetables with the chopped parsley, anchovies and extra-virgin olive oil.
