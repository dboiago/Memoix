## Coriander Chicken

[![coriandar cilantro spicy baked chicken recipe ](https://www.9jafoodie.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/thumb_DSC07191_1024-600x398.jpg)](https://www.9jafoodie.com/coriander-chicken/thumb_dsc07191_1024/)I think every year I fall in love with a herb. In 2014 it was rosemary, 2015 was the year of coriander (cilantro), 2016 is looking like the year of the dill but I shall wait to see as we are still very early in the year.  This chicken personifies everything about the 9jafoodie brand, it’s fresh, stupidly simple and overly delicious. The recipe was in part inspired by the [9jafoodie rice](https://www.9jafoodie.com/the-9jafoodie-rice/) and in part inspired by my famous [spicy baked chicken](https://www.9jafoodie.com/baked-spicy-chicken/). Yogurt is my secret ingredient for keeping chicken moist and juicy, it honestly changes everything.

I made this quickly for supper a little while ago, I didn’t have time to marinate it overnight. I think the flavors will be so much more pronounced if it’s marinated for 8 hours or more.

## Ingredients

-   6 attached chicken legs (or 12 chicken pieces)
-   1 cup (1/2 bunch) coriander (cilantro)
-   1 medium onion (chopped)
-   1-2 scotch bonnet peppers (ata-rodo)
-   1 green bell pepper
-   2 maggi cubes
-   Salt – to taste
-   1 teaspoon chipotle pepper or smoked paprika
-   3 tablespoons plain yogurt
