#### Tartare

-   200g of [beef fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-fillet-recipes), high quality, finely chopped by hand
-   1 1/2 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   2 tsp [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), chopped
-   2 tsp cornichons, chopped
-   1 [small shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [fresh parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
-   1/4 tsp Tabasco
-   2 [Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker® egg yolks](https://freshlayeggs.co.uk/)

#### Frites

-   500g of [russet potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), cut into thin frites
-   750ml of vegetable oil, for frying
-   1/2 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   40g of [Parmesan](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parmesan-recipes), finely grated
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), grated or minced
-   1 tbsp of [fresh parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), finely chopped

1

Prepare the frites: Soak the sliced potatoes in 1 litre cold water for 1 hour, then drain them and dry thoroughly

-   500g of [russet potato](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/potato-recipes), cut into thin frites

2

Heat oil to 150°C and fry in batches for 5–6 minutes until cooked but not browned. Remove and drain on paper towels

-   750ml of vegetable oil, for frying

3

Increase the oil temperature to 190°C and fry again until golden and crispy, about 2–3 minutes. Sprinkle with salt

-   1/2 tsp [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

5

While the frites are still hot, toss them in the mixture until evenly coated

6

Meanwhile, make the steak tartar. In a bowl, mix beef, mustard, capers, cornichons, shallot, parsley, Worcestershire sauce and Tabasco. Season with salt and pepper to taste

-   200g of [beef fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-fillet-recipes), high quality, finely chopped by hand
-   1 1/2 tsp [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   2 tsp [capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), chopped
-   2 tsp cornichons, chopped
-   1 [small shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely chopped
-   1 tbsp of [fresh parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
-   1/4 tsp Tabasco

7

Shape into two portions and place on plates
