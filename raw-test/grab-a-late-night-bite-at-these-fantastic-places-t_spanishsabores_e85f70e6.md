Most visitors quickly figure out that Spanish mealtimes are much later than they're used to. Locals don't sit down to lunch until around 2 p.m., and dinner isn't on the table until 9 at the earliest! But what if you want or need to eat even later than the _granadinos_ do? Whether you're arriving to town quite late or simply want to grab a bite to eat while you're [out on the town](https://spanishsabores.com/nightlife-in-granada/), there are plenty of place to eat late in Granada. Here's where you can grab a bite after dark.

![Patatas bravas are one of the most popular veggie-friendly tapas featured in our vegetarian guide to Malaga!](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/gf-tapas-chorizo-patatas-bravas-tomato-pimientos-de-padron.jpg)

Tapas make fabulous late-night bites!

### 1\. Bar Poe

Although it's open until 2 a.m., [Bar Poe](http://www.barpoe.com/) is more than just one of the best places to eat late in Granada—it's one of the best places to eat in Granada, period! This favorite local haunt is often packed to the rafters, and for good reason. Owned by a British-Angolan husband and wife team, Bar Poe serves up some of the most innovative tapas in the city, all with an international flair. The best part? They come FREE with your drink—and unlike many other bars in the city that follow this practice, you get to choose which of their many creative tapas you want!

_**Address**: Calle Verónica de la Magdalena, 40_

### 2\. Wursmet

Upon walking into [Wursmet](http://wursmet.com/), you'll feel as if you've been whisked away to Berlin without leaving Granada. Their unique concept of German street food is amazingly simple and completely one-of-a-kind in the city. Chow down on grilled-to-perfection sausages accompanied by tasty sauces and homemade bread, and wash it all down with an ice cold German beer. This is one of the best places to eat late in Granada if you're looking for something casual yet unique.

_**Address**_: _Calle Martínez de la Rosa, 23_

![Looking for a unique place to eat late in Granada? Check out the German street food at Wursmet!](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/4786078589_a75bc5d0d1_o.jpg)

Wursmet might just be home to the best currywurst outside Germany! Photo credit: [Kate Hopkins](https://bit.ly/2Exyzpz)

### 3\. La Tana

In addition to being one of the city's top [wine bars](https://spanishsabores.com/wine-bars-in-granada/), [La Tana](http://www.labotilleriagranada.es/tana/) also happens to be a great place to eat late in Granada! The inviting, rustic decor makes for the perfect place to enjoy a glass of Spanish wine from their incredible selection—and that's not to mention the fabulous food! They offer free tapas with each drink that pair wonderfully with the wines on offer. Be sure to ask about the wine of the month for a unique recommendation handpicked by the owners.

_**Address**_: _Placeta del Agua, 3_

![You'll love the incredible tapas and outstanding wine selection at La Tana, one of the best places to eat late in Granada!](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/carrillada-wine-.jpg)

Tasty tapas and incredible wine? Count us in!

### 4\. Bar Ávila

Walking into [Bar Ávila](https://www.facebook.com/avilabaryrestaurante/) is like walking into your favorite hometown bar with a Spanish flair. You'll immediately feel welcomed, both by the friendly local crowd and the hospitable staff. This simple, no-frills bar is the perfect place to eat late in Granada if you're looking for an unpretentious meal among locals. Their _jamón asado_ is among the best in the city, but they also make a deliciously refreshing _salmorejo_ and—for the adventurous—_caracoles!_ 

_**Address**_: _Calle Verónica de la Virgen, 16_

![Step out of your comfort zone as you eat late in Granada and try the caracoles at Bar Ávila!](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/Caracoles-3.jpg)

Try some _caracoles_ at Bar Ávila. You may be pleasantly surprised!

### 5\. El Conde

Nestled in the heart of the charming Realejo neighborhood, [El Conde](https://www.facebook.com/elcondegranada/) serves some of the most innovative tapas in Granada while remaining true to the roots of Spanish cooking. They offer an incredible variety of [vegetarian](https://devourtours.com/blog/vegetarian-food-in-granada/?tap_a=107727-beecd5&tap_s=3690317-6e3e55%22%20target=%22_BLANK%22%20rel=%22nofollow%22) dishes, and their lovely wine selection will delight _vino_ connoisseurs and newbies alike. All of this is set in an elegant, minimalist-inspired contemporary space that you won't want to leave. Join the locals and order another round!

_**Address**_: _Calle Rosario, 12_
