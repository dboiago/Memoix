Make sure you use the best beef you can find for this [steak](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/steak-recipes) tartare recipe from [Tom Aikens](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/chefs/tom-aikens). Brandy and Tabasco are added to give this classic [summery](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/british-summer-recipes) dish a real punch.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Steak tartare

-   550g of [beef fillet](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/beef-fillet-recipes), diced
-   25g of [banana shallot](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/shallot-recipes), finely chopped
-   40g of cornichons, chopped
-   40g of [baby capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), rinsed
-   1/2 [red chilli](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chilli-recipes), finely chopped
-   80ml of [tomato ketchup](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   20g of [Dijon mustard](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mustard-recipes)
-   90g of [mayonnaise](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/mayonnaise-recipes)
-   5 drops of Tabasco
-   14ml of [brandy](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/brandy-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [chives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/chive-recipes), chopped
-   1 pinch of [parsley](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/parsley-recipes), chopped
-   1 tsp [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes), plus extra for seasoning
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes), plus extra for seasoning

-   Round pastry cutter

2

Shape the mix into 4 patties using a round cutter (there will be sauce leftover in the bowl, do not discard as it is needed for step 4)

4

Plate up the steak tartare patties and drizzle with a little of the sauce left in the bowl. Top with a raw egg yolk and accompany with a slice of sourdough bread and rocket. Season and serve immediately

-   1 [egg yolk](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/egg-recipes)
-   1 tsp [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 pinch of [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   100g of [rocket](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/green-salad-recipes)
