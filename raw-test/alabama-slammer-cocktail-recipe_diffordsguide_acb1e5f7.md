### Glass:

Serve in an [Old-fashioned glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 1⁄2 fl oz

Southern/Louisiana-style whisky liqueur (e.g. Southern Comfort)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Adriatico Amaretto Liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/6681/adriatico-amaretto-liqueur)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Hayman's Sloe Gin](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/1478/haymans-sloe-gin)

2 fl oz

Orange juice (freshly squeezed)

3⁄4 fl oz

Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)

## How to make:

1.  Select and pre-chill an [OLD-FASHIONED GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of peach slice.
3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-strain-a-cocktail) into ice-filled glass.

## Review:

Rich in flavour and quite sweet with a citrus bite. Surprisingly peachy!
