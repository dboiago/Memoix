## Jamie Boudreau | Seattle

An early twentieth-century session recipe, the Chrysanthemum cocktail appears, with small variations in proportions, in influential texts like Hugo R. Ensslin’s _Recipes for Mixed Drinks_ (1916) and Harry Craddock’s _Savoy Cocktail Book_ (1930). Canon owner Jamie Boudreau’s update downgrades the absinthe from dashes to a rinse and incorporates citrus. “The Chrysanthemum is an old recipe, but I always found it unbalanced,” he says. “Adding a touch of lemon, but still stirring, elevates a recipe that tends to be sweet and flat.”

##### Ingredients

Serving: 1

-   absinthe
-   2 1/2 ounces dry vermouth, preferably Dolin dry
-   1/2 ounce Bénédictine
-   1/4 ounce lemon juice

##### Directions

1.  Rinse a coupe with a small amount of absinthe and dump.
2.  Combine the vermouth, Bénédictine, and lemon juice in a mixing glass, add ice, and stir until chilled.
3.  Strain into the absinthe-rinsed coupe.
