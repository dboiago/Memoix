### Glass:

Serve in a [Collins glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

8 fresh

Green grapes (seedless)

2 fl oz

[Ketel One Vodka](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/278/ketel-one-vodka)

5 fresh

Basil leaves

1⁄2 fl oz

Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)

1⁄2 fl oz

Vanilla sugar syrup

Top up with

[Thomas Henry Soda Water](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/6575/thomas-henry-soda-water)

Garnish: Basil leaf

## How to make:

MUDDLE grapes in base of shake. Add next four ingredients, SHAKE with ice and fine strain into ice filled glass. TOP with soda water.

## Review:

White grapes, vanilla, basil and lemon juice fortified with vodka.
