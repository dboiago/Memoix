## Ingredients for KFC Coleslaw

### For the Slaw:

-   **Green cabbage:** Finely chopping it creates the authentic restaurant texture. A food processor with an S-blade makes it easy.
-   **Carrot:** Adds a little sweetness and color. Again, finely chopped is key here to keep it from being too crunchy.
-   **Onion:** Adds a subtle savory bite that balances the sweet, tangy dressing.

### For the Dressing:

-   **Vegan mayonnaise:** Store-bought or [homemade](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/easy-vegan-mayo/), both work great.
-   **Sugar or maple syrup:** KFC coleslaw is known for being a bit sweet; while sugar is the authentic choice, maple syrup works for a more unprocessed option.
-   **Apple cider vinegar and lemon juice:** Adds the signature tangy flavor and brightness.
-   **Yellow or Dijon mustard:** For some complexity.
-   **Salt and black pepper:** Enhance all the flavors in the dressing.

**The secret to authentic KFC coleslaw isn't a long list of ingredients, it's the texture.** Finely chopping the cabbage, carrot, and onion creates that signature creamy, scoopable slaw that makes this copycat recipe taste so close to the original.

## How to Make KFC Coleslaw

## Tips and Variations

-   **Don't skip the chilling time:** Freshly made coleslaw is good, but after a couple of hours in the fridge it becomes _excellent_.
-   **Use a food processor:** I like to roughly chop the vegetables first, then pulse them a few times using the S-blade. Just be careful not to over-process or you'll end up with mush!
-   **Make it oil-free:** Swap the vegan mayonnaise for plain unsweetened plant-based yogurt or blended silken tofu. It will be a little less creamy, but the taste will still be great!
-   **Taste after chilling:** The flavors mellow as the slaw rests, so if you'd like a little more tang or sweetness, adjust it just before serving.

## Storage

Store leftover KFC coleslaw in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Give it a quick stir before serving, as some of the dressing may settle at the bottom.

#### For the slaw:

-   ▢ 6 cups green cabbage, very finely chopped
-   ▢ ½ cup carrot, peeled and finely chopped (about 1 large carrot)
-   ▢ 3 tablespoons white or yellow onion, very finely chopped

#### For the dressing:

-   ▢ ½ cup [vegan mayonnaise](https://amzn.to/42vXV4l)
-   ▢ 3 tablespoons [white sugar](https://amzn.to/3MwnLOc), (or maple syrup)
-   ▢ 2 tablespoons [apple cider vinegar](https://amzn.to/4jtoG01)
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon [lemon juice](https://amzn.to/3Sr4L7x)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon yellow or Dijon mustard
-   ▢ ½ teaspoon [salt](https://amzn.to/4jq9UHf)
-   ▢ ¼ teaspoon [black pepper](https://amzn.to/3Mt9F04)

-   **Prep the vegetables:** Finely chop the cabbage, carrot, and onion until very small and uniform. This is key for that classic KFC-style texture.
    
    **Tip:** A food processor makes this quick and easy. Roughly chop the vegetables, then pulse using the S-blade until finely chopped (be careful not to over-process).
    
    ![Chopped carrots, onion, and cabbage in glass bowl.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/square-KFC-Coleslaw-Copycat-02-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Make the dressing:** In a medium bowl, whisk together the vegan mayonnaise, sugar, apple cider vinegar, lemon juice, mustard, salt, and pepper until smooth.
    
    ![Whisking KFC coleslaw dressing in bowl.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/square-KFC-Coleslaw-Copycat-04-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Combine:** Add the cabbage, carrots, and onion to a large bowl. Pour the dressing over top and mix well until everything is evenly coated.
    
    ![Using two wooden spoons to toss coleslaw in glass bowl.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/square-KFC-Coleslaw-Copycat-06-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Chill (important!):** Cover and refrigerate for at least 1-2 hours before serving to allow the flavors to meld. It gets even yummier after resting.
    
-   **Serve:** Stir well before serving and enjoy!
    

**Storage:** Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

**Oil-free option:** replace the mayonnaise with plain unsweetened plant-based yogurt of blended silken tofu. It won't be quite as creamy but will still be delicious.
