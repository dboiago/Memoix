Spice 94

Move over Starbucks, there's a tastier spiced pumpkin drink in town! This is a great way to use up that leftover filling from your carved pumpkin. The sweet pumpkin flesh is warmed by the addition of fresh ginger. A little spice, a little fizz and a twist of citrus to enjoy on a crisp autumnal day.

## Ingredients

-   Seedlip Spice 94: 2 oz / 60 ml
    
-   Ginger and Pumpkin Shrub\*: 1 oz / 30 ml
    
-   Soda water: Top
    

## Glass & Garnish

-   Highball
    
-   Lemon twist
    

## Method

1.  Fill highball glass with ice
    
2.  Pour Seedlip Spice 94 and Shrub over the ice
    
3.  Top with soda water
    
4.  Gently stir and garnish
    

### \*Ginger and Pumpkin Shrub

-   Fresh ginger: 150 g (peeled)
    
-   Pumpkin flesh: 400 g
    
-   Cider vinegar: 2 cups
    
-   Caster sugar: 2 cups
    

Add all ingredients to mason jar and muddle. Leave for 24 hours in the fridge. Fine strain before serving.
