## Patatas Bravas

Portions: 10

## Ingredients

-   Ingredients
-   1kg waxy potatoes, such as Charlotte, cubed
-   3 sprigs rosemary
-   1tbsp olive oil
-   1600ml MAGGI Rich & Rustic Tomato Sauce
-   1tsp smoked paprika

## Method

Method  
Place the potatoes and rosemary in an oven tray and coat with the olive oil.  
Roast for 40 minutes in an oven preheated to 200˚C, turning halfway through the cooking time.  
While the potatoes are in the oven, warm the Rich & Rustic tomato sauce in a saucepan and stir through the smoked paprika.  
Serve the roast potatoes and rosemary in small sharing bowls topped with the spiced tomato sauce.
