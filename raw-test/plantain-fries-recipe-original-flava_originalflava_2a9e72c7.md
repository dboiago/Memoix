## Plantain Fries recipe

**Ingredients**

-   Plantain
-   2tbsp plain flour
-   2tbsp cornflour
-   half cup coconut milk
-   half cup water
-   2 cups vegetable oil

**Directions**

1.  Cut plantain in to chip shapes
2.   then put chips into bowl with these ingredients:  
    2tbsp plain flour  
    2tbsp cornflour  
    half cup coconut milk  
    half cup water
3.  Then mix together
4.  2 cups Vegetable oil in frying pan
5.  Carefully put chips one by one into heated pan.
6.  Fry until golden brown on each side
7.  Then put onto kitchen towel to dry.

## Enjoy!
