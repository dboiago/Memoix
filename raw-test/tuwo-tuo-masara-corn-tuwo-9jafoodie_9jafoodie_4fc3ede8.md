## Tuwo (Tuo) Masara (Corn Tuwo)

#### ![Tuwo Masara ](https://www.9jafoodie.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/DSC01119-540x358.jpg)Tuwo Masara with Miyan wake (Gbegiri) and sauced oxtail.

**Ingredients**

-   2 cups corn flour
-   3 cups water

**Direction**

Combine the Corn flour and water in a medium pot, place on medium heat and stir continuously until the mixture is thick and stretchy. Add ¼ cup of water, reduce the heat to minimum and leave the pot covered for 3mins. Stir until smooth.

Your tuwo is ready to serve.
