Grove 42

A bright and bittersweet non-alcoholic take on the classic spritz, centered around Seedlip Grove 42 - a sophisticated, bright, citrus blend of orange, lemon, lemongrass and ginger with a dry finish topped with tonic or soda water. This spritz offers a beautifully layered, refreshing experience.

## Ingredients

-   Seedlip Grove 42: 2 oz
    
-   Tonic water or club soda
    
-   Cubed ice
    

## Glass & Garnish

-   Wine Glass
    
-   Orange Slice
    

## Method

1.  Pour 2 oz. of Seedlip Grove 42 over ice in a wine glass
    
2.  Top with your favourite premium mixer
    
3.  Garnish with style
