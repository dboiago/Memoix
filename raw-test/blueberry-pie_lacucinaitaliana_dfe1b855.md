## Dissolve the yeast in warm milk and mix it with 2 cups of Italian 0 flour, 1/2 of the melted butter, 3 tablespoons of sugar, a pinch of salt and the g...

**Cooks in:** _3h 30mins \+ 2h 30mins_

**Levels:** _Medium_

**Serves:** _8_

### List of Ingredients

-   blueberries
-   Italian 0 flour
-   milk
-   butter
-   sugar
-   almond flour
-   1 TBSP. of fresh brewer's yeast
-   1 lemon
-   Brown sugar
-   Salt

### Method

1

Dissolve the yeast in warm milk and mix it with 2 cups of Italian 0 flour, 1/2 of the melted butter, 3 tablespoons of sugar, a pinch of salt and the grated rind of a lemon. You will obtain a soft dough; roll it into a ball and let it rise for 1 and a half hours, covered with a cloth. Then roll out the dough and use it to line a buttered cake pan (ø 10 in.) and trim the edges.

2

Fill the pie with blueberries and sprinkle the top with 2 tablespoons of brown sugar. Then knead the remaining flour with the almond flour, sugar and remaining butter, along with a pinch of salt. Crumble the mixture over the blueberries and bake the pie at 350 °F for 35 minutes. Let cool (it will take at least an hour) and serve.
