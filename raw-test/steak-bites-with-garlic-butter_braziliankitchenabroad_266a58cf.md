## Why I Love These Garlic Butter Steak Bites

_Oi, Gente!_

These steak bites are little fun flavor bombs.

And, yes — everyone on the internet has a version of this. Ina Garten adds red pepper flakes, NYT Cooking throws in soy sauce, others splash in Worcestershire, and some bloggers play around with all kinds of herbs.

And honestly? Those are all great ideas. This dish is super customizable and that is one of the reasons why it’s so fun.

The main idea is getting golden-seared steak bites that are juicy on the inside and dripping in buttery garlic goodness. After that, go wild with the add-ons if you want.

Still, you only need a handful of ingredients and less than 30 minutes to whip these up — no fuss, just flavor.

## How to Make Garlic Butter Steak Bites

**Prep the steaks.** Dice your steaks into pieces that are roughly the same size so they cook evenly. Ideally, these should be 1-inch/3cm pieces.

Season the steak cubes with a generous sprinkle of salt and pepper. Let them sit at room temperature for a few minutes, anywhere between 15-30 minutes. This rest helps them cook evenly and sear better.

**Sear the steak.** If your steaks bites “sweat” after sitting in salt and pepper, pat dry any excess moisture with a paper towel.

Heat a large skillet over medium heat, drizzle in the oil and sear the steak pieces in batches. Be sure that the steak is in a single layer and don’t crowd the pan! We want these to get a nice sear and to cook until they’re golden brown. It should take about 2 minutes per side.

Once cooked to perfection, remove the steak bites from the skillet, and set aside to rest on a clean plate. Repeat the process until all the steak has cooked.

[![collage of three images showing how to make and sear steak bites in a cast iron skillet on the stovetop](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Steak-Bites-with-Garlic-Butter-Steps-copy-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Steak-Bites-with-Garlic-Butter-Steps-copy.png)

**Make the garlic butter.** Once the steak has finished cooking, use the same pan to make the garlic butter.

Just lower the heat, and add in the butter to melt. Once the butter is melted, add in the minced garlic. Cook the garlic just until fragrant and slightly golden — don’t let it brown.

### Aline’s hot tips:

### switch things up!

If you’re feeling like switching things up a bit, you can add a little fresh thyme or rosemary to the melted butter along with the garlic! This is a simple way to make this dish even more delicious.

Other variations from around the web include adding red pepper flakes, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, and other fresh herbs that you love!

**Toss and serve.** Return the steak bites (and any juices!) to the skillet and toss to coat in the garlic butter sauce.

Finish with chopped parsley and serve right away.

[![cooked steak bites with garlic butter in a skillet](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Steak-Bites-With-Garlic-Butter-13-1-scaled.jpg)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Steak-Bites-With-Garlic-Butter-13-1-scaled.jpg)

### How to Serve It

I love to serve this steak bite recipe with [garlic mashed potatoes](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/instant-pot-garlic-mashed-potatoes/) for total garlic overload. It’s amazing! And if you want a similarly creamy side with more Brazilian personality, try it with [mashed yuca](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/yuca-puree/), instead. It’s so filling.

My favorite way to serve this, though, is with [Brazilian broccoli and rice](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/brazilian-broccoli-and-rice-arroz-de-brocolis/), and some [batata palha potato sticks](https://amzn.to/4jxSTuC) on top. It gives major Rio food vibes.

Or make it a party appetizer — just stick a toothpick into each individual steak bite and watch them disappear. Don’t forget to serve them with some crusty bread to soak up that garlic butter!

[![a fork spears a steak bite from a bowl of steak bites](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Steak-Bites-With-Garlic-Butter-28-scaled.jpg)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Steak-Bites-With-Garlic-Butter-28-scaled.jpg)

### How to Store It

Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 days. Reheat in a skillet with a little melted butter. (Avoid microwaving them because they can become tough and chewy.)

You can also freeze for up to 3 months — thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat gently.
