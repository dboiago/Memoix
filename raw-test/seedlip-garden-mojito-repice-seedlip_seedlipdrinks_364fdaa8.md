Garden 108

Mojito Cocktail | Non Alcoholic Cocktails | Seedlip

## Ingredients

-   Seedlip Garden 108: 2 oz
    
-   Simple Syrup: 1/2 oz
    
-   Lime juice: 3/4 oz
    
-   Club soda: Top
    
-   7-10 Mint leaves
    

## Glass & Garnish

-   Highball glass
    
-   Fresh mint sprigs
    

## Method

1.  Add all ingredients minus club soda in a cocktail shaker and fill with ice
    
2.  Shake & strain over fresh cubes of ice in a tall glass
    
3.  Top with soda and garnish with fresh mint sprigs
