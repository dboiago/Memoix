## [Lemon and Passion Fruit Bundt](https://www.theboywhobakes.co.uk/blog/2015/11/27/lemon-and-passion)

[Edd Kimber](https://www.theboywhobakes.co.uk/blog?author=549367d8e4b0ecaa40479a5b) [November 27, 2015](https://www.theboywhobakes.co.uk/blog/2015/11/27/lemon-and-passion)

I like big bundts and I cannot lie! Yes I may have just missed National Bundt Day but a boy can still celebrate. There is just something about a bundt cake that I love and this Lemon and Passionfruit version is about as simple as it gets. With the cold of winter slowly settling in this bright and zingy drizzle cake is the perfect pick me up, a great alternative to all the rich festive baking that is starting to take hold. For the full recipe visit the [Stork website](http://bakewithstork.co/lemondrizzle)
