This americano cocktail recipe is an Italian classic, with a recipe that dates back to the 1860s. A precursor to the [negroni](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/recipes/negroni-recipe)[](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/recipes/negroni-recipe), the americano offers a more relaxed, less intense approach to [aperitivo](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/features/best-italian-cocktails-aperitivo).

First published in 2017

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Americano

-   60ml of Campari
-   60ml of sweet vermouth
-   1 dash of soda water
-   ice
-   1 slice of [orange](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/orange-recipes)

1

Fill a heavy-bottomed highball glass with ice

2

Add the Campari and vermouth then top up with a splash of soda water

3

Garnish with a slice of fresh orange and serve
