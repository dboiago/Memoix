## **Dog Birthday Cake Ingredients**

### For the Cake

-   **Oat flour**: For a cake that's soft and easy to digest. You can make your own by blending rolled oats into a fine flour.
-   **Baking powder**: Helps the cake rise.
-   **Ripe banana**: For natural sweetness and moisture.
-   **Natural peanut butter**: Be sure the only ingredient is peanuts. 
-   **Water or unsweetened plant-based milk**: To bring the batter together. If you use a plant-based milk, the best option is a simple unflavored/unsweetened one that doesn't have a long list of ingredients.

### For the Frosting

-   **Natural peanut butter**: Adds rich flavor and makes the frosting irresistible for dogs.
-   **Unsweetened plant-based yogurt**: For a smooth, spreadable frosting. Again, make sure the ingredient list is short, simple, and free of artificial sweeteners.
-   **Optional decorations**: Blueberries, strawberries, raspberries, banana slices, grated carrot, dog treats, kibble.

## Tips and Variations

-   **Use natural peanut butter**: Always check the ingredient list and avoid added oils, sugars, and artificial sweeteners, especially xylitol.
-   **Keep portions reasonable**: This cake is a treat, not a meal, so serve small portions, depending on your dog's size.
-   **Swap out the yogurt**: You can spread peanut butter on the cake as frosting or swap the yogurt for additional mashed banana.
-   **Messy is cute:** Don't worry if this isn't a picture-perfect bakery cake. Dogs don't care if your frosting is perfectly smooth, they just care that it's delicious. 😄

![](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/square-Dog-Birthday-Cake-Recipe-23.jpg)

**Wondering if this cake is safe for dogs?** Yep! Every ingredient in this recipe is generally considered dog-friendly, but like any treat, it should be served in moderation. Always double-check that your peanut butter doesn't contain xylitol (sometimes listed as birch sugar), which is toxic to dogs.

## Storage

-   **Refrigerator**: Store leftover cake in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
-   **Freezer**: Freeze frosted or unfrosted cake for up to 2 months. Thaw in the refrigerator before serving.

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 35 minutes

Total: 45 minutes

Servings: 1 Makes 1 small cake (servings depend on how big or small your dog is)

#### For the cake:

-   ▢ 1 cup [oat flour](https://amzn.to/3S8jDGF)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [baking powder](https://amzn.to/3SwU5Eu)
-   ▢ ¾ cup mashed ripe banana, (about 2 medium bananas)
-   ▢ ¼ cup [natural peanut butter](https://amzn.to/43igKbj), (see note)
-   ▢ 4 - 5 tablespoons water or unsweetened plant-based milk

#### For the frosting:

-   ▢ ¼ cup [natural peanut butter](https://amzn.to/43igKbj), (see note)
-   ▢ ¼ cup unsweetened plant-based yogurt, (such as coconut, soy, or oat)
-   ▢ **For decorating:** blueberries, strawberries, raspberries, banana slices, grated carrot, dog treats, or kibble

-   **Preheat the oven:** Preheat oven to 325°F (160°C). Lightly grease two 4-inch round pans, or a small 6-8 inch pan, or 4 - 6 muffin cups.
    
-   **Make the batter:** In a medium bowl, whisk together the oat flour and baking powder. Add the mashed banana, peanut butter, and 4 tablespoons of water or plant milk. Mix into a very thick, sticky batter. If needed, add 1 more tablespoon of liquid to help it come together, but not more than that.
    
    ![Bowl of dog birthday cake batter.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/square-Dog-Birthday-Cake-Recipe-04-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Transfer to pan:** Spread the batter evenly into your prepared pan(s). The batter will be thick and sticky, so you can optionally wet your spatula or fingers to help smooth the top.
    
    ![Unbaked dog birthday cake layers in springform pans.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/square-Dog-Birthday-Cake-Recipe-05-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Bake:** Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden and a toothpick inserted comes out clean. (You may need to bake the cake a little less or a little longer depending on the size of your cake pans, so just keep an eye on them). Let the cake cool fully before frosting.
    
    ![Two dog birthday cake layers in springform pans.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/square-Dog-Birthday-Cake-Recipe-06-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Make the frosting:** In a small bowl, mix the peanut butter and yogurt until smooth. Add a water or plant milk one teaspoon at a time if needed to thin.
    
    ![Peanut butter frosting in bowl.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/square-Dog-Birthday-Cake-Recipe-08-360x360.jpg)
    
-   **Assemble & Decorate:** If making layers, spread frosting between layers and over the top. Add berries, banana slices, carrot, or dog treats if desired.
    
    ![This easy dog birthday cake recipe is soft, simple and made with wholesome, dog-friendly ingredients like banana, oats and peanut butter!](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/square-Dog-Birthday-Cake-Recipe-09-360x360.jpg)
    

**Note on Peanut Butter:** Ensure your peanut butter is natural (the runny kind). The only ingredient should be peanuts. Avoid added oils, sugars, and especially xylitol, as it is toxic to dogs.

**Serving Notes**: Slice the cake and serve in small portions depending on your dog’s size. This is a treat, not a full meal.

**Yogurt substitute:** The yogurt helps create a smooth, spreadable frosting, but you can swap it for a bit more mashed banana or skip it entirely and simply spread peanut butter on top.

**Storage:** Store in the fridge for up to 3 days. Can be frozen with or without frosting for up to 2 months.
