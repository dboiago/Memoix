## How to make Caramel Apple Butter

[![collage of two images showing chopped apples in a pot cooking down with spices](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Caramel-Apple-Butter-Step-1-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Caramel-Apple-Butter-Step-1.png)

**Core and dice the apples**, then place them on a pan with a cinnamon stick, the apple juice, the cachaça.

Bring to a quick boil, then reduce the heat and **let it simmer covered for 20-30 minutes**, until the apples are soft and you can squish the pieces with a fork.

Remove the cinnamon stick and discard.

[![collage of two images showing cooked down apples in a pot and the apples in a blender before blending](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Caramel-Apple-Butter-Step-2-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Caramel-Apple-Butter-Step-2.png)

**Add the cooked apples and the ground spices to a blender**. Puree for about a minute until smooth.

Be careful when you do this because the apples are very hot and the steam can cause the apples to splash on you.

**PRO TIP:** I like holding a kitchen towel over it and open the blender gently in case it splashes the towel will help.

Uncover the blender and **let the apples sit and cool** while you make the caramel.

[![collage showing how to pour sugar into a pan for caramel and the final caramel drizzling into the blender from above](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Caramel-Apple-Butter-Step-3-1024x683.png)](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Caramel-Apple-Butter-Step-3.png)

**Make the caramel**. Add the sugar and water to a pan over medium heat and cook until light gold… then turn off the heat.

**Add the butter, salt and cream**, and stir vigorously to combine. _The caramel will bubble vigorously while this happens, so be sure to watch yourself because this is extremely hot._

**Pour the caramel into the blender and blend again** for another minute.

Optionally, you can **add a splash of cachaça for a more flavor** and blend again.

**Portion into clean glass jars and let cool completely** before covering and refrigerating.

Let it refrigerate overnight before serving the first time so flavors can set properly.

## How to serve:

I love to use this caramel apple butter on cheeseboards throughout fall and winter. It’s such a special treat, especially served with a good goat cheese. This is a stunning combination!

I also love to spread it on bread as a quick snack, or use it to top homemade pancakes and waffles. I love it on ice cream, too.

And I also love to use it to make cocktails with!! These flavors work excellently with spiced rum, bourbon and of course, cachaça (check out my [cachaça apple butter cocktail](https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/cachaca-apple-butter-cocktail/) recipe).

## How to store:

Caramel Apple Butter typically keeps well in the fridge for about 2 to 3 weeks if stored in an airtight container.

You can also freeze it for longer storage, where it can last up to 3 months.
