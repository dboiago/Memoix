Under 15 minutes

Introduction

### About this Recipe

[print this recipe](#print-button-row)

I love how salads transform as soon as spring arrives. Tender green pea shoots, pink radishes, sweet garden peas and mild herbs like chives, dill and mint all match beautifully together. Topped with warm lemon-fragranced ricotta, this salad will make a fantastic lunch on its own or a colourful side to grilled salmon or chicken.

**NOTES:** If pea shoots aren’t available, opt for any tender green or a mild lettuce like Boston lettuce. And frozen peas, thawed and drained, work just fine here, since fresh garden peas have such a brief season.

-   **1 cup (225 g)** ricotta cheese
-   **¼ cup (25 g)** grated Parmesan cheese
-   **1½ tsp** finely grated lemon zest
-   **1 tsp** finely chopped fresh thyme
-   **4 cups (360 g)** pea shoots
-   **3 cups (315 g)** sliced cucumbers
-   **6** radishes, thinly sliced
-   **1 cup (210 g)** fresh garden peas
-   1 recipe Classic French Vinaigrette made with white wine vinegar and 1 Tbsp (4 g) Chopped fresh dill and 2 tsp chopped fresh mint

### Classic French Vinaigrette

-   Makes about ½ cup (125 mL)
-   Prep time: Under 5 minutes
-   2 tsp Dijon mustard
-   1 Tbsp (15 mL) white wine vinegar
-   3 Tbsp (45 mL) water
-   1 small shallot, finely diced
-   ⅓ cup (80 mL) extra-virgin olive oil
-   Salt and black pepper

In a mason jar with a lid, shake the mustard, vinegar, water and shallots until combined. Add the oil and shake well until emulsified (the oil and vinegar do not separate). Season to taste, then chill in the fridge. Pull from the fridge 15 minutes before using to allow the olive oil to liquefy (it sets up when cold). Shake the jar before dressing your salad or dish.

Note: The vinaigrette will keep in a closed jar in the fridge for up to 1 week.

## Step by Step Instructions

##### Step 1

Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).

##### Step 2

In a small bowl, stir the ricotta, Parmesan, lemon zest and thyme. Spread this into a small baking dish and bake, uncovered, for about 10 minutes to warm it through.

##### Step 3

While the ricotta warms, assemble the salad. Arrange the pea shoots on a platter or on individual salad plates. Top with the cucumbers, radishes and peas. Spoon the vinaigrette overtop and season lightly with salt and pepper.

##### Step 4

Dollop the warm ricotta onto the salad and sprinkle with chives. Serve immediately.
