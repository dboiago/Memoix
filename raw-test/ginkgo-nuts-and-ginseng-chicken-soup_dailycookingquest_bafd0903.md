## Ginkgo Nuts and Ginseng Chicken Soup

Author: [Anita Jacobson](https://dailycookingquest.com/about.html)

Categories: [Soup](https://dailycookingquest.com/categories/soup.html)

Cuisines: [Chinese](https://dailycookingquest.com/cuisines/chinese.html)

Ingredients: [Chicken](https://dailycookingquest.com/ingredients/chicken.html)

Prep Time: 30 mins

Cook Time: 1 hour

Total Time: 1 hour 30 mins

Serves: 4

[Print Recipe](https://dailycookingquest.com/card/ginkgo-nuts-and-ginseng-chicken-soup.html)

### Ingredients

-   1 free range chicken (Indonesian: ayam kampung), cut into 4-8 pieces
-   30 ginkgo nuts (Chinese: bai guo)
-   2 tablespoon ginseng
-   20 dried lotus seeds (Chinese: lian zi)
-   10 dried jujube fruit (Chinese: hong zao)
-   2 teaspoon salt
-   1 liter water

### Instructions

1.  Rinse and drain all the dried herbs to remove dust and dirt.
2.  Place all ingredients in a soup pot and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 1 hour or until chicken is tender and fully cooked.
3.  Remove from heat and serve warm.
