As it’s UK’s national pancake day, we thought we’d make our thick and tasty American Pancakes with plantain & syrup glaze. This fluffy treat compliments well with the crunchy plantain texture, and is a really simple recipe that will sure to get your tastebuds bouncing! Let us know what you think?

1.  ###### 1 tablespoon baking powder
    
2.  ###### 1 pinch of salt (optional)
    
3.  ###### 1 teaspoon brown or white sugar
    
4.  ###### 2 large eggs (beaten)
    
5.  ###### 30 grams melted butter
    
6.  ###### 300 millilitres milk
    
7.  ###### Vanilla essence
    
8.  ###### 225 grams plain flour
    
9.  ###### vegetable oil or butter for frying
    

Directions

1.  Pour all ingredients in a blender (Starting with milk) and mix
2.  Make sure the mixture is thick and smooth and rest for 15 minutes
3.  Pour 1 tsp of vegetable oil on a non stick frying pan and whip around with a tissue
4.  On medium heat, once hot, pour pancake mix into the pan from the middle making a circle shape.
5.  Cook for 1 minute on each side
6.  Fry plantain in shallow vegetable oil until golden brown on each side
7.  Decorate pancake with plantain and syrup
