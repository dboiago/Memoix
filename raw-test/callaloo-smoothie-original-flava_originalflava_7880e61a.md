This smoothie is a healthy antioxidants smoothie that will cleanse your system. Had a heavy weekend? over indulging? This smoothie recipe will make you feel new again. we have added key antioxidant ingredients to make you feel alive again.

-   6 Branches Fresh callaloo / 6 Tbsp. Tinned Callaloo
-   1 banana
-   1 tsp Ginger (1 small piece)
-   Squeeze of lemon juice
-   10 Ice cubes
-   1/2 cup orange juice
-   1tsp honey /sugar
-   1/4 cup of water (if desired

Blend all ingredients together untill smooth, Drain into a cup of ice and serve.
