Elevate your at-home mixology with the Lyre’s Rosa Negroni Set. This floral twist on the classic blends bittersweet citrus, aromatic botanicals and a hint of rose for a beautifully balanced, refreshing sip.

Flavour Profile

Bittersweet citrus, aromatic herbs and a hint of rose for a delicate, elegantly balanced Negroni-style serve

###### HOW TO ENJOY

For a classic Rosa Negroni mix 45mL Italian Spritz, 15mL oz Dry London, 30mL Apéritif Rosso and 4 fresh raspberries in a glass with ice.

**Bottle size:** 23.7 Fl Oz / 700mL  
**Closure:** Screw cap  
**Storage:** Store in cool, dry conditions. Once opened, refrigerate.  
**Serving note:** Designed to be mixed. Not intended to be consumed neat.

###### TASTE PROFILE

Lyre's Dry London Spirit: Firm flavors with juniper and citrus. Earthy notes fill the mid-palate which has lovely vinous qualities. The pepperberry adds warmth and delivers a generous, dry finish..

Lyre's Italian Spritz: The bittersweet flavor of the Italian Spritz is like sunshine itself, bright with flavors typical of its origins. The sweetness from orange is perfectly fused with the complex and astringent flavors of rhubarb, finishing dry and deliciously refreshing.

Lyre's Aperitif Rosso: Rich mouthful of flavors follows the bouquet with blood orange and vanilla taking the lead. Caramel, citrus pith and cacao brings balance, adding to the mouth coating mid-palate.

### FAQ's for Lyre's Rosa Negroni Set

##### What is included in the Lyre’s Rosa Negroni Set?

The set includes Pink London, Italian Orange, and Aperitif Rosso — designed for a vibrant, bittersweet alcohol-free Rosa Negroni with floral botanical lift.

##### How is a Rosa Negroni different from a classic Negroni?

It keeps the Negroni’s signature bittersweet structure, but shifts more floral and fruit-bright, with a softer, rosier botanical profile.

##### Is this a lighter alternative to a traditional Negroni?

Yes. It delivers the Negroni ritual without alcohol, which typically makes it lower in calories than a full-strength Negroni. Sugar can be kept low (this is a stirred, spirit-forward style), and it’s easy to serve simply over ice with citrus — no syrups needed.

##### How many cocktails does this set make?

With equal-parts builds at 30–50 ml, this set typically makes around 18–22 cocktails, depending on pour size.
