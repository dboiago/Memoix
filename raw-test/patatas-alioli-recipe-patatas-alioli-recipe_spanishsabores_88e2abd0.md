## Introduction

If you're a garlic lover, this dish is what you've been looking for your entire life. There's enough garlic here to make a vampire faint, which means that sharing this particular tapas dish is a must! It's a great side dish for meat and fish, and also makes for a great cold appetizer.

## Ingredients

![ingredients for patatas alioli.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Patatas-Alioli-Ingredients.jpg)

**Curious what you need** to make patatas alioli? Like the best Spanish recipes, you only need a few basic things!

-   **Potatoes:** I like to use Yukon Gold potatoes because they hold their shape when cooked but still are fluffy inside.
-   **Garlic:** There's a lot of garlic in this recipe--which is one of the reasons that this dish is so delicious!
-   **Olive Oil:** Use good quality extra virgin olive oil to make the alioli; the flavor will be amazing.
-   **Egg Yolk:** It's not traditional to add an egg yolk to the alioli, but I like to because it makes it a lot easier to emulsify the sauce.

## How to Make Patatas Alioli

If you’d like to see the full ingredients and instructions, _scroll to the bottom of the post_ for the printable recipe card.

![boiling and peeling the potatoes.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Pan-Fried-Cod-Process-Collage-1-1.jpg)

1.  _Wash the potatoes,_ then place them in a pot and cover them with cold water. Bring to a soft boil and **cook until fork tender, about 15 minutes.** (image 1)
2.  Let the potatoes cool, then **peel them** and set aside. (image 2)

![making the alioli.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Pan-Fried-Cod-Process-Collage-2-1.jpg)

3.  _Mash the garlic_ into a paste, then **add an egg yolk** and blend lightly with an immersion blender. **Very slowly add the olive oil** so it emulsifies. (image 3)
4.  Keep blending until the mixture thickens, then **add the lemon juice** and mix until combined. (image 4)

![dicing potatoes and topping with alioli.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Pan-Fried-Cod-Process-Collage-3.jpg)

5.  Cut the cooled and peeled boiled potatoes into **1-inch cubes.** (image 5)
6.  **Drizzle the potatoes** with the garlic alioli. (image 6)

![garnishing with parsley and serving.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Pan-Fried-Cod-Process-Collage-4.jpg)

7.  _Toss the potatoes to coat_ them in the alioli and **garnish with fresh chopped parsley.** (image 7)
8.  **Enjoy these patatas alioli** as a quick, easy summer tapa! (image 8)

## Recipe FAQs

**What is alioli made of?**

Traditional Spanish alioli is made from a mixture of mashed garlic and olive oil. The oil is slowly added to the garlic and is mixed well to emulsify it. The resulting sauce is thick and delicious!

**What is the difference between aioli and alioli?**

The name _aioli_ is the more common term used by English and French speakers, but the term _alioli_ is the term used in Spain. It's a combination of the word _al_ (garlic) and _oli_ (oil).

## Expert Tips

-   I like to use Yukon Gold potatoes for my patatas alioli recipe. They're good for frying, and the outside will crisp up nicely while the inside stays fluffy and soft.
-   My [alioli recipe](https://spanishsabores.com/easy-spanish-alioli-recipe-homemade-garlic-mayonnaise/) includes egg, which isn't traditional, strictly speaking. The most basic aliolis just use garlic (_al_) and olive oil (_oli_), but I find that adding an egg makes it way easier to emulsify!

![bowl of patatas alioli.](https://spanishsabores.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Patatas-Alioli-Featured-01-300x300.jpg)

-   ▢ 4 medium-sized potatoes unpeeled
-   ▢ 3 garlic cloves minced
-   ▢ ½ teaspoon salt
-   ▢ 1 egg yolk
-   ▢ ¾ cup extra virgin olive oil
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon lemon juice
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon fresh parsley chopped

-   Wash the potatoes, then place them in a saucepan and cover with cold water. Boil on high heat, then reduce to a medium-low and simmer for 15 minutes or until fork tender. Let them cool before peeling (about15 minutes).
    
-   While the potatoes are cooling, make the alioli. Mash the minced garlic into a paste with a mortar and pestle.
    
-   Place the garlic paste and the egg yolk into the container of an immersion blender. Pulse lightly to combine.
    
-   Gradually add the olive oil to the mixture, constantly blending to incorporate it as you pour. The mixture should slowly start to thicken as the oil emulsifies. Once all the oil is combined, add the lemon juice and blend.
    
-   Once the potatoes are cooled and peeled, slice them into 1-inch cubes. Toss them gently to mix them with the alioli.
    
-   Garnish with the chopped parsley and enjoy right away!
    

-   I like to use Yukon Gold potatoes for my patatas alioli recipe. They're good for frying, and the outside will crisp up nicely while the inside stays fluffy and soft.
-   My [alioli recipe](https://spanishsabores.com/easy-spanish-alioli-recipe-homemade-garlic-mayonnaise/) includes egg, which isn't traditional, strictly speaking. The most basic aliolis just use garlic (_al_) and olive oil (_oli_), but I find that adding an egg makes it way easier to emulsify!
