### Ingredients

2 cups Surmai/ Rawas, mince

2 tsp Ginger paste

1 tsp Red chilli, chopped

1 Onion, finely chopped

½ cup Coriander leaves, chopped

1 tbsp Cajun spice powder

¼ cup Corn flour

2 tbsp Refined flour

¼ cup Bread crumbs

Salt to taste

3 tbsp Oil, for frying

### Process

-   Take all the ingredients in a bowl and mix everything well.
-   Heat oil in a pan.
-   Meanwhile make small cakes out of the prepared mixture.
-   Place the prepared cakes on the heated pan and shallow fry on both the sides till nice golden brown and crispy.
-   Remove and serve hot along with chutney.
