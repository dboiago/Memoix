### Glass:

Serve in a [Shot glass](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1⁄2 fl oz

[Adriatico Amaretto Liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/6681/adriatico-amaretto-liqueur)

1⁄2 fl oz

Southern/Louisiana-style whisky liqueur (e.g. Southern Comfort)

1⁄2 fl oz

[Hayman's Sloe Gin liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/1478/haymans-sloe-gin)

1⁄4 fl oz

Orange juice (freshly squeezed)

1⁄4 fl oz

Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)

## How to make:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [SHOT GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare garnish of split [Luxardo Maraschino Cherry](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/2048/luxardo-maraschino-cherries) on rim.
3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) all ingredients with ice.
4.  [FINE STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/fine-double-straining) into chilled glass.
