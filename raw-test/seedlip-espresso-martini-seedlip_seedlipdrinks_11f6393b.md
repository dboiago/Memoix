Spice 94

A bold, non-alcoholic take on the classic Espresso Martini - perfect for coffee lovers who want to indulge. Seedlip Spice 94 is a warm, aromatic blend of allspice and cardamom, with citrus top notes to balance the long, bitter finish. Balanced with cold-brewed coffee concentrate, the roasted notes and silky texture of the drink come through, while a dash of simple syrup adds delicate sweetness and body.

## Ingredients

-   Seedlip Spice 94: 2 oz
    
-   Cold brew coffee concentrate: 2 oz
    
-   Simple Syrup\*: ½ oz
    

## Glass & Garnish

-   Coupe
    
-   Three coffee beans
    

## Method

1.  Add Spice 94, cold brew concentrate & sugar syrup to a shaker
    
2.  Add ice & shake
    
3.  Double strain into a coupe glass
    

\*Sugar Syrup: Warm equal parts water & sugar over medium heat. Stir until dissolved. Let cool.​​​​‌ ‍ ​‍​‍‌‍ ‌ ​‍‌‍‍‌‌‍‌ ‌‍‍‌‌‍ ‍​‍​‍​ ‍‍​‍​‍‌‍​ ‌‍ ‌‍ ‍‌
