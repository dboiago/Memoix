As the bar manager at Death & Co. in Denver, [Jake Powell](https://punchdrink.com/articles/best-new-bartenders-2023/) uses his self-described “mathematical” process to doggedly shape his drink concepts until they are not only balanced but efficient in build. Testing through a recipe is an exercise in editing, and the Alabaster, with just four ingredients in equal volume, displays the power of a well-edited drink in its balance of spirited backbone, citrusy zip and full-bodied texture and flavor.

##### Ingredients

Serving: 1

-   3/4 ounce lightly aged blended rum, such as Probitas
-   3/4 ounce Chareau aloe liqueur
-   3/4 ounce Velvet Falernum
-   3/4 ounce lime juice

##### Directions

1.  Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice and shake until chilled.
2.  Fine-strain into a Nick & Nora glass.
