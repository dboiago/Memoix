The fresh crunch of raw [celeriac](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/celeriac-recipes) and [apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/ingredients/apple-recipes) come together wonderfully in this festive, wintry [salad](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salad-recipes) from Raymond Blanc. Peppered with sweet dried cranberries and crunchy walnuts, it's all brought together with a Saint Agur blue cheese dressing for a rich, savoury, creamy finish.

Recipe © Raymond Blanc.

## Ingredients

### Metric

### Imperial

#### Salad

-   400g of [celeriac](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celeriac-recipes), peeled and julienned using a sharp knife, Japanese mandoline or spiralizer
-   2 pinches of [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   1 [Granny Smith apple](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/apple-recipes), cut into a mixture of thin slices and small dice
-   100g of [dried cranberries](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/cranberry-recipes)
-   60g of [walnuts](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/walnut-recipes), lightly crushed
-   2 [spring onions](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/onion-recipes), finely sliced

#### Dressing

-   75g of [Saint Agur Blue Cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blue-cheese-recipes), room temperature
-   80g of crème fraiche
-   1 tbsp of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

#### To finish

-   75g of [Saint Agur Blue Cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blue-cheese-recipes), cut into 0.5cm dice
-   2 [baby gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes), washed, drained and finely sliced

## Method

1

Mix the celeriac with the salt in a large bowl and set aside for 10 minutes to allow the celeriac to soften slightly

-   400g of [celeriac](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/celeriac-recipes), peeled and julienned using a sharp knife, Japanese mandoline or spiralizer
-   2 pinches of [sea salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)

2

Meanwhile, whisk together all the ingredients for the dressing until thoroughly combined

-   75g of [Saint Agur Blue Cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blue-cheese-recipes), room temperature
-   80g of crème fraiche
-   1 tbsp of [lemon juice](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lemon-recipes)

4

To serve, place the sliced lettuce on the base of a large serving platter (or individual bowls, if you prefer). Top with the salad and garnish with the diced Saint Agur

-   2 [baby gem lettuces](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/lettuce-recipes), washed, drained and finely sliced
-   75g of [Saint Agur Blue Cheese](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/blue-cheese-recipes), cut into 0.5cm dice
