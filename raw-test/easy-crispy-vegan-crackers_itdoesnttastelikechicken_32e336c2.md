Add the flour, nutritional yeast, Italian seasoning, salt, garlic powder, black pepper, and baking powder to a [food processor](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B005I6ZKCE/ref=as_li_qf_asin_il_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=itdottalich-20&creative=9325&linkCode=as2&creativeASIN=B005I6ZKCE&linkId=a28c0360c9f885d2c9f29254a451994d) and pulse several times to combine. Peel up each cracker and place on the prepared baking sheets, making sure the crackers don't touch. Re-roll any leftover dough scraps and cut more crackers until all the dough is used up.

![Bake until browned around the edges.](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/easy-crispy-vegan-crackers-recipe-crunchy-06.jpg)

Bake for 12 - 18 minutes, until the crackers are golden brown around the edges, making sure to keep an eye on them so they don't burn. If some crackers look browned before the rest of the crackers are finished baking, remove them from the pan and continue baking the rest. Let cool completely on the pan. Store crackers in an air-tight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.

## Easy Crispy Vegan Crackers

Just 9 ingredients to make these super crunchy crackers. Perfect for serving with your favourite vegan cheese, hummus, or any dip, or enjoy as a snack all on their own.

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 18 minutes

Total: 33 minutes

Servings: 8 (makes about 80 crackers)

-   Use a [pizza cutter](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B000W7FGFA/ref=as_li_qf_asin_il_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=itdottalich-20&creative=9325&linkCode=as2&creativeASIN=B000W7FGFA&linkId=e7376ec9f1db750084146f143608e790) or sharp knife to cut the dough into a grid pattern making crackers that are about 1 ½" square.
