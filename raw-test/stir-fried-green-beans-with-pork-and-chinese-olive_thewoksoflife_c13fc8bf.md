Today, I want to introduce you to a brand new dish, Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetables. Let’s hopscotch over the rather novel fact that the green beans are chopped short and address the principal uniqueness of this dish: the “olive vegetable.”

You are undoubtedly asking, what the _heck_ is an olive vegetable? After some digging on the internet, I discovered that it’s not only woefully grammatically questionable but also that “olive vegetable” is a condiment made with a combination of minced green olives and Chinese mustard greens.

I was REALLY surprised to find out that it’s not a preserved vegetable like Chinese preserved mustard greens, but that it’s actually cooked. The basic process is to boil the green olives for half of an hour, then stir-fry the olives with Chinese mustard greens along with oil and a few choice spices until everything is wilted.

(Note that you shouldn’t be alarmed when you see a couple of olive pits in the jar. Just pick them out.)

[![Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/chinese-olive-vegetable.jpg)](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-dried-preserved-ingredients/#olive-vegetables)

This unique concoction originated in Chao Zhou (潮州). The cuisine from this region is called  [Teochew or Chiu-chow cuisine](http://amzn.to/2DwYuy9). When I was in the professional world and traveled to that area for business many years ago, I had some of the best tasting food there.

I just remember all the dishes were so delicate and well-balanced.  It shares a lot of the same cooking techniques as Cantonese cuisine, but the ingredients sometimes are more elevated and surprising at times.

## Recipe Instructions

Marinate the ground pork with 1 teaspoon [Shaoxing wine](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-sauces-vinegars-oils/#shaoxing), 1 teaspoon [light soy sauce](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-sauces-vinegars-oils/) and a pinch of [white pepper powder](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-spices-condiments/#white-pepper). Let sit for 15 minutes.

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-8.jpg)

Heat 1½ tablespoons of oil in your [wok](https://thewoksoflife.com/how-to/chinese-cooking-tools/) over medium heat. Add the green beans, stir-fry, and spread the beans out in a single layer.

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-9.jpg)

Let cook for 20 seconds. Then stir and repeat the spreading step several times until the green beans are slightly wilted and cooked through. Turn the heat down to medium if needed to avoid burning.

It takes about 5 minutes to cook the green beans this way. Transfer your stir-fried green beans to a dish and set aside.

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-10.jpg)

Next, heat 1 tablespoon of oil in your wok over medium heat. Add the minced [ginger](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/#ginger) and [garlic](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/#garlic).

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-11.jpg)

Stir for a few seconds, turn up the heat, and add the pork.

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-12.jpg)

Stir-fry the pork until it’s cooked through.

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-13.jpg)

Now add the [preserved olive vegetable](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-dried-preserved-ingredients/#olive-vegetables), [Shaoxing wine](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-sauces-vinegars-oils/#shaoxing), and sugar.

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-14.jpg)

Stir-fry everything to combine. Add the green beans back to the wok along with the [dried chili peppers](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-spices-condiments/#red-dried-chili-peppers) and [sesame oil](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-sauces-vinegars-oils/#sesame-oil).

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-15.jpg)

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-2.jpg)

Stir for about a minute, salt to taste, and plate these Chao Zhou stir-fried green beans!

![Stir-Fried Green Beans with Pork and Chinese Olive Vegetable, by thewoksoflife.com](https://thewoksoflife.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/stir-fried-green-beans-5.jpg)

The flavor of the stir-fried green beans cooked with preserved olive vegetable is both delicious and addictive!

## Recipe

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 10 minutes

Total: 30 minutes

##### For the meat:

-   ▢ 4 ounces [ground pork](https://thewoksoflife.com/2018/01/how-to-grind-meat-without-a-grinder/) (or chicken, 115g)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [Shaoxing wine](https://thewoksoflife.com/shaoxing-wine-the-key-to-authentic-chinese-cooking/)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [light soy sauce](https://thewoksoflife.com/soy-sauce/)
-   ▢ 1/8 teaspoon [white pepper powder](https://thewoksoflife.com/what-is-white-pepper/)

##### For the rest of the dish:

-   ▢ 2 1/2 tablespoons vegetable oil (divided)
-   ▢ 1 pound green beans (450g, cut into ½-inch pieces)
-   ▢ 1 teaspoon [ginger](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/#ginger) (minced)
-   ▢ 3 cloves [garlic](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-chives-scallions-aromatics-peppers/#garlic) (minced)
-   ▢ ¼ cup [preserved olive vegetable](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-dried-preserved-ingredients/#olive-vegetables)
-   ▢ 1 tablespoon [Shaoxing wine](https://thewoksoflife.com/shaoxing-wine-the-key-to-authentic-chinese-cooking/)
-   ▢ ¼ teaspoon sugar
-   ▢ 6-7 [dried chili peppers](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-spices-condiments/#red-dried-chili-peppers) (deseeded and chopped into coarse pieces, optional)
-   ▢ ½ teaspoon [sesame oil](https://thewoksoflife.com/sesame-oil/)
-   ▢ 1/4 teaspoon [salt](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-spices-condiments/#salt) (or to taste)

-   Marinate the ground pork with 1 teaspoon Shaoxing wine, 1 teaspoon light soy sauce and a pinch of white pepper powder. Let sit for 15 minutes.
    
-   Heat 1½ tablespoons of oil in your wok over medium heat. Add the green beans, stir-fry, and spread the beans out in a single layer. Let cook for 20 seconds. Then stir and repeat the spreading step several times until the green beans are slightly wilted and cooked through. Turn the heat down to medium if needed to avoid burning. It takes about 5 minutes to cook the green beans this way. Transfer to a dish and set aside.
    
-   Next, heat 1 tablespoon of oil in your wok over medium heat. Add the minced ginger and garlic. Stir for a few seconds, turn up the heat, and add the pork. Stir-fry the pork until it’s cooked through. Now add the olive vegetable, Shaoxing wine, and sugar. Stir-fry everything to combine. Add the green beans back to the wok along with the dried hot peppers and sesame oil. Stir for about a minute, salt to taste, and serve!
