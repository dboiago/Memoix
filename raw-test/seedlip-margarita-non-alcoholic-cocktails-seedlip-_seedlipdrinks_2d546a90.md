Grove 42

A sophisticated, non-alcoholic rendition of the classic Margarita, the Seedlip Margarita combines our Notas de Agave with the natural sweetness of agave syrup and the tartness of fresh lime juice. Served over ice in a tumbler with a salted rim and garnished with a lime wheel, this non-alcoholic cocktail offers a refreshing and balanced flavor profile, making it an ideal choice for those seeking a mindful sipping experience without compromising on taste.

## Ingredients

-   Seedlip Notas de Agave: 2 oz
    
-   Agave Syrup: 1 tbsp
    
-   Fresh Lime Juice: ½ oz
    

## Glass & Garnish

-   Rocks
    
-   Salt rim
    
-   Lime wheel
    

## Method

1.  Add all ingredients into a shaker with ice
    
2.  Shake and strain over ice
    
3.  Garnish w/ a Lime wheel & half-rim of salt
