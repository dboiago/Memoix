## Ingredients for 6:

-   25cl [berry sorbet](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/fr/recette/sorbet-fruits-bois.html "Sorbet aux fruits des bois")
-   25cl [apricot sorbet](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/en/recipe/apricot-sorbet.html "Apricot Sorbet")
-   25cl [lemon sorbet](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/fr/recette/sorbet-citron.html "Sorbet au citron")
-   25cl [speculoos biscuit ice cream](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/fr/recette/glace-speculoos.html "Glace au spéculoos")

-   20 [meringue fingers](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/en/recipe/swiss-meringue.html "Swiss Meringue")

-   A few raspberries
-   A few red currants
-   A few blackberries

## Method for Ice Cream Vacherin:

1

Before starting this Ice Cream Vacherin recipe, make sure you have organised all the necessary ingredients. Stretch some cling film...

2

... on one side of a vacherin ring. Place the ring on a cake board, with the cling film side facing down.

5

Bury a few fresh raspberries in the layer of berry sorbet.

6

Spread another layer of sorbet, this time using a different flavour. I used [apricot sorbet](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/en/recipe/apricot-sorbet.html "Apricot Sorbet") for this recipe. Of course, you can use any flavours of your choice.

7

Spread a third layer of sorbet. I used [lemon sorbet](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/fr/recette/sorbet-citron.html "Sorbet au citron"). Spread into an even layer.

9

Sprinkle the surface with roughly chopped speculoos biscuits. Freeze to set.

10

When set, flip the cake and remove the sheet of cling film. Release the cake from the ring. For an easier operation, slightly heat up the sides of the ring with a blow torch, then gently slide the ring upwards. Arrange the meringue finger vertically all around the cake.

11

Use a little [Chantilly cream](https://www.meilleurduchef.com/en/recipe/chantilly-cream-whipped-cream.html "Chantilly Cream") to ensure the meringue sticks adhere to the sides.

12

Repeat the operation until the sides of the cake are completely coated with meringue fingers.

13

Pipe a lace of Chantilly cream around the edge of the cake.

14

Pipe a large swirl of Chantilly in the centre of the cake...

15

... and decorate the surface of the Vacherin with an assortment of red berries.

16

Reserve in the freezer until ready to serve. Enjoy!
