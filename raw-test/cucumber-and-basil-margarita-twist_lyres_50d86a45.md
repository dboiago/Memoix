Fresh, green and a little bit lucky. This Cucumber & Basil Margarita Twist brings garden-fresh vibes to your St Patrick’s Day celebrations — zesty lime, cooling cucumber and lively basil, all topped with Lyre’s Margarita. All the festive spirit, none of the morning-after mischief.

#### Ingredients

-   60mL [Lyre's Agave Blanco Spirit](https://lyres.com/en-uk/products/agave-blanco-spirit)
-   25mL fresh lime juice
-   15mL simple syrup
-   40g fresh cucumber (3-4 thick slices)
-   5-6 fresh basil leaves

#### Method

Muddle cucumber and basil gently in a shaker. Add Agave Blanco, lime juice and simple syrup with ice. Shake briefly to chill. Strain over fresh ice in a rocks glass.

#### Garnish

Cucumber ribbon or basil leaf
