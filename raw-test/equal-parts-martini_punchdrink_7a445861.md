## Jim Kearns, [Le Crocodile](https://punchdrink.com/recipes/equal-parts-martini/%E2%80%9Chttps://lecrocodile.com/%E2%80%9C) | Brooklyn, NY

Just because it’s not a bowlful of gin, doesn’t mean a Martini can’t still have a bone-dry flavor profile. This 50/50 version of the classic relies on Miró Extra Seco, an extremely dry, wormwood-forward, Spanish vermouth alongside Perry’s Tot Navy Strength gin. A whisper of absinthe ties the ingredients together.

##### Ingredients

Serving: 1

-   1 1/2 ounces ounces navy strength gin, preferably Perry’s Tot
-   1 1/2 ounces dry vermouth, preferably Miro Extra Seco
-   1 dash absinthe

Garnish: lemon twist, olive  

##### Directions

1.  Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass over ice and stir until chilled.
2.  Strain into a chilled coupe.
3.  Garnish with lemon twist and olive.
