#### **Blue Jasmine Rice**

(Optional, you can also just keep the rice plain white, or use brown, red, or black rice)

-   2 cups of chopped red cabbage (see note 1)
-   2 ½ cups water
-   1 ½ cups uncooked jasmine rice
-   ⅛ - ¼ teaspoon baking soda

#### **Budu Dressing**

\*Dressing can be made well in advance and kept in the fridge

-   3 Tbsp roughly chopped shallot
-   3 cloves garlic
-   3 inches lemongrass, bottom half only, finely sliced
-   3 thin slices ginger or galangal
-   ¼ - ½ cup (60-120 ml) water
-   ¼ cup  (60 ml) fish sauce
-   1 tsp (5 ml) fermented shrimp paste
-   100 g (3.5 oz) palm sugar, chopped
-   1\-2 tablespoon (15-30 ml) Thai tamarind paste, store bought or [homemade](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/making-tamarind-paste/)
-   2 makrut lime leaves, center ribs removed and torn into chunks

#### **Salad Components** (amounts are estimates only, see note 2)

-   3 Tbsp (45 ml) dried shrimp
-   ¾ cup (180 ml) dried shredded coconut
-   2 stalks lemongrass, bottom half only, very thinly sliced
-   10\-12 makrut lime leaves, center ribs removed and very finely julienned
-   6 segments pomelo, torn into small chunks (or sub grapefruit segments)
-   2 limes, cut into wedges 
-   Roasted chili flakes, to taste
-   A few fresh crunchy vegetables, julienned or finely chopped such as red/green cabbage, carrots, long beans, bean sprouts or cucumber
-   Dark leafy greens, finely julienned, such as wild betel leaves (traditional), Chinese broccoli or kale
