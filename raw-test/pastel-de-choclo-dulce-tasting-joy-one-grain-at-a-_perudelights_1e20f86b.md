### **Light and simple sweet corn cake.**

 [![](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-de-choclo-final-1024x721.jpg "pastel de choclo final")](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-de-choclo-final.jpg)As far as we know, the savory version of **_pastel de choclo_** has been a favorite in Peru since the XIX century, and ours is not the same as the famous one made by Chilean cooks. Just like in the States,there are hundreds of **recipes of corn cake** in Latin America, maybe because **corn** is such a staple in our diet and we are always figuring out different ways to enjoy it. In our kitchens we have every variety with all kinds of fillings: beef, chicken, olives, cheese, etc.

I love to have it as a sweet treat with coffee or tea. Here you have the recipe for the loveliest **corn cake** ever! The texture is wonderfully light and moist, with a tender crumb thanks to the cream cheese and butter. It has an intense **corn** flavor and the tiny amount of flour (can you believe it only has 2 tablespoons?) is enough to bind everything so the result is amazingly good and satisfying. [![](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo1-1024x768.jpg "pastel choclo1")](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo1.jpg)[ ![](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo2-1024x768.jpg "pastel choclo2")](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo2.jpg)[ ![](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo3-1024x768.jpg "pastel choclo3")](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo3.jpg)

Any type of **white corn** is good in this **cake**, and I have to confess that when I’m feeling lazy and don´t want to go to the supermarket, I just go to the freezer and take out a bag of **frozen Peruvian corn** that I buy in my Latin supermarket, thaw the amount needed for the recipe and proceed as indicated. It really is delicious this way too, though **fresh corn** is ideal; the fresher the better. **Yellow corn** will produce a sweeter version of the **cake**, which is not bad at all, but I recommend you stick to **white corn** for authenticity´s sake.

In some households they like to add raisins, cinnamon, or any other flavoring to the dough. I prefer a simpler style because I like the **corn** flavor to be the star of the **cake**. Remember, if you use older **corn**, the **cake** will be drier and somewhat tough. And never substitute **corn** with cornmeal or corn flour. That’s the way **corn cake** is made in the US, but our preparation is different, and it simply won´t work. [![](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo4-1024x768.jpg "pastel choclo4")](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo4.jpg)[ ![](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo5-1024x768.jpg "pastel choclo5")](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo5.jpg)[ ![](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo6-1024x768.jpg "pastel choclo6")](https://perudelights.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/pastel-choclo6.jpg)

Once the **cake** is ready, you can leave it covered in the counter and it will keep fresh and moist up to a couple of days. Serve it with caramel or vanilla ice cream, fresh fruit on the side (this is up to you), or custard over or under the **cake**. If you are feeling indulgent, serve it as breakfast with guava jelly instead of the custard, and a slice of cheese. (This is not Peruvian, but I like it.)

Here you have my own interpretation of [**Chef Flavio Solórzano**´s](https://perudelights.com/ocopa/) master recipe. Follow the instructions carefully and you´ll be charmed with the magic simplicity of this **cake**.

### **Sweet corn cake (Serves 6)**

-   **8 oz. fresh white corn (about 2 cups)**                
-   **8 oz. cream cheese (about 1 package)**
-   **1 stick unsalted butter (about  ½  cup)**              
-   **½ cup sugar**
-   **2 egg whites**
-   **3 egg yolks**
-   **2 teaspoons baking powder**
-   **2 tablespoons all-purpose flour**

 **Preparation:**

**\*Have all the ingredients at room temperature. Line the bottom of a 9 inch round pan with parchment paper. Preheat the oven to 350F.**

**\*Whip butter and sugar until creamy. Add egg yolks one by one, then the cream cheese. Whip at high speed for 5 minutes.**

**\*Process the corn in the blender or food processor until very fine. Add to the butter mixture with the baking powder and flour.**

**\*Meanwhile, whip the egg whites until soft peaks form and fold them very gently.**

**\*Pour the batter in the pan and bake for 45 minutes or until the cake is lightly golden and firm.**

**\*Remove the cake from the oven and cool in a rack.**
