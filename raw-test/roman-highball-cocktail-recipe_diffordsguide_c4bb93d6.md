### Glass:

Serve in a [Highball (max 10oz/300ml)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware)

Ingredients:

1 1⁄2 fl oz

[Amaro (e.g. Averna)](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/396/bws/amaro)

3⁄4 fl oz

[Ginger liqueur](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/257/domaine-de-canton)

1⁄2 fl oz

Lime juice (freshly squeezed)

1 2⁄3 fl oz

[Thomas Henry Soda Water](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/6575/thomas-henry-soda-water) chilled

### Prepare:

1.  Select and pre-chill a [HIGHBALL (MAX 10OZ/300ML) GLASS](https://www.diffordsguide.com/encyclopedia/329/cocktails/cocktail-glassware).
2.  Prepare skewered crystallised ginger for garnish.

### How to make:

3.  [SHAKE](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/cocktail-shakers) first 3 ingredients with ice.
4.  [STRAIN](https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1150/how-to-make-cocktails/how-to-strain-a-cocktail) into ice-filled glass.
5.  TOP with soda.

### Garnish:

6.  Garnish with skewered crystallised ginger.
