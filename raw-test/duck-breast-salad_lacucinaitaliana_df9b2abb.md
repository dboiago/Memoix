### List of Ingredients

-   smoked duck breast, sliced
-   3 OZ. of curly endive, washed
-   3 OZ. of Belgian endive, washed
-   baby spinach, washed
-   3 of tangerines
-   1 of pomegranate
-   Slivered almonds
-   Soy sauce
-   Extravirgin olive oil

### Method

1

In a pan, quickly toast a handful of almonds. Break up the lettuce leaves; remove the seeds from the pomegranate; peel 2 tangerines and separate them into wedges.

2

Prepare a vinaigrette by vigorously combining 2 1/2 tablespoons of oil with 2 tablespoons of tangerine juice and 1 teaspoon of soy sauce. Combine all of the ingredients to form the salad and dress with the vinaigrette.
