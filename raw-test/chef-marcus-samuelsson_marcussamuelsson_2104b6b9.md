## **Seat at the Table**

## Hungry for a solid slice of culture?

Tour the most iconic and influential restaurants and hidden gems in America with Seat at the Table. Join world-renowned chef Marcus Samuelsson, along with acclaimed pioneer of California cuisine chef Jonathan Waxman, on an inspiring journey. These James Beard Foundation award-winning restauranteurs welcome you as they set the table and the scene in this delicious podcast.

Hear the unique and unexpected ways that legendary, game-changing restaurants are made as Marcus and Jonathan meet with industry-leading culinary experts and reveal the brave entrepreneurs, cultural impact, and trailblazing history behind these delectable institutions.
