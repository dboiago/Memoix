## `Cheese Breadsticks Recipe | Mozzarella-Stuffed Pizza Dough Sticks`

These cheese breadsticks are stuffed with mozzarella string cheese and baked at 450°F for 10-12 minutes on a Baking Steel. Made from simple pizza dough (100g per stick), they're crispy on the outside, gooey in the middle, and perfect for dipping in marinara sauce. Whether you're using leftover dough, store-bought, or our [72-hour pizza dough](https://bakingsteel.com/blogs/recipes/72-hour-pizza-dough-recipe?_pos=1&_sid=95dd36132&_ss=r) recipe, this easy method delivers restaurant-quality breadsticks at home.  

## Why You’ll Love These Cheesy Breadsticks

They’re fun to make, need just a few ingredients, and come out with a golden, crunchy crust and a melty cheese pull every time. Perfect for dipping in warm tomato sauce or serving alongside pizza night.

## Ingredients

-   100 g pizza dough ball (per stick)
-   50 g cheese (mozzarella string cheese or any melting cheese)
-   Flour and semolina for dusting

## Directions

1.  **Preheat:** Place your [Baking Steel](https://bakingsteel.com/collections/steels/products/the-baking-steel-original) on the top rack and heat the oven to 450°F for 45 minutes. The hot steel gives you a perfectly crisp crust.
2.  **Shape the dough:** Divide the dough into 100 g balls. On lightly floured parchment, flatten each ball into a 6-inch oval to remove large air bubbles.
3.  **Add cheese:** Place a mozzarella stick in the center of each piece of dough.
4.  **Roll and seal:** Roll the dough over the cheese like a cigar. Pinch the seam and ends closed, then set seam-side down on the parchment.
5.  **Bake:** Launch onto your hot Baking Steel and bake for 10–12 minutes, until golden brown and crisp. For extra color, finish under the broiler for 30 seconds.
6.  **Serve:** Let cool briefly, then serve with warm tomato sauce for dipping.

## FAQ

**Q: How long to bake cheese breadsticks?**  
A: Bake cheese breadsticks at 450°F for 10-12 minutes on a preheated Baking Steel. They're done when golden brown and crispy. For extra color, finish under the broiler for 30 seconds.

**Q: What temperature to bake cheese breadsticks?**  
A: Bake at 450°F with your Baking Steel preheated for 45 minutes. The hot steel creates a crispy bottom crust while the cheese melts inside.

**Q: What cheese is best for cheese breadsticks?**  
A: Mozzarella string cheese works perfectly because it's pre-portioned and melts beautifully. You can also use cheddar, provolone, or a mozzarella-fontina blend.

**Q: How do you make cheese breadsticks from pizza dough?**  
A: Flatten 100g pizza dough balls into 6-inch ovals, place a mozzarella stick in the center, roll like a cigar, pinch the seams closed, and bake seam-side down at 450°F for 10-12 minutes.

**Q: Can you make cheese breadsticks ahead of time?**  
A: Yes. Shape and fill the breadsticks, then refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking. You can also freeze them after baking and reheat at 400°F for 5-7 minutes.

**Q: What do you serve with cheese breadsticks?**  
A: Serve with warm marinara sauce, garlic butter, or ranch dressing for dipping. They're also great alongside pizza, pasta, or salad.

**Q. Can I use store-bought dough?** A:Yes, just let it warm up to room temperature before shaping. It makes stretching easier and prevents tearing.

**How do I keep them from bursting open?** Be sure to pinch the seams tightly and place the seam side down when baking.

**Can I freeze them?** Absolutely. Bake first, cool completely, then freeze. Reheat in a 400°F oven for 5–7 minutes.
