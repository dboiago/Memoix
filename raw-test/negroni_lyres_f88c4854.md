### GET INTO THE SPIRIT

As cocktails go, the Negroni is as simple as they come. Stir down equal proportions of gin, Italian bitters and sweet red vermouth, then strain over ice. Garnish with a piece of orange rind, and away you go. Sometimes the simple things really are the best.

The Negroni was invented in 1919 by the French general Count Negroni, who famously asked a bartender to add a touch of gin rather than soda to his Americano, in honour of his last trip to London. It's an intensely aromatic cocktail, thanks to the botanicals present in all three spirit ingredients. The sweet, vinous character of the vermouth is balanced by the assertive bitterness of the Italian liqueur, with gin adding complexity and palate weight. In spite of its heady spirit content, it is eminently drinkable, and also looks damn fine in the glass.

Glowing red in hue, Negroni manages to be both sophisticated and simple at the same time. It's now celebrating being 103 years young.

### **3 INNOVATIVE COCKTAILS FOR NEGRONI-LOVERS**

Looking for a chilled afternoon to soak up the afternoon sun? These perfect recipes will get you on your way.

##### SMOKED CHOCOLATE BOULEVARDIER

**LOW CALORIE | NON-ALCOHOLIC | VEGAN**

**INGREDIENTS**  
30mL/1 Fl Oz Cacao infused Lyre’s American Malt  
30mL/1 Fl Oz Lyre’s Italian Orange  
30mL/1 Fl Oz Lyre’s Aperitif Rosso  
2 Dashes Fee Bros Chocolate Bitters  
Chocolate bitters infused wood

**METHOD:**  
Stir ingredients into mixing glass with ice. Strain into decanter, smoke for 10 seconds, place lid on decanter and swirl. Pour into glass filled its fresh large ice cube.

**GLASS:** Double Old Fashioned

**GARNISH:** White Chocolate, cherry bag

##### POMPELMO NEGRONI

**LOW CALORIE | NON-ALCOHOLIC | VEGAN**

**INGREDIENTS**  
30mL/1 Fl Oz Lyre’s Dry London Spirit  
15mL/0.5 Fl Oz Lyre’s Italian Spritz  
15mL/0.5 Fl Oz Lyre’s Aperitif Rosso  
15mL/0.5 Fl Oz Lemon Juice  
60mL/2 Fl Oz Capi Grapefruit Soda

**METHOD:**  
Shake all ingredients except grapefruit soda. Strain into glass, add soda, fill with ice.

**GLASS:** Fancy Tall / Highball

**GARNISH:** Pink Grapefruit Twist, Thyme Sprig

##### NEGRONI FRAGOLA

**LOW CALORIE | NON-ALCOHOLIC | VEGAN**

**INGREDIENTS**  
30mL/1 Fl Oz Lyre’s Dry London Spirit  
30mL/1 Fl Oz Lyre’s Italian Spritz  
30mL/1 Fl Oz Lyre’s Aperitif Rosso  
2 Fresh Strawberries chopped

**METHOD:**  
Light press strawberries in bottom of glass with barspoon/ muddler. Add all ingredients, fill with ice, stir.

**GLASS:** Double Old Fashioned

**GARNISH:** Long Cucumber Slice
