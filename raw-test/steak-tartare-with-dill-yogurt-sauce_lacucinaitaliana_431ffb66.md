**Cooks in:** _20mins_

**Levels:** _Easy_

**Serves:** _4_

### List of Ingredients

-   14 OZ. of beef
-   2/3 C. of Greek yogurt
-   Dill
-   Shallot
-   Lime
-   Fresh cilantro
-   Extravirgin olive oil
-   Salt
-   Pepper

### Method

1

Chop the beef using the blade of a knife until you achieve an even consistency. Season with a drizzle of oil, salt, pepper, and a handful of fresh chopped cilantro.

2

Chop a large handful of dill and add it to the yogurt; add in a small chopped shallot, the zest of one lime and a few drops of its juice, a drizzle of oil, and a pinch of salt.

3

Serve the meat garnished with cilantro and dill leaves and lime wedges.
