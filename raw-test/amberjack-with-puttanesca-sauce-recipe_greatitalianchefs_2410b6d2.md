Michelin-starred chef [Mauro Uliassi](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/chefs/mauro-uliassi) transforms the classic puttanesca sauce in this delicious starter featuring thinly sliced amberjack and raw prawn. Amberjack is a fantastic, underrated fish, with a tender texture and subtle, sweet taste. If unavailable Arctic char may be used as a substitute.

First published in 2015

### Metric

### Imperial

-   Blender
-   Dehydrator
-   Muslin cloth

1

To begin, prepare the capers. Desalt the capers by soaking them in warm water for 2 hours

-   200g of [baby capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), salted

2

Drain and dry the capers with kitchen paper and place on a baking tray. Dry in a dehydrator or oven overnight at 60°C

3

When ready to cook, blitz the oregano with the oil and strain through a muslin cloth

-   400g of extra virgin olive oil
-   30g of dried oregano

4

To make the puttanesca sauce, heat a medium saucepan with the olive oil. Fry the red onion until tender, then add the garlic and cook for a further 2 minutes. Stir in the passata, followed by the basil, coriander and salt and pepper to taste. Cook for 3 more minutes and remove from the heat

-   1 small red onion, chopped
-   1 [garlic clove](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/garlic-recipes), chopped
-   100g of [passata](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/tomato-recipes)
-   10g of [basil](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/basil-recipes)
-   [coriander](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/coriander-recipes)
-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [black pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)

5

Place the slices of amberjack on a plate. Season the raw prawns with salt and pepper and place in the centre with a little of the puttanesca sauce drizzled around

-   [salt](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/salt-recipes)
-   [pepper](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/peppercorn-recipes)
-   1 [amberjack fillet](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/amberjack-recipes), thinly sliced
-   8 [prawns](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/prawn-starter-recipes), peeled

6

Add the capers in oil, dried capers, anchovies and olives and a sprinkling of salad leaves. Finish with a drizzle of oregano oil and serve immediately

-   20 [baby capers](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/caper-recipes), in oil
-   10 [black olives](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/collections/black-olive-recipes), cut into cubes
-   5 [anchovies](https://www.greatitalianchefs.com/collections/anchovy-recipes)
-   baby salad leaves
