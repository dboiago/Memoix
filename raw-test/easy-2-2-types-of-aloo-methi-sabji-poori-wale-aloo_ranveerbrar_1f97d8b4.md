**Restaurant Style Aloo Methi**

Preparation time 10 minutes

Cooking time 15-20 minutes

Serve 2

**Ingredients**

1 tbsp Oil, तेल

¼ tsp Cumin seeds, जीरा

2 Garlic cloves, chopped, लहसुन

1 large Onion, chopped, प्याज

2 Green chilli, less spicy,  chopped,  हरी मिर्च

A pinch of asafoetida, हींग

½ tsp Turmeric powder, हल्दी पाउडर

½ tsp Degi red chilli powder, देगी लाल मिर्च पाउडर

1 tsp Coriander powder, धनिया पाउडर

½ cup fresh Tomato puree, ताज़े टमाटर की प्यूरी

Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार

¼ tsp Sugar, चीनी

little Water, पानी

1 cup fresh Fenugreek leaves, ताजा मेथी के पत्ते

2 medium size Potato, boiled, peeled, diced, आलू

Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार

¼ tsp Sugar, चीनी

Water, पानी

Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार

1 tsp Lemon juice,  नींबू का रस

**For Garnish**

½ tsp Dry fenugreek leaves, crushed, कसूरी मेथी

Coriander sprig, धनिया पत्ता

### Process

**Home Style Aloo Methi**

**For Soak Methi Leaves**

-   In a bowl, add fenugreek leaves, water, salt to taste and keep it aside for 5-10 minutes.

**For Tempering**

-   In a kadai, add oil, ghee once it’s hot, add cumin seeds and let it splutter well.
-   Add garlic cloves, onion and saute it well.
-   Add diced potato, salt to taste, turmeric powder, degi red chilli powder and mix everything well.
-   Cover it with the lid and cook on medium flame until the potatoes turn soft.
-   Add little water and saute well. Add tomato, green chilli, little water and mix it well.
-   Add fenugreek leaves and mix it well. Cover it with the lid and cook for 2 minutes on slow flame.
-   Transfer it into a serving dish. Garnish it with coriander sprig, ginger julienned and serve hot with puri or roti.

**Restaurant Style Aloo Methi**

**Process**

-   In a kadai, add oil once it’s hot, add cumin seeds and let it splutter well.
-   Add garlic cloves, onion and saute it well until light brown in color.
-   Add green chilli, asafoetida, turmeric powder, degi red chilli powder, coriander powder and saute it well.
-   Add fresh tomato puree, salt to taste, sugar and saute it well on medium flame.
-   Add little water, soaked fenugreek leaves and saute it well.
-   Add boiled potatoes, little water and toss it well. Check the seasoning once.
-   Finish it with lemon juice and mix it well.
-   Transfer it into a serving bowl, garnish it with dry fenugreek leaves, coriander sprig and serve hot with puri or roti.
